## Overview
[[Venrock]] is a venture-capital firm represented in the source as the lead investor in [[DollarShaveClub]]'s Series A and Series B rounds.

## Current Profile
The article presents Venrock as a high-conviction investor in a company that many existing and prospective investors initially declined to back. Its stated thesis treated Dollar Shave Club as a [[FullStackConsumerBrands]] model rather than merely e-commerce, emphasizing direct customer knowledge, suitable subscription economics, asymmetric marketing, differentiated margins, zero-sum categories, incumbent channel dependence, and learning from usage data.

## Key Characteristics
- Led Dollar Shave Club's Series A despite other investors' limited interest.
- Led the Series B after the company grew from $7 million to $20 million of reported annual revenue.
- Evaluated the company as an integrated consumer-products model rather than a storefront.
- Favored categories where direct customer relationships could challenge retailer- and broadcast-dependent incumbents.
- Benefited financially from increased ownership and the later acquisition.
