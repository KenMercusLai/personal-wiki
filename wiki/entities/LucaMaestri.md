## Overview
[[LucaMaestri]] appears in the Stratechery source as Apple's CFO, paired with [[TimCook]] in pushing Apple's Services narrative during a period of slowing iPhone unit growth.

## Current Profile
The source uses Maestri as part of Apple's financial-communication context rather than as a standalone biography. Thompson argues that Apple Services became financially meaningful because Apple's installed base was enormous, but he treats the Services narrative as incomplete unless understood through Apple's vertical hardware model. Maestri therefore appears as one of the executives whose reporting emphasis helped frame Services as an Apple growth story.
