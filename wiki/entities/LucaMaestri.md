## Overview
[[LucaMaestri]] appears in the Stratechery sources as Apple's CFO, paired with [[TimCook]] in pushing Apple's Services narrative during a period of slowing iPhone unit growth and in explaining the decision to stop reporting device unit sales.

## Current Profile
The sources use Maestri as part of Apple's financial-communication context rather than as a standalone biography. Thompson argues that Apple Services became financially meaningful because Apple's installed base was enormous, but he treats the Services narrative as incomplete unless understood through Apple's vertical hardware model. In the later source, Maestri announces that Apple will no longer provide iPhone, iPad, and Mac unit sales and explains that Apple optimizes revenue and gross-margin dollars. Thompson argues that this financial framing still leaves investors without the active-customer and revenue-per-user metrics needed to evaluate Apple's engagement story.

## Key Characteristics
- CFO of Apple in the source's 2018 strategy analysis.
- Associated with Apple's Services revenue narrative.
- Appears in the context of communicating financial growth as iPhone units slowed.
- Serves as part of the source's distinction between Services financial contribution and hardware-centered strategy.
- Publicly explains Apple's shift away from device unit disclosure.

## Evidence

- Financial context: [[apples-middle-age-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] connects that narrative to a period when iPhone growth had barely moved year over year and then declined for several quarters.
