## Overview
[[TribunePublishing]] is a media company cited in the source as a rapid reversal case for unlimited vacation.

## Current Profile
The essay says Tribune Publishing dropped an unlimited-vacation policy after one week. In the current wiki evidence, the company functions as a cautionary example of how quickly a benefit framed as flexibility can encounter concerns about ambiguity or employee consequences.

## Key Characteristics

## Qualifications
The source does not explain the complete decision process, worker response, replacement policy, or measured outcomes, and this wiki has not independently verified the linked report. The example therefore shows a reported reversal, not causal evidence that every unlimited-leave policy fails.
