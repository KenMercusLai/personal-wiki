## Overview
[[AriannaHuffington]] is presented in this wiki as the co-founder and editor-in-chief of [[HuffingtonPost]] and as an author advocating for sleep, recovery, and healthier work habits through [[TheSleepRevolution]].

## Current Profile
The interview frames Huffington as a media founder whose view of work changed after ill health and fainting from exhaustion. She argues that sleep is not opposed to ambition, but is a precondition for better cognition, emotion, creativity, and performance. Her profile in this source combines personal routine, family values, organizational practice, and media mission: she sleeps about eight hours most nights, avoids devices in her morning routine, promotes nap rooms and vacation-email deletion at HuffPost, and describes the company as a platform meant to inform, inspire, entertain, and empower.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founded [[HuffingtonPost]] in 2005 and remained as editor-in-chief after its sale to AOL.
- Advocates for sleep as a serious performance and well-being input.
- Uses a morning routine built around device avoidance, gratitude or intentions, movement, meditation, coffee, and natural waking.
- Connects burnout prevention to organizational systems, not only individual discipline.
- Treats energy conservation as practical, including repeat outfits and comfortable shoes.
- Links ambition with family security, unconditional love, and willingness to risk failure.
