## Overview
[[LinkedIn]] appears as a company context for scaling lessons, viral product mechanics, and [[SachinRekhi]]'s practitioner account of using [[NetPromoterScore]] in product planning.

## Current Profile
The scaling source uses LinkedIn as an organization that had to formalize hiring and communication as it grew across employee counts and offices. The virality source adds a product-growth angle: by showing connection counts prominently on profiles, LinkedIn made network size into a visible goal and nudged users to invite more people into the network. The NPS source adds an internal product-operations angle: Rekhi used LinkedIn experience to describe quarterly loyalty surveys, cross-functional analysis, verbatim comment categorization, and behavior correlation as inputs to roadmap planning.

## Key Characteristics
- Illustrates recruiting machinery that grows from simple to specialized.
- Uses recurring all-hands meetings for company-wide alignment.
- Reinforces culture through leadership behavior, hiring, rewards, and evaluation.
- Serves as a context for product-sense claims about Silicon Valley CEOs.
- Uses public connection counts as a vanity and network-growth surface.
- Provides a source context for quarterly NPS analysis tied to product planning.
