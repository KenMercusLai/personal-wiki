## Overview
[[ScottKraege]] is one of the five managing partners associated with [[MobiWirelessManagement]] and [[BluefishWirelessManagement]] in the co-CEO article.

## Current Profile
Kraege explains the practical norms behind Mobi's multi-leader structure: the partners have worked together for a decade, expect confrontation and debate, divide departmental responsibility, meet weekly at a roundtable, and support agreed decisions after conflict.

## Key Characteristics
- Managing partner in both Mobi and Bluefish.
- Leads new business marketing in the Mobi example.
- Treats confrontation, dialogue, and debate as necessary for shared leadership.
- Emphasizes transparency and unified support after decisions.
