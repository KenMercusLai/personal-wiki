## Overview
[[ScottKupor]] is the investor-author whose critique of 10-year option exercise periods prompts Adam D'Angelo's response.

## Current Profile
The source presents Scott Kupor as articulating an investor-side concern about extended exercise windows. His summarized argument is that allowing former employees to keep options longer can reduce shares available to compensate future employees and transfer value away from employees who remain to build the company.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the named critic in the source's equity-policy debate.
- Argues, as summarized by D'Angelo, that extended exercise windows can disadvantage continuing employees.
- Is associated with [[AndreessenHorowitz]] in the source.
- Helps bring the previously quieter investor objection into public discussion.
