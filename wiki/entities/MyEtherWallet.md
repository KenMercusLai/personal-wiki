## Overview
[[MyEtherWallet]] is the early Ethereum wallet interface co-created by [[TaylorMonahan]] and [[Kvhnuke]].

## Current Profile
The source describes MyEtherWallet as a simple interface that began when Ethereum users otherwise had to send Ether through command-line tooling. It grew from a flexible two-person side project into widely used Ethereum infrastructure during the DAO aftermath, ENS launch period, ICO boom, and phishing-heavy 2017 growth cycle. By the MyCrypto launch, the source says MEW's repository, domain, AWS instances, and social media accounts remained under [[Kvhnuke]]'s control while Monahan's assembled team moved to [[MyCrypto]].

## Key Characteristics
- Began as a simple "Ether Wallet Generator" style side project for early Ethereum users.
- Became important user-facing infrastructure during the DAO hard fork and withdrawal period.
- Faced major 2017 growth pressure from ICOs, ENS time windows, phishing sites, server scaling, and support volume.
- Represented a client-side wallet ethos around user control, open source, privacy, transparency, and trust minimization.
- Split organizationally from Monahan's team when [[MyCrypto]] launched as a new brand and structure.

## Evidence
- Origin: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says MEW started with two people and solved the early problem that Ether sending otherwise required command-line interaction.
- DAO role: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says MEW built the DAO withdrawal interface after the DAO hard fork.
- Growth pressure: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] links MEW's 2017 scale to rising Ether prices, ICOs, phishing, ENS, support load, and server scaling.
- Asset split: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says [[Kvhnuke]] retained control of the MEW GitHub repository, domain, AWS instances, and social media accounts.

## Qualifications
The source is written from Monahan's perspective during the launch of a competing successor brand, so the page should not treat it as a neutral full history of MEW or of the split.
