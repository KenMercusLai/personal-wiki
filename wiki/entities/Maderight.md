## Overview
[[Maderight]] was a software-powered apparel manufacturing startup that let companies submit designs and manage prototyping, production, and logistics through a web application.

## Current Profile
The source presents Maderight as a first-time-founder failure case rather than a neutral company profile. It was accepted into [[YCombinator]] and [[StartX]], raised venture funding, operated for three years, and shut down after failing to find [[ProductMarketFit]]. Its core tension was whether software could solve apparel customers' lead-time and quality problems when factories resisted adopting the technology.

## Key Characteristics
- Combined apparel manufacturing services with software for prototyping, production, and logistics.
- Received accelerator validation and venture funding but still failed to reach product-market fit.
- Learned from customers that slow lead times and product quality were major pain points.
- Chose a small China service team over deeper exploration of operating its own micro-factory.
- Split focus between existing high-touch service clients and a newer technology product after the fourth product iteration gained adoption.
- Hired a VP of sales before product-market fit and later judged that as premature.
