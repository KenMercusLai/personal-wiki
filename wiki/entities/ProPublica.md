## Overview
[[ProPublica]] is a nonprofit investigative-news organization represented here by a 2019 examination of the path from “free” tax-filing promotion to paid [[TurboTax]] upgrades.

## Current Profile
The source uses a consumer journey as an audit method: reporters search as a taxpayer might, build realistic low-income test profiles, record the screens and prices encountered, inspect client-side page data, compare the commercial route with the government-backed route, and place those findings inside the lobbying and regulatory context. This yields direct evidence about the observed interface while keeping broader claims dependent on public documents, institutional criticism, company statements, and reported legal interpretation.

## Key Characteristics
- Conducts consumer-facing investigations by reproducing product journeys and preserving visible outcomes.
- Combines interface observation with source-code inspection, policy documents, lobbying context, and requests for company response.
- Distinguishes the tested experience from the program rules that should have governed eligibility.
- Publishes practical implications, including historically specific refund information, alongside systemic criticism.

## Evidence
- Journey reconstruction: [[heres-how-turbotax-just-tricked-you-into-paying-to-file-your-taxes-propublica]] follows Google search, account creation, financial prompts, upgrade screens, IRS directories, and the separate Free File page.
- Technical inspection: [[heres-how-turbotax-just-tricked-you-into-paying-to-file-your-taxes-propublica]] reports the “NONFFA” classification found in page source.
- Institutional context: [[heres-how-turbotax-just-tricked-you-into-paying-to-file-your-taxes-propublica]] connects the interface findings to the IRS agreement, oversight criticism, lobbying, and proposed legislation.
- Right of reply: [[heres-how-turbotax-just-tricked-you-into-paying-to-file-your-taxes-propublica]] includes Intuit’s description of its commercial Free Edition and claimed Free File contribution.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one 2019 article rather than ProPublica’s full body of work. The controlled profiles establish what the reporters encountered, not population prevalence, and the source does not reproduce internal product-decision records for every design choice. Its filing thresholds, URLs, political context, and refund details are historical.
