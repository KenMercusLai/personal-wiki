## Overview
[[TomZahavy]] is an AI researcher represented in this wiki by his personal position paper on abductive reasoning, scientific invention, and the limits of current large language models.

## Current Profile
Zahavy argues that contemporary generative AI is strong at induction and deduction but does not obviously reproduce the physically grounded conceptual leap by which Einstein formulated the equivalence principle. He proposes physically consistent multimodal world models as a possible bridge. His later scope note is central to the profile: the argument is personal rather than an institutional position, does not deny that LLM-based systems make real discoveries, and is informed by his stated experience contributing to AlphaProof.
