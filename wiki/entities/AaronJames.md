## Overview
[[AaronJames]] is the author of the wiki source "A guide to becoming a senior product designer."

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Aaron James appears as a practitioner-author explaining product-design career progression. His contribution is not a broad biography but a structured career-development model: understand the local ladder, assess design skills, use feedback, set concrete goals, and revisit progress with a manager or mentor.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from a product-design practitioner perspective.
- Frames seniority as increased impact, influence, ambiguity tolerance, feedback judgment, and autonomy.
- Treats career planning as a practical document supported by rubric-based self-assessment and peer feedback.
- Warns that ladders can create competition and stress when designers use them to compare status.

## Evidence
- Practitioner guidance: [[a-guide-to-becoming-a-senior-product-designer]] presents a long-form guide for designers seeking senior-level growth.
- Seniority model: [[a-guide-to-becoming-a-senior-product-designer]] distinguishes junior and senior work through project scope, influence radius, feedback filtering, failure resilience, ambiguity, and autonomy.
- Planning method: [[a-guide-to-becoming-a-senior-product-designer]] recommends current-level assessment, studying the next level, setting concrete goals, and reviewing progress.
- Ladder qualification: [[a-guide-to-becoming-a-senior-product-designer]] warns that career ladders can become competitive or stressful if treated as public status contests.
