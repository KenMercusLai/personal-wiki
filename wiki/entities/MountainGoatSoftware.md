## Overview
[[MountainGoatSoftware]] is represented in the wiki as the author and publication context for a practical guide to start-stop-continue sprint retrospectives.

## Current Profile
The source presents Mountain Goat Software as a practitioner-oriented Agile and Scrum publisher. Its retrospective guidance emphasizes a repeatable facilitation sequence: collect desired starts, stops, and continues; vary participation formats; vote when ideation slows; select no more than three priorities; retire achieved continue items; and carry the previous list forward as optional context.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes practical [[AgileSoftwareDevelopment]] and Scrum guidance.
- Frames retrospectives around observable behavior changes.
- Uses facilitator variation, multi-voting, and priority limits to turn discussion into a small action set.
