## Overview
[[Legoizer]] is a web app by [[AmyWibowo]] and coworkers that converts uploaded images into Lego mural plans.

## Current Profile
The source describes Legoizer as a creative computing side project built with Matt Baker and Frank Lin. It converts image colors into Lego's limited color space, accounts for Lego coordinate proportions because bricks are not square pixels, estimates needed pieces, generates assembly instructions, and performs brick anti-aliasing. The embedded images show both a converted Starry Night-like result and a physical mural under construction.

## Key Characteristics
- Converts ordinary images into Lego mural instructions.
- Handles limited brick colors and non-square Lego geometry.
- Produces both digital plans and physical art artifacts.
- Fits the source's broader art-technology career thread.

## Evidence
- Conversion workflow: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says Legoizer maps colors to 33 Lego colors and counts required pieces.
- Geometry constraint: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says the app converts into Lego coordinate space because 1:1 Legos are taller than wide.
- Artifact evidence: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] includes images of a converted artwork and a mural being built.
