## Overview
[[NovaKwok]] is the author of a reflective essay connecting knowledge-work interruptions with a critical reading of 《自控力》.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Kwok writes from post-university engineering experience at PingCAP, where concurrent infrastructure work, technical learning, colleague requests, and chat messages made declining focus salient. The essay combines those observations with study lookup, image inspection, and explicit skepticism about claims that outrun their cited evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Engineering practitioner reflecting on attention and work design.
- Treats context switching as a major source of errors, fatigue, and reduced depth.
- Reads popular psychology critically rather than treating a book's citations as conclusive.
- Connects self-control to physiological state, motivation, stress, self-compassion, and long-term purpose.
