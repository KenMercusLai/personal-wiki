## Overview
[[NovaKwok]] is an engineer-author whose reflective essays connect attention, evidence checking, hands-on technical experimentation, and the cognitive consequences of AI-assisted work.

## Current Profile
Kwok writes from post-university engineering experience at PingCAP, where concurrent infrastructure work, technical learning, colleague requests, and chat messages made declining focus salient. Across the available essays, he combines personal observation with study lookup, image inspection, measurement, and explicit skepticism about claims that outrun their evidence.

The newer essay applies that posture to AI. Kwok reports using LLMs to research a suspension setup and implement a low-cost 25 Hz GPS, but keeps problem framing and validation human-owned through phone-sensor runs and a July 2026 track shakedown. He contrasts this practice with delegating diagnosis, coverage judgment, and consequential decisions to fluent systems that users may no longer be equipped to challenge.

## Key Characteristics
- Engineering practitioner reflecting on attention and work design.
- Treats context switching as a major source of errors, fatigue, and reduced depth.
- Reads popular psychology critically rather than treating a book's citations as conclusive.
- Connects self-control to physiological state, motivation, stress, self-compassion, and long-term purpose.
- Uses AI as an implementation and research aid while preserving measurement, primary-source checking, and final judgment.
- Treats cognitive offloading and automation bias as linked risks rather than assuming that faster execution preserves expertise.

## Qualifications
This profile is derived from two autobiographical essays and is not a comprehensive biography. Its accounts of work, motivation, vehicle modification, testing, and AI use are self-reported. The psychological and social claims synthesize primary references, secondary material, hypothetical examples, and anecdotes rather than original research, and the inspected vehicle charts do not independently validate the experimental setup or broader conclusions.
