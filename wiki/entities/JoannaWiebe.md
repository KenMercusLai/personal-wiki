## Overview
[[JoannaWiebe]] appears as the author of the landing-page social-proof article and is described by the source page as a conversion-focused copywriter and founder of Copy Hackers.

## Current Profile
In this source, Wiebe's practical contribution is a copywriting diagnosis of how landing-page proof can accidentally undermine conversion. She treats testimonials, share counts, comments, and celebrity associations as signals that need to be interpreted from the visitor's point of view, not merely displayed as generic credibility assets.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from a conversion-copywriting perspective.
- Connects persuasion psychology to concrete landing-page interface choices.
- Emphasizes prospect relevance over prestige or raw visibility.
- Treats missing or weak trust evidence as an active conversion risk.
