## Overview
[[JianyangGao]] is represented in this wiki as a postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich and the first author of the [[RaBitQ]] line of vector-quantization work.

## Current Profile
Gao describes RaBitQ as the main outcome of doctoral research at Nanyang Technological University under Cheng Long. In March 2026, Gao published a public account alleging that the [[TurboQuant]] paper mischaracterized RaBitQ's method and theory and used an undisclosed asymmetric performance comparison. The profile is limited to Gao's role in that research and dispute rather than a general biography.

## Key Characteristics
- First author and public advocate for the RaBitQ line of work.
- Frames random rotation as a central structural overlap between RaBitQ and TurboQuant.
- Argues for matched implementations, hardware, and parallelism in comparative quantization benchmarks.
- Uses private correction requests, public OpenReview discussion, and conference complaints to seek changes to the academic record.

## Qualifications

It does not provide the complete private correspondence or an independent adjudication, and its claims about what competing authors knew or deliberately did should not be treated as established intent. The profile therefore records Gao's stated role, position, and actions without resolving the dispute in his favor.
