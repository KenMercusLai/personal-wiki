## Overview
[[CS183C]] is the Stanford technology-enabled blitzscaling course that supplied the lecture notes compiled in the source.

## Current Profile
The source presents CS183C as a practitioner-oriented course taught by [[ReidHoffman]], [[JohnLilly]], Chris Yeh, and Allen Blue. Its structure organizes startup growth by organizational scale and brings in operators from companies such as [[Google]], [[Airbnb]], [[LinkedIn]], [[VMware]], [[Netflix]], and [[Stripe]] to discuss scaling from direct experience.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on technology-enabled [[Blitzscaling]] and company growth stages.
- Uses operator lectures and guest speakers rather than a single abstract framework.
- Treats scale as a change in organization, communication, culture, hiring, and CEO work.
- Uses OS1 through OS5 stages to compare employee, user, customer, and revenue scale.
