## Overview
[[Yelp]] is the consumer local-reviews and services company addressed in Talia Jane's 2016 open letter about low-paid Eat24 customer-support work.

## Current Profile
In this source, Yelp appears less as a product company than as an employer whose support labor underwrites a 24/7 food-delivery service. Jane contrasts Yelp's office perks, executive wealth, and $300 million Eat24 acquisition with support workers who struggle to afford food, rent, transit, heat, and medical copays. The source therefore makes Yelp a case for examining the gap between a tech company's public scale and the economic security of its lowest-paid workers.

## Key Characteristics
- Parent-company context for Eat24 after the food-delivery service's acquisition.
- Employer criticized for support wages that allegedly did not match Bay Area living costs.
- Workplace with visible office food and drink perks that Jane treats as a contrast to employee food insecurity.
- Company whose customer-support team helps sustain a 24/7 product promise.
- Public target of an employee open letter addressed to its chief executive.

## Evidence
- Acquisition context: [[an-open-letter-to-my-ceo-talia-jane-medium]] says Yelp spent $300 million to buy Eat24.
- Wage critique: [[an-open-letter-to-my-ceo-talia-jane-medium]] says Jane's after-tax hourly pay and rent left her unable to buy groceries.
- Perk contrast: [[an-open-letter-to-my-ceo-talia-jane-medium]] describes office snacks, bread, drinks, and coconut water as resources and symbols.
- Support-labor role: [[an-open-letter-to-my-ceo-talia-jane-medium]] says customer support is what makes Eat24 operate 24 hours a day.
- Executive address: [[an-open-letter-to-my-ceo-talia-jane-medium]] frames the letter as a direct appeal to Jeremy Stoppelman.

## Qualifications
The source is a first-person worker critique rather than a balanced company profile. It does not include Yelp's response, payroll data, benefits plan details, or independent retention figures, so company-wide conclusions should remain source-scoped.
