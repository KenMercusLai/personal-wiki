## Overview
[[CxEric]] is the bylined author of “牛鞭效应与AI热潮,” represented in the wiki through one essay applying a supply-chain feedback model to AI infrastructure investment.

## Current Profile
The available source shows a cross-domain explanatory style: it begins with a simplified retail example, extracts a systems mechanism, and then applies that mechanism cautiously to AI. CxEric explicitly stops short of declaring an AI bubble and instead states prospective observations that would strengthen or weaken the proposed “super bullwhip” interpretation.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses supply-chain and systems concepts to interpret technology-investment cycles.
- Separates real orders and reported financial results from independent evidence of terminal demand.
- Presents the AI bullwhip thesis as a qualified, falsifiable hypothesis rather than a categorical bubble claim.
