## Overview
[[JamesManyika]] is represented in this wiki as a McKinsey Global Institute director and coauthor of the 2015 article “Four fundamentals of workplace automation.”

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Manyika is one of three authors presenting preliminary research on the automation potential of work activities in the United States. The article emphasizes that widespread partial automation should lead organizations to redesign roles and processes, evaluate augmentation as well as replacement, and manage differences between technical feasibility and actual adoption.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of an activity-level workplace-automation framework.
- Associated with McKinsey Global Institute as a director at publication.
- Connects automation potential to organizational redesign and competitive capability.
- Preserves social, legal, safety, privacy, and distributional risks alongside modeled benefits.
