## Overview
[[AvivOvadya]] is represented in this wiki as a disinformation researcher warning that accessible synthetic-media tools could make fabricated events appear real and weaken confidence in reality.

## Current Profile
Ovadya supplies the motivating threat frame for [[CharlieWarzel]]'s voice-cloning experiment. He describes a future in which easy-to-use manipulation tools democratize falsification and introduces labels including “reality apathy,” “human puppets,” and the “Infocalypse.” The source demonstrates one narrow component of that warning through an impersonation call but does not independently establish the broader social forecast.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames synthetic media as a perception and trust problem rather than only a content-production capability.
- Warns that ease of use can broaden who is able to fabricate convincing events.
- Connects individual manipulations with possible generalized doubt about what is real.
