## Overview
[[DiscoveryLoop]] is presented as a company founded by former Google researchers to use AI for automating parallel experiments in science and engineering.

## Current Profile
Mai Yang reports that Jeff Dean left Google with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals to create Discovery Loop, with Google acting as a founding investor and cloud partner. The essay interprets Dean's earlier YC prediction—decompose problems, run automated experimental loops over subproblems, evaluate many trials, and recombine the results—as a close preview of the company's announced direction. Within this wiki, the company is therefore evidence of an automation thesis, not yet of delivered products or validated outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Targets scientific and engineering experimentation rather than general chat or content generation.
- Is described as proposing, executing, and analyzing many experiments in parallel.
- Is associated in the source with four former Google researchers.
- Extends agentic work from software execution into closed-loop empirical discovery.
