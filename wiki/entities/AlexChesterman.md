## Overview
[[AlexChesterman]] is represented in the wiki as the founder and leader of [[Zoopla]] in [[FredDestin]]'s retrospective.

## Current Profile
Destin credits Chesterman with maintaining a clear real-estate vision, aligning estate agents through equity, pursuing consolidation, demanding fast integration, and combining broad delegation with detailed intervention when operations appeared at risk. The profile is admiring and entirely source-bounded to Destin's perspective.

## Key Characteristics
- Maintained a narrow and repeatedly communicated company vision.
- Used stakeholder alignment and acquisitions as competitive instruments.
- Set demanding integration targets and treated successful execution as a repeatable capability.
- Delegated extensively while retaining the ability to examine operational detail.
- Modeled visible personal focus rather than emphasizing conference presence.

## Evidence
- Strategic alignment: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] says Chesterman offered meaningful equity to large estate agents and pursued a defining merger with FindAProperty.
- Integration standard: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] reports that he set and met a 90-day full-integration target, including billing migration.
- Leadership focus: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] describes him as repeating the vision, avoiding early conference distraction, delegating, and entering detail when risks emerged.

## Qualifications
The account comes from a supportive investor after a successful exit announcement. It does not include Chesterman's own explanation, employee views, failed decisions, governance tensions, or comparative evidence that the described style caused Zoopla's outcome.
