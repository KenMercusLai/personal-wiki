## Overview
[[SSU2000]] is a Microchip synchronization supply unit represented here as the timing chassis used in Telstra's mobile-network NTP architecture after a 2020 upgrade.

## Current Profile
In Hochstein's reconstruction, the chassis hosted separate NTP functions but could not let a Stratum 3 server use a same-chassis Stratum 2 server as its source, removing local redundancy in Sydney and Melbourne. Its GPS card supplied a high-accuracy failover source, yet affected hardware revisions had a week-number rollover defect that remained dormant until a post-September-2022 restart. Replacing Melbourne's chassis in July 2026 restarted the GPS card, moved its date back about 19.7 years, and poisoned the time distributed by its NTP server.

## Key Characteristics
- Hosts telecommunications timing and NTP functions.
- Imposed a same-chassis source limitation that changed Telstra's redundancy topology.
- Included GPS failover hardware capable of promoting an NTP server to Stratum 1.
- Had affected GPS-card revisions vulnerable to a restart-triggered week-number rollover.

## Evidence
- Architecture boundary: [[telestra-outage-report]] quotes the public report saying a Stratum 2 server could not provide time to a Stratum 3 server in the same chassis.
- Failover role: [[telestra-outage-report]] shows Melbourne's GPS card becoming the source for its NTP server after Sydney connectivity failures.
- Vendor warning: [[telestra-outage-report]] retains a September 2022 notice naming some SSU 2000 GPS hardware revisions as rollover-affected.
- Failure trigger: [[telestra-outage-report]] says chassis replacement restarted the card and moved its clock from July 2026 to November 2006.

## Qualifications
The account does not identify the exact affected card revision or fully explain the same-chassis limitation. Hochstein's security explanation is explicitly a guess, and the sources conflict over whether GPS failover belonged to Telstra's intentional design. This page does not establish behavior of unaffected SSU 2000 configurations.

## What Changed
- Established the SSU 2000's source-bounded role in Telstra's redundancy change, GPS failover, and outage trigger.
