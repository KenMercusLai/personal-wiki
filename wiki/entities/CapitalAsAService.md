## Overview
[[CapitalAsAService]] was [[SocialCapital]]'s online early-stage investment product for using submitted transaction data and automated diligence to evaluate companies and make funding decisions across geographies.

## Current Profile
The first-year retrospective describes CaaS as an attempt to turn early-stage investment screening into continuously available software. Founders self-selected into an online queue, supplied operating data, and could receive a decision within hours without travel or an existing investor relationship. The product aimed to compare companies within their categories, assess how effectively prospects became engaged customers, and return model-based feedback while complementing human judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses founder-submitted transaction and operating data as the primary diligence input described in the source.
- Seeks rapid, consistent, globally accessible early-stage funding decisions.
- Frames public-cloud standardization as an enabler of comparable company data.
- Combines automated screening with an asserted role for human judgment and founder feedback.
- Reported substantial first-year reach across countries, U.S. states, founder demographics, and sectors.

## Evidence
- Workflow: [[from-experiment-to-product-capital-as-a-service-one-year-later]] says founders applied online, submitted transaction data, and could receive funding decisions within hours.
- Throughput: [[from-experiment-to-product-capital-as-a-service-one-year-later]] reports about 5,000 applications considered, 520 validated, and 76 approved after the May 2017 launch.
- Geographic reach: [[from-experiment-to-product-capital-as-a-service-one-year-later]] reports applications from 48 countries and 31 states, with investments in 24 countries and 10 states.
- Diversity: [[from-experiment-to-product-capital-as-a-service-one-year-later]] reports that 30% of founders were women and 80% were non-white, while the portfolio spanned ten displayed sectors.
- Early portfolio signals: [[from-experiment-to-product-capital-as-a-service-one-year-later]] reports three up rounds, three companies raising, two M&A outcomes, and one strategic merger at the snapshot date.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to Social Capital's own June 2018 retrospective after roughly one year. The source does not publish the diligence model, feature definitions, training data, calibration, human override process, decision errors, investment amounts, rejected-company outcomes, independent demographic comparison, or realized portfolio returns. Its claims of transparency, consistency, precision, and reduced bias are therefore product goals rather than validated outcomes. Standardized transaction-data diligence may also exclude businesses whose quality is not legible through early digital operating data.
