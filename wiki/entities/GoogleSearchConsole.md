## Overview
[[GoogleSearchConsole]] is Google's site-owner interface for observing and managing a site's relationship with Google Search; in this source it is specifically the evidence and access surface used during an SEO consultant's technical and search audit.

## Current Profile
The guide recommends giving a prospective SEO read-only Search Console access during a paid audit rather than immediately granting write authority. That stage lets the consultant inspect relevant search information and propose work while the owner retains control over implementation. The source does not inventory Search Console features, compare it with other webmaster tools, or show that read-only access alone prevents misuse elsewhere on the site.

## Key Characteristics
- Operated by [[Google]] for site owners working with Google Search.
- Used as an evidence surface for technical and search audits.
- Supports a read-only access stage before a consultant receives broader authority.
- Complements rather than replaces owner review, reference checks, recommendation corroboration, and change tracking.

## Evidence
- Audit role: [[do-you-need-an-seo-search-console-help]] recommends a paid technical and search audit before the hiring decision.
- Permission boundary: [[do-you-need-an-seo-search-console-help]] says prospective consultants will probably need read-only Search Console access and should not receive write access at that stage.
- Governance context: [[do-you-need-an-seo-search-console-help]] places Search Console access inside a broader process of realistic estimates, transparent reasoning, trusted corroboration, and owner accountability.

## Qualifications
The source discusses Search Console only as part of consultant diligence and does not document its complete capabilities, permission model, data coverage, freshness, security, or later product changes. Read-only access limits changes inside this product but does not control access to a content-management system, hosting account, domain registrar, analytics service, advertising account, or other site infrastructure.
