## Overview
[[Seamless]] is a [[Grubhub]] subsidiary identified in the source as an additional registrant of restaurant-linked web domains.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki's evidence, Seamless appears only as part of the same food-ordering platform structure examined in the Grubhub investigation. The publication says it registered thousands of domains, but supplies no separate audit, operating explanation, or Seamless response in the provided article.

## Key Characteristics
- Operated as a Grubhub subsidiary in the article's 2019 account.
- Registered thousands of domains that the publication says appeared to correlate with real restaurants.
- Extends the investigation's domain-registration scale beyond Grubhub's reported 23,000-plus registrations.

- Corporate relationship: [[grubhub-is-buying-up-thousands-of-restaurant-web-addresses-that-means-mom-and-pop-cant-own-their-slice-of-the-internet-new-food-economy]] identifies Seamless as Grubhub's subsidiary.

## Qualifications
The source gives no exact Seamless count, registration dates, current status, separate contractual terms, or evidence that every domain served the same function. Some registrations may have expired, and the article reports Grubhub's general claim that the domain service was contract-authorized and discontinued.
