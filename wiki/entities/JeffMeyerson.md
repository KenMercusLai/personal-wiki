## Overview
[[JeffMeyerson]] is the author of the [[SoftwareEngineeringDaily]] essay "You Are Not A Commodity."

## Current Profile
In the available source, Meyerson writes as an advocate for engineers developing the ability to prototype, deploy, and own products rather than accepting narrow maintenance roles as inevitable. He links that opportunity to cheaper cloud infrastructure and modular internet services, while presenting the argument as motivational career advice rather than empirical labor-market analysis.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates independent product-building as a route to engineer ownership and creative control.
- Treats software infrastructure as decentralized productive capacity available to individuals and small teams.
- Frames greenfield prototyping as both a technical capability and a psychological transition.

## Evidence
- Career position: [[you-are-not-a-commodity-dev-community]] contrasts interchangeable corporate maintenance work with independent product creation.
- Infrastructure thesis: [[you-are-not-a-commodity-dev-community]] cites cloud, distribution, contracting, payment, and collaboration services as individual leverage.
- Capability thesis: [[you-are-not-a-commodity-dev-community]] urges engineers dependent on internal tooling to learn to build and deploy from scratch.

## Qualifications
The profile is derived from one polemical essay. It does not establish Meyerson's wider biography, the representativeness of his career claims, or comparative outcomes for engineers who follow the advice.
