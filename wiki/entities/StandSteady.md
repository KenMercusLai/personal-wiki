## Overview
[[StandSteady]] is a standing-desk company used in the source to show how outsourced warehousing and shipping can remove a physical growth constraint for a small merchant.

## Current Profile
Founder Day Martin started the company from her home in 2012 after a back injury made prolonged sitting difficult and she could not find a suitable standing desk on Amazon. She initially carried 20-pound desks to her porch for FedEx pickup, then began paying Amazon to warehouse and ship them in 2014. In her account, that transfer was necessary for the business to grow beyond founder-handled packing and dispatch.

## Key Characteristics
- Product company founded from an unmet personal need for a standing desk.
- Initially depended on manual, founder-run packing and carrier handoff.
- Shifted warehousing and shipping to Amazon as an operating-scale decision.
- Illustrates fulfillment outsourcing as leverage for a small seller of relatively heavy products.
