## Overview
[[LoganPaul]] is a social-media creator represented here as the opening case in [[DavidPerell]]'s argument about influencer-led [[NakedBrands]].

## Current Profile
Perell uses a temporary New York merchandise shop and its reported ten-block line to show online affinity becoming physical attendance, apparel purchase, and public fan ritual. The case demonstrates concentrated enthusiasm at one event, not the durability, breadth, or causes of Paul's commercial support.

## Key Characteristics
- Converts creator identity into branded merchandise and temporary physical retail.
- Attracts fans willing to coordinate time, travel, waiting, and public participation.
- Serves as a vivid but unmeasured example of personality-led brand attachment.

## Evidence
- Physical activation: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] reports a short-lived NOLITA pop-up and a line extending ten city blocks.
- Merchandise identity: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] describes fans seeking clothing associated with Paul's “Maverick” persona.
- Interpretive role: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] uses the event to motivate a broader thesis about young digital audiences.

## Qualifications
The source gives no attendance audit, sales figures, repeat-purchase data, fan interviews, comparison group, or later outcome. It was written in 2017 and does not support a current biographical or reputational assessment.

## What Changed
- Created a narrowly sourced profile of the pop-up case and its evidentiary limits.
