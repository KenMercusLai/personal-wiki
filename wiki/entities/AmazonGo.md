## Overview
[[AmazonGo]] is Amazon's cashierless physical-store experiment in the Forbes source, used as the clearest example of Amazon combining retail, grocery, payments, AI, cameras, sensors, and app identity into one horizontal system.

## Current Profile
The source describes Amazon Go as an 1,800-square-foot food-store experiment at the foot of Day 1 Tower that opened in January 2018. Shoppers use the Amazon app, take items from shelves, and leave without checkout lines, scanning, or swiping. The store combines learnings from Whole Foods, Amazon's algorithmic and hardware capabilities, AI, camera and sensor technologies, and Amazon Pay. The inspected storefront photo confirms a branded Amazon Go street-level store with app-opening signage and no ordinary checkout emphasis visible from the entrance.

## Key Characteristics
- Cashierless brick-and-mortar store experiment.
- Combines app identity, computer vision-like sensing, payments, physical retail, and grocery operations.
- Functions as a live data and capability-learning site for Amazon employees, residents, and visitors.
- Demonstrates horizontal integration across Amazon rather than only entry into a new retail vertical.
