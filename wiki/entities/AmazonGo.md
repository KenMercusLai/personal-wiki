## Overview
[[AmazonGo]] is Amazon's cashierless physical-store experiment, used as the clearest example of Amazon combining retail, grocery, payments, AI, cameras, sensors, and app identity into one horizontal system.

## Current Profile
The sources describe Amazon Go as an 1,800-square-foot food-store experiment at the foot of Day 1 Tower that opened in January 2018. Shoppers use the Amazon app, take items from shelves, and leave without checkout lines, scanning, or swiping. “Just Walk Out” combines computer vision, sensor fusion, machine learning, app identity, and Amazon Pay; one inspected storefront photo confirms the physical store and another retained image explicitly presents the experience as “no lines, no checkout.” This makes Go both a retail experiment and a payment-interface case in which checkout is absorbed into sensing and account identity.

## Key Characteristics
- Cashierless brick-and-mortar store experiment.
- Combines app identity, computer vision-like sensing, payments, physical retail, and grocery operations.
- Functions as a live data and capability-learning site for Amazon employees, residents, and visitors.
- Demonstrates horizontal integration across Amazon rather than only entry into a new retail vertical.
- Treats payment as an ambient account-and-sensing workflow rather than a separate checkout step.

## Evidence
- Store description: [[bezos-unbound-exclusive-interview-with-the-amazon-founder-on-what-he-plans-to-conquer-next]] describes Go as an 1,800-square-foot frictionless food-store experiment that opened in January 2018.
- Technology stack: [[bezos-unbound-exclusive-interview-with-the-amazon-founder-on-what-he-plans-to-conquer-next]] says Go combines AI, cameras, sensors, and Amazon Pay to identify what was taken, put back, and purchased.
- Whole Foods learning: [[bezos-unbound-exclusive-interview-with-the-amazon-founder-on-what-he-plans-to-conquer-next]] connects Go to knowledge gained from Whole Foods purchases.
- Visual evidence: [[bezos-unbound-exclusive-interview-with-the-amazon-founder-on-what-he-plans-to-conquer-next]] includes an inspected Amazon Go storefront photo at Amazon's Seattle headquarters.
- Checkout removal: [[everything-you-need-to-know-about-what-amazon-is-doing-in-financial-services]] describes Just Walk Out as computer vision, sensor fusion, and machine learning accessed through the Amazon app; its retained image labels the store cashierless and emphasizes “no lines, no checkout.”

## Qualifications
Both sources are 2018 snapshots and do not evaluate later rollout scale, economics, labor effects, accessibility, privacy implications, false detections, or technology accuracy. The CB Insights report's expectation of six more stores and possible Whole Foods integration was a forward-looking plan, not a demonstrated outcome.

## What Changed
- Added checkout removal as a financial-interface design, supported by a second inspected store image.
- Preserved planned expansion and Whole Foods integration as historical forecasts rather than outcomes.
