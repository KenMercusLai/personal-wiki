## Overview
[[KylieJenner]] is a celebrity entrepreneur represented as the founder, sole owner, product face, and principal distribution channel of [[KylieCosmetics]].

## Current Profile
By 2018, Jenner had converted television exposure, family fame, and an unusually large social following into a direct-to-consumer cosmetics business. She financed the first lip-kit run with modeling income, shaped products and launches, and promoted them directly to followers, while [[KrisJenner]], Seed Beauty, and [[Shopify]] supplied management, production, fulfillment, and storefront infrastructure. Her role was concentrated around taste, attention, ownership, and launch demand rather than a large internal operating organization.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded and owned 100% of Kylie Cosmetics at the time of the article.
- Used her personal social accounts as the company's dominant marketing and launch channel.
- Invested about $250,000 of modeling income in the first 15,000 lip kits.
- Combined product tastemaking with external manufacturing, fulfillment, and commerce infrastructure.
- Benefited from a pre-existing family television and social-media system.
- Faced business concentration risk because company demand was tied closely to her personal relevance.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on a single 2018 Forbes article and its private-company estimates. The article's "self-made" label obscures the unusual inherited advantages of family fame, media access, cross-promotion, management, and capital. It also does not establish whether Jenner personally performed every strategic function attributed to her or whether the model remained durable after publication.
