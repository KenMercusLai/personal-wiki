## Overview
[[PhilippeBourgau]] is a software-engineering blogger whose source note proposes using ordinary TODO comments as a lightweight way to track technical debt in source code.

## Current Profile
In the ingested article, Bourgau writes from practitioner experience with a team that replaced heavier `@TechnicalDebt` annotations after they proved too ceremonious and stale. His guidance combines tool pragmatism with team-process advice: use markers that existing tools already surface, agree on the convention with colleagues, document it, and revisit the debt regularly.

## Key Characteristics
- Practitioner author focused on lightweight software-engineering workflow.
- Advocates replacing heavy debt annotations with ordinary TODO comments when the team can sustain the practice.
- Treats technical-debt tracking as both a tooling and team-agreement problem.
- Uses concrete tool examples such as CodeClimate, SonarQube, IntelliJ, Rails, and grep.
