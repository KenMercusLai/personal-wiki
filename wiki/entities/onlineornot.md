Source: [2023: Focusing on a single product pays off]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off.md" >}})

OnlineOrNot is the product side of ROZENMD in this source. The article describes it as the main reason single-product focus paid off in 2023: learning from operating the business compounds, and fixes that improve adoption can be refined into further gains.

The source also shows the value of persistence around one product. People who rejected the pre-launch OnlineOrNot pitch later returned and became customers, some early free-tier users converted to paid plans, and bugs that took 15 months to affect people became worth fixing because the product had stayed in use long enough.

The article notes that a planned February 2024 anniversary post would celebrate OnlineOrNot's third year since its public launch on Twitter, and that its pre-launch version had been only a single input form that sent email alerts.
