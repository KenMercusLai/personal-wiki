## Overview
[[DonMacAskill]] is presented as [[SmugMug]]'s chief executive and "chief geek," leading a self-funded family photo-sharing company with a strong customer and culture orientation.

## Current Profile
In the source, Don is a young programming prodigy and repeat startup worker who started what became SmugMug in 2002, then brought in his father [[ChrisMacAskill]] and other relatives. He frames the company's culture as deliberately family-like rather than merely dorm-like, while also acknowledging early worries that non-family employees might feel alienated by the family core. His role combines technical identity, executive control, and stewardship of the company's customer-facing personality.

## Key Characteristics
- Chief executive and "chief geek" of [[SmugMug]].
- Started the venture that became SmugMug in 2002.
- Advocated a family-atmosphere culture as part of company identity.
- Wanted to retain control by rejecting outside investment or acquisition.
- Recognized the risk that a family-dense company could alienate new hires.

## Evidence
- Founder role: [[a-focused-family-business-los-angeles-times]] says Chris became the first to join Don in 2002 on the startup that became SmugMug.
- Executive role: [[a-focused-family-business-los-angeles-times]] identifies Don as the 30-year-old chief executive and "chief geek."
- Culture framing: [[a-focused-family-business-los-angeles-times]] quotes Don contrasting SmugMug's family atmosphere with Google's dorm atmosphere.
- Control posture: [[a-focused-family-business-los-angeles-times]] says the MacAskills, including Don's leadership, turned down investment and purchase offers.
- Family-risk awareness: [[a-focused-family-business-los-angeles-times]] quotes Don's concern that new hires might feel alienated or steamrolled by the family.
