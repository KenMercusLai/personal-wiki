## Overview
[[PattyMcCord]] is presented as a former Netflix chief talent officer and a culture and leadership adviser who recommends treating employees as capable contributors and employer fit as revisable.

## Current Profile
In [[VivianGiang]]'s article, McCord supplies the central argument for planned job mobility. She recommends that young people consider moving every three to four years because entering a new company creates a fresh learning curve, while employers should assume people arrive wanting to contribute and then assess whether the fit works. The profile therefore links talent philosophy, organizational fit, and individual skill development without establishing a universally optimal tenure.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Netflix chief talent officer in the source's account.
- Advises companies and entrepreneurs on culture and leadership.
- Treats employees as capable contributors rather than presumptively disloyal labor.
- Recommends periodic employer changes as a way to encounter steeper learning curves.

- Mobility heuristic: [[vivian-giang-you-should-plan-on-switching-jobs-every-three-years-for-the-rest-of-your-life]] attributes the three-to-four-year recommendation and learning-curve rationale to McCord.

## Qualifications
The source is a short journalistic article, not a complete account of McCord's work or Netflix's employment practices. It does not provide comparative retention, learning, pay, or performance evidence for the recommended cadence, and advice aimed at young workers may not transfer across occupations or constraints.
