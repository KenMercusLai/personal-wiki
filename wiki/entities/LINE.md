## Overview
[[LINE]] appears as a Japanese messaging app that lets brands advertise through customized stickers.

## Current Profile
The source presents LINE as the strongest branded-sticker advertising example. Companies can purchase the ability to publish customized stickers in LINE's sticker store; the sticker-store presence advertises the brand, and users who purchase the branded sticker can subsequently receive direct messages from the company.

## Key Characteristics
- Messaging app example of branded sticker advertising.
- Turns expressive chat assets into brand inventory.
- Connects sticker acquisition to later direct messaging from the brand.

## Evidence
- Sticker-store inventory: [[advertising-models-in-mobile-messaging-apps-mobile-dev-memo]] says companies can publish customized stickers in LINE's sticker store.
- Advertising presence: [[advertising-models-in-mobile-messaging-apps-mobile-dev-memo]] says stickers advertise the brand even before purchase because they remain visible in the store.
- Direct messaging: [[advertising-models-in-mobile-messaging-apps-mobile-dev-memo]] says companies can directly message users who purchase the branded sticker.
