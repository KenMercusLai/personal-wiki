## Overview
[[SoftwareBrothers]] is the software-development agency/source publisher behind the article on successful startup MVP examples.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Software Brothers appears as a service-oriented startup software source that uses well-known company origin stories to explain [[MinimumViableProduct]] strategy. The article's examples also act as marketing context for the agency's MVP-building services, so its claims are best read as practitioner guidance rather than independent startup-history research.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes practitioner-oriented startup and web-product advice.
- Frames MVPs as a way for startups to reach market quickly with only the core validating feature or behavior.
- Uses famous company stories to illustrate manual, landing-page, video, platform, and single-feature validation patterns.
- Positions the agency as able to help startups build MVPs.
