## Overview
[[Vane]] is [[AstroVela]]'s announced product family for multimodal AI, Physical AI, and related infrastructure.

## Current Profile
The founding source divides Vane into Vane Core, [[VaneData]], Vane RL, and Vane Agent. The names imply core, data, reinforcement-learning, and agent responsibilities, but the announcement supplies no contracts, interfaces, data flow, deployment topology, or boundary definitions among them. Only Vane Data receives a stated release plan in this source.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the umbrella name for AstroVela's infrastructure products.
- Includes four named components: Core, Data, RL, and Agent.
- Targets a company strategy centered on multimodal AI and Physical AI.
- Remains an announced architecture rather than a technically specified integrated system in the available source.

## Evidence
- Product identity: [[chong-xin-chu-fa-xiang-wei-zhi-hang-xing]] explicitly names the product Vane.
- Component list: [[chong-xin-chu-fa-xiang-wei-zhi-hang-xing]] lists Vane Core, Vane Data, Vane RL, and Vane Agent as the overall architecture.
- Company context: [[chong-xin-chu-fa-xiang-wei-zhi-hang-xing]] places the product under [[AstroVela]] and its multimodal and Physical AI bet.
- Roadmap: [[chong-xin-chu-fa-xiang-wei-zhi-hang-xing]] says Vane Data was planned for open source in July 2026.

## Qualifications
The source provides names and strategic positioning, not a product specification. Component responsibilities beyond their labels, interoperability, maturity, licensing, availability, benchmarks, security, and production adoption are unknown. A planned release date is not evidence of a completed release.
