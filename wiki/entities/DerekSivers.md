## Overview
[[DerekSivers]] is an entrepreneur and writer cited by Hawstein for decision filtering, business creativity, and openness to meeting people.

## Current Profile
The source uses Derek Sivers as an intellectual influence on [[Hawstein]]'s independent-creator philosophy. Sivers appears through three ideas: choosing only product ideas that produce a strong yes, treating business as a creative medium, and making oneself reachable to interesting people.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies a decision filter for selecting among product ideas.
- Influences the author's view that business can be practiced creatively.
- Models a personal website style that invites email conversation.
- Serves as part of the broader independent-creator reference network.

## Evidence
- Decision filter: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] invokes Sivers's book title "Hell Yeah or No" to describe choosing only highly compelling product ideas.
- Business creativity: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] says reading Sivers strengthened the author's sense that business is an art.
- Reachability: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] cites Sivers's website as a model for inviting strangers to say hello by email.

## Qualifications
The source does not analyze Sivers's work in depth; it uses him as a motivating reference for Hawstein's own practice.
