## Overview
The [[ZigSoftwareFoundation]] is presented as the institutional steward of Zig and the former recipient of Bun's recurring financial and technical-community engagement.

## Current Profile
According to [[AndrewKelley]], ZSF initially benefited from Bun's public credit and a donation worth US$60,000 per year. The relationship deteriorated as Bun's engineering practices and priorities diverged from the foundation's longer-term goals. Kelley says ZSF came to view Bun as a reputational liability, anticipated that acquisition would end the donation, and welcomed the Rust rewrite as a clean separation.

## Key Characteristics
- Institutional steward associated with the Zig project.
- Former recipient of a substantial recurring Bun donation.
- Held recurring meetings with Bun before the relationship ended.
- Attempted to influence Bun toward better engineering practices.
- Prioritized long-term language and community health over Bun's startup priorities.

## Evidence
- Financial relationship: [[my-thoughts-on-the-bun-rust-rewrite]] reports Bun's annualized US$60,000 donation and its later silent end.
- Technical relationship: [[my-thoughts-on-the-bun-rust-rewrite]] says ZSF reviewed Bun's code and offered guidance.
- Separation: [[my-thoughts-on-the-bun-rust-rewrite]] describes missed meetings, acquisition, donation loss, and relief at the Rust rewrite.

## Qualifications
This institutional profile relies entirely on Kelley's account and includes no ZSF records, donation statements, meeting notes, Bun response, or independent chronology. The reported motives and collective feelings of ZSF are therefore attributed claims.
