## Overview
[[RohanRajiv]] is represented in this wiki as the author of an A Learning a Day reflection on graduate-school uncertainty and the emotional usefulness of the line "all it takes is for one to work out."

## Current Profile
The source presents Rajiv through a first-person career and education story rather than a broad biography. He describes an earlier failed graduate-school application round, a second attempt shaped by doubts about GPA, GMAT, employer brand, and competition, and the eventual life-changing result of one successful acceptance. His contribution to the wiki is a compact mindset for uncertain selection processes: when the goal is fit, one right yes can outweigh many noes.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes reflective personal-growth material through [[ALearningADay]].
- Uses personal experience to make a general point about high-stakes searches.
- Frames graduate-school admissions as emotionally difficult when past rejection and imperfect credentials make the process feel personal.
- Emphasizes the value of a repeated grounding phrase during uncertainty.
- Connects the same logic to jobs, homes, relationships, and university applications.
