## Overview
[[EliudKipchoge]] is a Kenyan marathon runner who, in the source's September 2025 dataset, combines exceptional repeat performance with two official men's world records.

## Current Profile
Kipchoge has the greatest number of results at both elite thresholds analyzed: 18 marathons below 2:08 and 14 at or below 2:05. He set official records of 2:01:39 and 2:01:09, becoming one of three athletes since 1985 to improve the record twice.

His first record cut 1 minute 18 seconds from Dennis Kimetto's 2:02:57, the largest single improvement in the period analyzed. The source therefore presents him not only as a record setter but as the most consistently recurrent athlete among its fastest results.

## Key Characteristics
- Leads the dataset with 18 performances below 2:08.
- Leads the stricter dataset with 14 performances at or below 2:05.
- Set the official men's marathon world record twice.
- Lowered the record to 2:01:39 and later to 2:01:09.
- Produced the largest single record improvement in the source's 1985–2025 comparison, by 1 minute 18 seconds.
