## Overview
[[MikeDilworth]] is represented in the wiki as an agile and DevOps transformation leader quoted by [[IrmaKornilova]].

## Current Profile
Dilworth supplies the source's defining organizational claim: DevOps works only when the company treats it as a shared culture rather than assigning it to a specialist role or limiting it to development and operations departments.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames [[DevOpsCulture]] as company-wide participation.
- Rejects DevOps as a narrowly assigned role.
- Connects leadership backing with involvement from all product stakeholders.

## Evidence
- Company-wide framing: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] quotes Dilworth saying that the whole company must do DevOps for it to work.
- Stakeholder scope: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] places his statement beside the need for senior backing and broader product participation.

## Qualifications
The profile is limited to a short quotation and role description in one 2017 source. It does not establish Dilworth's complete views, employment history, or current position.
