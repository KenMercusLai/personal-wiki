## Overview
[[TimWestergren]] is a musician and founder represented through his account of building [[Pandora]] from Savage Beast Technologies, surviving its early cash crisis, and later moving from CEO to chief strategy officer.

## Current Profile
The source presents Westergren as both product specialist and stage-changing founder. He began around the [[MusicGenomeProject]], accumulated CEO, marketing, sales, business-development, product, and other responsibilities, and later concluded that the company would benefit from complementary leadership by [[JoeKennedy]]. His crisis account emphasizes visible founder sacrifice, candid information, careful hiring, and belief in the product, but it is his own retrospective and does not include employee accounts of prolonged unpaid work.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded Savage Beast Technologies in 1999 to address music discovery through B2B technology.
- Moved from music-domain work into multiple operating and executive responsibilities.
- Kept the company focused on software while relying on partners for hardware capability.
- Describes leading through financial crisis by sacrificing first, communicating candidly, and preserving camaraderie.
- Accepted a move from CEO to chief strategy officer as the company and his own fit changed.
- Defines entrepreneurial reward partly through the livelihoods and pride created for employees.

## Evidence
- Origin and role breadth: [[founder-of-pandora-on-lessons-from-near-dot-com-bust-to-billion-dollar-ipo-first-round-review]] says Westergren founded Savage Beast Technologies and moved from Music Genome work into CEO, CMO, and several other duties.
- Strategic focus: [[founder-of-pandora-on-lessons-from-near-dot-com-bust-to-billion-dollar-ipo-first-round-review]] describes his decision to leave kiosk hardware to IBM and keep Pandora focused on software.
- Crisis conduct: [[founder-of-pandora-on-lessons-from-near-dot-com-bust-to-billion-dollar-ipo-first-round-review]] reports early salary deferral, employee payments on personal credit cards, transparency, and careful team selection.
- Role transition: [[founder-of-pandora-on-lessons-from-near-dot-com-bust-to-billion-dollar-ipo-first-round-review]] says he became chief strategy officer after Kennedy joined as CEO and regarded their partnership as symbiotic.
- Entrepreneurial reward: [[founder-of-pandora-on-lessons-from-near-dot-com-bust-to-billion-dollar-ipo-first-round-review]] says seeing more than 700 people together at an all-hands made the company's livelihood creation especially satisfying.

## Qualifications
The profile is based on one founder-centered conference retrospective relayed by an investor publication. The source does not independently verify its operational figures or include employee, customer, investor, IBM, or Best Buy perspectives. Personal debt and extended unpaid labor may signal commitment, but they also create financial, power, and fairness risks that should not be generalized into a leadership prescription.
