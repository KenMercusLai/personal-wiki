## Overview
[[GregBrockman]] is represented through his January 2017 account of co-founding [[OpenAI]], recruiting its early team, and redefining his CTO role around whichever organizational or engineering constraint most limited research progress.

## Current Profile
Brockman's account combines institution building with hands-on systems work. He and [[SamAltman]] rallied the founding team, while he and [[IlyaSutskever]] shaped strategy, culture, and operating practices around equal respect for research and engineering. Before his engineering skills became the main constraint, Brockman organized people, recruiting, interviews, office infrastructure, servers, accounts, and the early Kubernetes cluster so researchers could focus.

When [[OpenAIGym]]'s code quality became a bottleneck, Brockman and Sutskever exchanged responsibilities and Brockman concentrated on shipping the library. He later applied the same engineering stance to [[OpenAIUniverse]], including programmatic VNC control and rapid fixes for research blockers. The durable profile is not simply “CTO as coder”: it is a technical founder reallocating attention among recruiting, operations, software, and organization design according to the current constraint.

## Key Characteristics
- Helped turn the OpenAI idea into a founding team and an operating organization.
- Treated research and engineering as complementary, equally valued sources of progress.
- Used operational support to protect research leaders' attention before engineering became the dominant bottleneck.
- Shifted into concentrated hands-on work to accelerate Gym and Universe research infrastructure.
- Framed software abstractions as dependent on close learning from researchers' actual workflows.
- Preferred long uninterrupted blocks for coding and restricted meeting placement while in implementation mode.

## Evidence
- Founding and recruiting: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] says Brockman helped define the organization and led much of the August-to-November recruiting effort while Sutskever remained at Google.
- Operational support: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] describes organizing the first team, interviews, work allocation, office supplies, servers, accounts, and cluster maintenance.
- Constraint-driven role shift: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] says Brockman and Sutskever swapped responsibilities once Gym engineering slowed research iteration.
- Research engineering: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] describes building Gym, learning which abstractions affected research workflows, developing Universe's VNC-based system, and fixing blockers during experiments.
- Focus practice: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] says a meeting could consume a half-day coding block and describes concentrating meetings into limited windows.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from one self-authored retrospective published in January 2017. It does not independently verify Brockman's causal role, the perspectives of other founders or recruits, the sustainability of his wake-to-sleep coding pattern, or later changes in OpenAI and his responsibilities. The source reports perceived acceleration but no controlled delivery, research, quality, or wellbeing outcomes.

## What Changed
- Created a source-bounded profile of Brockman as both founding organizer and hands-on research engineer.
- Distinguished his constraint-driven CTO model from the narrower claim that CTO work should always be coding.
