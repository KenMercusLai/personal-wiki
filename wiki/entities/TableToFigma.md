## Overview
[[TableToFigma]] is a Figma plugin by Thinkbuff and Eleven that applies tabular data to design files so users can batch-generate or update design assets.

## Current Profile
The source positions [[TableToFigma]] as Zhang Xiaoji's strongest small-product case. It began from a concrete workplace request to generate e-commerce hero images from Notion data, then differentiated from Google Sheets Sync by supporting more data sources and properties. Later versions added demand-led table generation, Google Sheets, CSV/XLSX, Airtable, color, spacing, and boolean-related binding support.

## Key Characteristics
- Solves a repetitive design-production problem by binding structured data to Figma layers.
- Originated from a specific internal request rather than a speculative market idea.
- Differentiates from a popular Google Sheets Sync competitor through broader data-source and property support.
- Gained stronger traction after demand-led feature changes and late-2023 monetization.
- Demonstrates the cost of delayed commercial design after accumulating thousands of free users.

## Evidence
- Repetitive production: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] describes designers manually changing product name, image, category, price, and promotion fields for many e-commerce items.
- Concrete origin: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] says a coworker asked whether a program could batch-create product-page images from Notion data.
- Differentiation: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] compares the product with Google Sheets Sync and frames multi-source support plus richer property binding as the opportunity.
- Traction: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] includes screenshots showing nearly five thousand users, visible late-2023 growth, and recent order revenue.
- Monetization lesson: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] argues that delaying paid features by several months likely wasted paying-customer conversions.

## Qualifications
The revenue and usage screenshots are period snapshots, not audited revenue history. The source also says the product still needed optimization, so traction did not imply product completeness.
