## Overview
[[PennyArcade]] is represented in this wiki as the gaming webcomic that introduced the named [[GreaterInternetFuckwadTheory]] and its chalkboard equation in 2004.

## Current Profile
The source centers one Penny Arcade installment, “Green Blackboards (And Other Anomalies),” published on March 19, 2004. By reducing a complaint about *Unreal Tournament 2004* behavior to a visual formula joining an ordinary person, anonymity, and an audience, the comic created a reusable piece of internet vocabulary that spread far beyond its immediate gaming context.

## Key Characteristics
- Gaming webcomic associated with commentary on online player behavior.
- Originator of the named theory and its memorable visual equation.
- Source of an image repeatedly reposted, discussed, and remixed across internet culture.
- Cultural origin rather than empirical authority for claims about anonymous behavior.

## Qualifications
This profile is restricted to Penny Arcade's role in originating one meme. It does not cover the webcomic's creators, publication history, business, broader work, controversies, or influence. The comic made a culturally durable observation but did not test the equation as a behavioral theory.
