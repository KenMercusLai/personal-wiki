## Overview
[[MathildeCollin]] is Front's founder/CEO in the wiki's sources, cited both for Front's retention-led growth evidence and for a founder-operator advice essay about building [[Front]].

## Current Profile
The Product Habits source uses Collin's published Front Series A deck as evidence that [[Front]] grew by retaining, expanding, and upselling existing accounts rather than relying only on new customer acquisition. Her own advice essay adds an operating profile: while building Front, she learned to trust customer and growth evidence, filter advice and benchmarks, preserve hiring standards, distinguish reversible from irreversible decisions, repeat mission, and transmit founder context to executives before giving them autonomy.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder/CEO context for Front.
- Associated with a public fundraising deck cited as retention evidence.
- Connected to a SaaS case where account expansion supports growth.
- Treats customer evidence and growth as stronger strategy signals than outside expert opinion.
- Frames advice, benchmarks, hiring, firing, decision reversibility, mission repetition, and executive onboarding as founder judgment problems.
