## Overview
[[MathildeCollin]] is Front's founder/CEO in the wiki's sources, represented through Front's retention-led growth evidence, her firsthand Series A process, and a founder-operator advice essay about building [[Front]].

## Current Profile
Collin's Series A retrospective presents her as a founder who prepared investor relationships before fundraising, waited for $100,000 in MRR, low churn, and a clear roadmap, and then concentrated the raise into a ten-day sprint because it consumed most of her attention. The deck's capital efficiency and 12-month account expansion landed well, while investor feedback exposed missing acquisition, projection, engagement, and buyer evidence. The Product Habits source later uses that deck to interpret [[Front]] through retention and upsell. Her advice essay broadens the operating profile: trust customer and growth evidence, filter advice and benchmarks, preserve hiring standards, distinguish reversible from irreversible decisions, repeat mission, and transmit founder context before giving executives autonomy.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder/CEO context for Front.
- Associated with a public fundraising deck cited as retention evidence.
- Connected to a SaaS case where account expansion supports growth.
- Treats fundraising as a concentrated, high-attention operating phase entered after explicit business-readiness signals.
- Treats customer evidence and growth as stronger strategy signals than outside expert opinion.
- Frames advice, benchmarks, hiring, firing, decision reversibility, mission repetition, and executive onboarding as founder judgment problems.

## Qualifications

The fundraising source omits deal terms, valuation, cohort definitions, and investor perspectives.
