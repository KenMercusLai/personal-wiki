## Overview
[[Zendesk]] appears in the sources as both a SaaS attribution case and a multi-product support-software example.

## Current Profile
One source says Zendesk selected a larger attribution vendor, integrated it with its data warehouse, and tracked paid display, email, paid social, organic social, SEO, SEM, and content touches. That work showed long prospect journeys and supported continued investment in display and content even when those channels were not the final clicked touch. The newer source uses Zendesk's support tool, live chat, knowledge base, call-center tool, and related products to illustrate [[MultiProductContentStrategy]]: because several products address a similar support-team audience, each product should receive its own top-, middle-, and bottom-of-funnel content coverage.

## Key Characteristics
- SaaS case for multi-touch attribution across paid, organic, email, search, and content.
- Shows that long nurture cycles can make last-touch attribution undercount earlier influence.
- Used attribution evidence to keep investing in display and content marketing.
- Connected content consumption to purchase likelihood, sales velocity, and deal size.
- Illustrates product-based content segmentation for several tools serving a similar support-team audience.
- Treats each product as needing full-funnel content rather than relying on portfolio-level coverage.

## Evidence
- Vendor and integration: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says Zendesk selected a larger attribution vendor and integrated it into the data warehouse.
- Long nurture cycle: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says prospects sometimes interacted with Zendesk ten, twenty, or thirty times before becoming leads.
- Channel insight: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says attribution prevented underestimating display's influence during nurture.
- Content value: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says Zendesk tracked which content made people more likely to buy, sped sales, or led to larger deals.
- Product portfolio: [[content-marketing-strategy-for-multi-product-companies]] lists support, live chat, knowledge-base, and call-center products that can be adopted separately or together.
- Content segmentation: [[content-marketing-strategy-for-multi-product-companies]] recommends a complete funnel for each Zendesk product because the target team remains broadly similar.

## Qualifications
The attribution source is an interview-based case summary and does not provide Zendesk's detailed model, vendor name, controlled experiment design, or full revenue data. The content-strategy source is a 2018 outside practitioner example; it does not establish that Zendesk actually used the proposed product matrix or provide results from doing so.

## What Changed
- Added Zendesk's multi-product support suite as the product-segmented side of a content-planning framework.
