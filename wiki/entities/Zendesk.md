## Overview
[[Zendesk]] appears as a SaaS attribution case where tracking many customer touches changed how the company valued display advertising and content marketing.

## Current Profile
The source says Zendesk selected a larger attribution vendor, integrated it with its data warehouse, and tracked paid display, email, paid social, organic social, SEO, SEM, and content touches. That work showed that prospects often interacted with Zendesk ten, twenty, or thirty times before becoming a lead. The attribution evidence supported continued investment in display and content because those channels influenced nurture, sales velocity, deal size, and revenue growth even when they were not the final clicked touch.

## Key Characteristics
- SaaS case for multi-touch attribution across paid, organic, email, search, and content.
- Shows that long nurture cycles can make last-touch attribution undercount earlier influence.
- Used attribution evidence to keep investing in display and content marketing.
- Connected content consumption to purchase likelihood, sales velocity, and deal size.

- Long nurture cycle: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says prospects sometimes interacted with Zendesk ten, twenty, or thirty times before becoming leads.
- Channel insight: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says attribution prevented underestimating display's influence during nurture.
- Content value: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says Zendesk tracked which content made people more likely to buy, sped sales, or led to larger deals.
