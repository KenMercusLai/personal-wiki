## Overview
[[Earendil]] is an AI-software organization that publishes introductory material about agent harnesses and presents [[PiAgentHarness]] and Lefos as its harness work.

## Current Profile
In the supplied article, Earendil advocates user-owned, locally run, provider-neutral agent software as a way to preserve model choice, customization, and custody of machine correspondence. It frames open software and protocols as instruments of human agency, but the source is Earendil's own product publication and does not independently evaluate its products or philosophy.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes explanatory material about agent harness architecture.
- Presents Pi as a minimal, extensible, open-source local harness.
- Presents Lefos as an email-oriented harness.
- Connects provider neutrality and local session custody to human agency.

## Evidence
- Product and philosophy: [[what-is-a-harness]] attributes Pi and Lefos to Earendil and argues for neutral open-source harnesses.
- Interface examples: [[what-is-a-harness]] contrasts Pi's terminal use with Lefos's email interaction and OpenClaw's messaging interfaces.
- Agency position: [[what-is-a-harness]] says model choice, local operation, customization, and retained sessions shift leverage toward users.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one first-party introductory article. It provides no corporate history, governance detail, product benchmark, security audit, portability test, adoption measure, or independent confirmation of the claimed extension ecosystem.
