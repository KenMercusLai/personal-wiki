## Overview
[[MadalynParker]] is represented here as an [[Olark]] employee whose direct 2017 message about taking sick leave for mental health became a widely shared example of workplace disclosure and managerial support.

## Current Profile
Parker told an internal out-of-office list that she was taking two days to focus on her mental health and hoped to return refreshed. She then asked permission to share [[BenCongleton]]'s supportive reply publicly. The source treats her candor as the initiating act in a larger discussion about whether employees can name mental-health needs without disguise or stigma.

## Key Characteristics
- Named mental health explicitly as the reason for taking sick leave.
- Communicated a bounded absence and an expected return to colleagues.
- Asked before publishing Congleton's internal response.
- Became the employee-side example in a viral discussion of mental health at work.
