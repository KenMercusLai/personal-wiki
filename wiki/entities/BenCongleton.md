## Overview
[[BenCongleton]] is represented here as [[Olark]]'s CEO and the author of a 2017 article advocating direct workplace support for mental health.

## Current Profile
Congleton describes replying supportively when [[MadalynParker]] told colleagues she was taking sick leave for her mental health. After Parker shared the reply publicly, he interpreted the unusually positive response as evidence that humane treatment was still seen as exceptional. His article frames employee health, empowerment, and motivation as executive responsibilities tied to organizational impact.
