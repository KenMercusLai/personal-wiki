## Overview
[[JamesLewis]] is a coauthor of the Legacy Mimic pattern article on Martin Fowler's site.

## Current Profile
In this wiki, Lewis is currently represented by the Legacy Mimic article's modernization guidance. The source treats legacy migration as a pattern and sequencing problem where temporary compatibility structures let teams extract capabilities without forcing a single risky replacement event.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of a legacy-displacement pattern article.
- Associated with incremental migration patterns and transitional architecture.
- Helps frame legacy compatibility as an explicit architectural concern rather than an accidental workaround.
