## Overview
[[CREAO]] is the AI agent platform company used as the central case study in the translated article about AI-first engineering.

## Current Profile
Within the source, CREAO is presented as a 25-person company with 10 engineers that rebuilt its product architecture and engineering workflow around AI agents. The case study claims that the company used agents to write production code, review pull requests, run tests, deploy, monitor, triage production issues, and shorten feature iteration loops.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates as an AI agent platform company in the source.
- Reorganized product architecture and engineering workflow around AI-first assumptions.
- Uses a highly automated delivery and monitoring pipeline for frequent production deployments.
- Treats human engineers as strategic reviewers, architects, investigators, and operators rather than line-by-line implementers.

- Company context: [[wei-shen-me-ni-de-ai-you-xian-zhan-lue-ke-neng-da-cuo-te-cuo]] describes CREAO as an AI agent platform with 25 employees and 10 engineers.
- Workflow redesign: [[wei-shen-me-ni-de-ai-you-xian-zhan-lue-ke-neng-da-cuo-te-cuo]] says the team rebuilt planning, implementation, testing, deployment, and organization around AI.
