## Overview
[[Spotify]] is a music-streaming service used in the source as an example of widgets and freemium access driving acquisition.

## Current Profile
The article presents Spotify's widgets as a distribution surface for artists and fans: songs and playlists can be shared on web pages or social platforms, but full listening routes back to Spotify accounts. It also treats Spotify's free ad-supported tier as a major growth mechanism compared with subscription-only music services.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses shareable widgets for songs and playlists.
- Routes preview attention back to Spotify account creation or app usage.
- Uses a free ad-supported tier alongside paid subscriptions.
