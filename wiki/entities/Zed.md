## Overview
[[Zed]] is represented here as the organization publishing [[ConradIrwin]]'s 2025 essay on the limits of LLM-led software engineering.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Zed's stated position is that people and agents can collaborate to build software, but current LLMs should remain tools under human direction because they do not reliably maintain the requirement and implementation models needed for non-trivial iteration.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports human-agent collaboration in software development.
- Distinguishes capable code generation from ownership of the wider engineering loop.
- Places current human engineers in the decision-making and verification role.

## Evidence
- Collaboration stance: [[why-llms-cant-really-build-software]] states Zed's belief in people and agents building software together.
- Control boundary: [[why-llms-cant-really-build-software]] says the human remains in the driver's seat and the LLM is a tool.
- Rationale: [[why-llms-cant-really-build-software]] ties that boundary to unstable context and mental-model maintenance.

## Qualifications
This page describes only the position stated in one company-published essay. It does not establish Zed's complete product strategy, implementation, organizational practice, or current policy.
