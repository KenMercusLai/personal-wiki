## Overview
[[Xnapper]] is a screenshot-formatting application created by [[TonyDinh]].

## Current Profile
Dinh presents Xnapper as his third successful product, discovered through rapid building and public audience response. He reports about $6,000 in monthly revenue by September 2023 and uses its public desktop and iOS demonstrations to illustrate how build-in-public posts can combine product validation, content, and distribution.

## Key Characteristics
- Narrow screenshot utility built and demonstrated rapidly.
- Product idea tested through direct audience response.
- Desktop product extended into an iOS experiment.
- Reported revenue contributor within a broader product portfolio.
- Example of public demos functioning as marketing content.

## Evidence
- Rapid validation: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] shows Dinh announcing a 24-hour screenshot-app build and requesting testers.
- Mobile response: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] retains an iOS demo post with 1,718 likes and 71,400 views.
- Revenue: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] reports roughly $6,000 per month.

## Qualifications
Social engagement indicates attention, not conversion or retention. The source gives no pricing, costs, cohort data, support load, platform split, or evidence isolating public posting from other acquisition channels.
