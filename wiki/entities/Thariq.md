## Overview
[[Thariq]] (`@trq212`) is a practitioner-author represented in the wiki through a field guide to finding uncertainty while working with [[Claude]] and [[ClaudeCode]].

## Current Profile
The available source presents Thariq as an experienced agentic coder who treats prompts, skills, plans, prototypes, references, notes, and explainers as instruments for exposing gaps between a user's intended map and the implementation territory. His central contribution is not a universal prompt recipe but an iterative workflow: disclose the user's starting knowledge, ask the model to find blind spots, externalize tacit preferences through alternatives, record deviations during implementation, and verify human understanding afterward. He illustrates the method with a Claude-edited launch video involving transcription, Remotion prototyping, timing, and color-grading learning.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames agentic coding skill as the ability to identify, communicate, and plan for different classes of unknowns.
- Uses visual HTML artifacts, prototypes, interviews, references, and decision-focused plans to make ambiguous intent inspectable.
- Lets agents continue conservatively through implementation surprises while requiring deviations to be recorded.
- Treats pitches, explainers, and quizzes as post-implementation tools for reviewer alignment and human understanding.

- Artifact practice: [[a-field-guide-to-fable-finding-your-unknowns]] recommends blind-spot passes, multiple prototypes, interviews, source-code references, decision-focused plans, and temporary implementation notes.
