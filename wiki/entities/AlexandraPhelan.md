## Overview
[[AlexandraPhelan]] is an academic author who documents a personal research and manuscript-writing workflow built around [[Zotero]], [[Obsidian]], and [[Pandoc]].

## Current Profile
In the available source, Phelan works across law and science and needs to collect, annotate, synthesize, draft, collaborate, and reformat references for different publication venues. Her contribution is a practitioner account of dividing those jobs across interoperable tools while keeping first drafts in Markdown.

## Key Characteristics
- Works across law and science, making citation-style switching an important workflow requirement.
- Prefers Markdown for stable, low-distraction drafting close to her notes.
- Uses Zotero as the long-lived source, PDF, annotation, and citation database.
- Uses Obsidian for linked synthesis, manuscript drafting, project management, and publication tracking.
- Documents a template-driven, citekey-centered workflow intended to become routine after setup.

## Evidence
- Research context: [[alexandra-phelan-an-updated-academic-workflow-zotero-and-obsidian]] says the author works across law and science and switches among APA, Bluebook, and Nature styles.
- Tool division: [[alexandra-phelan-an-updated-academic-workflow-zotero-and-obsidian]] assigns collection and citation data to Zotero and linked notes and drafting to Obsidian.
- Publication pipeline: [[alexandra-phelan-an-updated-academic-workflow-zotero-and-obsidian]] uses Pandoc, BibTeX, and CSL to export Markdown manuscripts to DOCX.
