## Overview
[[MattGiaro]] is a writer and creator represented through a self-reported minimalist Obsidian Zettelkasten workflow.

## Current Profile
Giaro describes a three-year practice in which [[Bear]] captures ideas, a short waiting period filters them, and [[Obsidian]] holds rewritten, linked permanent notes used to assemble published content. His advice consistently prioritizes low friction and output: one idea per note, at least one meaningful link, a flat vault, little metadata, and no required community plugins. The source links this system to more than 3,000 notes and a six-figure writing business, but it does not isolate the workflow's effect from Giaro's experience, audience, distribution, or other business inputs.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates separating rapid capture from slower processing and linked thinking.
- Uses a two-to-three-day delay to filter fleeting ideas before durable storage.
- Recommends rewriting ideas in one's own words and adding personal interpretation.
- Prefers one idea and at least one meaningful link per permanent note.
- Treats notes as reusable first drafts for content rather than as an archive to maximize.

## Evidence
- Workflow design: [[matt-giaro-how-to-use-obsidian-as-a-zettelkasten]] assigns capture to Bear and thinking, linking, and creation to Obsidian.
- Processing gate: [[matt-giaro-how-to-use-obsidian-as-a-zettelkasten]] says captured ideas remain in Bear for two or three days before promotion or deletion.
- Note standard: [[matt-giaro-how-to-use-obsidian-as-a-zettelkasten]] recommends one idea, own-words restatement, personal thought, a descriptive title, and at least one related-note link.
- Output orientation: [[matt-giaro-how-to-use-obsidian-as-a-zettelkasten]] says the author's articles are assembled from existing notes and presents content creation as the system's purpose.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one first-person tutorial. Its note count, duration, revenue category, and publishing claims are not independently verified, and the source supplies no comparison group, outcome measures, failed cases, or attribution analysis. The flat, Apple-centered, creator-oriented workflow may not transfer to collaborative, regulated, academic, archival, or heavily structured work.
