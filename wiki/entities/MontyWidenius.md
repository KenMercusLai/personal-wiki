## Overview
[[MontyWidenius|Michael “Monty” Widenius]] is a Finnish programmer and founder presented as the principal early author of [[MySQL]], a cofounder and CTO of [[MySQLAB]], the founder of [[MariaDB]], and a founding partner of [[OpenOcean]].

## Current Profile
The source portrays Widenius as a craft-led technical founder whose authority came from sustained hands-on programming rather than fundraising, networking, or managerial ceremony. He reports beginning serious programming as a teenager, building database precursors from 1981, incorporating customer feedback over many years, and continuing to code for most of his time even as MySQL AB grew to hundreds of employees. His later MariaDB and OpenOcean work is presented as an extension of the same commitment to developers and open-source production.

## Key Characteristics
- Lifelong, highly absorbing engagement with programming and repeated personal projects.
- Long-horizon database development grounded in practical customer feedback.
- Preference for durable, performant code that can be extended without wholesale rewriting.
- Technical leadership through demonstrated ability rather than meeting-heavy management.
- Strong commitment to open-source community participation alongside commercial sustainability.

## Evidence
- Programming craft: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] reports that Widenius bought his first computer in 1978, left university at 19, and described hours of coding as passing like minutes.
- Database work: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] says he developed MySQL precursors from 1981 and integrated customer feedback before the 1996 release.
- Code philosophy: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] attributes to him a standard of performant code that should remain extensible without needing a rewrite.
- Technical leadership: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] says he remained primarily a coder as MySQL AB's CTO and sought self-directed employees.
- Open-source continuity: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] connects his MySQL work to MariaDB, developer lunches, and OpenOcean's developer-focused investment mission.

## Qualifications
This profile comes from an admiring OpenOcean founder story about one of the firm's own founding partners. Exceptional-talent claims, the near-solo authorship narrative, and causal claims about leadership culture are not independently evaluated. The article's 1985 open-source date is also difficult to reconcile with its later MySQL product chronology.
