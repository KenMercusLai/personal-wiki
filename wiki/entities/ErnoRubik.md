## Overview
[[ErnoRubik]] is the Hungarian architecture professor credited in the source with creating the first Rubik's Cube prototype in 1974.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Rubik appears through the origin story of the puzzle that bears his name. The tutorial says he built a wooden prototype with six colored faces to demonstrate how components could move independently while the overall object remained intact. Its inspected historical image pairs the wooden cube with patent-style drawings of its internal movable structure.

## Key Characteristics
- Hungarian architecture professor in the source's account.
- Creator of the first Rubik's Cube prototype in 1974.
- Used the prototype to explore independent motion within a coherent structure.

## Evidence
- Professional and inventor role: [[ru-he-bu-bei-gong-shi-jie-kai-mo-fang-xiao-xue-sheng-ye-neng-kan-dong]] identifies Rubik as a Hungarian architecture professor and dates the prototype to 1974.
- Teaching purpose: [[ru-he-bu-bei-gong-shi-jie-kai-mo-fang-xiao-xue-sheng-ye-neng-kan-dong]] says the object was intended to show students how parts could move without breaking the whole.
- Physical prototype: [[ru-he-bu-bei-gong-shi-jie-kai-mo-fang-xiao-xue-sheng-ye-neng-kan-dong]] includes an inspected image of a wooden cube beside structural drawings.
