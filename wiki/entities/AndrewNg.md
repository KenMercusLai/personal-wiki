## Overview
[[AndrewNg]] is presented as an AI researcher and research leader who helped launch Google Brain, led its large unlabeled-image experiment, and later served as chief scientist at Baidu Research.

## Current Profile
The Fortune feature uses Ng to connect technical research with industrial strategy. He describes deep learning as learning input-to-output mappings from data, led the Google Brain experiment that found cat- and face-responsive units in unlabeled YouTube imagery, and argues that AI could become a general-purpose technology comparable to electricity. His warning to S&P 500 leaders is strategic: companies risk repeating their late response to the internet if they postpone an AI strategy.

## Key Characteristics
- Helped launch and lead Google Brain as a deep-learning-focused research project.
- Led a large experiment in learning recurring visual structure from unlabeled YouTube images.
- Later served as chief scientist at Baidu Research in the article's 2016 account.
- Explains practical deep learning as learning data-backed mappings from inputs to outputs.
- Frames AI as a general-purpose industrial technology and an urgent corporate strategy question.
