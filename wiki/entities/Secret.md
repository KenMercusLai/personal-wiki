## Overview
[[Secret]] appears in the source as both a disappointing startup-equity example and a case tied to founder secondary liquidity.

## Current Profile
The article uses Secret to show two sides of equity asymmetry: employees can hold equity that never becomes useful wealth, while founders may still obtain liquidity through private secondary transactions after funding rounds.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a negative employee-equity example.
- Illustrates founder secondary liquidity in contrast with employee illiquidity.
- Supports the claim that founders and employees do not experience equity in the same way.
