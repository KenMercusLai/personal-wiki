## Overview
[[HoraceDediu]] is the analyst-author of the Asymco source on Apple Watch's early business scale and strategic trajectory.

## Current Profile
In the source, Dediu analyzes [[AppleWatch]] as both a financial success and a strategic descendant of the [[IPhone]]. He estimates Watch revenue and units from Apple's reported "Other" segment and public comparison points such as [[Rolex]], then interprets Series 3's LTE and silicon improvements as signs that the product is moving toward more independent wearable computing.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses public revenue disclosures and estimates to reason about Apple product scale.
- Frames product categories through technology trajectories and historical analogy.
- Treats Apple Watch as an iPhone companion whose independence may increase over time.
- Reads health and fitness as the Watch's distinct market-creation direction.

## Evidence
- Watch estimates: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] estimates roughly 15 million Watches and $4.9 billion in annual Watch revenue.
- Historical analogy: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] compares Watch dependence on iPhone to the original iPhone's old dependence on a PC or Mac for activation.
- Product interpretation: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] says the Watch now performs small-screen versions of the iPod, internet communicator, and phone roles while moving toward health.
