## Overview
[[KenWheeler]] is represented in the wiki as the author of "A Bitter Guide To Open Source" and as an open-source JavaScript practitioner associated with [[Formidable]], [[SlickCarousel]], [[WebpackDashboard]], [[Spectacle]], and [[CashJS]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Wheeler as both a successful open-source creator and a self-critical maintainer. He frames his credibility through projects people used widely, then uses his maintenance failures, burnout, and guilt around [[SlickCarousel]] to give practical advice about project design, release readiness, delegation, and boundaries.

## Key Characteristics
- Served as Director of Open Source at [[Formidable]] in the source's framing.
- Created or was associated with multiple JavaScript open-source projects cited in the article.
- Treats open source as career leverage, community contribution, and a source of personal strain.
- Emphasizes practical repository infrastructure: docs, tests, types, CI, licenses, contribution docs, and release notes.
- Advises maintainers to delegate early and protect the core API from narrow user demands.
