## Overview
[[LouGerstner]] is the former IBM leader whom [[BenThompson]] credits with reframing IBM around enterprise services, integrated solutions, and externally focused cultural change.

## Current Profile
In the source's account, Gerstner learned that customers chose [[IBM]] less because every IBM product was best than because IBM could support them globally and assume responsibility for complex outcomes. He aligned that breadth with the emerging Internet and “e-business” transition. The essay treats his strategic preparation and cultural work as necessary conditions that made a favorable external shift usable, not as a repeatable slogan.

## Key Characteristics
- Started with customer problems rather than inherited product superiority.
- Treated IBM's breadth and global presence as differentiated assets.
- Positioned IBM as an accountable integrator during the Internet transition.
- Recognized inward-looking culture as a recurring strategic danger.
- Combined prior preparation with a favorable external technology shift.

## Evidence
- Customer discovery: [[ibms-old-playbook-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] recounts customers valuing IBM's presence everywhere they operated.
- Solution framing: [[ibms-old-playbook-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] attributes to Gerstner the view that IBM's breadth could deliver outcomes rather than best-of-breed products.
- Cultural warning: [[ibms-old-playbook-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] quotes his concern that IBM's inward-looking forces would persist after his tenure.
- Preparedness: [[ibms-old-playbook-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] says IBM had a strategy and cultural change underway when the Internet opening arrived.

## Qualifications
This profile is derived from Thompson's retrospective and quotations from Gerstner and Bob Young. It does not independently compare the turnaround's causes, costs, or stakeholder outcomes, and it should not imply that the same playbook fits a later cloud market.

## What Changed
- Created Gerstner's profile around customer discovery, integration strategy, culture, and strategic preparedness.
