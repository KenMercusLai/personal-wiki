## Overview
[[JoelTillinghast]] appears in the source as the investor-author used to express the "big money thinks small" alternative to macro fixation.

## Current Profile
The source cites Tillinghast's book Big Money Thinks Small as a compact statement of the course's macro-skepticism. In Wulc's summary, Tillinghast represents a concrete company-analysis style: instead of drowning in macro information, the investor asks practical questions about why customers buy, how the company differs from competitors, and what could make the company fail.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the source's positive model for concrete, company-level investment questions.
- Contrasts with macro prediction and information overload.
- Connects value-investing attention to product, customer, competitor, and failure analysis.
