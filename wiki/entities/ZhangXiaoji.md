## Overview
[[ZhangXiaoji]] is a Chinese slash programmer and independent creator who narrates a transition from layoff anxiety toward a small portfolio of paid software tools.

## Current Profile
The source presents Zhang Xiaoji as a programmer with design experience who tried short-video creation, returned to software after that experiment failed, and then built or co-built [[TableToFigma]], [[FitCurve]], and [[GlimmerCard]]. His current profile is less "solo coder" than small-product operator: he combines product discovery, design, development coordination, monetization, community feedback, public sharing, and overseas distribution experiments.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses personal layoffs and career anxiety as a trigger for independent-product experimentation.
- Combines programming and design sensibility to identify tool opportunities around Figma plugins and text-card layouts.
- Prefers validated small products that can ship quickly, cost little to maintain, and generate recurring or passive income.
- Learns through visible experiments, including failed short-video work, Midjourney community translation, affiliate promotion, and software launches.
- Treats marketing, traffic, brand IP, and public product storytelling as core creator capabilities.
