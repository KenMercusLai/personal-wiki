## Overview
[[Turkopticon]] is a worker-oriented reputation tool that lets Amazon Mechanical Turk workers review requesters and available jobs, countering some of the marketplace's native information asymmetry.

## Current Profile
The TechRepublic account positions Turkopticon as infrastructure created around [[AmazonMechanicalTurk]] rather than by it. AMT requesters could rate workers, reject completed work, and operate behind pseudonyms, while workers lacked a native reciprocal rating channel. Turkopticon gave workers a way to share requester experience before accepting work and therefore made hidden labor conditions somewhat more legible.

The tool was also part of a wider ecology of forums and organizing efforts that supplied practical guidance, social support, and collective visibility. It did not change Amazon's formal authority over qualification, payment, rejection, or support, so its role was informational and coordinative rather than equivalent to platform governance or labor protection.

## Key Characteristics
- Enables workers to review and rate requesters or available AMT jobs.
- Responds to asymmetric platform reputation, where requesters rate workers without native reciprocity.
- Helps workers assess payment and requester risk before accepting a task.
- Operates outside Amazon's formal support and adjudication mechanisms.
- Forms part of a broader worker-built support and organizing ecosystem.

## Qualifications
The source mentions Turkopticon briefly and does not measure adoption, rating quality, requester behavior change, worker earnings, dispute outcomes, manipulation risk, or current product status. A reputation layer can reduce some information gaps without correcting unilateral rejection, low prices, weak platform support, legal classification, or bargaining power.
