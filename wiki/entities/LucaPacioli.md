## Overview
[[LucaPacioli]] is the historical figure the accounting primer identifies as publishing the first complete description of double-entry accounting in 1494.

## Current Profile
The source presents Pacioli as "The Father of Accounting" because he wrote, printed, and distributed a complete description of double-entry accounting in vernacular Italian. It also qualifies that label by saying the actual creator is unknown and that the practice had been in use for at least two centuries before Pacioli described it.

## Key Characteristics
- Credited in the source as "The Father of Accounting."
- Published a complete description of double-entry accounting in 1494.
- Wrote in vernacular Italian, making the accounting description more widely consumable.
- Represents dissemination rather than original invention in the source's account.
