## Overview
[[SimonParkin]] is the journalist who reported the 2018 Gamasutra feature "The Great Video Game Exodus."

## Current Profile
In this source, Parkin combines worker exit narratives, a GDC tenure survey, and [[JAllard]]'s industry analysis to frame game-development turnover as a systemic labor problem rather than a failure of commitment by individual workers.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports through detailed career histories from multiple disciplines and countries.
- Connects individual exits to production finance, scheduling, compensation, geography, gender, and family life.
- Uses survey tenure figures as supporting context while relying mainly on interviews for mechanisms.

## Evidence
- Multi-voice reporting: [[gamasutra-the-great-video-game-exodus]] centers [[FrankDAngelo]], [[JAllard]], [[RickKelly]], [[AlejandroScrivano]], and [[KristenKoster]].
- Systems frame: [[gamasutra-the-great-video-game-exodus]] links project economics and production uncertainty to churn and burnout.
- Quantitative context: [[gamasutra-the-great-video-game-exodus]] cites the 2018 GDC State of the Industry survey's tenure distribution.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to one article. Its interview sample is selected around departure stories, and the cited survey distribution does not by itself measure exits or their causes.
