## Overview
[[Betaworks]] is the company that acquired [[Instapaper]] in 2013 and supplied a new team for infrastructure, redesign, extensions, ranking, and app updates.

## Current Profile
In the Instapaper retrospective, Betaworks marks the transition from founder-era development to a broader team operating a mature read-later product. Its period is associated with backend infrastructure improvements, a website redesign, a Chrome extension, InstaRank, Instapaper Daily, iOS 7-era redesign work, Android and iPad redesigns, localization, video support, and later discovery and email-digest features.

## Key Characteristics
- Acquired Instapaper in 2013.
- Brought a new team into product and infrastructure development.
- Worked on backend infrastructure and web redesign.
- Expanded browser, ranking, daily discovery, localization, video, and app redesign capabilities.
- Helped move Instapaper toward broader discovery and engagement surfaces.
