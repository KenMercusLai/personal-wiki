## Overview
[[SarahTavel]] is represented in the wiki through her first-person account of helping [[Pinterest]] scale from roughly five to 650 employees.

## Current Profile
Tavel describes three vantage points on Pinterest: investor, early product manager, and leader of discovery product across search, recommendations, and visual search. Her retrospective emphasizes operating judgment during scale: match metrics to meaningful user behavior, give strategic teams end-to-end capacity, diagnose the underlying problem behind vocal requests, accumulate user trust, and preserve a differentiated strategy through temporary growth stalls.

## Key Characteristics
- Observed Pinterest first as an investor and later from product leadership roles.
- Led discovery areas including search, recommendations, and visual search.
- Treats metric choice as a strategic decision that shapes team behavior.
- Favors organization structures that give strategic initiatives accountable, cross-functional ownership.
- Separates power-user requests from the underlying needs of broader and future cohorts.
- Frames trust and first-principles strategy as resources for surviving outages, bold changes, and growth plateaus.
