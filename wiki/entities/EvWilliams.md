## Overview
[[EvWilliams]] is a co-founder of [[Medium]] and the author of its five-year mission and subscription-strategy reflection.

## Current Profile
In the source, Williams presents media-system design as an incentive problem rather than merely a writing-tools problem. He argues that information shapes people, attention-funded media rewards compulsion and volume, and Medium should instead use reader payment and contributor rewards to support depth, knowledge, and understanding.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames publishing platforms as systems that shape understanding and behavior.
- Diagnoses online-media failure through the link between attention and money.
- Advocates direct reader payment as a way to align product and editorial incentives.
- Presents Medium's open paywall and Partner Program as platform-level responses.

## Evidence
- Mission framing: [[words-still-matter-3-min-read]] revisits Williams's founding claim that publishing tools should deepen understanding and broaden participation.
- Incentive diagnosis: [[words-still-matter-3-min-read]] argues that attention is commodified and rewarded without regard to quality, context, or conscious choice.
- Strategic response: [[words-still-matter-3-min-read]] proposes subscriptions, no ads, an open paywall, and member-value-based partner earnings.

## Qualifications
The page currently rests on one first-person company essay. It documents Williams's stated reasoning and proposed strategy, not the later results of Medium's subscription model or an independent evaluation of his media diagnosis.
