## Overview
[[RajeevMotwani]] is represented as the Stanford contact whose private February 2008 introduction connected [[AsheemChandna]] with [[JyotiBansal]] and the company that became [[AppDynamics]].

## Current Profile
Motwani sent Chandna a confidential note and slides for Bansal's venture, then called Singularity Technologies. In this source, his role is narrow but consequential: he transmitted enough trusted technical-network signal to prompt the meeting that led quickly to Greylock and Lightspeed's Series A investment.

## Key Characteristics
- Stanford-based member of Chandna's trusted network.
- Introduced Bansal's early company through confidential materials.
- Served as the relationship bridge between founder and investor.

## Evidence
- Introduction: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] reproduces the substance of Motwani's February 17, 2008 email sending Singularity slides to Chandna.
- Consequence: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] says the message led to Chandna's meeting with Bansal and the subsequent Series A process.

## Qualifications
The source supplies no broader biography or direct account from Motwani. It establishes only his introduction role, and the later success of AppDynamics should not be used to infer how predictive such referrals are generally.
