## Overview
[[AnneWojcicki]] is the founder of [[23andMe]] and the central advocate in the reported hospital-transfer case.

## Current Profile
The source presents Wojcicki as a biotechnology entrepreneur with biology, molecular-research, and financial-analysis experience who used her public visibility and personal network to help an uninsured, critically ill friend obtain insurance, a second opinion, and advanced care. Her actions combined social-media escalation, recruitment of legal and insurance help, improvised capture of clinical information, and direct specialist search. She treats the outcome as evidence of a system problem rather than a satisfactory model: care should not depend on a famous friend willing to complain loudly.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder of consumer-genetics company [[23andMe]].
- Publicly challenged payment and medical-record barriers affecting a critically ill friend.
- Used social reach and professional connections to recruit help and locate clinicians.
- Encouraged the family to photograph available clinical information as an interim workaround.
- Advocated broader reform in insurance counseling, legal support, second opinions, specialist navigation, and system simplicity.
