## Overview
[[EpicGames]] is the game developer the source associates with Unreal Tournament and the late-1990s PC FPS focus on online arena multiplayer.

## Current Profile
GamesRadar+ places Epic Games beside [[IdSoftware]] and [[Valve]] in the mature PC FPS ecosystem. Unreal Tournament is framed as a hectic, ultra-fast-paced online shooter with little interest in single-player, helping cement PC as the home of fast competitive FPS play at the turn of the century.

## Key Characteristics
- Built Unreal Tournament as a prominent late-1990s PC FPS milestone.
- Emphasized fast online arena multiplayer over single-player campaign structure.
- Helped diversify the post-Doom FPS ecosystem beyond run-and-gun clones.
- Represented a competitive PC branch running alongside Quake 3 Arena and Counter-Strike.

## Evidence
- Online focus: [[a-43-year-history-of-first-person-shooters-from-maze-war-to-destiny-2-gamesradar]] groups Unreal Tournament with Quake 3 Arena and Counter-Strike as online-focused late-1990s FPS landmarks.
- Arena pacing: [[a-43-year-history-of-first-person-shooters-from-maze-war-to-destiny-2-gamesradar]] describes Unreal Tournament and Quake as hectic, ultra-fast-paced titles with minimal regard for single-player.
- Genre maturation: [[a-43-year-history-of-first-person-shooters-from-maze-war-to-destiny-2-gamesradar]] says this period showed FPS had moved beyond the old "Doom clone" label into a wider creative ecosystem.
