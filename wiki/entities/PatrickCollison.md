## Overview
[[PatrickCollison]] is presented as a Stripe founder whose scaling lessons cover slow early hiring, communication past 150 people, and CEO priorities.

## Current Profile
The source uses Collison to show how early hiring discipline coexists with later communication formalization. Stripe took months to hire its first people, used week-long trials, and then experienced a major communication shift once the company grew beyond about 150 employees.

## Key Characteristics
- Values taking a long time to hire strong early people.
- Uses work trials as part of early hiring judgment.
- Treats 150 employees as a threshold where broadcast communication becomes necessary.
- Reduces CEO responsibilities to strategy, culture, senior management selection, and optional product leadership.

## Evidence
- Early hiring: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] says Collison described Stripe taking six months to hire its first two people.
- Work trials: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] describes week-long trials for many Stripe candidates.
- Communication threshold: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Collison that past 150 employees Stripe needed formal broadcast communication.
- CEO scope: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Collison on strategy, culture, senior management, and optional product.
