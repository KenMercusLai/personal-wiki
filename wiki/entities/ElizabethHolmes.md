## Overview

## Current Profile
The available capture attributes Theranos's failed blood-testing claims and secretive operating model directly to Holmes. It says she rejected medical experts' judgments about whether the technology worked and continued in the hope that it could eventually be made successful. This is a narrow profile derived from an editorial standfirst; the saved investigation, evidence, chronology, and response are absent.
