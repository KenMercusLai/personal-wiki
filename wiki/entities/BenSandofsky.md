## Overview
[[BenSandofsky]] is a software developer and co-creator of [[Halide]] who authored its first-year product and business retrospective.

## Current Profile
Sandofsky presents Halide as a nights-and-weekends product experiment built with [[SebastiaanDeWith]] and refined through support, user requests, platform changes, and sales feedback. His retrospective emphasizes accepting user-visible problems regardless of fault, protecting a narrow product identity, and balancing feature-driven revenue with necessary maintenance.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-created Halide with Sebastiaan de With.
- Frames shipping as a way to test product and business theories under real market conditions.
- Treats repeated support failures as product responsibility even when an operating-system behavior is the immediate cause.
- Advocates long-term focus and continued shipping through sales plateaus.

## Evidence
- Product role: [[halide-one-year-later-halide]] identifies the author and Sebastiaan as Halide's builders during its ten-month pre-launch period and first year.
- Operating judgment: [[halide-one-year-later-halide]] describes the delayed RAW workaround as a mistake caused by treating a recurring user problem as user error.
- Strategy: [[halide-one-year-later-halide]] documents product-scope filters, episodic releases, pricing experiments, and maintenance commitments.
