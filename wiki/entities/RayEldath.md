## Overview
[[RayEldath]] is represented here as a programmer and essayist reflecting on abstraction, layering, and higher-order computation.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Eldath combines personal learning history, practitioner examples, correspondence with programming-languages educator Dan Grossman, and an extended derivation of the Futamura projections. The 2024 edit shows a material change in judgment: it retreats from asking whether industrial programmers need the mathematical foundations of monads and functors, while retaining opposition to jargon used without explanatory or technical value.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes practitioner-oriented explanations of theoretical computer-science ideas.
- Uses operational examples to connect abstract concepts with debugging, performance, and implementation.
- Distinguishes useful abstraction from prestige-oriented mathematical vocabulary.
- Revises earlier judgments explicitly rather than presenting the essay as timeless or authoritative.
