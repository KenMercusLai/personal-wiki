## Overview
[[Paxful]] is presented as a peer-to-peer marketplace where an informal broker sold [[Bitcoin]] to international buyers in exchange for gift-card codes.

## Current Profile
In the 2018 case, Paxful supplied discovery, transaction history, and dispute resolution for trades that conventional payment rails did not intermediate. The broker assessed counterparties through prior transactions, received gift-card codes, and delayed bitcoin release until he could spend the balance. The marketplace reportedly charged about 1 percent, while the broker earned the much narrower spread between his bitcoin sale premium and the premium paid to reacquire bitcoin with newly purchased cards.

This structure widened payment access but did not establish lawful gift-card provenance. The platform's trust layer reduced counterparty uncertainty for the broker while retailer redemption remained the practical test of whether a code still held value.

## Key Characteristics
- Matched geographically dispersed bitcoin buyers and sellers using nonstandard payment instruments.
- Exposed prior transaction history for counterparty screening.
- Offered a court-like dispute process according to the profiled broker.
- Charged a reported fee of about 1 percent per transaction.
- Relied on participant and external retailer checks for gift-card validity and provenance risk.

## Evidence
- Marketplace role: [[inside-the-wild-west-world-of-gift-card-bitcoin-brokering]] follows a broker selling bitcoin to buyers worldwide through Paxful.
- Reputation layer: [[inside-the-wild-west-world-of-gift-card-bitcoin-brokering]] says the broker reviewed buyers' prior platform transactions before trading.
- Dispute layer: [[inside-the-wild-west-world-of-gift-card-bitcoin-brokering]] reports that Paxful helped adjudicate contested transactions.
- Fee and spread: [[inside-the-wild-west-world-of-gift-card-bitcoin-brokering]] reports a usual platform fee near 1 percent and a broker margin determined by two opposing premiums.
- External validation: [[inside-the-wild-west-world-of-gift-card-bitcoin-brokering]] shows the broker withholding bitcoin until the received card balance had been spent in a store.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one broker's 2018 account and one observed trade. It does not independently verify Paxful's rules, fee schedule, dispute outcomes, user scale, fraud rate, legal status, or later operations. A successful redemption shows that a code held value at that moment, not that it was lawfully acquired.
