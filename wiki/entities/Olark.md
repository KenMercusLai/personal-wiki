## Overview
[[Olark]] is represented here as the company where a 2017 internal mental-health leave message and supportive CEO reply became a public example of workplace care.

## Current Profile
The source identifies Olark as the workplace shared by CEO [[BenCongleton]] and employee [[MadalynParker]]. Parker used an internal out-of-office list to say she was taking sick time for mental health, and Congleton replied in support. Congleton also states that Olark's mission was to make business human, using the exchange to connect that aspiration with how leaders treat employees.

## Key Characteristics
- Used an internal out-of-office list for employee absence communication.
- Provided the organizational setting for direct mental-health leave disclosure.
- Was led in the source by Congleton, who publicly endorsed Parker's decision.
- Articulated a mission of making business human.
