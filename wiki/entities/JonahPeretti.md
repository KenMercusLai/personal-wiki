## Overview
[[JonahPeretti]] appears as the author of the "9 Boxes" memo to BuzzFeed employees.

## Current Profile
The source presents Peretti as BuzzFeed's strategic leader explaining how the company should respond to platform-dominated digital media economics. He argues that BuzzFeed can both survive a difficult media market and help reshape it by improving the publisher-platform relationship, diversifying revenue, building stronger media brands, and reorganizing teams around revenue opportunities.

## Key Characteristics
- Communicates strategy internally through a company-wide memo.
- Frames BuzzFeed's business challenges as part of a broader crisis in media, democracy, and culture.
- Advocates long-term investment in quality content despite short-term incentives to cut content costs.
- Treats diversified revenue and brand portfolios as the next stage of BuzzFeed's maturation.

## Evidence
- Strategic authorship: [[9-boxes]] is signed "Jonah" and addresses BuzzFeed employees about the company's future.
- Platform critique: [[9-boxes]] says big tech platforms capture much of the ad revenue while content creators are underpaid.
- Long-term content stance: [[9-boxes]] argues that quality content is BuzzFeed's best weapon in the fight for fair platform economics.
- Organizational design: [[9-boxes]] introduces the nine-box meeting plan and operating matrix.

## Qualifications
This profile only reflects Peretti's stance inside the 2017-2018 BuzzFeed strategy memo. It does not evaluate his broader career, later BuzzFeed performance, public-company outcomes, layoffs, acquisitions, or later changes in platform strategy.
