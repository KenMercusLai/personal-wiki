## Overview
[[AlexisRichardson]] is represented here as the author who named and explained [[Weaveworks]]'s early [[GitOps]] operating model.

## Current Profile
Richardson presents GitOps as mostly an assembly of established practices—declarative infrastructure, version control, peer review, delivery pipelines, and recovery—with a smaller layer of drift and synchronization tooling added for repository-to-runtime convergence. The wiki's evidence is limited to this practitioner article and does not attempt a broader biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Author of the source's first-person account of Weaveworks operations.
- Frames GitOps as developer tooling applied to infrastructure and application operations.
- Emphasizes recovery time, discoverable intent, and simple operational tooling.

## Evidence
- Authorship: [[gitops-operations-by-pull-request]] identifies Alexis Richardson as the author.
- Framing: [[gitops-operations-by-pull-request]] describes GitOps as 90% established best practices and 10% new tooling.
- Operational priority: [[gitops-operations-by-pull-request]] uses a destructive cluster incident to argue for optimizing mean time to recovery.

## Qualifications
