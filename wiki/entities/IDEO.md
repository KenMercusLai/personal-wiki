## Overview
[[IDEO]] is a design consultancy represented here through its 2017 acquisition of [[Datascope]] and its effort to apply [[HumanCenteredDesign]] to data science and artificial intelligence.

## Current Profile
The source portrays IDEO as treating algorithms, data, and machine learning as a new design medium rather than a purely technical specialty. Its acquisition of Datascope moved a 15-person data-science team into the Chicago office after four years of joint work, giving organizational form to an approach the companies called [[AugmentedIntelligence]]. IDEO's stated ambition was to design adaptive systems that serve human needs and learn with people, although the article reports no post-acquisition projects or measured outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Prominent design consultancy associated with human-centered design.
- Expanded its data-science capability by acquiring Datascope in 2017.
- Treats algorithms and machine learning as materials whose behavior and consequences can be designed.
- Promotes collaboration among designers, engineers, data scientists, and affected people.
- Applies its design framing to systems that change through data and feedback rather than remaining static artifacts.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one announcement-era article built largely from interviews with leaders of the acquiring and acquired companies. It establishes their stated rationale, not whether the acquisition produced better systems, prevented bias, changed data-science practice, or succeeded commercially. The article also notes criticism that design thinking may be inadequate for systemic problems, so IDEO's confidence should be read as an interested-party position rather than a demonstrated result.
