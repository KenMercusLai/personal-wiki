## Overview
[[Kik]] appears as a mobile messaging app using promoted brand chats as opt-in content channels.

## Current Profile
The source uses Kik as a primary example of opt-in messaging advertising. Brands can pay to appear in the Promoted Chats tab, and once users start a brand chat, the account can send ad-like content or answer questions through keyword-sensitive bots and links opened in Kik's native browser.

## Key Characteristics
- Messaging app example of promoted brand chat placement.
- Uses user-initiated brand engagement as the permission boundary.
- Connects chat advertising to lightweight bots and in-app web content.

## Evidence
- Promoted Chats: [[advertising-models-in-mobile-messaging-apps-mobile-dev-memo]] says advertisers can pay for visibility at the top of Kik's Promoted Chats tab.
- Bot replies: [[advertising-models-in-mobile-messaging-apps-mobile-dev-memo]] says brand accounts can respond to keyword-like questions with relevant content.
- Native browser: [[advertising-models-in-mobile-messaging-apps-mobile-dev-memo]] notes that Kik can load external content inside its native browser.

## Qualifications
The source uses Kik as a 2016 ad-format example and does not assess Kik's broader product trajectory, demographics, or later business performance.
