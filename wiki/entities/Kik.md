## Overview
[[Kik]] appears as a mobile messaging app in the wiki, first through promoted brand chats, later as a company-token precedent, and now as the platform context for [[TedLivingston]]'s 2016 chatbot thesis.

## Current Profile
The Mobile Dev Memo source uses Kik as a primary example of opt-in messaging advertising. Brands can pay to appear in the Promoted Chats tab, and once users start a brand chat, the account can send ad-like content or answer questions through keyword-sensitive bots and links opened in Kik's native browser. Livingston's source adds Kik's own platform ambition: its Bot Shop launched alongside Facebook's bot platform, and Kik Codes, web bubbles, invites, mentions, and million-user bots are presented as evidence that chat could become a service runtime and distribution layer. Coin and Crypto adds a separate crypto-era reference: Kik is cited as proof that a large established company had already launched a token, raising $75 million in a 2017 ICO.

## Key Characteristics
- Messaging app example of promoted brand chat placement.
- Uses user-initiated brand engagement as the permission boundary.
- Connects chat advertising to lightweight bots and in-app web content.
- Serves as Livingston's main example of chatbot-platform tooling, sharing mechanics, and early bot audience scale.
- Serves as a company-token precedent in a speculative article about whether Amazon could create its own cryptocurrency.

## Evidence
- Messaging advertising: [[advertising-models-in-mobile-messaging-apps-mobile-dev-memo]] says advertisers can pay for visibility in Kik's Promoted Chats tab, then use bot replies and Kik's native browser after users engage.
- Bot platform tooling: [[chat-is-the-new-browser-ted-livingston-medium]] cites Kik's Bot Shop, Kik Codes, web bubbles, invites, and mentions as bot ecosystem advances.
- Bot audience scale: [[chat-is-the-new-browser-ted-livingston-medium]] says Roll, DisOrDat, and Tickers the Bot each reached a million users on Kik.
- Company-token precedent: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] says Kik launched an ICO in September 2017 and raised $75 million.

## Qualifications
The sources use Kik as a 2016 ad-format example, a 2016 bot-platform example, and a brief 2017 ICO precedent. Livingston's source is an insider/platform-advocacy argument, so its bot ecosystem claims should be read as strategic positioning. The sources do not assess Kik's broader product trajectory, demographics, token design, regulatory history, or later business performance.
