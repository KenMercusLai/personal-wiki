## Overview
[[Sumo]] is represented in the source as the publisher of an ecommerce bundling article and vendor of a promoted cart-spend incentive tool.

## Current Profile
After using [[BestBuy]] to explain three [[ProductBundling]] patterns, the article says Sumo's team built an “Increase Average Order Value” Shortcut for Shopify, WooCommerce, and WordPress sites. Instead of requiring a merchant to configure a distinct bundle for every item, the tool shows site-wide notifications after a shopper adds something to the cart, offers a discount for reaching another spending threshold, and can expose a larger subsequent threshold.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes tactical ecommerce marketing guidance.
- Promotes a tool intended to raise cart spend after an initial add-to-cart signal.
- Uses configurable spend thresholds and percentage discounts.
- Positions site-wide incentives as a lower-configuration alternative to per-product bundles.

## Evidence
- Publishing role: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] uses Best Buy examples to teach bundling patterns before presenting Sumo's own product.
- Product scope: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] says the Shortcut works with Shopify, WooCommerce, and WordPress sites.
- Incentive flow: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] describes a 10% threshold notification, confirmation after qualification, and a later 20% threshold.
- Configuration claim: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] says merchants can choose the spending amount and percentage discount.

## Qualifications
The source is Sumo's own promotional copy and supplies no merchant sample, controlled experiment, adoption rate, conversion lift, realized AOV, margin impact, implementation detail, or current product-status evidence. The described platform compatibility and feature set are source-date claims, not current setup guidance.
