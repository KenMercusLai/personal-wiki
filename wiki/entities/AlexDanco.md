## Overview
[[AlexDanco]] is represented through three 2015-2016 essays that interpret technological shifts by asking where a system is constrained, how unbundling or abstraction expands capacity, when a new surface saturates, and where scarcity and strategic value move next.

## Current Profile
Danco's [[EmergentLayerTheory]] joins supply-side abstraction with higher-order [[JobsToBeDone]]: removing a constraint can weaken an incumbent profit pool, unlock unusually strong demand, and make a new network, standard, ecosystem, data asset, or platform layer scarce. His notification essay provides an earlier interface-level version of that reasoning: Outlook unbundles into apps, alerts rebundle into one tray, the time domain saturates, and spatial interfaces are proposed as new ambiguity-resolution capacity. His media essay then applies scarcity shifts to content, distribution, pull, push, ephemeral expression, and [[HereAndNowMedia]]. Across the three pieces, company histories and emerging products generate strategic hypotheses rather than deterministic predictions.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes about technology through shifts from scarcity to abundance and the new bottlenecks those shifts create.
- Uses bundling, unbundling, and saturation to explain why an initially efficient interface or market structure can become a new constraint.
- Combines supply-side capability change with customer purpose, expression, and experience.
- Uses selected company and product histories as retrospective strategic cases.
- Distinguishes access to an old scarce resource from ownership of the next scarce layer.
- Acknowledges uncertainty while still making strong interpretive claims about future value and profit pools.

## Evidence
- General framework: [[emergent-layers-chapter-3-explosive-growth-the-startup-medium]] connects abstraction, higher-order jobs, incumbent friction, and new strategic bottlenecks.
- Interface precursor: [[notifications-run-our-lives-now-is-there-room-for-any-more-alexdanco-com]] traces Outlook, apps, and the notification tray through unbundling, rebundling, and saturation, then speculates that spatial objects could expand triage bandwidth.
- Media application: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] moves from content and distribution through pull and push toward momentary expression and situated presence.
- Case method: [[notifications-run-our-lives-now-is-there-room-for-any-more-alexdanco-com]], [[emergent-layers-chapter-3-explosive-growth-the-startup-medium]], and [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] use recognizable products and companies to illustrate the model, including Outlook, Apple Watch, Facebook Chat Heads, Amazon, Google, Uber, Snapchat, and Twitch.
- Stated limits: [[emergent-layers-chapter-3-explosive-growth-the-startup-medium]] calls the framework non-deterministic, while [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] declines detailed prediction and admits that its Twitch interpretation is not based on personal use.

## Qualifications
These pages establish Danco's arguments, not a broader biography or proof that the framework predicts outcomes. The essays select visible cases, leave key constructs difficult to measure, and can underweight capital, regulation, distribution, execution, timing, accessibility, and failed analogues. The notification essay does not measure ambiguity, interruption, spatial-memory capacity, or Chat Heads outcomes, and moving alerts into space could increase clutter rather than bandwidth. The media article is especially speculative about future profit pools. All product snapshots are historical and should not be read as current evaluations.

## What Changed
- Added the earlier notification essay's unbundle-rebundle-saturate pattern as a precursor to Danco's later scarcity-shift framework.
- Added ambiguity resolution and interface bandwidth as recurring bottleneck questions.
- Qualified spatial-notification predictions with clutter, accessibility, and absent outcome evidence.
