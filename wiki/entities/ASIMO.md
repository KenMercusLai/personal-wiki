## Overview
[[ASIMO]] is Honda's humanoid robot, used in the source as the platform for [[AmyWibowo]]'s emotion-aware [[ReinforcementLearning]] work.

## Current Profile
The essay describes ASIMO as a four-foot-tall humanoid robot and as the concrete robot through which Wibowo explored reinforcement learning. In her project, ASIMO's video feed was paired with face-reading software so human reactions such as pleasure or frustration could guide robot learning.

## Key Characteristics
- Humanoid robot platform associated with Honda.
- Used for reinforcement-learning research in the source.
- Serves as an example of robotics work shaped by human emotional feedback.
