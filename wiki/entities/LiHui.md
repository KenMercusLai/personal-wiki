## Overview
[[LiHui]] (厉辉) is the author of a Chinese practitioner article that translates reported Anthropic team experience into a scenario-based AI coding workflow.

## Current Profile
Within the available source, Li Hui is represented as a practitioner synthesizing Claude-related case reports rather than as the original investigator of those cases. The article emphasizes matching human-agent interaction to task risk and uncertainty, then supporting autonomy with project instructions, version control, retries, specialization, and visual feedback.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames AI coding as a portfolio of collaboration modes rather than one universal workflow.
- Converts team anecdotes into operational patterns for individual or team practice.
- Treats failed agent attempts as an expected probabilistic cost that can be bounded with checkpoints.
- Emphasizes infrastructure and culture alongside model capability.

## Evidence
- Collaboration framework: [[claude-bian-cheng-gong-zuo-liu-chang-jing-hua-shi-jian]] separates synchronous critical-path work, asynchronous repetitive execution, and mixed exploration of unfamiliar domains.
- Workflow patterns: [[claude-bian-cheng-gong-zuo-liu-chang-jing-hua-shi-jian]] proposes checkpoint-and-retry, specialized agents, and screenshot-led iteration.
- Operating prerequisites: [[claude-bian-cheng-gong-zuo-liu-chang-jing-hua-shi-jian]] identifies `CLAUDE.md`, frequent commits, and tolerance for failed attempts as enabling conditions.
