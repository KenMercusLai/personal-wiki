## Overview
[[RobWalling]] is an entrepreneur represented in the wiki through the “Minimum Path to Awesome,” an onboarding heuristic for moving a customer quickly to a meaningful product result.

## Current Profile
The available source uses Walling’s heuristic to turn the abstract goal of fast value into product-specific milestones. An invoicing product might target the first sent invoice or first payment; proposal software the first sent proposal; and email software the first subscriber or campaign. The milestone is a hypothesis to test, not a universal event, and it should begin an experience that continues to provide value.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames onboarding around customer accomplishment rather than completed configuration.
- Uses concrete product-specific milestones to operationalize activation.
- Treats the correct milestone as an empirical question rather than a certainty.

## Evidence
Outcome-centered onboarding:
- [[people-need-my-product-but-do-they-want-it]] attributes the “Minimum Path to Awesome” phrase and invoice, proposal, subscriber, and email examples to Walling.

Experimental boundary:
- [[people-need-my-product-but-do-they-want-it]] preserves Walling’s qualification that a team may not yet know which milestone creates excitement and must begin with a guess.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one secondary quotation embedded in a 2017 practitioner essay. It does not establish Walling’s complete work, the original talk or article’s full context, or comparative evidence that the named milestones improve paid conversion or retention. A fast milestone can also be misleading when it is easy to trigger but weakly connected to durable customer value.

- Created a source-scoped profile centered on outcome-based onboarding and testable activation milestones.
