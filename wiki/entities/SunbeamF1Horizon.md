## Overview
[[SunbeamF1Horizon]] is a family of flip-style feature phones represented here by the Eagle model used for a three-month smartphone-replacement experiment.

## Current Profile
The tested Eagle is deliberately limited but not disconnected. It supports calls, SMS, downloaded HERE maps, optional Waze, and 4G tethering over Wi-Fi or USB. Its removable rear cover exposes the battery area, a nano-SIM slot, and MicroSD expansion. The first-use experience required almost no setup, call quality was ordinary, messaging was basic, and hotspot performance was sufficient for brief laptop email despite being slower than 5G.

The device appears to run an Android-derived BasicOS and advertises optional premium features. In the source, those facts create uncertainty rather than a completed assessment: the author had not established the Android version, operating-system provenance, source availability, security maintenance, application removability, subscription value, sideloading behavior, or ADB access.

## Key Characteristics
- Flip-phone form factor that is smaller than a Pixel 8 but larger than the author's older Sanyo handset.
- Removable back with visible nano-SIM and MicroSD slots and access to a replaceable battery area.
- Basic calls and SMS designed to minimize time spent interacting with the device.
- Downloaded HERE navigation, optional Waze, and Android-style 4G hotspot functionality over Wi-Fi and USB.
- Android-derived BasicOS with unresolved provenance, version, openness, and security questions.
- Optional premium features that can be ignored for basic use in the reported setup.

## Qualifications
This profile comes from one independently purchased unit during initial setup, not a specification audit, security review, teardown, long-term reliability test, accessibility evaluation, or completed three-month trial. The source does not verify the Android version, update policy, BasicOS authorship, battery characteristics, radio compatibility, navigation licensing, premium-feature scope, or application removability. Hotspot speed was assessed informally against 5G, and acceptable call quality and SMS behavior are one user's judgments.
