## Overview
[[StanfordMedicalCenter]] is the advanced-care destination in the reported transfer of [[AnneWojcicki]]'s critically ill friend.

## Current Profile
The article reports that the patient's family sought a second opinion at Stanford because advanced treatment was unavailable at [[SantaClaraValleyMedicalCenter]], but that Stanford would not evaluate the uninsured patient without a $1 million deposit. After advocates helped the patient obtain temporary coverage, Stanford accepted the transfer. Stanford did not discuss the case because of privacy rules and instead stated generally that very complex procedures may require assurance that continuing care can be funded.

## Key Characteristics
- Hospital sought for a second opinion and advanced treatment.
- Reportedly required a large deposit while the patient lacked insurance.
- Accepted the patient after emergency insurance was arranged.
- Declined case-specific comment under patient-privacy rules.
- Framed financial assurance generally as support for care needed after complex procedures.
