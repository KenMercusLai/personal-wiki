## Overview
[[Outlive]] appears in this wiki as [[PeterAttia]]'s longevity book and the source of a [[VO2Max]] graph that [[AndrewChen]] says changed his exercise behavior.

## Current Profile
The available source treats [[Outlive]] as a book whose chart makes age-related cardiorespiratory decline vivid. Chen does not summarize the whole book; he focuses on one graph that compares high, average, and low fitness percentiles against activity thresholds. In the wiki, the book therefore currently functions as a source of a capacity-reserve argument: future mobility depends partly on building enough cardiovascular capacity before age-related decline reduces the margin.

## Key Characteristics
- Longevity book by [[PeterAttia]].
- Supplies the VO2 max graph discussed by Chen.
- Connects fitness percentiles with later-life functional activities in the available source.
- Influences Chen toward running, Peloton, and outdoor cycling.

## Evidence
- Source identification: [[andrew-chen-fun-graph-from-peter-attias-book-outlive]] says the graph comes from [[Outlive]].
- Chart content: [[andrew-chen-fun-graph-from-peter-attias-book-outlive]] shows VO2 max decline across fitness percentiles and overlays task thresholds such as walking, jogging, carrying weight upstairs, and brisk stair climbing.
- Behavioral effect: [[andrew-chen-fun-graph-from-peter-attias-book-outlive]] says Chen resumed running and added cycling-oriented exercise after reading the book.
