## Overview
[[TrinityMcQueen]] is the United Kingdom insight consultancy that conducted the 2019 consumer study reported by cofounder [[SimonShaw]].

## Current Profile
In the available source, Trinity McQueen studies how the British public interprets common commercial behavioral interventions. Its reported work moves from observed skepticism in checkout research to a nationally representative experiment on hotel-booking scarcity and social-proof claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Conducts applied consumer and behavioral research.
- Combines qualitative observations with larger experimental samples.
- Examines trust, emotion, perceived intent, and resistance alongside persuasion.

## Evidence
- Research setting: [[consumers-are-becoming-wise-to-your-nudge-behavioral-scientist]] describes the organization as an insight consultancy and attributes the February 2019 research project to Shaw and his colleagues there.
- Study design: [[consumers-are-becoming-wise-to-your-nudge-behavioral-scientist]] reports a nationally representative sample of 2,102 British adults evaluating nine real-world hotel-booking claims.
- Research focus: [[consumers-are-becoming-wise-to-your-nudge-behavioral-scientist]] measures perceived sales pressure, distrust, belief, negative emotion, and reactance-related attitudes.

## Qualifications
The wiki has only Shaw's public summary, not Trinity McQueen's full report, questionnaire, sampling details, randomization procedure, or statistical analysis. No broader claims about the consultancy's clients or later work are supported here.
