## Overview
[[Snapchat]] appears as a camera-centered social app whose Discover, Stories, ephemeral messages, and Lenses combine opt-in brand content, low-stakes self-expression, and real-time transformation, while its capabilities and competitive position were shaped by acquisitions and Facebook's response.

## Current Profile
The sources converge on Snapchat as more than a disappearing-message utility. Stories and frequent camera use normalized momentary self-presentation; Lenses made the image sensor an input for expressive transformation; Discover provided an opt-in brand surface; and acquired companies were associated with mapping, hardware, avatars, search, codes, and computer vision. Danco places those pieces in a scarcity argument: the front camera and ephemerality made varied temporary selves easier to express, giving Snapchat a wedge into [[HereAndNowMedia]]. He nevertheless argues that a fleeting view into a friend's experience does not automatically create a durable shared place. Facebook's copied formats, broad networks, and Onavo telemetry add the competitive boundary.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses ephemerality and camera-first interaction to lower the cost of temporary self-expression.
- Originated Stories, whose copied implementations had different fit across Instagram and Messenger.
- Uses Lenses to transform faces and scenes into expressive messages in real time.
- Provides opt-in brand content through Discover and sponsored visual effects.
- Combined internal development with acquisitions associated with mapping, hardware, avatars, search, codes, and computer vision.
- Faced an incumbent able to copy formats across larger networks and reportedly observe rival usage through Onavo.
- Approaches shared presence without necessarily creating a stable digital “there,” according to Danco.

## Qualifications
The sources are 2016-2017 snapshots and do not cover Snapchat's complete later history. Claims about authenticity, identity variance, product empathy, daily return, camera-platform control, and presence are outside interpretations without comparative behavioral evidence. Acquisition timing and product resemblance do not establish technical causation or returns. Onavo reporting lacks raw telemetry and causal attribution. Ephemerality can reduce persistence pressure but does not guarantee safety, authenticity, intimacy, or beneficial outcomes.

## What Changed
- Added ephemerality as a mechanism for temporary identity variation rather than only content deletion.
- Added the distinction between expressive immediacy and a durable shared sense of place.
- Integrated the camera-company thesis with Danco's broader scarcity-shift framework.
- Preserved the account as a 2016 strategic interpretation, not a validated explanation of adoption or profit capture.
