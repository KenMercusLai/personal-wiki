## Overview
[[BoldCommerce]] is a Shopify-app company used in the source as evidence that a merchant-founded Shopify app business could become viable.

## Current Profile
The source presents Bold Commerce as a role model for [[AhmadIqbal]] before he left consulting. Iqbal describes Bold as a four-person Winnipeg team that began as merchants, found gaps in the Shopify App Store, built Shopify apps, and grew to nearly 300 employees without outside funding at the time of the article.

## Key Characteristics
- Shopify-app company used as a founder benchmark.
- Presented as merchant-founded rather than investor-first.
- Grew through the Shopify platform according to the source.
- Helped convince Iqbal that app subscriptions could support positive cash flow.

## Evidence
- Founder benchmark: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Bold Commerce's story convinced Iqbal that Shopify apps could become a full-time business.
