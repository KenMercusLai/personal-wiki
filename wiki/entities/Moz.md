## Overview
[[Moz]] is an SEO software company represented in the sources through both content-led SaaS growth and an early proposal for parallel individual-contributor and people-management career tracks.

## Current Profile
One source says Moz grew from an SEO blog and consulting firm into a software company with substantial annual revenue, while relying heavily on valuable content rather than paid marketing. Its weekday free-trial flow and membership conversion are presented as evidence that educational content can produce qualified demand.

Fishkin's 2013 essay presents Moz as an organization preparing roughly equivalent IC and people-management levels and salary ranges. Its examples give senior contributors wide strategic and cross-team influence while people managers concentrate on team health, empowerment, mentoring, reviews, resources, and coordination. This is a proposed and selectively illustrated operating model, not measured evidence of its later implementation or results.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as SEOmoz, an SEO blog and consulting firm.
- Grew into an SEO software company.
- Uses high-volume, high-value SEO education as acquisition.
- Reported in the source as converting a portion of free trials into paid membership.
- Proposed parallel IC and people-management paths with comparable levels and compensation.
- Used wide contributor influence and team-enabling management as distinct forms of senior responsibility.

## Evidence
- Content engine: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says Moz publishes valuable content at scale with little paid advertising.
- Trial conversion: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] reports 150 average weekday free trials, 52% conversion to membership, and longer subscription retention among those who stay.
- Parallel tracks: [[if-management-is-the-only-way-up-were-all-fd-sparktoro]] says Moz was developing a title and salary structure in which corresponding IC and manager levels would be paid roughly equally.
- Role examples: [[if-management-is-the-only-way-up-were-all-fd-sparktoro]] describes a senior IC contributing across engineering, data, marketing, publishing, and project creation, and a people manager concentrating on empowerment, resources, mentoring, one-on-ones, reviews, and HR work.

## Qualifications
The growth source reports marketing metrics but does not verify cohort retention, attribution, competitive context, or profitability. The career-structure source is a 2013 founder account describing a model under development; it does not report implementation fidelity, retention, pay equity, team performance, or later outcomes.

## What Changed
- Expanded Moz from a content-led SaaS example into a company-level case for parallel IC and people-management career tracks.
