## Overview
[[Wulc]] is the author of the essay [[chang]] and the article [[da-shu-ju-shi-fou-neng-gou-gai-zao-ni-de-hang-ye]]. The wiki now sees Wulc writing across reflective life philosophy and technical-business synthesis, from environmental fields to big-data industry transformation.

## Current Profile
The current source set presents Wulc as a blogger who can synthesize both personal-philosophical and technical-business material. In [[chang]], Wulc uses Chinese philosophical references, modern work experience, creativity writing, family psychology, and moral psychology to make readers notice the environments that quietly form them. In [[da-shu-ju-shi-fou-neng-gou-gai-zao-ni-de-hang-ye]], Wulc summarizes a big-data argument about deep learning, behavior data, full-population processing, and automated applications.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses broad cross-domain analogy to connect work, family, creation, and human nature.
- Frames personal growth as environmental discernment rather than pure willpower.
- Writes from first-person reflection while incorporating literary, philosophical, and popular-psychology references.
- Summarizes technical-business arguments into practical criteria, as in the three-part test for big-data industry transformation.
- Treats data-informed decision-making as a challenge to unsupported prior experience.

## Evidence
- Cross-domain synthesis: [[chang]] connects workplace incentives, family-of-origin patterns, intimate relationships, creative receptivity, and moral situations under the "field" metaphor.
- Environmental discernment: [[chang]] repeatedly argues that choosing where to live, whom to live or work with, and what to do is a choice of the field that will shape one's future.
- Reflective style: [[chang]] combines personal work burnout reflections with references to Rick Rubin, Naval Ravikant, Chinese classics, Shi Tiesheng, and the Stanford prison experiment.
- Technical-business synthesis: [[da-shu-ju-shi-fou-neng-gou-gai-zao-ni-de-hang-ye]] condenses a Zhihu Live on deep learning and big data into criteria for industry transformation.
- Evidence-over-experience stance: [[da-shu-ju-shi-fou-neng-gou-gai-zao-ni-de-hang-ye]] closes with the principle that data should outrank prior assumptions in business decisions.
