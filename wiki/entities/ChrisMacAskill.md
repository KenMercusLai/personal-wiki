## Overview
[[ChrisMacAskill]] is presented as a SmugMug co-founder, father of [[DonMacAskill]], former Fatbrain founder, and family patriarch whose entrepreneurial experience and personality shaped [[SmugMug]].

## Current Profile
The source describes Chris as a Stanford-trained geophysicist, lifelong photographer, motorcyclist, and serial entrepreneur who worked at NeXT, built Fatbrain with family help, and joined Don early in the startup that became SmugMug. Within SmugMug he appears as both business veteran and culture carrier: employees and customers know him as "Baldy," he participates heavily in family and customer rituals, and his personal warmth supports the company's informal identity.
