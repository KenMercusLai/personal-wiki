## Overview
[[BrettHemenwayFalk]] is a coauthor of “The AI Layoff Trap” and is identified in the paper as affiliated with the University of Pennsylvania.

## Current Profile
The source represents Falk through a theoretical economics model of competitive AI automation. The work shifts attention from the labor-market question of whether displaced workers eventually find new tasks to the product-market question of whether firms internalize the consumer demand lost during a transition.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-develops the [[AutomationDemandExternality]] model for labor-replacing AI.
- Frames competitive over-automation as potentially harmful to both workers and firm owners.
- Compares policy instruments by whether they alter the marginal automation decision.
- Treats income reabsorption and general-equilibrium demand recycling as boundaries on the result.
