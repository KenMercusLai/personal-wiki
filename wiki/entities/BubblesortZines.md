## Overview
[[BubblesortZines]] is [[AmyWibowo]]'s illustrated computer-science zine project, presented in the source as a self-published way to make CS more accessible and inclusive.

## Current Profile
The source says Wibowo left [[Airbnb]] to write and draw zines about computer-science topics after long wanting math and science books with more drawings. The project's Kickstarter funded quickly, and Wibowo valued the zine form because it gave her full control, lowered perfection pressure, and made cute drawings compatible with serious CS explanation.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses illustration and self-publishing to explain computer science.
- Frames zines as an accessible alternative to polished textbooks.
- Treats low perfection pressure as a creative enabler.
- Represents the author's move from employer-based work into independent educational creation.

## Evidence
- Project origin: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says Wibowo wanted illustrated math and science textbooks and chose zines as the medium.
- Funding and validation: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says the Kickstarter funded within an hour and attracted press attention around accessible, inclusive CS.
- Medium rationale: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says self-publishing gave creative control and permission not to be perfect.
- Planned topics: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] mentions operating systems, image processing, and computer languages among planned topics.
