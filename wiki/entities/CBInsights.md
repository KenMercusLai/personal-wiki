## Overview
[[CBInsights]] is represented in this wiki as the research and publishing organization behind the [[323-startup-failure-post-mortems]] startup shutdown compilation.

## Current Profile
The source presents CB Insights as a venture and startup research publisher that curates company failure evidence from founder goodbye letters, investor reflections, journalist investigations, and company shutdown notices. Its role in the wiki is not as a direct startup operator, but as an evidence aggregator: it turns many individual post-mortems into a pattern library for [[StartupFailurePatterns]], [[ProductMarketFit]], [[StartupRunway]], and adjacent startup-risk concepts.

## Key Characteristics
- Startup and venture research publisher.
- Curates startup shutdown narratives across sectors and years.
- Uses post-mortem evidence to expose recurring startup failure mechanisms.
- Links qualitative failure stories to broader failure statistics and reason taxonomies.

## Evidence
- Research role: [[323-startup-failure-post-mortems]] is a CB Insights compilation of startup failure post-mortems by founders, investors, and journalists.
- Pattern aggregation: [[323-startup-failure-post-mortems]] explicitly frames the obituaries as clues to why startups shut down, then connects them to CB Insights' broader startup-death and failure-reason work.
- Sector breadth: [[323-startup-failure-post-mortems]] covers consumer hardware, AR/VR, healthcare, autonomous vehicles, agtech, food delivery, ecommerce, social products, marketplaces, fintech, media, and enterprise software.

## Qualifications
The page is source-bounded to CB Insights' role as a compiler of startup post-mortems. The compilation is useful qualitative evidence, but it is not a controlled causal study; cases are selected from public shutdown narratives and investigative reports, which can overrepresent visible, venture-backed, English-language, or media-friendly failures.

## What Changed
- Created CB Insights as the research publisher behind the startup failure post-mortem corpus.

## Relationships
- [[StartupFailurePatterns]] - CB Insights supplies the multi-case evidence base for this concept.
- [[ProductMarketFit]] - many compiled cases cite missing demand, weak retention, or unconvincing growth.
- [[StartupRunway]] - the compilation repeatedly shows funding as the deadline on unresolved business-model and market-risk questions.
- [[MarketTiming]] - several failures involve markets or infrastructure maturing too slowly for the startup's financing path.
