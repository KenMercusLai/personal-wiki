## Overview
[[MonikaBickert]] is presented in a 2017 Facebook Newsroom article as Facebook's Director of Global Policy Management and as the author of a personal and policy-focused account of identity after death.

## Current Profile
Bickert uses her experience after her husband Phil's death to show that digital reminders can alternate between refuge and renewed pain. She then explains Facebook's memorialization, deletion, legacy-contact, and private-message policies as attempts to balance uncertain user intent, survivor needs, third-party privacy, and incomplete law.

## Key Characteristics
- Facebook policy leader writing from a first-person experience of bereavement.
- Treats digital memories as capable of both easing and intensifying grief.
- Frames posthumous account decisions as conflicts among multiple legitimate interests.
- Describes preservation of the deceased user's existing choices as Facebook's default when intent is unknown.
