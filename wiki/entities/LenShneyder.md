## Overview
Len Shneyder is represented in the wiki as the author of SendGrid's 2016 Black Friday and Cyber Monday email-volume analysis.

## Current Profile
In the SendGrid source, Shneyder explains email at platform scale for readers who usually experience email only as end users. His contribution is interpretive: he turns SendGrid's delivery and engagement data into practical claims about holiday retail volume, mobile email behavior, responsive templates, and subject-line brevity.

## Key Characteristics
- Email-scale interpreter: explains why billion-message sending creates non-obvious operational problems.
- Data-story author: uses SendGrid observations to infer retail and consumer behavior patterns.
- Practical marketer: translates device and subject-line data into email-template and copywriting implications.
