## Overview
[[DotMailApp]] was an email-client project by [[TobiasVanSchneider]] that the source uses as a personal case of [[SunkCostFallacy]].

## Current Profile
The article describes .Mail as a new email-client idea that van Schneider worked on for roughly two to three years before shutting it down. In hindsight, he says the project continued too long because pride, emotional investment, and fear of publicly giving up outweighed current reasons to continue. The inspected source image shows a red .Mail brand panel and a desktop mail-interface mockup with mailbox navigation and a compose window, confirming that the project was presented visually as a designed email client rather than only an abstract idea.

## Key Characteristics
- Was an email-client project.
- Was worked on for about two to three years before being shut down.
- Is framed by its creator as a sunk-cost example.
