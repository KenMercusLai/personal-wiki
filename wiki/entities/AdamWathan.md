## Overview
[[AdamWathan]] is a software developer and author represented through his 2016 launch of [[RefactoringToCollections]] and subsequent move from employment to full-time independent product work.

## Current Profile
Wathan's case combines technical subject expertise with a staged creator workflow. He narrowed an intimidating book ambition into a proposed mini-book, tested interest through friends and an existing blog audience, shared samples and progress, built a launch list, expanded the product when demand and content justified it, and used differentiated packages to capture more willingness to pay. He reports that the three-day launch generated $61,392 and supplied enough confidence to resign from his job two weeks later.

## Key Characteristics
- Developer-educator focused on PHP, Laravel, refactoring, and collection pipelines in this source.
- Uses small initial scope to overcome the activation cost of ambitious creative work.
- Shares unfinished work, examples, and commitments publicly to obtain feedback and maintain momentum.
- Builds owned email distribution alongside social reach.
- Packages technical knowledge across text, exercises, video, and working source code.
- Treats launch revenue as career evidence while openly describing uncertainty and impostor feelings.

## Evidence
- Product formation: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] traces the idea from a proposed 30-40 page guide through a 150-page book, exercises, four hours of screencasts, and a Laravel codebase.
- Audience and feedback: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] reports landing-page signups, sample chapters, blog posts, Twitter updates, reader replies, and preorder requests before launch.
- Commercial result: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] reports 905 package sales and $61,392 across a three-day launch sale.
- Career transition: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] says Wathan gave notice two weeks after launch and later reported $138,835 in eleven-month product revenue.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one founder-authored retrospective of one successful product. Revenue is gross rather than profit, costs and refunds are not disclosed, causal effects are not separated across audience, content quality, timing, social reach, email, pricing, or launch tactics, and the retained charts do not exactly match the prose totals. The case also benefits from prior subject expertise, roughly 1,500 launch-email recipients, about 4,000 Twitter followers, an existing code project, and peer support.
