## Overview
[[ChristophNakazawa]] is a software engineer and product builder represented here through a first-person account of an agent-centered development workflow and the engineering values he derives from it.

## Current Profile
The available source presents Nakazawa as an experienced engineer using [[Codex]] across Vite+, fate, Codiff, Athena Crisis, and Void. He reports delegating most implementation while retaining product and domain ownership, architectural direction, review, verification, and final acceptance.

His broader position is that agent leverage rewards strong context and judgment rather than eliminating engineering. He favors small ownership units, fast deterministic feedback, repository-local context, selective control of core dependencies, and designs that preserve future options. He also argues that engineering managers should remain technically capable as implementation becomes cheaper.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses coding agents for features, bug fixing, tests, refactoring, review support, and unfamiliar-language implementation.
- Treats product and domain ownership as the basis for directing and evaluating generated work.
- Plans conversationally before execution and begins bug fixes with a failing test when possible.
- Favors strict automated guardrails, fast changed-file tooling, and repository-local context.
- Adjusts inspection depth according to criticality, reversibility, verification strength, and design plausibility.
- Advocates small teams, explicit ownership boundaries, technical management, and architectural option value.
