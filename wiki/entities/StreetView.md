## Overview
[[StreetView]] is presented as Google's immersive street-level imagery system and a likely model for more dynamic future maps.

## Current Profile
The article treats Street View as the emblem of Google's ambition to create the most detailed practical map of the world. Images in the source show both a human-worn Street View capture rig above Rome and a Street View car in Paris, grounding the system in labor, specialized cameras, and physical collection routes. The same program also anchors the source's privacy warning because Street View cars were fined in Germany after collecting data from unencrypted Wi-Fi networks.

## Key Characteristics
- Extends maps from symbolic roads and labels into immersive visual presence.
- Depends on physical capture infrastructure such as cars and wearable camera rigs.
- Points toward a future version of [[GoogleMaps]] with more dynamic, up-to-date imagery and information.
- Creates privacy and surveillance risks when collection vehicles gather data beyond visible imagery.

## Evidence
- Immersive map role: [[a-selfie-for-the-planet]] calls Street View the most immersive and detailed map of the world and a possible emblem for Google's global ambitions.
- Capture infrastructure: [[a-selfie-for-the-planet]] includes images of a wearable Street View rig in Rome and a Google Maps Street View car in Paris.
- Future Maps direction: [[a-selfie-for-the-planet]] says future Google Maps may resemble a dynamic version of Street View with current information and imagery.
- Privacy case: [[a-selfie-for-the-planet]] cites Germany's investigation and fine over Street View cars collecting data from unencrypted Wi-Fi networks.

## Qualifications
The source focuses on Street View's symbolic role, collection apparatus, and privacy controversy rather than product coverage, technical processing, face/license blurring, or later privacy controls.
