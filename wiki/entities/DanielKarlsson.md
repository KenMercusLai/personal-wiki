## Overview
[[DanielKarlsson]] is a programmer, startup founder, and author represented in the wiki by an essay advocating early product release as a way to discover mistaken assumptions and missing domain knowledge.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Karlsson writes from painful experience with delaying release. He treats perfectionism and fear of criticism as practical product risks because they postpone contact with customers, leaving both false certainty and unknown unknowns untested while build costs accumulate.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from the perspective of a programmer and startup founder.
- Advocates customer contact and lightweight product tests before full implementation.
- Frames product release as a method for learning rather than only delivery.
- Describes fear of failure and perfectionism as barriers to evidence-seeking action.
