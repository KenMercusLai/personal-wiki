## Overview
[[LeslieLamport]] is identified by the source as the developer of the [[Paxos]] algorithm and author of its 1998 paper "The Part-Time Parliament."

## Current Profile
Within the bounded evidence, Lamport's role is the originator of a consensus algorithm designed to preserve a single chosen value despite node or network failures. The source connects his work to a prepare, accept, and commit account of Paxos without providing further biographical detail.
