## Overview
[[AmazonFallRiverFulfillmentCenter]] is the 1.2-million-square-foot Massachusetts non-sort warehouse used in the source to show how Amazon handled large, heavy, low-volume, and irregular merchandise.

## Current Profile
Opened in September 2016, the facility differed from Amazon's standard sort centers. It had fewer conveyors, no warehouse robots, more hands-on handling, multi-story pallet racks, improvised holders for thin long products, 230 powered industrial trucks, and custom box-on-demand machinery. The source reports 1,300 employees and daily throughput of 80,000 to 100,000 boxes, with nearly twice that potential volume during the holiday peak.

## Key Characteristics
- Non-sort facility specialized for big, bulky, and irregular inventory.
- Uses more direct human handling and powered industrial trucks than standard Amazon sort centers.
- Adapts storage to product geometry through pallet racks, randomized shelving, and holders for long items.
- Runs continuous custom-box production for products outside standard package sizes.
- Reportedly combines high item variety with substantial daily and holiday throughput.
