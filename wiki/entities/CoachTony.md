## Overview
[[CoachTony]] is the authorial identity behind the essay "A thing I wonder: do companies have too many employees?"

## Current Profile
The source presents Coach Tony as a startup or leadership coach reflecting on engineering organizations, venture-capital assumptions, and the relationship between talent and opportunity. His central contribution in the wiki is the claim that many employees may be underutilized because organizations fail to create situations where talent can become real output.

## Key Characteristics
- Questions headcount growth as a default sign of startup success.
- Interprets apparent employee nonperformance through opportunity and responsibility rather than fraud.
- Frames "10x people" as emerging from "10x situations."

## Evidence
- Headcount critique: [[a-thing-i-wonder-do-companies-have-too-many-employees]] describes a VC discussion where rapid hiring was treated as necessary for successful startups.
- Opportunity frame: [[a-thing-i-wonder-do-companies-have-too-many-employees]] argues that talented people need meaningful opportunity to produce outsized results.
- Employee-fulfillment claim: [[a-thing-i-wonder-do-companies-have-too-many-employees]] asks whether fewer, more trusted employees might be happier and more fulfilled.
