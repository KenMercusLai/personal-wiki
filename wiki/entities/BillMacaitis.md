## Overview
[[BillMacaitis]] is presented as a SaaS marketing executive who built attribution-oriented growth systems at [[Salesforce]], [[Zendesk]], and [[Slack]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Macaitis as the practitioner lens for [[MarketingAttribution]]. His core contribution is making marketing spend measurable enough that founders can scale budgets with confidence. He argues for early tracking after [[ProductMarketFit]], a data-oriented [[MarketingOperations]] hire, deep integration with engineering and data warehouses, and attribution models that connect marketing touches to downstream business outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- SaaS marketing executive associated with Salesforce, Zendesk, and Slack.
- Defines marketing attribution pragmatically as judging whether spend is wise.
- Advocates data-driven channel decisions over intuition-led budget debates.
- Recommends hiring marketing operations early.
- Treats attribution as a trust-building and change-management process inside startups.
