## Overview
[[MarcAndreessen]] appears in the source as the venture-capital voice contrasting change-oriented investing with Warren Buffett's change-resistant style.

## Current Profile
The essay quotes Andreessen saying venture capital bets for change while Buffett bets against it. Housel accepts the directional contrast but rejects a clean binary: venture investments still need timeless customer desires, and value investments still experience changing technologies, channels, and operating methods. Andreessen's role is therefore to supply the sharp formulation that the source then qualifies.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the venture-capital emphasis on technological and market change.
- Provides the contrast against Buffett-style value investing.
- Is used as a foil for the source's claim that all successful investments mix change and timelessness.
