## Overview
[[Beamer]] is a SaaS product for changelogs, release notes, targeted product announcements, user feedback, and product-marketing workflows, spun out of an internal tool built at [[Hibox]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Beamer as a focused response to weak feature discovery through tours, chat, email, and blogs. A minimal in-app notification feed became a separate product after customers and founder peers asked to use it; Product Hunt, AppSumo, direct support, and feedback from SaaS product teams then shaped the commercial offering. The company is described as profitable within six months of official launch and operated by a distributed team of five full-time and one part-time worker, but every performance figure comes from a founder-authored retrospective.

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on in-product communication of changes rather than general live chat or collaboration.
- Grew from an internal MVP into a standalone product through observed usage and external requests.
- Uses direct support and feedback from SaaS founders and product teams to guide product expansion.
- Relies on managed infrastructure and automation to support high reported usage with a small team.
- Acquires customers through referrals, word of mouth, product visibility, content, and SEO rather than reported paid advertising.
- Treats simplicity and narrow buyer focus as operating constraints and product advantages.

## Evidence
- Product origin and scope: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] says Hibox's internal notification feed became Beamer and expanded into targeted announcements, feedback, reactions, scheduling, push notifications, segmentation, customization, and analytics.
- Market pull and launch: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] reports inbound requests for the tool, first paid customers after Product Hunt, and 3,000 one-time AppSumo customers in one week.
- Commercial progress: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] reports profitability within six months, an NPS of 78, and average 20% month-over-month revenue growth over the preceding year.
- Operating leverage: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] attributes more than one billion monthly requests and nearly 200 million unique viewers to PaaS infrastructure, automation, and a five-full-time-person team.
- Distribution mix: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] says about half of leads came from referrals and word of mouth and half from content and SEO.

## Qualifications
The account is company-authored and supplies no audited ARR, cohort retention, churn, margin, customer-acquisition cost, or time series. Its prose says more than 5,000 companies used Beamer while a retained banner claims adoption by more than 20,000 products, apparently mixing dates or units. AppSumo's one-time purchases do not by themselves establish recurring-revenue quality, and the source recognizes that dependence on advocacy and search leaves growth partly outside the company's control.

## What Changed
- Created Beamer as a focused SaaS spinout whose product, distribution, and small-team operating model developed together.
- Recorded the difference between reported launch and growth signals and evidence of durable recurring economics.
- Added the source's explicit organic-channel concentration risk and internally inconsistent adoption counts.
