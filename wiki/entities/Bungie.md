## Overview
[[Bungie]] is the game developer the source connects to several major FPS transitions: story-rich Mac shooters, console control legitimacy, and always-online co-op progression.

## Current Profile
GamesRadar+ presents Bungie as unusually durable across FPS eras. Marathon gave the Mac a distinctive sci-fi FPS with narrative and multiplayer. Halo, after Microsoft's acquisition, became the defining Xbox FPS and showed how twin-stick controls, cinematic combat, AI, lore, and console multiplayer could make the genre work outside the PC. Destiny later carried Bungie's FPS lineage into always-online co-op and MMO-style progression.

## Key Characteristics
- Built Marathon as an early Mac FPS with stronger story and multiplayer ambitions.
- Used Halo to make console FPS controls, pacing, and combat feel convincing.
- Linked strong FPS mechanics with lore, sequels, and console multiplayer communities.
- Moved from discrete console shooter franchises toward persistent co-op live-service play.

## Evidence
- Marathon lineage: [[a-43-year-history-of-first-person-shooters-from-maze-war-to-destiny-2-gamesradar]] says Marathon's nuanced sci-fi plot and multiplayer suite foreshadowed Bungie's later work on Halo and Destiny.
- Halo transition: [[a-43-year-history-of-first-person-shooters-from-maze-war-to-destiny-2-gamesradar]] credits Halo with strong narrative, cinematic gameplay, enemy AI, and the best translation of FPS controls to twin-stick controllers.
- Console multiplayer: [[a-43-year-history-of-first-person-shooters-from-maze-war-to-destiny-2-gamesradar]] says later Halo installments and Xbox Live built deep console multiplayer.
- Destiny service model: [[a-43-year-history-of-first-person-shooters-from-maze-war-to-destiny-2-gamesradar]] describes Destiny as always online, community-driven, co-op, and persistent.

## Qualifications
The source gives a milestone-oriented view of Bungie's FPS work and does not evaluate Bungie's internal production methods, non-FPS history, or Destiny's later post-launch trajectory in depth.
