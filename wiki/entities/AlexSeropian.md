## Overview
[[AlexSeropian]] is a game developer and executive represented here through his account of leading Disney's core game group from 2009 to 2012.

## Current Profile
Seropian brings experience from cofounding [[Bungie]], working under [[Microsoft]] around the first Halo launch, and overseeing Disney-owned and external game studios. He contrasts Microsoft's greater willingness to trust product quality with Disney's stronger demand for predictable financial justification. His central judgment is that major game publishing needs institutional knowledge, several simultaneous bets, and a five-to-ten-year willingness to absorb failures before a hit pays for the portfolio.

## Key Characteristics
- Cofounded Bungie and spent nearly twelve years as its CEO.
- Worked at Microsoft after its Bungie acquisition and around the launch of the first Halo game.
- Led Disney's core game group and oversaw owned and external studios from 2009 to 2012.
- Diagnoses Disney's game problems as a combination of financial caution and weak institutional knowledge.
- Frames large-game publishing as a multi-project, long-horizon portfolio rather than a sequence of individually predictable returns.

## Qualifications
The article relies on Seropian's retrospective interpretation and does not independently test his portfolio estimate, compare all Disney and Microsoft project decisions, or disclose his own proposals and results at Disney. His experience gives the account informed access, but it may not represent finance leaders, franchise owners, employees at every acquired studio, or later outcomes.
