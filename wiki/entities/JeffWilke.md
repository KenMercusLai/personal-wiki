## Overview
[[JeffWilke]] appears in the Forbes source as the longtime Amazon lieutenant running consumer and retail operations.

## Current Profile
The source uses Wilke as an internal witness to Amazon's experimentation culture and capability learning. He says Amazon wants reversible experiments and failures to happen throughout the company without senior review, and he recalls objecting to Kindle because Amazon lacked hardware capability. Bezos accepted those risks because learning hardware was strategically important, making Wilke's account evidence for Amazon's patient capability-building logic.

## Key Characteristics
- Runs Amazon's consumer and retail operations in the source.
- Describes Amazon's tolerance for widespread experimental failure.
- Provides the Kindle anecdote showing how Amazon intentionally learned hard new capabilities.
