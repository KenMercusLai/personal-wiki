## Overview

## Current Profile

The PR article presents Appster as a company whose founders or team use PR through clear positioning, media outreach, and guest-publishing examples. The app landing-page guide extends that marketing role into product-demand validation, app-download acquisition, lead capture, and landing-page testing.
