## Overview
[[BrendanEich]] appears in the source as the leader of [[Brave]], the creator of JavaScript, and a Mozilla and Firefox co-founder whose browser history is used to make Brave's ambitions seem credible.

## Current Profile
The article's argument depends on Eich as a founder signal. It presents his role in JavaScript, Mozilla, and Firefox as evidence that the Brave team understands browser markets and has previously helped challenge a dominant incumbent. It also attributes to him a hypothetical example in which a user shopping for a car could receive most of the value of a highly qualified advertising lead.
