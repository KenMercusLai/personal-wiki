## Overview
[[HowieXu]] is an AI executive, entrepreneur, and lecturer represented here through [[MaiYang]]'s notes from an AI-era career masterclass.

## Current Profile
The source presents Xu's career in reverse chronological order: AI leadership across three Nasdaq-listed companies, founder and CEO of TrustPath before its acquisition by Zscaler, entrepreneur in residence at Greylock, and founder or head of VMware Networking. Mai Yang identifies his current role as Chief AI and Innovation Officer at Gen Digital and describes him as a Stanford guest lecturer, but the supplied article is a secondary profile rather than a verified institutional biography.

His teaching frames AI-era career impact through `EQ × TQ × IQ`, long-horizon trust, focused attention, AI-command capacity, execution intensity, and ownership of outcomes. He also argues that organizations should redesign workflows around a platform shift instead of automating inherited steps, and that engineering efficiency matters only when it connects to business or customer results.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a cross-company career spanning corporate building, venture capital, entrepreneurship, acquisition, and public-company AI leadership to frame career advice.
- Defines career impact through the interaction of EQ, TQ, and IQ rather than technical skill alone.
- Treats trust and reputation as durable assets built through repeated action in a community.
- Emphasizes attention allocation as content production becomes cheaper and noisier.
- Advocates outcome ownership and workflow redesign over task completion and local automation.
