## Overview
[[ShaunClowes]] is represented as a growth interviewer using product curiosity and repeated causal probing to test candidate depth.

## Current Profile
The source affiliates Clowes with [[Atlassian]] and asks candidates which work and mobile applications they choose and care enough about to explain. It treats those choices as evidence of product curiosity and early-adopter behavior. Clowes also recommends repeatedly asking “why” and separating an individual's contribution from a team's result to move beyond polished but shallow experience stories.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses software choices to explore product taste and industry curiosity.
- Looks for active experimentation with new or distinctive products.
- Probes the candidate's own contribution inside team-level outcomes.
- Uses repeated causal questions to test depth beneath rehearsed narratives.

## Evidence
- Product curiosity: [[growth-interview-questions-from-atlassian-surveymonkey-gusto-and-hubspot-guest-post-at-andrewchen]] attributes the new-computer and recent-mobile-app prompts to Clowes.
- Contribution depth: [[growth-interview-questions-from-atlassian-surveymonkey-gusto-and-hubspot-guest-post-at-andrewchen]] attributes questions separating “we” from the candidate's own pivotal involvement to Clowes.
- Causal probing: [[growth-interview-questions-from-atlassian-surveymonkey-gusto-and-hubspot-guest-post-at-andrewchen]] presents repeated “why” questions as Clowes's compact method for examining decisions.
