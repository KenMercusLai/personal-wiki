## Overview
[[Groupon]] is an online deals company used in the source as an example of sharing, referrals, and daily email driving growth.

## Current Profile
The source frames Groupon as a company that made bargain discovery socially shareable. After purchases, customers could tweet, like, or share offers; friend referrals and daily bargain emails reinforced the loop.

## Key Characteristics
- Online discount and deals provider.
- Encourages post-purchase sharing.
- Uses friend referrals and daily deal emails.
- Reported in the source as growing 228% in one year.

## Evidence
- Sharing loop: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says customers can share offers after a purchase.
- Referral and email: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] lists friend referrals, multiple purchases, and daily emails as Groupon growth tactics.
