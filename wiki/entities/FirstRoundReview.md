## Overview
[[FirstRoundReview]] is the startup-operating publication represented in this wiki by founder and operator interviews about executive hiring, marketplace product-market fit, and startup scaling decisions.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, First Round Review functions as a source of practitioner startup advice. The Zappacosta source presents an operator interview rather than a research study, converting Thumbtack's experience into a process founders can use for executive role design, interviews, references, and final fit checks. The Kadakia source uses the same practitioner format for product-market fit, turning ClassPass' pivots from search to Passport to subscription into lessons about customer behavior, marketplace transactions, metrics, and founder conviction.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes startup leadership and operating advice.
- Uses founder and operator interviews as practical management sources.
- Frames Zappacosta's executive-hiring process as advice for first-time and early-stage founders.
- Frames Kadakia's ClassPass story as advice for marketplace founders seeking product-market fit.
- Supplies source context for [[ExecutiveHiring]], [[ProductMarketFit]], and adjacent startup concepts.
