## Overview
[[FirstRoundReview]] is the startup-operating publication represented in this wiki by founder and operator material about executive hiring, marketplace product-market fit, product design, content marketing, and startup scaling decisions.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, First Round Review functions as a source of practitioner startup advice. The Zappacosta source converts Thumbtack's experience into a process founders can use for executive role design, interviews, references, and final fit checks. The Kadakia source turns ClassPass' pivots from search to Passport to subscription into lessons about customer behavior, marketplace transactions, metrics, and founder conviction. The Lieb source uses Bump, Flock, Google Photos, WhatsApp, and Twitter Moments to show how teams can reduce cognitive overhead. The Coleman article broadens the publication's operating scope into marketing: Contently's experience becomes a framework for audience research, editorial quality, distribution, measurement, and experimentation.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes startup leadership and operating advice.
- Uses founder and operator interviews as practical management sources.
- Frames Zappacosta's executive-hiring process as advice for first-time and early-stage founders.
- Frames Kadakia's ClassPass story as advice for marketplace founders seeking product-market fit.
- Frames Lieb's Bump/Flock/Google Photos story as advice for product teams designing for cognitive simplicity.
- Frames Coleman's Contently experience as a content-marketing operating system for startups.
- Supplies source context for [[ExecutiveHiring]], [[ProductMarketFit]], [[CognitiveOverheadInProductDesign]], and [[ContentLedAcquisition]].
