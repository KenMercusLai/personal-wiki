## Overview
[[PriceIntelligently]] is the SaaS pricing publisher behind the discounting source.

## Current Profile
The source presents [[PriceIntelligently]] as a data-oriented pricing voice for SaaS companies. Its role in the wiki is not as a product case study but as the source of a specific claim set about [[SaaSDiscounting]], [[SaaSPricing]], [[CustomerAcquisitionCost]], and [[CustomerLifetimeValue]].

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes practical SaaS pricing guidance.
- Uses company-level and unit-economics examples to argue about discounting.
- Treats pricing as a patient strategic process rather than a quick acquisition lever.

## Evidence
