## Overview
[[IanCartwright]] is a coauthor of the Legacy Mimic pattern article on Martin Fowler's site.

## Current Profile
In the ingested source, Cartwright contributes to practitioner guidance on incremental legacy displacement. The article frames [[LegacyMimic]] as a temporary compatibility pattern for replacing legacy systems while business processes continue to run across old and new components.

## Key Characteristics
