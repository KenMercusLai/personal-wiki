## Overview
[[MikeKrieger]] is represented as [[Instagram]]'s co-founder and CTO and as the first-time manager who led its engineering organization from a small generalist team into a multi-layer group of more than 300 engineers.

## Current Profile
In the 2017 [[FirstRoundReview]] interview, Krieger turns Instagram's growth into stage-sensitive engineering-management advice. He favors curious generalists and minimal coordination in the earliest phase, specialists and standards once platform or codebase demands exceed that model, early one-on-ones and deliberate management capacity, platform teams as a culture-forming intermediate structure, and product teams when functional staffing produces conflict and fragmented prioritization. He also describes coaching and candid learning from experienced managers as essential to his own transition.

## Key Characteristics
- Prioritizes goal-directed pragmatism over technical work pursued for its own sake.
- Treats curiosity and cross-disciplinary learning as core early-engineer signals.
- Regards early diversity work as path-dependent rather than deferrable.
- Accepts specialization while preserving the leverage of broad end-to-end knowledge.
- Advocates management behavior before formal management layers.
- Uses coaching, mentoring, and experienced hires to compensate for first-time leadership gaps.
- Treats organization structure as stage-sensitive infrastructure for product quality.

## Evidence
- Early-team judgment: [[instagram-co-founder-mike-krieger-on-engineering-team-growth-first-round-review]] describes Krieger's use of side-project prompts and practical work trials to identify curious, pragmatic generalists.
- Diversity and specialization: [[instagram-co-founder-mike-krieger-on-engineering-team-growth-first-round-review]] records his regret about late diversity emphasis and his later acceptance of specialists, standards, and focused platform expertise.
- Management development: [[instagram-co-founder-mike-krieger-on-engineering-team-growth-first-round-review]] describes delayed one-on-ones, internal and external manager selection, recurring role-fit checks, executive coaching, and learning from experienced reports.
- Structural evolution: [[instagram-co-founder-mike-krieger-on-engineering-team-growth-first-round-review]] attributes distinct value to generalist, platform, and product-team stages and says the product-team transition happened too late.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one retrospective interview published in 2017. It emphasizes Krieger's lessons rather than employee perspectives or comparative outcome data, and Instagram's acquisition by Facebook supplied recruiting, coaching, and management resources that many independent startups do not have. Headcount growth and product reach do not by themselves prove that every organizational choice was optimal.
