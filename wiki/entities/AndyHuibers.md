## Overview
[[AndyHuibers]] is presented as a [[Bump]] cofounder and close collaborator of [[DavidLieb]].

## Current Profile
The source uses Andy primarily to illustrate persistence, humor, and cofounder care under startup pressure. Lieb says he originally sent the Bump idea to Andy, recalls Andy's Dutch proverb about asking, describes a Halloween prank centered on Andy's distinctive habits, and later uses Andy washing his hair after an injury as the emotional proof behind the cofounder-selection lesson.

## Key Characteristics
- Early recipient of Lieb's original Bump idea.
- Associated with a persistence norm around asking despite likely rejection.
- Serves as a concrete example of cofounder closeness and care.
- Appears in the source's humor and culture stories as well as its survival stories.
