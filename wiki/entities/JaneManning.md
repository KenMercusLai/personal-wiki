## Overview
[[JaneManning]] is the product manager in the source's [[GoogleAdWords]] launch case.

## Current Profile
The source presents Manning as a young engineering manager asked to act as product manager for the AdWords effort when it faced resistance from Google's sales and engineering teams. Her case shows product management as objection handling and solution design: she listened to stakeholders' fears, enlisted respected engineering support, and helped shape an ad-placement formula that protected search quality while enabling self-service advertising.

## Key Characteristics
- Served as product manager for the early AdWords effort.
- Investigated sales and engineering objections before advocating a solution.
- Helped differentiate self-service side ads from salesperson-sold top ads.
- Supported an ad-ranking formula that combined price with click-through performance.
- Later returned to Google and worked with YouTube according to the source.

## Evidence
- PM assignment: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Manning was asked to serve as PM to get AdWords moving.
- Objection discovery: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says she sat with concerned people to understand advertising discomfort and cannibalization fears.
- Engineering coalition: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says she persuaded Georges Harik, who helped bring along engineers.
- Product design: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says the solution placed AdWords ads on the side and ranked them by price times performance.
- Launch work: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Manning wrote the first AdWords spec and worked side by side with engineers to launch.
