## Overview
[[JeffWeiner]] is presented as a LinkedIn operator whose scaling advice covers recruiting systems, culture reinforcement, all-hands communication, coaching, and product-led leadership.

## Current Profile
The source uses Weiner to show how the machinery behind company growth changes by scale. Recruiting moves from one person's job to a specialized organization, culture must be lived and reinforced through systems, communication scales through global all-hands, and leaders must coach people to solve problems instead of solving every problem themselves.

## Key Characteristics
- Maps recruiting complexity from 15 to 150 to 1,500 employees.
- Treats culture as a collective personality that must be practiced and reinforced.
- Uses all-hands meetings to repeat priorities and surface problems.
- Shifts leadership from problem solving to coaching.
- Argues that valuable Silicon Valley CEOs tend to have strong product sense.
