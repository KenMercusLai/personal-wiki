## Overview
[[CommitToVote]] was a Hillary Clinton campaign application for voter commitment and primary or caucus information.

## Current Profile
The source presents [[CommitToVote]] as a public-facing [[OfficialCampaignTechnology]] product that helped supporters pledge support and find practical voting information. It used Geo IP data from the campaign's CDN to infer a user's state and supported polling or caucus lookup in many states. The screenshot shows a mobile-friendly lookup result with map and location details, making the product more than a symbolic pledge surface.

## Key Characteristics
- Combined supporter commitment with voter education.
- Helped voters find polling or caucus locations.
- Used CDN Geo IP data to infer state.
- Served field and organizing goals through a public self-service product.
- Reached more than 574,000 polling or caucus lookups by the article date.

## Evidence
- Product purpose: [[a-first-peek-behind-the-scenes-of-hillary-clintons-technology-operation]] describes the app as letting voters commit to Hillary Clinton and access voting information.
- Location lookup: [[a-first-peek-behind-the-scenes-of-hillary-clintons-technology-operation]] says the app helped more than 574,000 voters find a polling or caucus location.
- Geo IP: [[a-first-peek-behind-the-scenes-of-hillary-clintons-technology-operation]] says the campaign used CDN Geo IP data to determine state.
- Mobile result: [[a-first-peek-behind-the-scenes-of-hillary-clintons-technology-operation]] includes screenshots showing a phone-based lookup flow with map and place details.

## Qualifications
The source reports usage volume but does not quantify turnout effect, accuracy rates, state coverage, or how the tool compared with volunteer voter-information sites.
