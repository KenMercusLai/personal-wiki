## Overview
[[AndreyAzimov]] is an independent product maker represented here through his 2018 decision to leave employment for a one-year profitability experiment.

## Current Profile
Azimov set a target of $1,000 in monthly product revenue by March 2019 while keeping living expenses near $500 per month in Bali. He planned to publish progress, launch products repeatedly, and begin with Sheet 2 Site; a May 2019 update reports that he launched seven apps, reached the revenue goal, and became Product Hunt's Maker of the Year. This profile is limited to his own retrospective article and does not independently verify the award, revenue path, or later career.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses explicit revenue, expense, and time constraints to define an independent-work experiment.
- Treats public progress reporting as a commitment device for finishing and consistency.
- Favors repeated product launches as an early search for willingness to pay.
- Presents limited coding experience as something to address through scoped delivery and learning.
- Grounds the decision partly in a low personal cost base and a tolerable worst-case fallback.

## Evidence
- Commitment design: [[i-quit-my-job-and-i-have-one-year-to-get-to-profitability]] specifies a one-year period, $1,000 monthly revenue target, and roughly $500 monthly expense target.
- Shipping strategy: [[i-quit-my-job-and-i-have-one-year-to-get-to-profitability]] says Azimov would launch products full-time and move from Sheet 2 Site to another idea if demand did not appear.
- Public accountability: [[i-quit-my-job-and-i-have-one-year-to-get-to-profitability]] says open goals and revenue reporting were intended to counter inconsistency and unfinished work.
- Reported outcome: [[i-quit-my-job-and-i-have-one-year-to-get-to-profitability]] adds in May 2019 that seven apps were launched and the goal was achieved.

## Qualifications
The evidence is a first-person success narrative with a short later update. It provides no complete product list, monthly revenue series, costs beyond the planned personal budget, hours, failed-launch detail, or independent verification, so it cannot establish which behaviors caused the result or how typical the path is.
