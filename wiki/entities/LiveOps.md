## Overview
[[LiveOps]] is a virtual call-center company used by [[LloydTabb]] as a case in finding operational proxies for service quality and interpreting anomalous customer interactions.

## Current Profile
In Tabb's account, LiveOps used independent-contractor agents who chose whether to work. Call length did not reliably indicate quality and extra sales predicted revenue rather than service, while attendance reportedly served as a proxy for accountability. The company then routed calls toward agents who consistently did what they had committed to do. The source also describes listening to statistically unusual calls, including long calls that proved engaging rather than defective, to prevent an aggregate metric from being interpreted without context.

## Key Characteristics
- Operated a virtual call-center model with independent-contractor agents in the reported period.
- Supplied a case where an upstream reliability behavior was more informative than obvious call-level measures.
- Used attendance as a reported proxy for accountability and an input to call routing.
- Allowed anomalous call data to be investigated through direct listening rather than assumed to indicate failure.

## Evidence
- Proxy search: [[im-sorry-but-those-are-vanity-metrics-first-round-review]] says call duration and extra sales failed to capture service quality adequately.
- Attendance signal: [[im-sorry-but-those-are-vanity-metrics-first-round-review]] reports that showing up as promised was the best available indicator of agent performance.
- Routing outcome: [[im-sorry-but-those-are-vanity-metrics-first-round-review]] says routing calls to the most reliable agents increased client revenue.
- Outlier interpretation: [[im-sorry-but-those-are-vanity-metrics-first-round-review]] describes a long-call agent whose slower speech coincided with unusually engaged customers.

## Qualifications
The profile is limited to Tabb's retrospective anecdotes and does not describe LiveOps' broader products, customers, labor conditions, present ownership, or current operations. No sample, model, timeframe, revenue magnitude, customer-quality measure, or alternative explanation is disclosed. Attendance may correlate with accountability without fully representing call quality, and routing work toward historically reliable contractors can create selection, opportunity, workload, and fairness effects not discussed in the source.
