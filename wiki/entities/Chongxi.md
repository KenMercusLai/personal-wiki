## Overview
[[Chongxi]] is an independent blogger represented here through a first-person explanation of the design, ownership, reading, and identity principles behind a personal website.

## Current Profile
Chongxi treats the blog as an evolving personal artifact rather than a finished visual product or traffic-optimization surface. Its restrained technical-document shell draws on Swiss International Style, NASA's worm-logo-era documentation, and the atmosphere of Arknights' Lone Trail, while its content spans philosophy, technical notes, diary material, and ordinary personal reflection.

The author links design and infrastructure through respect for writing. A paper-like article surface, spacious typography, peripheral ornament, and deferred author or recommendation material protect the act of reading; an independent domain, repository-held content, exportability, and a non-algorithmic informal stream protect the record itself. The recurring “Coffee · Code · Night” motif names the desired situation: quiet, slow reading that resembles the author's own late-night writing practice.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses functional restraint and document-like visual language to keep articles dominant.
- Treats independent publishing infrastructure as practical control over personal writing rather than technical display.
- Preserves both formal essays and small informal observations outside algorithmic feeds.
- Builds identity through repeated, low-volume cues instead of large or intrusive branding.
- Expects the blog to change incrementally through continued use rather than reach a final design state.
