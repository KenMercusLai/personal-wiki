## Overview
[[FeelTheBern]] is a volunteer-built policy explanation site for [[BernieSanders]]' 2016 campaign ecosystem.

## Current Profile
POLITICO presents FeelTheBern as more than a fan site. It compiled Sanders' policy positions with heavy footnoting, attracted millions of visitors, and let volunteers convert issue pages into downloadable canvassing fliers. The project shows how volunteer software can connect online persuasion, documentation, and offline organizing.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains Sanders' policy positions across issues such as climate change and immigration.
- Uses footnoted pages to support persuasion and credibility.
- Converts online content into downloadable canvassing material.
- Represents sustained volunteer effort rather than a campaign-staff product.
