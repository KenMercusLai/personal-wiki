## Overview
[[Appcanary]] is the startup context for [[PhillipMendoncaVieira]]'s account of leaving [[Clojure]] for [[Ruby]].

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Appcanary functions as a small-team startup case where technical elegance lost to product urgency, team familiarity, and operational simplicity. The company first built in Clojure, then threw that implementation away and returned to Ruby because the team was supposed to solve a business problem rather than spend scarce time paying a tool-learning and distributed-systems tax.

## Key Characteristics
- Appcanary is presented as a time-pressed startup rather than a language-research environment.
- Its first version was built in Clojure and later discarded.
- The team returned to Ruby because it knew Ruby well.
- The case links product urgency with tool familiarity and architecture restraint.
- The source uses Appcanary to discuss developer experience without claiming Clojure is globally wrong.
