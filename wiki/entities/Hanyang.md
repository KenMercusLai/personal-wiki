## Overview
[[Hanyang]] is a writer, creator, and founder associated in the source with [[FUNES]], the podcast [[ShiShuFeng]], and a practical AI-use method for humanities and content work.

## Current Profile
The source presents Hanyang as someone using AI in real production rather than as an abstract commentator on prompt tricks. His method comes from having to produce research, writing, editing, and cultural-content workflows at scale while still preserving accountability and signature-level ownership. He frames AI as a workbench and production-line component: useful when tasks, standards, source material, and verification are made explicit, but dangerous when treated as a black box that produces finished judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Develops AI-use principles from practical production work rather than generic prompt advice.
- Emphasizes traceable, controllable, verifiable processes as the condition for publishable AI-assisted work.
- Treats humanities and cultural production as material-grounded work where AI should compress, organize, and assist rather than replace research and taste.
- Presents authorship as a final accountability test: the user should be willing to sign the result.
- Connects AI practice to podcasts, writing, and FUNES-style cultural-material workflows.

## Evidence
- Production background: [[gei-ren-wen-gong-zuo-zhe-de-ai-shi-yong-zhi-nan]] says the method grew from daily AI-supported production around FUNES, writing, and related content work.
- Process standard: [[gei-ren-wen-gong-zuo-zhe-de-ai-shi-yong-zhi-nan]] states that AI use should be traceable, supervisable, verifiable, and controllable.
- Material grounding: [[gei-ren-wen-gong-zuo-zhe-de-ai-shi-yong-zhi-nan]] argues that real archives, oral histories, field interviews, and photographed material can matter more than having the newest model.
- Authorship test: [[gei-ren-wen-gong-zuo-zhe-de-ai-shi-yong-zhi-nan]] makes willingness to sign the final work the core quality-control threshold.
- Public venue: [[gei-ren-wen-gong-zuo-zhe-de-ai-shi-yong-zhi-nan]] describes the guide as first fully discussed publicly through the Shi Shu Feng podcast.
