## Overview
[[Penny]] is represented here as a pre-revenue, US-only personal-finance app that [[MitchellLee]] and a cofounder moved from a hackathon project to 500 reported signups in 2015.

## Current Profile
Penny's early development and distribution were tightly coupled. The founders watched users connect bank accounts, corrected onboarding bugs, recruited enthusiastic research participants, drew on personal networks, and then built a polished marketing page before testing community, listing, press, and expert-outreach channels. The reported growth curve is uneven: network and community efforts produced gradual gains, while an Android Police roundup and a later launch event produced sharp jumps.

## Key Characteristics
- Consumer personal-finance app developed from a hackathon project.
- Pre-revenue and restricted to the United States at the time of the source.
- Used bank-account linking as an observable early onboarding milestone.
- Reached early users through research participants, founder networks, communities, aggregators, press, and expert outreach.
- Relied on a dedicated marketing page to establish credibility and convert external traffic.
- Reportedly reached 500 signups without paid acquisition or an initial mainstream technology-press campaign.

## Evidence
- Product state and onboarding: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] describes the first non-founder user linking a bank account after the team fixed roughly 12 observed bugs.
- Initial audience: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] says users two through ten came largely from the most enthusiastic product-research participants.
- Network growth: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] attributes the move toward 50 users to Facebook and a group chat.
- Community acquisition: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] reports about 40 signups from 700 Show HN visitors and roughly 30 users from Reddit.
- Press spike: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] attributes almost 200 signups over two days to an Android Police roundup discovered through a user inquiry.

## Qualifications
The source does not define a signup consistently with activation, bank linking, ongoing use, retention, or payment. All counts and channel attributions are founder-reported, approximate, and historical, and the article does not establish Penny's later product or company outcome. The retained chart shows temporal association rather than causal isolation.
