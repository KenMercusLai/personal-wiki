## Overview
[[JoeKennedy]] is represented in the source as the CEO who joined [[Pandora]] when founder [[TimWestergren]] moved from CEO to chief strategy officer.

## Current Profile
The article supplies only Westergren's view of Kennedy, but that view makes Kennedy a case of complementary successor leadership. Westergren says the pair recognized that they could form a stronger leadership team together and that Kennedy relieved part of the founder's burden after many financing rounds and roughly eleven years of company development.

## Key Characteristics
- Joined Pandora as CEO during a founder-role transition.
- Worked alongside Westergren rather than displacing him from the company.
- Is described by Westergren as a complementary leadership partner.
- Helped distribute the leadership burden at a later company stage.
