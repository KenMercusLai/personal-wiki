## Overview
[[ConvertKit]] is an email-marketing software company used in the source as an example of target-market focus and transparent brand positioning around creators.

## Current Profile
The source presents ConvertKit as a company that did not merely compete as a generic email tool. By aiming at creators and communicating that focus, it gained a sharper position in a crowded market where broader tools risk looking interchangeable.

## Key Characteristics
- Positioned around creators rather than all possible email-marketing users.
- Used as an example of target-market focus creating differentiation.
- Also mentioned as a transparent company in the source's list of commodity-brand traits.

## Evidence
