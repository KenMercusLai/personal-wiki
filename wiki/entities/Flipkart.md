## Overview
[[Flipkart]] appears in the corpus as the ecommerce company cited to show that a founder should not be deterred by a global giant already holding the market.

## Current Profile
The Quora answer reviewed here lists Flipkart among the businesses that started anyway, in its case against [[Amazon]], which the answer describes as already holding the world market for online shopping. The claim is counterfactual rather than evaluative: if Flipkart had accepted the funded competitor as a reason not to start, the answer says, the company would never have existed. The source gives no detail about Flipkart's market position, funding, geography, or outcome, so the corpus records the company as an entry example in its competition material rather than as an analyzed business.
