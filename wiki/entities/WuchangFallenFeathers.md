## Overview
[[WuchangFallenFeathers]] is a Chinese Soulslike game used by [[Plum]] as a retrospective case of promotional expectation exceeding the experience many players believed they had been promised.

## Current Profile
The source portrays Wuchang as a fundamentally competent, relatively hard and dark Soulslike rather than a uniformly bad game. It argues that repeated invitation-only previews, creator coverage, sexualized promotion, nationalist framing, and resistance to criticism helped audiences imagine a different product; performance complaints, genre mismatch, absent expected subject matter, and inconsistent preview reporting then produced severe backlash at launch.

## Key Characteristics
- Described as a solid but not exceptional Soulslike with some faster combat elements.
- Promoted through three dedicated offline preview events and extensive media and creator access, according to the source.
- Became associated with audience expectations about history, identity, character presentation, difficulty, and technical performance.
- Serves as an expectation-management case rather than reliable evidence that every preview participant concealed problems.

## Evidence
- Product assessment: [[plumz-14418]] calls the game a good, hard Soulslike whose story is internally coherent despite disputed expectations.
- Promotion pattern: [[plumz-14418]] reports three large dedicated preview events, scarce access, and broad creator coverage.
- Backlash account: [[plumz-14418]] links launch criticism to performance, difficulty, content expectations, and a perceived mismatch with earlier preview impressions.
- Purchase constraint: [[plumz-14418]] claims soundtrack-plus-key bundling and third-party or console purchasing could weaken ordinary refund recourse.

## Qualifications
All characteristics derive from one polemical retrospective. The source supplies no performance benchmarks, sales and refund data, contracts, preview-participant interviews, representative player survey, or independent evidence for the claimed fate of the development team.
