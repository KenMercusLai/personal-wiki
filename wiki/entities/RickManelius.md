## Overview
[[RickManelius]] is the author of the personal and professional development essay "Assume Positive Intent."

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Manelius appears as a reflective author using a business-conflict story to argue for trust-first collaboration. His article combines a concrete project-budget crisis, a stakeholder's positive-intent intervention, and caveats about low-trust situations, bad-faith actors, and the risk-reward tradeoff of extending trust.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from personal professional experience rather than from a formal study.
- Frames trust as a practical efficiency and collaboration tool.
- Treats positive intent as useful but bounded by safety, history, and bad-faith risk.
