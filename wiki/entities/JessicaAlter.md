## Overview
[[JessicaAlter]] is represented in the wiki as the [[FounderDating]] co-founder who created a structured discussion checklist for prospective startup co-founders.

## Current Profile
The Founder Institute article identifies Alter as a FounderDating co-founder and an entrepreneur in residence at [[SocialCapital]]. Her contribution in this source is diagnostic rather than predictive: she organizes topics that founders often leave implicit—motivation, personal constraints, culture, roles, authority, equity, and legal conflicts—and recommends supplementing conversation with references and actual joint work.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames co-founder selection as explicit mutual diligence rather than an assumption based on friendship or family history.
- Extends fit beyond skills into incentives, cash needs, time, family commitments, culture, governance, and ownership.
- Treats adaptability and conflict behavior as important partnership signals.
- Prefers observable joint work and third-party references to self-description alone.

## Evidence

- Founder-diligence scope: [[jessica-alter-34-questions-to-ask-a-potential-co-founder]] groups prompts around personalities and incentives, personal priorities, working styles and culture, and roles and responsibilities.

## Qualifications
This profile is limited to one republished practitioner checklist. It does not establish Alter's complete career history, the checklist's original publication date, FounderDating's outcomes, or whether these questions predict founder-team durability. Several prompts touch sensitive personal or legal matters and require relevance, consent, and jurisdiction-specific care.
