## Overview
[[PaulNewson]] is represented in the wiki as a Google SRE Mission Controller who documented his preparation for and response to his first primary on-call incident with the [[GoogleComputeEngine]] SRE team.

## Current Profile
Newson's account is valuable less as an expert prescription than as a transition narrative. He moved through general and service-specific training, scenario exercises, shadowing, and secondary duty before assuming primary on-call responsibility. During the incident he served as Incident Commander while consulting a more experienced secondary, illustrating that command means owning coordination and decisions without pretending to possess all relevant expertise.

He also links live response to longer-term reliability work: progressive rollout and rollback limited the incident, while a blameless postmortem produced owned actions and shared review. The profile remains bounded to one company-published 2017 article and does not establish his wider career or later views.
