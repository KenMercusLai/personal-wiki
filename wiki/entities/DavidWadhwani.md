## Overview
[[DavidWadhwani]] is represented as the former Adobe executive recruited to become [[AppDynamics]] CEO in September 2015 and lead its transition toward larger-scale subscription operation and public-company readiness.

## Current Profile
The AppDynamics board identified Wadhwani after an extended executive search and a reported four-month courtship. The source credits his Adobe subscription-model experience and says his AppDynamics leadership emphasized executive-team development, business-metric benchmarking, subscription streams, high-lifetime-value customers, and account expansion.

During AppDynamics' IPO roadshow, Wadhwani met Cisco CEO Chuck Robbins about a merger proposal. The resulting agreement was signed and announced before AppDynamics began Nasdaq trading.

## Key Characteristics
- Recruited externally after AppDynamics had already reached substantial customer and employee scale.
- Brought experience from Adobe's subscription transition.
- Built out the senior executive team.
- Emphasized measurable subscription economics and customer lifetime value.
- Led the company through simultaneous IPO preparation and acquisition negotiation.

## Evidence
- Search and appointment: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] describes Chandna's initial meeting, a four-month courtship, and Wadhwani's September 2015 start as CEO.
- Operating focus: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] attributes metric benchmarking, subscription discipline, lifetime-value focus, and executive additions to Wadhwani and the team.
- Transaction role: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] says Chuck Robbins presented the merger vision directly to Wadhwani during the IPO roadshow.

## Qualifications
The evidence is an investor's transaction-era retrospective, not an independent assessment of Wadhwani's contribution. It does not isolate his effect from the founder, board, existing team, market conditions, or earlier customer momentum, and it reports no post-acquisition result.
