## Current Profile
The wiki currently represents Wait But Why through one illustrated long-form article about career choice. In this source, the publication style combines informal prose, recurring metaphors, stick-figure diagrams, and structured frameworks to make personal decision-making more inspectable.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes extended explanatory essays that turn abstract life decisions into concrete diagrams and named concepts.
- Uses humor and visual metaphors alongside serious self-analysis.
- Connects personal reflection with broader claims about society, conventional wisdom, and changing career landscapes.
