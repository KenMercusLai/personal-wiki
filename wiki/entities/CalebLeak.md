## Overview
[[CalebLeak]] is a software practitioner represented here through a first-person experiment combining coding agents, game development, hardware integration, and animal training.

## Current Profile
The source presents Leak as a former Meta research engineer who built a deliberately absurd but technically substantive game-generation system after his role was eliminated. His contribution is the surrounding system rather than the dog's input: he selected Godot, designed the prompt and constraints, built [[DogKeyboard]], connected the Raspberry Pi and feeder, added visual and interactive testing, and open-sourced the resulting tools and game.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds across software, hardware, networking, automation, and game-development boundaries.
- Treats coding-agent performance as a feedback and tooling problem rather than only a prompting problem.
- Uses playful projects to expose practical reliability limits such as broken UI, missing input, stale memory, and unwinnable levels.
- Publishes the described tools and game artifacts as open-source projects.

## Evidence
- System builder: [[i-taught-my-dog-to-vibe-code-games-caleb-leak]] attributes the prompt, DogKeyboard app, Raspberry Pi routing, feeder integration, testing tools, and game workflow to Leak.
- Practitioner context: [[i-taught-my-dog-to-vibe-code-games-caleb-leak]] says his Meta research-engineer role was eliminated in January 2026 and the project followed during that transition.
- Verification emphasis: [[i-taught-my-dog-to-vibe-code-games-caleb-leak]] says screenshot, scripted-input, scene-lint, shader-lint, and input-mapping tools were the largest quality lever.
- Open publication: [[i-taught-my-dog-to-vibe-code-games-caleb-leak]] links public repositories for DogKeyboard, TeaLeaves, and Quasar Saz releases.

## Qualifications
This profile comes from one self-authored project retrospective, not an independent biography or evaluation. The source does not isolate the effect of each tool, provide aggregate defect rates, or establish that the workflow transfers to consequential production software.
