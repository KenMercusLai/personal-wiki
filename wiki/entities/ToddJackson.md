## Overview
[[ToddJackson]] is a product leader represented in this wiki through a [[FirstRoundReview]] interview about defining, evaluating, and closing product-manager candidates.

## Current Profile
Jackson describes [[ProductManagement]] as articulating a winning product, rallying the team to build it, and iterating until it is right. Drawing on work across Google, Facebook, Twitter, and his own acquired startup, he turns that model into a hiring framework with stage-sensitive capabilities, varied candidate backgrounds, cross-functional interviews, a panel presentation, and candidate-specific closing.

His leadership model depends on influence and service: candidates should diagnose disagreements without blame, lead by example, accept necessary operational work, organize complex reasoning, and communicate under questioning. His closing advice is similarly individualized—identify what most motivates the candidate and explain truthfully how the role will provide it.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines product management through product vision, cross-functional mobilization, and iteration.
- Treats synthesis, communication, leadership, and organizational effectiveness as baseline PM capabilities.
- Accepts varied routes into product while looking for concrete evidence of building, technical learning, user concern, and influence.
- Uses staged interviews and a product-analysis presentation to observe reasoning and communication.
- Treats recruiting as reciprocal selling and tailors the close to the candidate's strongest motivation.
