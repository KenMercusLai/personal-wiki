## Overview
[[MichaelRothrock]] is presented in the source as an experienced software engineer whose Trust Topology work studies reliability in autonomous AI-agent coding pipelines.

## Current Profile
The source attributes to Rothrock a 97-day empirical study across eight projects, 5,109 gate checks, and 1,450 explicit rejections. His central claim is that agent reliability is not mainly a model property but an arrangement property: systems become dependable through layered deterministic checks, probabilistic reviewers, human escalation, and feedback patterns that migrate into stronger gates over time.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies agent reliability through empirical pipeline data.
- Defines reliability as a property of orchestration and verification arrangement.
- Classifies agent failures into structured categories such as omission, systematic error, and incoherence.
- Advocates larger or better verifiers rather than only larger generators.
