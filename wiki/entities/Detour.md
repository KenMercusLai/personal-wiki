## Overview
[[Detour]] is represented as the startup for which [[AndrewMason]] introduced [[ProgressiveEquity]], a precommitted program for redistributing part of exceptional employee-equity gains at a major liquidity event.

## Current Profile
The source describes Detour primarily through one compensation experiment rather than its product or operating history. Its proposed employee pool was divided equally between normal and progressive RSUs. The progressive portion stopped appreciating at half of a company-defined financial-independence threshold, feeding released value into a kicker pool for eligible employees at a large secondary, sale, or IPO. Detour also made the legal template public, but the post does not disclose the actual threshold or show that the company completed a redistribution.

## Key Characteristics
- Used an ex-ante equity rule to address the distribution of an exceptional startup outcome.
- Split employee grants between ordinary and capped progressive RSUs.
- Defined a separate kicker pool for redistributed value.
- Kept investors outside the program and made employee participation mandatory.
- Published program paperwork for external reuse and improvement.
