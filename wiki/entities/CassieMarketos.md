## Overview
[[CassieMarketos]] is presented as [[Kickstarter]]'s first employee, whose early work spanned customer service, project approval, creator support, editorial writing, newsletter curation, and project management.

## Current Profile
Marketos joined because she trusted the founders' commitment to helping people make creative work, not because she had evaluated Kickstarter as a likely startup success. Her broad, largely untitled role helped translate that mission into close creator support, visible examples, editorial choices, and emerging community norms. Looking back, she pairs pride in that intuitive community work with a candid account of stalled development: unclear goals, little feedback, reluctance to pursue management, and her own inexperience made it difficult to identify and address structural problems. Her later operating stance emphasizes explicit feedback needs, kind but direct conflict resolution, workplace self-advocacy, and a sense of self that can survive organizational failure.

## Key Characteristics
- Kickstarter's first employee in the interview's account.
- Literature graduate and former music-company and concert-venue worker rather than a conventional startup recruit.
- Early community operator who listened closely, supported creators, and curated projects through mission and storytelling judgment.
- Generalist whose role moved among support, editorial work, newsletters, project approval, and project management.
- Retrospective advocate for candid feedback, managing up, and separating personal worth from workplace structure.

## Evidence
- Entry and motivation: [[employee-1-kickstarter]] says Yancey Strickler's sincerity about enabling creative work persuaded Marketos to join within days, before the company had funding.
- Community practice: [[employee-1-kickstarter]] describes close contact with early creators, selection of exemplary projects, and later creators using those examples as templates.
- Editorial judgment: [[employee-1-kickstarter]] says compelling storytelling and representation of attainable project scales guided newsletter selection more than money raised.
- Role breadth: [[employee-1-kickstarter]] traces her work from customer service and troubleshooting through blogging, curation, editorial management, and project management.
- Workplace learning: [[employee-1-kickstarter]] attributes part of her stalled path to unclear goals and missing feedback, while acknowledging that she did not initially know what to request or seek management responsibility.
- Later practice: [[employee-1-kickstarter]] describes asking managers for candid feedback, following up directly, and addressing repeated interpersonal tension instead of internalizing it.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one retrospective interview and represents Marketos's interpretation rather than a complete employment history or independent assessment of Kickstarter. Her community judgments were deliberately intuitive, and the source does not measure their effects or include creators who were rejected, harmed, or dissatisfied. Claims about gendered internalization are experiential observations rather than comparative research.
