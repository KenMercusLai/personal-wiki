## Overview
[[Clearbit]] appears as a flexible data-enrichment SaaS example whose related tools and API can serve sales teams, marketing teams, and developers inside the same company.

## Current Profile
The source uses Clearbit to illustrate audience-based content segmentation. Because substantially similar data-enrichment capabilities are adapted to different functional teams, the recommended content unit is the audience rather than the individual tool: each audience should receive a complete path from awareness through consideration to purchase.

## Key Characteristics
- Data-enrichment offering used by several functions within one customer organization.
- Includes tools for sales and marketing teams plus an API for developers.
- Illustrates audience-based rather than product-based content segmentation.
- Requires full-funnel content treatment for each materially different audience.

## Evidence
- Offering and users: [[content-marketing-strategy-for-multi-product-companies]] describes Clearbit tools for sales, marketing, and developer integration.
- Segmentation choice: [[content-marketing-strategy-for-multi-product-companies]] treats the underlying offer as similar enough that audience differences should organize the content plan.
- Funnel coverage: [[content-marketing-strategy-for-multi-product-companies]] recommends awareness-, consideration-, and purchase-stage content for every target audience.

## Qualifications
The source is a 2018 practitioner illustration, not a complete product history or a measured account of Clearbit's actual editorial operations. It does not establish that Clearbit used the proposed framework or publish performance outcomes by audience.
