## Overview
[[LudwigPettersson]] is represented as Stripe's first designer and its creative director in a 2016 interview about design culture, process, and collaboration.

## Current Profile
Pettersson treats design as an amplifier of sound product fundamentals rather than a substitute for them. His operating model gives designers early product involvement and broad individual ownership while using visible work in progress, critique, and team support to prevent autonomy from becoming isolation. As the team grows, he favors the smallest process change that restores a useful behavior lost to scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Joined Stripe early as its first designer and helped establish its visual direction.
- Advocates design involvement from product inception through implementation.
- Connects creative quality to sustained experimentation and hands-on execution.
- Favors individual ownership supported by early sharing and collective critique.
- Prefers minimal, problem-triggered process over comprehensive organizational machinery.
- Warns that design needs product fundamentals and inspiration beyond the digital-design echo chamber.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one company-insider interview from 2016. It captures Pettersson's stated philosophy and Stripe's reported practices at that time, not independently measured team behavior, causal product outcomes, or his later roles and views.
