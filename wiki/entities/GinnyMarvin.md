## Overview
[[GinnyMarvin]] is the Search Engine Land author who reported Google's analysis of search activity attributed to television advertisements during the 2016 Super Bowl.

## Current Profile
In the wiki's only source about her work, Marvin translates Google-supplied event data into a short account of device mix, incremental search volume, timing by game quarter, brand rankings, and Google Trends interest. Her article preserves useful aggregate figures and charts, while supplying no independent audit of Google's attribution method or downstream advertiser results.

## Key Characteristics
- Search-marketing journalist covering the interaction between television advertising, mobile devices, Google Search, YouTube, and brand attention.
- Reports platform-released measurements while distinguishing device shares, aggregate lift, timing, brand rankings, and team-related Trends data.
- Provides a historically bounded 2016 report rather than a methodological or longitudinal study.

## Evidence
- Authorship and topic: [[google-82-of-super-bowl-ad-searches-happened-on-mobile-up-from-70-search-engine-land]] identifies Marvin as the author of the February 8, 2016 Search Engine Land report.
- Reported findings: [[google-82-of-super-bowl-ad-searches-happened-on-mobile-up-from-70-search-engine-land]] attributes 82% mobile share, more than 7.5 million incremental searches, a 40% year-over-year increase, and advertiser rankings to Google.
- Visual reporting: the retained charts in [[google-82-of-super-bowl-ad-searches-happened-on-mobile-up-from-70-search-engine-land]] show relative response across game periods and rank the ten brands generating the most search volume.
