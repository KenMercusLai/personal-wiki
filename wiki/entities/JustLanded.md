## Overview
[[JustLanded]] was a paid iOS travel app designed to make airport pickups easier by combining flight, traffic, and mapping information.

## Current Profile
Launched on the [[AppStore]] in June 2012, Just Landed reportedly reached hundreds of thousands of users, received strong reviews and press attention, and was repeatedly featured by [[Apple]]. Its continuing operation depended on commercial flight data and other external services. By 2016, the user mix had shifted toward professional drivers, airline workers, and travel companies whose high data consumption exposed a mismatch between variable service cost and one-time purchase revenue. [[JonGrall]] removed the app from sale in February 2016 and scheduled server shutdown for July 31, 2016.

## Key Characteristics
- Combined flight, traffic, and mapping data for airport pickup coordination.
- Distributed as a one-time paid iOS app.
- Reached meaningful usage and recognition without establishing durable economics.
- Depended on concentrated flight-data suppliers and replaceable supporting services.
- Experienced 100–500-times usage differences between professional and casual users, according to its developer.
- Was wound down through a five-month transition rather than an immediate cutoff.

## Evidence
- Product and adoption: [[just-landed-is-shutting-down-jon-grall-medium]] reports a June 2012 launch, hundreds of thousands of users, positive reviews, press attention, and App Store featuring.
- Dependency structure: [[just-landed-is-shutting-down-jon-grall-medium]] says flight data was impractical for the small team to source and normalize directly from airlines.
- Economic mismatch: [[just-landed-is-shutting-down-jon-grall-medium]] reports that professional cohorts consumed 100–500 times the annual flight data of casual users under pay-once pricing.
- Wind-down: [[just-landed-is-shutting-down-jon-grall-medium]] gives February 2016 removal from sale and a July 31, 2016 server shutdown date.
