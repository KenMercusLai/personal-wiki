## Overview
[[Drift]] is a software company used in the sources as both an example of [[CategoryCreation]] through the "conversational marketing" frame and the publisher context for a [[PatGrady]] interview after [[SequoiaCapital]] participated in its Series B financing.

## Current Profile
The differentiation source presents Drift as entering a crowded live-chat market and avoiding a feature-by-feature comparison by creating a new category label. The Grady interview source adds a funding and thought-leadership context: Drift used a Sequoia investor interview to connect its own market with broader startup advice about durable problems, category definition, culture, and scaling discipline.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered a crowded market with many live-chat alternatives.
- Used "conversational marketing" as a category frame.
- Associated category creation with stronger differentiation than feature claims.
- Connected the strategy to visible marketing leadership through Dave Gerhardt.
- Published a Sequoia investor interview after Grady participated in its Series B round.

## Evidence
- Crowded entry: [[cha-yi-hua-zhan-lue-zhi-nan-what-why-yu-how-36kr]] says Drift entered an already crowded real-time chat market.
- Category frame: [[cha-yi-hua-zhan-lue-zhi-nan-what-why-yu-how-36kr]] says Drift positioned around "对话式营销" rather than a single different feature.
- Leadership quote: [[cha-yi-hua-zhan-lue-zhi-nan-what-why-yu-how-36kr]] cites Dave Gerhardt on needing to create a category people would associate with Drift.
- Personal brand: [[cha-yi-hua-zhan-lue-zhi-nan-what-why-yu-how-36kr]] later uses Gerhardt's public presence as an example of personal-brand differentiation.
- Investor context: [[8-lessons-from-one-of-silicon-valleys-top-vcs-drift]] says [[PatGrady]] participated in Drift's Series B round and was interviewed by the company.
- Thought-leadership context: [[8-lessons-from-one-of-silicon-valleys-top-vcs-drift]] uses Drift's platform to present startup advice on category definition, first principles, market entry, and scale.

## Qualifications
The sources treat Drift as a positive category or publisher example but do not analyze its long-term business outcomes, whether the category frame alone caused growth, or how much Sequoia's investment affected the company.
