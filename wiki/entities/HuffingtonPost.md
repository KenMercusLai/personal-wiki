## Overview
[[HuffingtonPost]] is presented here as the online news and blogging platform co-founded by [[AriannaHuffington]] and used in the interview as a case for both nonstop media pressure and attempted burnout prevention.

## Current Profile
The source describes Huffington Post as beginning at Huffington's kitchen table in 2005 and later being sold to AOL for $315 million, with Huffington remaining in leadership. By 2016, the company is described as a 24-hour news organization with editions in 15 countries and a mission to inform, inspire, entertain, and empower. The same always-on news cycle creates temptation for editors, reporters, and engineers to match nonstop demand, so the interview emphasizes nap rooms, email norms, and vacation-email deletion as organizational countermeasures.

## Key Characteristics
- Online news and blogging platform co-founded by [[AriannaHuffington]] in 2005.
- Sold to AOL for $315 million while Huffington stayed in leadership.
- Operated as a 24-hour news organization with editions in 15 countries.
