## Overview
[[GroupMe]] is used as an example of a messaging product that first reaches recipients through SMS and then invites them into the fuller app experience.

## Current Profile
The source presents GroupMe as a first-message viral mechanism: the product uses an existing messaging channel to deliver the initial interaction, then includes an upsell toward the product's own channel. This works only if the in-product messaging experience is meaningfully better than staying on SMS or email.

## Key Characteristics
- Messaging app example.
- Uses existing SMS delivery for initial exposure.
- Depends on a differentiated in-product messaging experience.

## Evidence
- First-message channel: [[9-ways-to-build-virality-into-your-product-gabor-cselle-medium]] says GroupMe initially sent messages over SMS.
- Upsell path: [[9-ways-to-build-virality-into-your-product-gabor-cselle-medium]] says those messages included an upsell to the full experience.
- Limitation: [[9-ways-to-build-virality-into-your-product-gabor-cselle-medium]] warns that this strategy annoys users if the product is not better than the existing channel.

## Qualifications
The source provides a brief mechanism example and does not analyze GroupMe's retention, acquisition metrics, or later ownership.

## What Changed
- Created GroupMe as a first-message onboarding and messaging-virality example.
