## Overview
[[MobiWirelessManagement]] is an Indianapolis-based company managing mobile accounts for clients, used in the source as an extreme shared-leadership case with five CEOs or managing partners.

## Current Profile
The article says Mobi launched in 2008 with five high-school friends as CEOs and grew from the earlier [[BluefishWirelessManagement]] business. Since 2009, the article reports that Mobi revenue rose 600%, managed wireless lines grew 78%, and the two companies together employed 130 people.

## Key Characteristics
- Managed mobile accounts for clients.
- Operated with five leaders rather than one or two.
- Used departmental ownership across new business marketing, IT, finance, HR, and business development.
- Relied on weekly roundtable strategy meetings and open debate.
- Presented conflict as healthy when leaders ultimately support the shared decision.

## Evidence
- Origin: [[co-ceos-are-two-better-than-one]] says Mobi launched in 2008 from leaders who had previously built Bluefish Wireless Management.
- Growth: [[co-ceos-are-two-better-than-one]] reports 600% revenue growth since 2009 and 78% growth in managed wireless lines.
- Operating model: [[co-ceos-are-two-better-than-one]] describes departmental ownership and weekly roundtable meetings among the five leaders.

## Qualifications
The source treats Mobi as a success case but does not provide audited financials, client retention, governance documents, or succession details. Because Mobi had five top leaders, it should be used as evidence about shared leadership conditions rather than a pure two-person co-CEO structure.
