## Overview
[[DollarShaveClub]] is a direct-to-consumer shaving and personal-care company founded and led by [[MichaelDubin]], represented in the sources as both a viral-video acquisition case and a scaled consumer-brand challenger.

## Current Profile
The sources jointly show a broader system than the viral launch story alone. Founder-led comedy made a simple razor subscription memorable, but the later investor retrospective attributes scale to low-churn repeat purchasing, value and convenience, strong margins, direct customer data, reliable software, new-product adoption, customer service, and an explicit ambition to become a men's lifestyle brand. The author reports revenue rising from $7 million in year one to more than $150 million in year four, more than three million customers, and over 15% of U.S. men's razor-cartridge share before [[Unilever]] acquired the company.

## Key Characteristics
- Direct-to-consumer subscription business that began with razors and expanded toward personal care.
- Used founder-led comedy and conversation to challenge broadcast-dependent incumbents.
- Combined brand, product, software, customer data, service, and fulfillment in one operating model.
- Reportedly grew from $7 million of first-year revenue to more than $150 million in year four.
- Served more than three million people and reportedly exceeded 15% of U.S. men's razor-cartridge share.
- Pursued a lifestyle-brand vision in which razors were an entry category rather than the endpoint.

## Evidence
- Viral acquisition: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says the first founder-led comedy video reached 19 million views and made the subscription memorable.
- Operating model: [[dollar-shave-club-how-michael-dubin-created-a-massively-successful-company-and-re-defined-cpg]] links direct customer relationships to usage data, product refinement, service, value, convenience, and margin expansion.
- Scale: [[dollar-shave-club-how-michael-dubin-created-a-massively-successful-company-and-re-defined-cpg]] reports the four-year revenue sequence, customer count, and cartridge-share estimate.
- Category expansion: [[dollar-shave-club-how-michael-dubin-created-a-massively-successful-company-and-re-defined-cpg]] and its retained vision slide show an intended move from subscription razors to online-only personal-care and a lifestyle brand.

## Qualifications
The sources do not isolate the launch video's effect from pricing, product-market fit, paid acquisition, retail dynamics, execution, or broader category timing. The growth-hacking article's “$600 million business within three years” is not clearly identified as revenue, valuation, or another measure and conflicts with the investor retrospective if read as revenue. The later account is written by an investor who led two rounds and benefited from the acquisition; its operating figures are reported rather than independently audited here, and it omits acquisition cost, cohort retention, cash needs, acquisition price, and failure comparisons.

## What Changed
- Corrected the entity's name from “One Dollar Shave Club” to Dollar Shave Club.
- Reframed the company from a viral-video example to an integrated direct-to-consumer brand and operating system.
- Added its reported revenue, customer, market-share, product-expansion, and acquisition trajectory.
