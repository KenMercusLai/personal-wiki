## Overview
[[Coinglass]] appears in the wiki as the crypto-market data source for funding-rate heatmaps and rankings in the article.

## Current Profile
The source uses Coinglass screenshots to make funding-rate conditions visible. The inspected heatmap shows many listed coins shifting into orange zones labeled above 50% to 100% annualized funding around late February to early March 2024, and a separate ranking screenshot shows extreme positive and negative funding rates across exchanges and pairs.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides visual funding-rate market data in the article.
- Shows cross-asset and cross-date heatmap patterns rather than a single coin's yield.
- Displays ranked extreme positive and negative funding rates across exchange pairs.
- Serves as evidence that headline yields vary sharply by asset, venue, and time.

## Evidence
- Heatmap evidence: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] includes an inspected Coinglass funding-rate heatmap where many assets exceed the 50% annualized color threshold in the article's period.
- Ranking evidence: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] includes inspected highest/lowest funding-rate panels with examples such as Bybit 10000WEN/USDT at 0.6860% and OKX KISHU/USDT at -1.1964%.
- Market-regime evidence: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] uses Coinglass visuals to support the claim that bull-market funding can briefly reach hundreds or thousands percent annualized in smaller names.

## Qualifications
The screenshots are point-in-time evidence from the source and should not be read as current Coinglass data or current funding opportunities.
