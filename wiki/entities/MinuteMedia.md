## Overview
[[MinuteMedia]] is the sports-media parent company identified by [[MichalTurjeman]] as the organizational home of [[NinetyMin]] and [[TwelveUp]] in her 2016 design retrospective.

## Current Profile
Within the bounded source, MinuteMedia provides the setting for adapting a fan-participation product idea from football to American sports. The company is represented through that design assignment rather than through a broader account of its business, ownership, or later development.

## Key Characteristics
- Operated the 90min and 12up sports-media products in the source's 2016 account.
- Used an established football platform as context for designing an updated American-sports product.
- Employed a designer who did not personally match the products' sports-fan audience.

## Evidence
- Portfolio context: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] calls MinuteMedia the parent company of 90min and 12up.
- Design assignment: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] says Turjeman moved from assessing 90min to designing the new 12up product.
- Audience gap: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] frames the work as product design for sports fans by a designer with little sports knowledge.

## Qualifications
This profile is limited to one employee-authored 2016 essay and should not be treated as a current corporate history. The source gives no independent account of company strategy, organizational structure, business performance, or product outcomes.
