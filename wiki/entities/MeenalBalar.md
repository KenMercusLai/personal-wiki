## Overview
[[MeenalBalar]] is represented as a former [[Facebook]] Director of International Growth and then [[Remind]] VP of Marketing who treats growth as retention-gated, locally informed, measured, and cross-functional product work.

## Current Profile
Balar's framework begins before acquisition: teams must understand how prospective users discover a product, what prevents them from converting, and which conditions outside the interface shape their choices. Her Facebook examples include phone-only signup, prepaid-data sensitivity, feature-phone experience, local market expertise, and the discovery that apparently strong Indonesian engagement could represent spammy commerce rather than authentic value.

At Remind, she applies the same approach through shared metrics, usability observation, a lightweight mobile-web fallback, teacher invitation moments, offline school networks, and targeted notifications. She presents retention as evidence of real product value and as the gate before sustained organic or paid acquisition.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects growth strategy to local living conditions, devices, data costs, and discovery behavior.
- Treats retention and qualitative user value as prerequisites for acquisition scale.
- Combines instrumentation with direct observation to interpret what engagement actually means.
- Frames growth as shared execution across product, engineering, marketing, support, and leadership.
- Uses acquisition, activation, engagement, and virality as a linked growth sequence.
- Advocates both iterative funnel improvements and evidence-backed high-risk bets.
