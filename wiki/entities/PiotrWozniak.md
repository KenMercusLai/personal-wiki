## Overview
[[PiotrWozniak]] is the author credited for sources on goal setting, learning drive, knowledge valuation, and the philosophical idea of an [[IntrinsicallyValuableState]].

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Wozniak is represented as a learning theorist and SuperMemo-associated author whose work links educational motivation to a larger philosophy of purpose. In the goal-setting source, he argues that goals can help learners value knowledge, sustain curiosity, and avoid schooling or credential systems that turn study into painful compliance. In the intrinsically valuable state source, he extends this into a meta-goal: because science cannot currently determine whether objective intrinsic value exists, people should grow knowledge and model reality while searching for or refuting it.

His stance is qualified rather than conventional self-help. Some accomplished minds may thrive through curiosity alone, but younger learners often need humane goals and small decisions to protect their [[LearnDrive]]. At the existential level, Wozniak treats knowledge-seeking under uncertainty as more defensible than resting purpose on religion, pleasure, immortality, or arbitrary preference.

## Key Characteristics
- Associated here with theories of learning motivation, goal setting, and knowledge valuation.
- Treats curiosity and learning drive as stronger foundations than forced discipline.
- Distinguishes mature experts from younger students whose interests are still fragile.
- Recommends small, low-friction exploratory study rather than imposed dreams or painful commitments.
- Frames the search for objective intrinsic value as a scientific meta-goal for human purpose.
- Uses autobiographical philosophical reflection to connect knowledge growth with anxiety reduction and shared human orientation.

## Evidence
- Learning motivation: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] argues that goals help build love of learning by giving knowledge a valuation center.
- Qualified view of goals: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] says genius-level experts may not need explicit goals or self-discipline in the same way as younger learners.
- Humane study habits: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] recommends five to ten minutes of exploratory reading as a small decision that can seed interest.
- Abstraction over rote detail: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] uses law study to argue for general principles and big-picture thinking in unstable knowledge domains.
- Philosophical meta-goal: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] defines intrinsically valuable state as a non-instrumental target above ordinary goals.
- Knowledge under uncertainty: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] argues that current science does not determine intrinsic value, so the defensible response is to grow knowledge and model reality.
- Personal stakes: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] describes Wozniak's youth, failed attempts to interest others in the concept, and his claim that a clear meta-goal simplified his life.
