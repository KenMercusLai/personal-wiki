## Overview
[[PiotrWozniak]] is the author credited for the source on goal setting, learning drive, and the motivational value of dreams in education.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Wozniak is represented as a learning theorist and SuperMemo-associated author who argues that goals can help learners value knowledge, sustain curiosity, and avoid schooling or credential systems that turn study into painful compliance. His stance is qualified rather than absolutist: he says some accomplished minds can thrive through curiosity alone, but younger learners often need humane goals and small decisions to protect their [[LearnDrive]].

## Key Characteristics
- Associated here with theories of learning motivation, goal setting, and knowledge valuation.
- Treats curiosity and learning drive as stronger foundations than forced discipline.
- Distinguishes mature experts from younger students whose interests are still fragile.
- Recommends small, low-friction exploratory study rather than imposed dreams or painful commitments.

## Evidence
- Learning motivation: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] argues that goals help build love of learning by giving knowledge a valuation center.
- Qualified view of goals: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] says genius-level experts may not need explicit goals or self-discipline in the same way as younger learners.
- Humane study habits: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] recommends five to ten minutes of exploratory reading as a small decision that can seed interest.
- Abstraction over rote detail: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] uses law study to argue for general principles and big-picture thinking in unstable knowledge domains.
