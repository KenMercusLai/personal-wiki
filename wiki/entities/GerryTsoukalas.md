## Overview
[[GerryTsoukalas]] is a coauthor of “The AI Layoff Trap” and is identified in the paper as affiliated with Boston University.

## Current Profile
The source represents Tsoukalas through a theoretical economics model of competitive AI automation. The work argues that firms can foresee the aggregate demand consequences of layoffs yet still automate excessively because each captures its own cost saving while sharing the demand loss with rivals.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-develops the [[AutomationDemandExternality]] model for labor-replacing AI.
- Analyzes the gap between dominant-strategy firm behavior and the cooperative profit optimum.
- Distinguishes marginal corrective instruments from taxes and transfers that change payoff levels only.
- Preserves explicit limits around reemployment, spending rotation, financial markets, and interest-rate adjustment.
