## Current Profile
In a 2026 Build 03 talk, Zhao presents Loro's local-first CRDT foundation, Lody's current cross-platform coding-agent workflow, and a roadmap toward structured team context and heterogeneous multi-agent orchestration. His account combines direct product knowledge with founder advocacy.
