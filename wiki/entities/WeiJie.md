## Overview

Wei Jie (魏杰) is the author of the source and reports responsibility for the Chinese level dialogue and programming of [[WomanCommunication]].

## Current Profile

In the source, Wei Jie presents a practitioner-designed workflow for translating hundreds of puns using phonetic filtering and semantic retrieval, alongside a broader localization strategy for names and implicit gameplay cues. The profile is limited to the author's own account of this project.

## Key Characteristics

- Designed the machine-assisted pun retrieval workflow named [[MancoDB]].
- Handled Chinese level dialogue and programming for [[WomanCommunication]].
- Treats functional player experience as more important than literal preservation.
- Retains human deliberation as the final authority over machine-generated candidates.
