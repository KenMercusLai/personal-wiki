## Overview
[[WeiJie]] is the author of the translation note on using computation to localize pun-heavy dialogue in [[WomenCommunication]].

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Wei Jie appears as a translator-programmer who worked on the Chinese localization of [[WomenCommunication]], specifically Chinese level dialogue and programming. The source presents him as combining corpus search, NLP-style retrieval, and close human revision to preserve comedic and mechanical effects across languages.

## Key Characteristics
- Worked on Chinese level dialogue and programming for [[WomenCommunication]].
- Designed the [[MancoDB]] workflow to surface homophone-bearing translation candidates from large text corpora.
- Treats machine retrieval as an aid to translator judgment rather than a full replacement.
- Favors aggressive [[TranslationDomestication]] when pun structure is embedded in character names and setting assumptions.
- Analyzes localization at the level of player attention, mechanics, and discovery, not just sentence meaning.
