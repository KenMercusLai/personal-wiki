## Overview
[[AndreessenHorowitz]] is the venture-capital firm appearing in the wiki as a startup-equity debate participant, a publisher of consumer-internet market analysis, and a recommended source for startup metrics checklists.

## Current Profile
The sources use Andreessen Horowitz in four roles. In the Quora equity source, it is the institutional context for Scott Kupor's investor-side objection to extended option exercise windows. In the China livestreaming source, it is the publisher of Connie Chan's consumer-internet analysis, using Chinese mobile behavior as a possible guide to future global product patterns. In Lee's startup checklist source, it is a recommended source for metrics lists that help founders reason about making money and measuring progress. Happy Xiao adds an attributed culture claim: the firm is intensely competitive while avoiding strong emotional celebration of success.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides the venue or affiliation for the critique that prompted the response.
- Represents an investor-side perspective in a startup equity-policy debate.
- Is associated with concerns about future employee compensation and retained former-employee equity.
- Publishes consumer-internet analysis that treats China mobile products as directional signals.
- Connects market observation, product mechanics, monetization, and regulatory context in the livestreaming source.
- Provides startup metrics lists recommended as monetization and performance checklists.
- Is described second-hand as highly competitive without making emotional reward the purpose of the work.

## Evidence
- Institutional context: [[10-year-exercise-periods-make-sense-thoughts-quora]] identifies Scott Kupor at Andreessen Horowitz as the author of the post being answered.
- Investor resistance: [[10-year-exercise-periods-make-sense-thoughts-quora]] says some founders reported investors blocking moves to 10-year exercise periods.
- Counterpoint role: [[10-year-exercise-periods-make-sense-thoughts-quora]] contrasts the investor concern with Quora's market-based compensation reasoning.
- Publisher role: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] appears as an Andreessen Horowitz article by [[ConnieChan]].
- Market-analysis role: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] studies China's livestreaming boom through apps, virtual gifts, ecommerce, brands, creator economics, and regulation.
- Metrics checklist role: [[checklists-for-startups-david-lee-medium]] recommends Andreessen Horowitz's two startup metrics lists under "How to Make Money."
- Competitive culture: [[happy-xiao-leng-ku-zhi-dao]] attributes to [[MarcAndreessen]] the claim that the firm competes intensely without reacting strongly to wins.

## Qualifications
The Quora source does not claim Andreessen Horowitz as a firm uniformly opposes 10-year exercise periods. It discusses one public argument and reports broader investor resistance anecdotally. The livestreaming source is an authored market essay, so it should not be read as a complete institutional strategy or current firm view. The checklist source references a16z metrics posts but does not reproduce the metrics or validate them empirically. Happy Xiao's brief essay is a secondary paraphrase and does not establish how consistently the described competitive norm operated across the firm.
