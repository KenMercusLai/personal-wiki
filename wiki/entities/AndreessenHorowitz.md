## Overview
[[AndreessenHorowitz]] is the venture-capital firm connected in the source to Scott Kupor's critique of 10-year option exercise periods.

## Current Profile
The source uses Andreessen Horowitz primarily as the institutional context for an investor-side objection to extended option exercise windows. It does not analyze the firm broadly; its role is to surface a public argument that some investors may resist policies that let former employees retain vested equity longer.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides the venue or affiliation for the critique that prompted the response.
- Represents an investor-side perspective in a startup equity-policy debate.
- Is associated with concerns about future employee compensation and retained former-employee equity.
- Functions as a counterpoint to Quora's founder/operator argument.

## Evidence
- Institutional context: [[10-year-exercise-periods-make-sense-thoughts-quora]] identifies Scott Kupor at Andreessen Horowitz as the author of the post being answered.
