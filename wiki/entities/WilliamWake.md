## Overview
[[WilliamWake]] is represented in the wiki as the source of the INVEST acronym for evaluating [[UserStories]].

## Current Profile
The PBB article cites Wake's Extreme Programming Explored as the origin of INVEST: independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small or sized appropriately, and testable. In the wiki, his role is therefore a quality-check contribution to story writing inside the broader [[ExtremeProgramming]] and agile requirements tradition.

## Key Characteristics
- Associated with the INVEST acronym for user-story quality.
- Connected to [[ExtremeProgramming]] through Extreme Programming Explored.
- Provides a practical checklist for keeping stories useful for planning and delivery.
