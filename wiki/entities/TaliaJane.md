## Overview
[[TaliaJane]] is the author of the 2016 open letter to Yelp's CEO describing financial precarity while working in Eat24 customer support.

## Current Profile
The source presents Jane as a 25-year-old English-literature graduate who moved to the Bay Area for work near family and hoped to transfer from customer support into media work. Her account centers on the mismatch between full-time tech-adjacent employment and basic living costs: rent consumed most of her income, commute costs threatened her ability to reach work, and food insecurity made workplace snacks a survival resource. The update at the end of the source says she was let go after publication.

## Key Characteristics
- Authorial witness to lower-paid support work inside a consumer technology company.
- Worker whose budget details turn abstract wage criticism into a concrete cost-of-living case.
- Aspiring media worker initially using support work as a path into a different department.
- Public critic of the gap between office perks, executive wealth, and employee basic needs.
- Example of the employment risk attached to public worker speech.
