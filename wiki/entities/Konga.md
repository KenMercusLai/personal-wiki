## Overview
[[Konga]] is an e-commerce company identified in [[CelestineOmin]]'s account of SQL-driven customer recognition and retention work.

## Current Profile
Within the wiki's sole source, Konga is the operating context for a “customer of the week” program that selected the week's largest basket, emailed the customer a thank-you note, and attached a 2,000-naira voucher. Omin also describes a broader set of e-commerce workflows from a former role, but the source does not always explicitly attribute every workflow to Konga, so this profile does not do so.

## Key Characteristics
