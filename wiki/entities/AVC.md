## Overview
[[AVC]] is the publication context for [[FredWilson]]'s essays on investing practice and venture-market conditions.

## Current Profile
The ingested sources use AVC as the venue for both a conceptual investor framework and a data-based market reflection. One essay distinguishes active from passive investing; the other uses [[UnionSquareVentures]]' early-stage rounds to examine valuation inflation. AVC remains an authorial and publishing context here rather than a separately analyzed media business.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes investor-oriented reflections by Wilson.
- Hosts both conceptual investing arguments and portfolio-based market analysis.
- Functions here as a source venue rather than the central subject.

## Evidence
- Publication context: [[active-vs-passive-investing-avc]] is titled as an AVC article and presents Wilson's investing examples and conclusion.
- Conceptual role: [[active-vs-passive-investing-avc]] uses simple cases across public stocks, real estate, lending, angel deals, seed rounds, and venture investing to define the framework.
- Market-analysis role: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] presents Wilson's comparison of seed and Series A valuations and round sizes from USV's experience.
