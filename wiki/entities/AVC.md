## Overview
[[AVC]] is the publication context for [[FredWilson]]'s essays on investing practice, venture-market conditions, and startup experience.

## Current Profile
The ingested sources use AVC as the venue for a conceptual investor framework, a data-based market reflection, and a founder-pitch retrospective. One essay distinguishes active from passive investing, another uses [[UnionSquareVentures]]' early-stage rounds to examine valuation inflation, and a third recalls [[JeffLawson]] demonstrating [[Twilio]] during its seed pitch. AVC remains an authorial and publishing context here rather than a separately analyzed media business.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes investor-oriented reflections by Wilson.
- Hosts both conceptual investing arguments and portfolio-based market analysis.
- Preserves short first-person accounts of memorable startup and founder encounters.
- Functions here as a source venue rather than the central subject.

## Evidence
- Publication context: [[active-vs-passive-investing-avc]] is titled as an AVC article and presents Wilson's investing examples and conclusion.
- Conceptual role: [[active-vs-passive-investing-avc]] uses simple cases across public stocks, real estate, lending, angel deals, seed rounds, and venture investing to define the framework.
