## Overview
[[TaylorMonahan]] is the MyEtherWallet co-creator and author of the MyCrypto launch announcement.

## Current Profile
In the source, Monahan presents herself as the user-facing, support-heavy, product-and-company-building half of [[MyEtherWallet]]'s origin story. She describes falling in love with direct Ethereum community interaction, then being overwhelmed as ICOs, ENS, phishing, infrastructure scaling, and irreversible user mistakes turned a side project into high-stakes public infrastructure. Her response was to assemble a team, prepare a rewritten codebase, and launch [[MyCrypto]] as a more durable home for wallet development, support, education, and security.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-created [[MyEtherWallet]] with [[Kvhnuke]] as an early simple interface for Ethereum users.
- Treated user support, accessibility, misconception correction, and product education as core product work.
- Experienced the 2017 Ethereum boom as a severe support, security, infrastructure, and mental-health strain.
- Led the move from MEW as a side project toward [[MyCrypto]] as a team-backed company and brand.
- Framed the split as a risky but necessary response to the larger risk of organizational inaction.

## Evidence
- Origin role: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says MEW began with kvhnuke and tayvano and describes Monahan as the authorial "me" behind tayvano.
- User-facing work: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] emphasizes direct user conversations, accessibility fixes for blind users, and fear that a wrong product choice could lose user funds.
- Scaling strain: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] reports support rising from roughly 20 messages on a busy day to about 1,000, alongside late-night work and mental-health pressure.
- Organizational transition: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says Monahan's assembled team would build, support, educate, and secure MyCrypto and future products.

## Qualifications
The source is Monahan's first-person account of a contentious organizational split, so it strongly represents her perspective and does not provide [[Kvhnuke]]'s full account of the dispute or later history.

## What Changed
- Added Monahan as the founder-operator behind the MyCrypto launch and MEW-to-MyCrypto organizational transition.

- [[MyCrypto]] - led the new company, brand, and product direction.

- [[Ethereum]] - supplied the early community and high-growth blockchain context for her work.
