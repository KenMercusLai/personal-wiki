## Overview
[[BharathThota]] is a coauthor of the 2018 MIT Sloan Management Review article "Demystifying Data Monetization."

## Current Profile
In the wiki's evidence, Thota contributes to a strategy-and-operating-model account of how companies can create internal value from data and build external data businesses. The source presents a joint framework and does not isolate his individual claims or contribution.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of a framework separating internal and external [[DataMonetization]].
- Associated with a three-model spectrum for external offers: data, insight, and analytics-enabled platforms as services.
- Treats platforms, organizational design, governance, security, and privacy as part of monetization execution.

- Operating requirements: [[demystifying-data-monetization]] connects data platforms and analytics with governance, cybersecurity, privacy, and organizational capability.
