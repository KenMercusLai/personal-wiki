## Overview
[[GeorgeMusser]] appears in this wiki as the author of an Aeon essay on using [[Metaknowledge]] to improve [[WisdomOfCrowds]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Musser as a science writer translating behavioral-economics and social-cognition research into practical decision-making lessons. He uses a failed Skittles-estimation anecdote, scientific-history examples, prediction-market polls, and climate-knowledge discussion to show why crowd averages need more than raw confidence or headcount.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains technical social-science ideas through everyday examples and historical cases.
- Treats crowd wisdom as useful but fragile when common information is overcounted.
- Connects research on survey design, prediction markets, and expertise to public reasoning.
