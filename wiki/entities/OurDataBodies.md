## Overview
[[OurDataBodies]] is presented as a research project documenting low-income people's lived experiences with data collection and data-driven systems.

## Current Profile
The source cites Our Data Bodies to show that data collection is felt emotionally and materially by low-income research participants. Participants describe unease, frustration, overwhelm, fear, and anger around data collection, which supports the article's claim that privacy harms are not only hypothetical future risks. In this source, the project anchors the lived-experience side of [[PrivacyPovertyDivide]] alongside legal cases and survey statistics.

## Key Characteristics
- Documents how low-income people experience data collection.
- Captures emotional responses such as unease, frustration, fear, overwhelm, and anger.
- Supports a grounded account of privacy harm as lived vulnerability rather than abstract preference.
- Complements legal, survey, and security-usability evidence in the article.
