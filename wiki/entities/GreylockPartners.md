## Overview
[[GreylockPartners]] appears through Greylock Perspectives as a venture-capital publication context for founder advice about startup distribution, product learning, and growth.

## Current Profile
The sources use Greylock Perspectives as the venue for founder-facing strategy. [[JoshElman]]'s presentation explains how startups can use platform leverage while building independent demand; the unattributed "You Are Not Your Customer" essay argues that employees and early adopters become too fluent to represent newcomers reliably. Together they position growth as a problem of reaching users without mistaking either borrowed distribution or insider feedback for durable market understanding.

## Key Characteristics
- Venture-capital publication venue for founder advice.
- Connected to consumer startup growth, platform strategy, and customer-evidence discussion.
- Presents both platform dependence and feedback representativeness as practical founder decisions rather than purely technical issues.
