## Overview
[[BigCommerce]] is represented in the wiki as [[MitchellHarper]]'s former company and the setting for his account of raising $125 million across four venture rounds.

## Current Profile
Harper reports that BigCommerce was founded in Sydney and later moved to Austin and San Francisco. Its fundraising process involved about 150 investor pitches and approximately one acceptance per 40 rejections. He credits the resulting board and investor group to deliberate selection for personal trust, prior operating experience, geographic flexibility, future-round reserves, CEO support, understandable terms, and team treatment rather than to capital availability alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded in Sydney before establishing a presence in Austin and San Francisco.
- Reportedly raised $125 million over four venture rounds.
- Reached those rounds through a high-rejection fundraising process of about 150 pitches.
- Used investor working style, experience, reserves, location tolerance, and terms as selection criteria.
