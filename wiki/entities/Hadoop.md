## Overview
[[Hadoop]] appears in this corpus as [[XavierAmatriain]]'s example of technically important work associated with [[Yahoo]] whose commercial value was captured more visibly by outside companies.

## Current Profile
The source says Hadoop was "born at Yahoo" and contrasts Yahoo's limited business impact from the work with companies such as Cloudera and Hortonworks building businesses around it. Within this wiki, the example supports [[ResearchToProductTransfer]]: originating or advancing technology is not the same as integrating it into a product strategy and capturing its commercial value.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a research-to-commercialization example in the Yahoo diagnosis.
- Is associated by the source with Yahoo's technical research capability.
- Is contrasted with outside companies that commercialized the surrounding technology.

## Evidence
- Yahoo association: [[xavier-amatriains-answer-to-what-lessons-can-silicon-valley-tech-executives-learn-from-what-went-wrong-at-yahoo-quora]] uses Hadoop as the leading example of work from Yahoo with little claimed business impact there.
- External commercialization: [[xavier-amatriains-answer-to-what-lessons-can-silicon-valley-tech-executives-learn-from-what-went-wrong-at-yahoo-quora]] names Cloudera and Hortonworks as companies built around Hadoop.

## Qualifications
This source gives only a compressed rhetorical account and does not document Hadoop's full technical origins, Yahoo's precise contributions, its internal use, licensing and ecosystem dynamics, or the financial outcomes of Cloudera and Hortonworks. The claim should not be read as a complete history of the project.
