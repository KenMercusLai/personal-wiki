## Overview
[[MarcoZappacosta]] is the co-founder and CEO of [[Thumbtack]] whose First Round Review interview contributes a founder-led process for executive hiring.

## Current Profile
The source presents Zappacosta as a first-time founder who initially wanted to reinvent many aspects of company-building, then learned that experienced executives could be more valuable than founder improvisation for scaling leadership functions. His executive-hiring practice centers on role focus, fit conversations, extensive reference checks, and reciprocal vulnerability through 360-degree feedback.

## Key Characteristics
- First-time founder who had to learn senior executive evaluation as Thumbtack scaled.
- Treats experienced executives as company accelerators rather than threats to founder authority.
- Narrows executive searches around three essential strengths instead of broad laundry-list requirements.
- Uses references as the primary skill-validation mechanism for senior hires.
- Tests executive fit through ambition, motivation, failure, self-awareness, and vulnerability.
- Makes final evaluation reciprocal by sharing his own 360-degree feedback.

## Evidence
- Founder learning curve: [[assembling-an-executive-leadership-team-is-daunting-let-thumbtacks-ceo-help-first-round-review]] says Zappacosta launched Thumbtack after college, made mistakes in executive hiring, and later realized the value of an experienced executive team.
- Role focus: [[assembling-an-executive-leadership-team-is-daunting-let-thumbtacks-ceo-help-first-round-review]] describes his practice of reducing hiring plans to the three essential skills a role most needs.
- Reference discipline: [[assembling-an-executive-leadership-team-is-daunting-let-thumbtacks-ceo-help-first-round-review]] says he may speak with 10 to 20 references and spend as much time backchanneling as interviewing.
- Reciprocal vulnerability: [[assembling-an-executive-leadership-team-is-daunting-let-thumbtacks-ceo-help-first-round-review]] describes Zappacosta presenting reference themes to candidates, sharing his own 360 review, and asking about the candidate's team feedback.

## Qualifications
The profile is drawn from a single interview and presents Zappacosta's own account of his process. It does not independently evaluate whether each executive hire succeeded or whether the same process works outside Thumbtack's startup context.
