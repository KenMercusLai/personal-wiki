## Overview
[[MitchellLee]] is represented here as a [[Penny]] cofounder documenting the consumer finance app's path from hackathon project to its first 500 signups.

## Current Profile
Lee's account joins product development and distribution rather than treating the first users as a pure marketing problem. He and his cofounder recruited close contacts and enthusiastic research participants, watched early onboarding in person, fixed friction, then moved through their network, a marketing page, technical and interest-based communities, aggregators, incidental press, and expert feedback outreach. The source is a useful operating retrospective, not a verified or complete biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Cofounded the Penny personal-finance app with a collaborator named Alex.
- Used close observation of early onboarding to connect acquisition with product repair.
- Recruited early users from research participants and personal networks before seeking broader reach.
- Treated a credible marketing page as infrastructure for external distribution.
- Reported channel-level traffic and signup estimates while acknowledging that the tactics may not generalize.

## Evidence
- Early product learning: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] says Lee and his cofounder watched the first user encounter roughly 12 bugs and fixed them during onboarding.
- Staged recruitment: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] moves from close contacts and interviewees to personal networks, communities, app listings, press, and bloggers.
- Quantified channel account: [[mitchell-lee-your-first-500-users]] reports approximate visitor, click, and signup results for Show HN, Reddit, and Android Police.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to one 2015 first-person article. It does not establish Lee's later roles, Penny's later performance, the cofounder's full identity, or independently verified acquisition and retention outcomes. The source describes signups rather than active, retained, or paying users.
