## Overview
[[SurveyMonkey]] appears as a survey-tool example for collecting and analyzing [[NetPromoterScore]] feedback across channels.

## Current Profile
One source mentions SurveyMonkey as an available NPS survey solution rather than analyzing the company in depth. A second uses it as [[ElenaVerna]]'s 2016 company affiliation while attributing open-ended estimation, strength-oriented role design, and growth-trajectory questions to her. The wiki therefore connects SurveyMonkey both to survey tooling and a source-scoped practitioner affiliation, not to a documented company-wide interview system.

## Key Characteristics
- Named as an example of an NPS survey solution.
- Supports the source's preference for purpose-built collection and analysis tooling.
- Connected to cross-channel survey collection rather than broader company strategy.
- Historical company context for Elena Verna's growth-interview practices.
