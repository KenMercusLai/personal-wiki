## Overview
[[MitchellHarper]] is represented in the wiki by a 2016 practitioner essay that translates [[FredWilson]]'s three-part CEO framework into advice on strategy communication, critical hiring, and cash discipline.

## Current Profile
Harper presents CEO effectiveness as concentration on a few compounding responsibilities rather than personal ownership of every operating detail. His model asks the CEO to explain why the strategy matters until the whole company can repeat it, personally help close scarce talent, and preserve enough cash for adverse conditions. He also favors stable strategy, forward hiring plans, person-over-resume judgment, small marketing tests before scale, and equity-oriented executive incentives.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats prioritization as the central defense against an overextended CEO role.
- Connects strategy with repeated company-wide explanation and employee role clarity.
- Keeps the CEO personally involved in recruiting positions judged critical.
- Evaluates candidates through motivation, humility, values, behavior under pressure, and near-term stage fit.
- Frames runway around bad-case survival rather than forecasted best-case growth.
- Favors measured channel experiments before aggressive sales and marketing investment.

## Evidence
- CEO priorities: [[good-ceos-do-just-3-things-mitchell-harper-medium]] adopts Wilson's vision, talent, and cash framework and supplies operating guidance for each part.
- Communication: [[good-ceos-do-just-3-things-mitchell-harper-medium]] recommends frequent all-hands repetition of strategy, purpose, progress, and each employee's connection to the plan.
- Recruiting: [[good-ceos-do-just-3-things-mitchell-harper-medium]] advises advance planning and personal CEO involvement in critical candidate outreach, meetings, and closing.
- Candidate judgment: [[good-ceos-do-just-3-things-mitchell-harper-medium]] emphasizes character and an approximately 18-month company horizon over resume prestige or distant organizational needs.
- Cash discipline: [[good-ceos-do-just-3-things-mitchell-harper-medium]] recommends adverse-case runway, controlled burn, and small channel tests before scaling spend.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from one short first-person advice essay. Its numerical thresholds and claims about CEO market knowledge are heuristics rather than comparative findings, and the source provides no company cases, financial records, hiring outcomes, or evidence that the framework is complete across industries and stages.
