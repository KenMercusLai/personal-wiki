## Overview

## Current Profile
The source credits Knapp's writing with presenting a four-to-five-day method that moves rapidly to a realistic prototype and user test instead of beginning with intensive research. His role in this evidence is transmission and process advocacy; the article does not provide a wider biography or evaluate his complete body of work.
