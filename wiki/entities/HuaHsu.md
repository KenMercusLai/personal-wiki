## Overview
[[HuaHsu]] is represented as a New Yorker staff writer whose music discovery joins social recommendation, criticism, record stores, Bandcamp, and historical inquiry.

## Current Profile
Hsu describes learning through magazines, zines, radio, friends, and record-store clerks, then tracing backward from favored artists and scenes into their lineages. In 2018 he still relied more on people, publications, stores, and Bandcamp buyer trails than streaming, while observing that easy self-publication makes choosing worthwhile paths harder.

## Key Characteristics
- Explores musical lineages and histories backward from current interests.
- Relies on friends, publications, and record-store recommendations.
- Uses Bandcamp's shared-purchase trails for adjacent discovery.
- Frames abundance as an attention and navigation problem.
