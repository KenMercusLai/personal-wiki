## Overview
[[MehdiMiremadi]] is represented in this wiki as a McKinsey principal in Chicago and coauthor of the 2015 article “Four fundamentals of workplace automation.”

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Miremadi is one of three authors presenting preliminary research on the automation potential of work activities in the United States. The article translates an activity-capability model into operational implications: partial automation requires business-process redesign, changes the allocation of expert work, and creates adoption, transition, and governance questions beyond technical feasibility.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of an activity-level workplace-automation framework.
- Identified as a McKinsey principal in Chicago at publication.
- Connects technical automation potential to operational and organizational redesign.
- Treats adoption speed as industry-specific and dependent on software, hardware, capital, regulation, and leadership adaptation.
