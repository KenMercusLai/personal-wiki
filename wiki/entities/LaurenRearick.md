## Overview
[[LaurenRearick]] is represented as founder of the music blog *The Grey Estates* and a multi-channel music discoverer.

## Current Profile
Rearick searches Bandcamp by genre tags and follows users to see what they buy. She complements that intentional browsing with Spotify Discover and Release Radar, music websites, musicians' social posts, and a continuing preference for publications that highlight small and emerging acts.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses genre tags to begin directed exploration.
- Treats other listeners' purchases as social discovery signals.
- Combines platform recommendations with blogs and musician updates.
- Values publications that consistently surface smaller acts.

## Evidence
- Tag and buyer trails: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] describes Bandcamp searches across garage rock, dreampunk, riot grrrl, and punk plus following users' purchases.
- Platform mix: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] reports use of Spotify Discover, Release Radar, and Twitter.
- Editorial discovery: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] names several large and small sites as continuing filters for emerging music.
