## Overview

Woman Communication (《女性交流》／ウーマンコミュニケーション) is a Japanese independent game built around finding accidentally spoken taboo terms in everyday dialogue.

## Current Profile

In the source's account, the game's hundreds of phonetic jokes, pun-bearing character names, and hidden overlapping-word mechanic made its Chinese version a coupled translation and game-design problem. [[MancoDB]] supported dialogue candidate retrieval, while names and guidance sequences were adapted manually to preserve player reactions.

## Key Characteristics

- Makes recognition of taboo homophones the core play action.
- Contains hundreds of puns across dialogue, title, and character names.
- Includes a hidden “Double Shot” mechanic triggered by shooting overlapping portions of two terms.
- Its Chinese localization coordinates wordplay, naming, setting, scoring cues, and interface salience.
