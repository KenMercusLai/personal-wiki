## Overview
[[DianeGreene]] is presented as a VMware operator whose scaling lessons focus on early hiring difficulty, standards during hypergrowth, and written communication.

## Current Profile
The source uses Greene to show that hiring can become easier as a company gains definition, but standards must remain high. It also uses her VMware example to show how written weekly updates can help groups understand one another as the company grows.

## Key Characteristics
- Notes that early hiring can be harder when the company idea is still poorly defined.
- Warns not to lower hiring standards during rapid growth.
- Uses written weekly updates to improve cross-team awareness.
- Treats communication as a shared organizational practice, not only a CEO broadcast.

## Evidence
- Early hiring difficulty: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Greene that VMware was harder to hire for before it was well-defined.
- Hiring volume: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] says VMware later hired more than 100 people per month while preserving standards.
