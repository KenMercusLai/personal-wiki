## Overview
[[Fabric]] appears in the source as the acquired product context that brought [[Crashlytics]] into [[Firebase]] at [[Google]].

## Current Profile
The article presents Fabric through the narrow lens of the Crashlytics migration. The team joined Firebase at Google in January 2017 as part of the acquisition, and one acquisition goal was to bring Crashlytics into Firebase. Fabric also marks the older interface baseline: inspected images contrast a darker Fabric Crashlytics dashboard with a lighter Firebase version organized around clearer statistics, trends, filters, and issue tables.

## Key Characteristics
- Was acquired by [[Google]] in the source's context.
- Brought the Crashlytics team and product into Firebase.
- Represents the pre-redesign Crashlytics surface.
- The inspected Fabric dashboard shows dense navigation, filters, crash-free user/session metrics, graphs, and an issue table that the article says suffered from unclear hierarchy.
