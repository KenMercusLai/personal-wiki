## Overview
[[AaronHoliday]] is a venture-capital author represented by a [[645Ventures]] essay on why investors miss rare breakout companies.

## Current Profile
Holiday coauthors a seven-part framework for diagnosing mistakes of omission in venture investing. The represented approach asks investors to look beyond familiar founder patterns, personal product preferences, consensus, static market boundaries, and earlier category failures when evaluating long-run upside.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of the seven-part venture-capital blind-spot framework.
- Frames missed outlier investments as a central risk under power-law returns.
- Connects investor judgment to founder diversity, target-customer evidence, and independent thinking.
- Treats market expansion and behavior change as reasons present conditions can understate future opportunity.

## Evidence
- Authorship: [[venture-capital-blind-spots-645-ventures-medium]] credits Holiday and Nnamdi Okike as coauthors.
- Return logic: [[venture-capital-blind-spots-645-ventures-medium]] uses venture-return concentration to prioritize omission risk.
- Evidence standard: [[venture-capital-blind-spots-645-ventures-medium]] recommends customer surveys, early traction, and direct observation over investor gut feel.
- Market change: [[venture-capital-blind-spots-645-ventures-medium]] distinguishes sleeper and Trojan-horse markets and argues that improved technology or preferences can reopen failed categories.
