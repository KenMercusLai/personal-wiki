## Overview
[[Xiaoice]] is a conversational chatbot cited by Botnerds as a case where the "chatbot" label fits because conversation itself is the product's purpose.

## Current Profile
The source presents Xiaoice as a China- and Japan-used conversational companion rather than a task automation bot. In Botnerds' framing, Xiaoice is valuable precisely because users want to carry on conversations with it, making it different from business or service bots whose goal is to complete work through a constrained interaction.

## Key Characteristics
- Used as a positive example of a chat-first bot.
- Presented as having large-scale adoption in China and Japan.
- Valued for ongoing conversation rather than task completion.
- Helps distinguish true chatbots from broader bot or automation products.

## Evidence
- Category fit: [[chatbots-a-misleading-term-we-should-stop-using-botnerds-medium]] says the chatbot label is appropriate when the bot exists to be chatted with.
- Scale signal: [[chatbots-a-misleading-term-we-should-stop-using-botnerds-medium]] describes Xiaoice as having tens of millions of users and billions of conversations.
- Product contrast: [[chatbots-a-misleading-term-we-should-stop-using-botnerds-medium]] contrasts Xiaoice with B2B bots that should be understood as doing work rather than providing open-ended chat.

## Qualifications
The page reflects Botnerds' 2016 description and does not independently verify Xiaoice's current ownership, user count, geographic reach, or product design.
