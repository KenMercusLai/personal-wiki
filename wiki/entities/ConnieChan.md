## Overview
[[ConnieChan]] is the author of the Andreessen Horowitz analysis on China's livestreaming boom.

## Current Profile
The source presents Chan as a consumer-internet analyst focused on how Chinese mobile behavior can preview or inform global product evolution. Her article studies livestreaming as a bundle of self-expression, entertainment, monetization, ecommerce, creator economics, and regulation rather than as a single app feature.

## Key Characteristics
- Interprets Chinese mobile products as possible early signals for global consumer internet behavior.
- Frames livestreaming through both product mechanics and cultural context.
- Connects user behavior, monetization, ecommerce, and regulation in one market analysis.
- Uses screenshots and app examples to ground abstract product claims.

## Evidence
- Authorial role: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] is credited to Chan on Andreessen Horowitz.
- China-as-roadmap frame: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] argues that China can provide a directional product roadmap for mobile-first innovation.

- Visual evidence: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] includes Inke, Taobao Live, and bedroom-studio screenshots as product examples.
