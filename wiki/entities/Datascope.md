## Overview
[[Datascope]] was a Chicago-based data-science company founded by [[DeanMalmgren]] and [[MikeStringer]] and acquired by [[IDEO]] in 2017.

## Current Profile
The article presents Datascope as the technical half of a reciprocal partnership: IDEO could use data science to extend design, while Datascope could use [[HumanCenteredDesign]] to reconsider which data is collected, how it is collected, how algorithms are built, and which human outcomes matter. Its 15-person team had worked with IDEO for four years on projects including school-bus safety and future-mobility concepts before moving into IDEO's Chicago office. The companies named their shared framing [[AugmentedIntelligence]].

## Key Characteristics
- Chicago data-science consultancy founded in 2009.
- Founded by Dean Malmgren and Mike Stringer.
- Employed a 15-person team at the time of the 2017 acquisition.
- Collaborated with IDEO for four years before being acquired.
- Framed data-science practice itself as something that can be human-centered and deliberately designed.
