## Overview
[[DougRenert]] is a cofounding partner of [[TandemCapital]] and the author of a 2016 critique of treating brief early revenue growth as a sufficient startup-success signal.

## Current Profile
Renert writes from an investor's perspective shaped by early [[YCombinator]] Demo Days, Tandem's earlier pre-seed investments, a later move into $1 million-plus seed checks, and renewed attendance at the accelerator. His argument is stage-sensitive: sustainable growth deserves promotion, but many Demo Day companies are still exploring and should be evaluated through engagement, retention, user enthusiasm, and [[UnitEconomics]] as well as reported revenue change.

## Key Characteristics
- Venture investor associated with Tandem Capital.
- Attended early YC Demo Days and returned after Tandem moved into larger seed investments.
- Critiques single-metric interpretation rather than rejecting growth as evidence.
- Emphasizes authenticity, persistence, retention, engagement, and economics when judging early traction.

## Evidence
