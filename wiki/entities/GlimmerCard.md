## Overview
[[GlimmerCard]] is an iOS app, also referred to as 吉光卡片, for turning text or lightweight mixed content into more designed shareable cards.

## Current Profile
The source presents [[GlimmerCard]] as a customer-validated design tool for people who want attractive social posts without mastering typography, spacing, color, and layout. It began from repeated social comments asking how Zhang Xiaoji made his text images, then moved through Figma mockups, WeChat user conversations, early testing, App Store launch, and overseas user feedback.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves social creators who want polished text-card layouts with less design effort.
- Was validated before launch through comments, direct chats, mockups, and early user feedback.
- Uses the Apple ecosystem partly because iOS users are expected to have stronger willingness to pay.
- Revealed internationalization and feature expansion signals from early overseas buyers.
- Turned launch revenue into proof that the purchase flow and value proposition worked.

## Qualifications
The source presents early revenue and enthusiastic feedback, but also says the first version lacked image support and that later versions still needed to satisfy more functional scenarios.
