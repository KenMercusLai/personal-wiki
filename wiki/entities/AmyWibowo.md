## Overview
[[AmyWibowo]], also writing as sailor mercury, is represented in this wiki as an engineer, artist, researcher, and educator whose career combines robotics, creative interfaces, web engineering, art-tech side projects, and illustrated computer-science zines.

## Current Profile
The source presents Wibowo's career as a non-linear but coherent path toward combining art and technology. After studying at [[MIT]], she worked on [[ReinforcementLearning]] for [[ASIMO]] at [[HondaResearchInstitute]], led garment-pattern interface research at [[IgarashiLab]], worked as a startup web developer and later as a front-end/growth engineer at [[Airbnb]], and then left full-time employment to make [[BubblesortZines]]. Her profile is also shaped by repeated encounters with harassment and sexism, which the essay treats as part of why recovering the joy of computing became a creative and political act.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines engineering, art, craft, education, and writing rather than treating them as separate career tracks.
- Uses technical projects to make abstract or difficult domains more tangible.
- Treats uncertainty as something navigated through repeated experiments and decisions.
- Experienced harassment, sexism, underestimation, and credibility denial in technical workplaces.
- Built independent educational work to make computer science more accessible and inclusive.
