## Overview
[[Hotmail]] is an email service used in the source as a classic example of a product message carrying its own acquisition prompt.

## Current Profile
The source says Hotmail appended a promotional sign-off link to outgoing emails. Because every user sent messages to non-users, ordinary product use created repeated exposure and a direct signup path.

## Key Characteristics
- Web email service.
- Used outgoing email footers as an acquisition channel.
- Represents a simple product-integrated viral loop.
