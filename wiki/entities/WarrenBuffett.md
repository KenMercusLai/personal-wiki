## Overview
[[WarrenBuffett]] appears in the source as the value-investing comparison point for long-term bets on enduring business advantages.

## Current Profile
The essay uses Buffett's decades-long ownership of GEICO as a case where many surface details changed while the underlying advantage persisted. GEICO moved from cafeteria sales to broader direct insurance on phones, and analytics advanced from primitive tools to AI, but the cost and convenience advantage of selling directly rather than through brokers remained the compounding driver.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the "betting against change" side in the source's venture-versus-value comparison.
- Uses long holding periods to benefit from enduring business advantages.
- Serves as the GEICO example of timeless cost and convenience advantage amid changing channels.
