## Overview
[[OnlineOrNot]] is the main SaaS product in [[MaxRozen]]'s 2023 retrospective and the core product run by [[ROZENMD]].

## Current Profile
The source presents OnlineOrNot as a long-running product that began publicly from a very narrow alerting form and benefited in 2023 from accumulated product, customer, and business learning. Its growth is the article's main evidence that a single focused product can compound over time.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as ROZENMD's primary business product in the source.
- Grew partly through compounding operational learning rather than a one-time launch spike.
- Converted some previously skeptical or free-tier users after the product matured.
- Shows that long-lived products can surface delayed bugs and delayed customer decisions.
- Benefited indirectly from Max Rozen's writing outside the product itself.

## Evidence
- Core business role: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] describes ROZENMD as the business that runs OnlineOrNot.
- Compounding learning: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] says fixing and improving business issues increased adoption over time.
- Delayed conversion: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] reports that some pre-launch rejecters and early free-tier users later became customers.
- Delayed reliability work: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] notes bugs that took 15 months to affect people.
- Accidental discovery: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] says a ThinkPad article led thousands of readers to search for OnlineOrNot.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide revenue, retention, churn, customer-count, or product-feature details for OnlineOrNot. Its strongest evidence is founder observation about adoption and customer return patterns.
