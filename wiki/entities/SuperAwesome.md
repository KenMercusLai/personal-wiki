## Overview
[[SuperAwesome]] is presented in the 2018 source as a London-founded kidtech company that built infrastructure for companies and apps to engage children safely online.

## Current Profile
The retrospective follows SuperAwesome from a small 2013 startup through an early acquisition with unstable physical-server infrastructure into a multi-product organization. By 2018, Wöhle reports more than 270 customers, product-specific teams, and technology used by thousands of apps to support engagement with more than half a billion children each month. That growth drove increasing specialization across architecture, engineering management, product management, talent acquisition, customer work, and executive leadership.

## Key Characteristics
- Reframed an initially perceived product problem as infrastructure for a new kidtech category.
- Absorbed early operational complexity through an acquisition running on physical hardware.
- Expanded from a small generalist team into dedicated product and specialist leadership structures.
- Served a reported global customer and application base in child-safe digital engagement.
- Used leadership-role specialization to respond to changing scaling constraints.

## Evidence
- Category and reach: [[being-a-start-up-cto-or-how-i-fired-myself-enough-times-to-finally-become-cto]] says SuperAwesome came to treat safe child engagement as an infrastructure problem and reports use by hundreds of companies and thousands of apps.
- Early infrastructure: [[being-a-start-up-cto-or-how-i-fired-myself-enough-times-to-finally-become-cto]] describes an acquired UK swapping service running on physical hardware that required cleanup and migration.
- Product scale: [[being-a-start-up-cto-or-how-i-fired-myself-enough-times-to-finally-become-cto]] reports more than 270 customers and dedicated teams for a comprehensive product suite by early 2018.

## Qualifications
The reach, customer, product, and organizational figures are company-reported in a founder's 2018 retrospective. The source does not define its measurement method, independently validate safety outcomes, or cover later ownership, strategy, or operating results.
