## Overview
[[DanPupius]] is an engineering leader represented here through his work on evidence-based, candidate-centered hiring systems at [[Medium]] and [[Range]].

## Current Profile
The source describes Pupius as a former Google engineer who became Medium's Head of Engineering in 2012 and later co-founded Range. His own near-miss in Google's hiring process and his observation of false positives and false negatives informed a hiring model built around explicit team needs, observable behavior, multiple demonstration modes, interviewer calibration, measurement, and iteration.

## Key Characteristics
- Applies product-development mechanics to hiring and team formation.
- Separates observable candidate evidence from unsupported impressions and pedigree.
- Treats candidates, including rejected candidates, as users of the hiring process.
- Favors explicit decision authority with disagreement followed by commitment.
- Uses measurement and focused retrospectives to improve a documented process.
