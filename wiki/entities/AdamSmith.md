## Overview
[[AdamSmith]] is the founder-author who uses his move from [[Xobni]] to [[Kite]] to argue for planning an entrepreneurial career beyond one startup or acquisition.

## Current Profile
Smith presents entrepreneurship as a long career with branching transitions rather than a single company followed by permanent leisure. He says Kite gave him a chance to reuse lessons learned at Xobni and take another attempt at building at scale, while his broader preference is to build a lasting company early enough to experience changing problems and compound resources over time.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-author focused on long-horizon entrepreneurial career choices.
- Connects personal experience at Xobni and Kite to a broader career map.
- Prefers durable company-building while rejecting a universal career prescription.
- Treats operating, investing, larger-company work, rest, and retirement as materially different choices.
