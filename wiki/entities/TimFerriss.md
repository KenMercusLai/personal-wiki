## Overview
[[TimFerriss]] is used by [[DariusForoux]] as an example of someone publicly associated with a new category: lifestyle entrepreneurship.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Ferriss appears as an example of category association rather than as a full biographical subject. Foroux's point is that Ferriss did not need to invent every part of the lifestyle-entrepreneurship idea; being known for popularizing the category supplied the strategic advantage.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a personal-category example in Foroux's differentiation argument.
- Illustrates public association with a category as distinct from technical invention.
- Connects entrepreneurship advice with [[CategoryCreation]] and [[PersonalBranding]].

## Evidence
- Category association: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] says Ferriss was known for popularizing lifestyle entrepreneurship through The Four Hour Work Week.
- Invention qualification: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] explicitly says invention matters less than being known for the category.
- Strategy connection: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] uses Ferriss to translate product-category logic into personal positioning.
