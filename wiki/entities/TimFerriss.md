## Overview
[[TimFerriss]] is represented as an author and media curator associated with lifestyle entrepreneurship and with collecting practical lessons from high-profile practitioners.

## Current Profile
[[DariusForoux]] uses Ferriss as an example of category association: Ferriss did not need to invent every element of lifestyle entrepreneurship for *The 4-Hour Workweek* to make him publicly identified with the category. The example distinguishes public recognition from technical invention and connects personal positioning with [[CategoryCreation]].

[[ArnoldSchwarzenegger]]'s foreword for *Tools of Titans* adds a curatorial role. It describes Ferriss's podcasts and book as channels through which people encounter routines, philosophies, training advice, motivation, and unexpected lessons from business, entertainment, and sport. Within the bounded sources, Ferriss therefore appears both as a category-associated public figure and as an organizer of other people's practical knowledge.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a personal-category example in Foroux's differentiation argument.
- Illustrates public association with a category as distinct from technical invention.
- Uses podcasts and books to curate practitioner advice across several fields.
- Connects entrepreneurship advice with [[CategoryCreation]], [[PersonalBranding]], and continued learning.

## Evidence
- Category association: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] says Ferriss became known for popularizing lifestyle entrepreneurship through *The 4-Hour Workweek*.
- Invention qualification: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] says public association with the category matters more to the example than originating every underlying idea.
- Curatorial role: [[i-am-not-a-self-made-man-facebook]] describes Ferriss's podcasts as presenting lessons from people in business, entertainment, and sport.
- Book context: [[i-am-not-a-self-made-man-facebook]] identifies the essay as Schwarzenegger's foreword for *Tools of Titans* and praises the book's collection of advice.

## Qualifications
The wiki knows Ferriss through two brief, interested references rather than through a direct study of his work. Foroux uses him instrumentally to illustrate positioning, while Schwarzenegger is contributing a foreword and praising the book in which it appears. These sources do not evaluate Ferriss's selection methods, the representativeness or reliability of his guests' advice, the outcomes of applying it, or his broader publishing and business record.

## What Changed
- Expanded the profile from category association to include Ferriss's source-scoped role as a curator of cross-domain practitioner advice.
