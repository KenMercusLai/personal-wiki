## Overview
[[CrazyEgg]] is an analytics SaaS company used in the source as an example of aligning marketing with the product feature retained customers valued.

## Current Profile
The source presents Crazy Egg as a differentiated analytics product launched in a market where [[GoogleAnalytics]] already existed. Its early access page and marketing emphasized heatmaps and click visualization because dissatisfied Google Analytics users wanted to see where visitors clicked. The retention lesson is that cheap distribution should not push generic lead chasing; it should attract users around the specific value they are likely to keep using.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides heatmap and click-visualization analytics for web pages.
- Differentiated against Google Analytics by solving a missing visual behavior-tracking need.
- Used early-access conversations with potential customers to discover the sticky feature.
- Aligned marketing copy with the feature that best-fit users wanted.
