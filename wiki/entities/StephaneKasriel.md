## Overview
[[StephaneKasriel]] appears in the wiki as an engineer-turned-executive reflecting on how he learned to lead after becoming CEO of [[Upwork]] in April 2015.

## Current Profile
Kasriel describes an early identity built around computers and programming, beginning to code at age twelve in 1980s Paris and initially expecting a code-centered career. He also describes himself as more comfortable alone or in small groups than in crowds. His development into leadership was deliberate rather than presented as a personality transformation: he pursued unfamiliar projects and roles, studied strategy and people management through books and online courses, completed an MBA at INSEAD to learn from other entrepreneurs' mistakes, and converted networking demands into specific targets. As CEO, he says his work shifted toward motivation, empowerment, resourcing, and listening before trying to solve employees' problems.

## Key Characteristics
- Engineer and early programmer whose technical identity preceded his management career.
- Introvert who uses explicit goals to make large professional gatherings manageable.
- Deliberate learner who combined stretch assignments, reading, online courses, and an MBA.
- Executive who frames leadership around employee motivation, empowerment, and resources.
- Manager learning to listen before applying an engineer's solution instinct.
