## Overview
[[Piglei]] is the author/source site behind the ingested guide on AI coding behavior for software engineers.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Piglei appears as a software-engineering practitioner voice translating workplace experience with coding agents into team norms. The source is less interested in tool-specific prompting tactics than in durable engineering behaviors: accountability, collaboration, reviewability, verification, and the development needs of junior engineers.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes practitioner guidance for software engineers using AI coding agents.
- Frames AI agents as capability extensions whose output remains human-owned.
- Emphasizes team alignment around AI coding practices to reduce collaboration friction.
- Distinguishes guidance for all engineers from guidance tailored to junior engineers.
- Connects AI coding practice to software design, maintainability, testing, and learning habits.
