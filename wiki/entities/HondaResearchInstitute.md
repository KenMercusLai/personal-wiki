## Overview
[[HondaResearchInstitute]] is the Tokyo-area research setting where [[AmyWibowo]] worked on emotion-informed [[ReinforcementLearning]] for [[ASIMO]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Honda Research Institute as Wibowo's first post-college and graduate-school workplace in Japan. Her work used ASIMO's video feed and a face-reading SDK to infer whether a human collaborator was pleased or frustrated, then used that emotional signal as reinforcement feedback for robot learning.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the source's robotics research environment.
- Connects humanoid robotics with reinforcement learning.
- Uses human emotional response as feedback rather than only game-like win/loss signals.
