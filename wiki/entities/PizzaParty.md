## Overview
[[PizzaParty]] is a planned Shopify customer-experience app described by [[AhmadIqbal]] that sends free pizzas to a merchant's VIP customers.

## Current Profile
The source presents Pizza Party as a third app idea inspired by [[Nadeef]] experiments. Merchants define VIP criteria such as order value, lifetime value, or order frequency, confirm which customers should receive a pizza, pay for the gift, and the app takes a small percentage. Iqbal says he was deliberately having more customer conversations before launch because the handwritten-card product had failed after being overbuilt before validation.

## Key Characteristics
- Automates a customer thank-you gesture for VIP customers.
- Uses merchant-defined criteria such as order value, LTV, or order frequency.
- Came from Nadeef experiments where pizza gifts reportedly produced strong customer reactions and word-of-mouth sales.
- Was halfway built and not officially launched in the source.
- Explicitly reflects learning from a prior overbuilt handwritten-card failure.

## Evidence
- Product design: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says merchants choose VIP parameters and confirm recipients.
- Customer-experience signal: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Nadeef customers reacted strongly to free pizza thank-yous and generated follow-on revenue.
- Validation caution: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Iqbal was talking to more customers before publishing the app.

## Qualifications
Pizza Party was not officially launched in the source, and the ROI and word-of-mouth claims are anecdotal founder observations rather than a completed app-performance analysis.
