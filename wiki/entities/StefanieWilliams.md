## Overview
[[StefanieWilliams]] is the author of the 2016 response essay criticizing Talia Jane's open letter and arguing for work ethic, humility, and practical service work as answers to early-career financial hardship.

## Current Profile
The source presents Williams as a nearly 30-year-old English graduate who lost an office job shortly after the 2008 Lehman Brothers crash, then moved through hostess, cocktail waitressing, and bartending work before earning enough to live in New York City and pursue screenwriting. Her authority in the essay comes from autobiographical comparison: she says she accepted lower-status work, lived at home and with roommates, commuted, missed holidays, and used restaurant income to support a creative career. She uses that history to rebuke Jane's public appeal and to frame Jane's choices as entitlement rather than evidence of structural poverty.

## Key Characteristics
- Response author addressing Talia Jane's Yelp/Eat24 open letter.
- English graduate who presents post-recession service work as her own path to stability.
- Advocate of accepting low-status or temporary jobs when preferred career paths are unavailable.
- Critic of treating independent life in an expensive city as an entitlement early in a career.
- Source of a personal-responsibility counter-frame to Jane's employee-precarity narrative.
