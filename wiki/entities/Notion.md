## Overview
[[Notion]] is presented as the author's main database tool for idea capture, source organization, draft sharing, and archived creator activity.

## Current Profile
The source uses Notion as a flexible personal-knowledge and creator-operations workspace. The author builds an inspiration database, creates an iOS Shortcut that writes to it through the Notion API, shares drafts through public Notion pages, and archives posts plus interaction data from multiple platforms into Notion databases.

## Key Characteristics
- Stores inspiration notes with titles, bodies, links, images, and later action extraction.
- Supports API-based quick capture from iOS Shortcuts.
- Serves as a public draft-sharing surface for reader testing.
- Collects archived posts and interaction data from multiple platforms for later review.
- Functions as part of the author's broader personal knowledge and creator workflow.

## Qualifications
The source reports one creator's Notion-heavy workflow. It does not claim Notion is universally best; capture speed and tool fit remain constraints.
