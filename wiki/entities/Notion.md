## Overview
[[Notion]] is presented as a flexible database workspace for creator operations and a portable digital store for a hybrid Zettelkasten.

## Current Profile
The sources use Notion as a flexible personal-knowledge and creator-operations workspace. One author builds an inspiration database, creates an iOS Shortcut that writes to it through the Notion API, shares drafts through public pages, and archives posts plus interaction data from multiple platforms. [[HerbertLui]] uses it differently: after handwritten capture became difficult to search, he transcribed numbered cards into a database with creation dates and paper-copy status. He reports occasional desktop sluggishness but accepts the compromise because a Markdown export preserved individually named note files and left migration open.

## Key Characteristics
- Stores inspiration notes with titles, bodies, links, images, and later action extraction.
- Supports API-based quick capture from iOS Shortcuts.
- Serves as a public draft-sharing surface for reader testing.
- Collects archived posts and interaction data from multiple platforms for later review.
- Functions as part of the author's broader personal knowledge and creator workflow.
- Can represent a hybrid card archive through identifiers, timestamps, permanence flags, and paper-copy status.
- Offers a source-scoped Markdown exit path that reduced one practitioner's lock-in concern.

## Qualifications
The sources report two individual workflows rather than current product testing or comparative evidence. Neither claims Notion is universally best; capture speed, desktop responsiveness, migration fidelity, collaboration, privacy, and long-term custody remain constraints. The 2021 Markdown export shows that text files were produced, not that every property, relationship, attachment, or later product behavior transfers completely.
