## Overview
[[RyanHanna]] is the former U.S. Army officer, network administrator, self-taught web developer, and [[Sworkit]] creator profiled in a 2017 [[Codecademy]] founder story.

## Current Profile
Hanna presents his career change as an applied-learning sequence. After leaving the Army, he used Codecademy's Code Year to begin web programming, then built Sworkit to solve his own difficulty finding workouts suited to variable time, space, and equipment. The project helped him move from systems engineering into mobile consulting, full-time product operation, and finally a vice-president role after [[Nexercise]] acquired Sworkit.

His advice emphasizes personal utility, simple first versions, direct user learning, patience, research, and continued construction. The profile is compelling as one possibility case, but it comes from a platform-published retrospective that does not establish how often similar learners or side projects succeed.

## Key Characteristics
- Combined Army fitness knowledge with newly learned web-development skills.
