ROZENMD is described in the source as the narrator's business in France. It runs OnlineOrNot and sells the narrator's products.

In the 2023 review, ROZENMD continued growing and did so about twice as fast as expected at the start of the year. The source attributes that result mainly to compounding learning from operating OnlineOrNot and to continued sales from a book about React useEffect.
