## Overview
[[Bear]] is a note-taking application used in the source as the rapid-capture and incubation half of a two-app personal knowledge workflow.

## Current Profile
Within [[MattGiaro]]'s workflow, Bear is an inbox rather than the permanent knowledge base. It receives mobile ideas in four broad buckets—content ideas, material to consume later, actions, and projects—and holds them for two or three days. Ideas that retain value move to [[Obsidian]] for rewriting and linking; the rest are deleted. The source praises Bear's speed and Apple-device synchronization but does not evaluate its current capabilities, reliability, portability, privacy, or comparative performance.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides low-friction idea capture across the author's Apple devices.
- Separates fleeting material from the durable linked-note archive.
- Uses four lightweight buckets for content, consumption, actions, and projects.
- Supports a short incubation period before promotion or deletion.

- Capture role: [[matt-giaro-how-to-use-obsidian-as-a-zettelkasten]] describes Bear as the fast inbox for ideas that arise away from a desktop.

- Filtering role: [[matt-giaro-how-to-use-obsidian-as-a-zettelkasten]] keeps ideas in Bear for two or three days before moving survivors into Obsidian.

## Qualifications
This profile describes one user's Apple-centered setup, not a general or current product review. The source supplies no evidence about sync accuracy, export completeness, security, privacy, cost, non-Apple access, or whether the delay improves idea quality. Similar inbox behavior could be implemented with other low-friction capture tools.
