## Overview
[[ProjectDuplo]] was Pinterest's internal initiative to rebuild its mobile website as a full-featured progressive web app.

## Current Profile
Pinterest formed the project in July 2017 by combining engineers from its web-platform and growth teams. It rolled the rewrite out first to logged-in users and then to logged-out users, reaching full logged-out deployment in February 2018 and making mobile web a central signup platform.

## Key Characteristics
- Cross-functional team spanning web-platform and growth engineering.
- Began as a from-scratch mobile-web rewrite in July 2017.
- Used staged exposure before full logged-in and logged-out releases.
- Joined technical performance work to global access and conversion goals.

## Evidence
- Team and scope: [[a-one-year-pwa-retrospective-pinterest-engineering-blog-medium]] describes the July 2017 formation of a combined web-platform and growth team.
- Rollout: [[a-one-year-pwa-retrospective-pinterest-engineering-blog-medium]] records partial and full launches for logged-in and logged-out cohorts between August 2017 and February 2018.
- Product result: [[a-one-year-pwa-retrospective-pinterest-engineering-blog-medium]] says mobile web became Pinterest's leading platform for new signups after the new experience shipped.
