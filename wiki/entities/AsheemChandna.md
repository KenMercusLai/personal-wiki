## Overview
[[AsheemChandna]] is a [[GreylockPartners]] investor represented through his account of originating Greylock's [[AppDynamics]] investment and supporting the company through leadership succession, IPO preparation, and acquisition by [[Cisco]].

## Current Profile
Chandna says [[RajeevMotwani]] privately introduced him to [[JyotiBansal]]'s Singularity Technologies plan in February 2008. After an extended product discussion, he brought Bansal to Greylock's partner meeting and co-led the Series A with Lightspeed's Ravi Mhatre.

His retrospective also places him inside the later CEO search: Chandna met [[DavidWadhwani]], participated in a four-month courtship, and describes the board's aim of adding a leader capable of scaling AppDynamics as a future public company.

## Key Characteristics
- Originated from a trusted technical-network introduction.
- Moved quickly from founder diligence to a Series A term sheet.
- Supported the company through office space, financing, board work, and executive recruiting.
- Interprets the Cisco transaction as an acceleration opportunity rather than a rescue.

## Evidence
- Introduction and investment: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] recounts Motwani's email, Chandna's first meeting with Bansal, and the Greylock/Lightspeed Series A.
- Early support: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] says AppDynamics' initial team worked from Greylock's Campus Drive offices.
- Executive recruiting: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] places Chandna in the search and courtship that brought Wadhwani to AppDynamics.

## Qualifications
This is Chandna's own celebratory retrospective, so it highlights successful judgment and a favorable transaction while providing limited detail about setbacks, competing board views, financing terms, acquisition price, integration risk, or alternative histories.
