## Overview
[[Binance]] appears in the wiki as a cryptocurrency exchange and ecosystem example in Taresky's crypto-yield sources.

## Current Profile
One Taresky source uses Binance to contrast exchange lending product design: unlike an order-book lending model or an hourly auction model, Binance is described as a dark-pool matching system where users see a current rate, subscribe to flexible lending, and earn interest while the platform adjusts rates according to supply and demand. The stablecoin-mining source adds Binance as an on-ramp and ecosystem context: it names Binance as a common centralized exchange for Chinese users, and frames Binance Smart Chain as a lower-fee but more centralized chain whose assets ultimately depend on Binance's exchange and mapping infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides flexible crypto lending products in the article's comparison.
- Uses platform-controlled matching and dynamic rate adjustment rather than a transparent user-facing order book.
- Offers immediate, flexible participation but with lower transparency and, according to the source, usually lower yield.
- Functions as a common fiat-to-crypto on-ramp in the stablecoin-mining walkthrough.
- Anchors the BSC risk tradeoff: lower fees and many projects, but a more centralized trust model.
