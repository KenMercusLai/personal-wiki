Source: [3 Principles to Make Your Side Project Stand Out to Help You Land a Job in Tech]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/hack-career-side-projects-tech-job.md" >}})

Feross Aboukhadijeh is cited in the Hack Career article as the creator of YouTube Instant, a 2010 side project. The source says he built it in less than six hours, after which the project gained media attention and led to a YouTube job offer from Chad Hurley.

In the article, Aboukhadijeh's example serves as the exceptional version of a side project: the project itself appeared quick to build, but the author emphasizes that such outcomes usually rest on world-class skills developed over years.
