## Overview
[[EricSchmidt]] is presented as a Google operator whose scaling advice centers on timing, recruiting, small teams, and CEO path-clearing.

## Current Profile
The source uses Schmidt as an example of executive scaling judgment at [[Google]]. His recurring advice is to stay small until the product is working, then grow quickly; expect processes to break at every order of magnitude; treat recruiting as a major strategic system; and see the CEO as someone who sets direction and removes obstacles for high-performing product and engineering talent.

## Key Characteristics
- Advises keeping teams small until the product is working, then growing fast.
- Treats recruiting as central to company scaling and culture.
- Argues that great products often begin with very small teams under pressure.
- Frames CEO work as managing chaos and organizing around key talent.
