Source: [3 Years as a One Man Startup]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/3-years-as-a-one-man-startup.md" >}})

Steve Ridout is the author of this source and the maker of Readlang. In the article, he describes spending most of the previous three years building one language-learning web app as a one-person startup.

Ridout frames his situation as a tradeoff between independence, user impact, and opportunity cost. He compares Readlang's third-year profit with what he could earn through ordinary employment or contracting, while also noting that the product was growing and helping language learners.

The source presents his decision to continue as an explicit bet on compounding. Readlang was already ramen profitable, profits had grown sharply over the prior year, and he believed continued work could improve the product and extend that trajectory.
