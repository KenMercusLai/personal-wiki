## Overview
[[TerriPotts]] is represented in the wiki as a [[Raytheon]] engineering fellow and software technical director cited for small-step DevOps transformation.

## Current Profile
Potts's example turns broad transformation advice into a bounded adoption tactic: automate selected tests or builds for a few subteams, use the result as concrete evidence, and let early participants become advocates for wider change.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates starting organizational change with a few subteams.
- Uses build and test automation as an observable early intervention.
- Treats local success as evidence for broader [[DevOpsCulture]] adoption.

## Evidence
- Pilot scope: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] quotes Potts saying a whole program may be too large to change at once, while a few subteams can begin moving in the right direction.
- Reported result: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] says automation let one [[Raytheon]] team move from two integration procedures per month to 27 in one night.
- Diffusion mechanism: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] presents early pilot participants as possible advocates elsewhere in the organization.

## Qualifications
The case is reported secondhand in one practitioner article. It does not define an integration procedure, describe the baseline workload, or provide production reliability, security, customer, cost, or long-term adoption outcomes.
