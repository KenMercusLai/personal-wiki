## Overview
[[Oak]] is a meditation and breathing app founded by [[KevinRose]] and represented here through [[AJSmart]]'s remote redesign case study.

## Current Profile
The source describes Oak as a 2017 app for guided meditation, breathing exercises, goal setting, and progress tracking. A planned redesign of its meditation-selection screen expanded into a full visual and interaction redesign using watercolor artwork; after developer handoff and a three-to-four-month implementation period, the new version appeared in the App Store and received Product Hunt recognition.

## Key Characteristics
- Makes meditation and breathing practice more tangible through guided sessions, goals, and progress tracking.
- Was already used by members of AJ&Smart before the consultancy approached Rose with improvement ideas.
- Moved from vector graphics toward a Japanese-influenced watercolor visual direction.
- Reached launch after a four-week design engagement and a later developer implementation period.

## Evidence
- Product purpose: [[from-idea-to-app-store-a-design-sprint-case-study-ux-planet]] describes guided meditation, breathing, progress, and goal features.
- Redesign scope: [[from-idea-to-app-store-a-design-sprint-case-study-ux-planet]] says the brief grew from one selection screen to the whole app.
- Visual system: [[from-idea-to-app-store-a-design-sprint-case-study-ux-planet]] documents Japanese references, hand-painted art, and the decision to replace vectors with watercolors.
- Reception: [[from-idea-to-app-store-a-design-sprint-case-study-ux-planet]] reports App Store featuring and shows a number-three Product Hunt badge.

## Qualifications
This is an agency-authored success case without baseline usability evidence, representative-user test results, retention, rating changes, download data, or an independent account of launch performance. Recognition documents visibility, not the redesign's causal effect on user outcomes.
