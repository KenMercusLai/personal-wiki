## Overview
[[AtlasVenture]] is represented in the wiki as the venture-capital firm from which [[FredDestin]] backed [[Zoopla]].

## Current Profile
In Destin's account, Atlas Venture participated in Zoopla's 2007 seed financing and chose to lead the Series B alone after an outside lead investor withdrew during the November 2008 market crash. Octopus Ventures later helped complete that round.

## Key Characteristics
- Backed Zoopla from its seed stage.
- Increased its exposure after an external financing withdrawal.
- Made the follow-on decision primarily from conviction in the management team.

## Evidence
- Seed investment: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] says Destin wrote the 2007 seed check from Atlas Venture alongside Robin Klein.
- Follow-on decision: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] says the firm agreed to fund Series B alone after the proposed lead withdrew amid the 2008 crash.
- Round completion: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] says Octopus Ventures later completed the financing.

## Qualifications
The profile is limited to Destin's retrospective about one successful investment. It provides no fund-level context, contemporaneous decision record, financing terms, or dissenting view and should not be treated as a complete history of Atlas Venture.
