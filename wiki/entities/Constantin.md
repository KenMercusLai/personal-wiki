## Overview
[[Constantin]] (@constantout) is represented in the wiki as the author of a short practitioner playbook for finding a SaaS product's first users through Reddit.

## Current Profile
The source presents Constantin as an advocate for shifting zero-user founders from additional coding toward direct community participation. His proposed method combines subreddit research, locally appropriate posting, rule compliance, restrained frequency, and active comment engagement.

## Key Characteristics
- Gives concise, tactical marketing advice for early-stage SaaS founders.
- Treats community observation as preparation for publishing.
- Emphasizes participation and replies rather than product promotion alone.

## Evidence
- Audience research: [[constantin-reddit-marketing-strategy-for-saas-with-zero-users]] recommends studying top posts over multiple time windows before writing.
- Community fit: [[constantin-reddit-marketing-strategy-for-saas-with-zero-users]] tells founders not to plug a product when subreddit rules forbid it.
- Engagement practice: [[constantin-reddit-marketing-strategy-for-saas-with-zero-users]] recommends engaging with everyone who responds and posting about once per day without spamming.
