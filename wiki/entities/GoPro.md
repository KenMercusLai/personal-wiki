## Overview
[[GoPro]] is an action-camera company used in the source as an example of user-generated media turning customers into publicity.

## Current Profile
The article says GoPro marketed lifestyle and adventure rather than only camera technology. By featuring user footage, it made the product's output itself into advertising and encouraged prospective customers to imagine preserving and sharing their own extreme-sport experiences.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers affordable wearable cameras for action and extreme-sport contexts.
- Uses customer footage as marketing material.
- Frames the product around lifestyle and memory rather than specifications alone.
- Reported in the source as reaching a large IPO valuation.

## Evidence
- User footage: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says GoPro features footage from users on its website.
- Lifestyle positioning: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says GoPro marketing centers on lifestyle rather than technology.

## Qualifications
