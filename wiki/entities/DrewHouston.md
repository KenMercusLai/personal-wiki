## Overview
[[DrewHouston]] is presented as an [[MIT]] alumnus, cofounder and CEO of [[Dropbox]], and the speaker at MIT's 2013 commencement.

## Current Profile
Houston's address uses his early founder path to argue for problem-driven motivation, formative peer environments, action before readiness, and tolerance for correctable failure. He contrasts the declining energy he felt while operating the SAT-preparation company Accolade with the involuntary attention he gave a poker bot and later Dropbox, treating that contrast as evidence that durable effort can be pulled by a personally important problem. He also credits MIT relationships, including Adam Smith and Dropbox cofounder Arash Ferdowsi, with raising his ambition and changing his opportunities.
