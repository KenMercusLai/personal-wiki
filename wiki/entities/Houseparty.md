## Overview
[[Houseparty]] is presented in the source as a synchronous group video-chat app built by former Meerkat founders and shaped through direct work with teenage and college-age users.

## Current Profile
The 2017 article describes Houseparty as an always-ready social space: opening the app places a user on video and lets friends join, while rooms can also be locked. Because the adult founding team did not share its core teenagers' daily norms, it toured Midwestern and Southern schools in 2016, demonstrated prototypes, and solicited feedback on room capacity, presence notifications, and the feel of spontaneous socializing. Recruitment through alumni and family ties produced early adopters who could bring existing school friends onto the product.

The source connects that research loop to distribution. Dense school relationships made adoption spread locally, and the product's App Store rank reportedly peaked at number three in May 2016 before falling during the summer. The team interpreted the decline as seasonality: teenagers used Houseparty more, not less, when school put them in daily contact. By February 2017, the article reports roughly 20 million user-minutes per day, while also describing adults and families adapting the same live-room model to their own relationships.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses spontaneous, synchronous multi-person video rather than scheduled calls or asynchronous posting.
- Developed interaction details through school visits and direct target-user feedback.
- Recruited outside coastal technology hubs to reduce a tech-early-adopter sampling bias.
- Benefited from dense existing relationships among students.
- Could be reinterpreted by friends and families beyond its initial teenage audience.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to a 2017 magazine article and its company-, user-, and investor-reported observations. It supplies no cohort definitions, retention series, research protocol, independent usage audit, failed-school comparison, privacy analysis, safety assessment, or later ownership and shutdown history. Deliberately avoiding coastal technology hubs does not establish a representative national sample, and the seasonal App Store interpretation is observational rather than causal.
