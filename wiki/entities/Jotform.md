## Overview
[[Jotform]] is the bootstrapped form-builder company used by [[AytekinTank]] as the central example of a side project that eventually became a full-time business.

## Current Profile
The source presents Jotform as a product Tank developed before leaving full-time employment. It had reportedly reached 3.2 million users and 100 employees by the time of the article, without taking outside funding, making it a patient side-project-to-company example rather than a venture-backed overnight leap.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as one of Tank's products developed outside full-time work.
- Became Tank's core business after several years of side-project work.
- Reportedly reached millions of users and a 100-person organization.
- Grew without outside funding according to the source.
- Functions as a bootstrapped SaaS example connected to customer questions and product experimentation.

## Evidence
- Side-project period: [[aytekin-tank-jotform-how-to-build-a-startup-without-quitting-your-day-job]] says Tank improved Jotform while working full-time.
- Full-time transition: [[aytekin-tank-jotform-how-to-build-a-startup-without-quitting-your-day-job]] says Tank went all-in after five years.
- Scale claim: [[aytekin-tank-jotform-how-to-build-a-startup-without-quitting-your-day-job]] reports 3.2 million users and 100 employees.
- Funding model: [[aytekin-tank-jotform-how-to-build-a-startup-without-quitting-your-day-job]] says Jotform reached that scale without receiving outside funding.
- Customer contact: [[aytekin-tank-jotform-how-to-build-a-startup-without-quitting-your-day-job]] says Tank used early morning time to answer customer questions before his day job.
