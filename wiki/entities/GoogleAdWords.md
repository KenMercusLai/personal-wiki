## Overview
[[GoogleAdWords]] is Google's former name for the self-service advertising product now called Google Ads, represented in the sources through both its product origin and its 2018 advertiser-side role.

## Current Profile
The origin account presents AdWords as a product that nearly failed because sales feared cannibalization and engineers feared damage to search quality. Placement and a performance-adjusted ranking formula made self-service demand more compatible with relevance. The digital-marketing guide shows the later advertiser workflow across search and display: advertisers choose bids, keywords, demographics, behavior, placement, or campaign objectives, while Google matches that demand to its inventory and publisher supply. Both accounts use simplified auction explanations from different product eras.

## Key Characteristics
- Self-service advertising platform at Google.
- Faced internal resistance from both ad sales and engineering.
- Used side placement to distinguish self-service ads from top search-result ads sold by salespeople.
- Ranked ads by a combination of price and performance rather than bid alone.
- Served the advertiser or demand side of Google's search and display inventory.
- Supported CPC bidding for response-oriented campaigns and CPM bidding for impression-oriented campaigns.
- Used relevance or quality alongside bid in the source's simplified search-auction model.

## Evidence
- Internal resistance: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says sales feared self-service ads would diminish large-brand sales and engineers feared ads would hurt search relevance.
- Placement design: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says AdWords-generated ads were placed to the side of search results.
- Relevance formula: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says placement multiplied price per impression by click-through performance.
- Spec and launch: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Jane Manning wrote the first AdWords spec and worked with engineers to build and launch.
- Demand-side role: [[a-comprehensive-guide-to-digital-marketing-and-analytics]] contrasts advertiser use of AdWords with publisher use of AdSense.
- Auction mechanism: [[a-comprehensive-guide-to-digital-marketing-and-analytics]] and its retained diagrams show a simplified bid-times-quality-score ranking and next-advertiser pricing calculation.
- Campaign choice: [[a-comprehensive-guide-to-digital-marketing-and-analytics]] distinguishes CPC bidding for clicks from CPM bidding for impressions and branding.

## Qualifications
The sources are an origin story and a 2018 guide. They do not provide a complete or current auction specification, and they omit later quality-score changes, automation, privacy controls, regulatory issues, naming changes, and the wider evolution of Google's advertising business.

## What Changed
- Added the advertiser-side search/display workflow, CPC-versus-CPM distinction, and quality-score auction illustration.
- Clarified that the two source formulas are simplified accounts from different stages of the product.
