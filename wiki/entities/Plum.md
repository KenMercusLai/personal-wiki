## Overview
[[Plum]] is the author of Plum's Blog, represented here by an essay criticizing invitation-only promotion in China's single-player game market.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Plum writes as an interested player whose confidence in [[PhantomBladeZero]] fell after a delay despite years of previews and publicity. The author uses that reaction, the launch backlash around [[WuchangFallenFeathers]], and a recollection of an MKBHD argument about technology-media access to frame offline previews as an incentive problem rather than neutral product testing.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses first-person player expectations as the starting point for a broader media-incentive argument.
- Distinguishes a game's underlying quality from the expectations created around it.
- Prefers public demos and post-release player evidence to invitation-only access and coordinated creator coverage.
- Writes polemically and makes several industry claims without supplying primary records or systematic outcome data.

## Evidence
- Expectation revision: [[plumz-14418]] describes the author moving from schedule confidence to distrust after [[PhantomBladeZero]]'s delay.
- Comparative case: [[plumz-14418]] treats [[WuchangFallenFeathers]] as a decent but overpromoted game whose audience expectations contributed to backlash.
- Proposed remedy: [[plumz-14418]] argues that public demos let players test performance, controls, and fit more directly than curated events.
- Analytical limit: [[plumz-14418]] provides observation and interpretation rather than contracts, budgets, participant interviews, or comparative market data.

## Qualifications
This profile comes from one essay. It does not establish Plum's wider biography, professional role, access to the named studios, or whether every factual and causal claim in the article is accurate.
