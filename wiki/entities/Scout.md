## Overview
[[Scout]] is a Shopify abandoned-checkout alert app built by [[AhmadIqbal]] and a developer friend after manual phone follow-ups worked for [[Nadeef]].

## Current Profile
Scout began as a simple email alert: twenty minutes after an abandoned checkout, it sent the merchant the key order details and phone number so the merchant could personally call the customer. After months of internal use, the team turned the alert workflow into a Facebook Messenger bot and published it free on the Shopify App Store. The source frames Scout as an early product-market signal because it reached around 1,000 merchants organically over more than a year and received appreciative merchant reviews.

## Key Characteristics
- Originated from a manually proven abandoned-checkout callback process.
- Started as a very narrow alert workflow rather than a full interface-heavy app.
- Helped merchants take personal action instead of automating customer recovery.
- Later used Facebook Messenger as a merchant alert channel.
- Generated organic Shopify App Store adoption and positive reviews before major marketing.

## Evidence
- Manual proof: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Iqbal built Scout only after calling abandoned-checkout customers had worked for his own store.
- Narrow first version: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says the first prototype was effectively "if abandoned checkout, then email me" and took a weekend.
- Merchant adoption: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Scout reached about 1,000 merchants through organic App Store discovery over more than a year.
- Product iteration: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Scout's mission expanded from cart recovery toward identifying relationship-building opportunities.

## Qualifications
The source describes Scout while it was still free and explicitly says the team did not yet know whether users valued it enough to pay. The adoption and review claims are not independent long-term business validation.
