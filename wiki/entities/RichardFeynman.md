## Overview
[[RichardFeynman]] is cited in the source as the physicist and Nobel Prize winner associated with the [[FeynmanTechnique]] for learning and internalizing difficult subjects.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Feynman functions as the origin figure for a learning method built around simple explanation, review of weak points, and internalization. The article emphasizes his association with learning by teaching rather than his broader scientific biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Credited as the developer or namesake of the Feynman Technique.
- Associated with explaining knowledge in simple, clear language.
- Used as an authority for active, output-driven learning.
