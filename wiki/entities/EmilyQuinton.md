## Overview
[[EmilyQuinton]] is a photographer, teacher, community builder, and [[Makelight]] cofounder in [[StefLewandowski]]'s account of their family-centered creative business.

## Current Profile
Quinton had previously operated a wedding-photography microbusiness, taught face-to-face workshops, maintained the Makelight blog, and built an audience across Instagram, Pinterest, and Twitter. Those assets supplied both the subject matter and distribution for the first online photography course. The source presents her creative practice, teaching, and community work as the demand-side complement to Lewandowski's product and technical capabilities.

## Key Characteristics
- Photographer and photography educator.
- Built an audience before launching the paid online course.
- Supplied the existing Makelight identity and teaching practice.
- Cofounded a business designed around family life.
- Combined creative work with ongoing community support.
