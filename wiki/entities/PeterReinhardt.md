## Overview
[[PeterReinhardt]] appears as a [[TwilioSegment]] cofounder and operator associated with early fundraising and with repeating the company mission at every all-hands.

## Current Profile
The Segment retrospective places Reinhardt inside the four-person founding team and says he led the 2013 bridge-round outreach while the pre-revenue company had roughly three months of runway. Three of dozens of investor contacts replied; the company closed $2 million from e.Ventures and KPCB at a reported $10.5 million post-money valuation. A later advice source connects that operating history to scaling communication: as a company grows, all-hands meetings should repeatedly remind employees why the company exists and what change their work is meant to create.

## Key Characteristics
- Source of a mission-repetition communication practice.
- Connects all-hands meetings to purpose, not only status updates.
- Reinforces mission as repeated operating context during growth.
- Led Segment's investor outreach during its cash-constrained 2013 bridge round.
