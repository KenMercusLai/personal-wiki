## Overview
[[JeffHuang]] is represented in this wiki as the author of a personal productivity article about running academic work through a calendar and a never-ending plain-text file.

## Current Profile
The source presents Huang as a professor who has used one text file as his main work system for many years. His productivity advice is grounded in academic duties such as meetings, students, paper reviews, research notes, lab administration, ideas, annual-report material, and email follow-up. The entity's relevance here is source-scoped: Huang contributes a concrete example of [[TextFileProductivity]], not a broader biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a calendar to hold future commitments and possible future work.
- Uses a long-running text file as daily task list, meeting notes, research notebook, and searchable work archive.
- Values task sizing and workload reduction over maintaining an ever-growing todo list.
- Relies on simple tags, consistent wording, and text-editor search for retrieval.
- Uses lightweight email flags rather than inbox zero as the center of work control.
