## Overview
[[Disco]] is a Japanese chip-equipment manufacturer represented in this wiki by Bloomberg's account of its Personal Will internal market system.

## Current Profile
The source presents Disco as the world's largest maker of equipment that slices silicon ingots and wafers before they become electronic chips. Founded in 1937 as Dai-Ichi Seitosho Co., it evolved from cutting tools and diamond blades into semiconductor manufacturing equipment. Its management significance comes from adapting [[AmoebaManagement]] into a more individualized system where employees and teams trade services, bid for assignments, pay for resources, and earn bonuses through a virtual currency called Will.

## Key Characteristics
- Semiconductor-equipment manufacturer whose products cut silicon ingots and wafers.
- Long-running Japanese company founded before World War II as Dai-Ichi Seitosho Co.
- Uses Personal Will to price desks, PCs, meeting rooms, umbrellas, assignments, support work, and team services.
- Treats individuals as quasi-startups through auctions, internal billing, and quarterly virtual-currency balances paid in yen.
- Reports strong profitability and pay outcomes, while facing pressure, short-termism, and cultural-transfer concerns.
