## Overview
[[Chase]] is the bank that held [[HashiCorp]]'s original business account and later investigated fraud and processed the account's unusual closure.

## Current Profile
In [[MitchellHashimoto]]'s account, Chase provided an accessible branch where a first-time founder could open a business account, assigned a banker who noticed large funding deposits, and later recovered more than $100,000 in fraudulent transfers. The same branch-based system made exit cumbersome after the account was locked: electronic transfers became unavailable, a cashier's check had to be funded by a sufficiently large branch, and the final roughly $1 million withdrawal required advance coordination. The author expressly declines to treat this as proof of poor service because he had avoided the bank's relationship outreach and failed to close the obsolete account promptly.

## Key Characteristics
- Offered branch-based business account opening and relationship-manager outreach as HashiCorp's balance grew.
- Detected large incoming and outgoing transfers and contacted the account holder for relationship or verification purposes.
- Locked and investigated the legacy account after HashiCorp reported recurring fraudulent wires.
- Required branch funding and a cashier's check when the fraud lock prevented electronic transfer during closure.
- Is described through a mixture of direct experience and explicitly unverified second-hand claims about local branch incentives.

## Evidence
- Relationship outreach: [[my-startup-banking-story]] says the original banker called after the seed, Series A, and Series B deposits.
- Transfer verification: [[my-startup-banking-story]] says another Chase representative verified the roughly $35 million outbound wire before it processed.
- Fraud response: [[my-startup-banking-story]] says Chase locked the account, investigated for several months, and recovered all reported stolen funds.
- Closure constraints: [[my-startup-banking-story]] says the lock disabled electronic transfer and that a larger branch coordinated funding for the cashier's check.
- Incentive claim boundary: [[my-startup-banking-story]] attributes branch-level inflow and outflow metrics to a manager's explanation and does not verify whether it was policy or legally relevant.

## Qualifications
This is not a general evaluation of Chase. It is a single customer's retrospective, and the author says he did not engage with the bank enough to know how it might have served HashiCorp. The source supplies no Chase response, written policy, fraud cause, branch accounting rules, or comparison with another bank's handling of the same situation.
