## Overview
[[Zoopla]] is represented in the wiki as a UK property-technology company founded by [[AlexChesterman]] and backed at seed by [[FredDestin]] and Robin Klein.

## Current Profile
Destin presents Zoopla as a focused single-geography, single-vertical company that competed with Rightmove, publishers, and other startups through SEO, free pricing tools, equity alignment with estate agents, acquisitions, and fast integration. He reports that it raised about $20 million, listed publicly in 2014 at roughly $1.2 billion, and was the subject of an announced $3 billion acquisition in 2018.

## Key Characteristics
- Maintained a narrow real-estate mission rather than pursuing unrelated categories.
- Used product features and search distribution to separate from startup competitors.
- Aligned important industry participants through equity and transactions.
- Built core operational systems internally to improve visibility and iteration speed.
- Treated rapid post-acquisition integration as a repeatable operating capability.
- Ran board oversight through a small, stable set of KPIs.

## Evidence
- Focus and competition: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] says Zoopla stayed in real estate and used SEO plus free pricing tools to distinguish itself.
- Stakeholder and transaction strategy: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] describes estate-agent equity participation, acquisitions, and the 2012 FindAProperty merger.
- Core systems and metrics: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] says the company built even its billing system and retained a small KPI set for about six years.
- Integration capability: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] reports a 90-day full FindAProperty migration, including billing.
- Outcome and economics: [[fred-destin-10-years-to-3bn-ten-things-i-learned-from-zoopla]] reports the capital raised, IPO value, acquisition value, recurring revenue quality, and margin profile.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one celebratory investor retrospective written after an acquisition announcement. Its financial and operating figures are not independently verified here, it does not compare failed initiatives or integration costs, and it excludes perspectives from customers, employees, agents, competitors, and transaction counterparties.

## What Changed
- Established Zoopla as a focused vertical-company and acquisition-integration case.
