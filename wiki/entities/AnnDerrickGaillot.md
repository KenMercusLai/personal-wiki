## Overview
[[AnnDerrickGaillot]] is the author of The Outline's 2018 interview feature on finding new music in an algorithm-dominated environment.

## Current Profile
Gaillot frames streaming algorithms as one discovery route among older media, social relationships, specialist platforms, and physical music spaces. Rather than offering one prescription, she assembles six professionals whose routines expose differences in genre, role, access, taste, and depth of engagement.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes about music and culture.
- Uses multiple practitioner interviews to compare discovery routines.
- Frames algorithmic discovery through alternatives rather than rejecting platforms outright.

## Evidence
