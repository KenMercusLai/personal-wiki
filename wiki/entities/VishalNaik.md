## Overview
[[VishalNaik]] is the author of the Thoughtworks article "Architecting for Continuous Delivery."

## Current Profile
In this source, Naik writes from practitioner experience as a developer enabling continuous delivery. His argument links CD outcomes to architecture, test-suite design, and deployment-pipeline visibility rather than treating CD as a tooling checklist.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames continuous delivery as frequent, reliable, low-friction release.
- Connects monolithic codebase problems to build latency, ownership, technical debt, and deployment throughput.
- Advocates deliberate component extraction through libraries or services.
- Treats test-suite shape as a core delivery concern.
- Presents deployment pipelines as the backbone of CD.
