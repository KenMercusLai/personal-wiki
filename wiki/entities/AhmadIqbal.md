## Overview
[[AhmadIqbal]] is the founder-author in the source who moved from running [[Nadeef]], a Shopify store selling handheld bidets, into building Shopify apps around customer conversations and customer experience.

## Current Profile
The source presents Iqbal as both an ecommerce merchant and app developer. His product approach came from operating his own store: he manually called abandoned-checkout customers, measured the effect on recovery and later loyalty, then worked with a developer friend to systematize the workflow as [[Scout]]. He later applied the same merchant-first pattern to [[RavenCallback]] and [[PizzaParty]], while treating the failed handwritten-card app as a caution against falling in love with a solution before validating the problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Operated as a Shopify merchant before becoming a Shopify app developer.
- Left a senior technology-consulting role after preparing runway and studying whether Shopify apps could become a viable business.
- Used personal customer calls as discovery, recovery, trust-building, and product-development input.
- Favored testing and manual proof before systematizing a workflow into software.
- Interpreted customer experience as a small-business advantage over automated marketing tactics.
