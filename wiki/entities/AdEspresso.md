## Overview
[[AdEspresso]] is presented as an advertising-management platform and publisher that aggregates campaign spending into historical [[FacebookAdvertisingCosts]] benchmarks.

## Current Profile
The source says AdEspresso had managed more than $636 million in advertising spend and used campaign data from its users to compare cost per click, page like, and app install across 2017, 2018, and Q1 2019. Its role in the wiki is both operational and interpretive: it supplies aggregate observations while warning that the figures are reference points rather than prices an individual advertiser should expect.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates in Facebook advertising management and campaign analysis.
- Aggregates user campaign data into benchmark reports.
- Frames advertising cost as conditional on auctions, objectives, audiences, placement, timing, and relevance.
- Publishes optimization advice alongside historical descriptive data.

## Evidence
- Reported scale: [[facebook-ads-cost-the-complete-resource-to-understand-it]] says AdEspresso had managed more than $636 million in ad spend.
- Benchmark scope: [[facebook-ads-cost-the-complete-resource-to-understand-it]] covers 2017, 2018, and Q1 2019 cost patterns across clicks, page likes, and app installs.
- Interpretation boundary: [[facebook-ads-cost-the-complete-resource-to-understand-it]] repeatedly says its aggregate figures are guidelines rather than campaign-specific forecasts.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one first-party 2019 article. The source does not provide campaign counts, advertiser composition, sampling rules beyond selected objective constraints, weighting, variance, confidence intervals, or an independent audit of the reported spend. Its repeated chart embeds are invalid local files containing unrelated HTML, which prevents visual verification of the plotted evidence.
