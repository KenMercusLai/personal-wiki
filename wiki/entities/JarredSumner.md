## Overview
[[JarredSumner]] is presented as Bun's founder and leader, initially active in the Zig community and later responsible for Bun's venture-backed growth and Rust rewrite.

## Current Profile
[[AndrewKelley]] describes Sumner as a fast-moving learner whose early experimentation helped create [[Bun]] and attract attention, while arguing that the same speed became damaging when combined with management responsibility and venture-backed urgency. The source also credits Sumner with publicly acknowledging Zig and arranging a substantial recurring donation to the [[ZigSoftwareFoundation]]. Its later assessment is intensely negative: Kelley attributes poor communication, unrealistic expectations, low empathy, weak engineering discipline, and alleged unlawful employment practices to Sumner. Those claims remain allegations in a one-sided source, partly based on unnamed people.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded and led Bun in the source's account.
- Initially participated in and credited the Zig community.
- Arranged a reported US$60,000 annualized donation to ZSF before the relationship ended.
- Favored fast product and feature execution under venture-backed startup pressure.
- Is accused by Kelley of poor management and serious employment misconduct.

## Evidence
- Project and community role: [[my-thoughts-on-the-bun-rust-rewrite]] recounts Sumner's early Zig participation, Bun work, public credit, and donation.
- Operating style: [[my-thoughts-on-the-bun-rust-rewrite]] characterizes his approach as rapid experimentation that later produced management and code-quality problems.
- Allegations: [[my-thoughts-on-the-bun-rust-rewrite]] reports Kelley's conversations with interviewees and workers and his later firsthand information from two unnamed early-career programmers.

## Qualifications
The profile is limited to Kelley's hostile retrospective and should not be treated as an adjudication of Sumner's management, engineering, or legal conduct. The source supplies no employment documents, named witnesses, response from Sumner, or independent technical audit. Positive and negative descriptions are both filtered through the same relationship breakdown.
