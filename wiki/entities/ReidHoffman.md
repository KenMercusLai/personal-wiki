## Overview
[[ReidHoffman]] is presented as a CS183C instructor and a central source for the article's definition of [[Blitzscaling]].

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Hoffman is represented as a scaling theorist and operator-teacher. The source uses his comments to define blitzscaling as a high-speed, high-risk growth bet, to warn that startup advice changes by stage, and to frame when internal versus external leadership should be balanced.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines blitzscaling through the tradeoff between speed and support systems.
- Treats startup advice as stage-specific rather than universally applicable.
- Emphasizes competition as a reason to scale aggressively.
- Recommends balancing internal leaders with outside executives during scale.
