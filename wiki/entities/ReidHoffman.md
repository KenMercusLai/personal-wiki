## Overview
[[ReidHoffman]] is presented as a CS183C instructor, a central source for the article's definition of [[Blitzscaling]], and the interviewer who draws out [[JeffWeiner]]'s LinkedIn scaling lessons.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Hoffman is represented as a scaling theorist and operator-teacher. The scaling synthesis uses his comments to define blitzscaling as a high-speed, high-risk growth bet, to warn that startup advice changes by stage, and to frame when internal versus external leadership should be balanced. The Weiner session adds Hoffman's LinkedIn-specific role: he explains why common language becomes necessary beyond small-team scale, frames Kofman's coaching value as conceptual tools rather than motivational energy, and uses local-versus-global maxima to show why teams must optimize for the whole company mission.

## Key Characteristics
- Defines blitzscaling through the tradeoff between speed and support systems.
- Treats startup advice as stage-specific rather than universally applicable.
- Emphasizes competition as a reason to scale aggressively.
- Recommends balancing internal leaders with outside executives during scale.
- Treats common language as necessary once a company grows beyond informal all-hands scale.
- Uses global rather than local optimization to explain mission-aligned teamwork.
