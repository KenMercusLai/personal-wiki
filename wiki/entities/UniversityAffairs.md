## Overview
[[UniversityAffairs]] is the higher-education publication that hosted [[MelonieFullick]]'s essay on the recurring failures of university websites and the reader discussion appended to it.

## Current Profile
The available source represents University Affairs as a venue for commentary on how universities communicate and operate. Its role in this wiki is limited to publishing Fullick's 2016 essay and preserving a discussion in which higher-education web workers added operational explanations about staffing, distributed maintenance, internal politics, and recruitment priorities.
