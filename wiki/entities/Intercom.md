## Overview
[[Intercom]] is a SaaS company used in the source as an example of retaining customers by expanding from one tool into a broader customer-communication suite.

## Current Profile
The source presents Intercom as a product-led retention example. It began in the article's account as a chat-style customer communication popup, then broadened into live chat, in-app messaging, customer help desk, and public knowledge base tools. The retention lesson is that a SaaS product can stay relevant in a crowded market by covering more of the customer's adjacent workflow.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves customer communication workflows.
- Expanded from a narrow chat product into a multi-product platform.
- Uses platform access and free trials to encourage broader product engagement.
- Illustrates suite expansion as a retention response to competition.
