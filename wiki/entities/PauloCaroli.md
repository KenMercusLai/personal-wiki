## Overview
[[PauloCaroli]] is an agile and lean practitioner represented in the wiki as the author of the [[ProductBacklogBuilding]] canvas article and co-author of the Product Backlog Building technique with [[FabioAguiar]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Caroli as a [[Thoughtworks]] Expert Inception Facilitator based in Madrid with long-running experience helping organizations build digital products across Brazil, India, the United States, Latin America, and Europe. His practice lineage starts with encountering [[ExtremeProgramming]] in 2000 and develops through Agile and Lean processes, Product Backlog Building, Lean Inception, and retrospectives.

In the article, Caroli's distinctive contribution is practical facilitation. He turns user-story writing from a Product Owner bottleneck into a structured team activity that starts with personas and user activities, identifies features, breaks them into PBIs, and composes [[UserStories]] with acceptance criteria, tasks, UI notes, and enablers.

## Key Characteristics
- Agile and Lean practitioner focused on digital product success.
- Expert Inception Facilitator at [[Thoughtworks]] in the source biography.
- Co-author of the Product Backlog Building technique with [[FabioAguiar]].
- Connects [[ExtremeProgramming]] user-story lineage to contemporary backlog practice.
- Emphasizes collaborative backlog authorship rather than Product Owner-only story writing.
- Teaches practical facilitation structures such as personas, features, PBIs, ready/done agreements, and enablers.
