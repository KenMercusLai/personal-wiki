## Overview
[[GaborSzathmari]] is represented in the wiki as the lead author of a 2018 practitioner study of security risks created by expired law-firm domains.

## Current Profile
The source presents Szathmari as a cybersecurity practitioner associated with [[IronBastion]] and experienced in breach investigation and incident management. With [[JeremiahCruz]], he reports re-registering selected former Australian law-firm domains, restoring catch-all email, and testing how domain control affected correspondence, breach-service verification, and third-party account recovery.

## Key Characteristics
- Investigates domain, email, phishing, and account-recovery risks through practical demonstrations.
- Frames abandoned infrastructure as a continuing business-lifecycle liability.
- Focuses recommendations on both organizational custody and service-provider authentication controls.
- States ethical boundaries around not completing account takeovers or accessing recovered services.

## Evidence
- Research role: [[hacking-law-firms-with-abandoned-domain-names-gabor-szathmari-medium]] identifies Szathmari as an author and describes the six-domain research exercise.
- Professional context: [[hacking-law-firms-with-abandoned-domain-names-gabor-szathmari-medium]] describes more than ten years of cybersecurity experience and his work with Iron Bastion.
- Defensive emphasis: [[hacking-law-firms-with-abandoned-domain-names-gabor-szathmari-medium]] recommends persistent domain custody, account cleanup, MFA, unique passwords, and stronger portal recovery controls.
