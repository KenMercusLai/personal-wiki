## Overview
[[CaseyNewton]] is a technology journalist who wrote “Why note-taking apps don’t make us smarter” for [[TheVerge]].

## Current Profile
In this wiki, Newton is represented by an argument that productivity software has advanced information capture and storage faster than it has advanced thinking. He uses software-saturated farming and his own fragmented news-monitoring routine to show how more data can increase analytical burden, then raises generative AI as a possible but unresolved change to that pattern.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes about technology and productivity from a working journalist’s perspective.
- Frames note-taking as a problem of generating insight, not merely retaining information.
- Connects software adoption to data saturation and attention costs.
- Treats generative AI as an open question rather than an established remedy in the available excerpt.

## Evidence
- Productivity-software critique: [[why-note-taking-apps-dont-make-us-smarter]] argues that software has not progressed enough in improving thought.
- Overload examples: [[why-note-taking-apps-dont-make-us-smarter]] compares farmers overwhelmed by agricultural data with a journalist scanning several social feeds.
