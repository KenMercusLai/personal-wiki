Cloudflare appears in the source as the narrator's full-time employer. The article says that this job made it possible to pursue a patient, slow-growth project like OnlineOrNot, and that the narrator works better with a fixed two-hour window for shipping each morning.

The work section also connects Cloudflare to D1. A partner team began integrating an early-alpha product into wrangler, the command-line tool for Cloudflare Workers; after fixing bugs and contributing user-experience ideas, the narrator became one of the founding engineers of D1.
