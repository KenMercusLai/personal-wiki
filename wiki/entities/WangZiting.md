## Overview
[[WangZiting]] is the developer-blogger author of the 2018 technical retrospective, writing from hands-on work with side projects, container platforms, game backend design, and Redis-backed task queues.

## Current Profile
The source presents Wang Ziting as a practitioner who learns through shipping and reflecting on concrete projects. He compares stalled and released side projects, uses production work at [[LeanCloud]] to reason about [[Kubernetes]], containers, game backend architecture, and task queues, and treats technical design as inseparable from product usability and user feedback.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses annual technical retrospectives to extract process lessons from completed and unfinished work.
- Values fast, usable releases over long-running unpublished side-project development.
- Works across platform engineering, game backend features, Node.js services, Redis, and container infrastructure.
- Reasons about technology through product fit, user adoption, and concept cost rather than implementation alone.

## Evidence
- Retrospective practice: [[2018-nian-du-xiao-jie-ji-shu-fang-mian]] reviews side projects, work projects, architectural experiments, and technology judgments from 2018.
- Release orientation: [[2018-nian-du-xiao-jie-ji-shu-fang-mian]] contrasts [[DeployBeta]] as an overlong unpublished effort with [[Elecpass]] as a quickly released and personally used tool.
- Platform breadth: [[2018-nian-du-xiao-jie-ji-shu-fang-mian]] covers Kubernetes controllers, Dockerfile DSL work, [[ClientEngine]], cloud-engine task queues, and Redis Lua scripts.
