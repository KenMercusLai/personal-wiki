## Overview
[[SusanSu]] is represented as the guest author who assembled a practitioner guide to interviewing for growth roles.

## Current Profile

Her article synthesizes practices from five growth practitioners into a two-sided interview framework: employers observe reasoning, experimentation, resilience, strengths, and causal depth, while candidates investigate metrics, authority, resources, culture, and expected impact.
