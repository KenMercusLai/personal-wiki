## Overview
[[NestCam]] appears as a highly visible hardware example where an outdoor security camera's physical presence can make potential customers notice and remember the product.

## Current Profile
The source argues that an outdoor Nest Cam can be more viral than an indoor camera because people naturally notice cameras pointed toward public or semi-public spaces. Its physical placement creates awareness even though hardware loops are difficult to measure and iterate.

## Key Characteristics
- Smart-home security camera example.
- Uses physical visibility as a discovery surface.
- Illustrates the measurement and iteration limits of hardware virality.

## Evidence
- Visibility: [[9-ways-to-build-virality-into-your-product-gabor-cselle-medium]] says outdoor Nest Cams are usually installed in visible places and draw attention because people notice cameras.
- Product consideration: [[9-ways-to-build-virality-into-your-product-gabor-cselle-medium]] argues that visible cameras can make later buyers consider Nest.
- Hardware limitation: [[9-ways-to-build-virality-into-your-product-gabor-cselle-medium]] says hardware does not allow tight tracking or fast iteration through viral-loop stages.

## Qualifications
The source uses Nest Cam as a visibility example and does not provide sales attribution, conversion data, or a full Nest/Dropcam product history.
