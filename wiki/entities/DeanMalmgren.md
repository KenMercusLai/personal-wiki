## Overview
[[DeanMalmgren]] is represented here as a [[Datascope]] cofounder who describes human-serving algorithms as a continuing feedback loop among people, data, and algorithms.

## Current Profile
Malmgren gives the article's most operational account of [[AugmentedIntelligence]]: observe how people interact with a system, convert those observations into learning, and reapply the learning to algorithm design. This makes people part of an iterative control and discovery process rather than merely recipients of an automated output. The article does not specify how feedback is sampled, whose feedback counts, or how conflicts and harms are governed.

## Key Characteristics
- Cofounded Datascope with [[MikeStringer]].
- Describes human inclusion as necessary for algorithms intended to serve people.
- Frames learning as a repeated people-data-algorithm loop.
- Connects observed interaction with subsequent algorithm redesign.
