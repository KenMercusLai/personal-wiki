## Overview
[[PieterLevels]] is an independent product maker represented in this source as a friend and role model for [[AndreyAzimov]].

## Current Profile
Azimov credits Levels with encouraging him to act earlier on an unwanted career situation and cites Levels's “12 startups in 12 months” practice and reported five-figure indie-startup earnings as proof that repeated independent product building could become a viable path. The source supplies Azimov's interpretation rather than a full or independently verified profile of Levels's businesses.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a visible example of high-frequency independent product launching.
- Offers direct career encouragement to another aspiring maker.
- Is associated in this source with the “12 startups in 12 months” model.

## Evidence
- Career advice: [[i-quit-my-job-and-i-have-one-year-to-get-to-profitability]] attributes to Levels the argument that acting at 26 is preferable to remaining unhappy until 36.
- Role-model effect: [[i-quit-my-job-and-i-have-one-year-to-get-to-profitability]] says observing Levels build products made Azimov's desired path feel possible.
- Portfolio claim: [[i-quit-my-job-and-i-have-one-year-to-get-to-profitability]] describes Levels as the creator of 12 startups in 12 months and as earning five figures from indie startups.

## Qualifications
All claims here are source-scoped to Azimov's article. The note does not identify the relevant revenue period, product mix, costs, failures, or primary evidence for the earnings claim, and it should not be treated as a comprehensive biography.
