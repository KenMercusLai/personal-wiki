## Overview
[[JamesClear]] is an author represented in the wiki by an essay about how luck, effort, habits, preparation, and comparison scale jointly shape success.

## Current Profile
Clear's essay resists both pure merit and pure-fortune accounts. It uses Buffett's ovarian lottery to frame inherited conditions, Tu Youyou's artemisinin work to frame persistent effort, and two compact models to reconcile them: luck matters more in absolute and extreme outcomes, while choices and habits matter more within narrower comparisons; initial position is largely inherited, while repeated behavior can influence trajectory. His practical conclusion is that action and preparation increase exposure to opportunity and help people use good fortune when it appears, without putting fortune itself under their control.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes about behavior through compact, memorable models.
- Treats luck and effort as interacting explanations rather than mutually exclusive causes.
- Uses comparison scale to distinguish absolute from relative success.
- Uses trajectory to connect habits with long-term outcomes while preserving unequal starting conditions.
- Frames preparation and action as ways to increase exposure to opportunity rather than guarantee it.
