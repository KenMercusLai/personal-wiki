## Overview

## Current Profile
The source presents Ammons as an engineering leader focused on software products, engineering teams, team culture, process maturity, infrastructure reliability, and metrics. His concrete contribution to this wiki is a leadership model where team learning must be sanctioned during work, supported by recurring rituals, and modeled through public participation in the broader technical community.

- Uses lightweight team rituals such as lunch-and-learns, shared videos, and book clubs.
