## Overview
[[Range]] is the early-stage startup used in the source as a case for designing a hiring process around a balanced team, observable capability, and stage fit.

## Current Profile
The 2018 article describes Range as a startup co-founded by [[DanPupius]] to cultivate healthy, inclusive, and creative organizations. Its founders assessed their existing strengths and weaknesses, translated gaps into desired traits, values, and skills, declined to weight pedigree signals, grouped hiring attributes under Mission/Values, Teamwork, and Stage, and used offer acceptance as an early top-line signal while retention was still too delayed to assess.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames hiring around complementary team composition rather than isolated individual prestige.
- Defines company-specific attributes and examples of observable behavior.
- Excludes schools, grades, prior companies, and connections from candidate evaluation in the reported process.
- Distinguishes mission and values, teamwork, and early-stage suitability.
- Uses offer acceptance as an early process signal while reserving retention for a later horizon.

## Evidence
- Mission and founding context: [[develop-your-hiring-system-like-a-product-to-eliminate-bias-and-boost-retention-first-round-review]] describes Range as a healthy-organization startup co-founded by Pupius.
- Team-composition method: [[develop-your-hiring-system-like-a-product-to-eliminate-bias-and-boost-retention-first-round-review]] says the founders inventoried strengths and weaknesses before specifying complementary hires.
- Evaluation design: [[develop-your-hiring-system-like-a-product-to-eliminate-bias-and-boost-retention-first-round-review]] reports that Range excluded pedigree and defined rubric buckets with observable behaviors.
- Measurement: [[develop-your-hiring-system-like-a-product-to-eliminate-bias-and-boost-retention-first-round-review]] says the early team selected offer-acceptance rate as its top-line indicator.

## Qualifications
The profile reflects one 2018 interview about an early-stage process, not Range's later product, organization, or hiring outcomes. Offer acceptance cannot by itself establish selection quality, inclusion, employee performance, or retention.
