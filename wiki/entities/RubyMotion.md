## Overview
[[RubyMotion]] is the mobile development platform that [[AmirRajan]] says helped him create his games and that he later acquired.

## Current Profile

The source's key point is personal and economic: after [[ADarkRoom]] and later games created passive income, Rajan acquired the platform that had enabled some of his creative work, adding subscription revenue to games and contract income.
