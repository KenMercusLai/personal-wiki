## Overview
[[MattWarzel]] is represented in the wiki as a career-advice author who applies marketing concepts to career planning and job-search communication.

## Current Profile
Warzel frames a job candidate as an offer that must be understood, targeted, differentiated, and communicated consistently. His method combines introspection with market evidence: mission and assessments identify values and capabilities; research and SWOT connect them to labor-market conditions; resumes, interviews, online profiles, and elevator speeches translate them into employer-relevant benefits and next actions.

## Key Characteristics
- Applies strategic marketing tools to career and job-search decisions.
- Emphasizes transferable skills and measurable employer benefit over job-history recitation.
- Combines self-assessment, reflection, market research, and action planning.
- Advocates consistent but audience-adaptable communication across job-search channels.

## Evidence
- Marketing framework: [[business-insider-matt-warzel-brand-you-creating-and-self-marketing-yourself-to-find-a-job-during-tough-times]] maps product, promotion, place, and price onto candidate capabilities, outreach, employer access, and compensation.
- Candidate differentiation: [[business-insider-matt-warzel-brand-you-creating-and-self-marketing-yourself-to-find-a-job-during-tough-times]] recommends a tagline grounded in how the candidate has made or saved an employer money.
- Planning practice: [[business-insider-matt-warzel-brand-you-creating-and-self-marketing-yourself-to-find-a-job-during-tough-times]] links a mission statement, assessments, a career journal, market research, SWOT, goals, deadlines, and an elevator speech.
