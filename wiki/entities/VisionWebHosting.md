## Overview
[[VisionWebHosting]] was an unreleased shared-hosting project created by [[HitenShah]], [[NeilPatel]], and a five-person team after [[CrazyEgg]].

## Current Profile
Shah presents the project as a design-by-committee failure. The team tried to build a shared-hosting platform similar to Media Temple's Grid System, but contributors with different motivations repeatedly expanded or redirected the work. Launches slipped, progress slowed, and features drifted away from making hosting easier for customers. The project closed after two years with a reported loss exceeding $1 million.

## Key Characteristics
- Intended as a shared-hosting platform.
- Built by a five-person team including Shah and Patel.
- Suffered from conflicting contributor motivations and diffuse decision-making.
- Accumulated work not directly tied to easier shared hosting.
- Never launched and was closed after two years.
- Reportedly lost more than $1 million.

## Evidence
- Project identity: [[feature-creep-isnt-the-real-problem-product-habits]] says Shah and Patel attempted a Media Temple Grid System-like platform after Crazy Egg.
- Organizational failure: [[feature-creep-isnt-the-real-problem-product-habits]] attributes slow progress and delayed launches to too many contributors with different motivations.
- Product drift: [[feature-creep-isnt-the-real-problem-product-habits]] says many attempted features did not directly simplify shared hosting for customers.
- Outcome: [[feature-creep-isnt-the-real-problem-product-habits]] reports closure after two years and losses above $1 million.

## Qualifications
The available evidence is Shah's retrospective summary. It does not provide project records, scope history, customer research, spending detail, technical constraints, market conditions, or accounts from other team members, so design by committee is the reported diagnosis rather than an independently established sole cause.
