## Overview
[[ReedHastings]] is presented as Netflix's culture and leadership source in the CS183C scaling notes and as a cautionary example in an essay about simplified [[FounderOriginStories]].

## Current Profile
The scaling source uses Hastings to explain why Netflix wrote and published its culture deck, how a crisis-era layoff shaped its talent-density philosophy, and why the CEO role changes from doing everything to setting vision, focus, inspiration, and culture. McKendrick's essay gives Hastings a different role: it disputes the familiar claim that a $40 Apollo 13 late fee produced the idea for [[Netflix]] and uses that anecdote to show how a memorable origin story can displace a more complicated company history.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses written culture as a debatable and candidate-visible standard.
- Treats talent density as more important than keeping everyone.
- Advocates context over control for high-freedom organizations.
- Describes CEO evolution from hands-on work to strategic leadership.
- Serves as a cautionary case for treating polished founder anecdotes as literal causal history.

## Evidence
- Culture deck origin: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Hastings that candidates should see the culture deck and that writing made it debatable.
- Talent density: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Hastings on the 2001 layoff and higher productivity afterward.
- CEO evolution: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Hastings on moving from doing everything to vision, focus, inspiration, and culture.
- Origin-story dispute: [[why-you-should-ignore-every-founders-story-about-how-they-started-their-company-trevor-mckendrick]] rejects the late-fee anecdote as the actual origin of Netflix and argues that it misleads aspiring founders toward idea-first thinking.

## Qualifications
The culture and leadership material is limited to Hastings' remarks as represented in the scaling article and does not assess Netflix's broader management history. McKendrick asserts that the late-fee story did not literally produce Netflix but does not reconstruct the company's alternative origin history in the source, so this page treats it as a narrative caution rather than a complete factual correction.
