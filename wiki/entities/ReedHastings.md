## Overview
[[ReedHastings]] is presented as Netflix's culture and leadership source in the CS183C scaling notes.

## Current Profile
The source uses Hastings to explain why Netflix wrote and published its culture deck, how a crisis-era layoff shaped its talent-density philosophy, and why the CEO role changes from doing everything to setting vision, focus, inspiration, and culture.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses written culture as a debatable and candidate-visible standard.
- Treats talent density as more important than keeping everyone.
- Advocates context over control for high-freedom organizations.
- Describes CEO evolution from hands-on work to strategic leadership.
