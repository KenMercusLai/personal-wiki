## Overview

## Current Profile
The article mentions Wozniak mainly to establish Apple's founding myth: a small, hard-working company beginning in a garage and workshop rather than a polished corporate setting. It pairs him with [[SteveJobs]] as someone influenced by Bob Dylan and shows the founders' early workbench context, which supports the source's later argument that Apple's brand could draw on humility, craft, and cultural storytelling.
