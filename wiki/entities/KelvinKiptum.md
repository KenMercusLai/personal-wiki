## Overview
[[KelvinKiptum]] was a Kenyan marathon runner who set the official men's world record of 2:00:35 in Chicago in 2023 at age 23.9.

## Current Profile
Kiptum's marathon record in the source is unusually short and concentrated: he ran only three marathons, all within less than twelve months, and every one was below 2:02. Those performances occupied three of the seven fastest positions in the dataset and made him one of only four men represented below 2:02.

His Chicago result lowered [[EliudKipchoge]]'s 2:01:09 record. The source presents his young age and rapid three-race trajectory as evidence of exceptional unrealized potential, while noting that he died in a car crash a few months after setting the record.
