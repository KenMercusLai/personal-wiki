## Overview
[[LossesDon]] is a designer, developer, and writer represented through the seven-year evolution of [[AxiomDesignSystem]] and a 2026 essay on LLM-assisted writing published under the name LOSSES.

## Current Profile
The source portrays Losses Don as a design-system practitioner who values expressive visual language, accessibility, physical and spatial coherence, and carefully bounded developer APIs. Their work moves between interaction design and low-level browser implementation: Canvas drawing, SVG equivalence, CSS transforms, pixel alignment, rendering schedules, and cross-browser failures.

The rebuild also shows an expert-directed AI workflow. The author supplied the long-developed design concepts, specifications, taste, and failure diagnosis while several models accelerated code generation, optimization, and search over difficult implementation combinations.

The writing essay extends the same expert-directed stance to prose. Losses Don distrusts benchmark scores and generic style prompts as substitutes for task judgment, supplies substantial first-person material before asking for help, uses model questions to expose omissions, and treats delayed rereading plus full manual revision as necessary to preserve voice and catch reasoning errors.

## Key Characteristics
- Judges design systems by whether constraints raise the quality floor without suppressing expert expression.
- Treats visual effects as coherent semantic and engineering systems rather than independent demonstrations.
- Uses physical metaphor and accessibility as boundaries on visual customization.
- Works across component APIs, graphics mathematics, browser rendering, performance, and interaction details.
- Uses AI models as high-speed collaborators while retaining design direction and acceptance judgment.
- Treats authorial voice as a trained perception and revision problem, not a property that can be recovered through banned-word prompts alone.

## Evidence
- Design stance: [[ax-design]] distinguishes coherent systems from unintegrated effect libraries and critiques both excessive prescription and weak constraint.
- Engineering history: [[ax-design]] traces seven years from hand-built Reveal, elevation, acrylic, navigation, and pressure components to shared infrastructure.
- AI collaboration: [[ax-design]] credits Claude, Kimi, and DeepSeek with optimization, implementation, and debugging while emphasizing the role of prior expert work.
- Quality judgment: [[ax-design]] documents manual tuning of motion, constrained token APIs, browser fallbacks, and extensive boundary debugging.
- Writing workflow: [[sspai-110102]] documents author-first material capture, repeated model questioning, delayed review, blind output comparison, and manual rephrasing before publication.
- Model criticism: [[sspai-110102]] distinguishes reasoning and research usefulness from prose quality while treating its named model rankings as personal, version-specific experience.

## Qualifications
This profile derives from two self-authored practitioner reports. It does not independently establish project adoption, the exact division of labor between people and models, the generality of the reported performance improvement, or the accessibility and usability outcomes of the design decisions. The writing workflow has no matched quality study or reader evaluation, and its model-version judgments are personal snapshots rather than durable product rankings.
