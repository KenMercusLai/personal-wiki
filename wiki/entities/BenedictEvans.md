## Overview
[[BenedictEvans]] is a technology strategy analyst represented in the wiki through sources on the mobile ecosystem transition and Apple's services-era brand positioning.

## Current Profile
The mobile source presents Evans as a technology analyst synthesizing the smartphone transition after the first major phase of the iPhone-era platform war. His focus is not on one device feature, but on how scale, operating systems, apps, discovery, messaging, sensors, cars, TVs, watches, and ordinary user behavior reorganize the technology industry around mobile. The Apple services source extends that style into brand strategy: Evans reads Apple's 2019 News+, Arcade, Card, and TV+ event as an incremental services and churn-reduction move, but more importantly as an effort to turn privacy, curation, safety, and protection from scams into a consumer-facing Apple promise.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames mobile as an ecosystem-scale shift rather than a screen-size category.
- Uses platform strategy to connect hardware, operating systems, software, discovery, and distribution.
- Treats Apple, Google, Facebook, and Microsoft as actors whose strategies must be read through mobile's new center of gravity.
- Pays attention to ordinary user behavior, not only technology-industry enthusiasm.
- Reads services strategy through brand promise, trust, curation, and ecosystem retention rather than revenue alone.

## Evidence
- Ecosystem frame: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] argues that mobile is moving toward far larger scale than PCs and becoming the center of technology investment.
- Platform strategy: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] links ARM, [[IOS]], [[Android]], apps, messaging, discovery, and cloud productivity into one strategic transition.
- Actor analysis: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] compares [[Apple]], [[Google]], [[Microsoft]], and [[Facebook]] as mobile-era platform actors.
- User realism: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] closes by warning that technology insiders are not representative of mainstream users.
- Services and brand: [[apple-as-the-new-disney-benedict-evans]] argues that Apple's 2019 services event was both a churn-and-revenue move and a clearer articulation of Apple as a trusted, curated, anti-scam technology brand.
- Computing harm migration: [[apple-as-the-new-disney-benedict-evans]] links Apple's trust promise to a broader shift from lower-level technical worries toward adware, scamware, tracking, fraud, phishing, and manipulative app mechanics.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently knows Evans through a 2015 mobile-strategy article and a 2019 Apple services/brand article, so it should not generalize his broader body of work or later views from these two sources alone.
