## Overview
[[BenedictEvans]] is the author of the "16 mobile theses" source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Evans as a technology analyst synthesizing the smartphone transition after the first major phase of the iPhone-era platform war. His focus is not on one device feature, but on how scale, operating systems, apps, discovery, messaging, sensors, cars, TVs, watches, and ordinary user behavior reorganize the technology industry around mobile.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames mobile as an ecosystem-scale shift rather than a screen-size category.
- Uses platform strategy to connect hardware, operating systems, software, discovery, and distribution.
- Treats Apple, Google, Facebook, and Microsoft as actors whose strategies must be read through mobile's new center of gravity.
- Pays attention to ordinary user behavior, not only technology-industry enthusiasm.

## Evidence
- Ecosystem frame: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] argues that mobile is moving toward far larger scale than PCs and becoming the center of technology investment.
- Platform strategy: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] links ARM, [[IOS]], [[Android]], apps, messaging, discovery, and cloud productivity into one strategic transition.
- Actor analysis: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] compares [[Apple]], [[Google]], [[Microsoft]], and [[Facebook]] as mobile-era platform actors.
- User realism: [[16-mobile-theses-benedict-evans]] closes by warning that technology insiders are not representative of mainstream users.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently knows Evans only through this one 2015 mobile-strategy article, so it should not generalize his broader body of work or later views from this source alone.
