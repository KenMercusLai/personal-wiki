## Overview
[[TrickAJournalist]] is a satirical website and campaign created by [[NathanKontny]] to criticize automated, repetitive outreach to journalists.

## Current Profile
The campaign presented software that would supposedly barrage journalists until they covered a company, making normalized list-buying and repeated follow-up resemble obvious spam. Kontny extended the joke by proposing to fire customers attracted to the product and by buying ads on LinkedIn, Facebook, Google, and Reddit; he reports that Reddit unexpectedly rejected the ad while the other platforms accepted it. Each reversal became additional content, producing what he calls his most viral marketing campaign.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses an apparently real product offer to expose an objectionable marketing norm.
- Turns customer selection and advertising-platform response into further campaign stories.
- Depends on satire being understood; literal signups reveal a risk of audience misreading.
- Is supported only by its creator's retrospective account and unquantified performance claim.

## Evidence
- Satirical premise: [[viral-marketing-the-set-list-medium]] describes automated repeated contact with journalists as the campaign's deliberately offensive offer.
- Layered reversals: [[viral-marketing-the-set-list-medium]] says Kontny proposed firing interested customers and tested whether advertising platforms would accept the campaign.
- Reported result: [[viral-marketing-the-set-list-medium]] calls it Kontny's most viral marketing campaign but supplies no traffic, conversion, or comparison figures.

## Qualifications
The source does not measure the campaign's reach or business effect, and the fact that people attempted to sign up shows that satire can reproduce or attract the behavior it criticizes. Platform acceptance or rejection is also not evidence that the underlying outreach norm changed.

## What Changed
- Created the project as Kontny's first-person expectation-breaking campaign case.
