## Overview
[[SuperMemo]] is the spaced-repetition learning software and research project that [[PiotrWozniak]] presents as the central outcome of his goal-directed learning path.

## Current Profile
In the source, SuperMemo is less a neutral software mention than the concrete example of a high-value personal goal. Wozniak says that deriving an optimal review-interval function in 1985 led him toward programming, and that his desire to build SuperMemo made programming feel far more valuable than school-assigned electronics, calculus, metrology, or abstract algebra.

The project therefore functions as evidence for the [[KnowledgeValuationNetwork]] thesis: when knowledge is connected to a personally meaningful goal, the learner can persist, route around institutional constraints, and transform learning into a durable life direction.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented as Wozniak's most important professional and personal learning project.
- Originated from an attempt to compute optimal review intervals for long-term memory.
- Made programming knowledge highly valuable because it enabled the software's creation.
- Contrasted with school coursework that Wozniak experienced as lower-value and obstructive.
- Used as a case for how personal autonomy can redirect education toward meaningful learning.

## Evidence
- Origin point: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] says Wozniak derived an approximate function for optimal review intervals in 1985, leading toward spaced repetition and early SuperMemo.
- Programming motivation: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] says programming skills became valuable because they could make SuperMemo possible and accelerate learning across fields.
- School contrast: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] contrasts SuperMemo-oriented self-learning with mandatory school subjects that Wozniak saw as draining attention from his most important work.
- Autonomy claim: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] says Wozniak's thesis eventually focused on SuperMemo because a mentor allowed unusual student autonomy.

## Qualifications
This profile is source-scoped and autobiographical. The source does not independently evaluate SuperMemo's technical history, comparative effectiveness, or later product development.
