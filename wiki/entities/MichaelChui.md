## Overview
[[MichaelChui]] is represented in this wiki as a McKinsey Global Institute principal and coauthor of the 2015 article “Four fundamentals of workplace automation.”

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Chui is one of three authors presenting preliminary research on the automation potential of work activities in the United States. The article's central contribution is to replace whole-job prediction with analysis of activities and capabilities, then connect that model to role redesign, process transformation, augmentation, adoption constraints, and social risk.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of an activity-level workplace-automation framework.
- Associated with McKinsey Global Institute at publication.
- Connects technical capability assessment to jobs, processes, leadership, and workforce skills.
- Presents automation as both an augmentation opportunity and a source of transition and governance risk.
