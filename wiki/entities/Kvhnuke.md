## Overview
[[Kvhnuke]] is the MyEtherWallet co-creator who retained the core MEW assets after the MyCrypto split.

## Current Profile
In Monahan's account, kvhnuke helped start [[MyEtherWallet]] as a two-person Ethereum wallet side project. The source gives less detail about kvhnuke's own motivations than about Monahan's support and company-building experience, but it clearly identifies him as retaining the MEW GitHub repository, domain, AWS instances, and social media accounts when [[MyCrypto]] launched separately.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-created [[MyEtherWallet]] with [[TaylorMonahan]].
- Participated in the early side-project phase when MEW was built opportunistically outside day jobs.
- Retained control of central MEW technical and public assets after Monahan's team launched [[MyCrypto]].

## Evidence
- Co-founder role: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says MyEtherWallet started with kvhnuke and tayvano.
- Side-project context: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] describes early MEW work as voluntary, deadline-light, and separate from day jobs.
- Asset control: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says kvhnuke remained in control of the MEW GitHub repository, domain, AWS instances, and social media accounts.
