## Overview
[[Buffer]] is a social-media scheduling product used in the source as a content-led acquisition example.

## Current Profile
The article says Buffer grew from zero to 100,000 users largely through founder Leo Widrich's guest posts on third-party sites. The tactic borrowed existing audiences, used repeatable social-media article formats, and sent a portion of readers back to Buffer.

## Key Characteristics
- SaaS product for managing and scheduling social-media posts.
- Grew early users through guest blogging rather than only its own site.
- Treated proven content formats as reusable traffic patterns.
