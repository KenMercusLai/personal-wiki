## Overview
[[JeanetteHead]] is the author of "3 Strategies for Picking Your Battles as a Software Developer," a practitioner article about handling technical and product disagreements in software teams.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Head is represented as a software-development writer focused on pragmatic collaboration. Her article argues that disagreement is normal in software work, but that developers should decide when to press based on product impact, likelihood of influence, and the emotional cost to teammates.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes about software-team conflict as a normal part of collaborative product work.
- Emphasizes product and team value over personal preference in technical disagreements.
- Treats code review as both a quality practice and an interpersonal learning moment.

## Evidence
- Software-team conflict: [[3-strategies-for-picking-your-battles-as-a-software-developer]] defines winning as persuading others that an idea improves the product and losing as harming teammate contribution or product learning.
- Product-value standard: [[3-strategies-for-picking-your-battles-as-a-software-developer]] recommends asking whether a fight is worth the time saved, reliability gained, or quality improved.
- Review and learning: [[3-strategies-for-picking-your-battles-as-a-software-developer]] uses code-review examples to distinguish standards, performance, readability, pride, resentment, and shared learning.
