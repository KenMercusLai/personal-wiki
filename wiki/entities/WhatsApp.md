## Overview
[[WhatsApp]] is a messaging company used in the source as a small-headcount counterexample to the assumption that startup success requires hundreds of employees.

## Current Profile
The source does not analyze WhatsApp's product or acquisition history in depth. It uses one fact pattern rhetorically: a company could reportedly sell for $19 billion while having only 55 employees, which challenges venture-capital talk that treats rapid hiring and large teams as natural evidence of success.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a counterexample to headcount-as-success reasoning.
- Represents the possibility of extreme business impact from a small team.
- Appears in the source only through the acquisition/headcount anecdote.

## Evidence
