## Overview
[[GabeZichermann]] is represented in this wiki as the gamification author who introduced the [[SAPSRewardFramework]] in the source "Cash is for SAPS."

## Current Profile
The source presents Zichermann as a practitioner advising companies on gamified engagement. His main contribution here is the SAPS mnemonic: prioritize Status, Access, and Power before Stuff when designing rewards. He argues from consulting experience rather than published data in the captured text, and the page's comment material preserves pushback that the framework is a personal theory without research support.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames gamification through motivation and reward design rather than through game mechanics alone.
- Warns companies against treating cash or physical goods as the default engagement reward.
- Promotes SAPS as a simple hierarchy for choosing cheaper and stickier rewards.
- Uses practical examples such as CEO access, early deal access, moderator roles, and virtual-world privileges.
- Should be read as a practitioner voice in this source, not as an empirically validated authority.

- Tangible-reward warning: [[cash-is-for-saps-gamification-co]] says cash, goods, and weak prizes can be costly or demotivating.
