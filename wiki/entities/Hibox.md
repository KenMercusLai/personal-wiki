## Overview
[[Hibox]] is the team-messaging and task-management SaaS founded by [[MarianoRodriguezColombelli]] and [[SpencerCoon]] before they spun its internal release-notification tool into [[Beamer]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Hibox primarily as a focus and runway lesson. Its founders targeted non-technical small businesses broadly while [[Slack]] pursued a clearer niche, and Hibox's slower-than-planned growth left a roughly twenty-person, five-country organization under cash-flow pressure. The resulting downsizing preceded a semi-pivot in which the founding and core team moved to Beamer while Hibox continued under an independent team.

## Key Characteristics
- Combined team messaging with task management for small and medium businesses.
- Pursued a broad international, non-technical customer target that the founders later judged insufficiently focused.
- Built the internal notification feed that became Beamer.
- Reached a cash-flow crisis that led to branch closures, salary reductions, layoffs, and a smaller operating structure.
- Continued with an independent team after the founding and core team moved to the Beamer spinout.

## Evidence
- Product and audience: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] describes Hibox as a collaboration SaaS combining messaging and task management for non-technical small companies worldwide.
- Focus problem: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] contrasts Hibox's broad targeting with Slack's more focused entry and calls the original scope the founders' largest mistake.
- Financial pressure: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] reports a team approaching twenty people, branches in five countries, weak relative growth, difficult fundraising prospects, and subsequent downsizing.
- Product seed: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] says Hibox's dedicated changelog notification center produced the behavior and inbound interest that led to Beamer.
- Semi-pivot: [[halfway-there-the-road-to-1m-arr]] says a new company received the founding and core team while Hibox continued independently.

## Qualifications
Hibox is described only through a founder's retrospective designed to explain Beamer's success. The source provides no Hibox revenue, customer, retention, funding, or post-pivot performance data, and its comparison with Slack does not isolate focus from timing, product quality, distribution, capital, category dynamics, or execution.
