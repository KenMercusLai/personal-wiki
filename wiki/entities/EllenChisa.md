## Overview
[[EllenChisa]] is represented in the wiki as a product leader and former Kickstarter product manager discussing creator compensation, platform dependence, discovery, and meaningful media metrics.

## Current Profile
Chisa argues that different content markets need different revenue designs: advertising can fund mass reach, a few expensive buyers can fund specialist research, and crowdfunding can fund bounded projects, but moderately sized blogs, essays, and niche creators remain poorly served. She prefers a direct creator-audience relationship for resilience while recognizing that platforms make contribution aggregation and discovery easier. Her governing principle is to identify where value is created and let creators capture part of it, including value that later work derives from their ideas.

## Key Characteristics
- Product leader analyzing creator compensation as a value-allocation problem.
- Advocate for matching revenue models to content type, audience size, and willingness to pay.
- Proponent of direct creator-audience relationships as protection against platform change or disappearance.
- Supporter of platform aggregation and discovery when it reduces payment and attention friction.
- Critic of page views as an insufficient proxy for content quality or influence.

## Evidence
- Market segmentation: [[expert-revenue-models-for-new-media]] distinguishes mass advertising, high-priced specialist information, project crowdfunding, and an underserved middle.
- Relationship design: [[expert-revenue-models-for-new-media]] says she would rather build directly with an audience while accepting the convenience of platform aggregation.
- Value capture: [[expert-revenue-models-for-new-media]] argues that creative integrity is compatible with payment and that compensation should follow where value is created.
- Metric quality: [[expert-revenue-models-for-new-media]] favors read percentage, recommendations, and longer-run influence over raw page views.
- Discovery: [[expert-revenue-models-for-new-media]] identifies weak discovery for niche creators between famous names and known personal contacts.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one 2017 panel discussion rather than a full biography or operating record. The proposed segmentation and metrics are practitioner hypotheses without comparative conversion, retention, creator-income, or causal evidence, and the article does not resolve how later intellectual influence could be measured or compensated fairly.
