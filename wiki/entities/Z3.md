## Overview
[[Z3]] is a Microsoft Research SMT solver and theorem prover used here to search for agent actions that a proposed policy permits outside a reviewed maximum policy.

## Current Profile
Z3 extends Boolean satisfiability with theories useful for policy modeling, including integers, strings, and regular languages. OpenShell maps ports to integers, hosts and paths to strings, glob semantics to regular expressions, and policy composition to Boolean formulas. A satisfiable containment-violation query yields a concrete action; an unsatisfiable result establishes containment only for the encoded domain; an unknown result requires fail-closed handling.

## Key Characteristics
- Solves satisfiability modulo theories rather than Boolean SAT alone.
- Supports integers, strings, regular languages, and logical composition relevant to policy models.
- Can return a model that serves as a concrete policy-expansion witness.
- Distinguishes `sat`, `unsat`, and `unknown` outcomes.
- Requires domain-specific code to encode policy-language semantics accurately.

## Evidence
- Solver role: [[agent-policy-prover]] identifies Z3 as the engine behind OpenShell's containment examples and runtime approach.
- Theory mapping: [[agent-policy-prover]] maps ports, hosts, paths, globs, and policy composition to Z3 constructs.
- Witness behavior: [[agent-policy-prover]] demonstrates broad REST and raw layer-4 candidates returning concrete `sat` models while a narrow candidate returns `unsat`.

## Qualifications
Z3 does not supply the policy model or business meaning. Sound use depends on accurate domain constraints, faithful deployment semantics, a suitable reviewed maximum, and explicit handling of unsupported language features and `unknown` results.
