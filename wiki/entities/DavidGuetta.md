## Overview
[[DavidGuetta]] is the DJ and artist at the center of the fan-recording and personalized-artwork [[ThisOnesForYouCampaign]] for his UEFA EURO 2016 anthem.

## Current Profile
The source covers Guetta only in relation to the campaign. Fans were invited into a virtual recording experience to sing along to "This One's for You," potentially contribute voices to a final song, create team-themed cover art carrying their names, view embedded video of Guetta, share personalized pages, and enter a trip-to-Paris competition.

## Key Characteristics
- Artist associated with "This One's for You," described as the official UEFA EURO 2016 finals anthem.
- Central recognizable figure in a twelve-language participatory marketing application.
- Appeared in source imagery used to generate personalized, shareable album artwork.
