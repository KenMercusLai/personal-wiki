## Overview
[[IvanPepelnjak]] is the authorial voice behind the ipSpace.net article on adapting network design for automation.

## Current Profile
In this source, Ivan Pepelnjak presents network automation as a practical design and career question rather than a topology fashion. He argues that automation can be applied broadly, but that brittle or snowflake designs can make automation uneconomical, and that engineers should keep technical understanding while adapting to changing tooling.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames network automation through engineering tradeoffs rather than hype.
- Treats automation support as one requirement among security, convergence, jitter, and other network properties.
- Warns that automation code must stay synchronized with network design.
- Encourages engineers to adapt skills toward adjacent areas such as cloud networking or network automation.
