Source: [7 No-Nonsense Pieces of Startup Advice I Wish I Got When I Started]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/no-nonsense-startup-advice.md" >}})

Groove is the company used as the operating context for this startup advice source. The author writes from the perspective of having started Groove and learned lessons through its partnerships, mentor relationships, remote-team decision, customer conversations, supplier negotiations, and founder lifestyle choices.

In the source, Groove is less a product case study than a practical reference point for founder judgment. Its lessons center on filtering advice, protecting focus, building helpful relationships, talking directly with customers, treating brand and culture as defensible business assets, and learning to sell a company vision before the company has all the proof it wants.
