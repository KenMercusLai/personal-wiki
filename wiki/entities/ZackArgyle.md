## Overview
[[ZackArgyle]] is the Pinterest engineering manager who authored the company's one-year retrospective on rebuilding its mobile website as a progressive web app.

## Current Profile
The source presents Argyle as an internal practitioner reporting on the product rationale, engineering architecture, delivery timeline, performance controls, and observed business results of [[ProjectDuplo]]. His account is valuable implementation evidence but is also an interested company retrospective rather than an independent evaluation.

## Key Characteristics
- Engineering manager for Pinterest's Core Experience at the time of publication.
- Authorial voice for the company's 2017-2018 mobile-web rewrite.
- Connects engineering choices to accessibility, engagement, conversion, and growth goals.

## Evidence
- Role and authorship: [[a-one-year-pwa-retrospective-pinterest-engineering-blog-medium]] identifies Argyle as Engineering Manager, Core Experience.
- Technical account: [[a-one-year-pwa-retrospective-pinterest-engineering-blog-medium]] explains the team's use of Gestalt, code-splitting, route preloading, normalized state, service workers, and bundle controls.
- Outcome account: [[a-one-year-pwa-retrospective-pinterest-engineering-blog-medium]] reports year-over-year usage, engagement, and conversion changes after rollout.
