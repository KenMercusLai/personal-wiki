## Overview
[[KheHy]] is represented in this wiki as the RadReads author of a 2014 practitioner account about building a spreadsheet-based [[PersonalCRM]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Hy describes relationship maintenance as a recurring practice of making mutually useful introductions and sharing relevant information. Finding social networks noisy and business CRMs poorly matched to that goal, he adapted familiar Google Sheets features into a system he says he enjoyed updating several times a day. His four-part tag vocabulary reflects both his finance work and his interest in startups, while his implementation advice emphasizes controlled categories, restrained tag growth, comments, and a free-form escape hatch.

## Key Characteristics
- Practitioner-author focused in this source on personal-network maintenance and lightweight productivity systems.
- Builder of a Google Sheets contact system despite describing himself as not particularly technical.
- Uses industry, function, passions, and personal traits to support relationship-oriented retrieval.
- Prefers disciplined controlled tags over unconstrained vocabulary for dependable search.
- Frames the system around helping people through introductions, targeted information, and events rather than selling to them.

## Qualifications

The system's reported scale and benefits are self-reported, and the article does not compare outcomes with other contact-management practices.
