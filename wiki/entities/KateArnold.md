## Overview
[[KateArnold]] is the product manager in the source's early [[Netflix]] subscription-model case.

## Current Profile
The source presents Arnold as the product manager on a small Netflix team that needed a business-changing product solution when pay-per-rental DVD ordering was not compelling enough. Her work is framed as coordinating strategy, user validation, analytics, feature direction, finance, marketing, billing, and fulfillment so a subscription model could become viable rather than bankrupt the company.

## Key Characteristics
- Worked as product manager at Netflix when the company had fewer than 20 employees.
- Helped test and implement the subscription model.
- Drove product mechanisms such as the queue, ratings, and recommendations that made subscription economics workable.
- Coordinated across founders, users, analytics, finance, marketing, engineering, billing, and warehouse fulfillment.
- Later became a product leader at Shutterstock according to the source.

## Evidence
- Early Netflix context: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Arnold was the PM when Netflix was young, cash-constrained, and stuck near 300,000 customers.
- Subscription test: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says one test moved customers to a monthly subscription with unlimited movies.
- Business-model support: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says the queue, rating system, and recommendation engine helped customers want a blend of expensive and less expensive titles.
- Cross-company work: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] describes Arnold working with founders, users, analytics, finance, marketing, and warehouse fulfillment.
- Later role: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Arnold later became a product leader at Shutterstock.
