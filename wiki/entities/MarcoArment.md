## Overview
[[MarcoArment]] is identified in the source as the founder who announced [[Instapaper]] as a side project on January 28, 2008.

## Current Profile
The article uses Marco Arment mainly as Instapaper's origin point rather than as a full biographical subject. His role is historically important because the initial side project established the product's save-for-later premise before later teams, owners, platforms, and business models expanded it. The source also names the "Marco days" as an early era remembered by longtime users.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded Instapaper as a side project in 2008.
- Represents the product's earliest independent-development era.
- Built the initial premise around saving web items for later reading.
- Remains part of Instapaper's user-facing history even after later acquisitions.

## Evidence
