## Overview
[[DrDemento]] is represented as the long-running host of *The Dr. Demento Show* and a specialist curator of comedic music.

## Current Profile
In the 2018 interview, Dr. Demento says he is less driven to seek novelty for pleasure and often prefers familiar music. His radio role nevertheless creates a systematic discovery channel: he listens to direct submissions and draws on the Funny Music Project, whose curators' taste aligns with his program.

## Key Characteristics
- Curates a specialist radio outlet for comedic music.
- Listens to direct submissions sent or suggested for the program.
- Uses a taste-aligned specialist clearinghouse as an external filter.
- Distinguishes professional discovery obligations from personal novelty seeking.

## Evidence
- Program role: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] describes his weekly comedy-music show and its exposure function.
- Submission practice: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] reports a dozen or more weekly submissions and says he listens to everything sent or suggested.
- Personal qualification: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] records his reduced drive for novelty and preference for familiar listening.

## Qualifications
The evidence is a self-report from one 2018 interview. Submission volume and complete listening are not independently verified, and his specialist format is not representative of general radio or listener discovery.
