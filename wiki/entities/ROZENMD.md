## Overview
[[ROZENMD]] is [[MaxRozen]]'s business in France, described in the source as the company that runs [[OnlineOrNot]] and sells his products.

## Current Profile
The source presents ROZENMD as a small, founder-led product business whose 2023 growth exceeded expectations. Its main strategic lesson is not diversification, but concentrating on the existing product and allowing operational learning, long-term customer memory, and product maturity to compound.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates OnlineOrNot and sells Max Rozen's products.
- Grew about twice as fast as Rozen expected at the start of 2023.
- Combines SaaS revenue with product sales such as a React useEffect book.
- Remains compatible with the founder's full-time employment rather than replacing it immediately.
