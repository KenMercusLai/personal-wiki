## Overview
[[WenbinFang]] is the founder-operator who builds and runs [[ListenNotes]], a podcast search engine and database, as a one-person internet company on a deliberately boring technology stack.

## Current Profile
The source is a first-person account of how one operator runs a multi-product internet business without employees or fashionable infrastructure. Fang describes working from a private office, rarely working more than eight hours, developing on the master branch of a single repository, and optimizing for earning more from less time rather than saving money by spending more time. His stated posture toward technology is subtractive: keep the proven Django, PostgreSQL, Redis, and Elasticsearch core, configure machines with Ansible, release with a small shell script, monitor with rented services, and leave out Docker, Kubernetes, and serverless. The contribution to the corpus is an operating philosophy - boring tools, rented capability, small scope, fast release - rather than measured business or engineering results.

## Key Characteristics
- Runs a real internet company alone, with services and contractors substituting for headcount.
- Argues that the biggest barrier to building and releasing products is overthinking rather than missing tools or skill.
- Prefers mature, battle-tested technology and treats operational novelty as stage-dependent rather than automatically better.
- Optimizes for time and money leverage, aiming to earn more from less time instead of trading more hours for savings.
- Keeps the engineering surface small: one repository, master-branch development, few branches, and a short release path.
- Works through a broad set of rented SaaS tools for notes, email, payments, DNS, design, and workflow automation.
- Writes in a practitioner-retrospective register, giving concrete stack and process detail but no revenue, usage, or survival data.
