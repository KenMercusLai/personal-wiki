## Overview
[[JamesShore]] is represented in the wiki as an early [[ExtremeProgramming]] practitioner, author of The Art of Agile Development, and co-creator of the [[AgileFluencyModel]].

## Current Profile
The Fowler foreword presents Shore as a practitioner who helped make agile executable for teams rather than merely aspirational. His book is framed as a handbook for doing agile work properly, with later editions reflecting another decade of coaching experience.

Shore's role in the source is also model-building: with [[DianaLarsen]], he created the [[AgileFluencyModel]], which Fowler uses to distinguish basic customer-focused project management from deeper agile capability supported by technical excellence.

## Key Characteristics
- Early pioneer associated with [[ExtremeProgramming]].
- Author of The Art of Agile Development.
- Co-creator of the [[AgileFluencyModel]] with [[DianaLarsen]].
- Represents practice-centered agile learning rather than ceremony-only adoption.
- Fowler treats Shore's revised book as a guide for developing durable agile skills.
