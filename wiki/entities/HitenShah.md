## Overview
[[HitenShah]] is the authorial and practitioner voice behind the Product Habits source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Hiten Shah as a SaaS product commentator synthesizing Price Intelligently's SaaSFest points into a retention-first growth argument. His role in the wiki is tied to the claim that modern SaaS founders cannot rely on acquisition volume alone because competition, low switching costs, and cheap distribution make retained customer value the harder problem.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes about product and SaaS growth strategy.
- Uses SaaSFest and Price Intelligently material to frame acquisition as less defensible.
- Emphasizes product strength, customer usefulness, and retention over raw lead volume.
