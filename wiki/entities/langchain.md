The source says LangChain helps developers rapidly build AI applications that combine large language models with external data sources and APIs.

The source highlights LangChain modules for model calls, prompt templates, chains, memory, vector stores, and agents. It also notes that the framework was receiving strong startup and investor attention at the time of the article.
