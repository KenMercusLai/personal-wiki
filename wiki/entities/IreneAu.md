## Overview
[[IreneAu]] is a design leader represented in this wiki through a 2015 essay that treats design-team morale as an early signal of organizational conditions that will later appear in product quality.

## Current Profile
Au's account connects user experience to the whole company rather than isolating it within the design function. Strategy, scope, process, talent, implementation, and decision rights all reach the user through the product, while designers may lack the authority to repair problems whose causes sit outside their team.

She translates that diagnosis into a two-axis matrix for designers and CEOs. Designers can use morale and current quality together to infer the kind of challenge behind a job opportunity; executives can use the same signals to ask whether the company needs vision, influence, capacity, innovation, or design infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats design outcomes as manifestations of company-wide strategy, process, capability, and power.
- Distinguishes design-team morale as an anticipatory signal from product quality as accumulated output.
- Uses a four-quadrant diagnostic to connect observed conditions with different leadership responses.
- Gives designers and CEOs reciprocal responsibilities for evaluating and improving design environments.
- Emphasizes design's strategic role while recognizing that designers often cannot fix organizational causes alone.

## Evidence
- Organizational lens: [[ux-is-a-canary-in-a-coal-mine-irene-au]] says everything a company does ultimately affects user experience.
- Signal distinction: [[ux-is-a-canary-in-a-coal-mine-irene-au]] labels morale a leading indicator and product quality a lagging indicator of design health.
- Diagnostic method: [[ux-is-a-canary-in-a-coal-mine-irene-au]] maps positive or negative morale against good or poor product quality and proposes a different interpretation for each quadrant.
- Leadership response: [[ux-is-a-canary-in-a-coal-mine-irene-au]] recommends interventions spanning vision, process, empowerment, prioritization, staffing, innovation, and design infrastructure.
- Career judgment: [[ux-is-a-canary-in-a-coal-mine-irene-au]] asks designers to choose opportunities based on the challenge that fits them rather than current polish alone.

## Qualifications
This profile is limited to one 2015 practitioner essay. It does not establish Au's complete career, later views, or empirical validation of the matrix. The article supplies no morale instrument, longitudinal product outcomes, comparison group, or method for separating design conditions from engineering constraints, market forces, regulation, executive turnover, or team-specific differences.
