## Overview
[[AlexiaOhannessian]] is represented in the wiki as [[Trello]]'s 2016 international marketing lead and the operator interviewed about its localization and global launch.

## Current Profile
Ohannessian describes an evidence-led sequence: use current user behavior to select markets, run country experiments, test the translation production model, and then scale. She organized volunteers through one Trello board per language, paired a translation management system with glossaries and contextual material, selected reviewers, used deadlines and product rewards to sustain participation, and moved work to professionals when delivery reliability mattered more than volunteer enthusiasm.

Her role crossed marketing and product delivery. She coordinated with developers on extracted strings and pluralization, with local writers and journalists on terminology and original content, and with events, ambassadors, executives, and PR on launch visibility. The source therefore presents international marketing as orchestration across community, engineering, translation quality, and local activation.

## Key Characteristics
- Chose languages and markets from observed user demand rather than preference alone.
- Treated country launches as experiments whose results informed later investment.
- Designed structured volunteer workflows with context, glossaries, reviewers, peer discussion, deadlines, and rewards.
- Used hybrid sourcing when professional delivery reliability was required.
- Connected localization with local PR, events, ambassadors, content, and global launch material.
- Worked with developers on internationalization constraints including pluralization, gender, and right-to-left text.
