## Overview
[[AmirRajan]] is the software developer and indie game creator whose sabbatical led to the mobile version of [[ADarkRoom]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Rajan as a technically skilled developer who left high-paying corporate work after burnout and used years of savings to search for meaningful creative work. His profile is deliberately ambivalent: he becomes a rare success case when [[ADarkRoom]] reaches #1 on the [[AppStore]], yet he interprets that success through luck, privilege, anxiety, and the difficulty of sustaining indie game development as a livelihood.

## Key Characteristics
- Built a software career with strong school performance, internships, consulting roles, and high compensation before leaving corporate work.
- Used a sabbatical and personal financial sacrifice to search for work that felt meaningful.
- Reinterpreted and ported [[ADarkRoom]] to iOS after connecting with the original web game.
- Treats his success as luck-amplified rather than a generalizable formula.
- Continued making expressive games while avoiding ads, micro-transactions, and exploitative monetization.
- Returned to paid work after later games and game income were not enough to sustain the dream.

## Evidence
- Career base: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] says Rajan earned high compensation in corporate and consulting software roles before quitting.
- Sabbatical risk: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] says he sold possessions, liquidated 401k savings, and lived off accumulated savings.
- Game creation: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] describes Rajan redesigning and porting [[ADarkRoom]] to iOS over about four months.
- Luck framing: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] repeatedly says success depended on luck and that many equally committed developers never receive the same break.
- Monetization stance: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] says Rajan avoided ads, micro-transactions, energy bars, and similar monetization tactics.
- Return to work: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] says dwindling revenue eventually pushed him back into corporate or contract work.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one autobiographical article. It does not independently verify sales, later career details, or the full history of Rajan's games.
