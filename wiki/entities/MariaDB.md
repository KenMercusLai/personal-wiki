## Overview
[[MariaDB]] is a community-developed fork of [[MySQL]] founded by [[MontyWidenius]] after Sun was acquired by Oracle.

## Current Profile
The 2017 source presents MariaDB as the developer-oriented continuation of the MySQL line, involving many people who shared the earlier company's open-source commitments. It emphasizes accepting outside patches and reports that MariaDB had become the default database in many Linux distributions.

## Key Characteristics
- Direct technical and community lineage from MySQL.
- Founded after an ownership change around the original database.
- Presented as more receptive to outside developer patches.
- Staffed partly by former MySQL community members and colleagues.
- Reported as a default database in many Linux distributions at the time of publication.

## Evidence
- Origin: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] says Widenius founded MariaDB after Sun's acquisition by Oracle.
- Community continuity: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] reports that many strong open-source believers from MySQL followed him.
- Contribution posture: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] distinguishes MariaDB as more developer oriented and open to outsider patches.
- Distribution: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] says MariaDB was the default database in many Linux distributions.

## Qualifications
The article supplies no distribution list, adoption data, governance structure, technical comparison, compatibility boundaries, or independent evidence for “more developer oriented.” Its description is historical to 2017 and largely reflects the founder's perspective.
