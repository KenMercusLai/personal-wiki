## Overview
[[Labubu]] is a major [[PopMart]] character IP used in the source to analyze revenue concentration, IP lifecycle risk, and category-expansion potential.

## Current Profile
The source treats Labubu as both a strength and a risk. Its rapid revenue growth proves Pop Mart can create or operate a global hit, but its reported 38% revenue share means a trend reversal would materially affect the company. The author's optimistic case is that Labubu is not a static image: it can be refreshed through new poses, costumes, materials, plush formats, accessories, daily-use goods, collaborations, licensing, and spaces.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as Pop Mart's most important recent revenue-concentration example.
- Raises the unresolved question of whether a 2024-2025 trend can become stable long-term demand.
- Can be extended because the character form supports poses, styling, costumes, and category variation.
- Has potential to move beyond collectible figures into plush, pendants, accessories, daily goods, brand collaborations, and spatial experiences.
- Needs active IP maintenance to remain present across age cohorts and avoid fading after the initial trend.
- Could be damaged if management overuses the image and lets it become visually or commercially stale.

## Evidence
- Revenue concentration: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] says Labubu generated 368 million yuan in 2023 and 14.1 billion yuan in 2025, about 38% of Pop Mart revenue.
- Demand uncertainty: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] explicitly says stable global demand for Labubu toys is hard to calculate.
- Character refresh: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] argues that new looks can renew old-customer repurchase and reach new customers.
- Category expansion: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] lists plush, pillows, pendants, accessories, apparel, daily goods, parks, authorization, OEM production, and collaborations as ways to widen revenue.
- Maintenance risk: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] distinguishes durable IP recognition from stable annual revenue and warns that overexposure could still hurt the character.

## Qualifications
The cited revenue figures and future outlook come from the source essay. The page should not treat the long-term revenue level as known; the source itself says no one can answer whether styling and category expansion will fully offset trend decline.
