## Overview
[[Limboy]] appears in this wiki as the author of a reflective essay on creation, AI-enabled making, high standards, curiosity, and attention.

## Current Profile
The source presents Limboy as a writer synthesizing [[PaulGraham]]'s "make good new things" advice, [[SteveJobs]]' "make something wonderful" philosophy, and current AI tooling into a personal creation ethic. Limboy's emphasis is practical and motivational: make writing, apps, images, or other artifacts; use AI to lower the threshold; but preserve quality through strong inputs, high standards, and real curiosity.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames creation as a meaningful lifelong pursuit.
- Connects older maker philosophies with AI-era creative tooling.
- Treats high standards as necessary when average output becomes cheap.
- Values creation for learning, self-expression, peer discovery, and presence.

## Evidence
- Creation ethic: [[blog-limboy-make-good-new-things]] centers the essay on Graham's "make good new things" and Jobs' "make something wonderful."
- AI tooling: [[blog-limboy-make-good-new-things]] argues that AI coding assistants and image tools open formerly specialized fields to ordinary creators.

- Attention and memory: [[blog-limboy-make-good-new-things]] says finished creations help people focus on the present and leave knots on the rope of life.
