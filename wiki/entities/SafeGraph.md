## Overview
[[SafeGraph]] is presented in a 2019 founder-authored article as a [[DataAsAService]] company organized around facts about physical places.

## Current Profile
The source says SafeGraph connected place attributes through a place identifier and delivered details such as address, category, hours, geometry, and other attributes. It also uses the company to illustrate machine-learning-assisted data transformation, published schemas and change logs, free complementary Census data, and an aspiration to compound customer value by lowering price per data element.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses places as the central theme and join surface for its datasets.
- Combines acquisition, machine-learning-assisted transformation, and documented delivery.
- Presents accuracy improvement and change visibility as competitive signals.
- Supports joinability through a published schema and a place identifier.
- Uses free complementary public data as a way to make paid data easier to apply.

## Evidence
- Place-data model: [[blog-auren-hoffman-safegraph-data-as-a-service-bible]] describes attributes joined through the SafeGraph Place ID.
- Transformation: [[blog-auren-hoffman-safegraph-data-as-a-service-bible]] says the company used computer vision and machine learning to connect satellite imagery, addresses, and points of interest.
- Quality and delivery: [[blog-auren-hoffman-safegraph-data-as-a-service-bible]] points to published schemas and data-improvement change logs.
- Complement strategy: [[blog-auren-hoffman-safegraph-data-as-a-service-bible]] describes a simplified free Census Block Group download intended to help customers combine public and commercial place data.

## Qualifications
Every characteristic here comes from a 2019 article by the company's founder. The page does not establish current products, ownership, practices, coverage, accuracy, pricing, privacy performance, or market position, and claims about monthly compounding customer benefit are stated goals rather than audited results.
