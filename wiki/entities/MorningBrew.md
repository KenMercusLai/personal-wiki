## Overview
[[MorningBrew]] is a newsletter company represented here through an early college ambassador program used to acquire student subscribers across US campuses.

## Current Profile
The source describes Morning Brew iterating from a small, selectively recruited ambassador cohort to automated mass recruitment and then to a tiered hybrid. The hybrid preserved broad recruiting while giving active students advancement, peer access, coaching, scripts, and flyers. Its reported outcome suggests that reach depended on many local participants but subscriber production was concentrated among a small minority.

## Key Characteristics
- Used college students as local distribution partners for a newsletter audience.
- Treated ambassador recruitment and enablement as an iterative operating system rather than a one-time campaign.
- Combined automated top-of-funnel recruitment with selective high-touch support.
- Used recognition, private-group access, career value, and practical tools alongside experiments with money.

## Evidence
- Program iteration: [[happy-xiao-ru-he-kuo-san]] describes three semesters moving from selective recruiting, through automated volume, to a tiered hybrid.
- Concentrated contribution: [[happy-xiao-ru-he-kuo-san]] reports one early ambassador generating 1,000 registrations in two weeks and more than ten hybrid-program ambassadors exceeding 500 subscribers.
- Enablement and motivation: [[happy-xiao-ru-he-kuo-san]] describes coaching, peer learning, scripts, flyers, advancement, résumé value, and affection for the product.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one short secondary account of an early program. Its reported counts lack denominators, attribution, subscriber retention, acquisition costs, campus-level distributions, and comparison with Morning Brew's other channels. It therefore supports the program design and reported outcomes, not a complete company history or a causal explanation of later growth.
