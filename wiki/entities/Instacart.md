## Overview
[[Instacart]] appears as the company context for an integrated data-science operating model and a recommendation example in the First Round Review source.

## Current Profile
The source describes Instacart product teams as mixtures of engineers, data scientists, designers, and product managers organized around domains such as fulfillment, shopper tools, search, and recommendations. Engineers and data scientists report to a technical lead and share product objectives, while the VP of Data Science maintains mentoring, a cross-team professional community, and organization-wide initiatives.

Its recommendation example separates a simple recent-purchase feature from a data-intensive discovery problem: suggesting products a user has not bought requires learning from patterns across users. The case is used to illustrate both the value of behavioral data and the need for explicit scientist-engineer production responsibility.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses mixed product teams rather than a separate standalone data-science function in the reported period.
- Places engineers and data scientists under shared technical leadership.
- Maintains a data-science community and mentoring across locally integrated teams.
- Uses behavioral purchasing data to support product discovery recommendations.
- Treats production collaboration as part of data-science effectiveness.
