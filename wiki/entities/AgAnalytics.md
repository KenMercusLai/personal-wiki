## Overview
[[AgAnalytics]] is a Cornell University data platform presented as combining farm-operation and public data into agricultural decision-support tools.

## Current Profile
The platform connects to [[JohnDeere]]'s Operations Center and enriches farm data with sources such as soil, weather, satellite imagery, insurance information, and field-condition feeds. It supports farmer-facing estimates and forecasts and is positioned as an early foundation for a possible sustainability data hub, although the source gives no measured product, business, or environmental outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects to John Deere's Operations Center to access and analyze farm data.
- Combines private operational data with public and analytical sources rather than treating raw data as the final product.
- Offers tools for crop-insurance estimation, yield forecasting, risk management, imagery, and field monitoring.
- Could support aggregated regional sustainability reporting and food-supply-chain verification.

## Evidence
- Integration and enrichment: [[demystifying-data-monetization]] describes the John Deere connection and the addition of soil, weather, satellite, insurance, and field-condition information.
- Decision support: [[demystifying-data-monetization]] names insurance, yield, and risk tools available to farmers.
- Sustainability extension: [[demystifying-data-monetization]] reports collaboration with the Environmental Defense Fund to investigate a wider agricultural sustainability data hub.

## Qualifications
The source does not explain the platform's technical architecture, data rights, commercial terms, model accuracy, adoption, or measured impact. Its regional sustainability and supply-chain uses are prospective, and aggregation alone does not establish privacy or resistance to reidentification.

## What Changed
- Established Ag-Analytics as a data-enrichment and decision-support platform in the John Deere case.

## Relationships
- [[JohnDeere]] - supplies operational farm data through an Operations Center integration.
- [[DataMonetization]] - demonstrates value creation through enriched insights and tools rather than raw-data resale.
- [[DataFactories]] - performs collection, enrichment, transformation, analytics, and delivery functions.
