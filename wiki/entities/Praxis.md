## Overview
[[Praxis]] is the venture the source says emerged from a question about moving young people from their current position into a career they love at zero cost within a year.

## Current Profile
The available source uses Praxis as a founding anecdote rather than a company profile. Its role in the wiki is to illustrate a question-led venture process: the author did not claim to possess a complete answer, but built the organization so market feedback could help discover one.

## Key Characteristics
- Framed around a career-transition question for young people.
- Proposed a zero-cost path lasting one year or less.
- Built as a way to seek market feedback under uncertainty.
- Not described in enough detail to establish its operating model, outcomes, or present status.
