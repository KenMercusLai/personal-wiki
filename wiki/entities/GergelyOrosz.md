## Overview

## Current Profile
In the available source, Orosz combines Atlassian's public explanations and status updates with customer interviews and public discussion to reconstruct the outage's technical, operational, and commercial consequences. His analysis emphasizes that incident quality includes customer communication, executive ownership, support access, restoration tooling, and migration trust as well as root cause.
