## Overview
[[AlRies]] is cited by [[DariusForoux]] as a marketing author whose category-first positioning advice supports the claim that being different can beat being better.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Ries appears through Foroux's use of The 22 Immutable Laws of Marketing. The relevant idea is not marginal product superiority but the question of what category a new product or person can be first in.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides marketing support for category-first positioning.
- Frames novelty and firstness as stronger than marginal improvement.
- Appears jointly with [[JackTrout]] in Foroux's differentiation argument.

## Evidence
- Category question: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] cites Ries and Trout's advice to ask "First what?" when launching something new.
- Novelty over improvement: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] quotes Ries and Trout on people caring more about what is new than what is merely better.
- Strategy connection: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] uses the citation to connect [[DifferentiationStrategy]] with [[CategoryCreation]].
