## Overview
[[AmazonPrime]] is Amazon's membership program in the Forbes source, framed as the connective system linking retail, subscription revenue, fulfillment, entertainment, physical stores, and third-party seller economics.

## Current Profile
The source says Prime began as free two-day shipping for $79 a year, then grew into a 100 million-plus subscriber program that encouraged more buying while adding perks. By 2018 it was estimated to generate about $10 billion in 2017 subscription revenue, support billions in Prime Day spending, justify entertainment investment, increase the value of Fulfillment by Amazon, and underwrite physical retail expansion through delivery and pickup needs. Bezos explicitly rejects treating Prime as a standalone business because it is tied into Amazon's consumer offering.

## Key Characteristics
- Started as a shipping membership and became a multi-benefit subscription platform.
- Encourages shopping frequency while generating recurring subscription revenue.
- Connects retail, entertainment, fulfillment, third-party sellers, and physical stores.
- Makes Prime Day a member-only shopping event with large transaction volume.
- Requires physical beachheads for same-day delivery and pickup, supporting stores and grocery expansion.
