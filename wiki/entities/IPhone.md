## Overview
[[IPhone]] appears in the source as both the companion device for [[AppleWatch]] and the historical template for understanding how an Apple product can move from dependence to independent computing.

## Current Profile
The Asymco source uses the iPhone in two ways. First, Apple Watch still depends on iPhone activation and plan extension in 2017, making the Watch a companion product inside the [[MobileEcosystem]]. Second, the original iPhone once needed a PC or Mac for activation, yet later became independent; [[HoraceDediu]] uses that path to argue that Apple Watch may eventually cut its own family link. The original iPhone launch frame of a wide-screen iPod, internet communicator, and phone becomes the template for describing the Watch as a small-screen version of the same three roles.

## Key Characteristics
- Companion anchor for Apple Watch activation and connectivity in the source.
- Historical precedent for moving from computer dependence to standalone operation.
- Launch template built around iPod, internet communicator, and phone roles.
- Smartphone platform that Apple Watch can partially borrow usage from.

## Evidence
- Companion anchor: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] says the Watch still could not be activated without an iPhone.
- Independence precedent: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] says early iPhones had to be attached to a computer for activation but later stood alone.
- Launch template: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] recalls the iPhone launch as a wide-screen iPod, internet communicator, and phone.
- Usage migration: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] says the Watch was stealing alerts, messaging, calls, music, and maybe maps from the iPhone.

## Qualifications
This page is currently grounded only in the Apple Watch source's use of the iPhone. Other wiki sources discuss Apple and iOS more broadly, but this entity page has not yet been expanded from those source notes.
