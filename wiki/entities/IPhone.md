## Overview
[[IPhone]] appears as the companion device for [[AppleWatch]], the historical template for Apple products moving from dependence to independence, the current computer alternative that Watch and possible glasses may gradually reduce reliance on, and a product line where Apple can push both premium and entry-level pricing.

## Current Profile
The Asymco source uses the iPhone in two ways. First, Apple Watch still depends on iPhone activation and plan extension in 2017, making the Watch a companion product inside the [[MobileEcosystem]]. Second, the original iPhone once needed a PC or Mac for activation, yet later became independent; [[HoraceDediu]] uses that path to argue that Apple Watch may eventually cut its own family link. The original iPhone launch frame of a wide-screen iPod, internet communicator, and phone becomes the template for describing the Watch as a small-screen version of the same three roles. The Above Avalon product-theory source adds a product-ladder role: the iPhone is designed to be powerful enough to reduce the need for iPad and Mac, while its own tasks may later be handled by Apple Watch and possible [[SmartGlasses]]. In the 2018 XS/XR cycle, larger screens, A12 Bionic silicon, and more intelligent cameras make the iPhone an AR navigator and laptop/desktop alternative. The Above Avalon pricing source adds a segmentation role: stronger demand for higher-priced iPhone 7 Plus models pointed toward future premium iPhones, while bringing back the iPhone 6 in selected markets below iPhone SE showed Apple also lowering entry-level access.

## Key Characteristics
- Companion anchor for Apple Watch activation and connectivity in the source.
- Historical precedent for moving from computer dependence to standalone operation.
- Launch template built around iPod, internet communicator, and phone roles.
- Smartphone platform that Apple Watch can partially borrow usage from.
- Computer alternative strengthened by larger screens, custom silicon, and more capable cameras.
- Product-ladder step whose tasks may migrate down to Apple Watch and possible smart glasses.
- Pricing-segmentation product where premium models and lower-priced older models can coexist.
