## Overview
[[LiMu]] is the author of the essay [[li-mu-yong-sui-ji-ti-du-xia-jiang-lai-you-hua-ren-sheng]], where he turns a machine-learning optimization method into a compact philosophy of personal development.

## Current Profile
In the ingested source, Li Mu appears as a machine-learning educator-author using technical analogy to make life advice more concrete. His profile here is source-scoped: the wiki only records that this essay presents goals, persistence, exploration, rest, and restart through the metaphor of [[StochasticGradientDescent]].

## Key Characteristics
- Uses machine-learning concepts as explanatory metaphors for personal development.
- Treats ambition and persistence as compatible with rest and variable pacing.
- Emphasizes exploration, wrong turns, and humility about starting conditions.

## Evidence
- Technical metaphor: [[li-mu-yong-sui-ji-ti-du-xia-jiang-lai-you-hua-ren-sheng]] maps objective functions, gradients, step sizes, random starts, and convergence onto life choices.
- Pacing: [[li-mu-yong-sui-ji-ti-du-xia-jiang-lai-you-hua-ren-sheng]] says steps can be too small or too large and supports periodic rest.
- Exploration and humility: [[li-mu-yong-sui-ji-ti-du-xia-jiang-lai-you-hua-ren-sheng]] argues that people should look around, make some wrong decisions, and remember that starting near the endpoint is luck.
