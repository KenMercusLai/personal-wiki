## Overview
[[Gmail]] is Google's email service used in the source as an example of invitation scarcity creating demand.

## Current Profile
The source says Gmail launched with limited server capacity and converted that constraint into scarcity. By making the service invite-only and starting with opinion formers who could refer friends, Google made access feel exclusive.

## Key Characteristics
- Email service from [[Google]].
- Launched with invite-only access.
- Used scarcity and referral access to create curiosity and demand.

## Evidence
- Invite scarcity: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says Gmail launched by invitation only with about 1,000 initial opinion formers.
- Exclusivity effect: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says the strategy made signing up feel like joining an exclusive club.
