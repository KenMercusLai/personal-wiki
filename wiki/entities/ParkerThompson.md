## Overview
[[ParkerThompson]] is represented in the wiki by a 2017 Pivotal interview on engineering-team productivity, product-team design, startup scaling, technical craft, talent geography, and inclusive hiring.

## Current Profile
The source identifies Thompson as a former Pivot, an AngelList partner advising startups on scaling teams and products, and the person behind the pseudonymous Startup L. Jackson account. His management position is consistently team-centered: leaders should judge net group output, clarify cross-functional responsibilities, prefer problem-oriented craft over technology identity, and address inclusion before a homogeneous culture hardens.

## Key Characteristics
- Reframes the "10x engineer" debate around team-level productivity and downstream cost.
- Treats design, engineering, and product management as distinct but communicating responsibilities.
- Prefers collaborative, problem-oriented craftspeople over rigid technology identity.
- Distinguishes tiny product-validation teams from the organizational systems required after demand appears.
- Frames delayed inclusive hiring as compounding cultural debt.
