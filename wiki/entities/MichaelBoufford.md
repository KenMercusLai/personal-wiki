## Overview
[[MichaelBoufford]] is represented in this wiki as the Greenhouse engineering leader whose organization-design framework was summarized from FirstMark's 2017 CTO Summit.

## Current Profile
The source describes Boufford as [[Greenhouse]]'s first engineer in 2012 and later its VP of Engineering. His main relevance here is not biographical breadth, but his claim that engineering methods can be applied to scaling a software organization: define the problem, gather requirements, explore the design space, implement the smallest useful process, validate through feedback and data, and keep iterating.

## Key Characteristics
- Early engineering leader at Greenhouse, described as joining as the first engineer in 2012.
- Scaled the engineering team to about 60 engineers serving software used by 2,000 companies.
- Uses engineering design vocabulary to reason about team structure, values, culture, process, and validation.
- Frames organizational health around hiring, retention, productivity, empowerment, continuous improvement, respect, and self-healing.
- Treats feedback loops and metrics as necessary for validating whether an organization is working.

## Evidence

- Organization design method: [[build-a-healthy-organization-by-thinking-like-an-engineer]] presents his five-step process of problem definition, requirements gathering, exploration and design, implementation, and validation.
- Target state: [[build-a-healthy-organization-by-thinking-like-an-engineer]] gives his defining statement for a stable, self-healing, high-quality, productive, improving, empowered, and respectful software engineering team.
