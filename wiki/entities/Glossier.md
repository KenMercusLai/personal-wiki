## Overview
[[Glossier]] is a beauty company represented here as [[DavidPerell]]'s main example of an audience-first, influencer-led [[NakedBrands|Naked Brand]].

## Current Profile
The 2017 essay presents Glossier as emerging from [[EmilyWeiss]]'s existing Into the Gloss audience, sustaining loyalty through personal communication, and incorporating fan input into product ideas. This supports a community-participation narrative but does not establish the company's full growth mechanism or current condition.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered a community that its founder had cultivated before the first product launch.
- Blended founder identity, lifestyle imagery, company milestones, and product communication.
- Used commenter and fan participation as an input to product ideation.

## Evidence
- Pre-launch audience: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] describes Into the Gloss as preceding Glossier's first product.
- Founder-brand continuity: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] links Weiss's personal posting to the company's perceived accessibility.
- Participatory development: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] reports that the company crowdsourced new product ideas.

## Qualifications
The source is a 2017 interpretive essay, not company documentation or independent performance analysis. It does not separate community effects from product quality, visual identity, paid distribution, retail, financing, category growth, or founder celebrity.

- Created a qualified profile of Glossier's audience-before-product and participatory-brand case.
