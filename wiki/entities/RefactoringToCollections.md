## Overview
[[RefactoringToCollections]] is [[AdamWathan]]'s digital book and training product about applying functional-programming and collection-pipeline ideas to cleaner PHP code.

## Current Profile
The product began as a proposed 30-40 page mini-book but expanded into a roughly 150-page book with unit-test exercises, four hours of screencasts, and, in its highest tier, the source code to the Nitpick CI Laravel application. It launched through three discounted packages priced at $29, $59, and $135 and reportedly generated $61,392 from 905 purchases during May 18-20, 2016.

## Key Characteristics
- Teaches refactoring of loops and conditionals into collection pipelines.
- Grew from a narrow book concept into a multi-format training product.
- Included exercises in every package and screencasts in the two higher packages.
- Used an existing production Laravel codebase to differentiate the highest tier.
- Launched with a three-day 25% discount and an email-led promotion sequence.
- Produced a reported $67.84 average selling price, more than twice the book-only launch price.

## Evidence
- Scope and content: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] documents the expansion from a mini-book concept into the book, exercises, videos, and source project.
- Packaging: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] lists the $29 Bare Essentials, $59 Premium Training, and $135 Complete Reference packages.
- Launch result: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] reports 258, 427, and 220 sales across those tiers, respectively, totaling $61,392.
- Durability: [[the-61-392-book-launch-that-let-me-quit-my-job]] reports more than $100,000 within three months and $138,835 after eleven months.

## Qualifications
All product, sales, and long-tail revenue figures are self-reported gross revenue without costs, refunds, taxes, customer outcomes, or independent verification. The charted hourly values do not exactly reconcile with the stated daily and package totals, and the launch's results cannot be separated cleanly from Wathan's prior audience, topic fit, expertise, social network, discount, or timing.
