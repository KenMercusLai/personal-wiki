## Overview
[[GaryVaynerchuk]] is represented in this wiki as a marketing practitioner who argues that brands should create useful or entertaining content whose form fits the audience, platform, and consumption experience, then judge it by attention and action rather than reach alone.

## Current Profile
Across the two 2016 essays, Vaynerchuk presents content marketing as value before sale: help or entertain repeatedly, fit the platform and audience, earn real consumption, and only then expect a commercial result. [[content-is-king-but-context-is-god-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] subordinates the marketer's agenda to context, connects local execution to a stable company story, and extends that context internally through a shared north star and frequent leader communication. [[numbers-dont-matter-influence-does-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] adds a measurement rule: impressions, clicks, and follower totals have little standalone meaning when exposure is inattentive, interaction is accidental, or the audience is purchased; depth is visible through voluntary attention, redistribution, and action. His examples treat platform differences as operating context rather than permanent rankings.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames brands as media producers that earn commercial permission by providing value first.
- Treats platform psychology and audience intent as primary creative constraints.
- Opposes forced interruption when it steals time or produces misleading exposure and engagement signals.
- Separates nominal reach and follower volume from attentive consumption and downstream action.
- Treats a small but responsive audience as potentially more influential than a large indifferent or inauthentic one.
- Connects individual posts to a consistent long-term brand narrative.
- Uses close leadership communication to spread company goals and expressive norms.

## Evidence
- Value before sale: [[content-is-king-but-context-is-god-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] summarizes his "jab, jab, jab, right hook" sequence as repeated value followed by a sales ask.
- Contextual execution: [[content-is-king-but-context-is-god-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] contrasts shopping intent on Pinterest with social catch-up on Facebook and places platform and audience ahead of the marketer's agenda.
- Non-interruption: [[content-is-king-but-context-is-god-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] uses an accidental Acura mobile-ad click to argue that counted engagement can conceal annoyance and absent intent.
- Attention over reach: [[numbers-dont-matter-influence-does-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] distinguishes recorded impressions from actual consumption and says purchased followers with no response have no demonstrated value.
- Audience depth: [[numbers-dont-matter-influence-does-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] contrasts a smaller audience that buys or redistributes with a larger inactive audience and argues that one relevant share can initiate wider distribution.
- Platform-context tactic: [[numbers-dont-matter-influence-does-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] reports sending more than 1,000 advance books to Instagram influencers for substantial photo reviews because he expected high attention there in 2016.
- Brand coherence: [[content-is-king-but-context-is-god-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] says every tweet, comment, post, and image should ladder to a core story and company north star.
- Organizational transmission: [[content-is-king-but-context-is-god-gary-vaynerchuk-medium]] recommends sustained leader-employee contact so company goals and context spread through the organization.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to two short 2016 practitioner essays. They provide prescriptions, hypotheticals, and self-reported tactics rather than comparative evidence that value-first content, depth of attention, platform selection, or brand consistency improves sales or loyalty. Impression and follower counts can still be useful when definitions, cohorts, fraud controls, attention quality, conversion, cost, and downstream outcomes are supplied. The Instagram, Twitter, and Snapchat comparisons are historically situated, and the reported influencer campaign includes no response, conversion, cost, or counterfactual data. The quote-card claim that established influence makes starting over safer is plausible but unmeasured.

## What Changed
- Added the distinction between nominal reach and voluntary attention, redistribution, and action.
- Added audience responsiveness and authenticity as the conditions that make follower counts meaningful.
- Qualified the 2016 platform comparisons and influencer tactic as historical, self-reported, and outcome-free.
