## Overview
[[BrianBalfour]] is represented as a growth operator associated with retention-first strategy and behavior-segmented customer-feedback research.

## Current Profile
One source uses Brian Balfour to anchor a retention-first growth framing and identifies him as CEO of [[Reforge]] and former VP of Growth at [[HubSpot]]. A second, earlier practitioner article credits him with teaching [[DanWolch]] a workflow that starts from an activation or retention question, identifies behavior-defined user cohorts, and solicits qualitative explanations from the people whose histories fit that question. Together the sources connect retention strategy with targeted evidence collection, though neither is a direct methodological account from Balfour.

## Key Characteristics
- Growth-strategy voice cited for a retention-first growth principle.
- Associated in the source with Reforge leadership.
- Associated in the source with prior HubSpot growth leadership.
- Credited by another practitioner with teaching a behavior-segmented feedback workflow.

## Evidence
- Retention principle: [[acquisition-is-easy-retention-is-hard-product-habits]] cites Brian Balfour for the claim that retention is the foundation of growth.
- Reforge role: [[acquisition-is-easy-retention-is-hard-product-habits]] identifies him as CEO of [[Reforge]].
- HubSpot role: [[acquisition-is-easy-retention-is-hard-product-habits]] identifies him as former VP of Growth at [[HubSpot]].
- Research-method attribution: [[identify-users-with-the-most-valuable-feedback-startup-grind-medium]] says Balfour showed Wolch how to define the target cohort, locate users in event data, request direct replies, and code the results.

## Qualifications
The evidence consists of two secondary practitioner articles. The newer retention article cites a principle attributed to Balfour, while Wolch's earlier article credits him with a process but does not quote Balfour or show his original documentation. The wiki does not independently verify or summarize his broader body of growth work.
