## Overview
[[BrianBalfour]] is represented as a growth operator associated with retention-first strategy and behavior-segmented customer-feedback research.

## Current Profile
One source uses Brian Balfour to anchor a retention-first growth framing and identifies him as CEO of [[Reforge]] and former VP of Growth at [[HubSpot]]. A second, earlier practitioner article credits him with teaching [[DanWolch]] a workflow that starts from an activation or retention question, identifies behavior-defined user cohorts, and solicits qualitative explanations from the people whose histories fit that question. The 2016 interview essay adds Balfour's direct candidate-side diagnostic: probe retention, lifetime value, and funnel drop-offs until the company reaches a legitimate confidentiality boundary, then ask how a specific product change would actually move through the organization. Together the sources connect retention strategy, targeted evidence collection, and the operating conditions needed to act on growth analysis.

## Key Characteristics
- Growth-strategy voice cited for a retention-first growth principle.
- Associated in the source with Reforge leadership.
- Associated in the source with prior HubSpot growth leadership.
- Credited by another practitioner with teaching a behavior-segmented feedback workflow.
- Encourages candidates to investigate both growth metrics and the cross-functional path from idea to implementation.

## Qualifications
The retention and feedback evidence is secondary: one article cites a principle attributed to Balfour, while Wolch credits him with a process but does not show his original documentation. The interview essay quotes his advice directly but supplies no evidence that asking these questions predicts candidate or company outcomes. The wiki does not independently summarize his broader body of growth work.
