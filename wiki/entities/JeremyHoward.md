## Overview
[[JeremyHoward]] is a coauthor of the 2012 O'Reilly article introducing the [[DrivetrainApproach]] and is identified there as the founder of [[OptimalDecisionsGroup]].

## Current Profile
Within the source, Howard connects a general framework for objective-driven data products to ODG's insurance-pricing work. The article attributes to the firm a model assembly line combining customer price response, insurer profit, retention, simulation, and constrained optimization, while explicitly disclosing Howard's founder relationship to the case.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthored the objective-levers-data-models framework.
- Founded the insurance-pricing company used as the article's central commercial case.
- Advocates simulation and optimization as the bridge from predictive models to decisions.

## Evidence
- Authorship: [[designing-great-data-products-oreilly]] names Howard with Margit Zwemer and Mike Loukides.
- Founder role: [[designing-great-data-products-oreilly]] identifies Howard as the founder of Optimal Decisions Group.
- Technical position: [[designing-great-data-products-oreilly]] argues for objectives, levers, interventional data, component models, simulation, and optimization.

## Qualifications
This profile is deliberately limited to the supplied 2012 article. Its insurance claims are not independent evidence because Howard founded the featured company, and the source does not document his broader career, later work, or the exact division of authorship.
