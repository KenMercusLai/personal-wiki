## Overview
[[MelaniePowley]] is represented in this wiki as a University of Otago researcher and coauthor of a naturalistic university note-taking study.

## Current Profile
Powley and three colleagues obtained complete psychology-course notes after final examinations, analyzed ten target lectures, and compared note quantity and presentation measures with student performance. Their findings favor a conditional account in which fuller notes preserve broadly tested lecture material, without proving that simply increasing word count improves learning.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthored an observational study designed to preserve ordinary course and revision conditions.
- Helped examine both total note coverage and formatting-related features.
- Participated in a cautious interpretation that distinguishes strong prediction from causation.

## Evidence
Naturalistic scope:
- [[nye-et-al-student-note-taking-related-to-university-examination-performance]] credits Powley jointly for studying notes produced across 75 normal lectures and available to students during later preparation.

Measured findings:
- [[nye-et-al-student-note-taking-related-to-university-examination-performance]] reports strong quantity-performance associations but little consistent relationship for abbreviation, layout, underlining, or highlighting.
