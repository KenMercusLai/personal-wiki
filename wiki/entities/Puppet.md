## Overview
[[Puppet]] is represented here as the organization whose high-performing IT-team guidance informs [[IrmaKornilova]]'s DevOps transformation article.

## Current Profile
In this source, Puppet provides the practitioner framework behind building a DevOps business case, aligning development and operations incentives, selecting delivery and reliability metrics, and starting with small measurable changes. The page does not yet profile Puppet's software products independently of that role.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies practitioner guidance for organizational DevOps transformation.
- Connects cultural adoption with delivery and reliability measures.
- Supports small pilots as evidence for broader change.

## Evidence
- Guidance source: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] says its theories and practices came from a Puppet white paper on high-performing IT teams.
- Metric framing: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] groups lead time and deployment frequency with uptime, change failure rate, and mean time to recover.
- Adoption framing: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] presents small wins and internal advocates as the path to wider adoption.
