## Overview
[[OfferUp]] is presented as a broad local-commerce marketplace spanning multiple kinds of used goods rather than one narrow classifieds vertical.

## Current Profile
The source uses OfferUp as a proposed winner in Craigslist's for-sale category because the same person may want to sell both furniture and cars. That breadth is meant to pool attention and marketplace activity across adjacent local-commerce jobs. The author also identifies OfferUp as a portfolio company, so the profile records the strategic example without treating the prediction as independently validated performance evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Local-commerce marketplace covering multiple types of used goods.
- Category-horizontal alternative to single-product resale apps.
- Portfolio company used by the author to illustrate his rebundling thesis.
