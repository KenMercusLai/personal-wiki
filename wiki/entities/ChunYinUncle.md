## Overview
[[ChunYinUncle]] is the author who shares the authorized account of an independent developer's AI coding workflow.

## Current Profile
In the source, Chun Yin Uncle acts as an observer and synthesizer. He begins from skepticism prompted by a backend developer's warning about AI coding risk, asks a successful independent-developer friend for comparison, then publishes the friend's detailed workflow and adds community discussion from [[QuanXiao]].

## Key Characteristics
- Frames AI coding as a project-management risk when uncontrolled output pushes failure to the end.
- Uses an independent developer's authorized experience as the main evidence.
- Emphasizes task decomposition and precise expression as the hard parts of successful AI coding.
- Connects individual practice to broader programmer discussion inside [[QuanXiao]].
