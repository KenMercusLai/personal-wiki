## Overview
[[Meituan]] is presented as a China-based local-services platform combining transactions with substantial in-house fulfillment.

## Current Profile
The 2019 Skift source uses Meituan as a [[SuperApp]] comparison for [[GoogleMaps]]. Its app spans food and grocery delivery, hotel booking, group buying, movie tickets, and attraction access, while a large driver network performs physical fulfillment. This gives Meituan more control over transactions and customer experience than Google's partner-heavy advertising and referral model, but also brings infrastructure, support, and liability costs.

## Key Characteristics
- Bundles multiple local-commerce and travel services in one app.
- Operates a large physical delivery network rather than relying only on referral partners.
- Uses QR-mediated transactions for attraction entry in the source example.
- Competes through a transaction-and-fulfillment model distinct from Google's advertising-led Maps model.

## Evidence
- **Service breadth.** [[google-maps-is-ready-to-transform-the-world-of-superapps-a-skift-deep-dive-skift]] lists food delivery, hotels, group buying, movie tickets, grocery delivery, and other local services.
- **Operational control.** [[google-maps-is-ready-to-transform-the-world-of-superapps-a-skift-deep-dive-skift]] reports roughly 600,000 drivers supporting delivery operations.
- **Attraction transactions.** [[google-maps-is-ready-to-transform-the-world-of-superapps-a-skift-deep-dive-skift]] relays Meituan's claim that it sent two million visitors to attractions on one April 2019 holiday day, often using entrance QR scans.
- **Business-model contrast.** [[google-maps-is-ready-to-transform-the-world-of-superapps-a-skift-deep-dive-skift]] contrasts Meituan's operated services with Google Maps' advertising, API, and third-party fulfillment model.

## Qualifications
The page is grounded in one 2019 comparative article. Its scale figures are company-reported and not independently verified here, and the source does not provide profitability, service-quality, labor, regulatory, or later-outcome evidence. Broad service scope does not by itself establish consistent integration or user value.

- Added the tradeoff between customer-experience control and operational cost or liability.
