## Overview
[[AlexPressland]] is the BBC product manager in the source's out-of-home and mobile distribution case.

## Current Profile
The source presents Pressland as an individual-contributor product manager who saw how IP-based syndicated content could help the BBC reach audiences outside conventional broadcast contexts. Her work is framed as force-of-will product management in a large institution: she ran experiments with tailored content for specific venues, navigated editorial and legal resistance, and proposed a broader BBC Out Of Home strategy that helped shape BBC Mobile.

## Key Characteristics
- Led an early BBC content-syndication effort before the iPhone era.
- Looked for audiences not reached by conventional TV and radio broadcast channels.
- Ran experiments with tailored content for venue-specific electronic billboard screens.
- Pushed against editorial and legal constraints around new distribution contexts.
- Later ran product for Bauer Xcel Media according to the source.

## Evidence
- Syndication background: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Pressland had just led a product effort enabling the BBC to syndicate content.
- Audience search: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says she looked for UK audiences the BBC was not reaching through conventional broadcast media.
- Experiments: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] describes tailored content experiments for city-center electronic billboard venues.
- Organizational resistance: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says editorial and legal cultures were obstacles to the new model.
- Strategic result: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says the work fueled a shift toward content distribution and became the basis for BBC Mobile.
