## Overview
[[MikeStringer]] is represented here as a [[Datascope]] cofounder who defines [[AugmentedIntelligence]] and argues that data science should learn from [[HumanCenteredDesign]].

## Current Profile
Stringer describes augmented intelligence as technology extending and enhancing people's capabilities rather than existing separately from people or replacing them. He treats the IDEO-Datascope combination as reciprocal: data science gives designers a new medium, while design practice asks data scientists to reconsider inputs, methods, users' needs, and potential negative outcomes. He uses reported algorithmic discrimination as a reason to explore harms before systems reproduce them.

## Key Characteristics
- Cofounded Datascope with [[DeanMalmgren]].
- Defines augmented intelligence through extension of human capability rather than replacement.
- Argues that data scientists can learn from the human-centered design community.
- Treats raw-data selection, collection, and algorithm construction as design choices.
- Advocates exploring negative outcomes before deployment to reduce bias propagation.

## Qualifications
The source reports Stringer's position during the acquisition announcement and supplies no controlled comparison between his proposed process and conventional data-science practice. Anticipating harms through design research may expose risks but does not by itself correct biased data, objectives, institutions, deployment conditions, or power relationships.
