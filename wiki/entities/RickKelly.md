## Overview
[[RickKelly]] is a programmer whose path from defense contracting through ZeniMax, independent game development, and computer security illustrates the gap between creative ownership and sustainable game employment.

## Current Profile
Kelly entered games after seven years at Lockheed Martin, worked on Elder Scrolls Online login services, and valued access to veteran game engineers. Employer ownership terms for outside creations troubled him, and after launch he founded an independent company. His debut game reached prominent mobile-store rankings but did not keep him in the industry; he later chose computer-security work and preserved game making as personally owned hobby work.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered professional games after a stable technical career outside the industry.
- Valued large-studio learning while objecting to broad ownership of personal creations.
- Left responsibly after completing console-integration work.
- Demonstrated that visible indie-store success need not create a durable livelihood.
- Prioritized ownership of creative work over returning to available game jobs.
