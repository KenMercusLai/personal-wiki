## Overview
[[UPS]] is a parcel carrier presented in the 2016 source as both an essential Amazon partner and a company exposed to Amazon's effort to add its own delivery capacity.

## Current Profile
The article describes a mutually dependent but tense relationship. Amazon needed UPS capacity and said its business with the carrier was growing. A disputed surge in Amazon packages contributed to missed Christmas deliveries in 2013, after which UPS improved visibility into customer forecasts and Amazon accelerated its own sorting and delivery buildout. UPS CEO David Abney nevertheless described Amazon publicly as a good customer in a mutually beneficial relationship.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides network capacity Amazon still needed even while building overlapping infrastructure.
- Experienced peak-capacity stress during the disputed 2013 holiday surge.
- Improved visibility into customer holiday loads after the failure.
- Publicly described Amazon as a good customer in a mutually beneficial relationship.

## Evidence
- Partnership and dependence: [[will-amazon-kill-fedex]] quotes Bezos saying Amazon was growing its UPS business while adding supplemental capacity.
- Peak failure: [[will-amazon-kill-fedex]] describes UPS being overwhelmed in December 2013 amid disagreement over Amazon's tender timing and agreed volume.
- Operational response: [[will-amazon-kill-fedex]] says UPS subsequently developed better visibility into customers' holiday loads.
- Public stance: [[will-amazon-kill-fedex]] quotes CEO David Abney describing Amazon as a good customer and the relationship as mutually beneficial.

## Qualifications
The article preserves conflicting accounts of the 2013 failure and does not independently resolve responsibility. Its strategic interpretation is from 2016 and should not be treated as current.

## What Changed
- Created UPS as the clearest case of simultaneous Amazon partnership, dependency, and competitive exposure.

## Relationships
- [[Amazon]] - major customer, capacity partner, and builder of overlapping logistics infrastructure.
- [[FedEx]] - fellow carrier named in the same holiday-capacity and competitive debate.
- [[AmazonPrime]] - source of the fast-delivery and holiday-volume pressure placed on UPS.
- [[LogisticsVerticalIntegration]] - Amazon's internal buildout followed the carrier capacity failure described in the source.
