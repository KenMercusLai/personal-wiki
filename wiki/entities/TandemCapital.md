## Overview
[[TandemCapital]] is a venture firm represented in the wiki through [[DougRenert]]'s analysis of anonymized revenue and growth claims from 22 [[YCombinator]] Demo Day companies.

## Current Profile
The source describes Tandem as moving from a pre-seed model, where rising round sizes and valuations made YC deals difficult to support, into traditional seed investments of $1 million or more. Renert says the firm then resumed Demo Day attendance and backed one company in each of two recent cohorts. Tandem's analysis is used to question whether short-window growth rates deserve the predictive weight founders and investors can place on them.

## Key Characteristics
- Venture firm associated with pre-seed and later $1 million-plus seed investing.
- Produced the article's analysis of 22 anonymized YC revenue-growth claims.
- Used constant-rate extrapolation to illustrate the limits of reading a short growth window as a durable trajectory.
- Is presented through an interested partner's account rather than independent fund-performance evidence.

## Evidence
- Strategy shift: [[growth-as-a-false-signal-in-y-combinator-startups-techcrunch]] says Tandem returned to YC Demo Days after moving from its earlier pre-seed model into larger seed checks.
- Cohort activity: [[growth-as-a-false-signal-in-y-combinator-startups-techcrunch]] says the firm backed one company in each of the two recent cohorts discussed by Renert.
- Metric analysis: [[growth-as-a-false-signal-in-y-combinator-startups-techcrunch]] attributes the 22-company growth calculations to Tandem Capital.

## Qualifications
The source is written by a Tandem cofounder and provides no fund-return, selection, or comparative performance data. Company names, starting values, and later outcomes are omitted, and two revenue values rely on an assumed 15% transaction fee applied to GMV. The three chart files were unavailable during ingest, limiting verification to the aggregate figures repeated in prose.

## What Changed
- Created Tandem Capital as the investor and analysis context for the YC growth-signal critique.
