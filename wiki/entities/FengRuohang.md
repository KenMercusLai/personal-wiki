## Overview
[[FengRuohang]] is the author of the ingested essay defending deep AI use in public article writing.

## Current Profile
In this source, Feng presents himself as a database distribution author, entrepreneur, and high-frequency public writer who uses AI openly because it saves time without, in his view, reducing the depth or sharpness of the finished work. His writing workflow treats AI as a collaborator and multiplier while keeping topic choice, argument structure, source verification, revisions, title direction, and accountability with the human author.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses AI deeply and openly in article production rather than treating it as something to hide.
- Chooses topics from broad reading, thinking, conversation, and prior Claude discussions.
- Owns the argument skeleton before asking AI to expand a draft.
- Uses Gemini and ChatGPT for cross-checking while personally verifying critical facts.
- Revises AI drafts through several rounds before publication.
- Uses AI for titles, image prompt ideation, generated visuals, and final layout support.

## Evidence
- Open use: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] directly acknowledges deep AI use and rejects the need to conceal it.
- Topic ownership: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] says article subjects come from the author's reading, thought, exchange, and Claude conversations.
- Structure ownership: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] says the article's entry point, viewpoint, evidence, and logic are decided before AI drafts.
- Verification practice: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] describes cross-checking with Gemini and ChatGPT plus original-source checks for important facts.
- Revision practice: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] describes three to five rounds of argument, wording, and structure changes.
- Packaging workflow: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] describes using Codex for layout and AI for title and image prompt options.
