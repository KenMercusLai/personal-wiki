## Overview
[[Hawstein]] is an independent creator who left employment in 2018, first built the algorithm education product [[AlgoCasts]], and later shifted into overseas subscription SaaS.

## Current Profile
The source presents Hawstein as a technically strong, autonomy-oriented creator who learned to combine building with selling. His independent path began with content production, but the stronger long-term fit became [[BootstrappedSaaS]]: a small product business serving global customers through modular infrastructure such as [[Stripe]] and a lightweight U.S. company. He prefers remaining solo for now because freedom, low coordination overhead, and limited social interaction are central to how he wants to work.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses technical skill as a starting advantage, especially for quickly building complete SaaS products.
- Treats independent creation as a long-term path rather than a short experiment.
- Learns heavily from overseas Indie Hacker communities, blogs, podcasts, and creator stories.
- Values autonomy enough to avoid hiring even after researching support delegation.
- Practices active selling through reviews, outreach, content, ads, affiliate incentives, and customer support.
- Solves customer problems pragmatically by pruning the problem space instead of overbuilding.
