## Overview
[[Makelight]] is the bootstrapped education, community, membership, and creator-tools business described by cofounder [[StefLewandowski]] in a 2016 retrospective.

## Current Profile
Makelight grew from [[EmilyQuinton]]'s photography blog, workshops, and social audience into an online course teaching people to improve photography for Instagram, Pinterest, and blogs. The founders then combined courses and a membership community with tools for color and hashtag analysis. Lewandowski reports that the business reached $300,000 in turnover, a first $30,000 month, roughly 2,000 paying customers, several hundred members, and an NPS near 80, but the article ends before resolving how the company would finance its next stage.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as a simple paid online-course test.
- Served primarily creative entrepreneurs, Instagram users, and bloggers.
- Combined education, community, recurring membership, and software tools.
- Used preorders and customer revenue to finance production and operations.
- Built around family compatibility rather than a default unicorn outcome.
- Faced growth constraints despite reported early traction.

## Evidence
- Initial product: [[do-what-you-love-and-pay-the-bills-stef-lewandowski-medium]] describes a photography course launched with Squarespace, Mailchimp, Stripe, Instagram, and Facebook.
- Revenue model: [[do-what-you-love-and-pay-the-bills-stef-lewandowski-medium]] describes course sales, preorders, and several hundred monthly-paying members.
- Product portfolio: [[do-what-you-love-and-pay-the-bills-stef-lewandowski-medium]] pairs courses and community with color-analytics and hashtag-research tools.
- Reported traction: [[do-what-you-love-and-pay-the-bills-stef-lewandowski-medium]] gives the turnover, monthly revenue, audience, customer, membership, and NPS figures as of 2016.
- Financing boundary: [[do-what-you-love-and-pay-the-bills-stef-lewandowski-medium]] says bootstrapping cash flow was limiting growth and lists angel investment, crowdfunding, and loans as open options.

## Qualifications
All performance measures are first-party historical claims. Turnover is not profit, audience size is not active use, NPS is not retention, and the source supplies no margins, acquisition costs, cohort data, founder-labor accounting, financing outcome, or current company status.

## What Changed
- Created Makelight as a mixed education, community, membership, and tools business rather than a software-only bootstrapping case.
