## Overview
[[JohnDeere]] is an agricultural-equipment company used in the source as a case of customer-facing data monetization through farm-data integration and analytics.

## Current Profile
John Deere's Operations Center supplies detailed farm-operation data to [[AgAnalytics]], where it can be combined with public and analytical sources for insurance estimates, yield forecasts, risk management, satellite imagery, and field-condition monitoring. The case shows partnership-based data enrichment creating customer value and potentially supporting regional sustainability evidence, but the source does not disclose commercial results or farmer-level governance controls.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides an Operations Center integration through which farm data can reach Ag-Analytics.
- Contributes detailed agricultural data that becomes more useful when combined with soil, weather, satellite, insurance, and field-condition sources.
- Supports decision tools for yield, risk, crop insurance, and potentially conservation practice.
- Illustrates external [[DataMonetization]] through partnership rather than raw-data sale alone.

## Evidence
- Platform integration: [[demystifying-data-monetization]] identifies connection to John Deere's Operations Center as the first step in the agricultural analytics case.
- Enriched decisions: [[demystifying-data-monetization]] describes combined datasets supporting insurance, imagery, yield, risk, and real-time field analysis.
- Wider evidence: [[demystifying-data-monetization]] says anonymized, aggregated data could eventually quantify regional runoff, emissions, and conservation progress.

## Qualifications
The source is a 2018 strategic article, not an outcome evaluation. It reports no adoption, revenue, accuracy, farmer-consent, access-control, reidentification, or sustainability-impact measurements, and the proposed regional data hub is described as potential future work.

## What Changed
- Established John Deere's profile as the operational-data partner in an agricultural analytics and sustainability case.

## Relationships
- [[AgAnalytics]] - receives John Deere Operations Center data and combines it with other sources for farmer decision support.
- [[DataMonetization]] - the partnership is an example of creating external value from enriched operational data.
