## Overview
[[HenriDeshays]] is a Newfund venture capitalist credited as coauthor and co-investigator of a 2018 survey comparing founder and VC views of investor value and partner selection.

## Current Profile
The source explicitly asks readers to attribute the work to Deshays alongside [[CarlFritjofsson]] and [[OwenReynolds]]. Deshays helped frame and conduct the mirrored survey of founders and VCs, which found gaps in perceived investor impact, contact frequency, service uptake, and the importance of speed and brand. The available profile is limited to this study and his stated Newfund affiliation.

## Key Characteristics
- Venture-capital practitioner affiliated with Newfund in the source.
- Co-designer and coauthor of the founder–VC survey.
- Contributor to a practitioner-led critique of investor value-add claims.
