## Overview
[[SunilSadasivan]] is represented in the wiki as [[Buffer]]'s CTO and early technical team member who left the company in 2017 after strategic misalignment over Buffer's next phase.

## Current Profile
The source presents Sadasivan as an early technical leader who spent about four and a half years working with [[JoelGascoigne]] and shaped Buffer's product and culture. He supported product quality and company culture, but preferred pursuing them alongside team growth, senior leadership hiring, further fundraising, and a more traditional board setup. His departure is framed as a need for a path where he could be fully aligned and continue his own growth.

## Key Characteristics
- Buffer CTO and one of the company's earliest technical team members.
- Helped shape Buffer's product and culture over roughly four and a half years.
- Valued product quality and company culture while preferring a more growth-oriented operating path.
- Favored team growth, senior hires, further fundraising, and more traditional board structure.
- Planned to take time to decide his next step, with possibilities including another startup or a high-impact engineering role.
