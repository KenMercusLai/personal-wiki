## Overview
[[NickSoman]] is represented as a growth practitioner using unfamiliar systems and live experiment ideation to observe candidate thinking.

## Current Profile
The 2016 source affiliates Soman with [[Gusto]]. His “growth hack a city” prompt removes familiar product templates and asks candidates to reason about attracting residents and developing an ecosystem from zero. His live experiment-backlog exercise pushes candidates beyond prepared ideas, first testing breadth under time pressure and then asking for objective assessment.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses unfamiliar ecosystems to expose first-principles growth reasoning.
- Tests live experiment generation rather than prepared analysis alone.
- Pushes beyond an initial comfortable set of ideas.
- Pairs divergent ideation with later evaluation of idea quality.
