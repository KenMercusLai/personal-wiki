## Overview
[[Hardbound]] was a mobile storytelling startup described by [[NathanBashaw]] as an app and website for illustrated summaries of bestselling nonfiction books.

## Current Profile
In the source, Hardbound is a loved but financially strained consumer product. It had a featured app, outside investors, a committed team, 1,200 paying customers, nearly 100,000 downloads, about 250,000 web readers, and over 20 million tapped pages, but it was earning about $2,500 per month while spending about $13,000 per month. Investor skepticism focused on growth speed and the cost of producing high-quality story content.

## Key Characteristics
- Mobile-first illustrated nonfiction-summary product with both app and web readership.
- Showed meaningful engagement and customer affection before reaching sustainable economics.
- Depended on labor-intensive research, writing, editing, and illustration for each story.
- Faced investor concern that content production would be too costly to satisfy demand.
- Entered a pause in new story production while Bashaw considered model changes.
