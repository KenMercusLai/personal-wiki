## Overview
[[IvyExec]] is represented in this wiki as the publisher of a 2015 practitioner article distinguishing CTO and VP Engineering responsibilities in scaling startups.

## Current Profile
The available source places Ivy Exec in the executive-career and leadership-advice domain. Its article frames senior technical titles through operational responsibility: the CTO protects technical direction and edge, while the VP Engineering builds the people, program, roadmap, and budget systems that turn product vision into delivery.

## Key Characteristics
