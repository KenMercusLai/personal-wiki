## Overview
[[EBay]] is an online marketplace used in the source as an example of trust systems enabling stranger-to-stranger commerce.

## Current Profile
The source says eBay's growth depended on creating enough scale that buyers could find almost anything, but that scale required trust. Buyer ratings, escrow-like protection, and easy PayPal payments reduced transaction anxiety and made small sellers more willing to trade online.

## Key Characteristics
- Marketplace combining casual individual sellers and online stores.
- Depends on broad supply attracting buyer demand.
- Uses ratings and protection mechanisms to reduce trust barriers.
- Connected to [[PayPal]] as a payment enabler.

## Evidence
- Trust barrier: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says eBay addressed trust through buyer ratings and escrow protection.
- Payment convenience: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says easy payment by PayPal was key.
