## Overview
[[Heap]] is a product-analytics competitor presented in the source as differentiating through automatic website-event capture.

## Current Profile
The Product Habits essay says Heap entered after Mixpanel and KISSmetrics with a simple installation proposition: add one tracking script and capture website interactions automatically. [[HitenShah]] presents that capability as a strategically important signal KISSmetrics could have answered before Heap established momentum.

## Key Characteristics
- Competes in product and behavioral analytics.
- Is represented through a single-script automatic event-capture value proposition.
- Entered after earlier analytics products in the source's market history.
- Serves as a retrospective example of a competitor signal that an incumbent ignored.

## Evidence
- Product proposition: [[facebooks-shameless-copying-of-snapchat-and-what-it-means-for-your-product-strategy-product-habits]] says Heap automatically tracked website interactions after one script installation.
- Competitive timing: [[facebooks-shameless-copying-of-snapchat-and-what-it-means-for-your-product-strategy-product-habits]] describes Heap as entering later than Mixpanel.
- Reported traction: [[facebooks-shameless-copying-of-snapchat-and-what-it-means-for-your-product-strategy-product-habits]] reports more than 5,000 customers and $40 million raised at the article's 2017 publication date.

## Qualifications
This profile relies on one competitor founder's 2017 retrospective. It does not independently verify Heap's customer count, financing, capture coverage, data quality, privacy model, current product, or whether KISSmetrics could have copied the feature easily or stopped its growth.

## What Changed
- Created Heap as the automatic event-capture competitor in the KISSmetrics retrospective.
