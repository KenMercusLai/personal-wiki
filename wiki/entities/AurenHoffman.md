## Overview
[[AurenHoffman]] is a data-company entrepreneur and investor who authored a 2019 operating framework for [[DataAsAService]].

## Current Profile
In the source, Hoffman writes from experience running [[LiveRamp]] and [[SafeGraph]], investing in data companies, and speaking with their executives. He argues that data businesses should be evaluated on acquisition, transformation, delivery, factual quality, joinability, market share, declining unit prices, and long-run incremental economics rather than by importing SaaS metrics unchanged.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames DaaS as a category with operating economics distinct from both SaaS and cloud compute.
- Treats verifiable historical facts and continuous quality improvement as the product core.
- Advocates shared, durable identifiers that let customers join a provider's data with other datasets.
- Prefers scale benefits to flow into lower customer prices rather than monopoly price increases.
- Writes as an operator and advocate, not as an independent comparative researcher.
