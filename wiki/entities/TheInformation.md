## Overview
[[TheInformation]] is a premium technology-news publication founded by [[JessicaLessin]] and represented here through a 2016 profile of its early subscription model.

## Current Profile
The publication positioned itself against high-volume, ad-supported digital media by offering a small number of exclusive, deeply reported business stories behind a hard paywall. Its product extended beyond articles into member forums, informal events, a private Slack channel, and higher-priced investor access. The profile reports cash-flow positivity, rapid subscription growth, and geographic expansion, but does not disclose the operating metrics needed to test durability or later scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Charged $399 annually or $39 monthly for approximately two stories a day and member community benefits.
- Focused on exclusive, deep, or data-driven technology-business reporting rather than commoditized news volume.
- Added a $10,000 investor tier with private calls and in-person briefings.
- Used direct reader revenue, founder capital, conservative funding, and disciplined spending rather than venture funding or media partners.
- Sought growth by becoming indispensable to a core professional audience before entering adjacent sectors and geographies.
- Combined industry community with reporting, creating a tension between member value and editorial independence.

## Evidence
- Editorial product: [[inside-the-information-columbia-journalism-review]] describes the hard paywall, low story volume, exclusive coverage plan, reporting hires, and notable investigation of Nest founder Tony Fadell.
- Membership and pricing: [[inside-the-information-columbia-journalism-review]] documents standard and investor tiers, private community surfaces, informal events, briefings, and calls.
- Economics and growth: [[inside-the-information-columbia-journalism-review]] reports cash-flow positivity, more-than-doubled subscriptions, self-funding, an Asia bureau, and planned audience expansion.
- Independence tension: [[inside-the-information-columbia-journalism-review]] pairs tough reporting with criticism that elite social and subscriber relationships could compromise or appear to compromise coverage.

## Qualifications
The evidence is a contemporaneous 2016 profile, not a current company assessment. Subscriber totals, churn, acquisition costs, margins, and founder investment were not disclosed; the roughly 10,000-member estimate came from observers. Reported cash-flow positivity does not by itself establish scalable or durable profitability.
