## Overview
[[ZeroCater]] is an office-meal service founded by [[ArramSabeti]], represented through its 2013 origin story as a manually operated service that validated demand before adding software and raising institutional capital.

## Current Profile
The source frames ZeroCater around a recurring customer job: companies specify headcount, dietary constraints, budget, and schedule, while ZeroCater assembles customized orders from restaurants, food trucks, and caterers. Sabeti began with an inbox and spreadsheet, acquired the first customers through direct outreach and referrals, and looked for a technical cofounder when the spreadsheet reached 500 columns and billing consumed about 20 hours per week. After Y Combinator, a cofounder departure, and Demo Day, the company reportedly raised $1.5 million and by 2013 served more than 350 companies monthly and had delivered over one million meals.

## Key Characteristics
- Removes recurring meal planning and vendor coordination from company office managers.
- Began as a high-touch managed service with no custom software.
- Used founder-led selling to acquire and learn from early customers.
- Reached an operational bottleneck before investing in software automation.
- Passed through Y Combinator and raised capital despite a late cofounder departure.
- Reported substantial customer and meal volume within the source's 2013 time frame.

## Evidence
- Customer job: [[from-selling-scoops-of-ice-cream-to-founding-zerocater-techcrunch]] describes scheduled customized orders across restaurants, food trucks, and caterers based on team size and dietary needs.
- Early validation: [[from-selling-scoops-of-ice-cream-to-founding-zerocater-techcrunch]] says an office manager eagerly delegated lunch ordering and introductions grew one customer into several.
- Manual model: [[from-selling-scoops-of-ice-cream-to-founding-zerocater-techcrunch]] says the first operation used an inbox and large spreadsheet rather than custom code.
- Automation boundary: [[from-selling-scoops-of-ice-cream-to-founding-zerocater-techcrunch]] reports a 500-column scheduling sheet and roughly 20 weekly billing hours before Sabeti sought a technical cofounder.
- Financing: [[from-selling-scoops-of-ice-cream-to-founding-zerocater-techcrunch]] reports a first-place Alumni Demo Day vote and a $1.5 million raise after the technical cofounder quit.
- Scale: [[from-selling-scoops-of-ice-cream-to-founding-zerocater-techcrunch]] reports more than 350 company customers per month and over one million meals served by 2013.

## Qualifications
Every operational, financing, ranking, and scale claim comes from the founder's 2013 account and is not independently verified within the source. The page describes the historical service and should not be read as current product, customer, funding, leadership, or performance information. Manual demand validation does not by itself establish scalable unit economics, food quality, supplier outcomes, logistics reliability, or durable retention.
