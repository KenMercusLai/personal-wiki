## Overview
[[Twilio]] is the communications API company whose 2008 seed pitch [[FredWilson]] remembers as the best he had received.

## Current Profile
The supplied source frames Twilio as an abstraction over telephony complexity for software developers. [[JeffLawson]] represented that abstraction as five API calls and demonstrated it by live-coding an application that called Wilson's phone within about 30 seconds. Wilson wrote the retrospective as Twilio priced its 2016 IPO, supplying later company context without explaining the path between seed pitch and public offering.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents telephony capabilities through a compact developer-facing API.
- Made its core promise directly demonstrable through code.
- Was a [[UnionSquareVentures]] portfolio company in Wilson's account.
