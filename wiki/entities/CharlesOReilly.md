## Overview
[[CharlesOReilly]] is identified in the source as a Stanford Graduate School of Business professor who led a culture workshop for [[VMware]] leaders.

## Current Profile
At a 2008 VMware offsite, O'Reilly asked leaders what a new hire should do to get ahead. He then reframed their answers as the company's actual culture, exposing a gap between VMware's stated customer orientation and the behaviors its leaders associated with success.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses promotion and success behavior as a diagnostic for actual culture.
- Elicits tacit norms indirectly instead of asking participants to recite company values.
- Connects organizational culture to observable reward and punishment patterns.
