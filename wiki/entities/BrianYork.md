## Overview
[[BrianYork]] is a co-founder of [[Bliss]] and the author of a 2016 practitioner account that interprets the startup's repository size and technical-debt history alongside customer validation, fundraising, and hiring.

## Current Profile
In the ingested source, York presents technical debt as a deliberate business and engineering tradeoff. He and his co-founder initially favored feature speed while testing whether Bliss could attract paying customers, then shifted toward a more sustainable codebase after raising capital and hiring engineers. His central contribution is the claim that longitudinal engineering measures can help reconstruct a company's operating history.

## Key Characteristics
- Startup founder writing from direct operating experience at Bliss.
- Connects software-maintenance decisions to customer validation, fundraising, and hiring.
- Treats technical debt as manageable but not eliminable.
- Advocates tracking debt over time rather than judging one isolated measurement.
