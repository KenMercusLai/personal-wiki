## Overview
[[KevinRose]] is represented here as the founder of [[Oak]] and the remote client in [[AJSmart]]'s app-redesign case study.

## Current Profile
Rose participated as both domain expert and design decision-maker. He described the product and project constraints, supplied cross-industry inspiration, voted on hand-drawn concepts, recorded narrated feedback, and selected among prototype directions before the team produced the complete screen set.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded the Oak meditation and breathing app described in the source.
- Collaborated remotely with AJ&Smart from San Francisco while the design team worked in Berlin.
- Made subjective preferences concrete through inspiration examples, voting, and narrated prototype feedback.
- Approved the watercolor-led direction and aligned with the team before full screen production.
