## Overview
[[MartinaLauchengco]] is the product manager in the source's [[MicrosoftWordForMac]] recovery case.

## Current Profile
The source presents Lauchengco as a young Microsoft product manager who helped turn around Word 6.0 for Mac after a common-code-base strategy produced a slow, poorly received Mac release. Her case illustrates product management under pressure: the team had to protect the customer experience and platform fit despite internal pressure for code convergence and release efficiency.

## Key Characteristics
- Worked as product manager on Word for Mac during the 6.0 to 6.1 recovery.
- Represented the need for a Mac-specific customer experience rather than generic feature parity.
- Helped focus the team on performance, font loading, keyboard shortcuts, and press-visible workflows such as Word Count.
- Signed the apology letter and update package sent to registered users.
- Later worked at Netscape, LoudCloud, SVPG, and UC Berkeley in product, marketing, and teaching roles according to the source.

## Evidence
- Recovery role: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] introduces Lauchengco as the PM whose job was to help turn around Word for Mac.
- Platform fit: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says the team learned users valued what was different about the Mac, not only sameness across platforms.
- Performance focus: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] describes work on startup speed, fonts, keyboard shortcuts, and fast Word Count.
- Customer apology: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Word 6.1 was sent to registered users with an apology letter signed by Lauchengco.
