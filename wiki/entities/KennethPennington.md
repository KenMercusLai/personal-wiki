## Overview
[[KennethPennington]] was the digital director for [[BernieSanders]]' 2016 campaign in the POLITICO source.

## Current Profile
Pennington appears as the official campaign bridge to the volunteer technology community. His comments frame the campaign's digital ethos as unusually dependent on volunteers, and he contrasts Sanders' distributed approach with earlier efforts to create physical technology offices near Silicon Valley.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the official campaign's digital leadership.
- Maintains contact with volunteer technologists.
- Describes the campaign as volunteer-driven rather than centrally staff-driven.
- Treats geographic dispersion as compatible with campaign technical work.
