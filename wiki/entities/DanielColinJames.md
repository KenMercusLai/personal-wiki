## Overview
[[DanielColinJames]] is the author of the 2018 article "Your next browser will pay you," a promotional explanation of [[Brave]], [[BasicAttentionToken]], and browser-mediated advertising.

## Current Profile
In the source, James acts as an enthusiastic technology explainer rather than an independent analyst. He assembles user, publisher, and advertiser complaints about conventional ad technology into a case for Brave's opt-in, locally targeted, token-funded alternative. The article says Fospha commissioned the piece and discloses that James owned a small amount of BAT, both of which materially qualify its confident predictions.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames web-advertising reform as a redistribution of power from large platforms to users and publishers.
- Explains the Brave/BAT model through stakeholder incentives, revenue splits, and a hypothetical high-value lead.
- Uses historical browser competition and Brendan Eich's record to support a forecast of Brave adoption.
- Discloses both commissioning by Fospha and personal BAT ownership.

## Evidence
- Authorship and thesis: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] identifies James as author and argues that Brave will change internet economics.
- Explanatory approach: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] moves from ad-tech problems to Brave's blocking, private ads, rewards, and wider BAT ambitions.

## Qualifications
The source establishes James's role in this article but is not a general biography. Its commissioned status, referral link, and token ownership make the article useful as a record of the proposal and its marketing narrative, not as independent proof of product performance or adoption.
