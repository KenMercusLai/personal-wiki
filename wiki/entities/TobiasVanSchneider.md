## Overview
[[TobiasVanSchneider]] appears in the wiki as the designer-author of a Medium essay on [[SunkCostFallacy]], using his own writing process and the abandoned [[DotMailApp]] project as practical examples.

## Current Profile
The source presents van Schneider as a reflective designer and maker who turns a personal creative-work problem into a broader decision-making lesson. He notices that he is tempted to keep refining a weak article because of time and emotional investment, then generalizes the pattern into a warning about sunk costs in projects, jobs, food, transit decisions, and game design. His biography in the source also identifies him as a co-founder of [[Semplice]] and formerly an art director and design lead at [[Spotify]].

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from personal creative and design experience.
- Uses his own almost-failed writing decision to explain a behavioral bias.
- Treats [[DotMailApp]] as a public example of pride and emotional investment extending a project.
- Connects individual decision-making with product-design ethics.
- Is source-described as a [[Semplice]] co-founder and former [[Spotify]] art director/design lead.
