Pat Grady is the Sequoia partner interviewed in Drift's article "8 Lessons From One of Silicon Valley's Top VCs." The source says he joined Sequoia in 2007, had met with thousands of entrepreneurs, was named to the Forbes Midas Brink List earlier in 2017, and had invested in companies including Zoom, Qualtrics, Namely, and Medallia.

In the source, Grady's founder advice centers on first principles: companies exist to solve real problems, not to make people rich; large markets should be entered through narrow slices; and durable companies need a unique, compelling solution that competitors cannot easily copy. His scaling advice treats roughly 150 employees as a point where informal context weakens and systems, communication cadence, and culture become necessary.
