## Overview
[[RichHanna]] was the general manager who guided CNET through Amazon's Fall River warehouse for large and irregular merchandise.

## Current Profile
The source identifies Hanna as a 20-year U.S. Navy veteran who had joined Amazon three years earlier. He explains the non-sort facility through concrete examples of unusual inventory, confirms that some long items still qualified for two-day shipping, and reports the warehouse's 80,000-to-100,000-box daily throughput and larger holiday peak.

## Key Characteristics
