## Overview
[[JeffLawson]] is the founder and CEO of [[Twilio]] who appears in the wiki through [[FredWilson]]'s account of a 2008 seed pitch.

## Current Profile
In the supplied account, Lawson communicates Twilio's developer-platform thesis through compression and proof. He describes the complexity of telephony as five API calls, names those calls when challenged, and live-codes an application that makes Wilson's phone ring within about 30 seconds. The source presents him as both a technical builder and an unusually effective early-stage product communicator.
