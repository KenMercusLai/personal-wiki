## Overview
[[AirPods]] appear in the sources as Apple's ear-worn wearable category, evidence that Apple's post-iPhone strategy was moving onto the body, and a pricing case where Apple undercut most early truly wireless headphone competitors.

## Current Profile
The smart-glasses source does not analyze AirPods in technical detail. Instead, it groups AirPods with [[AppleWatch]] as part of Apple's growing wearables momentum: the wrist and ears are early body locations where Apple had already won meaningful user adoption before the proposed battle for the eyes. The pricing source gives AirPods a sharper market role. [[NeilCybart]] argues that at $159, AirPods were not simply expensive earbuds or free EarPods replacements, but "computers for your ears" whose sensors, W1 chip, and charging case made them Apple's second wearables product. The embedded launch image showing $159 reinforces the pricing point, and the comparison list places most named truly wireless competitors between $199 and $300. In that framing, AirPods are strategically important because they extend the [[IPhone]] ecosystem into smaller, more personal devices while also using aggressive pricing to pull users into Apple's wearable base.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents Apple's ear-worn wearable position in the source.
- Is paired with [[AppleWatch]] as evidence that Apple was gaining body-worn real estate.
- Helps make wearables a unit-volume growth story alongside plateauing iPhone sales.
- Supports the source's broader claim that post-iPhone strategy can be accessory-led before becoming paradigm-shifting.
- Serves as a case in [[ApplePricingStrategy]] because the $159 launch price undercut most early truly wireless competitors.

## Evidence
- Wearables momentum: [[above-avalon-apple-is-figuring-out-whats-next]] says Apple Watch and AirPods together had significant sales momentum on wrists and ears.
- Unit comparison: [[above-avalon-apple-is-figuring-out-whats-next]] argues combined Watch and AirPods unit sales would soon outsell iPad.
- Body real estate: [[above-avalon-apple-is-figuring-out-whats-next]] treats ears and wrists as steps before the next wearables battle for the eyes.
- Launch price: [[above-avalon-the-curious-state-of-apple-product-pricing]] and its embedded image place AirPods at $159.
- Competitor comparison: [[above-avalon-the-curious-state-of-apple-product-pricing]] lists Kanoa, Bragi Dash, Erato Apollo 7, Skybuds, Earin, Motorola VerveOnes+, Samsung Gear IconX, and Bragi Headphone as mostly higher-priced alternatives.
- Experience advantage: [[above-avalon-the-curious-state-of-apple-product-pricing]] argues the W1 chip and charging-case experience made price cuts by competitors insufficient to match AirPods.

## Qualifications
The smart-glasses source does not separate AirPods unit sales from Apple Watch sales in the embedded chart. The pricing source is a 2017 launch-era analyst comparison and does not include later AirPods models, margins, sales outcomes, audio-quality comparisons, or competitor trajectories.

## What Changed

- Added AirPods as the clearest underpricing case in Apple's accessible-luxury wearable strategy.
