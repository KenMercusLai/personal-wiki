## Overview
[[AirPods]] appear in the source as Apple's ear-worn wearable category and as evidence that Apple's post-iPhone strategy was already moving onto the body.

## Current Profile
The article does not analyze AirPods in technical detail. Instead, it groups AirPods with [[AppleWatch]] as part of Apple's growing wearables momentum: the wrist and ears are early body locations where Apple had already won meaningful user adoption before the proposed battle for the eyes. In that framing, AirPods are strategically important because they show that Apple can extend the [[IPhone]] ecosystem into smaller, more personal devices without those devices needing to replace the iPhone.
