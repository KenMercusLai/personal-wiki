## Overview
[[Tasty]] is BuzzFeed's food media brand used in the source as the leading example of a modern, service-oriented media brand with multiple revenue streams.

## Current Profile
The memo presents Tasty as more than a social-video property. It is a daily consumer-service brand built around food, shared interests, and practical usefulness, then monetized through platform distribution, advertising, commerce, apps, partnerships, books, products, and video development. Tasty becomes the model BuzzFeed wants to extend into Nifty, Goodful, and a new beauty and style brand.

## Key Characteristics
- Food-focused media brand that serves a broad audience through practical, shareable content.
- Represents BuzzFeed's desired move from single-channel media toward consumer-service brands.
- Connects social distribution on platforms such as Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram with direct products and partnerships.
- Provides the template for expansion into adjacent lifestyle verticals.

## Evidence
- Brand reach: [[9-boxes]] says Tasty is a highly liked food media brand on Facebook and reaches more people globally than any other food network.
- Consumer-service role: [[9-boxes]] frames Tasty as content that exists outside polarized political discourse, brings people together around food, and improves daily life.
- Revenue breadth: [[9-boxes]] shows Tasty revenue examples including a Walmart and Jet.com partnership, Penguin Random House cookbook deal, Tasty One Top, app adoption, Facebook publishing, YouTube shows, and coming shows such as Mom vs. Chef.
- Portfolio template: [[9-boxes]] says BuzzFeed will expand the model into Nifty, Goodful, and beauty/style.

## Qualifications
The source is an internal strategic memo and does not independently verify Tasty's audience, profitability, or later performance. It also emphasizes Tasty's strategic promise rather than possible risks around platform dependency, production cost, or commerce execution.

## What Changed
- Created Tasty as the source's flagship media-brand and revenue-diversification example.
