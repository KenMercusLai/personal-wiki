## Overview
[[Yahoo]] appears twice in the corpus: as [[MarissaMayer]]'s company context for using acquisitions to close a mobile capability gap, and as the incumbent that later entrants started against in both search and question-and-answer.

## Current Profile
The scaling source uses Yahoo to show how a large company can still face scale mismatch inside a strategic function. Mayer says Yahoo had only about 30 mobile-team members out of 14,000 employees and needed a much larger mobile organization, so acquisitions helped bring in teams whose founders could lead new divisions. The Quora answer reviewed here uses the company from the other direction, as the established competitor in two categories that later entrants entered anyway: it cites Yahoo's search engine as already famous when [[Google]] started and Yahoo's question-and-answer product as already used by many people when [[Quora]] started. That source names Yahoo only as the incumbent those entrants competed with and gives no account of Yahoo's own decisions, execution, or eventual position in either category.

## Key Characteristics
- Illustrates a strategic function being underbuilt relative to company size.
- Uses talent, building-block, and strategic acquisitions to address capability gaps.
- Shows that scale problems can occur inside a large company, not only between startup stages.
- Serves as the incumbent in the corpus's competition material, having been the established player in search and in question-and-answer before later entrants started.

## Evidence
- Mobile gap: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Mayer that Yahoo's mobile team was about 30 people out of 14,000.
- Acquisition strategy: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] says Mayer classified acquisitions into talent, building-block, and strategic categories and used acquired teams to rebuild mobile.
- Search incumbent: [[yash-gandhi-startup-idea-well-funded-competitor-quora]] cites Yahoo's search engine as already famous before Google stepped in.
- Question-and-answer incumbent: [[yash-gandhi-startup-idea-well-funded-competitor-quora]] cites a Yahoo question-and-answer product as already used by many people before Quora started.

## Qualifications
The scaling source does not evaluate whether Yahoo's acquisition strategy succeeded; it only presents Mayer's scaling rationale. The competition example is a short Quora answer that mentions Yahoo as a foil for the entrants and supplies no dates, market data, or view of Yahoo's side of either category.
