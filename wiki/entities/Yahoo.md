## Overview
[[Yahoo]] is presented as [[MarissaMayer]]'s company context for using acquisitions to close a major mobile capability gap.

## Current Profile
The source uses Yahoo to show how a large company can still face scale mismatch inside a strategic function. Mayer says Yahoo had only about 30 mobile-team members out of 14,000 employees and needed a much larger mobile organization, so acquisitions helped bring in teams whose founders could lead new divisions.

## Key Characteristics
- Illustrates a strategic function being underbuilt relative to company size.
- Uses talent, building-block, and strategic acquisitions to address capability gaps.
- Shows that scale problems can occur inside a large company, not only between startup stages.

## Evidence
- Mobile gap: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] cites Mayer that Yahoo's mobile team was about 30 people out of 14,000.
- Acquisition strategy: [[16-lessons-on-scaling-from-eric-schmidt-reid-hoffman-marissa-mayer-brian-chesky-diane-greene-jeff-weiner-and-more]] says Mayer classified acquisitions into talent, building-block, and strategic categories and used acquired teams to rebuild mobile.

## Qualifications
The source does not evaluate whether Yahoo's acquisition strategy succeeded; it only presents Mayer's scaling rationale.
