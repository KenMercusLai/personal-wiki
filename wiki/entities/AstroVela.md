## Overview
[[AstroVela]], named 星帆逐影 in Chinese, is an announced infrastructure startup betting on multimodal AI, Physical AI, and the systems behind them.

## Current Profile
The founding announcement presents AstroVela as a new company designed to make a concentrated AI-era infrastructure bet without an incumbent product portfolio or organizational history. Its named product family is [[Vane]], divided into Core, Data, RL, and Agent components. This is an intent and positioning statement: the source does not describe the company's legal structure, funding, customers, architecture, team roles, or operating results.

## Key Characteristics
- Positions itself as an AI-era infrastructure company.
- Selects multimodal AI and Physical AI as its primary opportunity areas.
- Organizes its announced product direction under the four-part [[Vane]] family.
- Frames practice-derived knowledge, problem selection, and customer understanding as intended sources of advantage.
