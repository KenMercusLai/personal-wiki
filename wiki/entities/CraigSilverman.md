## Overview
[[CraigSilverman]] is the BuzzFeed News journalist who reported the 2018 investigation into alleged app-install attribution fraud by Cheetah Mobile and Kika Tech apps.

## Current Profile
In this source, Silverman combines Kochava's attribution analysis, Method Media Intelligence's code review, affected-company responses, Google statements, analyst criticism, market estimates, and post-publication app-removal updates. The resulting account distinguishes observed or alleged behavior from the denials and explanations supplied by Cheetah Mobile and Kika Tech.

## Key Characteristics
- Reported on digital advertising fraud and mobile-app abuse for BuzzFeed News.
- Brought together attribution telemetry, captured app behavior, code analysis, company responses, and platform reaction.
- Updated the story after two implicated apps were removed from Google Play.
