## Overview
[[RANDCorporation]] is a research organization cited in the source for a vacation-use incentive.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki's current evidence, RAND appears only as an alternative to ambiguous unlimited leave: the essay says it paid an annual bonus to employees who used all of their vacation. The example treats full use of leave as a behavior worth rewarding rather than as a sign of weak commitment.

## Key Characteristics
- Research organization.
- Cited for rewarding employees who use their full vacation allowance.
- Used as an example of making the desired leave behavior explicit.

## Evidence
- Vacation-use incentive: [[unlimited-vacation-and-other-forms-of-guilt-based-management]] reports that RAND paid an annual bonus to employees who took all of their vacation.

## Qualifications
The source gives no program design, eligibility, payout, usage, retention, or wellbeing data, and this wiki has not independently verified the reported policy. The example supports a policy-design contrast rather than a conclusion about RAND's broader workplace culture.
