## Overview
[[Eat24]] is the food-delivery service where Talia Jane worked in customer support before publishing her open letter to Yelp's CEO.

## Current Profile
The source portrays Eat24 as a 24/7 food-delivery operation whose support workers handle customer and restaurant problems while struggling with low wages and uneven workplace resources. Jane treats the service's premise as bitterly ironic: an employee of a food-delivery app could not afford to buy food. Eat24 also appears as a transition path Jane hoped would eventually lead to media work within Yelp.

## Key Characteristics
- Food-delivery service acquired by Yelp for a reported $300 million in the source.
- Workplace where Jane performed customer-support labor.
- 24/7 operation dependent on support workers' availability.
- Product whose food-centered identity highlights the contradiction of employee food insecurity.
- Internal career doorway that Jane expected might lead to media work after a year in support.
