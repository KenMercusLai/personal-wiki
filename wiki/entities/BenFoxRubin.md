## Overview
[[BenFoxRubin]] is the CNET reporter who documented Amazon's Fall River non-sort fulfillment center and the broader challenge of selling bulky products online.

## Current Profile
In this source, Rubin combines an on-site warehouse tour with comments from Amazon, Shippo, Wayfair, and Stand Steady. His account connects facility design and custom packaging to wider competitive motives, merchant dependence, and the service-intensive last mile.
