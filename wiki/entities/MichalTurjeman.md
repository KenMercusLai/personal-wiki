## Overview
[[MichalTurjeman]] is a product designer represented here through a 2016 first-person account of designing sports-media products at [[MinuteMedia]] despite not personally identifying as a sports fan.

## Current Profile
Turjeman presents outsider status as a manageable design constraint rather than a disqualification. While working from [[NinetyMin]] toward [[TwelveUp]], she relied on direct questions, explicit user personas, willingness to discard favored ideas, and comparison of multiple solutions to keep her own preferences from standing in for the audience's.

## Key Characteristics
- Designs products for audiences whose interests may differ substantially from her own.
- Treats basic questions as necessary problem discovery rather than evidence of incompetence.
- Uses personas to separate designer preference from intended-user perspective.
- Balances intuitive first directions with an explicit alternative and pro/con comparison.

## Evidence
- Role and context: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] identifies Turjeman as a designer at MinuteMedia working on 90min and 12up.
- Outsider perspective: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] describes her as an ex-ballerina with limited sports knowledge who nevertheless found the products' core purpose compelling.
- Working method: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] recommends asking basic domain questions, returning to personas, and comparing two solutions through honest pro/con lists.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from one short self-authored practitioner essay. It does not include colleague or user perspectives, examples detailed enough to evaluate the resulting interface, or outcome evidence showing that the described process improved 12up.
