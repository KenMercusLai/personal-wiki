Source: [3 Years as a One Man Startup]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/3-years-as-a-one-man-startup.md" >}})

Readlang is the language-learning web app at the center of this source. Steve Ridout says he spent most of three years creating it and had previously written about the struggle to get it off the ground.

The article presents Readlang as a product with slow but meaningful traction. New signups rose from 2,800 in the first year to 36,800 in the third, and revenue reached about $16,500 in year three with about $14,500 in profit because expenses were low.

Readlang also serves as the source's evidence for user value. Ridout cites recent user feedback from language learners and a polyglot group to argue that the product genuinely helps people learn languages, which helps explain why he continued despite low personal compensation.
