## Overview
[[ADarkRoom]] is the sparse, incremental, text-based game that [[AmirRajan]] ported and reinterpreted for iOS.

## Current Profile
The source presents A Dark Room as both a creative breakthrough and a volatile platform-market success. Rajan discovered the web-based original during his sabbatical, received permission to port it, redesigned much of the experience, and released it to modest initial downloads. The game later went viral, reached #1 on the [[AppStore]], generated major revenue, and then declined, making it a case study in how indie games can combine deep creator meaning with unstable discovery and income.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as a web-based, incremental, text-based game that Rajan adapted for iOS.
- Connected strongly with Rajan because of its sparse presentation and expressive potential.
- Launched quietly, with about 30 first-day downloads and later roughly 10 daily downloads before the viral spike.
- Reached #1 on the [[AppStore]] and reportedly generated over $800,000 gross.
- Produced about 250,000 downloads during the #1 period before falling from the top spot.
- Later declined to roughly 100 daily downloads, turning the windfall into passive income rather than a stable full-time studio engine.

## Evidence
- Original format: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] describes the original as web-based, incremental, and text-based.
- Porting process: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] says Rajan got permission from Michael, redesigned much of the original, and released it after about four months.
- Early launch: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] reports about 30 downloads on the first day and low daily downloads before virality.
- Viral success: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] reports sudden daily revenue jumps, a #1 App Store ranking, a $20,000 sales report, and more than $800,000 gross.
- Decline: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] says the game eventually fell from #1 and later declined to about 100 downloads per day.
- Platform contrast: [[a-dark-room-from-sabbatical-year-to-800000]] says the later [[Android]] version reached #2 overall on Google Play, but iOS revenue was almost an order of magnitude better.

## Qualifications
The source is Rajan's retrospective and does not provide independent app-store analytics. It also foregrounds the mobile port, not the full authorship history of the original web game.
