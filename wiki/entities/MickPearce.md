## Overview
[[MickPearce]] is the architect cited for designing [[EastgateCentre]] in Harare with inspiration from termite-nest temperature regulation.

## Current Profile
The source presents Pearce as the concrete design example for [[InnovationAtIntersection]]. His work is used to show how a technique observed in one natural system can be translated into a building-design problem, producing a practical temperature-management result.

## Key Characteristics
- Architect associated with Eastgate Centre in the source.
- Represents cross-field transfer from biological observation to building design.
- Used as a practical example of old ideas recombined into new design.

## Evidence
- Building example: [[all-new-ideas-are-combinations-of-old-ideas]] says Pearce designed [[EastgateCentre]] using techniques observed in termite nests.
- Performance frame: [[all-new-ideas-are-combinations-of-old-ideas]] reports that Eastgate maintains an ambient temperature range despite wider outdoor variation.
