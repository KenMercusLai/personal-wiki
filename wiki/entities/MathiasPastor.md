## Overview
[[MathiasPastor]] is the author of "Avoiding Zombie Startups" and is presented in the source as a member of [[TheFamily]] advising prospective startup employees.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Pastor contributes a candidate-side framework for distinguishing high-momentum startup opportunities from companies sustained by weak signals. His advice combines investor and company diligence with organizational questions about employee equity, hiring intent, and internal transparency.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames startup employment as a sequence of concentrated career bets.
- Prefers operating and customer evidence over fundraising, awards, or publicity alone.
- Treats team incentives and information access as candidate diligence questions.

## Evidence
- Career-bet framing: [[avoiding-zombie-startups]] says employees may need to join several startups before finding one whose equity is worth the ride.
- Signal discipline: [[avoiding-zombie-startups]] tells candidates to investigate backers and prioritize building and selling over prestige signals.
- Organizational diligence: [[avoiding-zombie-startups]] recommends examining team equity, the reason for hiring, and founder transparency.

## Qualifications
The source provides a practitioner argument and contact context rather than a full biography of Pastor or comparative evidence for his screening rules. Its examples reflect venture-style startup expectations and a European ecosystem context.
