## Overview
[[Remind]] is represented as a mobile messaging startup connecting teachers, students, and parents, and as a 2015 case of growth built around retention, usability, school networks, shared measurement, and teacher activation.

## Current Profile
The source depicts Remind's growth practice as a company-wide system rather than a standalone marketing function. Product, engineering, marketing, support, leadership, and the growth team used accessible metrics and user observation to prioritize both incremental tests and larger bets. The company reportedly invested in data infrastructure early, used Chart.io and formerly Optimizely before building an internal testing tool, and planned around a back-to-school peak above 400,000 daily users.

Its product examples center teacher activation. App-store password failures diverted teachers to a weak mobile site, so Remind built a fast responsive web experience that could either retain them there or lead them back to the app. Teachers reached an “aha moment” when students appeared in their class list; one teacher in a school could prompt colleagues to join, while students and parents helped form a product-specific social graph grounded in offline school relationships.

## Key Characteristics
- Education messaging product linking teachers, students, and parents.
- Cross-functional growth ownership tied to one product roadmap.
- Early emphasis on measurement, funnel visibility, and experimentation infrastructure.
- Activation work based on observed teacher constraints inside and outside the product.
- Word-of-mouth growth through existing school relationships and easy invitations.
- Product-specific value milestone when teachers see students join their class.
