## Overview
[[ScottAdams]] is cited by [[DariusForoux]] as the Dilbert creator and career-advice writer behind the [[SkillStacking]] idea used in "Being Different Beats Being Better."

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Adams appears as a career-strategy reference rather than as a full biographical subject. Foroux presents him as an example of someone whose distinctiveness came from combining cartooning, business observation, and life-advice writing, and as the source of the claim that every additional useful skill can improve one's odds of success.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies the article's main practical mechanism for career differentiation.
- Represents a hybrid professional profile that is not reducible to one skill category.
- Emphasizes ordinary competence across multiple skills rather than world-class mastery of one skill.

## Evidence
- Skill-stacking source: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] attributes the skill-combination idea to Adams' career advice.
- Hybrid example: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] describes Adams as a cartoonist who writes about life advice and business.
- Competence threshold: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] quotes Adams to argue that being merely good at more than one skill can raise market value.

## Qualifications
The wiki has only Foroux's short treatment of Adams, so the profile should not be read as a full account of Adams' work, controversies, or broader career.
