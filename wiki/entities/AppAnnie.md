## Overview
[[AppAnnie]] appears in the wiki as the mobile analytics firm whose 2015 retrospective supplies comparative estimates for [[GooglePlay]] and Apple's [[AppStore]].

## Current Profile
In the VentureBeat report, App Annie is the evidence provider for a platform-economics snapshot: Google Play had far more downloads in 2015, while Apple's App Store generated much more revenue. Its role is not as a strategic actor in the article but as the measurement source behind claims about download volume, revenue concentration, market geography, and game revenue share.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides app-store market estimates.
- Supplies comparative data for Google Play and Apple's App Store.
- Frames mobile platform performance through downloads, revenue, geography, and category mix.

## Evidence
- Platform comparison: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] says the 2015 download and revenue estimates came from App Annie's retrospective report.
- Download metric: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] reports Google Play at 100% more downloads than Apple's App Store.
- Revenue metric: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] reports Apple's App Store at 75% more revenue than Google Play.

## Qualifications
The source is a VentureBeat report on App Annie estimates, not App Annie's complete original report. It gives headline platform metrics and selected regional and category observations, but it does not disclose methodology, absolute download counts, absolute revenue, or later market changes.

## What Changed
- Created App Annie as the analytics source behind the 2015 app-store comparison.
