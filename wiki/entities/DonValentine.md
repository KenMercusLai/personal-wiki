## Overview
[[DonValentine]] is presented in the source as [[SequoiaCapital]]'s founder and the origin of two durable Sequoia themes: connect technical invention to human problems, and pursue large markets through disciplined entry.

## Current Profile
The source does not profile Valentine broadly; it uses him as historical context for Sequoia's operating philosophy. [[PatGrady]] describes Valentine as a former chief marketing officer rather than an investor by trade, and links Sequoia's founding premise to the idea that brilliant engineers often need help connecting their work to human problems.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded Sequoia in 1972, according to the source.
- Brought a marketing/customer-problem lens rather than a conventional investor background.
- Influenced Sequoia's advice to target large markets.
- Serves as a continuity point while Sequoia updates its methods.

- Founding role: [[8-lessons-from-one-of-silicon-valleys-top-vcs-drift]] says Valentine founded Sequoia in 1972.
