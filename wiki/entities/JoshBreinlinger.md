## Overview
[[JoshBreinlinger]] is the author of A Crowded Space's marketplace-rebundling essay and an investor associated in the source with OfferUp and Upwork.

## Current Profile
Breinlinger's contribution to the wiki is a retrospective marketplace thesis. He argues that Craigslist's many apparent vertical unbundlers did not generally become the largest businesses; insufficient transaction frequency, transaction value, and user attention favored broader category platforms. Because he describes OfferUp and Upwork as portfolio companies, his analysis combines marketplace experience with a direct investment interest in two examples.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses transaction frequency, transaction size, and limited user attention to reason about marketplace breadth.
