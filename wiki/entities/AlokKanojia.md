## Overview
[[AlokKanojia]] is presented as a psychiatrist whose Creator Science interview helps the source author reframe creator data anxiety around controllable actions and complex attribution.

## Current Profile
The source uses Kanojia's interview as a psychological counterweight to platform-data fixation. His advice leads the author to distinguish controllable creative actions from uncontrollable outcomes and to recognize that both success and failure are only partly caused by the creator.

## Key Characteristics
- Helps creators focus on actions rather than outcome targets.
- Frames content success and failure as complex rather than fully self-caused.
- Gives the author a way to reduce self-worth attachment to platform results.
- Appears as a health and creator-psychology voice in the Creator Science ecosystem.
