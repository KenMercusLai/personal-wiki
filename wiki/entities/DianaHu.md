## Overview
[[DianaHu]] is presented as a Y Combinator partner, engineer, and entrepreneur whose interview technique turns a guest's historical achievements into concrete present-day questions.

## Current Profile
Mai Yang's account describes Hu as a Carnegie Mellon-trained computer-vision and machine-learning practitioner who worked at Intel Labs and OnCue, co-founded Escher Reality, joined Niantic after its acquisition, and later became a Y Combinator partner. The source values her less as a credentialed moderator than as an active interviewer: she compresses a past episode into a reusable move, then asks what its 2026 equivalent is or what the audience should calculate and do next.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines technical, founder, acquisition, and startup-advising experience in the source's account.
- Frames historical achievements as transferable actions rather than as biography alone.
- Pushes abstract answers toward concrete exercises, present-day equivalents, and audience takeaways.
- Uses follow-up questions to make vague concepts such as taste operational.
