## Overview
[[IrmaKornilova]] is represented in the wiki as the author of "DevOps is a culture, not a role!"

## Current Profile
Kornilova presents DevOps as an organizational transformation that joins stakeholder participation, leadership support, shared goals, delivery automation, and measurable small pilots. Her account emphasizes continual learning rather than treating DevOps adoption as a completed state.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates company-wide [[DevOpsCulture]] rather than role-based ownership.
- Connects collaboration with concrete [[ContinuousDelivery]] and automation practices.
- Frames business buy-in around speed, reliability, and security outcomes.
- Recommends small pilots and comparative delivery and operations metrics.

## Evidence
- Cultural framing: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] says everyone with a stake in the product should join the process early.
- Practice framing: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] names version control, continuous integration, configuration, test, and deployment automation.
- Adoption framing: [[devops-is-a-culture-not-a-role-irma-kornilova-medium]] recommends a compelling business case and small early successes.

The profile is based on one 2017 article and does not attempt a broader biography or establish the author's current role. Several examples and formulations are attributed within the article to [[Puppet]], [[MikeDilworth]], and [[TerriPotts]].
