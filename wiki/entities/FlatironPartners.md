## Overview
[[FlatironPartners]] is the venture-capital firm [[FredWilson]] says he co-founded at the beginning of the Internet boom.

## Current Profile
The supplied source uses Flatiron Partners only as the origin point for Wilson's dot-com-era wealth and concentrated exposure. Wilson says his family had no net worth before the firm, accumulated its wealth during 1996–2000, and then lost nearly all of it when a portfolio dominated by venture capital and Internet stocks collapsed.

## Key Characteristics
