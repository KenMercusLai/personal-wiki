## Overview
[[FlatironPartners]] is the venture-capital firm [[FredWilson]] says he formed with [[JerryColonna]] at the beginning of the Internet boom.

## Current Profile
One source uses Flatiron Partners as the origin point for Wilson's dot-com-era wealth and concentrated exposure. Wilson says his family had no net worth before the firm, accumulated its wealth during 1996–2000, and then lost nearly all of it when a portfolio dominated by venture capital and Internet stocks collapsed. A second identifies Colonna as Wilson's co-founder and uses Colonna's early board experience to illustrate how a former operator learned not to take over portfolio-company execution.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founded by Wilson at the beginning of the Internet boom.
- Associated in the source with wealth created during the 1996–2000 period.
- Provides historical context for Wilson's later emphasis on household diversification.
- Included an operator-turned-investor whose early board experience exposed the boundary between advising management and doing management's work.
