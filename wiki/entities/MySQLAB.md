## Overview
[[MySQLAB]] was the company founded in 1995 by [[MontyWidenius]], David Axmark, and Allan Larsson to develop and commercialize [[MySQL]].

## Current Profile
The article presents MySQL AB as a company organized around Widenius's database code while Axmark and Larsson handled the non-coding work. Its operating model joined open-source community development, paid commercial use, customer feedback, and work-sample-based hiring. The source says the company reached 550 people while Widenius remained a hands-on CTO, then was sold to Sun in 2008 for US$1 billion.

## Key Characteristics
- Built around database code and customer learning developed over many years.
- Split early founder responsibilities between Widenius's coding and his cofounders' other company work.
- Combined open-source distribution and contribution with commercial licensing revenue.
- Used public contributor behavior and code quality as recruiting evidence.
- Relied on self-directed employees to reduce direct supervision and meeting load.

## Evidence
- Formation and roles: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] says Widenius, Axmark, and Larsson founded the company in 1995, with Widenius coding and the others doing “everything else.”
- Product development: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] links the database's usability and reliability to accumulated real-customer feedback.
- Business model: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] describes paid licensing for enterprises using MySQL commercially.

- Outcome: [[founder-stories-a-hackers-hacker-openocean]] reports a 2008 sale to Sun for US$1 billion.
