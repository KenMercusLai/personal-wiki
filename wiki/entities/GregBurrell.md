## Overview
[[GregBurrell]] is represented in the wiki as a Netflix reliability engineer and coauthor of the Edge Engineering account that introduced [[FullCycleDevelopment]].

## Current Profile
Burrell's source connects team design with production responsibility. Netflix Edge Engineering's path from separate operations ownership through a partial hybrid to full-cycle teams is presented as an effort to reduce handoff loss and put operational feedback in the hands of developers who can change the service.

The account does not reject specialization. It gives centralized reliability, platform, and tools teams a leverage role, retains space for deep specialists, and makes broader ownership conditional on training, staffing, work prioritization, usable tooling, and sustainable on-call practice.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthored Netflix's 2018 account of the full-cycle developer model.
- Frames lifecycle ownership as a team feedback-system design rather than an expansion of unsupported individual duties.
- Treats central specialists and reusable tools as necessary enablers of domain-team autonomy.
- Explicitly acknowledges cognitive-load, preference, specialization, overload, and burnout trade-offs.

## Evidence
- Operating-model account: [[full-cycle-developers-at-netflix-operate-what-you-build]] names Burrell as a coauthor of the Edge Engineering journey.
- Ownership model: [[full-cycle-developers-at-netflix-operate-what-you-build]] links direct team responsibility with faster feedback and system remediation.
- Enablement conditions: [[full-cycle-developers-at-netflix-operate-what-you-build]] requires training, staffing headroom, platform partnerships, tooling, and protected rotations.
- Trade-offs: [[full-cycle-developers-at-netflix-operate-what-you-build]] preserves specialist roles and warns that unmanaged breadth and interrupt work can lead to burnout.

## Qualifications
The wiki has one coauthored, first-party Netflix source for Burrell. It does not isolate his individual contribution from those of Philip Fisher-Ogden, Dianne Marsh, or the wider Edge Engineering organization, and it supplies no independent evaluation of his work or the model's causal effects.
