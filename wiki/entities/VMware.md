## Overview
[[VMware]] is presented as [[DianeGreene]]'s company context for hiring and communication lessons during scale.

## Current Profile
The source uses VMware to show that early hiring can be difficult when the company idea is not yet well-defined, but hiring can become easier at scale without lowering standards. It also uses VMware's weekly writeups as an example of written communication spreading across teams.

## Key Characteristics
- Shows the contrast between ambiguous early hiring and high-volume later hiring.
- Treats hiring standards as non-negotiable even when hiring more than 100 people a month.
- Uses weekly written updates to help teams know what is happening across the organization.
