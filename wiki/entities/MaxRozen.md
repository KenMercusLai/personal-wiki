## Overview
[[MaxRozen]] is a web developer, writer, indie SaaS operator, and [[Cloudflare]] engineer represented here by a 2023 year-in-review essay about focusing on [[OnlineOrNot]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Rozen as a practitioner trying to become financially self-sufficient through web-development products while still valuing full-time employment. His 2023 work combines [[ROZENMD]], [[OnlineOrNot]], a React book, diary writing, Cloudflare work on [[CloudflareD1]], and hardware-writing experiments that incidentally increased product awareness.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds toward self-sufficiency through focused, revenue-generating web products.
- Prefers patient product compounding over many unrelated small bets.
- Uses writing as both public communication and private reflection.
- Keeps full-time work as a constraint and support structure for morning shipping.
- Combines software product work with practical developer and hardware writing.

## Evidence
- Focused product path: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] says ROZENMD grew faster than expected in 2023 and attributes OnlineOrNot's gains partly to accumulated business learning.
- Anti-portfolio view: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] argues that repeatedly moving unrelated products from zero to early MRR is harder than improving an existing working product.
- Writing practice: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] says diary posts help Rozen reflect on what he planned, did, and learned.
- Work constraint: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] says he does not expect to quit full-time work soon because two morning hours help him ship.
- Cloudflare role: [[2023-focusing-on-a-single-product-pays-off]] says he became one of the founding engineers of [[CloudflareD1]].

## Qualifications
The source is a personal annual retrospective, so it gives founder interpretation rather than audited revenue data, independent customer research, or a general base-rate comparison between focused products and small-product portfolios.
