## Overview
[[FOUR]] is [[SketchK]]'s first iOS app, a lightweight time-tracking product organized around four long-term goals and the popular 10,000-hour rule.

## Current Profile
FOUR moved from a rough idea to App Store approval in about three weeks and was then published on GitHub. Its four-item structure came from the author's calculation that four daily hours would take about ten years to reach 10,000 hours in one pursuit. The product was developed in Objective-C after comparative consideration of Swift, React Native, ReactiveCocoa, and RxSwift, while its interface emerged from competitor research, a four-icon Dribbble composition, and an eight-combination font study that ended with Lato. The source documents design intent and release, not usage or durability.

## Key Characteristics
- Limits its central presentation to four long-term goals.
- Frames time tracking through the popular 10,000-hour mastery idea.
- Was initially implemented in Objective-C with a planned Swift rewrite.
- Prioritizes a simple visual experience informed by competitor review.
- Was distributed through the App Store and released as open-source code on GitHub.

- Technical implementation: [[four-a-zero-experience-developers-first-open-source-app]] describes choosing Objective-C after considering several native, cross-platform, and reactive alternatives.
- Design process: [[four-a-zero-experience-developers-first-open-source-app]] compares five competing apps and several font combinations before selecting a simple four-icon layout and Lato.

## Qualifications
The source is the creator's launch retrospective and supplies no independent usability study, code review, download figures, retention, reviews, collaboration history, accessibility assessment, or maintenance record. The 10,000-hour model is a motivational theme rather than a validated estimate of how expertise develops, and a planned Swift rewrite is not evidence that the rewrite occurred.
