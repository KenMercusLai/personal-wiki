## Overview
[[MarcusMoore]] is represented as a Bandcamp senior editor and music journalist whose discovery practice crosses platforms, shows, record stores, email, genres, and recording credits.

## Current Profile
Moore treats unfamiliar shows and cross-genre comparison as research, using punk and jazz to find connections back into hip-hop. His professional role supplies Bandcamp browsing and advance email, while SoundCloud surfaces emerging rappers. He rejects algorithmic recommendation as a complete substitute for record-store relationships, physical browsing, liner notes, and active word of mouth.

## Key Characteristics
