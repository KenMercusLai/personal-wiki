## Overview
[[MargitZwemer]] is a coauthor of the 2012 O'Reilly article that introduces the [[DrivetrainApproach]] for building objective-driven data products.

## Current Profile
The source associates Zwemer with a four-step design method that moves from objective to controllable levers, required data, and models, then connects those models through simulation and optimization. It provides no separate biography or division of author contributions.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthored an objective-first data-product design framework.
- Connects predictive modeling to simulation, optimization, and implementable outcomes.
- Uses examples across search, insurance, recommendations, marketing, engineering, and autonomous systems.
