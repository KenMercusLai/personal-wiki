## Overview
[[AdamTaussig]] appears as the investor-author of the [[LightspeedVenturePartners]] essay "Broken is beautiful."

## Current Profile
The source presents Taussig as an investor who learned to value early consumer products that are visibly flawed but still used intensely. His lens is diagnostic: rather than treating polish as the main signal, he looks for cases where users tolerate friction because the product's core value is unusually strong.

## Key Characteristics
- Investor-author evaluating early consumer products.
- Treats user tolerance of flaws as possible evidence of real demand.
- Uses historical consumer-product examples to explain how rough beginnings can precede stronger mature products.

## Evidence
- Author role: [[broken-is-beautiful-lightspeed-venture-partners-medium]] is written from Taussig's investor perspective.
- Product-evaluation lens: [[broken-is-beautiful-lightspeed-venture-partners-medium]] says he developed a taste for broken products that are clearly working anyway.
- Example method: [[broken-is-beautiful-lightspeed-venture-partners-medium]] compares Uber, Stitch Fix, Twitter, HQ Trivia, and Android as imperfect products with strong user pull.
