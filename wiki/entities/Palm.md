## Overview
[[Palm]] appears in the wiki as a pre-iPhone smartphone and PDA incumbent that moved faster than BlackBerry to replace an obsolete software platform but still failed to build enough momentum with [[WebOS]].

## Current Profile
The source contrasts Palm with BlackBerry. Palm initially dismissed the iPhone, but it quickly accepted that its own software platform was antiquated and rushed out [[WebOS]] with the Palm Pre in 2009. Gassee treats this as less a case of blindness than of insufficient time, quality, and ecosystem momentum: the product was attractive but buggy, and the surrounding application ecosystem remained too weak to compete with iOS and Android.

## Key Characteristics
- Pre-iPhone mobile incumbent with an obsolete software platform.
- Reacted faster than BlackBerry by developing [[WebOS]].
- Failed because the Palm Pre and WebOS ecosystem were too little and too late.

## Evidence
- Dismissal then response: [[blackberry-meditation-at-the-grave-jean-louis-gassee-medium]] quotes Palm dismissing "PC guys" before describing its rush to Linux-based WebOS.
- WebOS launch: [[blackberry-meditation-at-the-grave-jean-louis-gassee-medium]] says WebOS shipped with the Palm Pre in spring 2009.
- Outcome: [[blackberry-meditation-at-the-grave-jean-louis-gassee-medium]] says Palm was sold to HP and quickly shut down after WebOS failed to gain momentum.

## Qualifications
The source gives a compact comparative account rather than a full Palm history. It does not separately analyze Palm's hardware, carrier, marketing, capital, developer-relations, or HP integration choices.

## What Changed
