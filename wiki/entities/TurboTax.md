## Overview
[[TurboTax]] is [[Intuit]]’s tax-preparation product, represented here through ProPublica’s 2019 comparison of its commercial Free Edition and its separate IRS-backed Free File/Freedom Edition.

## Current Profile
The observed journey made “free” highly visible at acquisition while making the scope of that promise conditional. ProPublica’s independent-contractor and uninsured-cashier profiles entered through the commercial site, supplied extensive information, and then encountered $119.99 and $59.99 upgrade screens. The genuinely free IRS-backed route used different naming and a distinct entry point; a TurboTax FAQ said it was not accessible from the regular website, and one Free File information page placed an eligibility control beside a “Start for Free” control that returned users to the commercial path.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers guided online tax preparation through multiple similarly named product paths.
- Promotes a commercial Free Edition for simple returns while historically operating a separate IRS-backed offer.
- Determines price eligibility after collecting substantial filing information in the observed commercial journey.
- Uses page-state classification and calls to action that can route users between free and paid funnels.
- Relies on search discovery, making query wording and result placement consequential to product access.

## Qualifications
These findings describe tested 2019 interfaces for the 2018 tax year, not TurboTax’s current products or every user’s experience. Two synthetic profiles cannot estimate incidence or total consumer harm. The screenshots demonstrate the screens and copy encountered, while intent is inferred from the combined journey and business-policy context rather than a complete internal design record.
