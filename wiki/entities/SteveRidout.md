## Overview
[[SteveRidout]] is the solo founder and author behind the 2016 article "3 Years as a One Man Startup," where he reflects on building [[Readlang]].

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Steve Ridout appears as a bootstrapped software creator making a continued bet on a product that is growing and helping users, but not yet compensating him well. His case adds a candid economic counterweight to more mature independent-creator stories: signups and revenue can compound, but the founder may still face high opportunity cost, uncertain projections, and repeated doubt about whether to continue.

## Key Characteristics
- Builds and operates Readlang as a one-person language-learning web app.
- Reports revenue numbers openly despite the reputational risk of appearing too small.
- Treats user impact as a serious reason to keep working even when income is weak.
- Weighs bootstrapping against contractor income and conventional software employment.
- Frames continued work as a bet on sustained growth, not as a proven financial outcome.
