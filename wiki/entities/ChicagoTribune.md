## Overview
The [[ChicagoTribune]] is represented through a 2017 first-party analysis of declining organic distribution for its Facebook posts.

## Current Profile
The newspaper had roughly half a million Facebook fans by March 2017 and usually published link posts about 20–24 times per day. Despite audience growth and a broadly stable content strategy, its Facebook page experienced a rapid increase in posts reaching at most 10,000 people, showing how a publisher's nominal audience can diverge from platform-mediated access to that audience.

## Key Characteristics
- Operated a large, comparatively stable newspaper audience on Facebook.
- Used predominantly link-based publishing, much like the newspaper peers in the source's comparison.
- Measured reach through averages, medians, and distribution buckets rather than follower count alone.
- Remained dependent on Facebook's opaque ranked feed for organic access to followers.

## Evidence
- Audience and cadence: [[facebooks-algorithm-isnt-surfacing-one-third-of-our-posts-and-its-getting-worse]] reports roughly half a million fans and an increase from about 20 to 24 posts per day.
- Content mix: [[facebooks-algorithm-isnt-surfacing-one-third-of-our-posts-and-its-getting-worse]] reports that 97.2% of posts were links, slightly high but comparable to peer newspapers.
- Reach deterioration: [[facebooks-algorithm-isnt-surfacing-one-third-of-our-posts-and-its-getting-worse]] reports 242 posts reaching at most 10,000 people in March 2017, up from 8 in December 2016.
- Audience-control gap: [[facebooks-algorithm-isnt-surfacing-one-third-of-our-posts-and-its-getting-worse]] says the page added more than 130,000 fans while many posts received diminished organic reach.

## Qualifications
The evidence is a single first-party historical analysis of Facebook Insights data. It does not establish the Tribune's current strategy, compare post quality at the individual level, or prove that Facebook rather than publishing choices, news cycles, competition, or other factors caused the reach change.
