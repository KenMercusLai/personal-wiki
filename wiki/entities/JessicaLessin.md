## Overview
[[JessicaLessin]] is a technology journalist and the founder, owner, editor, and chief executive of [[TheInformation]].

## Current Profile
The 2016 profile presents Lessin as a former Wall Street Journal reporter who built a premium technology-news publication around exclusive reporting, professional subscribers, and member community. Her dense Silicon Valley network helped establish the business and recruit an initial audience, but those same relationships created persistent questions about editorial distance. She argues that commercially valuable credibility requires tough reporting, while acknowledging that company management and talent selection were new disciplines she had to learn.

## Key Characteristics
- Built a journalism business around direct reader revenue rather than advertising scale.
- Combined editorial leadership, reporting, sales, product decisions, hiring, and geographic expansion in the founder role.
- Used representative customer interviews and an existing professional network to shape and launch the offer.
- Retained ownership through family funding and disciplined spending while leaving open the possibility of later investment.
- Operated inside the elite technology network she covered, creating both sourcing advantages and independence concerns.

## Evidence
- Business design: [[inside-the-information-columbia-journalism-review]] describes the $399 subscription, investor tier, member benefits, conservative funding, and focus on exclusive or deep reporting.

- Network and independence: [[inside-the-information-columbia-journalism-review]] documents close technology-industry relationships, criticism of coziness, and Lessin's claim that tough coverage is necessary for subscriber trust.
