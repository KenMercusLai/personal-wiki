## Overview
[[Nadeef]] is [[AhmadIqbal]]'s Shopify store selling handheld bidets and the operating context where his customer-experience experiments began.

## Current Profile
The source treats Nadeef as both a real ecommerce store and a laboratory for product discovery. Abandoned-checkout calls helped Iqbal diagnose purchase friction, recover sales, and discover that one-on-one pre-sale service created trust and repeat purchasing. The store also supplied later app ideas: [[Scout]] for abandoned-checkout alerts, [[RavenCallback]] for immediate sales calls, and [[PizzaParty]] for VIP customer thank-yous.

## Key Characteristics
- Shopify store selling handheld bidets.
- Faced abandoned checkout as a high-value leakage point near purchase.
- Used manual phone calls to discover why buyers abandoned and to recover sales.
- Produced evidence that recovered callers became high-LTV repeat customers.
- Served as the source of multiple Shopify app ideas.

## Evidence
- Store context: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Nadeef sold handheld bidets on Shopify.
- Abandoned-checkout problem: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says one in three final-checkout prospects failed to complete purchase.
- Recovery and learning: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] reports moving from about 10% automated recovery to 55% when customers were reached by phone.
- LTV signal: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says eight of the top ten customers had originally abandoned checkout and been personally called.

## Qualifications
The performance figures are founder-reported in a single retrospective. The source does not provide cohort tables, attribution controls, margin analysis, or later store performance.
