## Overview
[[LauraBehrensWu]] is the [[Shippo]] founder represented in the wiki through personnel advice and commentary on e-commerce shipping complexity and channel power.

## Current Profile
The startup-advice source presents Behrens Wu as a memorable personnel-decision voice. Her advice emphasizes that teams often overestimate the short-term disruption of removing someone and underestimate the continuing damage of leaving harmful behavior or fit in place.

Her operating domain is e-commerce shipping. As Shippo's founder and CEO, she explains that bulky orders may require scheduled drop-off, apartment carry-in, unboxing, and assembly, making them more expensive and complex than parcel delivery. She also frames Amazon's breadth as channel power: becoming the habitual place to search across product categories can make competition harder for other retailers. Her comments identify specialist carriers and packaging innovation as practical responses, but the evidence does not compare their outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder and CEO of shipping-software company Shippo in the 2018 source.
- Operator source on bulky-item delivery, packaging, and carrier specialization.
- Interprets broad product discovery as a source of retail-channel power.
- Frames firing as an organizational-health decision, not only a staffing decision.
