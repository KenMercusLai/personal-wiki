## Overview
[[FredGeorge]] is the practitioner credited in the source with the idea of [[ProgrammerAnarchy]].

## Current Profile
The available essay presents George only through an organizational idea: programmers choose their own work, management and product-management layers are removed, and engineers accept end-to-end responsibility. The source does not supply a biography, implementation case, or direct account from George.

## Key Characteristics
- Credited with the Programmer Anarchy model.
- Associated with high programmer autonomy and broad responsibility.
- Appears through a secondary description rather than direct evidence.
