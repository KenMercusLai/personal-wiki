## Overview
[[SerenityInvestor|Serenity]] is an anonymous investor whom [[serenity-qi-shi-lu]] portrays as a specialist in overlooked small-cap technology companies occupying hard-to-substitute supply-chain positions.

## Current Profile
The wiki knows Serenity only through 王翼之's short secondary essay. In that account, Serenity researches papers and bills of materials, opens positions before full market consensus, continues diligence while invested, and rotates capital as bottlenecks change. The source attributes spectacular performance and an early AXTI recommendation to this process, but provides no primary posts, trade ledger, portfolio definition, or independent verification. The defensible profile is therefore a source-scoped description of a claimed method, not an authenticated biography or performance record.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented as anonymous, with no verified identity, biography, institutional affiliation, or investable track record in the supplied evidence.
- Associated with [[ChokepointInvesting]], which seeks indispensable but underfollowed materials, equipment, and technologies in constrained supply chains.
- Described as converting initial structural research into an early position, then deepening diligence while capital is already at risk.
- Portrayed as rotating rather than buying and holding, moving capital toward the bottleneck judged most active.
- Associated with extraordinary performance claims that remain uncorroborated and internally under-specified.

## Evidence
- Identity and approach: [[serenity-qi-shi-lu]] calls Serenity an anonymous investor and describes the thesis-execution-diligence-rotation sequence.
- Research method: [[serenity-qi-shi-lu]] attributes the use of academic papers, bills of materials, supply-chain maps, patents, contracts, product yields, substitution threats, and customer insourcing signals to Serenity's process.
- Market positioning: [[serenity-qi-shi-lu]] says the approach favors hidden small-cap suppliers and action before institutional consensus.
- Reported outcomes: [[serenity-qi-shi-lu]] claims a 4,502% 2026 return and an early AXTI recommendation, but supplies no underlying records.

## Qualifications
Every characteristic comes from one interpretive article rather than Serenity's authenticated writing or audited account. The source gives no methodology for its return number, dates for the AXTI entry and exit, portfolio value, deposits or withdrawals, position sizing, leverage, realized results, drawdown, losing trades, survivorship selection, or benchmark. Its statement that a move from US$12 to US$70 produced a 1,000% floating gain is not supported by those prices alone. The profile must not be read as verification that Serenity invented the method, achieved the reported performance, or follows a formally Bayesian process.
