## Overview
[[BalajiSrinivasan]] appears as the speaker of "Silicon Valley's Ultimate Exit," arguing that technology builders should reduce dependence on political voice by creating peaceful alternatives, and as Coinbase's 2018 CTO explaining blockchain and token complexity.

## Current Profile
The exit talk presents Srinivasan as a Silicon Valley political-technology thinker. He frames exit as a meta-concept covering emigration, founding companies, open-source forks, customer switching, and opt-in societies. His argument is not merely that Silicon Valley should lobby harder; he says it should build systems that make Washington, D.C. and other paper-era institutions less able to impose bad policies on those who can choose alternatives. Fortune adds his company role at [[Coinbase]]: as CTO, he explains blockchains as unusually complex systems and tokens as a broader engineering surface that can securitize assets without every builder mastering the whole blockchain stack.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents exit as a peaceful complement to voice.
- Uses company competition, open-source forks, and emigration as parallel examples.
- Frames Silicon Valley as a rising institutional rival to older U.S. paper-era centers.
- Argues for technology that lowers switching, relocation, and jurisdictional barriers.
- Treats Bitcoin, 3D printing, telepresence, quantified self, drones, and smart property as exit-enabling infrastructure.
- Served as Coinbase CTO in Fortune's 2018 profile and argued that tokens could broaden blockchain use.
