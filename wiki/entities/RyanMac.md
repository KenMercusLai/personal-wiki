## Overview
[[RyanMac]] is represented here as the BuzzFeed News technology reporter who obtained and contextualized an internal memo about [[TBH]]'s school-by-school launch method after its acquisition by [[Facebook]].

## Current Profile
Mac's report combines the reproduced memo with acquisition history, teen-platform context, company response, and outside commentary. His central contribution to the current wiki is evidentiary: the memo documents TBH's claimed process and its intent to guide Facebook product development, while the reporting preserves Facebook's refusal to say whether it later employed the tactics.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports on technology companies and internal operating practices.
- Obtained and published a TBH memo shared inside Facebook.
- Distinguished access to a growth playbook from proof of its later implementation.

## Evidence
- Internal-document reporting: [[internal-facebook-note-here-is-a-psychological-trick-to-target-teens]] reproduces TBH's launch sequence and stated reason for sharing it.
- Context and response: [[internal-facebook-note-here-is-a-psychological-trick-to-target-teens]] places the memo after Facebook's acquisition and shutdown of TBH and records Facebook's refusal to comment on use of the tactics.

## Qualifications
The current profile is based on one 2018 article and is not a complete account of Mac's career. The report does not independently validate every claim in the founders' memo, the reported acquisition price, or the launch method's causal effect.

## What Changed
- Established Mac as the reporter and evidentiary intermediary for the TBH memo.
- Preserved the distinction between documented internal guidance and unproven Facebook implementation.
