## Overview
[[AppDynamics]] is an enterprise application-monitoring company represented through an early investor's account of its path from a 2008 startup called Singularity Technologies to a planned IPO and last-minute acquisition by [[Cisco]] in 2017.

## Current Profile
The source presents AppDynamics as a founder-led technical company that turned application monitoring into a broader application-intelligence and digital-customer-experience proposition. [[JyotiBansal]] supplied the founding vision, [[BhaskarSunkara]] helped build the initial engineering organization, and an early team of roughly a dozen engineers delivered the first product before sales and customer onboarding expanded.

By 2015, the company reportedly had hundreds of employees and more than a thousand enterprise customers. The board recruited [[DavidWadhwani]] as CEO to professionalize subscription metrics, executive leadership, and public-company readiness while Bansal became chairman. The company filed for an IPO, began its roadshow, and then accepted Cisco's acquisition proposal before trading began.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as Singularity Technologies around a thesis that application monitoring would become application intelligence and automation.
- Combined founder vision with an early engineering organization led by Bansal and Sunkara.
- Used recognizable early enterprise customers as adoption signals while building a broader customer base.
- Framed digital transformation and customer experience as business-result problems, not only infrastructure monitoring.
- Added a scaling CEO and senior executive team as it prepared for public-company operation.
- Reported strong subscription-account expansion through a [[SaaSLandAndExpand]] motion.
- Chose Cisco's acquisition proposal immediately before its planned Nasdaq debut.

## Evidence
- Formation and financing: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] says Greylock and Lightspeed co-led the Series A after Chandna met Bansal in 2008.
- Product and team: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] identifies Sunkara as the first hire and says about a dozen engineers built the initial product.
- Customer adoption: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] names Netflix, Priceline, Williams Sonoma, Electronic Arts, and TiVo as early customers and claims more than a thousand enterprise customers by 2015.
- Leadership scaling: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] says Wadhwani joined as CEO in September 2015 after a board-led search, while Bansal became chairman.
- Expansion economics: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] reports average customer purchases growing to five times the initial purchase and top-25 accounts to fourteen times.
- Transaction: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] says Cisco proposed a merger during the IPO roadshow and that the agreement was signed and announced before public trading began.

## Qualifications
The sole evidence is a Greylock investor's celebratory account written around the acquisition announcement. It does not independently verify customer counts, account-expansion multiples, Gartner rankings, IPO demand, valuation, or Cisco's proposed strategic benefits, and it cannot show whether the post-acquisition integration fulfilled those expectations.
