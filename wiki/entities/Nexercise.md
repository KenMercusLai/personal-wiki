## Overview
[[Nexercise]] is the fitness-application company described as acquiring and merging with [[Sworkit]] in [[RyanHanna]]'s 2017 retrospective.

## Current Profile
Before the transaction, Nexercise let users log fitness activity in real time or retrospectively and work toward prizes. Its cofounders Ben Young and Greg Coleman had previously met Hanna at a conference, later approached him about selling Sworkit, and brought him into the combined company as vice president of product and engineering.

The source presents the merged team's vision as making accessible, motivating fitness and rehabilitation support for busy adults, children, seniors, and patients. It does not supply transaction terms, integration detail, ownership structure, or evidence that the expanded mission produced outcomes for those populations.

## Key Characteristics
- Operated a fitness app based on activity logging and prize-oriented motivation.
- Acquired and merged with Sworkit after its founders approached Ryan Hanna.
- Added Hanna as vice president of product and engineering.
- Framed the combined product mission around broader fitness and rehabilitation accessibility.
