## Overview
[[Even]] is the small company whose 2017 self-description supplies the wiki's current case for values-led [[WorkplacePerkDesign]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Even as a 17-person company with an Oakland office, a mission of making life easier for people who lack financial advantages, and a culture emphasizing autonomy and mutual trust. Its workplace approach separates resources that employees can use outside work from amenities that draw leisure, consumption, or obligatory social life into the office. During an office expansion, it added private rooms, shared offices, and flexible spaces for different work modes while declining to add a recreation area.

## Key Characteristics
- Small company with 17 employees at the source's publication date.
- Describes autonomy and mutual trust as core workplace values.
- Offers minimum paid leave, flexible schedules, medical plans, equity, salaries, and flexible stipends.
- Designs office space around focused work and varying levels of collaboration.
- Rejects several conventional startup perks when they may increase distraction, pressure, or exclusion.

## Evidence
- Size and setting: [[anti-perks-even]] describes a 17-person team and its Oakland office in 2017.
- Benefits philosophy: [[anti-perks-even]] says Even supports employee life outside work through time, flexibility, coverage, compensation, ownership, and stipends.
- Office design: [[anti-perks-even]] reports adding private rooms, shared offices, and flex spaces after polling employees about work and collaboration needs.
- Deliberate exclusions: [[anti-perks-even]] lists daily catering, entertainment rooms, alcohol, mandatory fun, and pet-friendly offices as anti-perks.
- Mission: [[anti-perks-even]] closes by describing the company's goal as making life easier for people without access to such advantages.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one company-authored 2017 essay rather than a complete corporate history or current description. Its culture, mission, headcount, office arrangement, benefits, and policies may have changed, and the source provides no independent employee or outcome evidence.
