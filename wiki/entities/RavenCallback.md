## Overview
[[RavenCallback]] is a Shopify callback app from [[AhmadIqbal]]'s team that turns website visits into qualified sales calls.

## Current Profile
The source presents Raven Callback as a second product built from the same customer-conversation thesis as [[Scout]]. Instead of waiting for cart abandonment, Raven lets interested visitors request immediate phone contact, which Iqbal found more effective than contact forms or live chat for his small business context. The source reports a small paid merchant base and more than $500,000 in generated merchant sales over the prior three months.

## Key Characteristics
- Converts website interest into phone conversations.
- Extends the phone channel beyond abandoned checkout recovery.
- Targets merchants whose products benefit from one-on-one sales help, especially higher-priced purchases.
- Had early paid adoption rather than only free installs.
- Serves as a second systematized workflow from Nadeef's customer-experience experiments.

## Evidence
- Product role: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says Raven turns website visits into qualified sales calls.
- Form replacement: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] says forms and live chat felt too slow or effortful for many small-business buyers.
- Revenue effect: [[building-apps-for-shopify-fall-in-love-with-the-problem-not-the]] reports that merchants using paid plans generated over $500,000 in sales over three months.

## Qualifications
The sales figure is founder-reported and does not specify attribution method, merchant count denominator, churn, margins, or time horizon beyond the three-month claim.
