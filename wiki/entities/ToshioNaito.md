## Overview
[[ToshioNaito]] is the Disco executive and management-system designer credited by Bloomberg with creating and stewarding Personal Will.

## Current Profile
The source presents Naito as a law-educated worker who trained as an accountant at a milk producer before becoming interested in organizational management. At [[Disco]], he developed the Personal Will system after CEO [[KazumaSekiya]] asked for a management approach inspired by games such as Final Fantasy or Dragon Quest. Naito frames the result as a "free economic zone" inside the company, where work should operate through freedom and price signals rather than orders.

## Key Characteristics
- Designer and ongoing steward of Disco's Personal Will program.
- Background combines legal study, accounting training, and productivity-improvement work.
- Uses market language to justify internal pricing, auctions, and team billing.
- Borrowed scoring and competitive mechanics from games to make work feel measurable and engaging.
- Acknowledges cultural adjustment costs, including slower acceptance outside Japan.

- Career path: [[charging-employees-for-conference-rooms-helps-disco-boost-profit-bloomberg]] says he studied law, trained as an accountant, and became interested in organizational management.
