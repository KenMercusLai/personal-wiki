## Overview
[[SteveLopez]] is the Los Angeles Times columnist who reported the 2017 account of Californians moving to lower-cost places, especially Las Vegas, in search of a more attainable middle-class life.

## Current Profile
In the source, Lopez combines reader responses, five household examples, a 2016 census trend, and an economist's conditional forecast to argue that California's housing shortage and commuting burden were exporting residents who still valued the state. His role is both reporter and advocate: he acknowledges California's economic and cultural strengths but judges its housing response too weak and its widening affordability divide damaging to the middle class.

## Key Characteristics
- Los Angeles Times columnist writing about California housing affordability and migration.
- Reporter using household budgets, commutes, and housing choices to make an economic argument concrete.
- Critic of California's housing supply response and its consequences for working and middle-class residents.
- Author who distinguishes cost-driven relocation from conventional moves for higher pay or promotion.
