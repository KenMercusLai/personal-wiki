## Overview
[[BhaskarSunkara]] is represented as [[AppDynamics]]' first hire, an early engineering-team builder, and the company's product head and CTO at the time of [[AsheemChandna]]'s 2017 retrospective.

## Current Profile
Sunkara partnered with founder [[JyotiBansal]] to assemble the initial engineering team. The source says roughly a dozen engineers produced AppDynamics' first product, after which engineering and a single salesperson began onboarding early enterprise customers. It later identifies Sunkara as part of the senior leadership team retained through [[DavidWadhwani]]'s CEO tenure.

## Key Characteristics
- First hire at AppDynamics.
- Partnered with Bansal on initial engineering-team formation.
- Helped lead the team that delivered the first product.
- Remained a senior product and technology leader as the company scaled.
