## Overview

## Current Profile
The article presents Slack through its origin as [[TinySpeck]], a game company that later pivoted into the collaboration software company Slack Technologies. The case illustrates the uncertainty employees face when choosing whether to hold or sell startup equity before the winning outcome is visible.
