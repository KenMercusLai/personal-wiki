## Overview
[[GeorgeSoros]] appears in the source as the speaker whose 2018 World Economic Forum remarks criticized large social-media companies for engineered addiction, concentrated power, behavioral targeting, and threats to individual autonomy.

## Current Profile
The source presents Soros as a billionaire philanthropist and liberal activist concerned with threats to open society. Newport extracts two strands from Soros's Davos speech: social-media products may use casino-like techniques to make use compulsive, and their data and targeting capabilities may manipulate political behavior. Newport adopts the first concern as his primary focus while treating Facebook's emphasis on the second as strategically convenient.

## Key Characteristics
- Criticizes social-media companies for deliberately engineering compulsive use.
- Links concentrated data and targeting power to manipulation and reduced freedom of mind.
- Places platform conduct alongside wider threats to open society.
