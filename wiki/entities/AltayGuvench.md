## Overview
[[AltayGuvench]] is an engineer, musician, former freelancer, and partner in [[TenXManagement]] who supplied the agency's technical screening and credibility with programmers.

## Current Profile
The 2014 profile traces Guvench from music and a failed live-touring startup into self-taught front-end development, Ruby on Rails, employment at L.L.Bean, and freelance programming. After Michael Solomon negotiated a 50% rate increase and handled his contract and invoicing, Guvench moved from client to partner. He reviews applicants' code and products, evaluates communication, matches specialists to work, and describes the agency as a way to reduce freelance risk rather than merely find jobs.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines engineering experience with a working musician's understanding of creative representation.
- Learned programming after a startup failure exposed a gap between technical fascination and business needs.
- Values readable, concise, efficient code and the ability to enter unfamiliar technical domains quickly.
- Treats communication and client fit as part of technical professional quality.
- Frames representation as operational and negotiating support for freelancers who already attract work.

## Evidence
- Career transition: [[the-programmers-price]] describes Guvench learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Ruby on Rails before employment and freelancing.
- Client-to-partner path: [[the-programmers-price]] reports that Solomon raised his rate from $100 to $150 per hour and removed contract and billing work before Guvench joined the business.
- Technical standards: [[the-programmers-price]] records his emphasis on human readability, DRY code, and efficient database access.
- Agency role: [[the-programmers-price]] says he interviews candidates, reviews code and prior products, screens communication, and matches talent with projects.
- Freelance thesis: [[the-programmers-price]] attributes to him the goal of reducing feast-or-famine work and administrative burden.
