## Overview
[[LiJiaoao]] is the author of the ingested Pop Mart reading note and investment essay.

## Current Profile
The source presents Li Jiaoao as a blogger-investor interpreting [[PopMart]] through a reading of [[BecauseUnique]], public commentary, and product/business-model observation. His article is analytical rather than purely descriptive: it tests common doubts about blind boxes, [[Labubu]] concentration, and the company's ability to keep evolving.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses reading notes as a vehicle for investment reasoning.
- Separates business quality questions from valuation risk.
- Looks for operating mechanisms behind consumer-brand performance.
- Treats founder history and small retail episodes as evidence about management quality.
