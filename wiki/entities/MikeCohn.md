## Overview
[[MikeCohn]] is represented in the wiki as the author of User Stories Applied and a reference point for [[UserStories]] practice.

## Current Profile
The PBB article positions Cohn as one of the major references on user stories after their [[ExtremeProgramming]] origin. It also credits him with reframing the INVEST "S" from "small" to "sized appropriately," which makes story sizing contextual rather than rigidly tiny.

## Key Characteristics
- Author of User Stories Applied in the source's account.
- Treated as a major reference on user-story practice.
- Refined INVEST terminology by shifting "small" toward "sized appropriately."

## Evidence
- Reference role: [[blog-paulo-caroli-martinfowler-com-product-backlog-building-canvas]] says Cohn's User Stories Applied is considered a major reference on the topic.
- INVEST refinement: [[blog-paulo-caroli-martinfowler-com-product-backlog-building-canvas]] says Cohn renamed the S in INVEST from "small" to "sized appropriately."
- Contextual sizing: [[blog-paulo-caroli-martinfowler-com-product-backlog-building-canvas]] uses that refinement to acknowledge that stories may be larger or smaller depending on team context.
