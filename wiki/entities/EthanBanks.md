## Overview
[[EthanBanks]] is represented in this wiki as the author of a reflective career-advice article organized around what he would tell his younger self in his 20s, 30s, and 40s.

## Current Profile
Banks contributes a practitioner-advice perspective on career development. His article treats career growth as a mix of technical and business competence, risk timing, humility, hierarchy awareness, trustworthy employers, explicit goals, emotional intelligence, relationships, recovery, mental health, boundaries, patience, habits, restraint, and ownership of one's remaining time.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers age-staged career reflection rather than a single universal career formula.
- Separates competence from promotion, compensation, authority, and organizational trust.
- Connects career sustainability to recovery, boundaries, habits, restraint, and mental health.

## Evidence
- Age-staged structure: [[blog-ethan-banks-career-advice-id-give-to-20-30-and-40-something-year-old-me]] organizes advice for his 20-, 30-, and 40-something self.
- Organizational realism: [[blog-ethan-banks-career-advice-id-give-to-20-30-and-40-something-year-old-me]] warns that meritocracy, hierarchy, compensation, and dishonest owners are not solved by technical competence alone.
- Sustainability frame: [[blog-ethan-banks-career-advice-id-give-to-20-30-and-40-something-year-old-me]] presents relaxation, saying no, daily habits, patience, restraint, and mental health as part of later-career judgment.

## Qualifications
The article is self-reflective advice rather than systematic research. It gives compact lessons without detailed autobiographical evidence or measured career outcomes.
