## Overview
[[CNBC]] appears as the business-news publisher of reports on Amazon's planned database migration and Erik Finman's Bitcoin-funded entrepreneurial path.

## Current Profile
Across the two sources, CNBC functions as a business and technology news outlet using different reporting modes. The Amazon-Oracle article combines confidential sourcing with company statements, analyst context, market reaction, and executive remarks. The [[ErikFinman]] profile instead centers an interview subject's personal history, reported holdings, and forecasts while adding a separate analyst view and an explicit warning about [[Bitcoin]] volatility.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes business and technology reporting about public-company strategy and market competition.
- Uses confidential sourcing for Amazon's internal Oracle migration timeline.
- Pairs the migration report with company statements, analyst notes, stock-market reaction, and executive quotes.
- Publishes founder and investor profiles built around personal narrative, reported asset values, and direct quotations.
- Adds countervailing market context when presenting an interview subject's highly optimistic forecast.
