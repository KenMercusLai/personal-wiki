## Overview
[[AgileBits]] is the software company presented in the 2013 interview as the maker of [[OnePassword]].

## Current Profile
The source depicts AgileBits as a founder-led Mac software company whose flagship product expanded onto iOS through incremental adaptation and then a complete rewrite. Direct feedback influenced continued investment, desktop-free syncing, and support for less-technical users. The company also positioned limited data collection as part of being a security company, accepting weaker product-segment measurement as a consequence.

## Key Characteristics
- Grew around 1Password after an internal form-testing utility attracted external users.
- Used direct feedback and support requests to guide product commitment and platform scope.
- Expanded from a Mac-only base to iOS, with sales reportedly approaching a 50/50 split by 2013.
- Rebuilt 1Password 4 for iOS to use newer Apple capabilities and provide a longer-lived foundation.
- Supported Dropbox and iCloud synchronization without requiring a desktop computer.
- Limited user-data collection even when that made resource allocation less evidence-rich.

## Qualifications
The profile is historical to June 2013 and should not be read as a current company description. Its adoption, sales, technical-success, and beta-demand claims come from company participants, while the desktop-free share is explicitly estimated and broad usage telemetry was intentionally unavailable.
