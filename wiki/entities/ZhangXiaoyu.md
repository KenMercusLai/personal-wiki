## Overview
[[ZhangXiaoyu]] is presented in the sources as the teacher of a personal investing course and as a hedge-fund manager known to Wulc through the podcast 得意忘形.

## Current Profile
The sources represent Zhang Xiaoyu through three sections of his personal investing course rather than through a complete biography. His course is framed as practical guidance for ordinary investors: understand one's advantage over professional managers, avoid asset-preference traps, resist market timing, distrust overbroad macro prediction, measure risk by outcomes, diversify globally, avoid overpaying, control fees, audit one's own psychology, and convert principles into portfolio construction. The profile therefore centers on investor education, behavioral discipline, and implementable allocation rather than on his fund record.

## Key Characteristics
- Teaches personal investing for ordinary individual investors.
- Emphasizes avoiding obvious mistakes before seeking complex techniques.
- Contrasts individual-investor freedom with professional-investor pressure.
- Frames investing as a long-term, continuing process rather than a one-shot trade.
- Uses historical market cases to explain behavioral traps.
- Emphasizes valuation, margin of safety, cost control, and not confusing a good company with a good stock.
- Treats ordinary-investor success as dependent on self-knowledge, independent decision space, outside income, portfolio construction, rebalancing, factor exposure, and language skepticism.
