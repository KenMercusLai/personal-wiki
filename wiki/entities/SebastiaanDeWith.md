## Overview
[[SebastiaanDeWith]] is a designer, photographer, and co-creator of the iPhone camera app [[Halide]].

## Current Profile
The first-year retrospective associates de With with Halide's tactile camera experience, its iPhone X redesign, and educational articles about iPhone X design and RAW photography. The latter reportedly created more useful demand than the team's paid social-advertising experiments, joining product craft with value-providing distribution.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-created Halide with [[BenSandofsky]].
- Helped shape a professional, tactile camera interface around deliberate photography.
- Contributed to the iPhone X redesign that placed controls for one-handed use while exploiting the taller display.
- Wrote educational product-adjacent content that the team regarded as its most successful promotion.

## Evidence
- Product role: [[halide-one-year-later-halide]] identifies Sebastiaan and the author as Halide's creators.
- Design contribution: [[halide-one-year-later-halide]] links his design retrospective to the iPhone X interface overhaul.
- Content contribution: [[halide-one-year-later-halide]] says his RAW photography articles became popular and outperformed paid social advertising as promotion.
