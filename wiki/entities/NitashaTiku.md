## Overview
[[NitashaTiku]] is the BuzzFeed News journalist who reported "Ground Control To Silicon Valley" from the 2016 [[CodeConference]].

## Current Profile
In the available source, Tiku is both observer and participant in the technology-media access system she critiques. She credits conference co-founder [[KaraSwisher]] as a mentor, discloses previous employment by Gawker Media, and argues that social and professional proximity can give reporters information while discouraging the scrutiny needed for powerful subjects.
