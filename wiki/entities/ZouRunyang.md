## Overview
[[ZouRunyang]] is a software engineer, former technical manager, and startup HR leader represented here through a first-person essay on transferring engineering habits into people and organization work.

## Current Profile
The source says Zou studied software engineering for seven years, worked in programming and technical management for eight years, and moved in mid-2025 into the lead HR role at a startup. His essay presents the transition as a qualified transfer of methods: systems analysis, product thinking, explicit hypotheses, small experiments, failure containment, and recovery can help design organizations, but people cannot be treated as code, rapid iteration targets, or interchangeable system components.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings a software-engineering and technical-management background into HR leadership.
- Treats HR as organization and rule design undertaken with the CEO, not only as functional administration.
- Applies systems, product, and experimental reasoning to hiring, talent development, and organizational learning.
- Explicitly limits engineering metaphors through human irreversibility, slow change, trust, empathy, and sincere listening.
