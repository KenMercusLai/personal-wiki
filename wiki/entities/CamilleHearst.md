## Overview
[[CamilleHearst]] is the iTunes product manager in the source's American Idol integration case.

## Current Profile
The source presents Hearst as an Apple iTunes product manager who worked on integrations that helped move iTunes beyond early adopters toward a mass-market digital music habit. Her American Idol case shows product management at the boundary of product, marketing, media partnership, and fairness constraints: iTunes needed to become part of fan behavior without leaking popularity signals that would spoil or influence the show's voting drama.

## Key Characteristics
- Worked as a product manager on Apple's iTunes team.
- Helped during the transition from DRM-based to DRM-free music and broader mass-market adoption.
- Worked on American Idol integrations after Apple leaders made the business deal.
- Balanced engagement goals with the need not to reveal or influence voting outcomes.
- Later joined YouTube, led product at Hailo, and became CEO of Kit according to the source.
