## Overview
[[KylieCosmetics]] is the direct-to-consumer beauty company founded and wholly owned by [[KylieJenner]], represented as a fast-growing, celebrity-led, operationally light business.

## Current Profile
The company began with a scarce lip-kit launch in November 2015 and relaunched on [[Shopify]] in February 2016 with much greater inventory. Its demand engine combined Jenner's social following, product demonstrations, launch anticipation, and family amplification; its supply and delivery engine relied heavily on Seed Beauty. Forbes estimated more than $630 million in cumulative makeup sales by 2018 and nearly $800 million in company value, but also reported slowing growth, declining lip-kit revenue, and a valuation discount for dependence on one celebrity name.

## Key Characteristics
- Began with a narrow lipstick-and-liner kit before expanding into a broader cosmetics range.
- Used scarcity, short launch notice, and direct social promotion to create concentrated demand.
- Operated with a very small internal headcount relative to reported sales.
- Outsourced manufacturing, packaging, and fulfillment to Seed Beauty and online commerce to Shopify.
- Retained founder ownership rather than raising outside equity in the period described.
- Combined high margins and low marketing overhead with celebrity, supplier, and platform concentration risk.

- Modular operations: [[how-20-year-old-kylie-jenner-built-a-900-million-fortune-in-less-than-3-years]] describes Seed Beauty's production and fulfillment role and Shopify's storefront role.

## Qualifications
The company was private, so several numbers were estimates rather than audited disclosures. Kylie Cosmetics disputed Forbes's estimate of payments to Seed Beauty, and Forbes could not verify Kris Jenner's claim of stronger first-half 2018 growth. The source captures the company before later ownership, distribution, operating, or market changes and cannot establish long-term brand durability.

## What Changed
