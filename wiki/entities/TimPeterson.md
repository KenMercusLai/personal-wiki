## Overview
[[TimPeterson]] is the Marketing Land reporter whose March 2017 article examines the first year of consumer chatbots through interviews with brand, agency, platform, and technology executives.

## Current Profile
The available source presents Peterson as a reporter synthesizing competing explanations for weak chatbot adoption rather than advancing one vendor's platform thesis. He connects product interaction, natural-language limits, brand strategy, CRM, platform discovery, audience fit, and reported engagement examples, while preserving disagreement over Kik adoption in a postscript.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports across brands, agencies, messaging platforms, and bot-technology providers.
- Uses concrete bot interactions and attributed campaign figures to test industry claims.
- Separates conversational design problems from platform distribution and marketing problems.
- Preserves disputed evidence through an explicit post-publication correction.

## Evidence
- Multi-party reporting: [[inside-chatbots-year-of-growing-pains-were-at-an-inflection-point-marketing-land]] draws on interviews with roughly a dozen executives across the chatbot ecosystem.
