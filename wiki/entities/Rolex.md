## Overview
[[Rolex]] appears in the source as the incumbent watchmaker benchmark that [[AppleWatch]] had reportedly overtaken in revenue by 2017.

## Current Profile
The Asymco source uses Rolex as a contrast case: a watch company founded in 1905, producing about one million watches a year, with roughly $4.7 billion in 2016 sales and about $4,700 average revenue per watch. Against that benchmark, [[HoraceDediu]] estimates that [[AppleWatch]] reached about $4.9 billion in annual revenue only two and a half years after launch.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-established incumbent watchmaker.
- Produces far fewer units than Apple Watch in the source's comparison.
- Has much higher average revenue per watch than Apple Watch.
- Serves as a benchmark for Apple Watch's early revenue scale.

## Evidence
- Incumbency: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] says Rolex was founded in 1905.
- Revenue benchmark: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] cites about $4.7 billion in Rolex 2016 sales.
- Unit benchmark: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] says Rolex produces about one million watches a year.
- Apple comparison: [[a-small-screen-ipod-an-internet-communicator-and-a-phone-asymco]] estimates Apple Watch annual revenue above Rolex despite lower average revenue per watch.
