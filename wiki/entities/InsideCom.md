## Overview
[[InsideCom|Inside.com]] is represented here as a digital-news startup that stopped prioritizing its dedicated app and moved its summarized-news experience into email.

## Current Profile
The source describes Inside's app as well reviewed, featured by Apple, and positively received by users, but unable to achieve breakout growth. Its email alerts showed stronger behavior, and the company had accumulated 180,000 email addresses, so it segmented the list and tested a twice-daily Inside Daily Brief with the most interested audience.

The pivot preserved the editorial proposition while changing the delivery medium and operating structure. Calacanis estimated that the app required about 12 people across iOS, Android, web, product, design, technology, growth, and business roles, whereas the initial email operation needed three roles. Early results were encouraging but narrow: he reported a 50% open rate among the top 10% segment and faster list additions in the experiment's second month, without retention, revenue, or profitability evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Produced concise news summaries through a dedicated app before shifting emphasis to email.
- Had an existing list of 180,000 addresses that became the pivot's main reusable distribution asset.
- Used observed email opens rather than reviews or press as the key behavioral signal for the new direction.
- Reduced the reported delivery model from a cross-platform app team of about 12 people to a three-role newsletter operation.
- Positioned the Inside Daily Brief as a high-level, curated news summary delivered twice daily.

## Evidence
- App traction: [[knowing-when-and-how-to-pivot-or-why-didnt-news-apps-work]] says strong reviews, press, Apple featuring, and user feedback did not produce breakout app growth.
- Reusable audience asset: [[knowing-when-and-how-to-pivot-or-why-didnt-news-apps-work]] reports that Inside had built a list of 180,000 email addresses over several years.
- Engagement-led pivot: [[knowing-when-and-how-to-pivot-or-why-didnt-news-apps-work]] says people opened email alerts while failing to use the app, leading the company to move the experience into email.
- Early experiment: [[knowing-when-and-how-to-pivot-or-why-didnt-news-apps-work]] reports 50% opens in the most interested 10% segment, 800 list additions in January, and 3,900 more by nearly halfway through February.
- Operating simplification: [[knowing-when-and-how-to-pivot-or-why-didnt-news-apps-work]] contrasts an estimated 12-person cross-platform app team with a writer, chief executive, and growth or marketing practitioner for the email product.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to a founder-authored February 2016 pivot announcement. The article gives no app usage cohorts, absolute email recipient count, open-rate method, list-addition attribution, click or retention data, unsubscribe rates, revenue, margins, acquisition costs, or later outcomes. The top-segment results cannot be generalized to the full list, and the app-versus-email staffing comparison may omit differing scope and costs.
