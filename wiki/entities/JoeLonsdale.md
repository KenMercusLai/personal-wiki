## Overview
[[JoeLonsdale]] appears in the wiki as the [[8VC]] general partner who wrote "Bring in the Adults?" about when startups should add experienced management.

## Current Profile
The source presents Lonsdale as an investor-advisor speaking to startup CEOs and prospective early employees. His contribution to the wiki is a timing model for senior startup hiring: early companies should protect invention speed and founding-culture intensity, then add experienced operators quickly once revenue, product-market clarity, and repeatable business processes appear.

## Key Characteristics
- Gives venture-backed startup operating advice.
- Emphasizes stage-sensitive hiring rather than blanket preference for youth or experience.
- Treats advisors as useful early scaffolding around technical founders.
- Treats experienced executives as process leverage after the startup is ready to scale.

## Evidence
- Author role: [[bring-in-the-adults-8vc-news-medium]] lists Lonsdale as author and 8VC general partner.
- Early-stage model: [[bring-in-the-adults-8vc-news-medium]] says very young startups need talented builders, cultural fit, rapid iteration, and advisors more than full-time veteran management.
- Scaling model: [[bring-in-the-adults-8vc-news-medium]] says senior leaders become valuable when a company needs repeatable sales, HR, engineering management, marketing, finance, strategy, or other business processes.
