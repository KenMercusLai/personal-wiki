## Overview
[[PeterPathe]] appears in this wiki as the Microsoft executive whom [[StevenSinofsky]] credits for introducing the phrase "writing is thinking."

## Current Profile
The source presents Pathe briefly but meaningfully: he was VP of the Word team during a major growth era, and Sinofsky remembers learning from him both the phrase "writing is thinking" and a competitive framing about Excel as an advantage against WordPerfect. In this context, Pathe functions less as a biographical subject than as the origin point for a management aphorism about writing, thought, and company work.
