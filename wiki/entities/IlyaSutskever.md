## Overview
[[IlyaSutskever]] is represented in [[GregBrockman]]'s 2017 account as OpenAI's founding research visionary and as Brockman's complementary partner in designing the early organization's strategy, culture, and division of work.

## Current Profile
Brockman describes Sutskever as supplying machine-learning intuition while Brockman supplied engineering and team-building experience. They used that complementarity to define [[OpenAI]]'s research direction, culture, tactics, and equal valuation of research and engineering. The account also credits Sutskever's conviction, alongside Alex Krizhevsky's GPU implementation work, as important to AlexNet's ImageNet success.

The partnership was operational rather than merely advisory. Brockman initially tried to remove non-research work from Sutskever, but when [[OpenAIGym]] engineering constrained research progress they exchanged responsibilities: Sutskever absorbed administrative tasks while Brockman built the software. The episode presents technical leadership as a flexible allocation of scarce attention.

## Key Characteristics
- Served as the early organization's machine-learning research visionary in Brockman's account.
- Brought research intuition that complemented Brockman's engineering and team-building experience.
- Helped define OpenAI's strategy, culture, and operating practices before launch.
- Advocated an organization that could attract strong researchers through mission and academic openness backed by industry resources.
- Took on administrative work during the Gym push so engineering effort could address the research bottleneck.

## Evidence
- Research role: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] says the founders needed an AI technical visionary and considered Sutskever the strongest fit.
- Complementary partnership: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] contrasts Sutskever's machine-learning expertise with Brockman's engineering and team-building experience.
- Institution design: [[greg-brockman-define-cto-openai]] says the pair worked on strategy, culture, recruiting assumptions, and daily research practices before launch.

## Qualifications
This profile is based entirely on Brockman's appreciative first-person account and does not include Sutskever's own explanation or independent evidence about the division of work. The AlexNet attribution is secondhand through a remark Brockman attributes to Geoffrey Hinton and compresses a broader research and engineering history.
