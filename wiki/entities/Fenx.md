## Overview
[[Fenx]], also represented by the social handle `@haxfenx`, is the independent designer-developer behind [[GanFanShouCe|干饭手册]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Fenx as a product designer learning native iOS development through a five-month part-time build. He moved a household meal-recording practice from a Feishu-table MVP into a released SwiftUI app, personally covering product definition, interaction and visual design, implementation, cloud sharing, payments, localization, testing, compliance, and App Store review. His process combines close requirement control and real-device inspection with coding-agent assistance and spontaneous visual exploration. He describes the launch as both success—shipping a promise made to his partner—and failure because scope, energy use, and older-device performance were poorly managed.

## Key Characteristics
- Works across product design, visual design, engineering, testing, and distribution in a solo project.
- Uses coding agents intensively while retaining direct control over requirements and device-level acceptance.
- Favors inspiration-led visual experimentation beyond strictly necessary product scope.
- Treats recording as both a product purpose and a personal reason to write retrospectives.
- Publicly identifies failures and implementation detours alongside the shipped result.

## Evidence
- End-to-end role: [[95-zhi-zuo-wo-de-di-yi-kuan-ios-app-gan-fan-shou-ce]] describes Fenx designing and building the app, its cloud and payment infrastructure, four-language localization, widgets, compliance pages, and release process.
- Agent-assisted learning: [[95-zhi-zuo-wo-de-di-yi-kuan-ios-app-gan-fan-shou-ce]] reports using Cursor throughout, first with Anthropic models and later mainly GPT-5.3-Codex, while micro-managing each requirement.
- Design method: [[95-zhi-zuo-wo-de-di-yi-kuan-ios-app-gan-fan-shou-ce]] describes system UI as a baseline and spontaneous ideas as the source of randomized layouts, cards, shaders, and launch imagery.
- Reflective judgment: [[95-zhi-zuo-wo-de-di-yi-kuan-ios-app-gan-fan-shou-ce]] calls the shipped product a success and its unmanaged demand growth and performance a failure.
- Recording motive: [[95-zhi-zuo-wo-de-di-yi-kuan-ios-app-gan-fan-shou-ce]] reframes both the app and the essay as archives of shared life and imperfect progress.
