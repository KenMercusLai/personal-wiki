## Overview
[[AbBanerjee]] is represented here as the CEO and founder of [[ViewsHub]] and the author of an argument against treating a single chief executive as the sole embodiment of a company.

## Current Profile
Banerjee's available article focuses on organizational representation rather than on his biography or ViewsHub's operations. He argues that media, companies, and investors over-attribute company performance to the CEO, increasing executive burden and transition risk while obscuring the management team. His proposed response is to distribute public communication and recognition across functional leaders and teams.

## Key Characteristics
- Critiques the all-powerful, solitary-CEO narrative.
- Treats management and company performance as collective rather than individual work.
- Connects executive well-being with organizational expectations and communication design.
- Advocates wider public and investor visibility for management teams.
