## Overview
[[Imooc]] is the course platform Zhang Xuan used to sell programming courses during his freelancer period, making it the source's main example of platform-supported technical course income.

## Current Profile
The source presents Imooc as a practical distribution choice for a freelancer who needed income and visibility more than full platform control. Zhang Xuan considered self-hosting courses but chose a larger platform because it reduced promotion, video storage, and sustainability burdens. The tradeoff is platform constraints, but the source argues that faster exposure and income can be worthwhile when a freelancer needs a stable base.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides course distribution and visibility for technical instructors.
- Helped supply Zhang Xuan's main income across three freelancer years.
- Reduced the operational burden compared with self-hosted course sales.
- Works best in the source when courses are high quality and updated over time.
- Imposes some platform limits that are accepted in exchange for exposure and revenue.
