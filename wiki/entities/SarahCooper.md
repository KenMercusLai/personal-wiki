## Overview
[[SarahCooper]] is represented in this wiki as the writer and comedian behind a satirical article excerpted from *100 Tricks to Appear Smart in Meetings*.

## Current Profile
The source presents Cooper as a corporate and technology humorist whose satire targets meeting culture, workplace status performance, and the empty vocabulary of creativity and strategy. Her author bio says she created The Cooper Review, previously worked as design lead for Google Docs, and became a writer and speaker on corporate and tech humor.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes workplace satire focused on corporate and technology culture.
- Uses meeting advice formats to expose performative behavior rather than teach sincere productivity.
- Connects humor with prior experience in technology workplace settings.
- Uses simple illustrations and short captioned scenes as part of the satirical argument.
