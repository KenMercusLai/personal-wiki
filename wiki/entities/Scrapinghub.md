## Overview
[[Scrapinghub]] is the web-data-extraction company presenting the source's historical lessons from operating large e-commerce crawling projects.

## Current Profile
The article describes Scrapinghub as founded in 2010, the author of [[Scrapy]], and an operator of high-volume product-data pipelines. Its operating model combines crawl engineering, dedicated QA, distributed discovery and extraction, proxy management, and automated monitoring; the article also promotes the company's commercial services and [[Crawlera]].

## Key Characteristics
- Builds and operates large-scale web-data-extraction systems.
- Maintains the open-source Scrapy crawling framework.
- Treats extraction as a continuing engineering and QA operation rather than a one-off scraper build.
- Develops supporting infrastructure for crawl frontiers, proxy management, and automated quality monitoring.

## Evidence
- Scale and role: [[for-e-commerce-data-scientists-lessons-learned-scraping-100-billion-products-pages]] claims more than 100 billion product pages scraped since 2010 and billions of monthly page requests.
- Framework stewardship: [[for-e-commerce-data-scientists-lessons-learned-scraping-100-billion-products-pages]] identifies Scrapinghub as Scrapy's author.
- Operating model: [[for-e-commerce-data-scientists-lessons-learned-scraping-100-billion-products-pages]] describes a large project staffed by crawl engineers and QA engineers and backed by automated monitoring.
- Supporting products: [[for-e-commerce-data-scientists-lessons-learned-scraping-100-billion-products-pages]] presents Frontera and Crawlera as infrastructure around distributed crawling and proxy operations.
