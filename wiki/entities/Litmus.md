## Overview
Litmus is represented in the wiki as an external email-client and device-open tracking reference cited by SendGrid's holiday email analysis.

## Current Profile
The SendGrid source uses Litmus as a comparison point for mobile email opens. SendGrid's own data put mobile just under half of opens, while the cited Litmus tracking put Apple and Android device opens around 54%, reinforcing the article's claim that mobile was no longer an edge case for email marketers.

## Key Characteristics
- Email-client benchmark source: cited for regular tracking of opens by device.
- Mobile-email evidence: supports the claim that Apple and Android devices accounted for a majority-level share of opens.
- External comparator: provides an industry reference outside SendGrid's own platform data.
