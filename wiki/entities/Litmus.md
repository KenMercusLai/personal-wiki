## Overview
Litmus is represented in the wiki as an email-client analytics reference and as a historical example of making a newsletter's value and cadence visible before signup.

## Current Profile
The SendGrid source uses Litmus as a comparison point for mobile email opens: SendGrid's data put mobile just under half of opens, while the cited Litmus tracking put Apple and Android device opens around 54%. A separate 2016 Optimizely article shows a Litmus signup component placing screenshots from prior editions above a short form and stating an approximate monthly cadence plus a promise not to share the address. Together these sources represent Litmus through email analytics and a concrete expectation-setting interface, not its present product scope.

## Key Characteristics
- Email-client benchmark source: cited for regular tracking of opens by device.
- Mobile-email evidence: supports the claim that Apple and Android devices accounted for a majority-level share of opens.
- External comparator: provides an industry reference outside SendGrid's own platform data.
- Signup expectation-setting example: exposed past newsletter content, cadence, and an address-sharing promise before commitment.
