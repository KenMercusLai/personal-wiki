## Overview
[[WPEngine]] is a WordPress hosting company used in the source as an example of generous affiliate incentives.

## Current Profile
The source says WP Engine competed in a crowded hosting market by offering a high affiliate payout for referrals. The tactic makes customer acquisition partly performance-based because commissions are paid after referred signup.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides WordPress hosting and optimization.
- Uses an affiliate program to acquire customers.
- Pays a large per-signup commission according to the source.

## Evidence
- Affiliate payout: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says WP Engine pays $200 to users who refer a signup.
- Market fit: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] frames the program as cost-effective for a limited premium WordPress hosting audience.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide conversion rates, payout economics, churn, or comparison with other hosting acquisition channels.
