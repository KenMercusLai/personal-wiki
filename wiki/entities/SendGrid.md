## Overview
SendGrid is represented in the wiki as an email infrastructure and marketing platform whose large delivery volume gives it visibility into retail email behavior, device usage, open timing, and subject-line engagement.

## Current Profile
The SendGrid source emphasizes operating scale and behavioral analytics rather than product features. By sending 1.6 billion emails on Black Friday 2016 and more than one billion emails per day for much of that year, SendGrid becomes evidence for [[EmailMarketingAtScale]]: email marketing is both an infrastructure problem and a data source for retail timing, device mix, and engagement patterns.

## Key Characteristics
- Large-scale sender: SendGrid handled billion-message daily volume and a 1.6-billion-email Black Friday peak.
- Retail-channel observer: its holiday data connects email volume to Black Friday and Cyber Monday shopping behavior.
- Analytics-oriented operator: its Big Data Team analyzed device mix, open delay, subject-line length, and subject-line vocabulary.
- Mobile-design advocate: the source uses SendGrid open data to argue for responsive email templates across devices.

## Evidence
Large-scale sender:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] reports more than one billion emails per day for much of 2016 and 1.6 billion emails on Black Friday.

Retail-channel observer:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] frames Black Friday as the largest holiday email-volume peak and links retail demand to sustained weekend volume.

Analytics-oriented operator:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] describes SendGrid analyses of opens, mobile-versus-desktop behavior, subject-line length, and 80,000 English-language subject lines.

Mobile-design advocate:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] argues that responsive templates are necessary because desktop and mobile opens were close to parity.

## Qualifications
The article is a company-authored marketing blog post. Its data is useful as platform-scale operational evidence, but it does not disclose full sampling methods for every metric and should not be treated as an independent industry benchmark.

- Positioned SendGrid as evidence for email marketing as infrastructure plus behavioral analytics.
