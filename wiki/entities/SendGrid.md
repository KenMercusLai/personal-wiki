## Overview
SendGrid is represented in the wiki as an email infrastructure and marketing platform whose large delivery volume gives it visibility into retail email behavior, device usage, open timing, and subject-line engagement, and whose early API wedge is used as a product-scope example.

## Current Profile
The SendGrid sources present two different but compatible views. The holiday email analysis emphasizes operating scale and behavioral analytics: by sending 1.6 billion emails on Black Friday 2016 and more than one billion emails per day for much of that year, SendGrid becomes evidence for [[EmailMarketingAtScale]]. Yin's startup-scope essay uses SendGrid's early product as the opposite of incumbent feature parity: it began with an email-sending API rather than a full email-marketing interface, showing how a narrow wedge can later grow into a broader platform.

## Key Characteristics
- Large-scale sender: SendGrid handled billion-message daily volume and a 1.6-billion-email Black Friday peak.
- Retail-channel observer: its holiday data connects email volume to Black Friday and Cyber Monday shopping behavior.
- Analytics-oriented operator: its Big Data Team analyzed device mix, open delay, subject-line length, and subject-line vocabulary.
- Mobile-design advocate: the source uses SendGrid open data to argue for responsive email templates across devices.
- Narrow-wedge example: Yin uses SendGrid as a startup that first focused on sending email through an API instead of matching full email-marketing suites.

## Evidence
Large-scale sender:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] reports more than one billion emails per day for much of 2016 and 1.6 billion emails on Black Friday.

Retail-channel observer:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] frames Black Friday as the largest holiday email-volume peak and links retail demand to sustained weekend volume.

Analytics-oriented operator:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] describes SendGrid analyses of opens, mobile-versus-desktop behavior, subject-line length, and 80,000 English-language subject lines.

Mobile-design advocate:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] argues that responsive templates are necessary because desktop and mobile opens were close to parity.

Narrow-wedge example:
- [[build-a-product-that-fits-your-runway-elizabeth-yin]] says SendGrid initially focused on being an API that sent mail, without an interface or WYSIWYG email-marketing features.

## Qualifications
The holiday email article is a company-authored marketing blog post. Its data is useful as platform-scale operational evidence, but it does not disclose full sampling methods for every metric and should not be treated as an independent industry benchmark. Yin's SendGrid characterization is a brief startup-advice example rather than a full company history.
