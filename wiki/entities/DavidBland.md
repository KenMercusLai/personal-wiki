## Overview
[[DavidBland]] is identified by the source's Precoil branding and `@davidjbland` attribution as the practitioner behind its modern-team guidance.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Bland advises organizations on new-product work and describes assumptions mapping as a way to connect desirability, viability, and feasibility with design, product, and engineering. His team model combines those roles with dedication, evidence, continuing customer discovery, and diverse perspectives.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames product-team design around the assumptions and learning needs of new-product development.
- Treats design, product, and engineering as the minimum cross-functional core rather than a complete universal roster.
- Advocates data-influenced outcome accountability and continuing customer discovery.
- Connects team composition and safe disagreement with the assumptions encoded into products.
