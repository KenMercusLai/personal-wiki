## Overview
[[JonHughes]] is the volunteer coder who built [[VoteForBernie]], an unofficial 2016 primary and caucus voting-information site for supporters of [[BernieSanders]].

## Current Profile
The article presents Hughes as a clear example of high-leverage civic software built outside a formal campaign hierarchy. He had no direct campaign connection, but his site solved a practical voter-information problem and later influenced an official campaign tool. His role shows how a single volunteer project can become infrastructure for mass participation when search demand and campaign enthusiasm converge.

## Key Characteristics
- Built campaign software independently rather than as campaign staff.
- Focused on a simple voter-action problem: where and when to participate in primaries or caucuses.
- Reached a large audience through search visibility and supporter sharing.
- Demonstrates how unofficial volunteer tools can feed into official campaign systems.

## Evidence
- Independent creation: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] says Hughes built the site without a campaign connection.
- Practical workflow: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] describes the site as location-based guidance for voting in Democratic primaries or caucuses.
- Reach: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] reports more than 2 million unique views.
- Official influence: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] says the campaign later launched a related tool based on Hughes' primary finder.
