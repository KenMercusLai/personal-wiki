## Overview
[[GoogleHomepage]] appears as the digital-product exemplar in the source's argument that some interfaces may eventually be considered timeless.

## Current Profile
The source frames the Google Homepage as a late-1990s counter-position to web portals. While competitors such as MSN and Yahoo tried to become broad destination sites, Google's homepage stayed focused on one job: search. The inspected screenshot sequence labeled 1999, 2010, and 2017 shows a persistent center-weighted search interface, even as typography, links, account affordances, and visual treatment changed. The page therefore functions as the source's best digital example of a simple, recognizable interface core surviving long-term iteration.

## Key Characteristics
- Search-first interface rather than destination portal.
- Recognizable across 1999, 2010, and 2017 visual iterations.
- Used as the digital counterpart to the Porsche 911.
- Represents digital timelessness through restraint, focus, and careful iteration.

## Evidence
- Portal contrast: [[can-digital-products-be-timeless-startup-grind-medium]] says Google stayed focused on search while MSN and Yahoo tried to become destination sites.
- Core purpose: [[can-digital-products-be-timeless-startup-grind-medium]] describes the Google Homepage as built around the best possible search experience.
- Shared qualities: [[can-digital-products-be-timeless-startup-grind-medium]] describes the Google Homepage and Porsche 911 as simple, innovative, unobtrusive, engineered, iconic, beloved, and recognizable.
- Visual continuity: [[can-digital-products-be-timeless-startup-grind-medium]] includes inspected homepage screenshots from 1999, 2010, and 2017 that retain the central search box as the primary interface.

## Qualifications
The source evaluates the homepage as a design object and does not assess Google's broader search quality, advertising model, privacy tradeoffs, antitrust issues, or later interface changes. The timelessness claim is prospective and interpretive rather than a settled design-history consensus.
