## Overview
[[Netflix]] is presented as the company context for [[ReedHastings]]' culture-deck, talent-density, and context-not-control lessons.

## Current Profile
The source uses Netflix to show how written culture, talent density, and freedom-through-context can shape an organization after survival pressure. The 2001 layoff story becomes evidence that a smaller, denser team can get more done, and the public culture deck becomes a way to let candidates and employees debate the company's operating philosophy.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses written culture material as a candidate-visible and employee-debatable artifact.
- Links talent density with productivity and lower process burden.
- Prefers context over control when operating with few rules.
- Treats CEO role evolution as moving from doing everything to vision, focus, inspiration, and culture.
