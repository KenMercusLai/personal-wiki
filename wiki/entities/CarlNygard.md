## Overview
[[CarlNygard]] is represented in the wiki as the [[Thoughtworks]] Technical Principal who authored "Compliance in a DevOps Culture" on Martin Fowler's site.

## Current Profile
In this source, Nygard writes as a practitioner of digital platform strategy in regulated organizations. His contribution is to recast compliance as a delivery architecture problem: teams need objective evidence, validation constraints, auditability, and threat-model-aware trust boundaries, but the shape of that process must preserve DevOps autonomy and fast feedback as organizations scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames compliance as risk management for safe software delivery.
- Connects regulatory controls to [[ContinuousDelivery]] and DevOps operating goals.
- Analyzes compliance patterns through organizational scale and second-order effects.
- Argues for separating measurement from validation in mature [[ComplianceArchitecture]].
- Emphasizes returning pipeline ownership to development teams while preserving reusable controls.

## Evidence
- Compliance framing: [[blog-carl-nygard-martinfowler-com-compliance-in-a-devops-culture]] defines compliance through properties, measurements, evidence, constraints, validation, and audit.
- DevOps context: [[blog-carl-nygard-martinfowler-com-compliance-in-a-devops-culture]] places the problem inside team autonomy, frequent small releases, and low MTTR.
- Scale analysis: [[blog-carl-nygard-martinfowler-com-compliance-in-a-devops-culture]] compares manual, pipeline, composition, and point-of-change patterns as organizations grow.
