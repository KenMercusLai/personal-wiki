## Overview
[[Firebox]] is a gift and gadget e-commerce company used in the source as an affiliate-network growth example.

## Current Profile
The source says Firebox used multiple affiliate networks and social relationships with affiliates to drive revenue growth. The appeal is that affiliate acquisition can be self-financing when commission is paid against sales.

## Key Characteristics
- Gift and gadget e-commerce retailer.
- Used three affiliate networks simultaneously according to the source.
- Offered commission to website owners sending sales.
- Reported as generating more than £7 million through affiliates over the cited period.

## Evidence
- Affiliate scale: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says affiliates drove more than £7 million in revenue.
- Network breadth: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says Firebox worked with three affiliate networks to reach more potential partners.
