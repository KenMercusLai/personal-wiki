## Overview
[[PhantomBladeZero]] is a Chinese action game used by [[Plum]] as a prospective case of prolonged preview marketing outrunning verifiable release readiness.

## Current Profile
The source describes years of playable-event exposure, invited domestic and overseas media, trailers, technology demonstrations, and a publicized release date followed by a roughly fifty-day delay. Plum interprets that sequence as evidence that promotional visibility was mistaken for production completeness and predicts a meaningful risk of renewed delay or launch backlash.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented through combat previews, boss-difficulty discussion, trailers, media coverage, and technology demonstrations.
- Received invitation-only preview promotion involving domestic and overseas creators and outlets.
- Became a test case for whether repeated publicity creates unjustified confidence in schedule and quality.
- Its eventual quality, release stability, reception, and commercial result remain outside the evidence supplied by this source.

## Evidence
- Preview history: [[plumz-14418]] recounts playable show appearances beginning around 2024 and later large invitation events.
- Schedule signal: [[plumz-14418]] says the announced September 9 release moved to October 29 for additional polishing.
- Expectation effect: [[plumz-14418]] reports that sustained exposure led the author to infer the game was far along and likely to ship on time.
- Prospective warning: [[plumz-14418]] compares the resulting expectation burden with [[WuchangFallenFeathers]] but supplies no post-release outcome for Phantom Blade Zero.

## Qualifications
The page reflects one June 2026 opinion essay, not a verified development history. The article provides no studio schedule, milestone data, preview build provenance, budget, defect record, or independent evidence for its prediction of another delay.
