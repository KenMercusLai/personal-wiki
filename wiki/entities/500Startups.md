## Overview
[[500Startups]] is the venture investment platform used in the source as evidence for a high-volume early-stage portfolio strategy.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, 500 Startups is currently represented by McClure's description of investing in more than 1,000 companies over five years. The source presents the platform as a real-world counterexample to traditional small VC portfolios: its scale exposed many failures but also created more chances to capture rare centaur and unicorn outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates as a high-volume startup investor in the source.
- Uses a large portfolio to improve exposure to rare power-law winners.
- Treats many failures as expected portfolio behavior rather than evidence that the strategy failed.
- Provides the experiential basis for McClure's recommended early-stage portfolio sizes.

## Evidence
- Investment scale: [[99-vc-problems-but-a-batch-aint-one-why-portfolio-size-matters-for-returns]] says 500 Startups invested in more than 1,000 companies over five years.
- Outcome pattern: [[99-vc-problems-but-a-batch-aint-one-why-portfolio-size-matters-for-returns]] says most investments fail or return less than 1X, while a few become centaurs or unicorns.
- Portfolio-size model: [[99-vc-problems-but-a-batch-aint-one-why-portfolio-size-matters-for-returns]] uses the platform's experience to argue for 100-200 or more seed-stage investments.

## Qualifications
The source is promotional and argumentative, not a full independent audit of 500 Startups' realized fund returns. Its outcome rates are presented as experience-based assumptions rather than complete audited performance data.
