## Overview
[[OneDollarShaveClub]] is a subscription grooming company used in the source as an example of viral comedy video driving customer acquisition.

## Current Profile
The article says One Dollar Shave Club used crude, funny founder-led videos to make a simple razor subscription memorable. The subscription model created repeat revenue, but the viral video helped produce initial awareness.

## Key Characteristics
- Subscription business for razors and grooming products.
- Used founder-led comedy videos for acquisition.
- Reported in the source as becoming a $600 million business within three years.
- Its first major video is reported as reaching 19 million views.

## Evidence
- Viral video: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says the first video used a memorable comedic strapline and reached 19 million views.
- Subscription tie-in: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] links video attention to a repeat-purchase subscription model.
