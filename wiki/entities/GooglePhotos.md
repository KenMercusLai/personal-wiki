## Overview
[[GooglePhotos]] is presented as the later Google product that benefited from people and ideas carried forward from [[Bump]], [[Flock]], and Photoroll.

## Current Profile
The source does not describe Google Photos broadly; it positions the product as the eventual destination of Bump team's photo-sharing learning. After [[Google]] acquired Bump, the team moved quickly into Google work, retained nearly all members for years, and Lieb says they were fortunate to play a large role in creating Google Photos.

## Key Characteristics
- Receives lineage from Bump team's photo-sharing exploration.
- Is connected to unreleased Photoroll ideas rather than only the original Bump app.
- Serves as evidence that an exploratory startup path can lead to a later major product.
- Is tied in the source to unusually high post-acquisition team retention.

## Evidence
- Product lineage: [[bump-10-years-of-lessons-learned]] says Photoroll's unreleased ideas later came back in the form of Google Photos.
- Team continuity: [[bump-10-years-of-lessons-learned]] says the Bump team began work at [[Google]] after acquisition and that 15 of 17 members remained after five years.
- Creation role: [[bump-10-years-of-lessons-learned]] says the team played a large role in creating Google Photos.

## Qualifications
This page reflects one source's retrospective connection from Bump to Google Photos. It does not cover Google Photos' full product history, other teams, launch details, or feature-by-feature influence.
