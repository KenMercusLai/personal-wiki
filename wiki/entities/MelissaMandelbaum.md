## Overview
[[MelissaMandelbaum]] is represented in the wiki as a practitioner and writer who published a structured personal-growth framework based on experience at Percolate, Dropbox, and with leadership coaches.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Mandelbaum combines self-reflection, manager alignment, measurable quarterly goals, energy-aware strengths development, meditation, and reduced notification exposure into a plan she says she revisits each quarter. Her emphasis is practical and iterative: annual reflection establishes direction, quarterly objectives focus action, weekly review preserves visibility, and daily habits protect attention.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats personal growth as a designed, recurring practice rather than an occasional review.
- Separates durable values from motivations that may change over time.
- Uses measurable quarterly objectives and results to connect reflection with action.
- Distinguishes being good at an activity from being energized by it.
- Uses manager discussion and attention-protecting habits to support execution.

## Evidence
- Framework design: [[design-your-personal-growth-melissa-mandelbaum-medium]] organizes development around motivations, values, strengths and weaknesses, objectives and key results, energy, meditation, and proactive habits.
- Review cadence: [[design-your-personal-growth-melissa-mandelbaum-medium]] recommends annual updates for motivations, values, and baseline strengths; quarterly objectives and strengths choices; weekly OKR review; and daily meditation.
- Workplace alignment: [[design-your-personal-growth-melissa-mandelbaum-medium]] says sharing motives, values, and the completed plan with a manager can help align development opportunities.
- Energy distinction: [[design-your-personal-growth-melissa-mandelbaum-medium]] argues that learned competence can remain draining and that unrealized strengths may offer a more energizing development direction.

## Qualifications
The profile is based on one first-person 2017 essay, not a complete biography or an evaluation of Mandelbaum's later practice. The article reports personal benefit but provides no comparative outcome data for the framework, Strengths Profile, meditation, notification limits, or manager sharing.
