## Overview
[[ApolloGraphQL]] is the publication and developer-tooling context for the ingested comparison of [[GraphQL]] and [[RESTAPI]].

## Current Profile
In this 2017 source, Apollo GraphQL advocates GraphQL as an evolutionary change to familiar API mechanics rather than an entirely alien networking model. The article also points to Apollo Client for frontend result caching and Apollo Engine for server-side infrastructure, but it does not independently assess those products, the organization, adoption, or competitive position.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes technical education advocating GraphQL adoption through comparison with familiar REST concepts.
- Associates GraphQL with schema-driven queries, field resolvers, client-selected response shapes, and fewer round trips for related resources.
- Presents Apollo Client and the historical Apollo Engine as tooling responses to GraphQL caching and infrastructure needs.

## Evidence
- Publication role: [[graphql-vs-rest-apollo-graphql]] presents the GraphQL-versus-REST comparison under the Apollo GraphQL title.
- Technical position: [[graphql-vs-rest-apollo-graphql]] argues that small changes to familiar API mechanics can materially improve developer experience.
- Tooling context: [[graphql-vs-rest-apollo-graphql]] names Apollo Client for frontend caching and Apollo Engine for server-side caching and operational tooling.

## Qualifications
The available evidence is a vendor-associated educational article, not an independent profile or comparative product evaluation. Its tooling observations are historically bounded to 2017, and the source does not establish later names, features, adoption, performance, or organizational status.
