## Overview
[[Front]] is a collaborative email SaaS company represented through its retention-led growth, 2016 Series A process, and the founder-operator advice of [[MathildeCollin]].

## Current Profile
Product Habits presents Front as a sticky product in a competitive space. Rather than maximizing logos alone, Front focused on retained and expanding accounts: users added teammates, used collaborative email features, sent more email, upgraded plans, and produced enough expansion revenue for net negative churn. Collin's own Series A account supplies the earlier financing snapshot: at $100,000 in MRR with low churn and a clear roadmap, the 15-person company ran a ten-day process that produced three term sheets. Investors valued spending below ARR and 50% higher customer spending after 12 months net of churn, but challenged the scalability of organic acquisition and the absence of a spending model, engagement metrics, and a defined buyer. Her advice essay adds that Front used customer conversations and growth evidence to choose direction and later had to preserve founder context as executives joined.

## Key Characteristics
- Built around collaborative email workflows.
- Encourages team-wide usage through assignment, sharing, transparency, and collaboration tools.
- Treats onboarding as the beginning of account expansion rather than a separate acquisition step.
- Used retention and upsell to grow existing accounts.
- Reported net negative churn in the source's case narrative.
- Used customer and growth evidence to refine product direction.
- Entered its Series A with reported capital efficiency and expansion strength but incomplete acquisition, buyer, and projection evidence.

- Onboarding link: [[acquisition-is-easy-retention-is-hard-product-habits]] says Front encouraged adding team members, using more features, and sending more emails from day one.

## Qualifications
