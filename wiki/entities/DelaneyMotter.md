## Overview
[[DelaneyMotter]] is represented as founder of the music blog *Phluff Online* and an advocate of social and live music discovery.

## Current Profile
Motter discovers music through friends, journalists, fans, and the supporting acts encountered at shows. She sees Spotify as an accessible equalizer for people outside music communities, but says her own Spotify discoveries often remain isolated songs rather than becoming strong attachments to bands.

## Key Characteristics
- Relies on word of mouth within music-oriented social circles.
- Uses live bills to encounter bands beyond the act that drew her to a show.
- Distinguishes easy algorithmic access from deeper artist engagement.
- Recommends Spotify especially to listeners still learning their tastes.

## Evidence
- Social discovery: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] describes friends, journalists, and fans as recurring sources.
- Live discovery: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] explains how staying for other acts on a bill surfaces unfamiliar bands.
- Spotify qualification: [[finding-new-music-in-the-algorithm-age-the-outline]] reports usefulness for newcomers but limited conversion from recommended songs into favored bands in her own experience.

## Qualifications
This is one individual's 2018 account under student and work time constraints. It does not establish that live discovery generally produces deeper engagement than algorithmic recommendation.
