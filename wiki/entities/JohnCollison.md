## Overview
[[JohnCollison]] is a co-founder of [[Stripe]] who built the company's first payment prototype with his brother [[PatrickCollison]].

## Current Profile
The source presents John as Patrick's long-standing collaborator: the brothers built iPhone applications while in school, recognized online payment acceptance as the harder enabling problem, and launched the private `/dev/payments` prototype in January 2010. Their roughly two decades of shared experience is offered as one reason they could resolve conflict while building Stripe.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founded Stripe with Patrick Collison.
- Helped identify payment acceptance as a missing developer-friendly infrastructure layer.
- Built the early `/dev/payments` prototype while still in school.
- Represents the possible conflict-resolution value of a long pre-founding relationship.

## Evidence
- Origin and prototype: [[cs183c-session-11-patrick-collison-stripe-blitzscaling-class-notes-and-essays-medium]] says the brothers moved from building iPhone apps to launching `/dev/payments` in January 2010.
- Problem framing: [[cs183c-session-11-patrick-collison-stripe-blitzscaling-class-notes-and-essays-medium]] says they imagined a low-friction payments service analogous to one-click virtual-server provisioning.
- Founder relationship: [[cs183c-session-11-patrick-collison-stripe-blitzscaling-class-notes-and-essays-medium]] credits about twenty years of learning to resolve problems together.
