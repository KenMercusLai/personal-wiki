## Overview
[[GregElfrink]] is the author of a 2018 Moz guest article presenting [[EmpireFlippers]]' framework for valuing and preparing online businesses for sale.

## Current Profile
Elfrink writes from a broker's perspective, translating reported marketplace experience into a seller checklist. He treats valuation as the interaction of normalized cash flow and buyer confidence: operating history, growth, concentration risk, owner workload, transferability, and defensibility change what a buyer may pay for each month of profit. In the appended discussion, he also advocates brokerage support, describes agencies as comparatively hard to transfer, and acknowledges that high-multiple online businesses remain risky investments.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames website valuation through rolling average profit and a monthly multiple.
- Evaluates businesses from a prospective buyer's operating and risk perspective.
- Recommends diversification, systemization, delegation, and documented processes before sale.
- Treats niche authority, brand, differentiated products, and transferable commercial terms as possible moats.
- Writes as an interested brokerage practitioner rather than an independent valuation researcher.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to one 2018 guest article and its appended comment thread. The source does not provide Elfrink's broader biography, a transaction dataset, audited outcomes, buyer returns, or an independent comparison of brokered and private sales. His valuation ranges, fee discussion, and market observations are historical and commercially interested rather than current market guidance.
