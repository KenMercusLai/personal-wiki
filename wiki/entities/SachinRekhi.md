## Overview
[[SachinRekhi]] is the product leader and author of the practitioner guide to [[NetPromoterScore]], drawing on his work at [[LinkedIn]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Rekhi as a former LinkedIn Director of Product who led Sales Navigator and previously worked as an Entrepreneur-in-Residence at Trinity Ventures. His contribution to the wiki is not a full biography but a product-management operating account: he explains how a team can make NPS useful by collecting representative data, reading customer comments, involving the right stakeholders, and translating loyalty signals into roadmap work.

## Key Characteristics
- Product-management author focused on practical NPS implementation.
- LinkedIn operator whose NPS lessons came from product-team experience.
- Connects customer-loyalty measurement to product planning rather than treating it as a vanity score.
- Emphasizes direct customer voice through verbatim comment analysis.
- Treats methodology consistency and sampling bias as central measurement concerns.
