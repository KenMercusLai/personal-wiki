## Overview
[[MSNMessenger]] appears as the Microsoft instant-messaging product in which the modern [[TypingIndicator]] debuted with version 1.0 on July 22, 1999.

## Current Profile
The source places MSN Messenger between earlier chat systems that offered no response activity signal and systems that exposed every character in real time. Its typing indicator gave participants awareness that a reply was being composed without revealing the draft itself. A coded proof of concept, internal self-hosting, and later UI polish turned the underlying mechanism into a shipped interaction that other messaging products widely copied.

## Key Characteristics
- Launched version 1.0 in July 1999 with the typing indicator.
- Used activity state as a middle ground between silence and character-by-character draft exposure.
- Validated the initial mechanism through internal self-hosting before release.
- Separated the patented detection-and-communication mechanism from the polished shipping interface.
- Became the origin product for an interaction pattern later common across messaging and support chat.
