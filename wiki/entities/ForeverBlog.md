## Overview
[[ForeverBlog]] (十年之约) is a Chinese independent-blog project whose screened catalog is used in the source as a comparison cohort for blog longevity.

## Current Profile
The project admits established blogs under category-specific activity and originality requirements. The supplied source says lifestyle blogs must be at least one year old with more than 15 posts and at least 75% original content, while learning blogs must be at least one year old with more than 30 posts and at least 60% original content. These thresholds select for already-active publishers, so the catalog represents a more mature cohort than an unrestricted list.

From 2017-08-31 through 2022-02-05, the project reportedly cataloged 999 blogs and marked 149 as abnormal. [[YZQZSS]] calculates an annual abnormality rate near 3.5% but cautions that some entries were flagged for content issues rather than site closure and that higher-quality blogs may later return. The figure is therefore an upper bound within this selected cohort, not a general survival rate for Chinese independent blogs.

## Key Characteristics
- Curates Chinese independent blogs rather than accepting every discovered site.
- Requires at least one year of prior operation for the two categories described in the source.
- Uses category-specific post-count and original-content thresholds.
- Maintains an abnormal status that can reflect content problems as well as site unavailability.
- Supplies a selected comparison cohort for studying [[LinkRot]] and publisher persistence.

## Evidence
- Admission screen: [[chinese-blogs-link-rot-2022]] reproduces the project requirements for lifestyle and learning blogs.
- Catalog scale: [[chinese-blogs-link-rot-2022]] reports 999 collected blogs and 149 abnormal entries across the stated 2017–2022 interval.
- Durability comparison: [[chinese-blogs-link-rot-2022]] calculates about 3.5% annual abnormality and argues that true closure is lower because the abnormal category is broader.

## Qualifications
The source attributes the data to a project administrator but does not include the underlying dataset, status rules, audit date for each blog, or a reproducible calculation. Survivorship and admission selection are central: requiring one year of history and substantial original output excludes many new or intermittent blogs before observation. “Abnormal” is not equivalent to permanently gone, and later revival would further reduce measured disappearance.

## What Changed
- Created the entity as the curated comparison cohort in the link-rot analysis.
- Distinguished the project's broad abnormal status from confirmed site closure.
- Made its maturity and originality screens explicit sources of selection bias as well as possible quality signals.
