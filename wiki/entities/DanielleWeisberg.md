## Overview
[[DanielleWeisberg]] is a former NBC News producer and co-founder of [[TheSkimm]], represented in the wiki through a 2015 joint interview about her newsroom path, founder relationship, audience insight, and the company's early operations.

## Current Profile
Weisberg's account connects a longstanding interest in politics and media with hands-on newsroom work: internships in public relations and MSNBC, election-period desk work in NBC's Washington bureau, research for network programs, and associate producing in New York. She and [[CarlyZakin]] moved from study-abroad acquaintances to friends, roommates, and business partners. In the founding narrative, Weisberg emphasizes the observed audience gap, morning-email choice, emotional cost of leaving NBC, unsustainable first-six-month workload, and the difficulty of learning fundraising without formal business training.

## Key Characteristics
- Media founder with an editorial and production background in political and broadcast news.
- Co-creator of an audience-routine thesis for delivering current-events context by morning email.
- Co-founder whose relationship with Zakin developed over years before becoming a business partnership.
- First-time fundraiser who describes learning through repeated rejection rather than prior business training.
- Candid about the health and operating costs of continuous early-stage work.

## Evidence
- Career path: [[get-that-life-how-two-friends-started-the-skimm-from-their-apartment]] traces Weisberg from political and media study through PR and MSNBC internships, NBC's Washington bureau, network research, and associate producing.
- Product thesis: [[get-that-life-how-two-friends-started-the-skimm-from-their-apartment]] attributes the audience-gap diagnosis and morning-email routine to conversations with busy friends and observation of changing news consumption.
- Founder relationship: [[get-that-life-how-two-friends-started-the-skimm-from-their-apartment]] says Weisberg and Zakin reconnected at NBC, became close friends and roommates, and only then became partners.
- Operating strain: [[get-that-life-how-two-friends-started-the-skimm-from-their-apartment]] says six months of around-the-clock work became unsustainable and that first-time fundraising produced roughly nine rejections for every acceptance during one period.

## Qualifications
This profile comes from one success-oriented joint interview and reflects Weisberg's own retrospective account. It does not independently verify career dates, subscriber growth, fundraising results, company performance, or the relative contribution of each founder, and it should not be treated as a complete biography or as proof that severe early overwork was necessary.
