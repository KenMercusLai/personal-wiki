## Overview
[[StefLewandowski]] is the author and cofounder in the source's account of turning [[Makelight]] from a photography course experiment into a bootstrapped family business.

## Current Profile
Lewandowski describes a background spanning computer science, AI, design-agency work, startup studios, writing, speaking, and rapid prototyping. In Makelight, he combined those capabilities with [[EmilyQuinton]]'s photography teaching and audience: the initial course used hosted services rather than custom code, while later engineering focused on removing operational work and creating creator-facing analytics tools. His financing position is pragmatic rather than categorically anti-investor—bootstrap to preserve optionality, then evaluate outside capital once proven demand and constrained growth make the tradeoff concrete.

## Key Characteristics
- Cross-disciplinary technical and design builder.
- Cofounder pursuing family-compatible, location-flexible work.
- Uses lightweight demand tests before custom engineering.
- Prioritizes automation of costly internal work.
- Treats customer conversation as a product-discovery loop.
- Favors bootstrapping for optionality while remaining open to later financing.
