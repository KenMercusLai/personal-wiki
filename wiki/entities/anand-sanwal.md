Anand Sanwal is the author of "54 Screwups as a Startup CEO" and writes from the position of founder and CEO of CB Insights. In the source, he frames the article as a public list of mistakes he made while the company moved from leaner early conditions into a growing organization with more revenue, more people, and higher execution demands.

The article is useful because Sanwal treats the CEO role as an operating-learning role. His mistakes span culture, hiring, pricing, sales, product prioritization, administration, and personal habits, showing how founder behavior can shape or distort the systems a startup eventually depends on.
