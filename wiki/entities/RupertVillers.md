## Overview
[[RupertVillers]] is an [[AspenInsurance]] co-CEO quoted in the source on why shared leadership fit a complex global insurance business.

## Current Profile
Villers argues that Aspen's geographic and product diversity made it difficult for one person to cover all time zones while maintaining desired control. He frames successful co-CEO work as grounded in longstanding trust, different skill sets, plain speech, and the ability to sort out disagreements directly.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-CEO at Aspen Insurance.
- Emphasizes global coverage and control as reasons for shared leadership.
- Values plainspoken disagreement and complementary skill sets.
- Previously worked in a co-CEO pairing with John Cavoores.
