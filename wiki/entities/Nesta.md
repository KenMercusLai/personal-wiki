## Overview
[[Nesta]] is the research and innovation organization that published the 2014 article applying creative-occupation criteria to data science.

## Current Profile
Within this source, Nesta connects creative-economy mapping with data-skills research. Its authors use prior occupational-classification work and the *Model Workers* study to argue that recognizing the creative structure of data science could improve capability building, talent management, education, and recruitment communication.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes research spanning creative occupations and data-science talent.
- Develops a five-criterion heuristic for classifying creative work.
- Translates research classifications into management and skills-policy implications.

## Evidence
- Publication context: [[data-science-work-is-creative-work-p1]] is a Nesta article grounded in earlier Nesta creative-economy research.
- Program connection: [[data-science-work-is-creative-work-p1]] draws on the *Model Workers* study of how leading companies recruit and manage data talent.

## Qualifications
The page reflects one historical publication and does not describe Nesta's full mission, programs, governance, or current organization. The article's occupational scoring is a heuristic rather than an independently validated measurement.
