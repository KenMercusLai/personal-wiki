## Overview
[[SamAltman]] is presented as a startup advisor whose cited lesson is to hire slowly before product-market fit.

## Current Profile
The source uses Altman to state the pre-scale hiring rule: the best startups hire the least and slowest in the beginning, while weaker ones mistake headcount for progress. His contribution anchors the article's distinction between discovery-stage discipline and scale-stage recruiting urgency.

## Key Characteristics
- Warns that early headcount can create burn and organizational inertia.
- Distinguishes pre-product-market-fit hiring from post-fit scaling.
- Uses Airbnb, Dropbox, and Stripe as examples of slow first hires.
