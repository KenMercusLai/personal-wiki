## Overview
[[KerenLehavot]] is a University of Washington clinical psychologist cited as a co-author of the 2012 case report that anchors the Nautilus article on clinicians searching for patients online.

## Current Profile
In this source, Lehavot's role is evidentiary rather than advisory: her article supplies the extreme Facebook-triggered hospitalization case that makes the ethical problem concrete. The case shows how a clinician's prior online search, an autofilled Facebook result, and an alarming profile image could lead to police involvement, involuntary hospitalization, and a rupture in patient trust.
