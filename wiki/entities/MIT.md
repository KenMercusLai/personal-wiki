## Overview
[[MIT]] appears in this wiki as the engineering-school context for [[AmyWibowo]]'s technical formation and as the origin setting for a face-reading SDK later used in her robotics research.

## Current Profile
The source presents MIT primarily through Wibowo's personal path. She chose engineering school there because she wanted serious mathematics and science while still caring deeply about art. The essay also says a face-reading SDK developed at the MIT Media Lab became part of her [[ASIMO]] reinforcement-learning work at [[HondaResearchInstitute]].

## Key Characteristics

- Represents a path where art interest coexisted with advanced technical study.
