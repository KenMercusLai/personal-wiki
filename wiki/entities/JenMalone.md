## Overview
[[JenMalone]] is represented as a co-music supervisor on FX's *Atlanta* who discovers and licenses music through platforms and professional networks.

## Current Profile
Malone uses SoundCloud for hip-hop, rap, and trap releases, follows related artists and friends' playlists on Spotify, and bookmarks music encountered in Instagram Stories for weekly review. Her Atlanta network and contacts among artists, managers, lawyers, licensing companies, labels, and publishers also function as a distributed discovery system.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines platform search with local and professional relationships.
- Uses SoundCloud as an emerging-artist and licensing channel.
- Applies a recurring bookmark-and-review habit to short social-media encounters.
- Treats Spotify as a useful starting point for non-specialist listeners.
