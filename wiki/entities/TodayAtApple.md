## Overview
[[TodayAtApple]] is Apple's free in-store education and events program, used by [[BenThompson]] as evidence that [[Apple]] wants customers to experience device ownership as an ongoing relationship with Apple rather than a one-time hardware transaction.

## Current Profile
The Stratechery source presents Today at Apple as more strategically revealing than the 2018 MacBook Air, Mac mini, and iPad Pro announcements around it. [[TimCook]] frames Apple retail stores as places that enrich customers' lives by educating and inspiring them, while Angela Ahrendts describes Today at Apple as curriculum around photography, music, gaming, app development, Everyone Can Code, and Everyone Can Create. The program runs at large scale, with Apple claiming more than 18,000 weekly sessions across 505 stores and millions of attendees. Thompson argues that the sessions are nominally free but practically tied to Apple device ownership and Apple ID participation, making them a relationship and engagement layer around the installed base.

## Key Characteristics
- Free in-store sessions around creative and technical use of Apple devices.
- Connected to Apple curricula such as Everyone Can Code and Everyone Can Create.
- Scaled globally through Apple's retail footprint rather than through a purely online platform.
- Uses stores as gathering places for local customers, educators, entrepreneurs, artists, musicians, photographers, and developers.
- Serves Apple's broader strategy by turning device ownership into continuing engagement with Apple.

## Evidence
- Program scope: [[apples-social-network-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] reports Apple's claim of more than 18,000 sessions per week across 505 retail locations.
- Curriculum: [[apples-social-network-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] says the program covers photography, music, gaming, app development, Everyone Can Code, and Everyone Can Create.
- Store role: [[apples-social-network-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] quotes Cook framing stores as places for education, inspiration, and creativity.
- Local gathering: [[apples-social-network-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] highlights Photo Walks and local creative talent as examples of real-world social interaction.
- Relationship strategy: [[apples-social-network-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] argues Today at Apple shows customers are buying into an ongoing relationship with Apple, not merely a device.

## Qualifications
The source is a 2018 strategy interpretation and uses Apple's own event presentation for scale claims. It does not independently measure attendance quality, customer retention, hardware sales effects, or whether Today at Apple produced durable community relationships.
