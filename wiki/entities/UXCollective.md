## Overview
[[UXCollective]] is a design publication context represented in this wiki by practitioner-facing UX and product-design articles.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, UX Collective appears through both a humor article about UX slogans and a long-form guide to becoming a senior product designer. The publication context matters because both sources assume practitioner familiarity with product-design work: one critiques professional design language through satire, while the other turns design ladders, rubrics, feedback, and career goals into a growth framework.
