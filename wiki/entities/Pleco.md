## Overview
[[Pleco]] is a Chinese dictionary and language-learning application founded by [[MikeLove]], represented here as a Palm-era product that later used free mobile distribution, paid content and feature add-ons, licensed differentiation, and cross-platform availability.

## Current Profile
Pleco launched for Palm in 2001 with licensed handwriting recognition and an exclusive edited dictionary. By its December 2009 iOS launch, competing dictionary apps already existed, but Pleco combined a strong free product with in-app purchases for dictionaries and specialist learning features. The account frames its enduring position as the result of business capabilities—publisher negotiation, pricing, customer knowledge, product investment, and platform coverage—working together with software development.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves the focused need of Chinese-language lookup and learning.
- Differentiated through licensed content and handwriting recognition as well as software features.
- Uses a capable free application as the entry point to modular paid add-ons.
- Sustains premium bundle pricing relative to typical low-priced mobile apps in the source.
- Maintains iOS and Android availability to support category-wide recommendations.

## Evidence
- Focused origin: [[pleco-building-a-business-not-an-app]] says Pleco began on Palm in 2001 for Chinese-language learners.
- Differentiated inputs: [[pleco-building-a-business-not-an-app]] identifies Motorola handwriting recognition, the Pocket Oxford Chinese dictionary, and later additional licensed dictionaries as key advantages.
- Modular monetization: [[pleco-building-a-business-not-an-app]] lists paid dictionaries, stroke-order diagrams, fonts, document reading, and OCR on top of a free base app.
- Premium pricing: [[pleco-building-a-business-not-an-app]] reports iOS bundles initially priced at $50, $100, and $150 and a later $40 feature-only basic bundle.
- Cross-platform position: [[pleco-building-a-business-not-an-app]] reports lower Android revenue but treats the version as a way to remain the default recommendation across phone platforms.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one 2014 strategy article. It does not include audited business results, user research, competitor testimony, licensing costs, platform fees, retention, or Pleco's subsequent product history. The reported product and pricing details should be read as a historical snapshot rather than current specifications.

## What Changed
- Established Pleco as a business-system case rather than only a dictionary application.
- Added the distinction between direct platform profitability and cross-platform brand coverage.
