## Overview
[[LouisePendry]] is identified in the source as an Exeter University psychologist studying the benefits of closed Facebook Groups.

## Current Profile
Pendry's quoted assessment separates relational and informational value. She says friendship and connection help retain people in [[Facebook]] Groups, while older-style forums remain easier for finding specific information because Facebook Group search is difficult to narrow.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies closed Facebook Groups.
- Recognizes friendship and connection as Facebook Group retention mechanisms.
- Identifies targeted information retrieval as an advantage of traditional forums.
