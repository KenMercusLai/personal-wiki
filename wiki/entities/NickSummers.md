## Overview
[[NickSummers]] is the Engadget reporter who documented and personally tested the [[DataDollarStore]] exchange in London in 2017.

## Current Profile
Summers's account combines observation with a small first-person privacy test. He initially considered surrendering broad phone access for a print, switched to the lower-cost mug exchange, and selected non-identifying photographs. That reaction supplies a concrete example of explicit disclosure changing behavior, not representative evidence about consumer valuation.

## Key Characteristics
- Technology journalist reporting on the Data Dollar Store campaign.
- Participant-observer who completed the least intrusive merchandise exchange.
- Explicitly limited disclosure by selecting photographs without identifiable people.

## Qualifications
The source captures one reporter's experience inside a publicity campaign. It does not show that his reaction represents other shoppers or ordinary digital-service decisions.
