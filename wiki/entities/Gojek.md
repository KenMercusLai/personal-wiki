## Overview
[[Gojek]] is presented by an employee-author as a startup that expanded from ride-sharing into food delivery, online ticket sales, and other services by pursuing adjacent forms of customer value.

## Current Profile
The source uses Gojek as its culminating example of [[ValueHacking]]. It says the company reached a valuation above US$3 billion within ten years while moving beyond ride-sharing into food, ticketing, and additional markets. The strategic point is that an existing customer network and operational base can support new use cases, but the passage is promotional and does not establish which expansion decisions caused growth or whether they created durable economic value.

## Key Characteristics
- Began from ride-sharing in the source's account.
- Expanded into food delivery, online ticket sales, and additional services.
- Is used as an example of pursuing adjacent customer value rather than optimizing one original use case.
- Had an employee relationship with the source author, making the account interested rather than independent.

## Evidence

## Qualifications
