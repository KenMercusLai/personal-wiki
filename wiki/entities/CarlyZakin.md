## Overview
[[CarlyZakin]] is a former NBC-family producer and co-founder of [[TheSkimm]], represented in the wiki through a 2015 joint interview about her news career, partnership with [[DanielleWeisberg]], early launch tactics, and product vision.

## Current Profile
Zakin describes an early interest in news and magazines followed by political science, film, and creative-writing study, publishing and NBC internships, and production roles across CNBC, MSNBC, and Peacock Productions. Her founder account stresses how little infrastructure the pair initially had, how they jointly created theSkimm's voice, and how they used personal outreach, repeated referral requests, and an unsolicited [[HodaKotb]] mention to gain early attention. She frames the longer-term aim as recreating the institutional utility of morning television for an audience organized around mobile routines rather than a fixed broadcast schedule.

## Key Characteristics
- Media founder with experience across financial news, documentaries, political writing, and television production.
- Co-creator of theSkimm's conversational editorial voice and email-first delivery.
- Early distribution operator who used broad direct outreach and explicit reader referral requests.
- Co-founder whose partnership followed years of friendship and shared living rather than an immediate startup match.
- Product thinker who defines the company around audience routines and morning-media utility rather than one channel.
