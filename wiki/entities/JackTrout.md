## Overview
[[JackTrout]] is cited by [[DariusForoux]] as a marketing author whose work with [[AlRies]] supports category-first differentiation.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Trout appears through Foroux's use of The 22 Immutable Laws of Marketing. His cited role is to make category ownership more important than claiming incremental superiority over competitors.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides marketing support for asking what category a new offer can be first in.
- Treats market perception and novelty as central to positioning.
- Appears jointly with [[AlRies]] in Foroux's argument for difference over betterment.

## Evidence
- Category question: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] cites Ries and Trout's advice to ask "First what?" rather than only asking how a product is better.
- Novelty over improvement: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] quotes Ries and Trout on interest in what is new.
- Strategy connection: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] uses Trout's cited work to connect [[DifferentiationStrategy]] and [[CategoryCreation]].
