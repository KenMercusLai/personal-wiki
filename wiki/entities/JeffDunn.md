## Overview

## Current Profile
Dunn introduces himself as having joined [[Juicero]] as CEO in November before writing the April 2017 note. He uses his career history at Coca-Cola, Bolthouse Farms, and Campbell Fresh to establish credibility in food, nutrition, and sustainability. His role in the source is both operational and communicative: he defends Juicero's connected system after criticism while offering refunds and inviting direct feedback.
