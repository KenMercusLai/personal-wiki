## Overview
[[Shazam]] is a music-recognition app used in the source as an example of offline visibility becoming word-of-mouth growth.

## Current Profile
The article says Shazam turned a product constraint into a growth cue: when background noise makes song recognition difficult, users hold phones toward speakers, making the app's use visible to nearby people. That visible behavior prompts questions and creates social discovery around the product.

## Key Characteristics
- Music-recognition app centered on identifying songs from ambient audio.
- Used as an example of product behavior creating offline curiosity.
- Reported in the source as having reached 500 million downloads.

## Evidence
- Offline visibility: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says users holding phones toward speakers helped others notice and ask about Shazam.
- Growth metric: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] reports 500 million downloads.

## Qualifications
The source infers that visible use contributed to downloads but does not compare it with app-store distribution, music partnerships, mobile adoption, or press.

- Created the entity page for Shazam as an offline word-of-mouth growth example.
