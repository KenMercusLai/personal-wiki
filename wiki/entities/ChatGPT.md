## Overview
[[ChatGPT]] is the AI assistant used in the source as one of the models for cross-checking facts in AI-assisted article drafts.

## Current Profile
In the source, ChatGPT is not the central writing partner but a verification support tool. [[FengRuohang]] sends drafts to ChatGPT alongside [[Gemini]] to look for factual issues, then treats agreement among models as provisional and personally checks important facts against original sources.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports cross-checking of AI-generated article drafts.
- Operates as a secondary verification aid rather than the owner of the article's question or argument.
- Requires human follow-up for critical facts.
- Helps illustrate the source's broader point that AI tools can support accountable writing without replacing author judgment.

## Evidence
- Cross-checking role: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] says drafts are sent to Gemini and ChatGPT for cross-verification.
- Provisional agreement: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] says agreement among several AI systems is treated as passing ordinary checks, while important facts still require original-source review.
- Human ownership: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] keeps topic choice, structure, revision, and responsibility with the author.

## Qualifications
This page reflects ChatGPT's role in one author's workflow. It does not describe current ChatGPT product capabilities, model versions, pricing, or official usage guidance.
