## Overview
[[ChatGPT]] appears in the wiki as an AI assistant used for writing workflows, including cross-checking facts, generating rough drafts, brainstorming questions and outlines, cleaning up spoken ideas, and revising tone.

## Current Profile
In Feng Ruohang's source, ChatGPT is not the central writing partner but a verification support tool. [[FengRuohang]] sends drafts to ChatGPT alongside [[Gemini]] to look for factual issues, then treats agreement among models as provisional and personally checks important facts against original sources. [[AndrewChen]] uses ChatGPT more directly as a writing companion: it can produce rough drafts, lists of questions, outlines, voice-cleaned text, and tone rewrites, but he treats those outputs as raw material because default prose can be stiff, generic, and short on examples or statistics.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports cross-checking of AI-generated article drafts.
- Supports blank-page reduction through rough first drafts that can be criticized and rewritten.
- Helps brainstorm questions, outlines, topics, and book-scale structures where partial usefulness is enough.
- Supports voice-to-text cleanup and end-stage tone rewriting.
- Requires human follow-up for critical facts.
- Helps illustrate the source's broader point that AI tools can support accountable writing without replacing author judgment.

## Evidence
- Cross-checking role: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] says drafts are sent to Gemini and ChatGPT for cross-verification.
- Provisional agreement: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] says agreement among several AI systems is treated as passing ordinary checks, while important facts still require original-source review.
- Human ownership: [[shi-de-wo-yong-ai-xie-wen-zhang-za-di]] keeps topic choice, structure, revision, and responsibility with the author.
- Drafting support: [[andrew-chen-how-i-use-ai-when-blogging-and-writing]] shows ChatGPT producing a Gen Z/video essay draft that Chen judges too stiff and under-evidenced but still useful as a starting point.
- Brainstorming support: [[andrew-chen-how-i-use-ai-when-blogging-and-writing]] shows ChatGPT producing VR-app questions that mix dull prompts with more interesting angles around esports, content creation, training, tourism, health, and real estate.
- Workflow integration: [[andrew-chen-how-i-use-ai-when-blogging-and-writing]] says Chen now writes with WordPress in one window and ChatGPT in another, copying, pasting, and spinning up new chats.

## Qualifications
This page reflects ChatGPT's role in source-scoped writing workflows. It does not describe current ChatGPT product capabilities, model versions, pricing, or official usage guidance. Chen's source is from the early generative-AI writing period and includes desired capabilities, such as fresher data and larger document context, that may not describe current product limits.
