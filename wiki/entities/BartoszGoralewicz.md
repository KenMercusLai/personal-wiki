## Overview
[[BartoszGoralewicz]] is an Elephate cofounder and technical SEO practitioner represented here through his October 2018 analysis of Google Flights' reported organic-visibility collapse.

## Current Profile
Goralewicz presents Google Flights as an unusually instructive case because Google operates both the product and the search system whose crawling and indexing requirements it allegedly mishandled. His diagnosis joins JavaScript rendering, redirect chains, trailing-slash URL competition, blocked scripts, index coverage, and internal links into one [[TechnicalSEO]] explanation. The source establishes his stated practitioner perspective and reasoning, not independent proof of Google's traffic, revenue loss, or root cause.
