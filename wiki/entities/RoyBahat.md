## Overview
[[RoyBahat]] is represented in the wiki as an investor-author who designs warm-introduction email workflows to preserve recipient choice and reduce coordination work for requesters and introducers.

## Current Profile
Bahat treats introductions as repeated relationship infrastructure rather than an informal favor with no process cost. His preferred workflow is opt-in: the requester supplies a concise, self-contained email in their own voice; Bahat adds his knowledge of the recipient and his judgment of the requester; the recipient decides whether to proceed; and only then is the requester added to the chain. The design assigns each participant the information they are best placed to supply while minimizing rewriting, thread cleanup, and follow-up.

## Key Characteristics
- Proponent of opt-in introductions that preserve the recipient's ability to decline before contact details and obligations are joined.
- Advocate for requester-written, forwardable context rather than ghostwritten messages or incomplete blurbs.
- Treats concise subject lines, fresh threads, and one message per recipient as operational requirements rather than cosmetic preferences.
- Frames coordination efficiency as consequential because small delays compound across repeated introductions.

## Evidence
- Opt-in structure: [[introductions-and-the-forward-intro-email-also-by-roy-bahat]] says Bahat first asks whether the recipient is willing, then adds the requester after a positive reply.
- Division of labor: [[introductions-and-the-forward-intro-email-also-by-roy-bahat]] assigns identity, organization, motivation, and ask to the requester while reserving recipient knowledge and endorsement for the introducer.
- Message design: [[introductions-and-the-forward-intro-email-also-by-roy-bahat]] recommends a concise fresh chain, a useful subject line, sufficient context, and one email per introduction.
- Scale rationale: [[introductions-and-the-forward-intro-email-also-by-roy-bahat]] says five to ten introductions a day can become thousands per year, making small process costs material.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to one 2016 practitioner essay, not a biography or comprehensive account of Bahat's investing and operating work. The essay gives one successful example but no comparative response, meeting, or relationship outcomes. Its workflow is most applicable when an introducer already knows both the recipient and enough about the requester to offer a credible judgment.
