## Overview
[[SketchK]], also identified by the source's original publication as Zhang Siqi, is the author and first-time iOS developer behind [[FOUR]].

## Current Profile
The source presents SketchK as a 27-year-old energy-technology graduate who left management-training and startup work to try software development without formal computing education. A Swift developer conference made the field feel unusually open to recognition through visible work, and he responded by designing, shipping, and open-sourcing FOUR in about three weeks. His account combines technical learning with competitor study, interaction design, typography, App Store release, and public collaboration, but it does not report later employment, adoption, or maintenance outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered iOS development from an energy-sector educational and work background.
- Used a small shipped app as a concrete learning and credibility artifact.
- Treated interface and typography decisions as part of software quality.
- Chose Objective-C pragmatically while planning a later Swift rewrite for comparison.
- Published the code to record growth and create opportunities for collaboration.

## Evidence
