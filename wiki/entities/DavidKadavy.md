## Overview
[[DavidKadavy]] is an author and bootstrapped solopreneur presented in this source as a practitioner testing the reliability and practical value of email experimentation.

## Current Profile
Kadavy describes using [[Mailchimp]] to run identical variants across 56 campaigns and more than 750,000 emails over eight months. He uses the false-looking winners from that exercise to challenge casual reliance on relative lifts and nominal significance, especially in businesses without the traffic, statistical expertise, or decision value needed to justify formal A/B tests. His position is resource-sensitive rather than an absolute rejection of measurement: test consequential questions when they can be powered and understood, use [[AATesting]] to calibrate intuition, and otherwise preserve attention for product vision and iterative shipping.

## Key Characteristics
- Bootstrapped solo operator evaluating experimentation under small-organization constraints.
- Uses a long-running first-person email exercise to demonstrate sampling noise.
- Emphasizes absolute business value and qualified prospects over isolated click or open lifts.
- Treats attention spent designing tests as an opportunity cost against creative and product work.
- Recommends calibration and statistical caution rather than automatic deference to experiment dashboards.

## Evidence
- Experiment scale: [[david-kadavy-a-a-testing-how-i-increased-conversions-300-percent-by-doing-absolutely-nothing]] reports 56 campaigns, more than 750,000 total sends, and eight months of identical-variant tests.
- Statistical caution: [[david-kadavy-a-a-testing-how-i-increased-conversions-300-percent-by-doing-absolutely-nothing]] discusses baseline rates, sample size, power, significance, false positives, and false negatives.
- Business judgment: [[david-kadavy-a-a-testing-how-i-increased-conversions-300-percent-by-doing-absolutely-nothing]] argues that clicks can differ in prospect quality and that test value must exceed the effort required.
- Operating context: [[david-kadavy-a-a-testing-how-i-increased-conversions-300-percent-by-doing-absolutely-nothing]] describes the business as a fully bootstrapped solopreneur operation with a six-figure year.

## Qualifications
The wiki's profile is bounded to one 2015 first-person essay. Its campaign counts and business claims are self-reported, the underlying results are not reproduced in full, and the source does not establish Kadavy's broader career, later views, or whether the same tradeoffs apply to organizations with larger samples and specialist experiment teams.
