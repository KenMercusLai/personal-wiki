## Overview
[[Firebase]] appears in this source as Google's developer platform into which [[Crashlytics]] was redesigned and rebuilt after the [[Fabric]] acquisition.

## Current Profile
The article frames Firebase less as a full platform history and more as the destination environment that forced Crashlytics to change. Because Firebase used [[MaterialDesign]] and had a different visual design system from Fabric, moving Crashlytics into Firebase required a visual update. The design team treated that required update as a chance to repair product-level user experience problems rather than simply porting existing features.

## Key Characteristics
- Owned by [[Google]] in the source's acquisition context.
- Became the target home for Crashlytics after the Fabric team joined Google in January 2017.
- Used [[MaterialDesign]], creating a visible mismatch with the older Fabric Crashlytics interface.
- Provided the product and interface context for the Crashlytics redesign.
- The inspected Firebase screenshots show clearer cards, filters, event trends, issue tables, and issue-detail areas than the older Fabric surface.

## Evidence
- Acquisition context: [[advocating-for-a-complete-product-redesign-google-design-medium]] says the Fabric team joined Firebase at Google in January 2017.
- Migration goal: [[advocating-for-a-complete-product-redesign-google-design-medium]] says one goal of the acquisition was to bring Crashlytics to Firebase.
- Design-system constraint: [[advocating-for-a-complete-product-redesign-google-design-medium]] says Firebase used Material Design and a very different visual design system.
- Redesign context: [[advocating-for-a-complete-product-redesign-google-design-medium]] includes inspected before/after screenshots that contrast Fabric Crashlytics with Firebase Crashlytics.

## Qualifications
The source does not describe Firebase's full product architecture, business model, or broader developer-tool suite. It uses Firebase mainly as the redesign destination and design-system constraint.
