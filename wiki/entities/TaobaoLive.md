## Overview
[[TaobaoLive]] is Alibaba's livestreaming commerce app in the source, where sellers broadcast product reviews, launches, and discounts.

## Current Profile
The source presents Taobao Live as the clearest ecommerce adaptation of China's livestreaming wave. Sellers use live video to describe merchandise, show products, promote limited-time offers, and sometimes receive virtual gifts, blending shopping utility with creator-style interaction.

## Key Characteristics
- Turns seller presentation into a live product-discovery surface.
- Supports reviews, launches, and limited-time discounts.
- Blends ecommerce with personality-driven livestreaming.
- Demonstrates that livestreaming can become a vertical rather than only a standalone entertainment app.
