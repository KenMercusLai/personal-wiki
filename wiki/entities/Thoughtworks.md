## Overview
[[Thoughtworks]] is the technology consultancy represented by sources on [[ContinuousDelivery]] architecture, compliance in DevOps cultures, technology-radar practice, and [[ExtremeProgramming]]-grounded agile delivery.

## Current Profile
In these sources, Thoughtworks appears as both a publisher of practitioner delivery guidance and a consultancy context for digital platform strategy. The continuous-delivery article originally came from Snap CI's The Pipeline blog and notes that Snap CI and Go.CD were both Thoughtworks products serving different delivery-pipeline contexts. Nygard's compliance article adds regulated delivery as another Thoughtworks-facing practice area: compliance controls should integrate with CI/CD without turning pipelines into centralized bottlenecks.

Thoughtworks also appears as the format reference for technology-radar practice. [[MYOB]] follows the Thoughtworks Technology Radar format to communicate which technologies teams should adopt, trial, or avoid.

Fowler's foreword adds a practice-history role: [[MartinFowler]] says he found a home at Thoughtworks, where teams used [[ExtremeProgramming]] techniques to help clients build new software products and displace legacy systems.

Caroli's PBB article adds an agile facilitation role. [[PauloCaroli]] is identified as a Thoughtworks Expert Inception Facilitator, and his [[ProductBacklogBuilding]] canvas turns personas, features, PBIs, [[UserStories]], acceptance criteria, and ready/done agreements into collaborative product planning.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes practitioner guidance on continuous delivery.
- Associated with hosted and on-premise CI/CD pipeline products in the source.
- Uses CD language that joins tooling with architecture, tests, and workflow.
- Publishes DevOps compliance guidance that links platform strategy, auditability, and team pipeline ownership.
- Provides the public Technology Radar format used as a model for internal organization-level technology guidance.
- Serves as Fowler's practice context for applying XP and agile technical practices to client software work.
- Hosts agile facilitation expertise around Product Backlog Building and collaborative user-story writing.
