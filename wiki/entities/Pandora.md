## Overview
[[Pandora]] is a music-discovery company founded by [[TimWestergren]] in 1999 as Savage Beast Technologies and represented in the source from its B2B origins through a public offering.

## Current Profile
The source portrays Pandora as a long, non-linear startup case rather than an overnight dot-com success. It began as pure B2B discovery technology, won a [[BestBuy]] kiosk test with a rough prototype, refused to expand from software into hardware, endured a prolonged period of scarce cash and unpaid work, and later recast its leadership by pairing Westergren in strategy with [[JoeKennedy]] as CEO. By the retrospective, the company had completed an IPO and employed more than 700 people.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as a B2B technology response to music abundance and weak discovery.
- Used a rough web-kiosk prototype to win a consumer-tested Best Buy project.
- Maintained a software focus while relying on IBM for hardware capability.
- Survived an early cash crisis with unusually severe employee and founder sacrifice.
- Shifted leadership roles as the company moved through financing and growth stages.
- Connected its product identity to the [[MusicGenomeProject]].
