## Overview
[[TypingMind]] is a bring-your-own-key interface for ChatGPT-compatible API use created by [[TonyDinh]].

## Current Profile
Dinh says he registered the domain one day after OpenAI announced the ChatGPT API, completed the first version over a weekend, and launched it on 6 March 2023. The product added chat organization and convenience features absent from the web interface at the time, moved from a $9 launch price toward $39, and reportedly generated $22,000 in first-week license revenue and about $30,000 per month on average by September 2023, alongside a business version.

## Key Characteristics
- Rapid response to a newly available platform API.
- Bring-your-own-API-key interface with no recurring usage fee in the launch positioning.
- Product grounded in the founder's own frustrations with the contemporary ChatGPT interface.
- Incremental pricing as features were added.
- Consumer product extended toward a business-customization offer.

## Evidence
- Timing and launch: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] dates the prototype and public release to the week after the ChatGPT API announcement.
- Product positioning: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] retains the launch post advertising history search, folders, integrations, a prompt library, and user-supplied API keys.
- Commercial response: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] reports daily first-week revenue and later monthly averages.

## Qualifications
All commercial figures are founder-reported and omit refunds, payment fees, API-related support, acquisition attribution, costs, retention, and revenue split between consumer and business offers. Rapid timing does not establish that speed alone caused the outcome; Dinh already had a large audience and broad implementation skill.

## What Changed
- Created the entity as a rapid platform-opportunity and audience-assisted launch case.
