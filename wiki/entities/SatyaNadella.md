## Overview
[[SatyaNadella]] is the [[Microsoft]] leader quoted by [[BenThompson]] as advocating company-owned human and token capital rather than surrendering institutional learning to a small number of frontier-model providers.

## Current Profile
In Thompson's account, Nadella proposes that companies build learning loops above replaceable generalist models. Human judgment sets goals and interprets outcomes; private evaluations measure performance against business-relevant results; private reinforcement environments learn from internal traces; and company knowledge preserves institutional memory. The strategic test is portability: a firm should be able to switch the underlying model without losing the accumulated expertise of its system.

Nadella also frames this as a political-economy boundary. If a few models absorb industry knowledge and economic returns, he argues, AI could repeat the dislocation associated with globalization. Thompson treats the warning as both a substantive sovereignty argument and an interested response from a software incumbent threatened by frontier labs moving directly into user workflows.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates joint accumulation of human judgment and organization-owned AI capability.
- Treats private evaluations, reinforcement environments, knowledge, and workflow traces as compounding firm-specific assets.
- Uses model switchability as the test that institutional learning remains under company control.
- Warns that concentration of AI value in a few models may hollow out existing industries and lose political legitimacy.
- Is presented through Thompson's strategy essay rather than through an independently evaluated implementation.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one Thompson essay quoting one Nadella social-media essay, not a complete account of Nadella's views or a measured Microsoft deployment. The source does not establish that firms can build portable learning loops, that private reinforcement environments outperform provider-integrated systems, or that model switching preserves behavior, cost, security, and accumulated knowledge in practice. Thompson also interprets Nadella's warning through Microsoft's competitive exposure, which is an incentive analysis rather than proof of motive.
