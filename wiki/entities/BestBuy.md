## Overview
[[BestBuy]] is a consumer-electronics retailer represented through a historical ecommerce product-page bundling case and an earlier in-store kiosk test used to evaluate music-discovery technology.

## Current Profile
One source presents Best Buy's “Our Experts Recommend” widget as a way to raise basket value while keeping the core product's advertised price competitive. The widget places a selectable package beneath the main product-page content, lets a shopper remove unwanted items, and offers one action to add the remaining set to the cart. Its examples span complementary electronics and media, multiple variants of the same toy, and a hair straightener paired with a same-value Sephora gift card.

The Pandora retrospective adds an earlier product-testing role. It says a replica store beneath Best Buy's Richfield headquarters was used for consumer tests before wider rollout and that a seven-person [[Pandora]] team won a kiosk project after hundreds of consumers responded well to its rough web prototype. That account comes from Pandora founder [[TimWestergren]], not from Best Buy records.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses product-page recommendations to assemble selectable multi-item offers.
- Matches bundle composition to complementary, repeat-purchase, or gift contexts.
- Reduces the navigation and cart work needed to buy several related items.
- Preserves the core item's listed price while attempting to increase total basket value.
- Used a replica store to test prospective customer experiences before network deployment, according to the Pandora account.

## Evidence
- Widget design: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] describes a selectable “Our Experts Recommend” module with a single add-multiple-items action.
- Complementary bundle: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] pairs Beats headphones with a speaker, portable charger, and iTunes gift card.
- Same-product bundle: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] offers four Fingerlings Baby Monkey variants together.
- Gift bundle: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] pairs a hair straightener with a same-value Sephora gift card.
- Basket arithmetic: [[ecommerce-product-bundling-strategy-hint-baby-monkey]] calculates potential increases of $190.98 and $44.97 for two displayed offers.
- Prototype evaluation: [[founder-of-pandora-on-lessons-from-near-dot-com-bust-to-billion-dollar-ipo-first-round-review]] says Best Buy tested a Pandora web kiosk with hundreds of consumers in a replica store beneath its headquarters.
- Vendor selection: [[founder-of-pandora-on-lessons-from-near-dot-com-bust-to-billion-dollar-ipo-first-round-review]] reports that the small Pandora team beat larger competitors for the kiosk business.

## Qualifications
Neither source is Best Buy documentation or an independent outcome study. The promotional Sumo article shows offer composition and arithmetic but provides no impressions, take rate, controlled comparison, realized AOV, gross margin, return behavior, customer satisfaction, or profit contribution. The Pandora article provides Westergren's retrospective account of the kiosk test but no contract, test protocol, deployment result, or Best Buy perspective. Claims that either tactic caused broader commercial success are unsupported by the supplied evidence.
