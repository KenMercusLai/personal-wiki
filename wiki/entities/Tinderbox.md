## Overview
[[Tinderbox]] is a macOS visual note and content-mapping application used in the source to analyze relationships among notes, ideas, and concepts.

## Current Profile
Chaperon uses Tinderbox as an optional visual layer alongside plain-text permanent notes. Its boxes are notes connected by labeled arrows rather than inert flowchart shapes, allowing a map to express components, directions, examples, conditions, and qualifications. The visual layer fits Chaperon's thinking style but is not presented as a required element of Zettelkasten.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents concepts as notes that can also appear as visual boxes.
- Supports directed and labeled relationships among notes.
- Adds visual analysis to a text-centered personal knowledge workflow.
- Can express conditions, examples, oppositions, and nested conceptual structure.
- Carries a learning-curve and cost tradeoff in the source's 2019 account.
