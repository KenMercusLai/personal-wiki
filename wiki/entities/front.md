Source: [9 Most Useful Pieces of Advice I've Received]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/mathilde-collin-nine-useful-pieces-of-advice.md" >}})

Front is the company context for Mathilde Collin's advice collection. The source says Front started as a collaborative email client and that Collin evaluates the advice through first-hand experience building it.

In the article, Front is used as the operating environment for several founder lessons: follow growth instead of outsourcing product direction to experts, protect early hiring quality because it compounds, stay close to irreversible decisions, repeat the mission in all-hands meetings, and onboard executives long enough to transfer founder instinct before giving them autonomy.
