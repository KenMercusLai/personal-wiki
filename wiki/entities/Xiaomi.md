## Overview
[[Xiaomi]] appears in the source as a Chinese hardware company using livestreaming for product demonstration and launch spectacle.

## Current Profile
The source presents Xiaomi as a brand experimenting with livestreaming's ability to make product attributes visible in real time. It used a long Mi Max battery stream as a proof-by-duration stunt and Xiaomi Live for a drone launch watched by a large audience.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses livestreaming as product demonstration rather than only advertising.
- Turns hardware claims into live events watched at scale.
- Shows how brands can adapt social livestreaming mechanics to launches.
- Connects China livestreaming to broader mobile-first product marketing.

## Evidence
- Battery demonstration: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says Xiaomi streamed a Mi Max until the battery gave out, lasting 19 hours.
- Audience scale: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says the Mi Max stream was watched by more than 39 million people at some point.
- Product launch: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says Lei Jun launched the Xiaomi Drone through Xiaomi Live to more than a million viewers.
