## Overview
[[EmilyReinhold]] is represented in the wiki as the author of a 2016 [[Uber]] Engineering account of building [[Tincup]] during the company's migration toward microservices.

## Current Profile
Reinhold's contribution is a concrete description of the organizational and technical platform surrounding one service: RFC review, separation of business logic from persistence, globally replicated storage, asynchronous I/O, routing and service discovery, typed interfaces, load testing, container isolation, and controlled disruption. The source presents these practices as the infrastructure that enabled service ownership rather than as independent tools.

## Key Characteristics
- Documents a first-party Uber microservice implementation.
- Connects service design review to duplicate-work prevention and collaboration.
- Treats production readiness as a combination of contracts, capacity tests, isolation, and resilience experiments.
