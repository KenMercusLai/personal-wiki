## Overview
[[HerbertLui]] is a writer represented here through a retrospective on building and adapting a roughly 800-card Zettelkasten over one year.

## Current Profile
Lui treats note-making as infrastructure for future writing rather than as a collectible method or software identity. His workflow began with handwritten 4×6 cards, numbered placement, and frequent review, then became hybrid when physical retrieval grew slow: he scanned about 300 cards, transcribed new paper notes, and kept a Notion database that could export Markdown. He emphasizes regular practice, gradual idea development, and personal adaptation over fidelity to a canonical Zettelkasten procedure.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses regular handwritten card creation to make capture sustainable and bounded.
- Develops book and article ideas through numbered card threads accumulated over months.
- Treats review and finding a card's placement as the central mechanism for rediscovery and connection.
- Moves from paper to a hybrid digital workflow when retrieval friction becomes material.
- Values exportability and personal fit more than loyalty to a specific tool or orthodox method.
