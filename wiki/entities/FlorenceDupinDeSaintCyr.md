## Overview
[[FlorenceDupinDeSaintCyr]] is an artificial-intelligence researcher represented in this wiki as coauthor of a formal account of joke comprehension based on belief change, defeasible reasoning, surprise, revelation, and norm violation.

## Current Profile
In the current source, Dupin de Saint-Cyr and [[HenriPrade]] extend their earlier belief-revision model of narrative jokes. Their contribution distinguishes a punchline that merely conflicts with expectation from one that also explains the setup, then adds a listener-relative account of incongruity as reasoning that exposes a violated strict or default norm.

## Key Characteristics
- Develops formal models of belief revision and nonmonotonic reasoning.
- Applies symbolic AI methods to the cognitive structure of joke comprehension.
- Treats funniness as listener-relative rather than an intrinsic property of a statement.

## Evidence
