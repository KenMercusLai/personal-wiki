## Overview
[[TwitterMoments]] is presented as a [[Twitter]] feature that curated tweets, images, and videos around trending topics.

## Current Profile
In Lieb's cognitive-overhead argument, Twitter Moments is a negative product-scope example. He says it could be enjoyable in isolation, but it made Twitter's purpose less obvious for first-time users because browsing or creating Moments felt unlike drafting a tweet or scrolling a feed. The entity is therefore useful in the wiki as a product-identity and feature-variety caution rather than as a complete history of Moments.

## Key Characteristics
- Curated trending-topic collections inside Twitter.
- Presented as different from Twitter's core tweet-and-feed interaction.
- Used as a cognitive-overhead example where a feature can blur what a product is for.
