## Overview
[[LoongsonCup]] is presented as a long-running student computing competition jointly sustained by Loongson personnel and university teachers to support computer-organization and CPU education.

## Current Profile
The source uses the competition's tenth anniversary as a positive example of ecosystem work that compounds through repeated operations rather than one-time sponsorship. Faculty initially contributed voluntarily, while Loongson's education unit later committed staff, prizes, experts, infrastructure, and attention to fairness and participant experience. The claimed benefits include student learning, recruitment, and an entry path into the industry, but they are reported through one former participant's retrospective rather than measured outcome data.

## Key Characteristics
- Connects student competition with computer-organization and CPU education.
- Had operated for ten years at the time described by the source.
- Relies on sustained participation from both university teachers and Loongson's education unit.
- Treats fair enforcement, dependable infrastructure, efficient administration, and participant learning as core operating work.
- Is presented as supporting both student development and company recruitment.

- Continuity and staffing: [[zhi-suan-qia-sheng-tai-jin-xiao-yuan-zhi-wo-jian-jie-ge-de-jiao-xue-yun-wei-bian-cheng-diao-ban-zi-xiao-bi-ji]] describes a decade of annual effort by teachers, company staff, and invited experts.
- Operating model: [[zhi-suan-qia-sheng-tai-jin-xiao-yuan-zhi-wo-jian-jie-ge-de-jiao-xue-yun-wei-bian-cheng-diao-ban-zi-xiao-bi-ji]] emphasizes fairness, anti-cheating enforcement, platform reliability, administration, prizes, and participant experience rather than sponsorship alone.
- Ecosystem role: [[zhi-suan-qia-sheng-tai-jin-xiao-yuan-zhi-wo-jian-jie-ge-de-jiao-xue-yun-wei-bian-cheng-diao-ban-zi-xiao-bi-ji]] presents the competition as a learning, recruitment, and industry-entry mechanism.

## Qualifications
The available account is favorable, first-person, and supplied without participation totals, completion or learning measures, fairness audits, recruiting conversion, budget, or comparisons with other competitions. It supports the importance of continuity and operations, not a causal claim that the competition produced Loongson ecosystem adoption.

## What Changed
- Added the competition as a concrete ten-year example of sustained education-ecosystem investment.
- Separated the operating requirements of competition quality from simple financial sponsorship.
