## Overview
[[NickyCase]] is presented in the wiki as a creator whose advice on explanation design informs the source author's tutorial-writing process.

## Current Profile
The sources use Nicky Case's explanation advice as a practical craft reference. The authors draw from Case to show why explanations should foreground what people can care about, move from concrete examples to abstractions, use pictures, examples, and analogies, and test drafts with real readers before publication. The creator-anxiety source emphasizes the testing side: reader feedback helps the author escape perfectionism by replacing imagined problems with concrete revision signals.

## Key Characteristics
- Offers explanation-design advice that the source author applies to tutorial writing.
- Encourages explaining why the topic matters rather than inventing artificial relevance.
- Supports a concrete-before-abstract sequence for unfamiliar ideas.
- Uses pictures, examples, and analogies as tools for making abstractions understandable.
- Recommends early real-world testing of explanations with actual listeners or readers.
- Inspires creators to treat articles as testable artifacts rather than private perfection projects.
