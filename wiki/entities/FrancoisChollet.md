## Overview
[[FrancoisChollet]] is represented in the wiki as the author of a 2018 practitioner checklist on software development, [[APIDesign]], and technical careers.

## Current Profile
The source presents Chollet as a principle-led software practitioner concerned with the total consequences of technical work. His guidance joins code readability and testing with product restraint, user empathy, domain-shaped interfaces, documentation, career agency, teamwork, and explicit ethical responsibility.

His preferred working style combines a bias toward simplicity with reversible experimentation. Teams should make recurring rules explicit, automate mechanical checks, try uncertain ideas in an environment that supports early reversal, and slow decisions when error would cost more than delay.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats code as communication and readability as a core engineering function.
- Frames API design as user-experience and mental-model design.
- Favors simplicity, product restraint, explicit processes, automation, and reversible iteration.
- Measures career progress through impact, teamwork, agency, and values rather than title or management span.
- Treats technical choices as ethical choices with real effects on access, incentives, benefit, and harm.
