## Overview
[[Bitfinex]] appears in the wiki as a cryptocurrency exchange example for order-book lending.

## Current Profile
The source uses Bitfinex as the transparent order-book lending contrast: lenders choose how much to lend and borrowers choose what rate they will accept. Taresky says the current Bitfinex rate in the article's moment was only around 10%, and even though the author had worked on an automated lending bot, the article did not recommend that opportunity at the time.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents a visible order-book model for crypto lending.
- Lets lending amount and rate be chosen by market participants.
- Is treated as less attractive in the article because the cited current rate was low relative to alternatives.

## Evidence
