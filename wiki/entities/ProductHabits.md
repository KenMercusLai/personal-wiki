## Overview
[[ProductHabits]] is a publication represented in the wiki by [[HitenShah]] essays about SaaS growth, retention, competition, and product execution.

## Current Profile
The sources present Product Habits as a venue for case-based product strategy. One essay argues that mature SaaS markets make retention and product strength more important than raw acquisition volume. A second uses Facebook's copying of Snapchat Stories and KISSmetrics' missed responses to Mixpanel and Heap to argue that teams should learn from competitors as well as customers and should not treat originality as more important than closing a real value gap.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes product and SaaS strategy articles.
- Frames SaaS growth through acquisition, retention, competition, and customer behavior.
- Uses company examples, historical metrics, and founder retrospectives to extract product lessons.
- Favors direct operational prescriptions while leaving some causal and welfare claims unmeasured.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently has two Product Habits sources, so the profile remains a narrow sample rather than a publication history. Both are persuasive practitioner essays; company metrics, retrospective counterfactuals, and claims about competitive welfare are not independently established.

## What Changed
- Added competition-aware execution and selective imitation as a second editorial theme.
- Qualified the case-driven prescriptions as practitioner arguments rather than comparative outcome evidence.
