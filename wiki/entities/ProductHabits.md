## Overview
[[ProductHabits]] is the product and SaaS publication context for the source "Acquisition is Easy. Retention is Hard."

## Current Profile
The source presents Product Habits as a venue for SaaS growth and product strategy commentary. Its role in the wiki is tied to a retention-focused argument: in mature SaaS markets, founders should treat product strength, customer value, and retention as the foundation for growth rather than chasing acquisition volume alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes product and SaaS strategy articles.
- Frames SaaS growth through acquisition, retention, competition, and customer behavior.
- Uses company examples and practitioner sources to extract product lessons.
