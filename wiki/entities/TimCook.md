## Overview
[[TimCook]] appears in the Stratechery sources as Apple's CEO, associated with the Services narrative, the insistence that Apple's active-device base is the right strategic measurement lens, and the retail framing behind [[TodayAtApple]].

## Current Profile
The Apple middle-age source presents Cook as managing Apple after the Steve Jobs rebirth period, when the premium smartphone market had matured and Apple's largest opportunities increasingly came from its existing installed base. Thompson cites Cook's earnings-call framing around Services and active devices to argue that Apple remained a vertical hardware company: Services mattered because they made Apple devices more valuable, and active devices mattered because Apple could sell users more hardware, services, and ecosystem-specific accessories. The Apple social-network source adds that Cook introduced Today at Apple as part of the retail mission to educate and inspire customers, but also quotes his refusal to release a user count because Apple preferred active devices as the more accurate measure.

## Key Characteristics
- CEO of Apple in the source's 2018 strategy analysis.
- Publicly emphasized Services growth as iPhone unit growth slowed.
- Preferred active devices over user counts as the measurement frame for Apple.
- Represents the mature-Apple operating context after Steve Jobs' return-and-growth era.
- Frames Apple retail stores as education and inspiration venues through Today at Apple.

## Evidence
- Services narrative: [[apples-middle-age-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] says Cook and [[LucaMaestri]] had been pushing the narrative that Apple was a services company since the 1Q 2016 earnings call.
- Hardware model: [[apples-middle-age-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] quotes Cook's 2016 premium-product answer as evidence that Apple remained focused on differentiated hardware.
- Active devices: [[apples-middle-age-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] cites Cook saying Apple would not release a user number because active devices were the more accurate and proper lens.
- Mature context: [[apples-middle-age-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] contrasts Cook's strategic questions with the different circumstances Steve Jobs faced.
- Retail mission: [[apples-social-network-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] quotes Cook introducing Today at Apple as part of Apple's effort to educate and inspire users in stores.
- Metrics dispute: [[apples-social-network-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] argues Cook's active-device preference was less useful to investors than active-customer and revenue-per-user metrics.
