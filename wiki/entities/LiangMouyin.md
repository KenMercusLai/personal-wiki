## Overview
[[LiangMouyin]] is the author of "为什么你不该沉迷知识管理," a short essay criticizing excessive fascination with note methods and personal knowledge-management tools.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Liang Mouyin contributes a restraint-oriented view of knowledge management. The article treats tool comparison, method imitation, and knowledge-management content consumption as potential distractions unless they help the author produce concrete work through a personally fitting workflow.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from personal experience with knowledge-management tool overload.
- Emphasizes simplifying methods and software around actual personal needs.
- Treats public writing output as a stronger test of PKM than the elegance of a note system.

## Evidence
- Tool overload critique: [[liang-mouyin-wei-shen-me-ni-bu-gai-chen-mi-zhi-shi-guan-li]] describes being overwhelmed by constant discussion of apps and note methods.
- Simplified workflow: [[liang-mouyin-wei-shen-me-ni-bu-gai-chen-mi-zhi-shi-guan-li]] names INKP, [[Obsidian]], flomo, and WeChat as a smaller tool stack.
- Output test: [[liang-mouyin-wei-shen-me-ni-bu-gai-chen-mi-zhi-shi-guan-li]] argues that output reveals whether knowledge management is actually useful.

## Qualifications
The source is a reflective essay rather than a general empirical study. It gives a personal corrective to PKM tool-chasing, but it does not test when more elaborate workflows may be justified by larger archives, collaboration, research demands, or professional constraints.
