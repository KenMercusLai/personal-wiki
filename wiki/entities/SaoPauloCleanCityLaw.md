## Overview
[[SaoPauloCleanCityLaw]] is the outdoor-advertising ban [[DavidHumphrey]] uses as a physical-world analogy for software-enabled ad blocking.

## Current Profile
In the source, Sao Paulo's Clean City Law mandated the removal of billboards and outdoor advertising. Humphrey compares that legal code to Apple's iOS 9 Safari content-filtering APIs: both change the rules of a public information space and make ad-free experience possible, forcing a broader conversation about how commerce can be funded without pervasive advertising.

## Key Characteristics
- Removed outdoor billboards and advertisements from a major metropolis.
- Provides the article's main non-digital comparison for ad-free public space.
- Shows that changing the rules of an environment can disrupt advertisers while leaving commerce and civic life to reorganize.
