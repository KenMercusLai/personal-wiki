## Overview
[[NathanBashaw]] is the founder-author of the Hardbound update, reflecting on leaving [[GeneralAssembly]] to build mobile-first interactive books and later confronting [[Hardbound]]'s failed fundraising period.

## Current Profile
Bashaw appears in this source as a mission-driven founder who believed mobile storytelling could become a deeper way to read and learn. After two years of building Hardbound, he reports meaningful traction, a team he loved working with, investor interest that did not convert into a round, and a painful responsibility to help teammates find other work. His post emphasizes learning from failure without turning it into self-hatred.

## Key Characteristics
- Built Hardbound from a belief that mobile visual storytelling could reshape nonfiction reading.
- Communicates startup failure with unusually direct operational detail about fundraising, revenue, spend, and team consequences.
- Treats founder responsibility as compatible with self-compassion and curiosity.
- Preserves a broader mission around technology, storytelling, perspective, empathy, and knowledge after the immediate business model falters.

## Evidence
- Origin and mission: [[a-terrible-horrible-no-good-very-bad-hardbound-update]] says Bashaw left General Assembly to pursue visual, interactive mobile books.
- Operational candor: [[a-terrible-horrible-no-good-very-bad-hardbound-update]] reports 72 investor conversations, about $2,500 monthly revenue, and about $13,000 monthly spend.
- Responsibility frame: [[a-terrible-horrible-no-good-very-bad-hardbound-update]] distinguishes "couldn't" from "didn't" as a way to stay in learning mode.
- Continued purpose: [[a-terrible-horrible-no-good-very-bad-hardbound-update]] states the mission as using technology and storytelling to widen perspective, deepen empathy, and sharpen knowledge.
