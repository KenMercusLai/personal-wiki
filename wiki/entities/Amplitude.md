## Overview
[[Amplitude]] is described in the source as a product-analytics company helping teams understand and improve digital products, and it is the employer context for author [[JustinBauer]].

## Current Profile
In the available 2018 essay, Amplitude appears primarily as the perspective behind a product-team doctrine: teams should be able to inspect user behavior, connect product work with engagement and retention, and use evidence for learning rather than post-hoc validation. The source does not evaluate Amplitude's product, customers, market position, or effectiveness independently.

## Key Characteristics
- Presented as a product-analytics company serving product teams from startups through large enterprises.
- Associated with self-service access to behavioral data and investigation of customer journeys.
- Supplies the commercial and organizational context for Bauer's emphasis on analytics-informed product work.

## Evidence
- Company description: [[good-product-teams-vs-bad-product-teams]] describes Amplitude as helping teams build products that work better for users and grow their businesses.
- Operating context: [[good-product-teams-vs-bad-product-teams]] says Bauer encountered hundreds of product teams annually through his vice-president role at the company.
- Analytics position: [[good-product-teams-vs-bad-product-teams]] recommends product analytics for understanding actual use, customer journeys, engagement, stickiness, retention, and impact.

## Qualifications
The evidence is a company-adjacent practitioner article, not an independent profile or product assessment. Its scale claims are author-reported, its product-analytics benefits are not comparatively measured, and the source predates later company, product, and market developments.
