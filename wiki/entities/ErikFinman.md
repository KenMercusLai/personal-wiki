## Overview
[[ErikFinman]] is presented in a 2017 CNBC profile as a teenage Bitcoin investor and founder of the online tutoring company [[Botangle]] who chose entrepreneurial and internet-based learning over high school completion and college.

## Current Profile
Finman's reported financial path joined unusually early cryptocurrency exposure with entrepreneurship: he invested a $1,000 gift in [[Bitcoin]] at age 12, sold his initial holdings for about $100,000, used the proceeds to start Botangle, and later sold its technology for 300 bitcoin rather than $100,000 cash. CNBC reported that his 403 bitcoin were worth about $1.09 million in June 2017. He treated this outcome as evidence for learning through business execution and free online resources, but the profile is an exceptional success story rather than comparative evidence about education or investment strategy.

## Key Characteristics
- Entered Bitcoin in 2011 at age 12 with a $1,000 gift and guidance from his brother Scott.
- Converted early investment gains into operating capital for an education startup.
- Chose 300 bitcoin over a larger nominal cash offer when selling Botangle's technology in 2015.
- Managed his own and his family's Bitcoin investments after the sale.
- Preferred practical business experience and autonomous online learning to formal credentials.
- Articulated a strongly optimistic view of Bitcoin and peer-to-peer blockchain services.

## Evidence
- Investment path: [[cnbc-erik-finman-bitcoin-millionaire-who-skipped-college]] reports the $1,000 initial investment, late-2013 sale, 300-bitcoin Botangle transaction, and 403-bitcoin holding.
- Entrepreneurial execution: [[cnbc-erik-finman-bitcoin-millionaire-who-skipped-college]] says Finman founded Botangle in 2014 and later found a buyer for its technology.
- Learning position: [[cnbc-erik-finman-bitcoin-millionaire-who-skipped-college]] quotes Finman rejecting a GED and college while favoring business experience, YouTube, and Wikipedia.
- Technology thesis: [[cnbc-erik-finman-bitcoin-millionaire-who-skipped-college]] records his belief that blockchain systems could remove intermediaries and central hubs.

## Qualifications
The profile captures Finman's self-description and asset values at one point in June 2017. It does not independently audit his holdings, compare his educational or business outcomes with alternatives, or show that his highly concentrated cryptocurrency strategy is broadly repeatable; survivorship, family support, timing, and extreme Bitcoin volatility materially limit generalization.
