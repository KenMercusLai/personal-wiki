## Overview
[[VoteForBernie]] is the unofficial voter-information website built by [[JonHughes]] for the 2016 [[BernieSanders]] campaign ecosystem.

## Current Profile
The site turned a confusing civic task into a simple location-based lookup: supporters could find when and where to vote in their Democratic primary or caucus. POLITICO frames it as a flagship example of [[VolunteerCampaignTechnology]] because it reached millions of visitors and later informed the campaign's official voter tool.

## Key Characteristics
- Gives primary and caucus participation guidance based on user location.
- Operated outside the official campaign while still supporting Sanders' electoral goals.
- Became a high-search-visibility entry point for people asking how to vote in the primaries.
- Influenced the official campaign's later voting-information tool.

## Evidence
- User need: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] describes the site as a way to identify where and when to vote.
- Reach: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] reports more than 2 million unique views.
- Unofficial origin: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] says Hughes had no campaign connection when he built it.
- Official reuse: [[2016-bernies-army-of-coders-politico-magazine]] says a later campaign tool was based on Hughes' primary finder.

## Qualifications
The article reports audience scale and campaign influence as of February 2016. Later traffic, accuracy, maintenance, and election effects are outside this source.
