## Overview
[[ThomasStanley]] is represented in the wiki as the researcher Hardy cites for an association among incentive-based compensation, business ownership, courage, and affluence.

## Current Profile
The source opens with a Stanley quotation claiming that courage develops under risk and that many affluent Americans are business owners or incentive-paid employees. Hardy uses this attribution to support a broad prescription for performance-based work. The saved article does not identify the underlying study, definitions, sample, controls, or causal design, so the claim cannot be independently evaluated from this source alone.
