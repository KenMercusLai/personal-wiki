## Overview
[[EmpireFlippers]] is an online-business brokerage represented in the source through its historical valuation model, seller-preparation advice, marketplace claims, and transaction support.

## Current Profile
The source presents Empire Flippers as a broker for content sites, SaaS products, ecommerce stores, and other profitable digital assets. Its stated valuation method starts with rolling 12-month average net profit and applies a monthly multiple shaped by earnings direction, operating history, failure concentration, buyer workload, and defensibility. The company also positions brokerage as preparation, buyer filtering, negotiation, migration, and earn-out support, although those benefits and its claim that brokered sellers often net more are not independently tested in the source.

## Key Characteristics
- Brokers acquisitions of profitable online businesses and digital assets.
- Uses a monthly-profit multiple rather than only an annual EBITDA multiple in the described framework.
- Evaluates seller earnings alongside growth, risk concentration, transferability, and defensibility.
- Offers a valuation tool and describes support across preparation, buyer qualification, negotiation, migration, and earn-outs.
- Has a direct commercial interest in encouraging owners to value and sell businesses through a broker.

## Qualifications
This profile relies on a company representative's 2018 marketing-oriented article and replies. It does not audit completed transactions, seller proceeds, commissions, time to sale, failed listings, buyer returns, valuation-tool accuracy, or the claim that brokered sellers usually outperform private sellers after fees. The reported ranges and fee tiers are historical snapshots and should not be treated as current terms.
