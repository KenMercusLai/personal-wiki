## Overview
[[JohnLilly]] is presented as a CS183C instructor whose scaling lessons concern stage transitions, support functions, competition timing, and repeated communication.

## Current Profile
The source uses Lilly to explain the move from OS1 to OS2 and from informal to explicit communication. He distinguishes product-building teams from the support functions that defend and enable them, says competition matters more once the opportunity becomes visible, and recommends a small set of repeated CEO messages as companies grow.

## Key Characteristics
- Differentiates product-building work from support and defense functions.
- Treats competition as more salient once the market opportunity becomes less contrarian.
- Emphasizes repeated simple messages for organizational alignment.
- Uses Mozilla experience to explain communication at larger scale.
