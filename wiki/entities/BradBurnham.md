## Overview
[[BradBurnham]] is a [[UnionSquareVentures]] partner represented through the early investment reasoning that preceded the firm's 2005 investment in [[Indeed]].

## Current Profile
While raising USV's first fund with [[FredWilson]] in 2003 and 2004, Burnham proposed that online job listings could adopt a search-like model: employers would publish jobs on their own sites and buy relevant traffic rather than pay a centralized board to host each listing. The partners discussed the model and decided in advance that they would act if a matching company appeared. Wilson later recognized Indeed through a John Battelle post, and USV pursued the self-funded founders until it invested in fall 2005. The source attributes the originating model and an interest in business-model disruption to Burnham, but gives no broader biography, decision record, or independent account from him.

## Key Characteristics
- Explored application-layer internet opportunities with Wilson while raising USV's first fund.
- Framed job search as a business-model shift from paid listings to employer-hosted jobs and paid traffic.
- Used advance discussion to turn a broad investment thesis into a recognizable company pattern.
- Participated in USV's sustained pursuit of Indeed after the founders initially declined outside capital.

## Evidence
- Prior model: [[knowing-what-you-are-looking-for-avc]] attributes the search-style job-listing idea to Burnham during 2003-2004 fundraising travel.
- Prepared commitment: [[knowing-what-you-are-looking-for-avc]] says Burnham and Wilson decided they would pursue a company matching the model before discovering Indeed.
- Investment pursuit: [[knowing-what-you-are-looking-for-avc]] says the firm persisted after Indeed's self-funded founders were initially unsure about accepting its investment.
