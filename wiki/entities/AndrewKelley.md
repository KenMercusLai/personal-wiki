## Overview
[[AndrewKelley]] is presented as the leader of the Zig project and a representative of the [[ZigSoftwareFoundation]] commenting on Bun's departure from Zig.

## Current Profile
Kelley offers an insider's retrospective on the relationship among [[JarredSumner]], [[Bun]], and the Zig community. His technical position is that the rewrite debate misattributes organizational and engineering-process failures to language choice: testing, fuzzing, review, defect removal, compile configuration, and disciplined control of technical debt require sustained work regardless of implementation language. His social account is more adversarial, combining direct project interactions, community reports, resentment, and allegations from unnamed former job seekers or workers.

## Key Characteristics
- Leads the Zig project in the source's account.
- Speaks from direct technical and institutional contact with Bun.
- Prioritizes long-term language-project goals over Bun's requested tooling priorities.
- Argues that engineering resources and practices are more causally important than a Zig-versus-Rust dichotomy.
- Openly acknowledges resentment while welcoming Bun's migration away from Zig.
