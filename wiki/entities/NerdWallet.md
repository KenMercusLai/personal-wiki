## Overview
[[NerdWallet]] is the company context for a source about revising an engineering job architecture as the engineering organization grew and its earlier career rubric became less consistently interpreted.

## Current Profile
In this wiki, NerdWallet appears as an engineering organization using biannual reviews and a standard job architecture to evaluate engineers on a common playing field. The source says the architecture had been last updated more than two years earlier, when the team was about 25 engineers, and was being revised after the engineering organization grew to roughly 100 people.

The profile is specifically about engineering career architecture, not NerdWallet's product, market, or financial history. The company uses Product and Platform as broad engineering categories, and the article shows its concern that promotion criteria should not structurally privilege platform engineers over product engineers.

## Key Characteristics
- Maintains biannual engineering review cycles with a standard job architecture.
- Reworked an older engineering career rubric after scaling from about 25 to roughly 100 engineers.
- Frames engineering growth around values, expectations, rationale, and examples rather than a checklist of promotion tasks.
- Uses six individual-contributor engineering levels from Software Engineer I through Principal Engineer.
- Distinguishes Product and Platform engineering contexts while trying to evaluate both fairly under one architecture.
- Treats career architecture rollout as an organizational change requiring manager feedback, broad explanation, and future iteration.
