## Overview
[[PerHaraldBorgen]] is the author and founder presented in the source as a co-creator of [[BugRex]] and a co-founder of Scrimba.

## Current Profile
Borgen argues that early product teams should count validated assumptions rather than lines of code. In the BugRex account, he and Andreas Hennie operationalized that position by connecting hosted services for the complete customer-and-expert workflow and accepting compromises that left nonessential business questions for later.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames product progress in terms of assumptions tested.
- Uses existing services to shorten the path to external evidence.
- Treats no-code prototyping as a conditional route rather than a universal formula.
- Accepts temporary branding and workflow compromises when they preserve the intended test.
- Is identified in the source as a Scrimba co-founder.

## Evidence
- Product philosophy: [[building-products-without-coding-learning-new-stuff-medium]] argues that code can slow validation when existing tools can test the idea.
- Prototype practice: [[building-products-without-coding-learning-new-stuff-medium]] describes Borgen and Hennie combining five hosted services into BugRex.
- Qualification: [[building-products-without-coding-learning-new-stuff-medium]] explicitly rejects treating the approach as one-size-fits-all.
- Founder identity: [[building-products-without-coding-learning-new-stuff-medium]] closes by identifying Borgen as a Scrimba co-founder.
