## Overview
[[PerHaraldBorgen]] is an author and product builder represented through two 2016 accounts: a service-composed [[BugRex]] prototype and a [[Xeneta]] machine-learning experiment for sales-lead triage. He is also identified in the BugRex source as a co-founder of Scrimba.

## Current Profile
Borgen's accounts share a pragmatic, test-first posture. For BugRex, he and Andreas Hennie connected hosted services to test demand and payment behavior before building custom software. At Xeneta, he assembled a fallible data-acquisition and NLP pipeline to test whether company descriptions could help sales representatives sort prospects, reporting 86.4% test accuracy while acknowledging likely dataset bias and the need for better data and models.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames product progress in terms of assumptions tested.
- Uses existing services to shorten the path to external evidence.
- Treats no-code prototyping as a conditional route rather than a universal formula.
- Accepts temporary branding and workflow compromises when they preserve the intended test.
- Treats data acquisition and labeling as substantial parts of an applied machine-learning system.
- Positions predictive scoring as rough prioritization before human review.
- Is identified in the source as a Scrimba co-founder.

## Qualifications
The profile is limited to two first-person 2016 practitioner articles. They do not establish Borgen's broader career, the later outcomes of BugRex or Scrimba, or the production impact of Xeneta's classifier. The reported lead-model accuracy comes from one small balanced dataset and does not establish error costs, generalization, or sales results.
