## Overview
[[ZebrasUnite]] is the founder-and-funder movement represented by the 2017 essay "Zebras Fix What Unicorns Break," which organizes around durable companies that pursue profit and social benefit together.

## Current Profile
In the ingested source, Jennifer Brandel, Mara Zepeda, Astrid Scholz, and Aniyia Williams develop the zebra metaphor and call for a coordinated alternative to unicorn-oriented startup finance. The initiative aims to connect founders, investors, philanthropists, institutions, and advocates; pool practices and resources; and build capital and community infrastructure for [[ZebraCompanies]]. The essay announces DazzleCon as a gathering intended to turn a loose network into a more cohesive movement.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-led movement built around a profit-and-purpose company model.
- Critiques rapid-exit, shareholder-first, and growth-at-all-costs startup incentives.
- Treats peer cooperation and shared infrastructure as sources of company resilience.
- Brings founders, investors, philanthropists, corporations, and advocates into one ecosystem-building effort.
- Uses writing, convening, and shared practice to make alternative company models visible.

## Evidence
- Model definition: [[zebras-fix-what-unicorns-break-jennifer-mara-astrid-aniyia-medium]] introduces zebras as real, profitable, socially beneficial, mutualistic, durable, and capital-efficient companies.
- Incentive critique: [[zebras-fix-what-unicorns-break-jennifer-mara-astrid-aniyia-medium]] contrasts sustainable growth and shared prosperity with quick exits, consumption, and shareholder primacy.
- Mutual support: [[zebras-fix-what-unicorns-break-jennifer-mara-astrid-aniyia-medium]] argues that companies banding together can protect one another and create stronger collective output.
- Coalition design: [[zebras-fix-what-unicorns-break-jennifer-mara-astrid-aniyia-medium]] invites founders, investors, foundations, corporations, thinkers, and advocates to participate.
- Convening: [[zebras-fix-what-unicorns-break-jennifer-mara-astrid-aniyia-medium]] presents DazzleCon as a place to pool resources, ideas, patterns, and practices.

## Qualifications
The page currently represents Zebras Unite through its founding-era manifesto, not through an independent history or outcome evaluation. The essay documents ambitions and examples but does not establish the movement's later organizational form, scale, financing record, company survival outcomes, or measured social impact. Its critiques and proposed alternatives should be read in their 2017 context.

## What Changed
- Created the entity from the movement-building call in "Zebras Fix What Unicorns Break."
- Captured its coalition, convening, and shared-infrastructure role alongside its company-model advocacy.
