## Overview
[[DevinLeonard]] is the Bloomberg Businessweek journalist who reported the 2016 feature "Will Amazon Kill FedEx?"

## Current Profile
Leonard examines Amazon's logistics expansion through infrastructure announcements, carrier economics, interviews with Amazon and industry participants, and Wilmington, Ohio's experience losing and potentially regaining airfreight employment. His account distinguishes Amazon's immediate need for supplemental capacity from the longer-term possibility of competing with established parcel carriers or selling logistics services to other businesses.

## Key Characteristics
- Reports on corporate strategy through operational detail and local economic effects.
- Separates observed infrastructure buildout from longer-horizon analyst forecasts.
- Preserves disputes between Amazon, carriers, and outside experts rather than presenting one account as settled.

## Evidence
- Reporting scope: [[will-amazon-kill-fedex]] traces Amazon's aircraft, trailers, sorting facilities, local delivery, postal partnerships, and freight-forwarding authority.
- Local consequences: [[will-amazon-kill-fedex]] uses Wilmington's DHL closure and Amazon airfreight trial to show how logistics-network decisions affect a small city.
- Competing interpretations: [[will-amazon-kill-fedex]] contrasts Amazon's supplemental-capacity explanation, carrier skepticism, and forecasts of a future external logistics business.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently contains one Leonard article, a 2016 snapshot written while Amazon's air network was emerging. It does not establish his broader body of work or evaluate the article's predictions with hindsight.
