## Overview
[[KISSmetrics]] is a product-analytics company presented through founder [[HitenShah]]'s retrospective as an early innovator that later lost ground by slowing execution and discounting competitor signals.

## Current Profile
The source says KISSmetrics built a rough one-month MVP around three researched problems: slow and difficult funnel reporting, slow instrumentation debugging, and the absence of identity-linked behavioral history. Early users praised its fast funnels, real-time debugging, API, and people-centered event timeline. Shah's retrospective then turns KISSmetrics into a cautionary case: continued customer research did not compensate for ignoring changes in pricing, platform focus, automatic capture, and sales presentation introduced by [[Mixpanel]] and [[Heap]].

## Key Characteristics
- Built product analytics around fast funnel creation, implementation debugging, and identity-linked event histories.
- Used an intentionally rough one-month MVP to test pain points found through review analysis, surveys, and interviews.
- Earned early user praise for funnels, real-time event visibility, its API, and person-level timelines.
- Later remained web-focused while competitors responded to mobile analytics demand.
- Discounted competitor pricing and feature signals that the founder later judged strategically important.

## Evidence
- Early problem selection: [[facebooks-shameless-copying-of-snapchat-and-what-it-means-for-your-product-strategy-product-habits]] identifies three customer-research findings that shaped the MVP.
- Initial execution: [[facebooks-shameless-copying-of-snapchat-and-what-it-means-for-your-product-strategy-product-habits]] says the first version was built in one month despite slow, buggy, and unattractive implementation.
- User response: five retained testimonial screenshots in [[facebooks-shameless-copying-of-snapchat-and-what-it-means-for-your-product-strategy-product-habits]] praise funnels, debugging, the API, and person-level data.
- Competitive misses: [[facebooks-shameless-copying-of-snapchat-and-what-it-means-for-your-product-strategy-product-habits]] identifies Mixpanel's free plan, mobile focus, and demo appeal plus Heap's automatic capture as signals KISSmetrics did not answer.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on a founder's retrospective argument, not comparative product telemetry, customer churn data, financial records, or interviews with competitors and former customers. It does not establish that copying any one Mixpanel or Heap feature would have changed KISSmetrics' outcome, nor does it evaluate the cost, technical feasibility, strategic fit, or later histories of the companies.
