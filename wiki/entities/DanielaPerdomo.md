## Overview
[[DanielaPerdomo]] is the volunteer technologist identified by POLITICO as the person who spearheaded [[FeelTheBern]].

## Current Profile
The article uses Perdomo to show the intensity and perceived efficiency of Sanders' volunteer tech labor. Her unpaid work on FeelTheBern is contrasted with expensive professional campaign and super PAC websites, making her a representative of the campaign's outsider, contributor-driven software model.

## Key Characteristics
- Led a major volunteer-built campaign information project.
- Worked outside the formal paid campaign structure.
- Framed volunteer web production as capable of outperforming expensive professional political technology.
- Connected technical labor with persuasion and field organizing through [[FeelTheBern]].
