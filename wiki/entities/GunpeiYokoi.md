## Overview
[[GunpeiYokoi]] is the [[Nintendo]] inventor whom the source associates with the [[GameBoy]] and the philosophy of [[LateralThinkingWithWitheredTechnology]].

## Current Profile
The article presents Yokoi primarily as a product thinker who sought new consumer value by recombining mature, inexpensive, well-understood technology. In the Game Boy case, this meant accepting a less advanced display and other specification limits in exchange for price, battery practicality, portability, and a viable mass-market product. His role is used to explain the design logic, not to supply a full biography or sole-inventor history.

## Key Characteristics
- Nintendo inventor associated with the original Game Boy.
- Formulated or popularized “Lateral Thinking with Withered Technology.”
- Prioritized novel combinations of existing technology over raw technical novelty.
- Treated consumer accessibility and practical use as central engineering outcomes.

## Evidence
- Product philosophy: [[game-boys-have-infinite-lives-at-30-they-live-on-in-unexpected-ways-los-angeles-times]] describes Yokoi's approach as creating something new for consumers from existing technology.
- Game Boy application: [[game-boys-have-infinite-lives-at-30-they-live-on-in-unexpected-ways-los-angeles-times]] connects that philosophy to a handheld that was less sophisticated than rivals but cheaper and easier to carry and power.
- Claimed continuity: [[game-boys-have-infinite-lives-at-30-they-live-on-in-unexpected-ways-los-angeles-times]] says the philosophy remained visible in Nintendo's less powerful but differently positioned Switch.

## Qualifications
The source offers only a brief retrospective description of Yokoi and does not document his complete career, collaborators, decision authority, original wording, or the development process behind the Game Boy. The philosophy should not be treated as a complete causal explanation for the console's success.
