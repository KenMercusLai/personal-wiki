## Overview
[[EmilyWeiss]] is the founder of [[Glossier]], represented here through [[DavidPerell]]'s 2017 account of audience-first, personality-led beauty brand building.

## Current Profile
Perell portrays Weiss as building an audience through Into the Gloss before introducing Glossier, then blending company communication with informal personal posts and audience participation. In the essay, this creates intimacy and lets followers feel close to both founder and product development, but no evidence isolates the effect of transparency from product, distribution, aesthetics, capital, or celebrity.

## Key Characteristics
- Built a beauty-oriented audience before launching the product company used in the case.
- Combined founder lifestyle communication with company and product storytelling.
- Invited audience input into product ideas, making participation part of the brand narrative.

## Evidence
- Audience before product: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] says Weiss began Into the Gloss in 2010 and developed a community before hinting at Glossier's first product.
- Personal communication: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] describes informal Instagram posts alongside entrepreneurship and company updates.
- Product participation: [[naked-brands-adventures-in-consumer-technology-medium]] says Glossier crowdsourced new product ideas from commenters and fans.

## Qualifications
The profile rests on one admiring 2017 essay. It supplies no audience cohort data, product-development records, conversion analysis, retention results, or comparison with less founder-visible beauty brands.
