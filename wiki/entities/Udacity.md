## Overview
[[Udacity]] is an online-course platform discussed in the source through its 2013 partnership with [[SanJoseStateUniversity]].

## Current Profile
In the article, Udacity represents the higher-education version of early MOOC analytics. Its platform captured clickstream data, but the cited preliminary report summarized learner effort through measures such as completed problems and video-watching minutes, finding that effort measures were stronger predictors of course passing than other examined variables.

## Key Characteristics
- Supplies real-time learner clickstream data for online higher-education courses.
- Appears in the article as an example of effort measures overshadowing other predictors.
- Connects platform analytics with remedial and introductory university-course delivery.

## Evidence
- Partnership case: [[big-data-mooc-research-breakthrough-learning-activities-lead-to-achievement-edtech-researcher-education-week]] discusses Udacity's summer 2013 San Jose State University courses.
- Effort variables: [[big-data-mooc-research-breakthrough-learning-activities-lead-to-achievement-edtech-researcher-education-week]] says the analysis used problems completed and video minutes.
- Outcome link: [[big-data-mooc-research-breakthrough-learning-activities-lead-to-achievement-edtech-researcher-education-week]] reports that accountable activity was associated with passing.
