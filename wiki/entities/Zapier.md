## Overview
[[Zapier]] is a software service that lets people connect web applications and run automated workflows without writing conventional integration code.

## Current Profile
The 2016 company retrospective describes Zapier as beginning in late 2011 as a nights-and-weekends project by three recent graduates in Columbia, Missouri. Its first release connected 34 applications; customer requests then drove a developer platform, a catalog of more than 650 apps, multi-step workflows, dynamic searches, and data formatting. By May 2016 the company reported more than one million registered users, four million configured Zaps, and more than one million automated tasks per day. A fully remote team of more than 40 people worked across eight time zones.

## Key Characteristics
- User-facing connective layer among specialized web applications.
- No-code workflow builder spanning one-to-one and multi-step automation.
- Developer platform through which third-party services can add integrations.
- Customer-request-driven product and integration expansion.
- Fully distributed company operating across eight time zones in the source period.
- Company-reported scale of one million users and one million daily automated tasks by May 2016.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to a celebratory company-authored post from May 2016. Its user, workflow, task, and time-saving figures are not independently audited, registered users are not the same as active users, and the article gives no retention or task-distribution data. Historical claims should not be read as Zapier's current product catalog, headcount, or scale.
