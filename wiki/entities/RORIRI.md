## Overview
[[RORIRI]] is the author of sources explaining LLM terminology, context management, and AI-era education.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, RORIRI appears as an AI commentator who connects practical agent tooling terms to broader questions of cognition and education. One source ties MCP, Skills, RAG, Memory, prompt caching, and Computer Use to the shared problem of shaping LLM context; the newer education source asks what abilities humans need when AI handles more execution, emphasizing problem definition, model judgment, abstraction, writing, and taste as problem sense.

## Key Characteristics
- Explains LLM tooling concepts through the common problem of context management.
- Distinguishes prompt-level guidance from structured tool-call interfaces.
- Treats memory and RAG as related retrieval mechanisms.
- Argues for active context compression over passive summarization.
- Frames Computer Use as a packaging layer over lower-level agent action mechanisms.
- Extends computational thinking for AI-era education through statistics, abstraction/programming, and academic writing.
- Treats taste as the human capacity to notice and care about real questions before AI execution begins.
