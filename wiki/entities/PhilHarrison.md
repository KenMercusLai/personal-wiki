## Overview
[[PhilHarrison]] is represented in the source as the [[GoogleStadia]] leader who acknowledged concern about Google's history of abandoning products and defended Stadia as a significant cross-company effort.

## Current Profile
The April 2019 article quotes Harrison through a Kotaku interview conducted around Stadia's announcement. He said he understood the concern about whether Google would sustain the platform and pointed to its scale of investment. The source treats that response as candid but not conclusive because similarly broad organizational investment had not guaranteed the continuation of Google+.

## Key Characteristics
- Stadia executive speaking publicly during the platform's launch period.
- Acknowledged rather than dismissed concern about Google's shutdown reputation.
- Used investment scale and cross-company participation as commitment signals.

## Evidence
- Acknowledgement: [[googles-constant-product-shutdowns-are-damaging-its-brand-ars-technica]] reports Harrison saying he understood the concern.
- Assurance: [[googles-constant-product-shutdowns-are-damaging-its-brand-ars-technica]] reports him describing Stadia as nontrivial and a significant cross-company effort.
- Visual evidence: the retained photograph in [[googles-constant-product-shutdowns-are-damaging-its-brand-ars-technica]] shows Harrison presenting the Stadia controller on stage.

## Qualifications
This profile is limited to one article's excerpts from a launch-period Kotaku interview. It does not establish Harrison's complete responsibilities, the platform's internal commitments, or later outcomes. The source author's Google+ comparison is a qualification of Harrison's assurance, not evidence about his intent.
