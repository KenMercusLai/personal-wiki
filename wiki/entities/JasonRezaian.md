## Overview
[[JasonRezaian]] is a Washington Post correspondent whose 18-month detention in Iran and January 2016 release open Fortune's profile of [[JeffBezos]].

## Current Profile
The source describes Rezaian as having been held on vague espionage charges while reporting in Iran. After his release and transfer to a U.S. base in Germany, Bezos arrived to take Rezaian and his wife Yegi wherever they wished to go, ultimately flying them to Key West. Fortune uses the episode to illustrate an owner's personal duty of care toward people doing dangerous institutional work.

## Key Characteristics
- Reported for the Washington Post in Iran.
- Was detained for 18 months on charges the source describes as vague and unjust.
- Was reunited with his wife Yegi after release.
