## Overview
[[SteveSchlafman]] is represented in the wiki by a 2017 practitioner essay on the qualities, experiences, and institutional pathways that may produce strong venture investors.

## Current Profile
Schlafman frames venture-investor quality as a combination of learnable capability, interpersonal judgment, independent conviction, network development, and long-duration experience. He is interested in whether software, education, organizations, and relationships can make investor selection and training more systematic, but he doubts that a short program can substitute for apprenticeship across company problems and market cycles. His stated objective includes widening the candidate pool across gender, race, and geography.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses a multidimensional trait model rather than treating investing ability as one credential or personality type.
- Treats communication, interviewing, validation, and synthesis as investor capabilities analogous to journalism.
- Regards mentorship and experience across cycles as central to developing investment judgment.
- Explores scalable investor-development systems while keeping the measurement of soft skills and networks unresolved.
- Connects talent development with lowering capital, network, experience, and geography barriers to venture entry.

## Qualifications
