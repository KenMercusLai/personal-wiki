## Overview
[[SeshadhriComandur]] is described as an Amazon Scholar and University of California, Santa Cruz professor whose graph-theory work supplied the mathematical foundation for AWS's flat random-graph data-center network.

## Current Profile
Comandur joined after answering [[RatulMahajan]]'s request for graph-theory and routing expertise despite having no prior data-center background. Amazon credits him with a mathematical model that explained and bounded the simulated behavior, plus an equation whose solution determined the reproducible fiber arrangement inside each ShuffleBox.

## Key Characteristics
- Applies algorithms and abstract network mathematics to an industrial physical-network problem.
- Supplied analytical justification beyond empirical simulation.
- Helped convert randomness into a standardized and repeatable wiring construction.

## Evidence
- Entry into the project: [[aws-random-graph]] recounts Comandur responding to Mahajan's internal request in 2023.
- ShuffleBox design: [[aws-random-graph]] attributes the governing equation for its internal wiring arrangement to Comandur.
- Proof role: [[aws-random-graph]] says his modeling explained why the design worked and predicted its behavior across scale and load.
