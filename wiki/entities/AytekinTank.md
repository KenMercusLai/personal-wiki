## Overview
[[AytekinTank]] is the [[Jotform]] founder and article author who presents side projects as a practical path from after-hours experimentation to a bootstrapped company.

## Current Profile
The source frames Tank as a founder who began building products as a college hobby, continued improving them while working full-time as a programmer, and waited five years before leaving his job to focus fully on Jotform. His advice favors small protected time blocks, customer-value extras, fun, flexible deadlines, and experimental pacing over dramatic all-in startup leaps.

## Key Characteristics
- Built products first as a hobby and side project.
- Continued product work while holding a full-time programming job.
- Waited five years before making Jotform his full-time work.
- Presents bootstrapping as compatible with patient experimentation.
- Advises founders to protect small time pockets without overburdening side projects with rigid deadlines.
