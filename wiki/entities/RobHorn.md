## Overview
[[RobHorn]] is a coauthor of the Legacy Mimic pattern article on Martin Fowler's site.

## Current Profile
In the ingested source, Horn is represented through architecture guidance for incremental legacy replacement. The article describes how temporary mimic components can preserve legacy-facing contracts while new systems take over selected business capabilities.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of a legacy-displacement pattern article.
- Associated in this wiki with transitional architecture and migration sequencing.
- Explains old/new system coexistence through service-providing and service-consuming mimic examples.
