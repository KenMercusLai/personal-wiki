## Overview
[[Taresky]] is a practitioner-author whose ingested sources explain crypto yield strategies to beginners while foregrounding the risks behind high apparent returns.

## Current Profile
The sources present Taresky as a crypto practitioner writing for readers who may be skeptical of crypto but willing to understand its yield mechanics and risks. One source explains exchange lending and [[FundingRateArbitrage]] as bull-market arbitrage practices; the other explains [[StablecoinYieldFarming]] through DEX liquidity, AMMs, platform incentives, and vault compounding. Across both, the author uses plain-language analogies and high-yield examples, then repeatedly narrows them through custody, stablecoin, smart-contract, execution, behavioral, and legal-risk qualifications.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from the perspective of a practitioner with prior bull-market and bear-market arbitrage experience.
- Uses intentionally attention-grabbing yield framing while narrowing the claim through explicit risk disclaimers.
- Explains exchange lending, perpetual funding, stablecoin mining, AMM liquidity, and vault compounding for non-specialist readers.
- Treats behavioral discipline and operational custody as part of strategy because a yield seeker can become the losing trader through gambling, signing, custody, or exit mistakes.
- Presents concrete platform examples as time-sensitive learning aids rather than durable recommendations.

## Evidence
- Practitioner stance: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] says the author has used crypto arbitrage through prior cycles and writes from a full-time arbitrage perspective.
- Risk framing: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] twice says the headline is clickbait and excludes systemic risks such as exchange failure and stablecoin trust failure.
- Beginner explanation: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] walks through lending, perpetual contracts, funding fees, APY math, and beginner execution tools; [[blog-taresky-bai-hua-qu-kuai-lian-wen-ding-bi-wa-kuang-shi-shen-me]] walks through DEXs, AMMs, liquidity provision, stablecoin mining, and wallet setup.
- Behavioral and custody warning: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] asks readers whether they can resist gambling when surrounded by bull-market gains, while [[blog-taresky-bai-hua-qu-kuai-lian-wen-ding-bi-wa-kuang-shi-shen-me]] warns about seed phrases, token approvals, OTC exits, and misleading APY.
- Platform-example caveat: [[blog-taresky-bai-hua-qu-kuai-lian-wen-ding-bi-wa-kuang-shi-shen-me]] explicitly says its Belt Venus example became unsuitable and should be treated as process learning, not copied.
