## Overview
[[JohnGruber]] is the writer and publisher of [[DaringFireball]], represented here through [[JohnSaddington]]'s account of the publication's direct sponsorship business.

## Current Profile
The source describes Gruber as an Apple-focused independent publisher who began blogging in 2002 and moved Daring Fireball toward full-time work through repeated monetization experiments. By the period Saddington discusses, Gruber sold expensive direct RSS sponsorships, let sponsors write the principal placement, and added a later editorial thank-you post.

## Key Characteristics
- Long-running Apple-focused writer and Daring Fireball publisher.
- Experimented with advertising, memberships, merchandise, and direct sponsorship while moving toward full-time publishing.
- Preferred direct sponsorship to CPM-priced advertising in the source's account.
- Combined sponsor-authored placement with a later publisher-authored acknowledgment.
- Built enough repeat demand that Saddington describes the sponsorship inventory as consistently booked.

## Evidence
- Publishing history: [[does-sponsoring-daring-fireball-actually-work-john-saddington]] summarizes Gruber's move from a 2002 blog toward full-time operation in 2006.
- Sponsorship model: [[does-sponsoring-daring-fireball-actually-work-john-saddington]] describes direct email coordination, PayPal payment, an RSS sponsorship, and a later thank-you post.
- Pricing and demand: [[does-sponsoring-daring-fireball-actually-work-john-saddington]] reports 2015 placement prices of roughly US$8,000-US$10,000 and says repeat customers helped keep inventory booked.

## Qualifications
This profile is filtered through Saddington's notes on a Gruber talk and his experience as a sponsor. It is not a complete history of Gruber, Daring Fireball, its audience, or its later commercial model, and the reported pricing and booking state are historical.
