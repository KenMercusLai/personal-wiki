## Overview
[[WaltMossberg]] is presented as a prominent consumer-technology reviewer and [[KaraSwisher]]'s long-term editorial, conference, and business partner in [[AllThingsD]] and [[Recode]].

## Current Profile
The 2014 profile describes Mossberg as a Washington-based journalist who deliberately kept geographic distance from the technology industry while reviewing its products. After helping Swisher join *The Wall Street Journal*, he partnered with her to replace conventional sponsor-led conferences with live editorial interviews and later to build AllThingsD. Their complementary positions—Mossberg as consumer advocate and Swisher as deeply sourced industry reporter—supported a profitable event and publication operation before they left Dow Jones to launch Re/code.

## Key Characteristics
- Reviewed technology from a consumer-oriented position outside Silicon Valley.
- Recruited and partnered with Swisher across newsroom, conference, and independent-publication work.
- Insisted that their conference remain under editorial rather than corporate conference control.
- Rejected sponsor-reserved speaking slots, prepared executive speeches, and generic panel formats.
- Served as an important personal ally to Swisher as well as a business partner.
