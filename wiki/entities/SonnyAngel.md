## Overview
[[SonnyAngel]] is an external designer-toy IP whose strong sales helped [[PopMart]] recognize the designer-toy category and later pursue self-operated IP.

## Current Profile
In the source, Sonny Angel is a strategic discovery point rather than the main subject. Its sales strength exposed a demand pattern in Pop Mart stores, while later supplier constraints pushed [[WangNing]] toward seeking owned or controlled characters instead of depending on agency goods.

## Key Characteristics
- Worked as a high-performing product inside Pop Mart's pre-IP retail stage.
- Helped identify designer toys as a focus category.
- Created strategic vulnerability because dependence on external IP limited Pop Mart's control.
- Influenced Pop Mart's later pursuit of self-operated IP such as [[MOLLY]].

## Evidence
- Category discovery: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] says Pop Mart noticed Sonny Angel was its best-selling agency product around 2014.
- Control problem: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] says pressure from the Sonny Angel rights holder in 2015 contributed to Wang Ning's decision to seek designer-toy IP.
- Strategic shift: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] places Sonny Angel before Pop Mart's later signing of [[MOLLY]].

## Qualifications
The source discusses Sonny Angel only through Pop Mart's development history, so this page should not be treated as a complete account of the IP itself or its rights holder.

## What Changed
- Created the Sonny Angel profile as a trigger in Pop Mart's category discovery and IP-control shift.
