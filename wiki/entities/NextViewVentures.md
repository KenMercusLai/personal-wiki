## Overview
[[NextViewVentures]] is represented in the wiki as the venture-capital publisher of founder-facing advice on presenting and reasoning about market size.

## Current Profile
In the supplied 2016 article, NextView Ventures is skeptical of pitch slides that present a large market figure as self-validating. Its advice focuses on diligence quality: distinguish measurable existing demand from adjacent expansion and newly created demand, then expose the assumptions, sources, and calculations behind the estimate. The profile here is limited to that publication role and does not attempt to describe the firm's wider investment history or portfolio.

## Key Characteristics
- Venture-capital firm publishing practical fundraising guidance for founders.
- Treats market sizing as an inspectable reasoning process rather than a pitch-deck spectacle.
- Uses an existing, expanding, and created-market framework to separate different forecasting problems.
- Assumes investors will perform independent diligence rather than accept a founder's headline estimate.
