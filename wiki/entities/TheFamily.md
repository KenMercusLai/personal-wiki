## Overview
[[TheFamily]] is the startup-support organization associated with [[MathiasPastor]] in "Avoiding Zombie Startups."

## Current Profile
The source presents The Family as a European startup organization that educates prospective employees, connects talent with portfolio founders, sometimes hires high-potential people without predefined roles, and advises startups on employee-equity plans. These descriptions come from an affiliated author and function partly as promotion for its free Paris-based school, Lion.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports startup founders and prospective startup employees.
- Uses flexible role discovery for some high-potential hires.
- Treats understandable employee ownership as a commitment mechanism.
- Operates or promoted the free Lion school for startup employees in Paris.

## Evidence
- Ecosystem support: [[avoiding-zombie-startups]] says The Family's founders were seeking talented employees and that ecosystem education benefits its startups.
- Flexible hiring: [[avoiding-zombie-startups]] says the organization sometimes hired high-potential people without a fixed role and trusted them to find useful work.
- Equity advice: [[avoiding-zombie-startups]] says The Family advised European startups on employee-equity-plan structure with Florent of Ekwity.
- Employee education: [[avoiding-zombie-startups]] describes Lion as a free Paris school for startup employees founded by The Family.

## Qualifications
This profile is limited to claims in a 2019 article written by an affiliated author. It does not independently establish The Family's broader history, outcomes, governance, or the later status of its programs.
