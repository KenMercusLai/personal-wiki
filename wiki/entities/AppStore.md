## Overview
[[AppStore]] is Apple's iOS app marketplace, presented in the wiki as both a distribution and visibility platform for [[Instapaper]] and an economically concentrated mobile marketplace that out-earned [[GooglePlay]] despite lower download volume in 2015.

## Current Profile
The Instapaper article uses the App Store as a marker of mobile-platform timing. Instapaper was among the first apps available when the store launched in July 2008, received launch-day downloads and early features, later changed pricing and freemium status through the store, and was again featured around iPhone X launch support. The App Annie/VentureBeat source adds the marketplace-economics layer: in 2015 Apple's App Store had fewer downloads than Google Play but generated 75% more revenue, with revenue growth driven mainly by China, the U.S., and Japan and games contributing 75% of iOS App Store revenue. The App Store therefore appears as both a distribution channel and an economic surface where iOS user spending could outweigh Android download scale.

## Key Characteristics
- Provided early distribution when Instapaper launched as an iOS app in 2008.
- Created visibility through App Store features and launch-era placement.
- Hosted pricing transitions across free, paid Pro, price drops, and freemium availability.
- Continued to shape product milestones through iOS 11 and iPhone X-specific updates.
- Generated much higher revenue than Google Play in the 2015 App Annie estimate despite lower download volume.
- Drew revenue growth mainly from China, the U.S., and Japan in that source.

## Evidence
- Early launch: [[10-years-of-instapaper]] says Instapaper was one of the first apps available after Apple's App Store launched in July 2008.
- Initial traction: [[10-years-of-instapaper]] reports more than 2,000 downloads on the first day and more than 15,000 before the end of that month.
- Pricing surface: [[10-years-of-instapaper]] records Instapaper Pro pricing, the 2011 removal of the free app, later iOS price changes, and the 2014 return to free App Store availability through freemium.
- Later visibility: [[10-years-of-instapaper]] says the iPhone X-specific update was featured at launch on Apple's new App Store.
- Revenue lead: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] reports Apple's App Store generated 75% more revenue than Google Play in 2015.
- Revenue geography: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] says App Store revenue growth was driven primarily by China, the U.S., and Japan.
- Games mix: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] says games contributed 75% of iOS App Store revenue.

## Qualifications
The Instapaper source is not an App Store history and covers only the aspects of Apple's marketplace that mattered to Instapaper's distribution, pricing, and launch visibility. The App Annie/VentureBeat source is a 2015 relative market snapshot and does not provide absolute revenue, methodology, later App Store policy changes, subscriptions, advertising, regulatory pressure, or current platform economics.
