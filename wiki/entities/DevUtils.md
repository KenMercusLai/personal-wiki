## Overview
[[DevUtils]] is a macOS application by [[TonyDinh]] that combines frequently used developer utilities in one offline interface.

## Current Profile
Dinh says he built the first version in about two weeks, validated it with friends and coworkers, and initially sold lifetime access for $9. Hacker News and Product Hunt produced short traffic and sales spikes, but the rapid decay after each launch pushed him toward audience-building as a longer-lived distribution channel.

## Key Characteristics
- Offline bundle of developer utilities for macOS.
- Rapidly built successor to a six-month abandoned log-viewer project.
- Initially sold through a low-priced one-time license.
- Early demand validated through direct feedback and launch communities.
- Distribution case showing the limits of one-off launch traffic.

## Evidence
- Product and development: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] describes the two-week build and shows the multi-tool interface.
- Validation and pricing: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] reports favorable friend and coworker feedback followed by a $9 price.
- Launch behavior: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] records a 241-point Show HN post and temporary Product Hunt traffic.

## Qualifications
The source provides no download, conversion, retention, support-cost, or lifetime-revenue series. Early feedback came from people personally connected to the founder, and launch-site attention does not establish durable demand.
