## Overview
[[TenXManagement]] is the talent agency profiled in a 2014 New Yorker article for representing freelance programmers and other digital specialists using a model adapted from music management.

## Current Profile
The agency was founded by music managers Michael Solomon and Rishon Blumberg, with engineer-musician [[AltayGuvench]] later joining as a partner. Unlike employer-paid recruiters, it aligned its revenue with represented talent through a 15% share and concentrated on freelancers. Its service joined technical and communication screening with rate negotiation, contracts, invoicing, collection, project matching, and client mediation. The article also describes an ambition to package complete product teams, but closes with the agency still searching for a repeatable customer-acquisition channel.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents technologists rather than acting as an employer's recruiting agent.
- Combines technical vetting with communication screening and business representation.
- Uses percentage-based compensation intended to align the agency with freelancer earnings.
- Tries to reduce freelancing's administrative burden, project volatility, and scope ambiguity.
- Envisions packaging cross-functional project teams as an alternative to full-time hiring.

## Evidence
- Representation model: [[the-programmers-price]] says the agency charged represented talent 15% and worked mostly with freelancers.
- Vetting: [[the-programmers-price]] describes questionnaires, interviews, communication screening, code reviews, and inspection of applicants' prior work.
- Business operations: [[the-programmers-price]] assigns negotiation, contracting, invoicing, collection, payouts, matching, and problem resolution across the partners.
- Reported effect: [[the-programmers-price]] says Guvench's rate rose from $100 to $150 per hour and reports that aggregate client earnings doubled between 2013 and 2014.
- Expansion ambition: [[the-programmers-price]] describes a packaging model spanning concept, design, implementation, testing, optimization, branding, and marketing.

## Qualifications
The profile is a journalistic snapshot from 2014, not current company documentation or an independent evaluation of agency outcomes. It gives no complete denominator for applicants, engagements, retention, utilization, disputes, or earnings distribution. Reported rate and earnings gains can reflect selection, market timing, hours, or client mix as well as representation, and the 15% alignment remains incomplete if short-term billings conflict with long-term freelancer or client interests.
