## Overview
[[DanSaffer]] is the author credited in Jennifer Tang's article with the four-component model of [[Microinteractions]].

## Current Profile
Within the available secondary account, Saffer's contribution is an analytic decomposition of a small interaction into trigger, rules, feedback, and loops or modes. The model separates what starts an exchange, what behavior is permitted, how the result becomes perceptible, and how repetition or special states alter it.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames microinteractions as structured behavior rather than isolated visual polish.
- Distinguishes user-initiated triggers from condition-based system triggers.
- Treats feedback as the perceptible verification of an interaction.
- Uses loops and modes to account for repetition, duration, and alternate operating states.
