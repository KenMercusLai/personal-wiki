## Overview
[[NickBabich]] is a product-design writer represented in this wiki by a 2016 practitioner article about mobile empty states.

## Current Profile
Babich treats an interface’s unpopulated and failure states as part of the designed product experience. His framework combines functional explanation, an appropriate emotional tone, and a clear next action, while recognizing that first use, errors, and user-cleared content call for different messages.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames transient and blank interface states as consequential product surfaces.
- Connects first-use guidance with onboarding, action prompting, and product personality.
- Uses selected mobile-product examples to communicate practical design heuristics.

## Evidence
- Empty-state framework: [[empty-state-mobile-app-nice-to-have-essential-ux-planet]] organizes the design task around educating, delighting, and prompting action.
- Context sensitivity: [[empty-state-mobile-app-nice-to-have-essential-ux-planet]] distinguishes first-use, error, and user-cleared states and asks designers to explain why content is absent.
- Example-led teaching: [[empty-state-mobile-app-nice-to-have-essential-ux-planet]] uses trip-planning, invoicing, collaboration, entertainment, storage, and portfolio interfaces as illustrations.
