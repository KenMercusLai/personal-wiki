## Overview
[[CarlFritjofsson]] is a venture capitalist and coauthor of a 2018 survey comparing how founders and VCs understand investor value, contact, operational support, and financing-partner selection.

## Current Profile
Fritjofsson writes from inside venture capital while questioning the industry's “value-added investor” narrative. With [[HenriDeshays]] and research support from [[OwenReynolds]], he reports separate samples of 121 founders and 98 VCs and interprets the gaps as evidence that investors overestimate their contact, contribution, and service impact. His role in the source is both practitioner and co-researcher, so the article combines useful self-critique with the limits of a voluntary unmatched survey.

## Key Characteristics
- Venture-capital practitioner willing to challenge investor self-assessment.
- Co-designer and coauthor of a mirrored founder-and-VC survey.
- Emphasizes relationship chemistry, decision speed, and founder-side measures of investor help.

## Evidence
- Authorship and framing: [[do-vcs-really-add-value-founders-say-sometimes-by-carl-fritjofsson]] identifies Fritjofsson as a VC who suspects investors oversell their company-level impact.
- Research role: [[do-vcs-really-add-value-founders-say-sometimes-by-carl-fritjofsson]] credits him and Deshays with undertaking the study, supported by Reynolds and the Kauffman Fellows Program.
- Interpretation: [[do-vcs-really-add-value-founders-say-sometimes-by-carl-fritjofsson]] argues that founders discount tangible VC value-add and that speed and terms can outperform brand in winning deals.
