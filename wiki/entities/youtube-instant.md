Source: [3 Principles to Make Your Side Project Stand Out to Help You Land a Job in Tech]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/hack-career-side-projects-tech-job.md" >}})

YouTube Instant appears in the Hack Career article as the main example of a rare "Field of Dreams" side project. The source says Feross Aboukhadijeh built the project in 2010 in less than six hours, after which it became a media sensation and drew a YouTube job offer from co-founder Chad Hurley.

The article uses YouTube Instant mainly as a contrast case. Its point is not that every candidate needs a similarly viral project, but that this kind of side project depends on unusually strong underlying skill and unusual public attention. Most applicants should instead make ordinary projects useful by ensuring relevance, depth, iteration, and visibility.
