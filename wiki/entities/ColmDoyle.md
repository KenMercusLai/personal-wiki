## Overview
[[ColmDoyle]] is the author of a 2016 essay arguing that traditional App Store release notes are a weak primary communication channel for modern consumer applications.

## Current Profile
The supplied source identifies Doyle through authorship rather than a biography. His argument focuses on the mismatch between build-level notes and user-specific product exposure, and favors contextual, measurable in-app feature communication. The source explicitly keeps APIs and SDKs outside that claim.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from a practitioner perspective about mobile application development.
- Treats feature flags, entitlements, and server-controlled behavior as challenges to uniform release descriptions.
- Favors contextual product communication and product refinement over polishing low-read store notes.
