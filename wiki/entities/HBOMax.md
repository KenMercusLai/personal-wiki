## Overview
[[HBOMax]] is presented in this wiki as a streaming service whose valuable content catalog is weakened by poor app-level UX judgment.

## Current Profile
Built for Mars frames HBO Max as a product with strong entertainment demand but weak execution details. The article says the service has beloved content such as Game of Thrones, yet its app ratings trail other major streaming services. The source's diagnosis is not that the entire app experience is unusable; instead, the app repeatedly lets style, ambiguity, and missed flow design override functional clarity.

The case focuses on practical defects: oversized imagery slows the app, free-versus-paid availability is communicated by absence rather than explicit state, a sticky menu partially covers list endings, the free-episode experience retraces rather than advances toward subscription, and account creation lets the iOS keyboard hide final fields and CTA controls. Within the wiki, HBO Max therefore serves as a concrete case where product quality depends on fine-grained interaction details after the core content proposition is already compelling.

## Key Characteristics
- Has valuable streaming content, but the app experience receives poor public ratings in the source.
- Prioritizes high-quality imagery in ways that can damage perceived speed on slower connections.
- Uses ambiguous absence-of-evidence cues for free-versus-paid episode availability.
- Leaves partially occluded list items that can create broad but underreported irritation.
- Misses a conversion path after free-episode viewing compared with the [[AppleTV]] example.
- Shows avoidable mobile form usability problems during account creation.

## Evidence
- Ratings gap: [[built-for-mars-the-ux-of-hbo-max-vs-netflix]] says HBO Max had the lowest app-store ratings among the major streaming services in the article's comparison.
- Catalog-versus-app split: [[built-for-mars-the-ux-of-hbo-max-vs-netflix]] quotes a review distinguishing strong content from an embarrassing app.
- Performance issue: [[built-for-mars-the-ux-of-hbo-max-vs-netflix]] says HBO's comparative images are more than four times larger than the industry standard.
- Availability ambiguity: [[built-for-mars-the-ux-of-hbo-max-vs-netflix]] says HBO Max distinguishes free-trial episodes from subscription-only episodes through absence of evidence.
- Silent frustration: [[built-for-mars-the-ux-of-hbo-max-vs-netflix]] says a sticky menu partially covers final list items, producing a widespread issue users may not explicitly report.
- Journey weakness: [[built-for-mars-the-ux-of-hbo-max-vs-netflix]] says HBO Max plays a free episode and returns the user to the prior state rather than continuing into a subscription path.
- Form weakness: [[built-for-mars-the-ux-of-hbo-max-vs-netflix]] says the iOS keyboard covers final fields and the CTA during account creation.

## Qualifications
The profile is source-scoped to one Built for Mars case study and reports its comparative UX interpretation rather than current HBO Max product status, later Max rebranding changes, or independent app-store data. The source itself also says the overall experience is actually quite good, so the critique should be read as a diagnosis of repeated UX finesse failures rather than a claim that the product has no working value.
