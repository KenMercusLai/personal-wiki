## Overview
[[JohnGottman]] appears in the wiki through [[GarryTan]]'s use of Gottman's relationship-failure frame to explain danger signs in [[CoFounderConflict]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Gottman as the psychologist associated with the Four Horsemen concept: defensiveness, criticism, contempt, and stonewalling. Tan applies that relationship frame to startup co-founders, arguing that the same behaviors indicate that founder conflict has become corrosive rather than productive.

## Key Characteristics
- Is cited as a psychologist.
- Is associated in the source with the Four Horsemen relationship concept.
- Provides a relationship-failure lens that Tan transfers to co-founder dynamics.
