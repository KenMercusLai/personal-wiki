## Overview
[[JasonLemkin]] is the author of a [[SaaStr]] essay arguing that recruiting strong direct reports is a defining qualification for startup VPs.

## Current Profile
In this wiki's source, Lemkin writes as a SaaS operator-advisor focused on the organizational risks that emerge after early revenue. His practical hiring test asks founders to verify whom a VP candidate personally recruited, speak with two of those hires, and judge the concrete results they produced. He treats inability to recruit as a leadership ceiling that may require placing a stronger executive above an otherwise capable manager.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames executive recruiting as a scaling discipline for SaaS companies.
- Distinguishes a candidate's own hires from teams inherited through promotion or management changes.
- Uses role-specific outcomes to evaluate whether recruited direct reports were genuinely strong.
- Advocates fast organizational correction when a VP cannot build a capable team.

## Evidence
- Recruiting priority: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] calls recruiting the first job of every VP.
- Candidate diligence: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] recommends talking with a prospective VP's two best personally recruited reports.
- Outcome focus: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] proposes quota, product shipment, engineering quality, lead generation, churn, NPS, and CSAT as role-specific checks.
- Corrective action: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] recommends hiring above a VP who proves unable to recruit rather than waiting for repeated failed hires.
