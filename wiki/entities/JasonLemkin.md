## Overview
[[JasonLemkin]] is a [[SaaStr]] author represented in the wiki through practical heuristics for executive recruiting and private SaaS revenue estimation.

## Current Profile
Across the available sources, Lemkin writes as a SaaS operator-advisor who compresses ambiguous scaling questions into quick checks. His hiring test asks founders to verify whom a VP candidate personally recruited, speak with two of those hires, and judge their concrete results. His revenue-estimation shortcut multiplies LinkedIn headcount by a funding- and business-model-adjusted [[RevenuePerEmployee]] range. Both methods are starting points for diligence rather than measured universal laws: the recruiting article's numerical failure rates are uncited, while the revenue article uses selected examples and mixes ARR with GAAP comparisons.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames executive recruiting as a scaling discipline for SaaS companies.
- Distinguishes a candidate's own hires from teams inherited through promotion or management changes.
- Uses role-specific outcomes to evaluate whether recruited direct reports were genuinely strong.
- Advocates fast organizational correction when a VP cannot build a capable team.
- Uses public headcount and calibrated revenue-per-employee assumptions to estimate private SaaS scale.
- Presents quick heuristics as useful ranges or screening checks rather than precise measurements.

## Evidence
- Recruiting priority: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] calls recruiting the first job of every VP.
- Candidate diligence: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] recommends talking with a prospective VP's two best personally recruited reports.
- Outcome focus: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] proposes quota, product shipment, engineering quality, lead generation, churn, NPS, and CSAT as role-specific checks.
- Corrective action: [[vps-that-cant-hire-jason-m-lemkin-medium]] recommends hiring above a VP who proves unable to recruit rather than waiting for repeated failed hires.
- Revenue baseline: [[jason-lemkin-how-to-figure-out-your-competitors-revenues-in-about-70-seconds]] proposes $150,000 per employee for well-funded companies and $200,000 for modestly funded companies beyond a few million dollars in ARR.
- Calibration: [[jason-lemkin-how-to-figure-out-your-competitors-revenues-in-about-70-seconds]] adjusts the estimate for funding, growth, and freemium or self-funded operation.
- Plausibility checks: [[jason-lemkin-how-to-figure-out-your-competitors-revenues-in-about-70-seconds]] compares selected Marketo, [[HubSpot]], and YouSendIt estimates with cited figures.

## Qualifications
This profile is limited to two advice essays and does not establish Lemkin's broader career or body of work. The hiring article's numerical failure rates and timelines lack supporting data. The revenue article is a 2012 practitioner heuristic rather than a systematically evaluated estimator: it assumes reasonably complete LinkedIn headcount, shifts multipliers by company characteristics, selects its examples, and compares ARR with GAAP revenue in places.
