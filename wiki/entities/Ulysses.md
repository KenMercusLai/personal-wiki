## Overview
[[Ulysses]] is a professional writing app for Mac, iPad, and iPhone whose team announced a move from separate one-time platform purchases to one subscription in August 2017.

## Current Profile
The source presents Ulysses as a mature, continuously changing productivity product rather than a finished release. Since its 2013 launch, the team says it delivered nine major feature releases without additional payment, replaced at least 80 percent of the original app, and quadrupled its functionality. That evolution increased code, staffing, maintenance, and compatibility work while revenue remained dependent on attracting new customers and generating release publicity.

The proposed subscription unlocks every platform, supports a full trial plus monthly, annual, and student plans, and shifts canceled accounts to read-only access with export rather than locking away documents. The team frames this as a way to fund ongoing quality, compatibility, support, and incremental releases while avoiding separate Mac and iOS upgrade purchases.

## Key Characteristics
- Cross-platform writing environment intended for use across Mac, iPad, and iPhone.
- Mature product whose team reports extensive replacement and expansion after its initial release.
- Shifted from separate one-time platform purchases to one recurring entitlement.
- Uses continued reading and export after cancellation as a document-access safeguard.
- Treats operating-system, device, and file-format compatibility as recurring product obligations.
- Frames predictable revenue as support for quality work, staffing, experimentation, and faster support.

## Evidence
- Product evolution: [[why-were-switching-ulysses-to-subscription-building-ulysses-medium]] reports nine major free feature releases, at least 80 percent replacement of the original app, and fourfold functionality growth after the 2013 launch.
- Cross-platform role: [[why-were-switching-ulysses-to-subscription-building-ulysses-medium]] says the app is most useful across Mac, iPad, and iPhone and illustrates how version skew can make files incompatible.
- Subscription design: [[why-were-switching-ulysses-to-subscription-building-ulysses-medium]] describes one entitlement across devices, a time-limited full trial, monthly and annual plans, and discounted student pricing.
- Exit behavior: [[why-were-switching-ulysses-to-subscription-building-ulysses-medium]] says cancellation disables editing while preserving reading, export, and compatibility updates.
- Operating rationale: [[why-were-switching-ulysses-to-subscription-building-ulysses-medium]] argues that recurring revenue reduces dependence on launch spikes and supports maintenance, iteration, staffing, and support.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from the Ulysses team’s own 2017 justification at the moment of transition. Its sales and subscription charts are conceptual, it supplies no audited cost, revenue, churn, retention, or customer-value data, and it does not establish whether the promised accessibility, release cadence, quality, or long-term document access were achieved.
