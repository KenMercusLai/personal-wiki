## Overview
[[TinyHabits]] is [[BJFogg]]'s book presented in the source as a deeper public explanation of the [[FoggBehaviorModel]] and related [[BehaviorDesign]] methods.

## Current Profile
The source positions Tiny Habits as an extended, accessible account of Fogg's behavior-design system. It says the book explains the behavior model in depth for a global audience and connects it to other models and methods for lasting change. The source does not summarize the book's full method beyond tying it to the Fogg Behavior Model.

## Key Characteristics
- Authored by [[BJFogg]].
- Presents the [[FoggBehaviorModel]] for a broad audience.
- Connects the model to other behavior-design methods.
- Focuses on small changes and lasting change.
