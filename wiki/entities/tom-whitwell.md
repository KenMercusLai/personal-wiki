Source: [52 Things I Learned in 2017]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/tom-whitwell-52-things-i-learned-in-2017.md" >}})

Tom Whitwell is the author of this Fluxx Studio Notes source. The article identifies him as a Senior Consultant at Fluxx and presents the list as a set of facts learned between projects and book editing work.

In this source, Whitwell's role is less that of a single-domain analyst than a curator of useful anomalies. His 52-item list moves across technology adoption, media economics, medical devices, infrastructure, market forecasts, consumer behavior, research methods, poverty, and environmental systems.

The source is useful in the wiki because it turns curiosity into product and systems prompts: observe how people actually use tools, notice when old systems persist, check whether samples represent the population, and ask what almost prevented a customer from buying.
