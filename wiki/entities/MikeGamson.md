## Overview
[[MikeGamson]] appears in the source as LinkedIn's enterprise-sales leader and an example of business leadership aligned with member-first product values.

## Current Profile
The source uses Gamson to show how LinkedIn balanced consumer membership with enterprise revenue. [[JeffWeiner]] says LinkedIn's first value was that members come first, which could conflict with a typical enterprise-sales culture where the next sale dominates. Gamson is presented as a sales leader who believed in that member-first principle and described his hiring target as business people and business leaders rather than only salespeople.

## Key Characteristics
- Led LinkedIn's enterprise-sales function in the source.
- Supported the member-first value despite enterprise revenue pressure.
- Treated sales hiring as business-leadership hiring.
- Used diversity as a competitive and decision-quality advantage, not only as a fashion.

## Evidence
- Member-first sales alignment: [[cs183c-session-19-jeff-weiner-linkedin]] says LinkedIn could execute enterprise lines because Gamson believed members came first.
- Business leadership: [[cs183c-session-19-jeff-weiner-linkedin]] says Gamson preferred to hire business people and business leaders rather than only salespeople.
- Diversity rationale: [[cs183c-session-19-jeff-weiner-linkedin]] says Gamson wanted diverse teams because he wanted to win.
