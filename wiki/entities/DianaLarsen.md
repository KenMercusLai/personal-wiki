## Overview
[[DianaLarsen]] is represented in the wiki as the co-creator of the [[AgileFluencyModel]] with [[JamesShore]].

## Current Profile
In the Fowler foreword, Larsen appears through her collaboration with Shore on a model for understanding how teams develop agile skill. The source uses that model to separate shallow project-management-level adoption from the technical and collaborative skill needed for high productivity and reliability.

Her page is therefore currently anchored to agile coaching and capability development rather than to a broad biographical profile.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-creator of the [[AgileFluencyModel]].
- Associated with agile skill development and coaching practice in the source.
- Helps frame agile maturity as a progression of capabilities rather than a binary adoption label.
- Connected to the distinction between basic Scrum-like value focus and deeper reliable delivery.

## Evidence
- Model collaboration: [[blog-martin-fowler-foreword-to-the-art-of-agile-development]] says Shore worked with Larsen to create the Agile Fluency Model.
- Capability framing: [[blog-martin-fowler-foreword-to-the-art-of-agile-development]] uses the model to describe different ways people develop skill with Agile approaches.
