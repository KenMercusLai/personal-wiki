## Overview
[[BrettKlika]] is represented in this wiki as a coauthor of a 2013 practitioner article proposing body-weight [[HighIntensityCircuitTraining]] for time-constrained adults.

## Current Profile
In the bounded source, Klika writes with [[ChrisJordan]] from the [[HumanPerformanceInstitute]] and frames exercise programming around safety, effectiveness, time efficiency, and portability. The article combines a review of prior HICT research with a concrete 12-station routine, while acknowledging contraindications and performance goals for which traditional training may be better. The source does not provide enough biographical evidence for a broader career profile.
