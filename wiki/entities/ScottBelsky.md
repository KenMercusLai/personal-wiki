## Overview
[[ScottBelsky]] is the author of a 2018 practitioner thesis about the aggregate competitive force of micro brands and the infrastructure enabling them.

## Current Profile
Belsky interprets Instagram discovery, targeted advertising, modular commerce services, small-batch manufacturing, and design as a system that lowers the cost of testing consumer brands. He discloses an investment in packaging supplier Lumi and prior angel investments in several larger modern brands, so the source combines operating observation with an investor's ecosystem perspective.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames competition as a long tail of small brands rather than a contest among category dominators.
- Emphasizes designers as the central creative operators in low-overhead brand formation.
- Examines social targeting, supply chains, commerce software, packaging, and fulfillment as complementary infrastructure.
- Questions whether venture capital is necessary for brands that can validate demand with little inventory exposure.

## Evidence
- Market thesis: [[attack-of-the-micro-brands-positive-slope-medium]] argues that thousands of small brands can collectively pressure incumbents.
- Operating model: [[attack-of-the-micro-brands-positive-slope-medium]] describes rapid brand tests using targeted advertising and small-batch or on-demand manufacturing.
- Ecosystem view: [[attack-of-the-micro-brands-positive-slope-medium]] identifies designers, Instagram, Shopify, packaging suppliers, and fulfillment providers as likely beneficiaries.
- Investor context: [[attack-of-the-micro-brands-positive-slope-medium]] discloses Belsky's Lumi investment and angel investments in modern consumer brands.
