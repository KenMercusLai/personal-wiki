## Overview
[[CollaborativeFund]] is the publication and investing-firm context for [[MorganHousel]] essays in the wiki corpus on durable strategy and behavioral financial judgment.

## Current Profile
Within these sources, Collaborative Fund matters as the venue for Housel's investing and business-strategy writing. One article uses venture-capital language but pushes against change-only thinking, arguing that long-term investments need stable customer desires as well as new distribution, software, or capital structures. The later beliefs article is less company-specific and more behavioral: it treats risk, incentives, uncertainty, debt, savings, expectations, and reputation as recurring judgment problems.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes strategy and investing writing.
- Serves as the venue for Housel's argument about stable customer demand.
- Serves as the venue for Housel's aphoristic behavioral-finance and decision-making beliefs.
- Appears here as context rather than as the primary subject of analysis.
