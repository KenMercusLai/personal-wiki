## Overview
[[CollaborativeFund]] is the publication and investing-firm context for [[MorganHousel]] essays in the wiki corpus on durable strategy, behavioral financial judgment, and repeatable learning from business history.

## Current Profile
Within these sources, Collaborative Fund matters as the venue for Housel's investing and business-strategy writing. One article uses venture-capital language but pushes against change-only thinking, arguing that long-term investments need stable customer desires as well as new distribution, software, or capital structures. The beliefs article is less company-specific and more behavioral: it treats risk, incentives, uncertainty, debt, savings, expectations, and reputation as recurring judgment problems. The repeatability essay returns to business and investing history, arguing that learners should emulate transferable skills while filtering out one-time conditions.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes strategy and investing writing.
