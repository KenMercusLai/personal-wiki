## Overview
[[EastgateCentre]] is the Harare, Zimbabwe office-building example used in the source to illustrate cross-field innovation through termite-inspired temperature regulation.

## Current Profile
The sources present Eastgate Centre as an architectural case where [[MickPearce]] translated observed termite-nest temperature-stabilization techniques into building design. They report that the building maintains an ambient temperature between 73 and 78 degrees Fahrenheit while outside temperatures vary from 58 to 88 degrees Fahrenheit. The original source's embedded photograph shows the building facade and exterior shading or structural texture, supporting identification of the building but not adding new measured claims.

## Key Characteristics
- Located in Harare, Zimbabwe in the source's description.
- Used as an example of biological observation informing architecture.
- Reported to maintain a narrow internal temperature range despite broader outside variation.
- Serves as a concrete case for [[InnovationAtIntersection]].
