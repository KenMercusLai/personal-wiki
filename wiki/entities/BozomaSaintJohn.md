## Overview
[[BozomaSaintJohn]] is presented by Complex as the Apple Music marketing leader responsible for making the service legible, culturally alive, and discoverable to mainstream audiences.

## Current Profile
The article frames Saint John as Apple Music's public meaning-maker. Coming from music and entertainment marketing at Pepsi, she describes Apple Music as a living brand with personality and sees her role as providing the microphone and megaphone for what artists and the service are doing. She emphasizes discovery, attention, global ambition, and the productive conflict that comes from building something emotionally charged and new.

## Key Characteristics
- Translates Apple Music's purpose for people who have not paid for streaming before.
- Frames Apple Music as a living brand rather than a static service.
- Treats marketing as discovery infrastructure in a noisy media environment.
- Brings entertainment-marketing experience into Apple's technology context.
- Sees passionate conflict inside the team as improving the product.
