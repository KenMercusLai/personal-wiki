## Overview
[[FitCurve]] is a Figma plugin that helps designers draw smooth curves.

## Current Profile
The source uses [[FitCurve]] as the cleanest example of a narrow small-product bet: a designer friend's problem fit criteria of low development and maintenance cost, quick launch, and potential passive income. Zhang Xiaoji says it took roughly one week to develop, and screenshots show early Figma marketplace usage and paid purchases.

## Key Characteristics
- Targets a narrow design workflow rather than a broad platform problem.
- Was selected because the need matched low-cost, quick-launch product criteria.
- Benefited from Figma's plugin-marketplace exposure for new products.
- Supplies portfolio diversification rather than primary income.

## Evidence
- Narrow workflow: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] says a font-design friend could not find a convenient smooth-curve tool on Figma.
- Quick launch: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] describes the product as completed in about one week.
- Marketplace exposure: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] includes Figma analytics screenshots with thousands of users/views and purchases.
- Diversification: [[zhang-xiaoji-jian-ru-jia-jing-xie-gang-cheng-xu-yuan-de-shu-zi-you-min-zhuan-xing-zhi-lu]] frames Fit Curve as smaller income alongside [[TableToFigma]] and [[GlimmerCard]].

## Qualifications
The source reports modest monthly revenue and a decline in later usage after launch exposure, so the product supports portfolio resilience rather than proving a large standalone business.
