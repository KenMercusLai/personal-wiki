## Overview
[[MattHaughey]] is a web publisher and entrepreneur represented here as the author of a practical argument for learning and career development through side projects.

## Current Profile
Haughey uses his own path from learning HTML during graduate school to web work, early blogging, and running a company as an example of a side pursuit changing professional direction. His advice is deliberately small-scale: identify one skill or thing to create, replace a low-cost use of time, work for a few weeks, review the result, and share when ready.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats side projects as experiments in learning, making, and possible career change.
- Grounds the argument partly in his own transition from an HTML hobby to web work and company building.
- Recommends one bounded project and a short review cycle rather than an indefinite portfolio of ambitions.

## Evidence
- Career path: [[elevate-yourself-with-side-projects-the-official-slack-blog]] says Haughey began learning HTML as a graduate-school hobby in 1995 and later moved into web work, blogging, and company leadership.
- Practical method: [[elevate-yourself-with-side-projects-the-official-slack-blog]] recommends choosing one desired skill or creation, giving it a few weeks, and reviewing progress.
- Broader purpose: [[elevate-yourself-with-side-projects-the-official-slack-blog]] presents happiness and a broader skill range as valid outcomes even when a project does not become a job.

## Qualifications
The profile comes from one short motivational essay and does not independently document Haughey's wider career or measure the typical outcomes of his advice. Its famous and personal examples are illustrative survivor stories rather than a base-rate study.
