## Overview
[[GrahamHarman]] is presented in the source as the object-oriented ontology thinker whose vocabulary of real and sensual objects and qualities supports the article's theory of art and metaphor.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Harman is the main theoretical authority for the new aesthetics cluster. The essay attributes to him both the claim that objects withdraw from relations and the more technical schema of real objects, sensual objects, real qualities, and sensual qualities. It then uses his account to describe art as object-quality tension and metaphor as a drama in which the reader substitutes for the withdrawn object.

## Key Characteristics
- Associated with [[ObjectOrientedOntology]] in the source.
- Distinguishes real objects, sensual objects, real qualities, and sensual qualities.
- Treats object relations as partial translations rather than exhaustive revelations.
- Extends object-oriented ontology into aesthetic accounts of art and metaphor.
- Provides the source's claim that readers perform the role of the real object in metaphor.

## Evidence
- OOO role: [[cong-hai-de-ge-er-de-chui-zi-dao-yin-yu-de-xi-ju-xing]] presents Harman as the thinker whose terms organize the essay.
- Fourfold schema: [[cong-hai-de-ge-er-de-chui-zi-dao-yin-yu-de-xi-ju-xing]] summarizes real/sensual objects and real/sensual qualities.
- Art account: [[cong-hai-de-ge-er-de-chui-zi-dao-yin-yu-de-xi-ju-xing]] says Harman follows Ortega in seeing art as access to the life of things through RO-SQ tension.
- Metaphor account: [[cong-hai-de-ge-er-de-chui-zi-dao-yin-yu-de-xi-ju-xing]] cites Harman's Object-Oriented Ontology for the idea that the reader becomes the substitute real object.

## Qualifications
This profile is limited to the essay's use of Harman. It does not evaluate his broader work, his relation to speculative realism, or philosophical objections to object-oriented ontology.
