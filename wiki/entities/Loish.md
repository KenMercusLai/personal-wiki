## Overview
[[Loish]] is a digital artist and animator represented in the wiki through a 2016 discussion of artistic development and [[ArtBlock]].

## Current Profile
The available source presents Loish as both a visual example of long-term craft development and a practitioner offering psychologically modest advice. Her paired 2004 and 2016 drawings show changes in anatomy, volume, gesture, detail, and line confidence. She recommends comparing current work with one's own past work, rather than only with professionals, so that visible progress can interrupt perfectionism and fear of failure.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses redraws of older sketches to make skill development visible.
- Names volume, gesture, and confident linework as areas that improved over time.
- Connects art block with unrealistically high expectations and fear of failure.
- Recommends self-comparison across time as an alternative to harsh professional comparison.
- Frames reduced self-criticism as one practical response to creative blockage.

## Qualifications
The wiki currently has one secondary article quoting Loish rather than her complete original post or a broader account of her career. The selected before-and-after drawings document difference but do not isolate the effects of daily practice, instruction, feedback, time, or selection. Her advice is practitioner testimony, not clinical guidance for persistent anxiety or mental-health conditions.
