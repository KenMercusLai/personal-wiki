## Overview
[[Wayfair]] is an online home-goods retailer used in the source as a comparison case for dedicated large-item delivery capacity.

## Current Profile
In the May 2018 snapshot, Wayfair said its Wayfair Delivery Network operated in 22 metropolitan areas and was designed for large, bulky, and fragile home goods such as armoires, sheds, and vanities. The company presented the network as a response to rising consumer expectations for convenient service across increasingly difficult product categories and cited a 1,050-pound outdoor pizza oven as one extreme shipment.

## Key Characteristics
- Online retailer focused in the source on bulky and fragile home goods.
- Operated a dedicated large-item delivery network in selected metropolitan areas.
- Treated delivery capability as part of the customer experience rather than only outsourced transport.
- Used as evidence that oversized e-commerce requires category-specific infrastructure.
