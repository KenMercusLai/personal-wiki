## Overview
[[ElenaVerna]] is represented as a growth practitioner using open-ended estimation and strength-oriented role design in interviews.

## Current Profile
In the 2016 source, Verna is affiliated with [[SurveyMonkey]]. She uses a Golden Gate Bridge toll estimate to observe how candidates choose variables and reason under uncertainty, not to demand one correct answer. She also argues that hiring should identify exceptional strengths and consider whether a role can be shaped around them instead of focusing only on weaknesses or an urgent organizational gap.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses open-ended estimation to observe reasoning under uncertainty.
- Evaluates process and curiosity rather than one predetermined answer.
- Looks for candidate strengths and potential upside alongside liabilities.
- Treats role fit as dynamic because growth responsibilities change with the business.

- Strength orientation: [[growth-interview-questions-from-atlassian-surveymonkey-gusto-and-hubspot-guest-post-at-andrewchen]] attributes to Verna the advice to identify excellence and align or shape the role around it.
