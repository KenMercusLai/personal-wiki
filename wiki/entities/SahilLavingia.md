## Overview
[[SahilLavingia]] is represented as [[Gumroad]]'s founder and the author of a first-person account of its transition from a venture-style full-time startup to a profitable, contractor-only, asynchronous company.

## Current Profile
Lavingia says a failed 2015 fundraising attempt and subsequent layoffs reduced Gumroad from 23 full-time employees to himself. Burnout and the desire to reclaim time then shaped a company model built around no routine meetings or internal deadlines, extensive writing, hourly contractors, incremental shipping, and creator earnings as a single north star.

His account explicitly rejects universal imitation: the model emerged accidentally, retains exceptions such as tax deadlines, and trades conventional career progression and benefits for autonomy. As both founder and beneficiary, he presents a coherent rationale and company metrics but does not supply independent employee sampling or causal comparison.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded Gumroad in 2011 and remained responsible through its post-layoff restructuring.
- Reframed the company objective from growth at all costs to freedom at all costs.
- Favors written asynchronous coordination and contributor autonomy over recurring meetings, deadlines, and quarterly goal systems.
- Uses creator earnings as Gumroad's reported north-star outcome.
- Treats reduced working time as an opportunity for creative practice and life outside the company.
- Publicly describes pay rates, hiring trials, benefits gaps, and the limitations of Gumroad's model.

## Evidence
- Company transition: [[no-meetings-no-deadlines-no-full-time-employees]] says Lavingia led Gumroad from 23 full-time employees in 2015 through layoffs and a return to a one-person company in 2016.
- Operating philosophy: [[no-meetings-no-deadlines-no-full-time-employees]] links his burnout and move to Utah with no-meeting, no-deadline norms and the phrase "freedom at all costs."
- Coordination and product work: [[no-meetings-no-deadlines-no-full-time-employees]] describes shared queues, written communication, a 24-hour response norm, creator earnings as the north star, and incremental releases.
- Employment design: [[no-meetings-no-deadlines-no-full-time-employees]] reports hourly worldwide rates, internal pay transparency, an unpaid challenge, a paid trial, no company benefits, and few promotion paths.
- Transfer boundary: [[no-meetings-no-deadlines-no-full-time-employees]] says the system arose by accident and should not be copied wholesale.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to one founder-authored January 2021 essay. Company metrics, employee preferences, productivity claims, and attributed quotations are not independently verified here. The account may understate selection effects, worker dependence, classification and legal differences across jurisdictions, coordination failures, invisible management work, or contributors who did not thrive in the system.
