## Overview
[[TwelveUp]] is the American-sports media product that [[MichalTurjeman]] describes designing at [[MinuteMedia]] as an upgraded and updated version of [[NinetyMin]].

## Current Profile
The product serves as the practical case behind Turjeman's argument for designing outside one's own interests. Because she had little knowledge of American sports, the assignment made basic questioning, personas, separation from personal preference, and comparison of alternative solutions especially visible parts of the design process.

## Key Characteristics
- Targets American-sports audiences in the source's account.
- Inherits its high-level product premise from 90min while requiring revision rather than direct copying.
- Was designed by a practitioner who did not personally match the intended sports-fan audience.
- Provides the case through which the source explains user-centered design under domain unfamiliarity.

## Evidence
- Product scope: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] says 12up is about American sports.
- Product lineage: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] describes the assignment as creating an upgraded and updated version of 90min.
- Design constraint: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] says Turjeman began with effectively no American-sports knowledge and therefore relied on questions, personas, and explicit option comparison.

## Qualifications
The source does not show the final product, identify which design decisions shipped, report direct user research, or measure audience response. The page therefore represents 12up primarily as a design case, not as a complete or current product profile.
