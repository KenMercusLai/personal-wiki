## Overview
[[HClaireBrown]] is the journalist who reported the source's investigation into [[Grubhub]] and [[Seamless]] domain registrations, restaurant microsites, and phone-order commissions.

## Current Profile
Brown's reporting combines a restaurant operator's account, invoice inspection, domain-registration research, example-site comparisons, company testimony, and Grubhub's response. The article uses those materials to examine how a delivery platform can turn restaurant-branded discovery into commission-bearing traffic while distinguishing verified documents and company statements from owner inference.

## Key Characteristics
- Investigated more than 23,000 Grubhub-registered domains and additional Seamless registrations associated with restaurant names.
- Connected domain registration, search visibility, forwarding numbers, and commission attribution into one platform-control mechanism.
- Included restaurant owners with materially different views of whether platform-created web presence helped or harmed them.
- Preserved Grubhub's denial of cybersquatting and its claim that contracts authorized the practice.
