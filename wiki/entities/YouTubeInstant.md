## Overview
[[YouTubeInstant]] is the 2010 side project cited by Hack Career as a rare project that became remarkable enough to generate unsolicited job interest.

## Current Profile
The source uses YouTube Instant as the archetypal "Field of Dreams" side project: a small but striking artifact that attracted media attention and a direct job offer. Its role in the wiki is not as a product case study but as a boundary case for side-project career advice, showing why candidates should not assume that merely building something will create attention.

## Key Characteristics

- Became media-visible enough to generate a direct YouTube job offer.
- Functions in the source as an exceptional side-project outcome, not the default benchmark.
