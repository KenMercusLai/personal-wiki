## Overview
[[SimonShaw]] is a cofounder of [[TrinityMcQueen]] and the author represented here through a 2019 Behavioral Scientist article about consumer responses to familiar commercial nudges.

## Current Profile
Shaw approaches behavioral science as a market-research practitioner attentive to how consumers interpret the intent behind influence tactics. He uses qualitative observation and a national experiment to argue that intervention effects change as audiences learn, contexts evolve, and repeated tactics acquire manipulative associations.

## Key Characteristics
- Combines qualitative customer interviews with population-level experimental research.
- Focuses on the trust and emotional effects of marketing tactics, not only immediate transactions.
- Treats heuristics and intervention efficacy as dynamic outcomes of feedback between people and their environment.
- Calls for practitioners to consider public welfare and the shared reputation of behavioral science.

## Evidence
- Research role: [[consumers-are-becoming-wise-to-your-nudge-behavioral-scientist]] identifies Shaw's Trinity McQueen team as conducting the February 2019 British study.
- Mixed-method perspective: [[consumers-are-becoming-wise-to-your-nudge-behavioral-scientist]] begins with a customer interview and develops the observation into an experiment with 2,102 adults.
- Ethical stance: [[consumers-are-becoming-wise-to-your-nudge-behavioral-scientist]] argues that marketers owe responsibilities to the public and to other practitioners whose tools may be weakened by overuse.
