## Overview
[[BillSimmons]] is the sportswriter and founder used by [[BenThompson]] to illustrate how an individual creator brand can own its audience and editorial operation while relying on shared publishing infrastructure.

## Current Profile
The source presents Simmons as an exceptional writer with a large following who built [[TheRinger]] as an independent sports-and-culture publication and podcast network. His partnership with [[VoxMedia]] preserves ownership and editorial independence while outsourcing technology and advertising sales, making him the creator-side case for [[FacelessPublishingInfrastructure]].

## Key Characteristics
- Built a creator-led sports and culture brand around an existing personal audience.
- Uses writing to acquire attention and podcasts as a stronger monetization format.
- Retains ownership and editorial independence while sharing backend capabilities.
- Benefits from a scaled advertising seller without building a duplicate sales organization.

## Evidence
- Creator leverage: [[faceless-publishers-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] identifies Simmons as a writer whose personal following can support a separate media brand.
- Cross-format model: [[faceless-publishers-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] reports his claim that podcast revenue more than covered both the podcasts and website.
- Independence: [[faceless-publishers-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] says Simmons retained The Ringer's ownership and editorial control under the Vox Media partnership.
- Shared operations: [[faceless-publishers-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] quotes Simmons emphasizing Vox Media's sales and technology capabilities.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one 2017 strategic essay, which reports Simmons's revenue characterization without audited figures and does not establish the partnership's later performance.
