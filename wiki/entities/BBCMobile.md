## Overview
[[BBCMobile]] is the source's product/distribution case for extending BBC reach through IP-based content distribution beyond conventional broadcast contexts.

## Current Profile
The source presents BBC Mobile as emerging from Alex Pressland's earlier "BBC Out Of Home" work. That work used IP-based syndication and venue-specific experiments to show that BBC content could be distributed into contexts beyond homes, cars, TVs, and radios. The case emphasizes product management as institutional change: new technology, audience measurement, editorial norms, and legal licensing had to move together.

## Key Characteristics
- Grew from BBC Out Of Home and IP-based syndicated content experimentation.
- Targeted audiences not reached through conventional broadcast channels.
- Used tailored content for specific venues and contexts rather than replaying ordinary TV programming.
- Required persuasion across editorial and legal cultures.
- Became a large-reach mobile offering in the source's account.

## Evidence
- Out-of-home origin: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says Pressland proposed BBC Out Of Home after experiments with syndicated content.
- Reach strategy: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says she looked for UK audiences not reached by conventional broadcast media.
- Contextual content: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says venue screens needed content tailored to place and audience rather than home-TV content.
- Organizational barriers: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] identifies editorial and legal obstacles.
- Mobile impact: [[behind-every-great-product-silicon-valley-product-group]] says the work became the basis for BBC Mobile and reached more than 50 million weekly users worldwide.

## Qualifications
The source does not provide independent audience data, detailed product architecture, later BBC app history, or full rights/licensing context. Its reach figure is reported as part of a retrospective product-management story.
