## Overview
[[JohnLuo]] is a psychiatrist and University of California professor of psychiatry and medical informatics cited for practical cautions about disclosing online search findings to patients.

## Current Profile
The source uses Luo to show the interpersonal and informatics risks of patient-targeted online search. He notes that some patients directly ask clinicians to view online material, such as YouTube pages, because it may reveal mood, speech, and thought organization. But he also warns that clinicians must consider how disclosure affects the therapeutic relationship, particularly with paranoid patients, and he highlights medical records as valuable targets for data theft.
