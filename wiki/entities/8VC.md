## Overview
[[8VC]] appears in the wiki as the venture-firm and publication context for [[JoeLonsdale]]'s startup-hiring essay "Bring in the Adults?"

## Current Profile
The source presents 8VC through its advice to CEOs about seniority, experience, and executive hiring in technology startups. Its relevant wiki role is not portfolio analysis but operating guidance: early startups should avoid over-importing mature-company process, while scaling startups should add experienced leaders once repeatable functions need ownership.
