## Overview
[[Ethereum]] is the blockchain ecosystem whose early usability, DAO crisis, ENS launch, ICO boom, and asset-value growth form the operating context for the MyEtherWallet-to-MyCrypto story.

## Current Profile
The source treats Ethereum less as a protocol specification than as a fast-growing community and operational environment. In 2015, it was small enough that wallet builders could talk directly with early users; by 2017, rising Ether values, ICO participation, phishing pressure, ENS time windows, and the DAO aftermath turned wallet UX and support into high-stakes infrastructure work.

## Key Characteristics
- Began the source's story as a small, tight-knit, optimistic community.
- Initially lacked approachable wallet interaction for ordinary users, creating space for [[MyEtherWallet]].
- Experienced the DAO hard fork and DAO withdrawal needs as a major 2016 ecosystem event.
- Saw explosive 2017 growth through Ether price increases, ICOs, and ENS-related workflows.
- Made wallet mistakes unusually costly because user errors or phishing could produce irreversible fund loss.

## Evidence
- Early usability gap: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says early Ethereum users otherwise had to send Ether through the command line.
- Community context: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] describes the early Ethereum community as small, tight-knit, and optimistic.
- DAO aftermath: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says MEW built a DAO withdrawal interface after the hard fork.
- 2017 pressure: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] ties support and infrastructure stress to ICOs, ENS, phishing, and rising Ether value.
