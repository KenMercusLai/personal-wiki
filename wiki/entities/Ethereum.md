## Overview
[[Ethereum]] is a blockchain ecosystem represented in the wiki through wallet-infrastructure growth pressure, as a possible but throughput-limited retail cryptocurrency option, and as an exchange-listed asset whose market structure exposed Coinbase to flash-crash risk.

## Current Profile
The MyCrypto source treats Ethereum less as a protocol specification than as a fast-growing community and operational environment. In 2015, it was small enough that wallet builders could talk directly with early users; by 2017, rising Ether values, ICO participation, phishing pressure, ENS time windows, and the DAO aftermath turned wallet UX and support into high-stakes infrastructure work. Coin and Crypto adds a different, much thinner view: Ethereum is grouped with Bitcoin as a major cryptocurrency whose then-cited transaction speed would not satisfy [[Amazon]]-scale checkout demand. Fortune adds an exchange-market-structure view: Coinbase's June 21, 2017 Ethereum flash crash showed that crypto trading venues needed safeguards comparable to circuit breakers when a large sell order triggered automated liquidations.

## Key Characteristics
- Began the source's story as a small, tight-knit, optimistic community.
- Initially lacked approachable wallet interaction for ordinary users, creating space for [[MyEtherWallet]].
- Experienced the DAO hard fork and DAO withdrawal needs as a major 2016 ecosystem event.
- Saw explosive 2017 growth through Ether price increases, ICOs, and ENS-related workflows.
- Made wallet mistakes unusually costly because user errors or phishing could produce irreversible fund loss.
- Was cited by Coin and Crypto as a major cryptocurrency but too slow for Amazon-scale retail payments at the article's stated 15 transactions per second.
- Was listed on Coinbase early enough that a whale sale on June 21, 2017 exposed missing exchange trip-wire controls.

## Evidence
- Wallet-infrastructure pressure: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says early Ethereum users otherwise had to send Ether through the command line, and later ICOs, ENS, phishing, and rising Ether value created severe support and security pressure.
- Community and DAO context: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] describes the early community as small and optimistic, then names the DAO aftermath as a major workflow that MEW supported.
- Retail-payment comparison: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] lists Ethereum at 15 transactions per second and treats that as insufficient for Amazon's peak transaction volume.
- Exchange-market stress: [[coinbase-wants-to-be-too-big-to-fail-fortune]] says an Ethereum whale sale drove prices from $320 to under 10 cents on June 21, 2017 before rebounding, prompting Coinbase to cancel victims' side of trades.

## Qualifications
This page reflects Ethereum through a wallet-founder retrospective, a speculative 2017 retail-payments article, and a Coinbase exchange incident, not a general technical, economic, or governance history of the protocol. The payment-throughput comparison and flash-crash details are source-scoped and should not be treated as current performance or market-structure assessments.
