## Overview
[[BizStone]] appears in the wiki as the co-founder and CEO of [[Jelly]] reflecting on practical CEO lessons drawn from his new role and from prior years working around other CEOs.

## Current Profile
Stone presents himself as new to the formal CEO role when he and Ben founded Jelly, but not new to observing executive behavior. His source is explicitly provisional: he offers five lessons that may evolve, grounded in experience at companies such as [[Twitter]] and [[Google]] and in the personal discomfort of CEO responsibilities such as firing. His leadership model emphasizes frequent communication, broad advice-seeking, cultural protection, recognition, and the willingness to decide without complete certainty.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-CEO using early Jelly experience to articulate practical operating principles.
- Treats communication as a fear-reduction and alignment responsibility.
- Uses outside advice selectively rather than treating CEO authority as self-sufficiency.
- Frames firing as an uncomfortable but necessary duty when behavior or performance damages the company.
- Values praise and recognition as meaningful beyond monetary rewards.
- Defines leadership as decisive movement under uncertainty rather than always knowing the answer.

## Evidence
- CEO role: [[ceo-advice-from-a-new-ceo-the-biz-stone-collection-medium]] says Stone became CEO when he and Ben founded [[Jelly]].
- Communication and context: [[ceo-advice-from-a-new-ceo-the-biz-stone-collection-medium]] argues that even small companies need frequent CEO communication because missing knowledge creates fear.
- Advice-seeking: [[ceo-advice-from-a-new-ceo-the-biz-stone-collection-medium]] describes soliciting outside launch advice for Jelly and acting on the musician's advice to temper utopian brand rhetoric.
- Culture protection: [[ceo-advice-from-a-new-ceo-the-biz-stone-collection-medium]] says a high-performing but poisonous employee can damage the whole company if not removed.
- Recognition: [[ceo-advice-from-a-new-ceo-the-biz-stone-collection-medium]] recalls a [[Google]] peer-recognition bonus where the praise mattered more than the money.
- Decisiveness: [[ceo-advice-from-a-new-ceo-the-biz-stone-collection-medium]] uses Captain Picard as a model for leading without all the answers.

## Qualifications
The page is based on a single 2016 personal essay, so it should be read as Stone's self-described leadership philosophy at that time rather than a full biography or independent assessment of his tenure at Jelly, Twitter, or Google.
