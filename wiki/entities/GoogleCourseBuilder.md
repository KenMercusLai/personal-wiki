## Overview
[[GoogleCourseBuilder]] is the online-course platform discussed in the source through Google's Mapping with Google course.

## Current Profile
The article uses Google Course Builder as a case where large-scale learner analytics were reduced to simple activity-completion variables. Reich highlights that the research compared learners who did or did not complete activities with final-project completion, again finding that students who completed activities were more likely to complete the course project.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents Google's online-course infrastructure in the article's MOOC analytics critique.
- Provides a case where rich analytics were simplified into binary activity variables.
- Links activity completion with final-project completion in the cited Mapping with Google study.
