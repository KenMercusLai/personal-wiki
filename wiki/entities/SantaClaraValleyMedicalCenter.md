## Overview
[[SantaClaraValleyMedicalCenter]] is the initial treating hospital in the reported transfer case involving [[AnneWojcicki]]'s critically ill friend.

## Current Profile
The article says the patient was told at Valley Medical that he had run out of options, prompting his family to seek a second opinion at [[StanfordMedicalCenter]]. It reports that the family was told medical records would take five to ten business days to obtain, obstructing evaluation and specialist discovery. Valley Medical declined to discuss the individual case because of privacy rules and said generally that its electronic records travel and transfer between doctors.
