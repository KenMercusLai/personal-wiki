## Overview
[[FundersClub]] is the venture-capital organization that published the wiki's guide to evaluating and maintaining co-founder fit.

## Current Profile
In this source, FundersClub presents itself as drawing on reviews of thousands of technology startups, its portfolio, and a network of founders and investors. Its role is primarily editorial and advisory: it assembles practitioner examples into a framework spanning relationship history, capability testing, complementary roles, strategic alignment, equity, communication, and support. It also supplies a descriptive chart of 177 portfolio companies with founder-count data, without connecting team size to investment or company outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes practitioner guidance for startup founders.
- Frames founder relationships as material operating and investment risk.
- Uses founder and investor anecdotes to build prescriptive advice.
- Supplies portfolio-level descriptive data about founding-team size.

## Evidence
- Advisory role: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] presents FundersClub as a venture-capital organization sharing lessons from startup review, investment, founders, and investors.
- Relationship framework: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] organizes advice around trust, capabilities, roles, success definitions, equity, recurring time together, and mutual support.
- Portfolio data: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] charts founder counts for 177 FundersClub portfolio companies, with two founders the largest category at 51%.
- Practitioner method: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] relies on named founder stories and recommendations rather than a controlled comparative study.
