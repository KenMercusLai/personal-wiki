## Overview
[[FundersClub]] is a venture-capital organization represented in the wiki through practitioner guides to co-founder fit and startup operations automation.

## Current Profile
Across the two sources, FundersClub presents itself as drawing on reviews of thousands of technology startups, its portfolio, and a network of founders and investors. Its role is editorial and advisory: it assembles named practitioner examples into frameworks for founder relationships and operational automation. The co-founder guide spans trust, capability testing, complementary roles, alignment, equity, communication, and support; the automation guide spans marketing, sales, billing, manufacturing design, and software delivery while retaining human oversight. Both are prescriptive and illustrative rather than causal studies of startup outcomes.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes practitioner guidance for startup founders.
- Frames founder relationships as material operating and investment risk.
- Treats selective automation as a way to protect attention, capacity, and organizational agility.
- Uses founder and operator anecdotes to build prescriptive advice.
- Supplies portfolio-level descriptive data about founding-team size.

## Evidence
- Advisory role: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] presents FundersClub as a venture-capital organization sharing lessons from startup review, investment, founders, and investors.
- Relationship framework: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] organizes advice around trust, capabilities, roles, success definitions, equity, recurring time together, and mutual support.
- Portfolio data: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] charts founder counts for 177 FundersClub portfolio companies, with two founders the largest category at 51%.
- Automation framework: [[founders-guide-to-automation-fundersclub]] recommends automating stable recurring work while preserving monitoring, escalation, and personal attention where judgment or learning matters.
- Practitioner method: [[a-guide-to-co-founder-fit-fundersclub]] and [[founders-guide-to-automation-fundersclub]] rely on selected stories, recommendations, and self-reported figures rather than controlled comparative studies.

## Qualifications
The two historical guides do not characterize FundersClub's broader investment thesis, current portfolio, performance, organization, or present-day practices. The startup-review claim, portfolio chart, company scale figures, and automation outcomes are self-reported. The sources do not explain case selection, missing data, maintenance costs, failure rates, company outcomes, or whether the examples are representative.

## What Changed
- Expanded the profile from co-founder advice into operational automation guidance.
- Added the boundary between automation and human judgment as a recurring advisory theme.
