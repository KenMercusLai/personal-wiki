## Overview
[[Wistia]] is presented as a B2B video-hosting and marketing company that grew in [[YouTube]]'s shadow by serving business needs the broader consumer platform did not prioritize.

## Current Profile
The source frames Wistia less as a head-to-head YouTube competitor than as a specialist that benefited from YouTube's market-making work. YouTube made web video normal, set performance expectations, priced broad hosting at free, and inspired businesses to use video; Wistia used that demand to build paid tools for businesses that wanted lead capture, signup growth, education, support, integrations, and marketing-specific control.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates as a business-video specialist rather than a general consumer video platform.
- Benefited from [[YouTube]] making online video common, reliable, and culturally salient.
- Used YouTube's free anchor price as indirect protection because the market looked unattractive to many entrants.
- Built value through B2B features, learning content, support, integrations, and partnerships.
- Illustrates how a startup can grow in a niche that is too specialized for a giant to prioritize.

## Evidence
- Specialist position: [[building-a-business-in-the-shadow-of-a-giant-noteworthy-the-journal-blog]] says Wistia focused on business-video tools rather than pleasing everyone on the planet.
- Market creation: [[building-a-business-in-the-shadow-of-a-giant-noteworthy-the-journal-blog]] says YouTube made online video viable and important for businesses.
- Competitive shelter: [[building-a-business-in-the-shadow-of-a-giant-noteworthy-the-journal-blog]] says YouTube's free price made video hosting look unattractive to other entrants.
- B2B feature depth: [[building-a-business-in-the-shadow-of-a-giant-noteworthy-the-journal-blog]] says Wistia built tools for lead capture, signup growth, business education, support, integrations, and partnerships.
- Niche opportunity: [[building-a-business-in-the-shadow-of-a-giant-noteworthy-the-journal-blog]] argues that business video hosting can be too small for YouTube to prioritize while still large enough for Wistia.
