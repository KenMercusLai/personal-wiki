## Overview
[[TheSleepRevolution]] is [[AriannaHuffington]]'s book and social-media campaign context for the interview's argument that sleep should be treated as a cultural, scientific, and workplace priority.

## Current Profile
The interview describes The Sleep Revolution as the result of Huffington's post-exhaustion study of sleep, rest, technology boundaries, and breaks. It is framed as an attempt to use sleep science to legitimize rest in a culture that often treats overwork as a badge of seriousness. Within the wiki, the entity currently functions as the media artifact behind Huffington's broader [[SleepAsPerformanceEnhancer]] argument.

## Key Characteristics
- Book and social-media campaign associated with [[AriannaHuffington]].
- Emerged from Huffington's own ill health after long hours and insufficient sleep.
- Synthesizes research by Huffington and scientists on sleep's importance.
- Presents sleep science as a way to change work and productivity culture.

## Evidence

- Scientific framing: [[arianna-huffington-on-sleep-mornings-and-work-the-early-hour]] says the book draws on research by Huffington and scientists and argues that sleep science legitimizes sleep's benefits.
