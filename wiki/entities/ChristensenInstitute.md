## Overview
[[ChristensenInstitute]] is the think tank and source publisher represented in this wiki by its introductory page on [[JobsToBeDone]].

## Current Profile
Within the supplied source, the Christensen Institute presents Jobs to Be Done as a decision-research lens and applies it to social-sector innovation questions. Its examples concern Nigerian access to electricity, health-care leaders' understanding of patients and consumers, and parental demand for microschools, positioning the framework as broader than commercial product marketing.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes practitioner explanations of innovation theory.
- Frames choice around desired progress and changing circumstances.
- Applies Jobs to Be Done across education, health care, and economic-development settings.
- Advocates attention to functional, social, and emotional dimensions of decisions.

## Evidence
- Theory publisher: [[christensen-institute-jobs-to-be-done-theory]] defines and explains the Jobs to Be Done lens.
- Application range: [[christensen-institute-jobs-to-be-done-theory]] discusses solar adoption in Nigeria, health-care customer needs, and microschool enrollment.
- Research emphasis: [[christensen-institute-jobs-to-be-done-theory]] recommends uncovering decision stories and shared circumstances rather than relying only on direct explanations.
