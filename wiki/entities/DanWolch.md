## Overview
[[DanWolch]] is presented as a product manager who developed and documented a behavior-segmented customer-feedback workflow while working on [[HubSpot]] sales products.

## Current Profile
In the available 2016 article, Wolch argues that talking to more customers is insufficient when a growing product has hundreds of thousands of active users. He selects respondents around activation or retention questions, extracts individual event histories, asks for direct email replies, follows up on explanations, and manually codes recurring themes. He credits [[BrianBalfour]] with teaching him the process and presents inexpensive tools as a way to shorten the distance between product teams, user behavior, and actionable feedback.

## Key Characteristics
- Product manager associated with HubSpot sales products in the source period.
- Advocates question-led selection of customers rather than reliance on loud or convenient feedback.
- Combines behavioral analytics with direct qualitative outreach and follow-up.
- Favors low-friction tools and a short analytical pass over elaborate research infrastructure.

## Evidence
- Work context: [[identify-users-with-the-most-valuable-feedback-startup-grind-medium]] says Wolch worked on HubSpot sales products as they grew to hundreds of thousands of active users.
- Sampling method: [[identify-users-with-the-most-valuable-feedback-startup-grind-medium]] segments super-active, drive-by, and engaged-but-lapsed users from event histories.
- Outreach method: [[identify-users-with-the-most-valuable-feedback-startup-grind-medium]] describes personal reply-by-email requests, follow-up questioning, and manual feedback buckets.

## Qualifications
The profile is bounded to one self-authored 2016 article. It does not independently verify Wolch's role, the claimed active-user scale, response rates, or the effect of his method on product decisions and outcomes, and it should not be read as a complete career profile.
