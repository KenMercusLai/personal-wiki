## Overview
[[MurrayDavis]] is presented as a sociologist whose 1971 analysis asked why some theories attract broad attention while others remain obscure.

## Current Profile
Through Kontny's summary, Davis argues that interesting propositions commonly deny an accepted assumption: what appears to be X is actually non-X. The marketing essay adapts this descriptive account of attention to a prescriptive creative filter, moving beyond Davis's original scientific-theory context. That transfer is suggestive but not validated by the source as a general causal law of virality.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies perceived interestingness rather than treating truth alone as the explanation for intellectual spread.
- Identifies negation of accepted assumptions as a recurring proposition form.
- Provides a theoretical lens later adapted by Kontny to marketing and filmmaking.
- Appears only through a secondary summary and short excerpt in the ingested source.
