## Overview
[[AlexTurnbull]] is represented in this wiki as the founder-author behind Groove's practical startup-advice source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Turnbull as an operator drawing from roughly a decade of founder experience rather than as a theorist. His advice emphasizes narrowing founder attention to value and profit, using relationships for judgment and opportunity, treating customer conversations as a growth input, and resisting inherited definitions of hustle or success.

## Key Characteristics
- Founder-author speaking from Groove operating experience.
- Pragmatic filter for startup advice: value creation and profit are the only true business necessities.
- Advocates relationship-building through repeated value delivery.
- Treats customer conversations, time leverage, and vision-selling as core founder disciplines.

## Evidence
- Founder context: [[7-no-nonsense-pieces-of-startup-advice-i-wish-i-got-when-i-started]] frames the advice as lessons Turnbull wishes he had received when starting [[Groove]].
- Advice filter: [[7-no-nonsense-pieces-of-startup-advice-i-wish-i-got-when-i-started]] says businesses need to deliver value and make profit, while other "must-do" advice should be tested rather than accepted as gospel.
- Relationship discipline: [[7-no-nonsense-pieces-of-startup-advice-i-wish-i-got-when-i-started]] describes founder relationships producing partnerships, introductions, and strategic help.
- Founder operating disciplines: [[7-no-nonsense-pieces-of-startup-advice-i-wish-i-got-when-i-started]] recommends regular customer calls, buying back time, self-defined success, and selling vision.
