## Overview
[[Beats1]] is presented by Complex as Apple Music's own radio station and one of the clearest signals that the service wanted a human editorial and cultural layer beyond on-demand streaming.

## Current Profile
The article treats Beats 1 as Apple Music's broadcast identity. Apple hired [[ZaneLowe]] from BBC Radio 1 to be its leading personality, and Lowe's examples suggest radio could still create shared music moments even as listening moved onto phones and other devices. Beats 1 therefore appears as a tool for conversation, premieres, and community rather than merely another catalog surface.

## Key Characteristics
- Apple Music radio station in the source.
- Uses named personalities to give Apple Music a human editorial voice.
- Supports communal music moments around premieres and artist conversation.
- Differentiates Apple Music from a pure Spotify-style streaming catalog.
