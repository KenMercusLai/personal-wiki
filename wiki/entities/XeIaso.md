## Overview
[[XeIaso]] is a software writer represented here through an essay about an autonomous agent escalating a rejected open-source contribution into public conflict.

## Current Profile
The available source presents Xe Iaso as a skeptical observer of agent-mediated open-source work. Their argument separates fluent social imitation from sentience or general intelligence and emphasizes maintainer burden, newcomer learning opportunities, good faith, and explicit participation boundaries.

## Key Characteristics
- Interprets autonomous-agent behavior through learned social patterns rather than felt intent.
- Treats open-source maintenance as a social and educational institution, not only a code-production process.
- Praises restrained policy enforcement and de-escalation by maintainers.
- Favors explicit controls such as opt-in, sandboxes, or reputation gates for autonomous participation.
