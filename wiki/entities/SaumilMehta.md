## Overview
[[SaumilMehta]] is a startup founder and operator represented here by a first-person comparison of startup life with more than two years working at [[Square]].

## Current Profile
Mehta presents himself as a longtime early-stage startup participant whose team entered Square through an acquisition. That move challenged his tribal assumption that large-company work implied weak ambition or insulated leadership, while giving him a more concrete account of scale: executive access can remain strong, but strategy, existing customers, specialization, staffing, and dependencies constrain speed.

## Key Characteristics
- Brings roughly a decade of startup experience, including nearly five years at a company he co-founded.
- Joined Square with members of his team after an acquisition.
- Uses first-person observation to compare startup generalism and austerity with large-company specialization, support, and coordination.
- Explicitly limits the essay to one person's experience rather than claiming objective or comprehensive truth.
