## Overview
[[HenriPrade]] is an artificial-intelligence researcher represented in this wiki as coauthor of a possibility-theoretic and nonmonotonic account of how listeners revise beliefs when understanding jokes.

## Current Profile
In the current source, Prade and [[FlorenceDupinDeSaintCyr]] connect AGM/Katsuno-Mendelzon belief revision with default rules and ordinal possibility distributions. They use that bridge to define surprise, revelation, potential funniness, and norm-relative incongruity while emphasizing that the result captures ingredients of humor rather than laughter as a whole.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses possibility orderings to connect default reasoning with belief revision.
- Applies formal uncertainty and knowledge-representation methods to joke comprehension.
- Preserves listener dependence and explicit limits in the proposed humor model.

## Evidence
- Research role: [[2309.02009]] identifies Prade as coauthor of the represented model.
- Technical contribution: [[2309.02009]] constructs plausibility preorders from strict and default knowledge and uses them to analyze context-punchline pairs.
- Qualified interpretation: [[2309.02009]] states that surprise, revelation, and incongrity remain insufficient for a complete or unique account of humor.
