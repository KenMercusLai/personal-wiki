## Overview
[[JocelynGoldfein]] is the author of a practitioner essay connecting organizational culture to observable rewards, consequences, and role models.

## Current Profile
Goldfein recounts participating in a 2008 [[VMware]] leadership offsite during a period of rapid growth. She uses [[CharlesOReilly]]'s exercise and examples of public recognition, assignments, promotion, marginalization, and executive behavior to explain how employees infer an organization's actual norms.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from firsthand participation in VMware's high-growth leadership context.
- Distinguishes professed values from the behaviors that visibly produce success.
- Allocates cultural influence across individual contributors, managers, founders, and CEOs.
