## Overview
[[NextVR]] is presented as a live and on-demand virtual-reality programming company and the [[NBA]]'s production partner for a recurring slate of immersive basketball broadcasts and highlights.

## Current Profile
The 2017 source describes NextVR as specializing in sports, concerts, and award shows. Its multi-year NBA agreement expanded the league's earlier experiments into one live League Pass game per week, 25 total broadcasts covering all 30 teams, and on-demand Finals highlight packages designed to approximate a courtside perspective.

The partnership's strategic value was not only distribution. NBA executive [[JeffMarsilio]] argued that committing to one partner and a repeated schedule allowed each production to improve, making NextVR part of a cumulative broadcast-learning process. The source provides no NextVR audience, cost, reliability, or financial measures.

## Key Characteristics
- Produced both live and on-demand immersive programming.
- Served as the NBA's anchor partner for repeatable rather than isolated VR broadcasts.
- Supported a recurring production schedule intended to create learning across games.
- Offered a remote courtside perspective while remaining complementary to television and mobile.

## Evidence
- Partnership scope: [[inside-the-nbas-virtual-reality-strategy]] reports a multi-year agreement, weekly live League Pass games, 25 broadcasts, all-team coverage, and Finals highlight packages.
- Learning role: [[inside-the-nbas-virtual-reality-strategy]] quotes Marsilio linking single-partner commitment and scheduling to improvement after every game.
- Product boundary: [[inside-the-nbas-virtual-reality-strategy]] presents the experience as immersive and access-expanding but not equivalent to arena attendance or a near-term substitute for conventional viewing.

## Qualifications
This profile reflects a 2017 partner-centered article and not NextVR's complete business history or later corporate outcome. It contains no independent comparison of production quality, viewer comfort, latency, camera constraints, customer retention, costs, or economics.

## What Changed
- Established NextVR as the NBA's recurring live-production and Finals-highlight partner.
- Identified repeated production learning as a core purpose of the partnership.
- Kept audience and commercial performance unresolved.
