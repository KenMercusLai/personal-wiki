## Overview
[[Leetao]], credited as LeetaoGoooo on the source page, is a blogger and software creator represented through a reflection on AI-assisted idea validation, creative motivation, and narrow product scope.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Leetao uses AI to discuss ideas, assess feasibility, define minimal experiments, and evaluate results through observable checkpoints. He also describes a limit to that practice: repeated negative research and experiment conclusions made abandonment easy enough that analysis began displacing hands-on craft. He responded by restarting [[Memox|memox]], cutting most of its code, and releasing [[VoiceFloat]] as a single-purpose product.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses AI as a partner for feasibility discussion and minimum-experiment design.
- Prefers observable and testable steps over ambiguous conclusions.
- Values empirical feedback while recognizing that filtering can suppress creative practice.
- Treats radical scope reduction and shipping as ways to recover momentum.

## Evidence
- Validation workflow: [[guo-qing-sui-bi-leetao]] describes moving from an idea through AI discussion, a minimum experiment, and a complete feedback loop.
- Creative tension: [[guo-qing-sui-bi-leetao]] says repeated AI-supported reasons to abandon ideas left him thinking without practicing.
- Product response: [[guo-qing-sui-bi-leetao]] reports reviving memox after removing about 80% of its code and releasing VoiceFloat around one core task.
