## Overview
[[Leetao]], also credited as LeetaoGoooo, is a blogger and software creator represented through reflections on AI-assisted product validation, personal-agent engineering, creative motivation, and narrow product scope.

## Current Profile
Leetao uses AI to discuss ideas, assess feasibility, define minimal experiments, and evaluate results through observable checkpoints. He also describes a limit to that practice: repeated negative research and experiment conclusions made abandonment easy enough that analysis began displacing hands-on craft. He responded by restarting [[Memox|memox]], cutting most of its code, and releasing [[VoiceFloat]] as a single-purpose product.

His later agent work applies the same experimental posture to [[Kuafu]], a personal runtime inspired by OpenClaw, Bub, and Pi Mono. He iterated from sandboxed Telegram and model-routing experiments toward host CLI access, coding/review agents, durable lessons, and [[ToastPlan]] task tracking. The experience left him skeptical that framework changes can raise model competence indefinitely, but more confident that AI can accelerate the creative work he values even as manual coding recedes.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses AI as a partner for feasibility discussion and minimum-experiment design.
- Prefers observable and testable steps over ambiguous conclusions.
- Values empirical feedback while recognizing that filtering can suppress creative practice.
- Treats radical scope reduction and shipping as ways to recover momentum.
- Iterates on agent runtime, tool-access, memory, and collaboration architecture through working prototypes.
- Distinguishes the enjoyment of creation from the manual act of writing code.
