## Overview
[[SensorTower]] appears as a mobile app analytics firm whose App Intelligence data is used to analyze the durability of No. 1 U.S. iPhone download-chart rankings on the [[AppStore]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Sensor Tower as the measurement provider behind a two-year App Store ranking study. Its data covers first-time No. 1 U.S. iPhone download-chart apps in 2015 and 2016, then tracks how long those apps remained in the top 25. In the wiki, Sensor Tower therefore contributes a retention-like view of app-store visibility: reaching No. 1 can create attention, but most apps lose high chart placement quickly.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides App Intelligence data for mobile app ranking analysis.
- Measures U.S. iPhone App Store download-chart outcomes in the source.
- Frames No. 1 app rank as a visibility event whose durability can be measured over time.

## Evidence
- Data provider: [[74-of-no-1-iphone-apps-dont-last-a-month-in-the-top-25]] attributes the ranking analysis to Sensor Tower App Intelligence data.
- Sample definition: [[74-of-no-1-iphone-apps-dont-last-a-month-in-the-top-25]] tracks 53 apps that reached No. 1 for the first time on the U.S. App Store iPhone download chart in 2015 and 2016.
- Durability finding: [[74-of-no-1-iphone-apps-dont-last-a-month-in-the-top-25]] reports that 74% of those apps left the top 25 within one month and only 8% remained after three months.

## Qualifications
The source reports Sensor Tower's own App Intelligence findings and does not disclose the complete dataset, ranking methodology, category breakdown, acquisition-spend data, or later App Store ranking outcomes. The page should not be read as a full company profile for Sensor Tower.
