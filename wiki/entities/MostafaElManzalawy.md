## Overview
[[MostafaElManzalawy]] is the author of a 2017 [[LumenDatabase]] study of fake websites, backdated articles, and suspicious copyright notices aimed at removing unwanted pages from Google Search.

## Current Profile
In the source, El Manzalawy is a Sarah Lawrence College graduate and NYU Gallatin master's student studying technology and global wealth inequality, working as a Berkman Klein Center intern on data protection and privacy-law research for Lumen. His investigation combines notice discovery, sender and language comparison, domain-history records, web archives, Google removal outcomes, and press reports to identify likely instances of the [[StolenArticleScam]].

## Key Characteristics
- Researched a preliminary dataset of 42 suspicious DMCA notices targeting 52 URLs.
- Used multiple external chronology signals rather than accepting displayed publication dates at face value.
- Treated notice wording, punctuation, sender identity, and domain registration as coordination clues.
- Presented the project as unfinished and called for broader investigation and crowdsourced reporting.
