Sources: [54 Screwups as a Startup CEO]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/anand-sanwal-54-screwups-startup-ceo.md" >}})

CB Insights is the company at the center of Anand Sanwal's startup CEO reflection. The source describes it as a technology and product-driven company that, by the time of the article, had growing revenue, a larger team, and stronger execution than in its lean early years.

In the article, CB Insights becomes a case study in turning early startup improvisation into operating systems. Its lessons include writing down culture, improving onboarding, tightening hiring, moving from cheap pricing to value-based pricing, learning founder-led sales, building a conversational marketing voice, meeting customers in person, and making product priorities less reactive to competitors.

The source also notes CB Insights' earlier ChubbyBrain name, its newsletter growth, its data-driven research content, and the importance of explaining its data mining technology without letting marketing jargon obscure the customer value.
