## Overview
[[YY]] is a Chinese online entertainment and livestreaming platform used in the source as evidence that livestreaming can monetize primarily outside advertising.

## Current Profile
The source presents YY as an established Chinese livestreaming company whose revenue mix demonstrates the power of virtual goods and user payment. It is also part of the talent-professionalization story through the 1931 girl-band project and part of the globalization story through its stake in Bigo Live.

## Key Characteristics
- Demonstrates non-ad monetization in Chinese livestreaming.
- Shows platform-led professionalization of user-generated entertainment.
- Participates in the international expansion of Chinese livestreaming patterns.
- Serves as a contrast to ad-dependent digital media models.

## Evidence
- Advertising share: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says online advertising was 9.0%, 4.0%, and 1.1% of YY's net revenue in 2013, 2014, and 2015.
- Professional project: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says YY created the 18-member girl band 1931 from auditions.
- Globalization link: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says YY owns a large stake in Bigo Live, targeting Southeast Asia.

## Qualifications
The source uses YY as a 2016 example and does not provide full company financials, later strategy, or current market position.
