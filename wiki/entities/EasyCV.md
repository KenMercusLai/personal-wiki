## Overview
[[EasyCV]] is Zhang Xuan's independent resume-building product for programmers, presented as both a product experiment and a learning vehicle for full-stack development, DevOps, copywriting, and marketing.

## Current Profile
The source describes EasyCV as an early-stage independent product rather than a mature income engine. It had about 900 registered users, 954 resumes created, and 2,500 yuan in revenue at the time of the article. Its greater value in the source is educational: [[ZhangXuan]] used it to practice product development, launch documentation, marketing, and business-model validation.

## Key Characteristics
- Targets programmers who need resume generation and related guidance.
- Was developed over roughly half a year and launched as a public product experiment.
- Produced modest early revenue compared with the author's living-cost needs.
- Served as a full-stack and DevOps learning project using TypeScript, Docker, Alibaba Cloud ACR, and GitHub Actions.
- Required non-code work such as templates, help documents, resume guides, launch writing, and promotion.
