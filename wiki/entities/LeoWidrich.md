## Overview
[[LeoWidrich]] is represented in the wiki as [[Buffer]]'s co-founder who left the company in 2017 after strategic misalignment about Buffer's next phase.

## Current Profile
The Buffer source presents Widrich as a formative early leader whose product, customer, team, and culture work shaped the company across its first six years. By 2017, his preferred path diverged from [[JoelGascoigne]]'s recommitment to a long-term, sustainable, fully remote, culture-forward company. The departure was not framed as failure or conflict over competence, but as [[FounderVisionAlignment]] reaching a point where different company-building instincts required different next chapters.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founder of Buffer and long-time collaborator with Joel Gascoigne.
- Helped shape Buffer's product and culture during the company's first six years.
- Preferred a different company-building path than Gascoigne's 2017 sustainable-growth vision.
- Left Buffer while remaining a non-executive board member and advisor.
- Planned to start something new, likely a SaaS product around HR or diversity.
