## Overview
[[GooglePlay]] is Google's Android app marketplace, represented in the wiki by a 2015 comparison where it led Apple's [[AppStore]] in downloads but trailed it in revenue.

## Current Profile
The VentureBeat source presents Google Play as the volume side of mobile app-store economics. In 2015, Google Play reportedly generated 100% more downloads than Apple's App Store, a larger lead than the prior year's 60% gap. That growth was driven by emerging markets including Brazil, India, Indonesia, Turkey, and Mexico. The same source also shows Google Play's monetization gap: despite its download lead, Apple's App Store generated 75% more revenue, and games accounted for 90% of Google Play revenue.

## Key Characteristics
- Carries Android's broad app-download scale.
- Grew its download lead over Apple's App Store in 2015.
- Was especially fueled by emerging-market download growth in the source.
- Trailed Apple's App Store in revenue despite higher downloads.
- Relied heavily on games for store revenue.

## Qualifications
The source is a 2015 market snapshot based on reported App Annie estimates. It does not give absolute downloads or revenue, distinguish paid apps from in-app purchases or subscriptions, analyze Google Play policy, or describe later Google Play economics.
