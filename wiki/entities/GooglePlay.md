## Overview
[[GooglePlay]] is Google's Android app marketplace, represented in the wiki by a 2015 download-versus-revenue comparison and a 2018 investigation showing how extremely popular permission-rich apps could create distribution, privacy, attribution-fraud, and enforcement risks.

## Current Profile
The VentureBeat source presents Google Play as the volume side of mobile app-store economics: in 2015 it generated twice as many downloads as Apple's [[AppStore]], driven by emerging markets, while Apple's store generated 75% more revenue. The BuzzFeed News source adds a governance counterweight to that reach. Eight apps with more than 2 billion combined Google Play downloads were alleged to monitor searches or installations and manipulate last-click attribution; Kika Keyboard was described as the store's most popular keyboard, and CM Launcher 3D had been promoted as a Google Play go-to app. Google initially said it had not confirmed fraud and requested more information; after publication, two Cheetah apps disappeared from the store, although Cheetah said their removal was voluntary and temporary.

## Key Characteristics
- Carries Android's broad app-download scale, especially in emerging markets.
- Trailed Apple's App Store in 2015 revenue despite leading it in downloads.
- Relied heavily on games for store revenue in the 2015 snapshot.
- Acts as a discovery and trust layer, not merely a download host.
- Distributed and promoted apps later implicated in a large-scale attribution-fraud investigation.
- Holds platform-governance responsibility for investigating abuse and deciding whether apps remain available.

## Evidence
- Download reach: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] reports 100% more downloads than Apple's App Store in 2015, widening from 60% in 2014, with Brazil, India, Indonesia, Turkey, and Mexico driving growth.
- Monetization profile: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] says Apple's store generated 75% more revenue and games contributed 90% of Google Play revenue.
- Distribution and endorsement risk: [[android-apps-with-more-than-2-billion-total-downloads-are-committing-ad-fraud]] reports more than 2 billion combined downloads across eight implicated apps and says CM Launcher 3D was promoted as a go-to app.
- Search and permission surface: [[android-apps-with-more-than-2-billion-total-downloads-are-committing-ad-fraud]] says Kika Keyboard monitored Play Store searches and Cheetah apps detected new installations to target active bounties.
- Enforcement response: [[android-apps-with-more-than-2-billion-total-downloads-are-committing-ad-fraud]] records Google's initial non-confirmation and later absence of CM Locker and Battery Doctor, alongside Cheetah's claim that it removed them voluntarily.

## Qualifications
The economic evidence is a 2015 App Annie snapshot without absolute download or revenue totals. The governance evidence is a disputed November 2018 investigation, not a current assessment of Google Play policy or enforcement. App download counts show exposure potential rather than the number of fraudulent transactions, and the article does not establish whether Google removed the two apps or whether Cheetah's voluntary-removal explanation was correct.

## What Changed
- Expanded Google Play from a reach-and-monetization profile into a distribution, trust, and enforcement actor.
- Added the qualification that massive download scale can magnify harm when permission-rich apps evade abuse controls.
