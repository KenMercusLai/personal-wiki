## Overview
[[FabriceBellard]] is presented in this source as the programmer behind [[FFmpeg]] and several other unusually compact or technically ambitious systems projects.

## Current Profile
The RTSP article mentions Bellard after showing FFmpeg-based recording, using him as a symbol of deep systems-programming capability. It credits him with creating FFmpeg under the pseudonym Gerard Lantau, and lists QEMU, TinyCC/TCCBOOT, QEmacs, JSLinux, digital TV experiments, and pi-computation work as part of the same profile. A newer essay invokes the same reputation to challenge the idea that programming is easy, but contributes no additional project history.

## Key Characteristics
- Credited as the creator of FFmpeg.
- Associated with QEMU, TinyCC, TCCBOOT, QEmacs, and JSLinux.
- Presented as a systems programmer capable of compact, technically surprising implementations.
- Used rhetorically by the author to emphasize FFmpeg's power and technical lineage.
- Cited by a second source as an example of exceptional programming skill.

## Evidence
- FFmpeg creation: [[shi-yong-ffmpeg-yuan-cheng-du-qu-rtsp-jian-kong-shi-pin-liu]] says Bellard created FFmpeg in 2000 under the pseudonym Gerard Lantau.
- Systems projects: [[shi-yong-ffmpeg-yuan-cheng-du-qu-rtsp-jian-kong-shi-pin-liu]] lists QEMU, TinyCC/TCCBOOT, QEmacs, a digital TV system, and JSLinux.
- Pi computation: [[shi-yong-ffmpeg-yuan-cheng-du-qu-rtsp-jian-kong-shi-pin-liu]] says Bellard announced a pi-computation record on December 31, 2009.
- Craft example: [[code-was-never-the-hard-part-is-an-insult-to-all-programmers]] cites Bellard to reject the claim that coding skill is trivial.
