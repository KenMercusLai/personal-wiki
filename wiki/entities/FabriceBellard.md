## Overview
[[FabriceBellard]] is presented in this source as the programmer behind [[FFmpeg]] and several other unusually compact or technically ambitious systems projects.

## Current Profile
The article mentions Bellard after showing FFmpeg-based RTSP recording, using him as a symbol of deep systems-programming capability. The source credits him with creating FFmpeg under the pseudonym Gerard Lantau, and lists QEMU, TinyCC/TCCBOOT, QEmacs, JSLinux, digital TV experiments, and pi-computation work as part of the same profile.

## Key Characteristics
- Credited as the creator of FFmpeg.
- Associated with QEMU, TinyCC, TCCBOOT, QEmacs, and JSLinux.
- Presented as a systems programmer capable of compact, technically surprising implementations.
- Used rhetorically by the author to emphasize FFmpeg's power and technical lineage.
