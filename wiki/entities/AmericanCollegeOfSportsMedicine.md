## Overview
The [[AmericanCollegeOfSportsMedicine]] (ACSM) is presented as a professional organization that uses certifications, programs, and publishing to help health and fitness professionals apply research.

## Current Profile
The 2013 editorial places ACSM between scientific research and frontline health and fitness work. It credits the organization with decades of research translation through its certifications and other programs and names [[ACSMHealthAndFitnessJournal]] as one reason that practitioner-facing translation exists. This is an institutional self-description from an affiliated editor, not an independent evaluation of ACSM's standards, reach, or effects.

## Key Characteristics
- Operates in the health, fitness, exercise, and sports-medicine professional domain.
- Offers certifications and other professional programs.
- Supports a practitioner-facing journal intended to translate science into practical guidance.
- Is presented as helping professionals distinguish evidence from commercial fads and gimmicks.

## Evidence
- Programs and certifications: [[from-the-editor-acsm-s-health-fitness-journal]] credits ACSM with decades of work translating research through certifications and other programs.
- Publishing role: [[from-the-editor-acsm-s-health-fitness-journal]] identifies its health and fitness journal as a channel for translating science for practitioners.
- Practice orientation: [[from-the-editor-acsm-s-health-fitness-journal]] frames the issue around trend interpretation and applied health topics.

## Qualifications
The only evidence is a short editorial written by the journal's editor-in-chief. It does not inventory ACSM's programs, describe certification standards, measure practitioner or patient outcomes, or independently verify the organization's claimed translation role. The profile is historical to 2013 and should not be read as a current organizational audit.
