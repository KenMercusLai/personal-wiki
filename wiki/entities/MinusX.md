## Current Profile
In the source, MinusX appears as an agent-building company that studied [[ClaudeCode]] request behavior and incorporated many of its design lessons into its own product. The page is source-scoped: the wiki currently knows MinusX primarily through its interest in building Claude Code-like experiences for chat-based LLM agents.

- Uses Claude Code as a reference implementation for simple loops, prompts, tools, and todo management.
- Treats practical debuggability and steerability as product-design priorities.
