## Overview
[[BonnieNardi]] is an anthropologist and human-computer interaction researcher represented here through her 1993 book *A Small Matter of Programming*.

## Current Profile
The source credits Nardi with a bold thesis that ordinary people can and should learn to program computers. [[AdamWiggins]] uses her research as the basis for a later design argument: end-user programming becomes more accessible when tools remove setup friction and organize their language and interface around a recognizable task.

## Key Characteristics
- Studies interactions between people and computers.
- Authored *A Small Matter of Programming* in 1993.
- Provides the research frame behind the source's argument for broad programming capability.
- Is represented indirectly through Wiggins's interpretation rather than through the book's full text.
