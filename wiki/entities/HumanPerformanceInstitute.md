## Overview
The [[HumanPerformanceInstitute]] is the Orlando-based organizational setting from which [[BrettKlika]] and [[ChrisJordan]] describe their body-weight [[HighIntensityCircuitTraining]] approach.

## Current Profile
The source presents the institute through its work with high-performing professionals facing time pressure, travel, fatigue, and competing work and family demands. Its stated programming goal is to help clients manage physical energy through exercise that is safe, effective, efficient, portable, and independent of specialized equipment. This is a narrow institutional self-description within a coauthored article rather than an independent account of the organization.

## Key Characteristics
- Works with high-performing professionals from multiple industries.
- Frames physical energy management as support for sustained performance and engagement.
- Prioritizes exercise programs compatible with time pressure and frequent travel.
- Serves as the practice context for the article's body-weight HICT approach.
