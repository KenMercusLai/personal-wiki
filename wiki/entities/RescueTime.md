## Overview
[[RescueTime]] is a digital activity-tracking product and the publisher of a 2018 analysis of email and instant-messaging overlap with productive work.

## Current Profile
In the available source, RescueTime acts as both measurement provider and productivity-advice publisher. It analyzes anonymized activity data from nearly 50,000 users, reports large amounts of [[CommunicationMultitasking]], and recommends more deliberate use of email and IM. Because the article promotes RescueTime and does not disclose enough methodology for independent replication, its measurements are useful as a large product-data observation rather than a neutral causal study.

## Key Characteristics
- Collects digital activity data intended to show users where their time goes.
- Publishes aggregate productivity analyses based on anonymized user data.
- Frames email and instant messaging as sources of task switching and focus fragmentation.
- Combines its own product telemetry with findings cited from academic and practitioner research.
- Has a commercial interest in making digital time use visible and actionable.

## Evidence
Product telemetry and scale:
- [[communication-multitasking-you-only-get-1h-12min-day-without-email]] says RescueTime analyzed anonymized data from nearly 50,000 users.

Communication analysis:
- [[communication-multitasking-you-only-get-1h-12min-day-without-email]] reports communication-tool overlap with productive work and limited communication-free productive time.

Advice and commercial context:
- [[communication-multitasking-you-only-get-1h-12min-day-without-email]] recommends deliberate communication use while inviting readers to sign up for RescueTime to measure their own device activity.

## Qualifications
The available profile comes from RescueTime's own promotional blog. The article does not disclose its sample construction, representativeness, event-classification rules, statistical distribution, uncertainty, or enough graph detail to reproduce the reported figures. Its causal interpretation also relies partly on research conducted outside the RescueTime dataset.

## What Changed
- Established RescueTime as a time-tracking product and publisher of aggregate productivity analysis.
- Added its 2018 communication-multitasking study and commercial measurement context.

## Relationships
- [[CommunicationMultitasking]] - RescueTime measured and popularized this pattern using anonymized product telemetry.
- [[AttentionManagement]] - the product frames activity measurement as a way to protect focused work.
- [[PersonalProductivity]] - RescueTime positions visibility into digital time use as an input to behavior change.
