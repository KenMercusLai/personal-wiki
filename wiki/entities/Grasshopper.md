## Overview
[[Grasshopper]] is a small-business phone-system company represented here through an article evaluating billboard advertising for entrepreneurs and small businesses.

## Current Profile
The source uses Grasshopper as both publisher and case example. Grasshopper tried billboard advertising, but the article reports that the visible campaign attracted more pitches from other advertisers than clear customer sales. The company therefore becomes a cautionary example for [[BillboardAdvertising]]: public awareness may not convert into attributable demand, especially when the channel broadcasts the fact that the company is spending on marketing.

## Key Characteristics
- Publishes small-business marketing advice in this source.
- Serves as a case where a billboard created advertiser attention more clearly than customer demand.
- Uses the article to contrast billboards with more targeted and durable acquisition channels.

## Evidence
- Publisher context: [[billboards-for-small-businesses-costs-advice-and-thinking-twice]] is a Grasshopper article about billboard costs, placement, and fit.
- Billboard outcome: [[billboards-for-small-businesses-costs-advice-and-thinking-twice]] says Grasshopper's billboard produced more sales calls from advertisers than customer sales.
- Channel comparison: [[billboards-for-small-businesses-costs-advice-and-thinking-twice]] suggests SEO, PPC, lead generation, blogging, PR, email, and other channels as part of a healthier marketing strategy.
