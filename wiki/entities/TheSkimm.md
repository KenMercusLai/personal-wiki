## Overview
[[TheSkimm]] is a digital media company founded by [[DanielleWeisberg]] and [[CarlyZakin]] in 2012, initially around a conversational morning email for busy professional women who wanted current-events context fitted to their routines.

## Current Profile
The two sources represent complementary early views of theSkimm. The founder interview describes an audience-first origin: Weisberg and Zakin drew on newsroom experience and conversations with friends, chose email because it matched morning phone behavior, and seeded the newsletter through personal outreach, explicit referral requests, and early earned media. A later media-economics panel presents the company as moving beyond a sponsored free newsletter toward a mixed portfolio that included a paid forecasting app and considered video and commerce. Together they show a company using one repeatable audience need and brand relationship across distribution and revenue experiments, not evidence that any one format or business model guaranteed durability.

## Key Characteristics
- Began as a conversational current-events email designed around the morning routines of busy professional women.
- Founded by two former NBC News producers whose editorial background supplied both subject knowledge and an initial professional network.
- Used direct network seeding, repeated five-friend referral asks, reader responsiveness, and early earned media to build initial awareness.
- Expanded from a free sponsored newsletter toward paid product and prospective video and commerce revenue.
- Framed its broader ambition as recreating the useful role of morning television for a mobile, routine-driven audience.

## Evidence
- Audience and format: [[get-that-life-how-two-friends-started-the-skimm-from-their-apartment]] says the founders observed that busy friends lacked time for television, Twitter, and fragmented headlines, then selected morning email to fit existing phone behavior.
- Early acquisition: [[get-that-life-how-two-friends-started-the-skimm-from-their-apartment]] reports a few thousand initial signups after broad founder-network outreach, five-friend requests, and a Today show mention by [[HodaKotb]].
- Company formation: [[get-that-life-how-two-friends-started-the-skimm-from-their-apartment]] describes a 2012 launch with two months of savings, eighteen months of apartment work, and venture-backed hiring beginning in 2014.
- Revenue portfolio: [[expert-revenue-models-for-new-media]] presents a sponsored free newsletter, paid forecasting app, and potential video and commerce as mission-consistent diversification.

## Qualifications
Both sources are favorable practitioner accounts from 2015 and 2017, not audited company histories. They provide no subscriber cohorts, retention, acquisition costs, revenue mix, margins, churn, or later outcome data. The origin interview cannot separate product fit and referrals from personal networks, celebrity endorsement, and subsequent press, while the panel's monetization example does not demonstrate that the mixed portfolio became profitable or durable.
