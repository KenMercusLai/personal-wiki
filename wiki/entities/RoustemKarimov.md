## Overview
[[RoustemKarimov]] is represented as [[DaveTeare]]'s cofounder in the origin story of [[AgileBits]] and [[OnePassword]].

## Current Profile
According to Teare's 2013 recollection, Karimov and Teare worked at World Vision Canada, adopted PowerBook G4 computers, and chose independent work rather than return to PCs when their Java tooling performed poorly on Macs. Their experiments eventually produced OS X Form, the form-saving utility that evolved into 1Password.

## Key Characteristics
- Worked with Teare at World Vision Canada before their independent software venture.
- Encouraged Teare's initial interest in Macs.
- Chose independent work with Teare after Mac-side Java performance created friction in their day jobs.
- Cofounded the company and product line later represented by AgileBits and 1Password.
