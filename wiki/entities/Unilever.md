## Overview
[[Unilever]] is a global consumer-goods company represented in the source as the acquirer of [[DollarShaveClub]].

## Current Profile
Within this source's narrow scope, Unilever appears as an incumbent that recognized the strategic significance of a scaled direct-to-consumer challenger. The acquisition is framed as giving Dollar Shave Club resources to compete globally and pursue [[MichaelDubin]]'s broader personal-care vision, not as evidence about the integration's later performance.

## Key Characteristics
- Large consumer-goods incumbent.
- Acquired Dollar Shave Club after its reported rapid growth and U.S. razor-share gains.
- Expected by the source to supply global resources and reach.
- Serves as an example of an incumbent buying a direct-to-consumer model rather than building the described relationship from scratch.
