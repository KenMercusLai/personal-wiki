Source: [9 Most Useful Pieces of Advice I've Received]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/mathilde-collin-nine-useful-pieces-of-advice.md" >}})

Mathilde Collin is the author of the source and writes from first-hand experience building Front. The article is not a general list of quotes detached from context; Collin frames each piece of advice by whether it proved useful in operating the company.

In the source, Collin's founder judgment centers on filtering outside input through company-specific evidence. She treats expert advice, benchmarks, and other founders' lessons as useful only when their context fits, while giving more weight to customer conversations, observed growth, and deliberate transfer of founder instinct to executives.
