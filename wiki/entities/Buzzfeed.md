## Overview
[[Buzzfeed]] is a digital media company represented in the wiki as both a viral-format growth case and a publisher trying to diversify revenue under platform pressure.

## Current Profile
The growth-hacking source presents Buzzfeed's early digital strength as mastering shareable formats such as quizzes, where participation creates social distribution. The "9 Boxes" memo adds a more mature operating picture: BuzzFeed is an independent digital media company facing platform-dominated ad economics, responding through quality content, fair-payment pressure on platforms, a multi-revenue model, a portfolio of media brands, and a brand-by-revenue-stream operating matrix.

## Key Characteristics
- Digital media company known for shareable entertainment and news.
- Uses quizzes and other formats designed for social distribution.
- Converts user participation into social traffic.
- Faces platform-power pressure from companies such as [[Facebook]] and [[Google]].
- Pursues diversified revenue through advertising, commerce, platform revenue, studio development, licensing, and merchandise.
- Builds a portfolio of brands including BuzzFeed, BuzzFeed News, and lifestyle brands such as [[Tasty]].

## Evidence
- Quiz loop: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] describes questionnaire formats whose results users share on Facebook.
- Viral mastery: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] frames Buzzfeed's growth as understanding content and distribution in the digital age.
- Platform pressure: [[9-boxes]] says Google and Facebook capture much of digital ad revenue and underpay professional content creators.
- Revenue diversification: [[9-boxes]] says BuzzFeed expected revenue outside direct-sold advertising to rise from about a quarter in 2017 to about half in 2019.
- Operating model: [[9-boxes]] shows a nine-box matrix crossing BuzzFeed, BuzzFeed Media Brands, and BuzzFeed News with advertising, commerce, and studio opportunities.

## Qualifications
The growth source is a retrospective tactic catalog, while the memo is an internal strategic statement. Together they show BuzzFeed's intended strategy and self-understanding, but they do not verify later revenue outcomes, profitability, layoffs, public-market performance, or later platform-policy changes.

## What Changed
- Expanded Buzzfeed from a viral-format case into a publisher-platform and revenue-diversification case.
- Added Tasty and the nine-box matrix as the source's operating-model evidence.
