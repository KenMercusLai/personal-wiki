## Overview
[[Buzzfeed]] is a digital media company used in the source as an example of mastering shareable content formats.

## Current Profile
The source says Buzzfeed's growth came from understanding which digital content formats people share and how those formats move through social platforms. Quizzes are the main example: users complete them, share results, and draw friends into the same interaction.

## Key Characteristics
- Digital media company known for shareable entertainment and news.
- Uses quizzes and other formats designed for social distribution.
- Converts user participation into social traffic.
