## Overview
[[EdsgerWDijkstra]] was a Dutch computer scientist and software engineer credited in the source with designing [[DijkstrasAlgorithm]] and publishing it in 1959.

## Current Profile
The source presents Dijkstra through the origin story of his shortest-path algorithm. It says he conceived the method while considering travel from Rotterdam to Groningen, deliberately kept the design free of avoidable complexity, and later described the roughly 20-minute invention as an unexpectedly important part of his reputation. The same source identifies his 1959 paper, “A note on two problems in connexion with graphs,” as the first publication of the algorithm.

## Key Characteristics
- Dutch computer scientist and software engineer.
- Designer of the greedy shortest-path method now bearing his name.
- Published the algorithm in a three-page paper in 1959.
- Associated the design with deliberate avoidance of unnecessary complexity.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on one introductory secondary article and its excerpt from a 2001 interview, not a comprehensive biography or direct reading of the 1959 paper. The reported “20 minutes” is a retrospective autobiographical account and does not by itself document every step in the algorithm's development.
