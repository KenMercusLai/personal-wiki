## Overview
[[VivianGiang]] is the author of a 2016 Fast Company article presenting frequent employer changes as a potentially useful career-development strategy.

## Current Profile
In the source, Giang synthesizes changing attitudes toward job hopping, a secondary compensation claim, and [[PattyMcCord]]'s advice that young workers consider changing companies every three to four years. Her contribution is journalistic framing rather than original outcome research: she links mobility to learning, performance, advancement, and changing expectations of employer loyalty.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes about career and workplace behavior.
- Frames job hopping as a potentially deliberate development strategy rather than an automatic resume defect.
- Uses expert commentary and linked secondary reporting to build the case.
