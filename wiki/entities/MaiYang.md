## Overview
[[MaiYang]] is a creator and writer represented through two AI-era learning essays: one on action over passive success-content consumption and one translating [[DwarkeshPatel]]'s preparation practices into a reusable study workflow.

## Current Profile
Across the sources, Mai Yang moves from motivational posture to concrete method. The first essay argues that consuming success formulas should not substitute for self-owned goals, building, and feedback from real work. The second treats [[DeepPreparation]] as a way to resist shallow information volume: select a small number of important materials, interrogate them with AI, generate bounded review prompts, connect them to an existing worldview, and invest enough research to make conversations valuable.

The second article is explicitly a synthesis of other materials rather than original measurement. Mai Yang's distinctive contribution is the engineering-style translation—review, refactor, knowledge cache, and minimum viable actions—plus explicit limits on card volume and study scope intended to make the method sustainable.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents AI adoption as a matter of action and self-honest desire.
- Uses founder examples to make abstract motivation concrete.
- Translates external interviews and articles into practical learning frameworks.
- Frames LLMs as accelerators for interrogation, counterargument, review, and knowledge connection.
- Uses small, bounded actions to reduce the gap between an inspiring method and repeatable practice.

## Evidence
- Action framing: [[blog-maiyang-naval-insights-taking-action-with-ai]] opens from a Cursor Meetup Xiamen closing summary and repeatedly says people should act toward AI.
- Example-based argument: [[blog-maiyang-naval-insights-taking-action-with-ai]] uses Musk, Zuckerberg, Jobs, and Bezos as founder examples of desire-driven action.
- Publication pattern: [[blog-maiyang-naval-insights-taking-action-with-ai]] links to a MaiYangAI Twitter/X post and the embedded poster notes ChatGPT summarization.
- Learning framework: [[mai-yang-dwarkesh-ru-he-yong-ai-zuo-shen-du-zhun-bei]] organizes Patel's reported practices into deep preparation, trust, quality-over-volume, AI dialogue, spaced review, and worldview integration.
- Action design: [[mai-yang-dwarkesh-ru-he-yong-ai-zuo-shen-du-zhun-bei]] recommends beginning with one or two chapters, limiting each chapter to ten cards, reviewing for five to ten minutes, and using transfer questions.

## Qualifications
The sources give little biographical information beyond author attribution, a management-transition remark, and meetup, blog, and social-publication context. The Naval essay uses famous founders as motivational examples without demonstrating that extreme desire causes success. The Dwarkesh article is a secondary synthesis whose “10x” efficiency language, guest-motivation claims, and learning benefits are not independently measured. This remains a source-scoped author profile, not a general biography.
