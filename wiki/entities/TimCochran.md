## Overview
[[TimCochran]] is represented in the wiki as a Thoughtworks technical director and co-author of guidance on talent constraints in growing technology companies.

## Current Profile
In the source, Cochran combines technology-strategy and scaleup concerns with recruiting operations. The article treats deteriorating quality, manual delivery work, overloaded specialists, and difficult development environments as talent-system evidence rather than problems isolated from hiring.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects hiring capacity with developer experience, automation, quality, and organizational design.
- Uses Thoughtworks client practice and internal experience as the basis for recommendations.
