## Overview
[[BrandonByars]] is represented in the wiki as the author of "You Can't Buy Integration" and as Head of Technology for Thoughtworks North America at the time of the source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Byars as a software-architecture practitioner arguing that integration is a strategic design discipline rather than a commodity product purchase. His article connects enterprise architecture, API strategy, commercial integration platforms, developer experience, and organizational agility.

## Key Characteristics
- Argues against treating integration tools as substitutes for integration architecture.
- Frames clean capability interfaces as the strategic goal of integration.
- Uses diagrams and enterprise examples to show how implementation-focused thinking creates technical debt.
- Positions commercial integration DSLs as useful only inside bounded tactical contexts.
