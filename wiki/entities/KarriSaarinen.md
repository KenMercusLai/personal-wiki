## Overview
[[KarriSaarinen]] is represented here as [[Linear]]'s co-founder and CEO and as a former design leader at [[Airbnb]] and early [[Coinbase]], based on [[MaiYang]]'s synthesis of a 20VC interview and public Linear material.

## Current Profile
The source attributes to Saarinen a product-led view of growth: external spending can accelerate a curve without proving durable value, while quality growth should emerge from a product customers want to keep using. His product judgment favors primary workflows over feature-checklist competition, and his founder-involvement stance favors close understanding of product, users, and quality without defining leadership as doing every task personally.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats sustainable customer value as a stronger growth foundation than spending or promotional acceleration.
- Connects product quality with operating routines rather than aesthetic claims alone.
- Prefers focused primary workflows over broad platform or checklist positioning.
- Frames founder leadership as informed presence in consequential product decisions.

## Evidence
- Growth stance: [[everyone-can-build-almost-no-one-can-judge]] attributes the contrast between externally accelerated growth and product-driven quality growth to Saarinen's 20VC interview.
- Product scope: [[everyone-can-build-almost-no-one-can-judge]] says Saarinen rejects proving superiority through feature checklists and instead prioritizes the main work users need to complete.

- Career context: [[everyone-can-build-almost-no-one-can-judge]] identifies him as Linear's co-founder and CEO, formerly a principal designer at Airbnb and an early Coinbase design leader.
