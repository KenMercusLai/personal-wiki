## Overview
[[Greenhouse]] is represented in this wiki as the company case for [[MichaelBoufford]]'s engineering-led organization-design framework.

## Current Profile
The source presents Greenhouse as a software company whose engineering organization grew from Boufford as first engineer in 2012 to about 60 engineers supporting software used by 2,000 companies. Its significance in the wiki is as a case where team health, culture, and structure are treated as systems to design and validate, not merely as informal byproducts of startup growth.

## Key Characteristics
- Software company context for an engineering-team scaling case.
- Reported as serving 2,000 companies at the time of the article.
- Engineering team reportedly grew to about 60 engineers with almost no voluntary attrition.
- Organization design is framed around stable retention, high-quality hiring, empowerment, respect, productivity, and continuous improvement.
- Culture and process choices are treated as foundational standards that may be difficult to roll back once established.

## Evidence
- Scale case: [[build-a-healthy-organization-by-thinking-like-an-engineer]] says Greenhouse's software was used by 2,000 companies and that its engineering team reached 60 engineers.
- Retention claim: [[build-a-healthy-organization-by-thinking-like-an-engineer]] reports almost no voluntary attrition during that scaling period.
- Organizational target: [[build-a-healthy-organization-by-thinking-like-an-engineer]] ties the Greenhouse case to a desired team state of stability, self-healing, high quality, productivity, improvement, empowerment, and respect.
- Foundational practices: [[build-a-healthy-organization-by-thinking-like-an-engineer]] lists starting boundaries such as always improving, respectful communication, regular professional development conversations, front-line change support, and managers working for engineers.

## Qualifications
The source is a practitioner article and conference-talk summary, not a full company history or independent audit of Greenhouse's retention, management practices, product scope, or later organizational outcomes.
