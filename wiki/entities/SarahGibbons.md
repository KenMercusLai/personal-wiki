## Overview
[[SarahGibbons]] is a [[NielsenNormanGroup]] author represented in the wiki through a shared brand vocabulary for UX decisions.

## Current Profile
With [[KateKaplan]], Gibbons defines brand as an enduring customer promise rather than a logo or product name and organizes its translation into UX through personality, attributes, vision, mission, and principles. The source positions the vocabulary as a cross-team alignment tool whose components guide decisions at different scales.

## Key Characteristics
- Coauthor of the wiki's source on brand vocabulary in UX.
- Distinguishes brand meaning from controlled branding expression.
- Connects organizational direction with concrete product, interaction, visual, and copy decisions.
