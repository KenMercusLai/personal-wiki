## Overview
[[SpencerCoon]] is the co-founder of [[Hibox]] and [[Beamer]], described as making the semi-pivot from the collaboration company to the focused release-communication SaaS with [[MarianoRodriguezColombelli]].

## Current Profile
The source places Coon alongside Rodriguez Colombelli throughout the company transition: they founded Hibox, discussed the pivot with mentors, created a new structure for Beamer, and moved the founding and core team into it. The article gives much less individual detail about Coon than about its author, so his profile is limited to co-founding and strategic-transition roles.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-founded Hibox with Rodriguez Colombelli.
- Participated in recognizing demand for the internal notification tool that became Beamer.
- Joined the decision to spin Beamer into a new company after early external traction.
- Moved with the founding and core team into the Beamer structure while Hibox continued independently.
