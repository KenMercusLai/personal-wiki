## Overview
[[MarissaMayer]] is presented as a Google and Yahoo operator whose scaling lessons cover process breakage, acquisitions, culture, communication cadence, and CEO path clearing.

## Current Profile
The source uses Mayer to connect [[Google]] operating lessons with [[Yahoo]] scaling problems. She relays [[EricSchmidt]]'s order-of-magnitude process warning, describes using acquisitions to build mobile capability, cites Google and Facebook as strong-culture examples, and explains Google's executive cadence of staff meetings, strategy reviews, one-on-ones, and full-company meetings.

## Key Characteristics
- Emphasizes that processes break at each order of magnitude.
- Uses talent, building-block, and strategic acquisition categories.
- Treats strong culture as recognizable even without explicit branding.
- Values structured executive communication cadence.
- Frames the CEO as setting direction, defending teams, and removing obstacles.
