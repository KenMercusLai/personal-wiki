## Overview
[[Posterous]] appears as [[GarryTan]]'s startup case for how fast growth can hide co-founder relationship problems until the company faces a harder phase.

## Current Profile
The source presents Posterous as a startup co-founded by Tan in 2008 that grew 10X yearly and became a top 200 Quantcast website before growth flatlined by the end of 2010. Its role in the wiki is not a full product history; it is an example of how operational success can delay founder conflict rather than resolve it. When growth slowed, an unmaintained co-founder relationship became a central risk at exactly the moment the company needed better collaboration.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a concrete case where growth masked founder relationship debt.
- Was co-founded by Garry Tan in 2008.
- Grew quickly enough to become a top 200 Quantcast website in the source's account.
- Experienced flatlining growth by the end of 2010.
- Illustrates why co-founder history has to be maintained as a current working relationship.
