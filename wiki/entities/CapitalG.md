## Overview
[[CapitalG]] is represented as [[Alphabet]]'s growth-equity fund, focused primarily on later-stage startup investments in [[JasonRowley]]'s 2018 analysis.

## Current Profile
CapitalG occupies a distinct stage mandate within Alphabet's investing system rather than duplicating [[GV]]'s broader venture portfolio. The reported portfolios overlapped only at Gusto and Pindrop; CapitalG joined Gusto's Series B and led Pindrop's Series C. That pattern is consistent with stage specialization but does not establish investment independence, performance, or a complete mandate.

## Key Characteristics
- Alphabet-affiliated growth-equity fund.
- Focused primarily on late-stage deals in the source.
- Shared only two reported portfolio companies with GV.
- Participated in Gusto's Series B after GV had invested at seed.
- Led Pindrop's Series C while GV also participated.

## Evidence
- Mandate: [[jason-rowley-a-peek-inside-alphabets-investing-universe]] characterizes CapitalG as a growth-equity fund investing mainly at later stages.
- Gusto overlap: [[jason-rowley-a-peek-inside-alphabets-investing-universe]] reports both GV and CapitalG in the Series B after GV's seed investment.
- Pindrop overlap: [[jason-rowley-a-peek-inside-alphabets-investing-universe]] says CapitalG led the Series C in which GV participated.
- Portfolio separation: [[jason-rowley-a-peek-inside-alphabets-investing-universe]] finds no other GV-CapitalG portfolio overlap in its Crunchbase data.

## Qualifications
The evidence is a historical Crunchbase snapshot and may omit undisclosed transactions or depend on entity resolution. Two overlapping investments cannot establish the degree of organizational coordination, information sharing, mandate discipline, or comparative performance.
