## Overview
[[DavidLee]] is the author of the Medium source that curates startup checklists for entrepreneurs.

## Current Profile
The source presents Lee as a practitioner who evaluates and works with startups, and who still uses external checklists from Peter Thiel, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, and Bill Gurley. The page is source-scoped: it records his role as curator of a startup-evaluation toolkit rather than a general biography.

## Key Characteristics
- Curates startup operating checklists rather than presenting an original framework.
- Recommends established venture and founder sources for startup evaluation.
- Emphasizes the execution gap between writing checklists and actually using them.

## Evidence
- Curator role: [[checklists-for-startups-david-lee-medium]] says Lee recommends three startup checklist areas and a marketplace-specific addition.
- Practitioner context: [[checklists-for-startups-david-lee-medium]] says he still uses the checklists after evaluating and working with many startups.
- Execution warning: [[checklists-for-startups-david-lee-medium]] says writing checklists and simple rules down is easy while execution is hard.
