## Overview
[[BasicAttentionToken]] (BAT) is the cryptocurrency presented in the source as a unit and transfer mechanism for attention within [[Brave]]'s advertising system.

## Current Profile
The article treats BAT as the settlement layer connecting users, publishers, and advertisers. Users would earn tokens for viewing optional ads, publishers would receive tokens tied to user attention, and users could automatically return some rewards to favored sites or creators. Brave is described as the initial distribution and validation channel, after which developers could embed the token model in other attention-based applications.

## Key Characteristics
- Denotes and can be exchanged for user attention in the source's model.
- Transfers a proposed share of ad revenue to the person receiving the ad.
- Supports automatic contributions to publishers and creators.
- Connects advertiser spending with locally measured attention and publisher compensation.
- Is forecast to expand from Brave into messaging, games, podcasts, and streaming.

## Evidence
- Medium of exchange: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] defines BAT as the currency used to move value through the proposed ad system.
- User reward: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] says browser-delivered ads would send 70% of revenue to users.
- Publisher support: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] says users could redirect earned tokens to sites and creators.
- Expansion thesis: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] forecasts integrations across multiple media and application categories.

## Qualifications
The source is a 2018 advocacy article by an author who disclosed owning BAT. It describes planned allocation rules and broad adoption scenarios, including a speculative $49 high-value lead example, without independently demonstrating demand, stable token value, publisher income, regulatory fit, or realized cross-application adoption.

## What Changed
- Created BAT as the token and settlement entity in the Brave advertising proposal.
