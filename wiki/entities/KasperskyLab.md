## Overview
[[KasperskyLab]] is the cybersecurity company that staged the 2017 [[DataDollarStore]] pop-up in London.

## Current Profile
In the available source, Kaspersky Lab appears as the organizer of an experiential privacy-awareness campaign. It replaced money with photos, messages, or phone access to make routine online data collection feel like an explicit economic exchange. The source does not document what happened to submitted data, the campaign's governance, or its measured effect.

## Key Characteristics
- Cybersecurity company behind the Data Dollar Store campaign.
- Used physical retail and exclusive artwork to dramatize the economic value of personal information.
- Framed the store as an awareness exercise rather than a proposed future commerce model.

- Organizer: [[inside-the-store-that-only-accepts-personal-data-as-currency-engadget]] identifies Kaspersky Lab as the company running the one-week London pop-up.
