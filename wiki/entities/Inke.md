## Overview
[[Inke]] is a Chinese social livestreaming app used in the source as a primary example of mobile, interaction-centered livestreaming.

## Current Profile
The source presents Inke as an app that helped reframe livestreaming around strangers watching and chatting with a personal broadcaster through the phone's front camera. It illustrates the category's key mechanics: virtual gifts, beautycam tools, sound controls, event livestreams, and broadcaster-audience interaction.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents the social livestreaming shift from scheduled performance toward mobile chatroom entertainment.
- Uses digital gifts as attention, payment, and interaction signals.
- Provides beautification and audio tools that lower broadcaster production barriers.
- Hosts both personal streams and large entertainment events in the source's examples.

## Evidence
- Category example: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] names Inke as an app that reframed livestreaming as social livestreaming.
- Gift UI: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] shows an Inke broadcaster receiving a sports-car sticker gift.
- Beauty tools: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says Inke has a beautycam feature that smooths skin and removes blemishes.
- Audio tools: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] shows an Inke equalizer with background, voice, and pitch controls.
- Event example: [[16-observations-on-livestreaming-in-china-andreessen-horowitz]] says "I'm a Singer" broadcast an episode on Inke to 10 million users.
