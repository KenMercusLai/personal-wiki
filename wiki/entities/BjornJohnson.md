## Overview
[[BjornJohnson]] is the author of an 8th Light article connecting vertically sliced [[UserStories]], [[PERTEstimation]], and simultaneous team estimates.

## Current Profile
The source presents Johnson as a software practitioner concerned with turning large projects into manageable, deliverable work. His method first narrows a feature to one user-visible path with acceptance criteria, then treats its schedule as an uncertain team judgment rather than a concrete commitment.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames project planning around small deliverable user outcomes.
- Contrasts end-to-end stories with work isolated inside technical layers.
- Uses three-scenario estimation to make adverse conditions and blockers discussable.
- Favors independent, simultaneous team input over an estimate dominated by one voice.
