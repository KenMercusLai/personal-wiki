## Overview
[[BenEine]] is the street artist whose shirts, mugs, and prints were exchanged for personal data at the 2017 [[DataDollarStore]].

## Current Profile
Within the source, Eine is both the merchandise collaborator and the campaign's principal interpreter. He argues that making disclosure immediate and transactional can cause people to inspect what they otherwise give away routinely, and that people should understand and share in the value created from their information.

## Key Characteristics
- Street artist supplying exclusive merchandise for the Data Dollar Store.
- Spokesperson for making routine personal-data exchange visible and emotionally concrete.
- Advocate, in this source, for recognizing and rewarding people's contribution of information.

## Evidence
- Collaboration: [[inside-the-store-that-only-accepts-personal-data-as-currency-engadget]] identifies Eine's shirts, mugs, and prints as the store's goods.
- Awareness thesis: [[inside-the-store-that-only-accepts-personal-data-as-currency-engadget]] records his contrast between routine data disclosure and deliberate exchange for an object.
- Compensation thesis: [[inside-the-store-that-only-accepts-personal-data-as-currency-engadget]] quotes his view that people should receive a benefit when others obtain their information.

## Qualifications
The source presents brief campaign commentary rather than a developed valuation, ownership, consent, or compensation framework. It does not establish Eine's broader views or test whether user rewards would make collection fair.
