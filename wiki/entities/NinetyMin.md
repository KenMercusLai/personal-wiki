## Overview
[[NinetyMin]] is a football-media platform described by [[MichalTurjeman]] as a [[MinuteMedia]] product built around letting fans be heard rather than only publishing articles about football.

## Current Profile
In the source, 90min supplies both the conceptual foundation and the design material for [[TwelveUp]]. Turjeman valued its participatory core, while still needing to examine the existing product critically and identify what should or should not carry into an updated product for a different sports context.

## Key Characteristics
- Centers fan participation as its defining idea in the source's account.
- Serves football audiences rather than American sports broadly.
- Acts as the predecessor and comparison point for 12up's design.
- Required critical decomposition despite the team's emotional investment in the existing product.

## Evidence
- Product purpose: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] distinguishes 90min from an ordinary football-article platform by describing it as a place for fans to be heard.
- Design inheritance: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] says 12up was intended as an upgraded and updated version of 90min.
- Redesign tension: [[designing-a-product-youre-not-going-to-use-michal-turjeman-medium]] describes the difficulty of identifying weaknesses in work colleagues had built with strong emotional commitment.

## Qualifications
The profile is based on one designer's brief 2016 retrospective. It does not describe 90min's full feature set, audience composition, operating model, measured outcomes, or subsequent product changes.
