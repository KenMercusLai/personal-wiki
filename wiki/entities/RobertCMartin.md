## Overview
[[RobertCMartin]] is cited in the source as “Uncle Bob,” author of *The Clean Coder* and the named source of the “Flying Fingers” team-estimation technique.

## Current Profile
The article uses Martin's work as practical guidance for making software estimation collaborative. Team members consider a story individually and reveal point estimates simultaneously, limiting the ability of one early or loud voice to set the visible answer before others respond.

## Key Characteristics
- Identified by the source with the nickname “Uncle Bob.”
- Cited as the author of *The Clean Coder*.
- Credited with the “Flying Fingers” estimation technique.
- Associated in this source with simultaneous estimate reveal and team discussion.
