## Overview
[[YanceyStrickler]] is presented in the available source as an outgoing CEO of [[Kickstarter]] and a writer reflecting on how founders can grow into a more strategic leadership role.

## Current Profile
In the 2017 essay, Strickler recounts a breakfast conversation with an unnamed founder whose company and leadership team were performing well even as the founder felt less personally useful. Strickler reframes that result as evidence that the founder had done central CEO work: building a capable organization. His model places the CEO's distinctive contribution in direction, hiring, strategic perspective, communication, guidance, and decisions rather than ownership of every idea or operating detail.

Strickler also treats perspective as a practice. A leader has to create distance from routine office work and use peers, customers, reading, solitude, or other perspective-changing experiences to view present choices through the intended future. The vision then remains a conversation with the team rather than a substitute for their judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Kickstarter CEO at the time of the essay, after announcing that he would step down.
- Frames CEO effectiveness through the success of the organization and leaders the CEO assembled.
- Treats direction-setting and hiring as central chief-executive responsibilities.
- Recommends deliberate distance from operational detail to regain strategic perspective.
- Presents vision and execution as a continuing conversation between CEO and team.
