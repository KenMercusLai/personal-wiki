## Overview
[[AtulGawande]] is used by [[DariusForoux]] as an example of [[SkillStacking]] and appears in the Forbes source as the healthcare leader hired to run the Bezos-Buffett-Dimon nonprofit initiative.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Gawande appears first as a specialist whose communication skills make the underlying domain expertise more visible and differentiated. Foroux invokes him to show that adding writing and speaking to a technical or professional base can create unusual market value. The Forbes source adds an organizational role: Gawande is hired as CEO of a nonprofit healthcare initiative backed by Amazon, Berkshire Hathaway, and JPMorgan Chase, intended to explore better and lower-cost care for their employees.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a specialist-plus-communication example for skill stacking.
- Illustrates how writing and speaking can differentiate technical expertise.
- Connects professional credibility with public explanation.
- Leads the Bezos-Buffett-Dimon nonprofit healthcare initiative in the Forbes source.
- Represents healthcare expertise brought into a large-employer care-cost experiment.

## Evidence
- Specialist example: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] asks readers to imagine a surgeon who can also write and speak well, then names Gawande.
- Communication stack: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] lists writing and public speaking as skills that help people stand out.
- Career differentiation: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] uses Gawande to show how a professional can become distinctive without abandoning the core field.
- Healthcare leadership: [[bezos-unbound-exclusive-interview-with-the-amazon-founder-on-what-he-plans-to-conquer-next]] says Bezos, Buffett, and Jamie Dimon hired Gawande to lead a nonprofit initiative for better care at lower cost.
- Scale of experiment: [[bezos-unbound-exclusive-interview-with-the-amazon-founder-on-what-he-plans-to-conquer-next]] says the three companies employed about 1.2 million people before dependents.

## Qualifications
The wiki's evidence remains narrow: one source uses Gawande as a skill-stacking example, and one Forbes profile names his healthcare-executive role. It does not independently assess his medical writing, research, leadership performance, or the later outcome of the nonprofit initiative.
