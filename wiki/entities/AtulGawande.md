## Overview
[[AtulGawande]] is used by [[DariusForoux]] as an example of [[SkillStacking]]: a surgeon whose writing and public communication create a distinctive professional profile.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Gawande appears as a specialist whose communication skills make the underlying domain expertise more visible and differentiated. Foroux invokes him to show that adding writing and speaking to a technical or professional base can create unusual market value.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as a specialist-plus-communication example for skill stacking.
- Illustrates how writing and speaking can differentiate technical expertise.
- Connects professional credibility with public explanation.

## Evidence
- Specialist example: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] asks readers to imagine a surgeon who can also write and speak well, then names Gawande.
- Communication stack: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] lists writing and public speaking as skills that help people stand out.
- Career differentiation: [[being-different-beats-being-better-darius-foroux]] uses Gawande to show how a professional can become distinctive without abandoning the core field.
