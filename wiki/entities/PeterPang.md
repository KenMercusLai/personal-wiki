## Overview
[[PeterPang]] is the author of the translated essay "Why Your AI-First Strategy Is Probably Wrong" discussed in the source.

## Current Profile
The source presents Peter Pang as a CTO-like builder at [[CREAO]] who led a rapid rearchitecture of the company's product and workflow around AI agents. His argument emphasizes that AI-first work requires redesigning engineering systems, roles, validation loops, and company operations rather than merely adopting coding assistants.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates AI-first workflow redesign rather than AI-assisted tool use.
- Presents [[CREAO]] as a case study in agent-centered engineering operations.
- Emphasizes critical thinking, architectural judgment, and product taste as increasingly valuable engineering skills.
- Describes a disruptive organizational transition with anxiety, role changes, and heavy founder/CTO effort.
