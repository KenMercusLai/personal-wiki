## Overview
[[WebOS]] appears in the wiki as Palm's Linux-based smartphone operating system, launched with the Palm Pre as an urgent response to the iPhone-era platform shift.

## Current Profile
The source treats WebOS as evidence that technical recognition alone was not enough. Palm moved quickly, produced an attractive modern platform, and still failed because the product was buggy, the ecosystem lacked momentum, and the market had already begun consolidating around iOS and Android. WebOS later moved to HP and then LG's smart-TV context.

## Key Characteristics
- Linux-based smartphone platform created by [[Palm]].
- Arrived with the Palm Pre in 2009.
- Was attractive but buggy in the source's characterization.
- Failed to build the app and market momentum needed against iOS and Android.

## Evidence
- Platform role: [[blackberry-meditation-at-the-grave-jean-louis-gassee-medium]] says Palm rushed to develop Linux-based WebOS after accepting its prior platform was obsolete.
- Launch timing: [[blackberry-meditation-at-the-grave-jean-louis-gassee-medium]] says WebOS shipped with the Palm Pre in spring 2009.
- Later path: [[blackberry-meditation-at-the-grave-jean-louis-gassee-medium]] says HP later sold WebOS to LG for smart TVs.

## Qualifications
The page reflects WebOS's role in one smartphone-transition essay. It does not evaluate WebOS's technical architecture, developer tools, patents, user experience, HP strategy, or LG television platform in detail.

## What Changed
- Created WebOS as Palm's late smartphone platform response.
