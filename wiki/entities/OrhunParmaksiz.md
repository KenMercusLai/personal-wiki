## Overview
[[OrhunParmaksiz]] is a Rust and open-source developer who writes about responsible AI-assisted programming.

## Current Profile
In the source, Parmaksız presents himself as an active but selective user of [[GitHubCopilot]] and [[Codex]]. Experience building a cargo tree terminal interface led him away from unrestricted agent control and toward a mixed workflow: use AI where it removes drudgery or supplies missing speed, keep personally rewarding implementation work, and retain a final human quality pass. His public position is neither anti-AI nor indifferent to quality; it is that developers should disclose and experiment with their workflows while accepting full responsibility for what they release.

## Key Characteristics
- Uses AI coding tools pragmatically rather than treating either manual coding or full automation as an identity.
- Values understanding, maintainability, release safety, and end-product quality.
- Treats enjoyment and craft as legitimate criteria when dividing work between human and agent.
- Writes from an open-source maintainer and curator perspective, where rapid project creation increases discovery and trust costs.
- Raises AI-code licensing and FOSS ethics as unresolved questions rather than offering legal conclusions.

## Evidence
- Workflow experimentation: [[write-less-code-be-more-responsible-orhuns-blog]] recounts moving from broad Codex control to exhaustive commit review and then to a mixed workflow.
- Quality ownership: [[write-less-code-be-more-responsible-orhuns-blog]] repeatedly says the developer remains responsible for the finished product and public contribution.
- Open-source perspective: [[write-less-code-be-more-responsible-orhuns-blog]] connects rapid AI-assisted project creation with curation overload, maintenance uncertainty, and unsafe-release risk.
