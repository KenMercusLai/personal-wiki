## Overview
[[MarkZuckerberg]] appears in the source as the Harvard dorm founder example for learning by building before a perfect plan exists.

## Current Profile
The source uses Zuckerberg's creation of Thefacebook as an illustration of immediate action. He is presented as someone who saw a campus-connection need, implemented the first product quickly, and then used scaling and feature problems as live learning material.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents fast implementation before a complete business plan.
- Is tied to Facebook's early campus-connection need.
- Serves as an example of learning from real product problems after launch.

## Evidence
- First implementation: [[blog-maiyang-naval-insights-taking-action-with-ai]] says Zuckerberg created Thefacebook in a Harvard dorm.
- Anti-perfection frame: [[blog-maiyang-naval-insights-taking-action-with-ai]] says he did not wait for a perfect business plan or patent strategy.
- Learning loop: [[blog-maiyang-naval-insights-taking-action-with-ai]] frames server scaling and feature iteration as immediate learning after the product became popular.

## Qualifications
The source gives a motivational origin-story version of Zuckerberg and Facebook. It does not cover later Facebook strategy, governance, harms, monetization, acquisitions, regulation, or contested historical details.
