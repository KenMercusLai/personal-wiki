## Overview
[[JeffMarsilio]] is represented as the [[NBA]] vice president responsible for strategy and business development in domestic digital media and as the executive explaining its 2017 virtual-reality strategy.

## Current Profile
Marsilio argued that meaningful progress required replacing one-off trials with one partner, a committed schedule, and learning from every broadcast. He put live games first, with rich off-court storytelling as a second content bucket, and expected VR to become an additive third viewing option rather than immediately replace television or mobile.

His account combined optimism with restraint. He highlighted strong social reaction, engagement duration, enjoyment, international opportunity, and potential interaction, but acknowledged modest audience numbers, early-stage technology, difficult headset marketing, and the risk of overconfident predictions.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocated sustained production commitment as the path from experimentation to product improvement.
- Prioritized live-game immersion while supporting storytelling and exploratory interactive uses.
- Evaluated early VR through engagement quality and learning as well as reach.
- Expressed a strong long-term adoption thesis while acknowledging present uncertainty and modest scale.

## Qualifications
The page is based on one 2017 interview and reflects Marsilio's role and views at that time. The source supplies no later evaluation of his forecasts and no quantified data for engagement, enjoyment, social response, global adoption, or production improvement.
