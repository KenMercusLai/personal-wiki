## Overview
[[NeilCybart]] is the author and operator behind [[AboveAvalon]], an independent subscription-supported Apple analysis publication, and appears in the wiki as an Apple product, financial, ecosystem, growth-driver, content-distribution, and personal-computing strategy analyst.

## Current Profile
The subscription source presents Cybart as a former Wall Street analyst who left that world to launch Above Avalon in 2014 after seeing room for independent analysis amid online publishing dislocation. His operating thesis is that subscribers pay for perspective, not just Apple news summaries. The smart-glasses source demonstrates that perspective by reading WWDC 2018 as evidence that Apple's AR, Siri, machine-learning, Watch, and AirPods work pointed toward future [[SmartGlasses]]. The buyback source shows the Wall Street side of his Apple analysis: he models how cash flow, foreign cash, tax reform, debt issuance, board authorization, and market skepticism could allow an unusually large [[ShareBuyback]] program. The growth-story source applies the same financial/product lens to Apple's 2018 revenue rebound, arguing that [[AppleGrowthLevers]] were led by higher iPhone ASPs but likely to shift toward Services and wearables. The content-distribution source shows his ecosystem strategy lens: he rejects a simple services-over-hardware story and instead argues that [[Apple]] can use [[AppleMusic]], [[AirPlay2]], [[AppleTV]], and [[HomePod]] to balance scale, mixed-device households, and premium first-party experiences. The connecting-the-dots source makes his product philosophy explicit through [[AppleProductTheory]], where Apple advances by making technology more personal and moving tasks from Mac to iPad to iPhone to Watch and possible glasses. He therefore emphasizes strong voice, deep expertise, trust, and a focused Apple-centered philosophy while using subscriptions to avoid the incentives of page-view-driven publishing.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Wall Street analyst who moved into independent Apple analysis.
- Founded Above Avalon before launching its subscriptions in 2015.
- Frames Apple analysis around perspective and philosophy, not only event coverage.
- Uses subscriptions to align his writing incentives with quality and trust.
- Treats subscriber community and support as part of the publication's durability.
- Interprets Apple product, capital-return, services, media, hardware, and form-factor moves through longer-term ecosystem strategy.
- Compares Apple's revenue drivers and product categories by durability rather than treating all growth or devices as equivalent.

## Qualifications
The subscription source is autobiographical and promotional in places, so Cybart's claims about uniqueness, reader trust, and model strength should be read as operator perspective rather than independent market measurement. The smart-glasses source is an analyst forecast from 2018 and should not be treated as confirmed product outcome without later evidence. The growth-story source is a 2018 interpretation and uses Cybart's estimates for installed base, flagship iPhone sales, Apple Watch users, and AirPods unit potential. The buyback source is a 2017 scenario dependent on tax reform, share price, cash flow, and management behavior. The content-distribution source is a 2019 strategy interpretation and does not measure later adoption or business outcomes. The wiki does not yet include outside sources on his broader Apple analysis record.
