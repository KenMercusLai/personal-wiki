## Overview
[[NeilCybart]] is the author and operator behind [[AboveAvalon]], an independent subscription-supported Apple analysis publication, and appears in the wiki as an Apple product, financial, ecosystem, and content-distribution strategy analyst.

## Current Profile
The subscription source presents Cybart as a former Wall Street analyst who left that world to launch Above Avalon in 2014 after seeing room for independent analysis amid online publishing dislocation. His operating thesis is that subscribers pay for perspective, not just Apple news summaries. The smart-glasses source demonstrates that perspective by reading WWDC 2018 as evidence that Apple's AR, Siri, machine-learning, Watch, and AirPods work pointed toward future [[SmartGlasses]]. The buyback source shows the Wall Street side of his Apple analysis: he models how cash flow, foreign cash, tax reform, debt issuance, board authorization, and market skepticism could allow an unusually large [[ShareBuyback]] program. The content-distribution source shows his ecosystem strategy lens: he rejects a simple services-over-hardware story and instead argues that [[Apple]] can use [[AppleMusic]], [[AirPlay2]], [[AppleTV]], and [[HomePod]] to balance scale, mixed-device households, and premium first-party experiences. He therefore emphasizes strong voice, deep expertise, trust, and a focused Apple-centered philosophy while using subscriptions to avoid the incentives of page-view-driven publishing.

## Key Characteristics
- Former Wall Street analyst who moved into independent Apple analysis.
- Founded Above Avalon before launching its subscriptions in 2015.
- Frames Apple analysis around perspective and philosophy, not only event coverage.
- Uses subscriptions to align his writing incentives with quality and trust.
- Treats subscriber community and support as part of the publication's durability.
- Interprets Apple product, capital-return, services, media, and hardware moves through longer-term ecosystem strategy.

## Qualifications
The subscription source is autobiographical and promotional in places, so Cybart's claims about uniqueness, reader trust, and model strength should be read as operator perspective rather than independent market measurement. The smart-glasses source is an analyst forecast from 2018 and should not be treated as confirmed product outcome without later evidence. The buyback source is a 2017 scenario dependent on tax reform, share price, cash flow, and management behavior. The content-distribution source is a 2019 strategy interpretation and does not measure later adoption or business outcomes. The wiki does not yet include outside sources on his broader Apple analysis record.
