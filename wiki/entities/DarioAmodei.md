## Overview
[[DarioAmodei]] is represented in the source as [[Anthropic]]'s CEO and the author of a proposal to pace frontier AI development.

## Current Profile
The source presents Amodei as arguing for slowing or coordinating frontier progress because of catastrophic and loss-of-control risks. [[BenThompson]] disputes that framing and interprets the proposal through Anthropic's competitive position: more time could reduce commercialization overhangs and is requested when [[OpenAI]] is portrayed as setting the frontier.

This is an adversarial secondary account rather than a direct summary of Amodei's essay. It establishes that Thompson links Amodei, Anthropic's founding rationale, safety advocacy, and competitive incentives; it does not establish Amodei's full argument, his motives, or the policy's merits.

## Key Characteristics
- Identified as Anthropic's CEO and a public advocate of pacing frontier-model development.
- Associated with catastrophic-risk and recursive-self-improvement concerns in Thompson's account.
- Presented as having co-founded Anthropic after losing trust in Sam Altman and OpenAI.
- Interpreted by Thompson through the overlap between stated safety goals and Anthropic's need for time to build product and economic moats.

## Evidence
- Policy role: [[frontier-overhangs]] identifies Amodei's “We Must Pace the Frontier” essay as the proposal under examination.
- Risk framing: [[frontier-overhangs]] contrasts Amodei's recursive-self-improvement concern with Thompson's concrete misuse and cybersecurity framing.
- Institutional history: [[frontier-overhangs]] says Anthropic arose because Amodei and cofounders did not trust Sam Altman and OpenAI.
- Competitive interpretation: [[frontier-overhangs]] argues that pacing would be advantageous to Anthropic when OpenAI is portrayed as leading.
