## Overview
[[MyCrypto]] is the Ethereum wallet company and brand announced by [[TaylorMonahan]] in February 2018 after the MyEtherWallet split.

## Current Profile
The source presents MyCrypto as a new domain, company structure, support surface, and product codebase designed for the next stage of Ethereum wallet infrastructure. It inherits the client-side, open-source, privacy-minded, transparency-focused ethos Monahan associates with MEW, but is intended to support a larger team, more explicit legal and financial structure, audited React/TypeScript development, desktop apps, mobile apps, and ongoing security education.

## Key Characteristics
- Created as a new brand and company after [[MyEtherWallet]]'s side-project structure became insufficient.
- Aimed to provide wallet development, support, user education, and security work for Ethereum and broader blockchain users.
- Positioned around a balance among safety, control, ease of use, privacy, decentralization, and trustlessness.
- Used new support channels, social media accounts, and a MyCrypto-owned GitHub organization.
- Planned an audited React/TypeScript codebase, followed by public beta testing, desktop apps, and mobile apps.

## Evidence
- Launch rationale: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says MyCrypto was designed with next-level scaling in mind from the beginning.
- Product commitments: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] lists goals around empowering blockchain interaction while staying free, open source, privacy-minded, transparent, and client-side.
- Technical transition: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says the new React/TypeScript codebase had been audited and was moving toward public beta.
- User transition: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] gives users a choice between MEW and MyCrypto while promising continued support for users regardless of product.

## Qualifications
The source is the launch announcement, not a later retrospective, so it records intended 2018 direction rather than the full realized history of MyCrypto.

## What Changed
- Added MyCrypto as a new Ethereum wallet entity created to carry MEW-like wallet work into a more scalable company structure.
