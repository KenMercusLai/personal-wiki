## Overview
[[BrianChesky]] is presented as Airbnb's founder-operator whose scaling lessons connect competition, unscalable customer work, culture, and CEO priorities.

## Current Profile
The source uses Chesky to show how [[Airbnb]] moved from early host intimacy to rapid international scale. He describes competition and government as existential threats, credits unscalable host work for creating customer love, treats culture as a mission lived by the founder, and says post-product-market-fit CEO work centers on hiring, strategy, and culture.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats competition as a trigger for fast international scaling.
- Values intense early customer love over broad weak adoption.
- Uses founder behavior and repeated messages to transmit culture.
- Treats hiring and interviewer training as culture infrastructure.
- Frames post-fit CEO priorities as hiring, strategy, and culture.
