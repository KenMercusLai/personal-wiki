## Overview
[[BrianChesky]] is presented as Airbnb's founder-operator whose scaling lessons connect competition, unscalable customer work, culture, CEO priorities, and the early importance of trust in stranger-to-stranger hospitality.

## Current Profile
The sources use Chesky to show how [[Airbnb]] moved from early host intimacy to rapid international scale while treating trust as foundational. The scaling source describes competition and government as existential threats, credits unscalable host work for creating customer love, treats culture as a mission lived by the founder, and says post-product-market-fit CEO work centers on hiring, strategy, and culture. The trust source names Brian with Joe and Nate as founders who recognized from the beginning that trust would be crucial to Airbnb.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats competition as a trigger for fast international scaling.
- Values intense early customer love over broad weak adoption.
- Uses founder behavior and repeated messages to transmit culture.
- Treats hiring and interviewer training as culture infrastructure.
- Frames post-fit CEO priorities as hiring, strategy, and culture.
- Is named in Airbnb's own account of trust as a founding marketplace requirement.
