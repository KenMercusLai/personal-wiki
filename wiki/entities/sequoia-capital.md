Sequoia Capital is the venture capital firm represented by Pat Grady in the Drift interview summary. The source describes Sequoia as a world-renowned VC firm and says Grady had joined it in 2007.

The article uses Sequoia as a case study in selective investing and institutional reinvention. Grady says the firm looks for fundamental companies that can define and dominate a category for decades, and describes first-meeting-to-investment odds of about 500 to 1. He also connects the firm's current operating evolution back to founder Don Valentine's original belief that brilliant engineers still needed help connecting products to human problems.
