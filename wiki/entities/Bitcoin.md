## Overview
[[Bitcoin]] is presented as the incumbent cryptocurrency whose market leadership could be threatened by merchant adoption decisions, scaling limits, and large-platform payment choices.

## Current Profile
Coin and Crypto treats Bitcoin as both a popular speculative asset and an aspirational currency. The article says Bitcoin's rapid rise attracted risky household-level investment, but also lists practical weaknesses: high volatility, difficult recovery after hacks, and slower transaction throughput than would be needed for a large retailer such as [[Amazon]]. In this frame, Bitcoin's vulnerability is not only technical competition from altcoins; it is the possibility that a major merchant platform could legitimize another cryptocurrency or proprietary token first.

## Key Characteristics
- Held the incumbent "number one cryptocurrency" position in the source's framing.
- Drew speculative investment attention despite severe volatility and recovery risks.
- Was presented as too slow for Amazon-scale checkout demand at roughly seven transactions per second.
- Needed widespread merchant adoption to function as currency rather than only as an investment vehicle.
- Faced competitive pressure from cryptocurrencies claiming better anonymity, programmability, scaling, or platform partnerships.

## Evidence
- Investment hype and risk: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] describes teenagers, families, and billionaires putting money into Bitcoin while warning about volatility and hacks.
- Throughput constraint: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] contrasts Bitcoin's transaction speed with Amazon's peak retail transaction volume.
- Merchant-adoption dependence: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] argues Bitcoin must move from investment asset to merchant-accepted currency to sustain currency ambitions.
- Platform-threat scenario: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] says Amazon adopting a rival or creating its own coin could weaken Bitcoin's top position.

## Qualifications
This page currently reflects one speculative 2017-era article, not a complete technical, monetary, regulatory, or market history of Bitcoin. The source's Amazon scenarios are hypotheses rather than evidence of later Amazon action.

## What Changed
- Created Bitcoin as a cryptocurrency incumbent vulnerable to merchant-adoption and platform-choice pressure.
