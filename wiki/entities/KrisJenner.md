## Overview
[[KrisJenner]] is the Kardashian-Jenner family manager represented as the operating and commercial coordinator behind the scaling of [[KylieJenner]]'s cosmetics business.

## Current Profile
The article places Kris Jenner between family fame and company execution. She guided Kylie's early endorsement work, brought in [[Shopify]] after the initial lip-kit sellout, found the manufacturing relationship with Seed Beauty's principals, handled finance and public relations, and helped evaluate major strategic moves. Her role was compensated through a reported 10% management share of her children's earnings, making the family network both a distribution system and a managed portfolio of commercial ventures.

## Key Characteristics
- Managed Kylie Jenner's career and major business decisions.
- Connected the first product sellout to scalable commerce and manufacturing partners.
- Handled finance and public relations for a small internal company.
- Applied experience from other Kardashian-Jenner ventures to Kylie Cosmetics.
- Occupied the central managerial position in the source's family-business infographic.
