## Overview
[[Brave]] is the browser presented by [[DanielColinJames]] as the launch platform for private, opt-in advertising mediated through [[BasicAttentionToken]].

## Current Profile
The 2018 source presents Brave as a browser that first rejects conventional advertising and tracking, then substitutes a browser-controlled market. Ads are optional, matching and attention measurement are said to remain on the device, and BAT distributes value among users, publishers, and advertisers. The article positions speed, privacy, user rewards, publisher funding, and lower fraud as mutually reinforcing, but it mostly describes a proposed or early-stage system and predicts adoption rather than evaluating mature results.

## Key Characteristics
- Blocks conventional ads and trackers by default in the source's product framing.
- Offers optional browser-delivered and publisher-page ads instead of mandatory surveillance-based advertising.
- Claims to target from locally held preference data without exposing personal data to Brave or advertisers.
- Uses BAT to reward attention and let users support sites and creators.
- Is presented as the initial proving ground for a token model intended to spread beyond browsers.

## Evidence
- Default blocking: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] describes ad and tracker blocking as the first step around the incumbent ad-tech system.
- Local private matching: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] says ads are served through the browser and personal data remains on-device.
- Revenue allocation: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] gives proposed shares for publisher ads and browser-delivered user ads.
- Ecosystem role: [[your-next-browser-will-pay-you-by-daniel-colin-james]] calls Brave a launchpad for BAT adoption in other application categories.

## Qualifications
This profile is bounded to a promotional September 2018 source. Its performance numbers, privacy guarantees, four-million-user figure, revenue percentages, advertiser analytics, and network-effect forecast are not independently verified here and should not be read as a current product description.

## What Changed
- Created Brave as the browser implementation in the source's attention-market proposal.
