## Overview
[[UnionSquareVentures]] is an early-stage venture-capital firm represented through a 55-company valuation sample and a 2018 investment thesis favoring trusted consumer brands with customer-aligned incentives.

## Current Profile
The sources present USV from two complementary angles. Wilson uses 55 new portfolio companies associated with three funds to estimate directional changes in seed and Series A pricing across North America and Europe, with the greatest density in New York City and the Bay Area. Wenger describes an evolution of the firm's investment thesis toward “trusted brands,” defining trust through incentives aligned with customers over the long term and pointing to a growing set of consumer-subscription investments across media, music, health, education, finance, and publishing.

## Key Characteristics
- Led or co-led seed, Series A, and some Series B rounds in the source's six-year sample.
- Invested across North America and Europe, with particular density in New York City and the Bay Area.
- Supplied a 55-company internal sample for comparing 2012- and 2017-timeframe valuations and round sizes.
- Had too little recent Series B activity for Wilson to infer a reliable Series B trend.
- Used customer-aligned incentives as the meaning of “trusted brands” in its 2018 thesis.
- Held a growing 2018 portfolio of consumer-subscription companies, including hybrid ad-supported and paid tiers.

## Evidence
- Portfolio composition: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] reports 16 seed, 31 Series A, and 8 Series B rounds led or co-led across 55 new USV portfolio companies.
- Geographic scope: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] says USV invests in North America and Europe and identifies the Bay Area as its most expensive market and Europe as its least expensive.
- Analytical limit: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] says the firm's recent Series B data was insufficient for a conclusion.
- Trusted-brand thesis: [[continuations-by-albert-wenger-from-advertising-to-subscriptions-and-the]] says trusted brands align company incentives with customers and end users over the long term.
- Subscription portfolio: [[continuations-by-albert-wenger-from-advertising-to-subscriptions-and-the]] names USV investments across music, health, education, finance, and publishing as evidence of the firm's interest in consumer subscriptions.

## Qualifications
This remains a narrow historical profile from two firm-affiliated essays. The 55-company sample is not a market census, and the subscription list is a point-in-time portfolio illustration rather than evidence of company outcomes. The sources do not cover USV's ownership, full fund history, complete thesis, team, performance, or investments outside the described samples.

## What Changed
- Added the trusted-brand and consumer-subscription dimension of USV's 2018 investment thesis.
- Preserved the distinction between portfolio examples and evidence of investment or company outcomes.
