## Overview
[[UnionSquareVentures]] is an early-stage venture-capital firm represented through Del.icio.us's 2005 financing, a later 55-company valuation sample, and a 2018 investment thesis favoring trusted consumer brands with customer-aligned incentives.

## Current Profile
The sources present USV from three historical angles. [[JoshuaSchachter]] says he approached the firm in early 2005 because Del.icio.us was failing under its traffic; the company raised US$1 million “on $3 million” and worked from a closet-sized office at USV, although the interview does not define the valuation convention or document the round. Wilson later uses 55 new portfolio companies associated with three funds to estimate directional changes in seed and Series A pricing across North America and Europe. Wenger then describes an evolution of the firm's investment thesis toward trusted brands with customer-aligned incentives and consumer subscriptions.

## Key Characteristics
- Led or co-led seed, Series A, and some Series B rounds in the source's six-year sample.
- Invested across North America and Europe, with particular density in New York City and the Bay Area.
- Supplied a 55-company internal sample for comparing 2012- and 2017-timeframe valuations and round sizes.
- Had too little recent Series B activity for Wilson to infer a reliable Series B trend.
- Used customer-aligned incentives as the meaning of “trusted brands” in its 2018 thesis.
- Held a growing 2018 portfolio of consumer-subscription companies, including hybrid ad-supported and paid tiers.
- Financed and housed Del.icio.us during its early 2005 effort to stabilize and grow before its Yahoo sale.

## Evidence
- Portfolio composition: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] reports 16 seed, 31 Series A, and 8 Series B rounds led or co-led across 55 new USV portfolio companies.
- Geographic scope: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] says USV invests in North America and Europe and identifies the Bay Area as its most expensive market and Europe as its least expensive.
- Analytical limit: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] says the firm's recent Series B data was insufficient for a conclusion.
- Trusted-brand thesis: [[continuations-by-albert-wenger-from-advertising-to-subscriptions-and-the]] says trusted brands align company incentives with customers and end users over the long term.
- Subscription portfolio: [[continuations-by-albert-wenger-from-advertising-to-subscriptions-and-the]] names USV investments across music, health, education, finance, and publishing as evidence of the firm's interest in consumer subscriptions.
- Del.icio.us financing: [[did-i-make-a-mistake-selling-del-icio-us-to-yahoo]] reports a US$1 million early-2005 raise “on $3 million” and a small office inside USV.

## Qualifications
This remains a narrow historical profile from two firm-affiliated essays and one founder interview. The Del.icio.us account does not define whether “on $3 million” is pre-money or post-money, name all investors, or provide financing documents. The 55-company sample is not a market census, and the subscription list is a point-in-time portfolio illustration rather than evidence of company outcomes. The sources do not cover USV's ownership, full fund history, complete thesis, team, or performance.

## What Changed
- Added Del.icio.us's reported 2005 financing and small-team office arrangement as an early historical portfolio episode.
