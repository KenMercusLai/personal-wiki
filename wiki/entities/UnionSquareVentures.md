## Overview
[[UnionSquareVentures]] is the venture-capital firm whose led or co-led early-stage investments provide the dataset for [[FredWilson]]'s 2018 valuation analysis.

## Current Profile
The source presents USV as an early-stage investor active in North America and Europe, with its greatest portfolio density in New York City and the Bay Area. Wilson reviews the 55 new portfolio companies associated with the firm's three funds since its third early-stage fund was raised in 2012, using the firm's experience to estimate directional changes in seed and Series A pricing rather than to claim a market-wide census.

## Key Characteristics
- Led or co-led seed, Series A, and some Series B rounds in the source's six-year sample.
- Invested across North America and Europe, with particular density in New York City and the Bay Area.
- Supplied a 55-company internal sample for comparing 2012- and 2017-timeframe valuations and round sizes.
- Had too little recent Series B activity for Wilson to infer a reliable Series B trend.

## Evidence
- Portfolio composition: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] reports 16 seed, 31 Series A, and 8 Series B rounds led or co-led across 55 new USV portfolio companies.
- Geographic scope: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] says USV invests in North America and Europe and identifies the Bay Area as its most expensive market and Europe as its least expensive.
- Analytical limit: [[valuation-inflation-avc]] says the firm's recent Series B data was insufficient for a conclusion.

## Qualifications
This is a narrow profile derived from one partner's descriptive article. It does not cover USV's ownership, full fund history, investment thesis, team, complete portfolio, performance, or investments the firm did not lead or co-lead.
