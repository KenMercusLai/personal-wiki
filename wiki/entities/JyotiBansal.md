## Overview
[[JyotiBansal]] is the founder of [[AppDynamics]], represented through Greylock investor [[AsheemChandna]]'s account of the company's formation, financing, leadership succession, and sale to [[Cisco]].

## Current Profile
Bansal left Computer Associates after its acquisition of Wily and began developing an application-monitoring venture first called Singularity Technologies. Chandna describes him as a technically strong founder with a clear view of application intelligence and automation, then recounts moving from an introduction and whiteboard session to a Series A term sheet within days.

Bansal led AppDynamics through its early product and customer formation. As the company prepared to scale toward public markets, he and the board recruited [[DavidWadhwani]] as CEO; Bansal became chairman rather than remaining the operating CEO.

## Key Characteristics
- Founded the company that became AppDynamics after working at Computer Associates/Wily.
- Articulated an early application-intelligence and automation thesis.
- Converted an investor introduction into rapid Series A financing.
- Built the early company with first hire [[BhaskarSunkara]].
- Participated in a deliberate CEO succession as AppDynamics entered a later scaling stage.
