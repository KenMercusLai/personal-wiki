## Overview
[[GoogleAnalytics]] is the incumbent analytics product that the source uses as the contrast case for Crazy Egg's heatmap positioning.

## Current Profile
The source presents Google Analytics as a widely used free analytics product that still left some users with unmet needs. [[CrazyEgg]] used those gaps, especially the desire to visualize page clicks through heatmaps, as the basis for differentiated product messaging.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves as the incumbent analytics reference point in the Crazy Egg case.
- Is described as free and useful to many people.
- Lacked the heatmap-specific behavior visualization that Crazy Egg emphasized.

## Evidence
- Incumbent role: [[acquisition-is-easy-retention-is-hard-product-habits]] says Crazy Egg entered a market where Google Analytics already existed.
- Free and useful baseline: [[acquisition-is-easy-retention-is-hard-product-habits]] says Google Analytics was free and helped many users.
- Missing feature: [[acquisition-is-easy-retention-is-hard-product-habits]] says dissatisfied users wanted heatmapping to track every click on the page.

## Qualifications
The source uses Google Analytics only as a 2017 comparison point for Crazy Egg. It does not describe Google Analytics' full feature set, ownership, or later product changes.

## What Changed
- Created Google Analytics as the incumbent contrast in Crazy Egg's positioning case.
