Source: [7 Questions to Ask Founders Before Joining Their Startup]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/7-questions-founders-before-joining-startup.md" >}})

CarDash is the company context behind the startup diligence source. The article uses CarDash as a first-person example when discussing the tension between slow hiring and fast growth, noting that hiring too quickly to meet short-term goals can compromise long-term culture.

The source also mentions CarDash's Y Combinator affiliation as something the company is proud of, while cautioning that accelerator participation is not itself a business milestone. It is useful only insofar as it accelerates business milestones.

In the article's final example, CarDash is framed around belief in the mission: people who join understand the problems in automotive service and want to modernize the customer service experience.
