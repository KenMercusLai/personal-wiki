## Overview
[[AlexSchleifer]] is represented as Airbnb's then-VP of Design and an advocate for treating product design as an intentional organizational discipline rather than a late-stage visual service.

## Current Profile
Schleifer connects design's talent-pipeline problem to its unclear public identity, inconsistent organizational placement, narrow career paths, and fragmented production conventions. His proposed remedy joins co-equal engineering-product-design leadership, parallel IC and management advancement, and operational systems that align tools, components, and language across functions.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames product design as an under-defined and still-maturing profession.
- Advocates co-equal design, engineering, and product-management authority from inception through launch.
- Separates senior individual-contributor advancement from people management.
- Treats design operations, technical fluency, and shared terminology as supports for creative craft.

## Evidence
- Professional diagnosis: [[defining-product-design-a-dispatch-from-airbnbs-design-chief-first-round-review]] identifies weak role education, nonstandard organization, and few visible role models as barriers to design's maturity.
- Organizational model: [[defining-product-design-a-dispatch-from-airbnbs-design-chief-first-round-review]] describes Airbnb's peer EPD leaders and co-equal project leads.

- Operating discipline: [[defining-product-design-a-dispatch-from-airbnbs-design-chief-first-round-review]] links design ops, component systems, file conventions, and shared vocabulary to cross-functional speed.
