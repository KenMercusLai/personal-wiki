## Overview
[[MariamNaficy]] is presented as a founder-operator whose scaling lessons concern internal leadership development, outside executive hiring, prioritization, and post-fit growth.

## Current Profile
The source uses Naficy to show that companies can combine internally grown leaders with outside specialists. It also uses her CEO-role comments to show how founders move away from knowing every detail and toward objectives, numbers, product, strategy, and new growth avenues.

- Treats unique company knowledge as easier to grow internally than import.

- Prioritizes initiatives by projected revenue growth.
- Sees post-fit hurdles in revenue scaling, new verticals, and new geographies.
