## Overview
[[Desk]] is [[JohnSaddington]]'s OS X blogging application and the product promoted in his two [[DaringFireball]] sponsorships.

## Current Profile
Desk is presented as a minimal writing-focused desktop application supporting Markdown, WYSIWYG editing, and direct publishing to multiple blogging platforms. The first sponsorship coincided with a sharp sales increase, high Mac App Store rankings, extensive review interest, press testimonials, and Apple's Best of 2014 recognition. The source does not isolate which outcome the sponsorship caused, and the second campaign was weaker.

## Key Characteristics
- Mac-exclusive blogging and writing application in the source period.
- Supported Markdown and WYSIWYG editing.
- Published directly to WordPress, Blogger, Tumblr, Typepad, Movable Type, Facebook Notes, and Squarespace.
- Used a deliberately minimal interface centered on prose.
- Reached high Mac App Store productivity and overall-sales ranks after the first sponsorship.
- Was named among Apple's Best Apps of 2014 according to the source.
