## Overview
[[Salesforce]] appears as an early company context where [[BillMacaitis]] built custom [[MarketingAttribution]] capability before the attribution-vendor market matured.

## Current Profile
The source uses Salesforce primarily as the first stage in Macaitis's attribution experience. It says he built his own system there because the company had many custom tracking needs around events and fewer vendor options were available. Salesforce therefore functions as a case for when build-versus-buy decisions may favor internal attribution infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- SaaS company context for early attribution-system building.
- Example where custom event tracking needs pushed toward building internally.
- Part of the Salesforce-Zendesk-Slack sequence used to argue that measured marketing can become a growth advantage.

- Custom build: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] says Macaitis built his own system at Salesforce because of custom event-tracking needs and a thinner vendor market.
- Growth sequence: [[attribution-marketing-creating-a-growth-engine-at-salesforce-zendesk-and-slack-for-entrepreneurs]] places Salesforce alongside Zendesk and Slack as companies where attribution-supported marketing became a competitive advantage.
