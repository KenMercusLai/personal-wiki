## Overview
[[VikramAdve|Vikram S. Adve]] is a computer scientist, [[LLVM]] co-creator, and co-author of the retrospective source; the article identifies him as the Donald B. Gillies Professor of Computer Science at UIUC.

## Current Profile
Adve framed the research goal that became LLVM: flexible dynamic-compilation techniques for arbitrary programming languages rather than a runtime tied to one managed-language model. His 2000 NSF CAREER proposal supported much of the project's early work, and his Advanced Compiler Construction teaching supplied design ideas including SSA, separation of concerns, retargetable code generation, and interprocedural compilation. The source also connects his research group to later work on virtual instruction sets, pointer analysis, memory safety, and secure system execution.

## Key Characteristics
- Formulated the broad research program for language-independent static and dynamic compilation.
- Co-designed LLVM's founding goals with Chris Lattner.
- Secured the NSF CAREER funding described as essential to early development and open-source distribution.
- Connected established compiler research ideas into the project's initial design goals and teaching context.
- Led or supported related research in virtual ISAs, interprocedural analysis, memory safety, and secure execution.
