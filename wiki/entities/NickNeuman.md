## Overview
[[NickNeuman]] is represented in this wiki as the author of a late-2017 essay about token-based employee compensation and internal workplace markets.

## Current Profile
Neuman proposes that actively traded project tokens could give employees earlier liquidity than private-company options, let later employees buy ownership exposure, and support peer payments for tasks or recognition. His hand-drawn motivation cycle links ownership and profit to morale, product effort, adoption, and token value. He also foregrounds failure conditions: short-term price promotion, weak market liquidity, insider trading, privacy-enabled evasion, and the need for vesting, supervision, and holdings transparency.

## Key Characteristics
- Explores employee tokens as incentive and internal-market infrastructure rather than only as fundraising assets.
- Treats early liquidity as both a recruiting advantage and a source of short-termism.
- Presents the model as conditional on genuine token utility, durable liquidity, governance, and long-term alignment.

## Evidence
- Token compensation thesis: [[employee-incentives-in-a-tokenized-world-token-economy]] compares employee tokens with stock options and proposes secondary ownership and peer exchange.

- Risk boundary: [[employee-incentives-in-a-tokenized-world-token-economy]] raises market-depth, short-termism, insider-conduct, privacy, and enforcement questions rather than presenting token liquidity as unconditionally beneficial.
