## Overview
[[ACSMHealthAndFitnessJournal]] is a practitioner-facing publication of the [[AmericanCollegeOfSportsMedicine]] described as translating health and fitness science into practical professional guidance.

## Current Profile
The 2013 editorial defines the journal by its intermediary role: it packages research and professional topics for people working directly with patients and clients. The previewed issue combines a forward-looking fitness-trends survey with applied features on hydration and cognition and waist circumference and cardiometabolic risk, plus columns on probiotics, bone health, Pilates, ethics, injury prevention, foot blisters, and energy. That breadth demonstrates editorial scope, but the source does not evaluate the articles' methods or the journal's effect on practice.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves health and fitness professionals rather than presenting itself solely to research specialists.
- Treats translation of scientific work into practical steps as a core purpose.
- Combines annual field-orientation material with applied topic features.
- Uses recurring columns to cover clinical, ethical, training, preventive, and client-education questions.

## Qualifications
This profile is based on the editor-in-chief's promotional introduction to one 2013 issue. It does not establish the publication's current scope, editorial independence, peer-review model, audience reach, evidence quality, or effects on professional decisions and health outcomes.
