## Overview
[[RoniSmith]] is represented in the wiki as a Thoughtworks recruiting leader and co-author of operational guidance for scaling talent acquisition and inclusion.

## Current Profile
In the source, Smith helps frame hiring as a capacity-planned, measurable partnership between recruiting and the business. The article joins funnel design, interviewer training, candidate feedback, internal mobility, junior-talent development, deliberate diversity work, and post-hire effectiveness into one talent system.

## Key Characteristics
- Co-authors a system-level account of recruiting during scaleup growth.
- Emphasizes recruiting capacity, operational support, measurement, and continuous improvement.
- Treats candidate experience, inclusion, internal opportunity, and onboarding readiness as hiring outcomes.

## Evidence
- Recruiting operations: [[martin-fowler-thoughtworks-bottleneck-02-talent]] describes recruiter capacity planning, coordinator support, funnel tooling, business partnership, and process mapping.
- Candidate feedback: [[martin-fowler-thoughtworks-bottleneck-02-talent]] uses surveys and segmented NPS as examples of process-learning inputs.
- Inclusion and development: [[martin-fowler-thoughtworks-bottleneck-02-talent]] recommends deliberate sourcing, evidence-based interviews, referral limits, internal posting, and junior-talent programs.
