## Overview
[[BlackMagic]] is a Twitter analytics, CRM, and engagement product created by [[TonyDinh]] and later sold after Twitter API pricing changed.

## Current Profile
Black Magic began as a follower-progress ring around Dinh's profile image and added a $4 monthly customization tier. The Magic Sidebar extension shifted the product toward analytics and CRM for larger accounts; Dinh reports growth from roughly $300 MRR when he left employment to $4,000 by February 2022, $13,349 in October 2022, and about $14,000 before an API-pricing shock led to a $128,000 sale.

## Key Characteristics
- Subscription product originating from a public coding experiment.
- Customer-value pivot from novelty engagement feature to analytics and CRM utility.
- Product whose growth and marketing were intertwined with Dinh's Twitter audience.
- Platform-dependent business exposed to API access and pricing decisions.
- First reported recurring-revenue product and exit in Dinh's portfolio.

## Evidence
- Origin and first subscriber: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] shows the progress-ring experiment, $4 membership, and first paying member.
- Product pivot and growth: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] connects the Magic Sidebar beta and launch to the climb toward $4,000 MRR and later shows $13,349 MRR.
- Platform shock and exit: [[tony-dinh-my-solopreneur-story-zero-to-45kmo-in-2-years]] reports the end of free API access, a quoted $42,000 monthly tier, and a $128,000 sale.

## Qualifications
Revenue, sale price, causal attribution to the sidebar, and the affordability of API tiers are reported by Dinh without contracts, cost records, churn data, or buyer evidence. The retained Twitter announcement establishes the free-access policy change but not the later quoted enterprise price.
