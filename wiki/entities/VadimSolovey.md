## Overview
[[VadimSolovey]] is the author of a 2017 [[DoiTInternational]] case study about replacing [[Mixpanel]] with a custom Google Cloud analytics pipeline for [[JellyButtonGames]].

## Current Profile
The source presents Solovey as a cloud practitioner documenting a production architecture rather than as an independent evaluator. His account covers the requirements, service boundaries, autoscaling design, streaming transformation, reported costs, and five-week delivery timeline.

## Key Characteristics
- Writes from the implementation side of a client cloud-migration project.
- Emphasizes low-latency ingestion, loss avoidance, near-real-time analysis, and elastic capacity.
- Frames architecture value through both operating cost and analytics flexibility.
