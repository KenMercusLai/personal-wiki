## Overview
[[Botangle]] was an online education company founded by [[ErikFinman]] in 2014 to connect frustrated students with teachers over video chat.

## Current Profile
Finman funded Botangle with roughly $100,000 from selling his initial [[Bitcoin]] investment. CNBC reports that he found a buyer for the company's technology in January 2015 and chose payment of 300 bitcoin, then worth a little over $200 each, instead of a $100,000 cash offer. The source does not describe product usage, learning outcomes, revenue, or the buyer.

## Key Characteristics
- Addressed student frustration by matching learners with teachers through video chat.
- Was financed from Finman's early Bitcoin gains rather than a reported institutional funding round.
- Produced a technology sale with a choice between cash and Bitcoin consideration.

## Evidence
- Product and purpose: [[cnbc-erik-finman-bitcoin-millionaire-who-skipped-college]] describes Botangle as an online education company for finding teachers over video chat.
- Founding capital: [[cnbc-erik-finman-bitcoin-millionaire-who-skipped-college]] says Finman used about $100,000 from his first Bitcoin sale to launch it.
- Exit terms: [[cnbc-erik-finman-bitcoin-millionaire-who-skipped-college]] reports that an unnamed buyer offered $100,000 cash or 300 bitcoin for the technology.

## Qualifications
The available source is a founder-centered profile, not a company history. It gives no evidence about learner volume, teacher supply, educational quality, revenue, retention, transaction structure, intellectual-property scope, or post-sale operation.
