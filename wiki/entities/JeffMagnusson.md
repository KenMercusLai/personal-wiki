## Overview
[[JeffMagnusson]] is the author of a 2016 Stitch Fix Technology essay proposing a platform-centered alternative to thinker-doer handoffs in data-science departments.

## Current Profile
Magnusson presents the model from his experience at [[StitchFix]] and from interviewing data-platform engineers. He argues that data scientists should own domain-specific work from ETL through production algorithms or APIs, while engineers should maximize leverage by building horizontal platforms, services, abstractions, and reliability mechanisms. His account emphasizes autonomy and accountability over specialization efficiency and warns that platform teams must anticipate needs before local teams create fragmented substitutes.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates end-to-end production ownership for data scientists.
- Frames platform engineering as horizontal enablement rather than downstream implementation labor.
- Treats autonomy, accountability, and iteration velocity as deliberate organizational design goals.
- Presents the model as a context-specific Stitch Fix blueprint rather than a universal optimum.
