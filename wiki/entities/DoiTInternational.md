## Overview
[[DoiTInternational]] is the cloud consultancy that co-designed and implemented [[JellyButtonGames]]'s custom event-analytics pipeline in the source.

## Current Profile
The article presents DoiT as helping startups architect, build, and operate data systems on Google Cloud Platform and AWS. In this case it worked with Jelly Button personnel to move product analytics from Mixpanel to a pipeline built from GKE, Pub/Sub, Dataflow, and BigQuery.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides cloud architecture and data-engineering services.
- Works across Google Cloud Platform and AWS in the source's description.
- Participated in architecture, proof-of-concept development, coding, and live-traffic testing.
