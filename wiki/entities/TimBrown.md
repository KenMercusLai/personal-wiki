## Overview
[[TimBrown]] is represented here as an [[IDEO]] leader arguing that data, algorithms, and machine learning constitute a new medium for design.

## Current Profile
Brown frames the [[Datascope]] acquisition as a response to systems that evolve more like living processes than static artifacts. In his account, designers need enough imagination and technical skill to shape algorithmic systems toward desirable human outcomes, and those systems need to learn with people. He expects masterful [[HumanCenteredDesign]] to improve AI overall while conceding that individual projects can fail because teams misunderstand a problem, are still learning, or ask the wrong initial questions.

## Key Characteristics
- Presents data science and machine learning as materials for design.
- Distinguishes adaptive systems from artifacts that can be designed once and left unchanged.
- Argues for systems that learn collaboratively with human beings.
- Defends human-centered design as generally beneficial without claiming universal success.
- Identifies mobility, circular economy, and education as prospective design-and-data domains.

## Qualifications
This profile derives from one announcement-era interview and records Brown's strategic argument rather than a demonstrated causal result. The article provides no evaluation of the acquisition, no implemented AI case with measured outcomes, and no evidence that design methods alone can resolve discrimination, opacity, or systemic inequality.

## What Changed
- Created Brown's profile around algorithms as design media and adaptive human-system collaboration.
- Preserved his distinction between general improvement and guaranteed success in particular cases.
