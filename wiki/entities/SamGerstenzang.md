## Overview
[[SamGerstenzang]] is a startup and venture-capital writer represented here by a 2017 argument against unconditional “founder friendly” board behavior.

## Current Profile
Gerstenzang presents company-building as the board's objective and treats founder status as neither a guarantee of permanent CEO fit nor permission for investor domination. His account gives founders an information and intuition advantage from operating close to the market, while assigning directors responsibility for cross-company pattern recognition, executive standards, difficult succession judgments, and moral independence. He also distinguishes formal board oversight from a confidential coaching relationship, arguing that each role is useful but carries different obligations.

## Key Characteristics
- Advocates company-centered rather than founder-centered startup governance.
- Treats founder leadership fit as stage-dependent rather than permanent.
- Gives founder operating knowledge substantial weight against board micromanagement.
- Expects directors to challenge, evaluate, and act rather than offer unconditional personal support.
- Separates the board's evaluative duty from an investor's coaching or consigliere role.

## Evidence
- Governance objective: [[founder-friendly-no-thanks-sam-gerstenzang-medium]] says boards and founders are there to build companies and products rather than protect egos.
- Stage-dependent leadership: [[founder-friendly-no-thanks-sam-gerstenzang-medium]] distinguishes the CEO capabilities needed before product-market fit from those needed for incentives and new product lines at scale.
- Founder advantage: [[founder-friendly-no-thanks-sam-gerstenzang-medium]] says operating proximity gives founders more detail, repetitions, market understanding, and valuable intuition than directors usually possess.
- Director duty: [[founder-friendly-no-thanks-sam-gerstenzang-medium]] argues that experienced board members should recognize excellence and recurring growth patterns while supplying effort and moral backbone.
- Role separation: [[founder-friendly-no-thanks-sam-gerstenzang-medium]] uses the conflict between grading and teaching to distinguish board oversight from coaching without a board seat.

## Qualifications
The profile derives from one short, strongly worded practitioner essay. It does not establish Gerstenzang's broader career, investment record, board experience, or later views, and it provides no comparative outcome evidence for founder succession. Its historical references to Plaxo, Facebook, Benchmark, and Uber are illustrative and incomplete rather than independently documented cases.
