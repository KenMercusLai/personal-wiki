## Overview
[[AndreChaperon|André Chaperon]] is represented here as a marketer and creator documenting a personal knowledge-processing workflow for writing and idea development.

## Current Profile
Chaperon describes moving from scattered tools and unprocessed captures toward a personal adaptation of Zettelkasten. His workflow uses quick paper or mobile capture, permanent notes written in his own words, contextual retrieval, plain-text storage, and an optional visual layer. He evaluates the system by whether it supplies usable material for creative work rather than by note volume alone.

## Key Characteristics
- Treats personal knowledge management as upstream infrastructure for writing and creation.
- Uses a modified Zettelkasten rather than claiming strict historical fidelity.
- Prioritizes low-friction capture, contextual retrieval, and open plain-text storage.
- Uses visual maps alongside prose because he identifies as a visual thinker.
- Presents the workflow as an evolving personal practice rather than a finished universal method.
