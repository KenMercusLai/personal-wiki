## Overview
[[EmanuelMaidenberg]] is a UCLA clinical professor quoted by NPR as an outside commentator on research about buying time through time-saving services.

## Current Profile
Within this wiki, Maidenberg functions as the article's cautious clinical voice. He treats time-saving services as a potentially interesting stress-management tool, but he emphasizes that the evidence still leaves open whether the positive-emotion effect is sustained or mainly immediate.

## Key Characteristics
- Clinical professor of psychiatry and biobehavioral sciences at UCLA.
