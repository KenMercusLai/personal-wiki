## Overview
[[TripAdvisor]] is a travel-review platform used in the source as an example of SEO, user-generated content, badges, and partnerships reinforcing growth.

## Current Profile
The source says TripAdvisor's search-engine understanding and steady addition of reviews helped it rank for travel searches. It also describes hotel review badges as a two-sided growth mechanism: hotels receive visible proof, while TripAdvisor gains backlinks and traffic.

## Key Characteristics
- Travel review and recommendation platform.
- Benefits from a growing body of searchable user reviews.
- Uses hotel badges to turn review proof into referral links.
- Partners with American Express to combine booking and review activity.

## Evidence
- SEO and content: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says TripAdvisor ranks highly in travel searches partly because reviews continually add searchable content.
- Badge loop: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says hotels display badges that link traffic back to TripAdvisor.
- Partnership: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] describes the American Express partnership for card-linked booking and review activity.

## Qualifications
The source does not separate SEO, review supply, backlinks, brand, partnerships, and travel demand as causal factors.
