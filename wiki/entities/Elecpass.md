## Overview
[[Elecpass]] is Wang Ziting's side project used in the retrospective as a positive example of releasing quickly, using the product personally, and returning for focused iteration.

## Current Profile
Elecpass matters in the source because it spent most of its life in a released state. Wang Ziting says he released two versions in about half a month in October 2017, used the project himself for more than a year, and then spent a concentrated week in November 2018 shipping v3 with filtering, layout improvements, stronger editing, and a Windows build.

## Key Characteristics
- Demonstrates fast early release and staged improvement.
- Stayed usable and released rather than sitting with half-finished features.
- Benefited from the author's own long-term use before a focused v3 update.
- Shows side-project scope control through concentrated iteration.
