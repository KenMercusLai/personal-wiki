## Overview
[[MichaelPorter]] is a Harvard Business School strategy scholar cited in the source for defining differentiation as an effort to achieve a profitable, unique market position.

## Current Profile
The source uses Porter as the canonical strategy reference for [[DifferentiationStrategy]]. His role in the article is not biographical; he supplies the conceptual anchor that differentiation is about strategic position, not surface-level messaging.

## Key Characteristics
- Strategy scholar associated with competitive positioning.
- Cited as defining differentiation around profitable uniqueness.
- Used as an authority for treating differentiation as business strategy rather than copywriting.
