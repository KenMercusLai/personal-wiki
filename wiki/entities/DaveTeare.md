## Overview
[[DaveTeare]] is represented in the source as an [[AgileBits]] cofounder and developer who helped turn an internal form-filling idea into [[OnePassword]].

## Current Profile
In the 2013 interview, Teare describes a founder path driven by platform enthusiasm, direct user contact, and iterative product commitment. His account links 1Password's survival beyond its planned month to customer feedback, then shows support conversations identifying desktop-free and less-technical users whom the original Mac-centered assumptions did not fit. He also articulates a real operating tradeoff: collecting less behavioral data was consistent with a security company's values but left product allocation decisions dependent on support signals and judgment.

## Key Characteristics
- Cofounded AgileBits with [[RoustemKarimov]] after both left work at World Vision Canada.
- Helped originate 1Password as OS X Form for repetitive web-form testing.
- Treats direct customer feedback as both product evidence and founder motivation.
- Describes platform change as a reason to iterate and eventually rewrite the iOS product.
- Accepts reduced measurement as a consequence of limiting user-data collection.

## Evidence
- Founder origin: [[dave-teare-at-wwdc-how-one-month-for-1password-became-8-years-the-mac-observer]] says Teare and Karimov moved from World Vision Canada into independent software work after adopting Macs.
- Product origin: [[dave-teare-at-wwdc-how-one-month-for-1password-became-8-years-the-mac-observer]] traces OS X Form from saved test forms to password storage and the 1Password name.
- Customer orientation: [[dave-teare-at-wwdc-how-one-month-for-1password-became-8-years-the-mac-observer]] says direct feedback caused repeated extensions of the original one-month project.
- Platform response: [[dave-teare-at-wwdc-how-one-month-for-1password-became-8-years-the-mac-observer]] describes bookmarklet-based iOS access, continued iteration, and a full 1Password 4 rewrite.
- Privacy tradeoff: [[dave-teare-at-wwdc-how-one-month-for-1password-became-8-years-the-mac-observer]] says the company avoided broad user tracking and therefore could not precisely answer segment questions.

## Qualifications
This profile rests on one promotional-context WWDC interview and Teare's retrospective account. It does not independently establish founding causality, product security, sales performance, user composition, or whether the complete rewrite was the best engineering choice.
