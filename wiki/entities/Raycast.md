## Overview
[[Raycast]] is a Mac productivity tool represented here through [[MaiYang]]'s report that keyboard-driven use reduced reliance on both mouse and trackpad.

## Current Profile
The source presents Raycast as a tool that became embedded enough in Mai Yang's workflow to generate repeated recommendations, an internal team demonstration, and a small onboarding website. The retained post also embeds another user's claim that extensive Raycast keyboard shortcuts reduced mouse use. Together these observations show enthusiastic practitioner adoption, not comparative evidence that Raycast improves productivity for typical users.

## Key Characteristics
- Supports a keyboard-centered way of interacting with a Mac in the reported workflows.
- Can become a subject for peer teaching, team sharing, and setup demonstrations.
- Is represented through user testimony rather than a feature inventory or measured comparison.

## Evidence
- Workflow substitution: [[what-my-most-read-tweets-taught-me-about-the-twitter-algorithm]] says Mai Yang used the trackpad less after adopting Raycast and quotes another user who reported barely touching the mouse.
- Advocacy and teaching: [[what-my-most-read-tweets-taught-me-about-the-twitter-algorithm]] says Mai Yang shared Raycast repeatedly, presented it internally, and built a small getting-started site.
- Visible response: [[what-my-most-read-tweets-taught-me-about-the-twitter-algorithm]] retains a screenshot showing 20,000 views, 3 replies, 2 reposts, 62 likes, and 90 bookmarks at capture time.

## Qualifications
The source does not inventory Raycast's capabilities, compare it with Spotlight or other launchers, measure time saved, or separate product utility from familiarity and shortcut investment. The embedded user statement and Mai Yang's account are testimonials, while the visible post metrics establish distribution rather than product effectiveness.
