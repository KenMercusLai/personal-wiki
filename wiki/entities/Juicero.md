## Overview
[[Juicero]] was a food-technology startup whose 2017 CEO note defended a connected system for delivering and pressing fresh raw produce.

## Current Profile
In this source, Juicero presents itself as a long-term attempt to make fresh, raw fruits and vegetables easier and more enjoyable to consume. The company frames its product as a system rather than a standalone appliance: a Press, Produce Packs, app reminders, direct organic-produce sourcing, doorstep delivery, and supply-chain data are supposed to work together. The note appears during a public criticism cycle about hacking and hand-squeezing Produce Packs, so Juicero's profile is inseparable from a contested value proposition.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames its mission around healthier habits, nutrition, obesity challenges, and raw plant-based food.
- Treats hardware, software, produce, sourcing, logistics, and customer reminders as one connected product system.
- Defends the Press as food-safety and quality infrastructure, not merely a squeezing device.
- Uses transparency, listening language, and a refund guarantee to answer public criticism.
- Depends on customers perceiving value beyond the liquid output of a Produce Pack.

## Evidence
- Mission framing: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] says Juicero's mission is to make fresh raw fruits and vegetables dramatically easier and more enjoyable to consume.
- System design: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] describes the Press, Produce Packs, app, organic sourcing, farm relationships, and doorstep delivery as parts of one system.
- Press role: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] says the connected Press enables recall response, calibrated pressing, and supply-chain management for produce with about an eight-day lifespan.
- Public response: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] extends a 30-day Happiness Guarantee refund to any customer who feels Juicero is not making healthy habits easier.
- Value tension: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] argues that hand-squeezing misses the intended system value while acknowledging headlines about hacking and hand-squeezing.

## Qualifications
The source is a company-authored CEO note written during reputational pressure, so it should be read as advocacy rather than independent evidence of customer satisfaction, economics, safety performance, or product-market fit. It does not provide customer data, refund uptake, production costs, or external validation of the claimed system benefits.

## What Changed
- Created Juicero as a connected food-hardware startup case where system value became publicly contested.
