## Overview
[[HubSpot]] is an inbound-marketing software company used in the source as an example of free tools and educational content driving demand.

## Current Profile
The source presents HubSpot as both seller and proof of inbound marketing. Blogs and ebooks attract marketers, while the free Website Grader diagnoses site problems and routes users toward HubSpot's paid services.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides tools for marketers managing inbound marketing.
- Uses its own content marketing as proof of its product philosophy.
- Offers Website Grader as a free diagnostic and acquisition tool.
