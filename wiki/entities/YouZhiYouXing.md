## Overview
[[YouZhiYouXing]] is an investment-related organization represented in this wiki through [[MengYan]]'s retrospective on its mission and product philosophy.

## Current Profile
The source places YouZhiYouXing at the intersection of investor education, fund advisory, and choice architecture. Meng Yan links its founding-era ambition to reducing Chinese stock-market volatility and helping more people benefit from long-term growth, then uses the “four pots of money” model as an example of changing the investor's first question from product selection to life purpose and time horizon.

The resulting profile is mission-led but source-limited. The essay explains the desired behavioral and market effects of the approach; it does not provide product adoption, client outcome, revenue, risk, or comparative performance evidence.

## Key Characteristics
- Frames investing as a long-term participation problem rather than only a fund-selection problem.
- Uses purpose and time horizon to separate liquid, stable, long-term, and protection needs before product choice.
- Treats interface defaults and investor prompts as consequential allocation mechanisms.
- Associates advisory value with diversification, accompaniment, and behavioral support rather than recent-return promotion.
- Holds an explicit ambition to improve investor outcomes and reduce market volatility.
