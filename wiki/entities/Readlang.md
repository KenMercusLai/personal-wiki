## Overview
[[Readlang]] is the language-learning web app that [[SteveRidout]] spent three years building as a one-person startup.

## Current Profile
The source presents Readlang as a small but growing bootstrapped product with real user affection and low operating expenses. It is not portrayed as an explosive startup success; instead, it is a ramen-profitable product whose founder sees enough growth and learner benefit to justify continuing despite weak near-term income.

## Key Characteristics
- Serves language learners through a web-app product rather than consulting or content alone.
- Grew new signups from 2,800 in year one to 36,800 in year three.
- Grew yearly revenue from roughly $700 to roughly $16,500 over the same period.
- Remained low-expense enough to produce about $14,500 profit in year three.
- Earned user testimonials describing it as sticky and meaningfully helpful for language learning.
- Still left its founder earning less than minimum wage relative to the time invested.

## Evidence
- Product identity: [[3-years-as-a-one-man-startup-steve-ridout-medium]] describes Readlang as the language-learning web app Ridout built over three years.
- Signup growth: [[3-years-as-a-one-man-startup-steve-ridout-medium]] reports 2,800, 9,300, and 36,800 new signups across the first three years.
- Revenue and profit: [[3-years-as-a-one-man-startup-steve-ridout-medium]] reports about $700, $4,100, and $16,500 revenue, with about $14,500 profit in year three.
- User value: [[3-years-as-a-one-man-startup-steve-ridout-medium]] includes user feedback comparing Readlang favorably with Memrise, Anki, and FluentU.
- Founder economics: [[3-years-as-a-one-man-startup-steve-ridout-medium]] says the founder still earned less than a minimum-wage job after accounting for time.
