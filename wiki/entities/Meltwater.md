## Overview
[[Meltwater]] is represented here through a historical customer account of buying access to media-contact data after finding the company's homepage messaging difficult to interpret.

## Current Profile
The source describes Meltwater's underlying offer as a paid database of reporters' email addresses and phone numbers, while its captured homepage foregrounded “Welcome to Outside Insight,” lifestyle imagery, media-intelligence language, and a demo request. The author eventually found a phone number, spoke with a salesperson, bought the product, and reports being encouraged to add higher-priced features. This supports a narrow profile of the historical sales experience, not a current or comprehensive company description.

## Key Characteristics
- Offered the source author paid access to media-contact information.
- Used broad “Outside Insight” positioning on the captured homepage.
- Routed the author toward a human sales conversation before purchase.
- Served as the essay's primary example of tension between a simple customer job and abstract B2B messaging.
- Is associated by the author with an upsell-oriented explanation for homepage ambiguity.
