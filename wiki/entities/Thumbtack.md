## Overview
[[Thumbtack]] is a local-services marketplace company used in the source as the operating context for assembling an experienced executive leadership team.

## Current Profile
The article describes Thumbtack as a company launched in 2008 to connect consumers with local professionals and as a founder-led startup that eventually filled executive seats across engineering, product, marketplace, finance, HR, operations, marketing, and legal. Within the wiki, its main significance is not the marketplace model itself but the organizational lesson that a scaling company needs experienced functional leaders.

- Local-services marketplace connecting consumers with professionals.
- Founded in 2008 and described as having supported millions of projects across the United States.
- Serves as Zappacosta's case for moving from founder reinvention toward experienced executive leadership.
