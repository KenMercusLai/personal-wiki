## Overview
[[Thumbtack]] is a local-services marketplace represented both as the operating context for executive-team building and as a category-horizontal platform spanning multiple household service needs.

## Current Profile
The First Round Review article describes Thumbtack as a company launched in 2008 to connect consumers with local professionals and as a founder-led startup that eventually filled executive seats across engineering, product, marketplace, finance, HR, operations, marketing, and legal. Breinlinger's essay adds a marketplace-design interpretation: people may need both a painter and a handyman, so a broad local-services platform can serve adjacent jobs more efficiently than separate micro-vertical apps.

## Key Characteristics
- Local-services marketplace connecting consumers with professionals.
- Category-horizontal coverage across multiple household service needs.
- Founded in 2008 and described as having supported millions of projects across the United States.
- Serves as Zappacosta's case for moving from founder reinvention toward experienced executive leadership.
- Filled senior seats across multiple functions, including engineering, product, marketplace, finance, HR, operations, marketing, and general counsel.
- Executive hiring at Thumbtack emphasizes role-specific strengths, references, fit, and vulnerability.
