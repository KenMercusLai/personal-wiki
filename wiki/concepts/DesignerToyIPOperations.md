## Definition
[[DesignerToyIPOperations]] is the business practice of cultivating character IP through repeated product design, retail presentation, category expansion, licensing, collaborations, and ongoing audience recognition.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents designer-toy IP operations as Pop Mart's deeper business model. A character such as [[Labubu]] can persist only if it is maintained: the operator keeps it visible, refreshes forms and styling, and expands it from collectible figures into plush goods, accessories, daily products, collaborations, licensing, and commercial spaces. This makes IP operations more dynamic than selling one fixed toy, but it also creates management risk if novelty turns into overexposure.

## Key Claims
- IP longevity depends more on active maintenance than on the character's initial quality alone.
- Refreshing poses, styling, materials, and scenes can renew customer interest and reach new groups.
- Category expansion widens revenue beyond collectible toys into consumable or daily-use goods.
- Licensing, OEM production, and brand collaborations can expand an IP without owning every production asset.
- Daily-use products can both generate replacement demand and reinforce recognition.
- Overuse remains a real risk because broad exposure can make a character feel stale or cheap.

## Evidence
- Maintenance logic: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] compares IP to a durable building that still needs repair to remain present over time.
- Design refresh: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] says a humanoid character such as [[Labubu]] can keep changing poses, decorations, and styles.
- Category expansion: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] describes moving from vinyl figures into plush, pillows, pendants, accessories, apparel, daily goods, parks, and other uses.
- Asset-light expansion: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] notes that categories outside designer toys can use outsourcing, licensing, and co-branding.
- Overexposure qualification: [[pao-pao-ma-te-yin-wei-du-te-du-hou-gan-he-wang-ning-mai-xing-xing-bang-de-gu-shi-li-ji-ao]] says the important concern is whether management excessively drains Labubu's value.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source cannot answer whether category expansion will offset a trend decline in [[Labubu]]. It also relies on analogy to long-lived IP such as Hello Kitty and Mickey Mouse without proving that Pop Mart's characters will follow the same path.
