## Definition
[[NineBoxMediaOperatingModel]] is BuzzFeed's matrix for organizing three content engines against three revenue streams, producing nine strategic work areas.

## Current Synthesis
The source's diagram makes BuzzFeed's strategy operational. Columns represent BuzzFeed, BuzzFeed Media Brands such as Tasty, Nifty, and Goodful, and BuzzFeed News. Rows represent advertising, commerce, and studio. Each cell names revenue opportunities or example projects, so teams can see where their work contributes and where cross-functional teams need to support growth.

## Key Claims
- The matrix turns a broad diversification strategy into visible operating areas.
- Content engines are expected to grow revenue across more than one business line.
- Support teams such as technology, product, marketing, research, legal, communications, finance, and people are framed as cross-grid collaborators.
- The model treats revenue diversification as a company-wide coordination problem.

## Evidence
- Column structure: [[9-boxes]] shows BuzzFeed, BuzzFeed Media Brands, and BuzzFeed News as the three content-engine columns.
- Row structure: [[9-boxes]] shows advertising, commerce, and studio as the three revenue-stream rows.
- Cell examples: [[9-boxes]] lists BuzzFeed advertising products such as BuzzFeed Audience Engine, turnkey ad products, programmatic, and platform revenue share; Media Brands commerce items such as Tasty books, housewares, One Top, and affiliates; and News commerce items such as book club, paid events, and content licensing.
- Collaboration frame: [[9-boxes]] says shared teams should drive collaboration across the grid.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The matrix is a planning and alignment tool, not proof that each box can become a profitable business. Some cells include existing lines while others are "coming soon" or project examples, so the diagram mixes current operations, near-term plans, and strategic aspirations.
