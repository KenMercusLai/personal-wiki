## Definition
[[FreemiumAcquisition]] is an acquisition strategy that uses a free tier, free trial, free download, or free utility to reduce adoption friction, demonstrate value, and create a larger pool for later paid conversion or monetization.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats free entry as a recurring route from attention to adoption. [[Shopify]] uses a free trial to let small businesses experience store-building before committing; [[Spotify]] uses an ad-supported tier to build traffic and later subscriptions; HideMyAss offers free privacy tools alongside paid VPN services; [[HubSpot]]'s free Website Grader creates value while directing users toward paid inbound marketing products. Free entry becomes a growth hack when it is tied to a conversion path, data loop, brand habit, or network expansion rather than treated as generosity alone.

## Key Claims
- Free entry lowers perceived risk for users who are unsure whether the product will work for them.
- A free tool can both deliver value and reveal a paid problem the company can solve.
- Freemium can grow an audience before subscription conversion, advertising revenue, or premium add-ons.
- Free trials work best when the user can reach meaningful activation within the trial window.
- Free acquisition still needs unit-economics discipline because adoption alone does not prove profitable growth.
- Free products can also create indirect demand for related hardware, services, or ecosystem purchases.

## Evidence
- Trial activation: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says [[Shopify]] used a 14-day free trial to reduce small-business risk around opening an online store.
- Free utility to paid funnel: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] presents [[HubSpot]]'s Website Grader as a free diagnostic that sends traffic toward paid services.
- Ad-supported scale: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] says [[Spotify]]'s free ad-supported use helped it lead in traffic while also supporting subscriptions.
- Free download plus add-ons: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] frames [[PokemonGo]] as a free download that created demand for premium additions and related devices.
- Free privacy products: [[51-examples-of-growth-hacking-strategies-techniques-from-the-worlds-most-innovative-businesses]] uses HideMyAss as a freemium privacy-service example.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not calculate conversion rates, retention, payback period, support costs, or cannibalization. Free-entry tactics can attract low-intent users, train customers not to pay, or become expensive if infrastructure and support costs scale faster than paid conversion.
