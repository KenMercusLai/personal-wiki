## Definition
[[ResearchToProductTransfer]] is the organizational capability to move ideas, prototypes, and technical discoveries from research into products, operations, distribution, and measurable business or user impact.

## Current Synthesis
The source separates research productivity from innovation impact. [[Yahoo]] could host prolific laboratory work and technically important ideas while still failing, in [[XavierAmatriain]]'s account, to move those ideas quickly and effectively into the business. [[Hadoop]] is used as the sharpest illustration: the source associates its origin with Yahoo but points to outside companies that built businesses around it.

Transfer is therefore not an automatic consequence of invention. It needs strategic selection, product ownership, incentives, integration capacity, execution speed, and a route from technical work to users or revenue. This complements [[LongNoseInnovation]], which describes commercialization as a long refinement process rather than a single laboratory event.

## Key Claims
- Research output and business impact are different organizational outcomes.
- Technology transfer needs explicit mechanisms linking researchers, product teams, operations, and strategy.
- A company can contribute important technology while capturing little of its later commercial value.
- Transfer failures can compound weak focus when many ideas lack a clear path into products.
- External ecosystem success does not by itself prove that the originating organization could or should have pursued the same business.

## Evidence
- Research-versus-impact distinction: [[xavier-amatriains-answer-to-what-lessons-can-silicon-valley-tech-executives-learn-from-what-went-wrong-at-yahoo-quora]] says Yahoo Labs generated many strong ideas but that most had no business impact because the organization lacked quick, effective transfer.
- Hadoop example: [[xavier-amatriains-answer-to-what-lessons-can-silicon-valley-tech-executives-learn-from-what-went-wrong-at-yahoo-quora]] contrasts Hadoop's association with Yahoo against businesses built around it by Cloudera and Hortonworks.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not measure Yahoo Labs output, define transfer speed, identify which ideas were suitable for commercialization, or compare the value Yahoo received from internal use, ecosystem influence, recruiting, patents, or open-source adoption. The Hadoop account is compressed and does not establish that Yahoo could have captured the same value as later specialist companies. Research can also create option value and public infrastructure without becoming a direct product line.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to distinguish idea generation from organizational commercialization capability.

## Related Concepts

- [[ProductManagement]] - product ownership connects technical possibility with user and business outcomes.
- [[StartupFocus]] - strategic selection determines which research paths receive transfer resources.
- [[OrganizationalLeverage]] - reusable technology creates impact only when the organization can deploy it into work or products.
