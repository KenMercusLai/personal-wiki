## Definition
[[LearningHowToLearn]] is the trainable ability to find, evaluate, retain, and apply knowledge with less wasted time and attention.

## Current Synthesis
In this source, learning how to learn is the skill bundle that turns information abundance into usable advantage. Simmons emphasizes evidence quality, disconfirmation, diverse knowledge, cognitive-bias awareness, selective intake, and application. The bundle complements the wiki's active-learning and focused-reading material: selection improves the input, while explanation, practice, and output test whether the knowledge became usable. The source outlines the bundle but does not compare specific training methods or demonstrate that its commercial course improves outcomes.

## Key Claims
- Information selection is itself a learnable skill rather than a passive prelude to learning.
- Evaluating evidence requires looking for disconfirmation as well as support.
- Diverse knowledge and cognitive-bias awareness can reduce insular or reflexive judgment.
- Learning is incomplete if high-value information is not retained and applied.
- Input filtering and active output address different parts of the learning process.

## Evidence
- Skill bundle: [[while-everyone-is-distracted-by-social-media-successful-people-double-down-on-an-underrated-skill]] names scientific reasoning, diverse knowledge, and bias awareness as components.
- Selection goal: [[while-everyone-is-distracted-by-social-media-successful-people-double-down-on-an-underrated-skill]] defines the result as finding and applying breakthrough knowledge with less wasted time.
- Practice context: [[while-everyone-is-distracted-by-social-media-successful-people-double-down-on-an-underrated-skill]] presents the framework as the result of Simmons's reading and observation of entrepreneurs and leaders.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The essay is a conceptual and promotional overview, not an evaluated curriculum. Its emphasis on elite entrepreneurs risks survivor bias, and the scientific-method discussion is simplified. Learning effectiveness also depends on prior knowledge, retrieval practice, feedback, motivation, and domain-specific experience, none of which is developed in detail here.

## What Changed
- Established information selection and evidence evaluation as explicit parts of learning skill.
- Connected proactive input filtering with the wiki's existing output- and practice-oriented learning methods.
