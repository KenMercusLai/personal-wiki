Product storytelling past present future is a communication pattern for making product direction legible after a team has converged on a goal. The source argues that product work often begins with divergent thinking, but teams and stakeholders need a digestible narrative once a direction has been chosen.

The story should explain the relevant past, describe the current state, and then show what the future looks like with the product in the world. Data matters, but the source warns against making the narrative overly complicated. The point is to help the audience understand why this product work should exist and what change it is expected to create.

The source also pairs narrative with quantitative models. A model of product impact helps stakeholders reason about return on investment and decide whether the planned work deserves the time being assigned to it.
