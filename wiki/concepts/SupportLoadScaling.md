## Definition
[[SupportLoadScaling]] is the operational problem of scaling customer or user support when usage growth, time sensitivity, domain complexity, and downside risk make informal help channels insufficient.

## Current Synthesis
The MyCrypto launch source shows support load becoming a company-shaping constraint. MyEtherWallet's early support work fit a tiny community and a two-person side project, but 2017 changed the support surface: rising Ether values, ICO congestion, ENS deadlines, phishing attempts, infrastructure scaling, and novice users turned ordinary help requests into urgent, high-value incidents. In this context, support is not a back-office cost center; it becomes part of product safety, trust, mental-health sustainability, hiring, documentation, and company structure.

## Key Claims
- Support load can increase nonlinearly when product adoption, asset value, and user stakes rise together.
- Informal founder-led support can build deep user understanding but may become unsustainable under high growth.
- Time-sensitive, irreversible user problems require faster and more specialized support than general onboarding questions.
- Hiring support in specialized security-sensitive domains is hard because trust, technical fluency, empathy, and best-practice discipline all matter.
- Support pressure can force organizational change when it threatens user safety, founder health, team reliability, or product continuity.

## Evidence
- Nonlinear growth: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says support grew from roughly 20 messages on a busy day to about 1,000 during the 2017 ICO surge.
- Founder learning and strain: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] links direct user conversations to product learning, then describes sleep loss and mental-health pressure as requests kept increasing.
- Time sensitivity: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] gives ENS bid reveal deadlines as an example where delayed support could cost users large amounts of Ether.
- Hiring constraint: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] says trustworthy, knowledgeable support staff were hard to find in the blockchain space.
- Organizational response: [[a-new-beginning-mycrypto-com-mycrypto-medium]] presents [[MyCrypto]] as a structure for a team that could keep building, supporting, educating, and securing users.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The evidence comes from a particularly high-stakes crypto-wallet context, where support pressure is amplified by irreversible transactions and asset values. Lower-risk products may experience support scaling more gradually or solve more of it through self-service tooling, automation, or community support.

## What Changed
- Added support load scaling as a startup-operations concept where user help demand can drive company structure and product safety.
