## Definition
[[SmartGlasses]] are eye-worn computing devices that combine cameras, displays, sensors, assistants, machine learning, and augmented-reality interfaces in a form factor light and useful enough for everyday wear.

## Current Synthesis
The sources present smart glasses as Apple's likely next major personal-computing frontier after the iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods. The argument is not that glasses were ready in 2018; it is that Apple was already preparing the technical, developer, behavioral, and retail foundations for them. [[ARKit]] seeds AR behavior on iPhones and iPads, Siri Shortcuts and machine learning suggest proactive assistance, [[AppleWatch]] teaches body-worn computing and may carry supporting technology, and [[AirPods]] show momentum in personal wearables. The connecting-the-dots source places glasses inside [[AppleProductTheory]] as the next step below Apple Watch: a visual, glance-and-voice-driven product that would handle tasks now given to Apple Watch and iPhone. The strategic claim is that many iPhone, Watch, AR, assistant, custom-silicon, and miniaturization investments make more sense when cameras and displays no longer have to be held in the hand.

## Key Claims
- Smart glasses are framed as the next wearables battle after wrists and ears.
- Handheld phones and tablets are useful AR seeding devices but awkward long-term AR viewfinders.
- The category depends on technology becoming light, attractive, and comfortable enough for all-day wear.
- Apple can prepare before launch by seeding ARKit, developers, Memoji-like camera behaviors, and proactive assistant patterns across existing devices.
- Apple Watch may become a support layer for glasses by carrying body-worn technology and assistant learning.
- Apple's advantages include hardware/software integration, custom silicon, miniaturization, developers, retail demos, fashion knowledge, and health use cases.
- In Cybart's product ladder, glasses would make technology more personal by taking tasks from Apple Watch and iPhone rather than simply replacing either product.

- Advantage stack: [[above-avalon-apple-is-figuring-out-whats-next]] lists integration, core technology control, manufacturing miniaturization, AR work, developers, fashion, health, and retail demos as Apple's smart-glasses advantages.
- Product-ladder role: [[above-avalon-connecting-the-apple-dots]] shows glasses as the category intended to handle tasks formerly given to Apple Watch and iPhone.
- Interface thesis: [[above-avalon-connecting-the-apple-dots]] identifies glances, voice, enhanced vision, custom silicon, and Apple Watch miniaturization as ingredients for lightweight glasses.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources are explicitly speculative about Apple's future product direction in 2018. They do not prove that Apple would ship mass-market glasses, that consumers would accept them, that AR use cases would justify all-day wear, or that glasses would surpass the iPhone user base. They also treat Apple's advantages as strategic positioning rather than measured product outcomes.
