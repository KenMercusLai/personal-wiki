## Definition
[[CreativePresence]] is the state of receptive, relaxed attention in which a creator prepares the conditions for inspiration to arrive and turns it into a complete work before its momentum dissipates.

## Current Synthesis
The sources treat creation as a field and system problem rather than a pure act of command. Ideas are described as visitors or signals, and the creator's task is to keep the inner room open enough to receive them. The practice is paradoxical: absorb strong influences and train taste, but release the demand to produce greatness on command; stay sensitive, but finish imperfect drafts while the initial life of the idea is present. The creator-anxiety source adds a practical layer: inspiration is easier to use when captured quickly, fed by strong inputs, and supported by activities that relax attention.

## Key Claims
- Creation often feels less like ownership than like receiving and translating a signal.
- Sensitivity can make creators more vulnerable while also giving them a finer antenna for meaning.
- Creative practice requires both skill cultivation and non-grasping openness.
- Completing an imperfect whole can preserve more creative DNA than polishing fragments.
- Regular presence makes inspiration more likely to find a usable channel.
- Inspiration also needs capture systems and rich inputs so fleeting ideas can become future work.

## Evidence
- Reception metaphor: [[chang]] draws on creativity writing to present ideas as visitors and creators as translators rather than owners.
- Sensitivity and pain: [[chang]] uses Shi Tiesheng and other creator examples to argue that deeper vulnerability can increase receptivity to hidden meanings.
- Paradoxical practice: [[chang]] says creators should absorb good material, shape taste, keep beginner's mind, and cultivate a quiet environment while loosening anxiety about greatness.
- Draft completion: [[chang]] argues that the first spark has momentum and that a complete imperfect draft is often more useful than a perfect fragment.
- Regular presence: [[chang]] recommends showing up to the field daily rather than waiting passively for inspiration.
- Capture and inputs: [[xuan-ti-zhuan-ye-xing-wan-mei-zhu-yi-shu-ju-he-ling-gan-zhe-xie-chuang-zuo-jiao-lv-gai-ru-he-ying-dui]] uses a centralized idea database, shortcuts, quality sources, comments, conversations, walks, showers, and exercise to make inspiration more available.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The field source uses metaphysical language about signals, the universe, and ideas choosing creators; these should be read as creative phenomenology rather than verified causation. The creator-anxiety source is more operational but remains a personal workflow, not proof that a specific tool stack produces inspiration. Both accounts may fit essayistic and artistic work better than domains where creation depends mainly on collaboration, deadlines, or formal constraints.

## What Changed
- Added idea capture, high-quality inputs, and relaxed activities as operational supports for inspiration.
