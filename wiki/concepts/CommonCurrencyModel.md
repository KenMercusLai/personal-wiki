## Definition
[[CommonCurrencyModel]] is the valuation model, discussed in the source, in which diverse reward and knowledge signals are integrated into a shared final valuation signal.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses the common currency model to connect [[KnowledgeValuationNetwork]] with neuroscience. Wozniak describes orbitofrontal cortex-centered valuation as a possible integration point where knowledge-based valuation, reward expectation, and bodily or sensory valuation can converge into a final "wanting" or "liking" signal.

This model matters for the wiki because it gives the motivational theory a possible neural implementation. A learner's pleasure in creative insight or learn-drive activation may, in this framing, share reward pathways with more basic pleasures while still preserving specificity through the active concept maps that produced the valuation.

## Key Claims
- Diverse rewards may be converted into a shared final valuation signal.
- The orbitofrontal cortex is presented as a likely hub for higher-order valuation.
- Knowledge-based valuation can combine with reward expectation and bodily valuation.
- Concept-map activation supplies specificity, while final valuation requires convergence.
- The model helps explain why learning and creativity can feel rewarding rather than merely useful.

## Evidence
- OFC hub: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] says many researchers treat the valuation system as centered on the orbitofrontal cortex, with more anterior regions handling more abstract valuation.
- Signal integration: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] gives the example of knowledge valuation from medial OFC, reward expectation from nucleus accumbens, and food valuation from the insula jointly influencing a meal choice.
- Specificity and convergence: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] argues that concept maps provide specificity while a single final response requires convergent valuation.
- Learning reward: [[zhi-shi-gu-zhi-wang-luo]] suggests that pleasure from creativity or learn drive may be neurochemically and anatomically comparable to other reward states.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source acknowledges debate over whether rewards are truly converted into a common signal or retain source-specific properties. The page therefore treats the common currency model as a useful explanatory frame within the source, not as a settled resolution of reward neuroscience.
