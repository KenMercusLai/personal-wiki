Continuous product iteration cadence treats shipping as a learning mechanism rather than only a delivery event. The source favors shipping about every two weeks across a three-month cycle, producing a sequence that keeps engineers moving, reduces accumulated defects, and gives users visible updates.

The cadence distinguishes fuller integer releases from earlier partial releases. Integer versions such as V1, V2, and V3 are fully built features based on user and data insight. Half versions are earlier shipments that test an idea and collect more data before the team commits additional resources.

Each iteration gives the team a chance to fix new bugs, explain what changed, and answer larger product questions with observed behavior. The cadence therefore supports validation before the team invests too much time in a direction that may not work.
