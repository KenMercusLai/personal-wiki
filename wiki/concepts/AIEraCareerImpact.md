## Definition
[[AIEraCareerImpact]] is [[HowieXu]]'s source-scoped framework for career leverage as the interaction of emotional and attention capacity, ability to direct AI resources, and intelligence plus sustained execution intensity.

## Current Synthesis
The source expresses the model as `Career Impact = EQ × TQ × IQ`. EQ is redefined as emotion plus “eyeball,” or the ability to win attention and trust; TQ, “Token Quotient,” is the ability to command AI capacity beyond knowledge of a particular tool; IQ combines intelligence with intensity, emphasizing how far a person will push execution when AI makes ordinary production cheaper. The multiplicative form is rhetorical rather than measured, but it conveys that a severe weakness in one dimension can limit the practical value of the others.

The surrounding career thesis is broader than the formula. Repeatable skills can be absorbed into AI systems, while judgment, taste, contextual understanding, professional trust, attention, and responsibility for outcomes remain differentiating in the source's account. Superlinear returns then come from compounding assets such as reputation and relationships plus threshold-crossing work, not merely greater hourly effort. This makes the model a career heuristic about where to invest, not a validated score or claim that human judgment will remain permanently unautomatable.

## Key Claims
- Career leverage depends on interacting human, AI-orchestration, and execution capacities rather than technical skill alone.
- TQ concerns directing increasingly large amounts of model and agent work toward a reliable outcome, not memorizing tool interfaces.
- Trust and reputation compound through long-term reliable participation and cannot be created on demand when an opportunity appears.
- Attention becomes more valuable as AI lowers content-production cost and expands noise.
- Judgment, taste, leverage, and outcome ownership gain relative importance when routine execution becomes cheaper.
- Superlinear careers combine compounding with threshold effects rather than treating time and reward as proportional.

## Evidence
- Formula and dimensions: [[ting-de-shi-hou-quan-dong-yi-dong-bi-quan-fei-gui-gu-xu-lao-shi-da-shi-ke-shang-de-eq-tq-iq]] records `EQ × TQ × IQ` and the summary “Win hearts. Command tokens. Show intensity.”
- Trust and attention: [[ting-de-shi-hou-quan-dong-yi-dong-bi-quan-fei-gui-gu-xu-lao-shi-da-shi-ke-shang-de-eq-tq-iq]] connects long community participation with reliability and argues that cheap AI content makes signal selection more important.
- Leverage and accountability: [[ting-de-shi-hou-quan-dong-yi-dong-bi-quan-fei-gui-gu-xu-lao-shi-da-shi-ke-shang-de-eq-tq-iq]] links gold-collar work to judgment, taste, and leverage, then asks people to own outcomes rather than tasks.
- Superlinear mechanism: [[ting-de-shi-hou-quan-dong-yi-dong-bi-quan-fei-gui-gu-xu-lao-shi-da-shi-ke-shang-de-eq-tq-iq]] maps [[PaulGraham]]'s exponential growth and thresholds to compounding and breakthrough.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The model comes from one masterclass as reconstructed by an attendee. The source supplies no operational definitions, scale, comparison group, longitudinal data, or evidence that the dimensions are independent or literally multiplicative. “TQ” may reward access to compute, money, data, authority, and support as much as individual ability. Trust can be lost, exclusionary networks can compound unequal access, attention can be captured without value, and intense execution can amplify a wrong direction or create burnout. AI may also automate parts of judgment, taste, orchestration, and relationship work, so the proposed human remainder should be treated as a current strategic claim rather than a permanent boundary.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to preserve the formula together with its wider trust, attention, leverage, and outcome-ownership context.
- Marked the multiplicative equation as a practitioner heuristic rather than a measured career score.
