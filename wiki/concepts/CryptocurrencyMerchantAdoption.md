## Definition
[[CryptocurrencyMerchantAdoption]] is the process by which cryptocurrencies become accepted and usable as payment rails by merchants, especially large platforms whose distribution can legitimize one token, protocol, or payment experience over another.

## Current Synthesis
The Coin and Crypto source frames merchant adoption as a bridge between cryptocurrency speculation and currency use. It argues that a retailer as large as [[Amazon]] could shape the cryptocurrency market not merely by accepting [[Bitcoin]], but by choosing a faster competitor, staying out of crypto payments, or launching an Amazon-controlled token usable across its own services. The concept is therefore less about generic payment availability and more about platform selection power: checkout scale, transaction speed, user experience, and ecosystem incentives can decide which cryptocurrency becomes practically visible to mainstream buyers.

## Key Claims
- Merchant adoption is treated as necessary for a cryptocurrency to move from investment asset toward everyday currency.
- Large merchants can act as market validators because their payment choices expose many consumers to a selected currency or token.
- Transaction throughput and checkout waiting time are practical adoption constraints, not just protocol trivia.
- Platform-owned tokens can compete with open cryptocurrencies when the platform controls services, discounts, and user demand.
- The source's evidence is speculative and scenario-based, so the concept should remain qualified until supported by concrete merchant-payment adoption data.

## Evidence
- Currency bridge: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] says Bitcoin needs widespread merchant adoption to cross from investment to currency.
- Platform validation: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] argues that any cryptocurrency Amazon adopted would receive a major support surge.
- Checkout constraint: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] contrasts Bitcoin and Ethereum throughput with Amazon's peak Prime-sale transaction demand.
- Proprietary-token scenario: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] imagines an Amazon-created cryptocurrency spanning Prime, Twitch, Audible, and purchase discounts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not provide transaction-cost data, user-demand surveys, regulatory analysis, or evidence that Amazon actually planned a cryptocurrency rollout. It also predates later cryptocurrency market cycles and payment-infrastructure developments, so its scenarios should be treated as a 2017 strategic thought experiment.

## What Changed
- Added merchant adoption as a cryptocurrency platform-power concept centered on Amazon-scale payment choices.
