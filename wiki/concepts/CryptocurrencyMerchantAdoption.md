## Definition
[[CryptocurrencyMerchantAdoption]] is the process by which cryptocurrencies become accepted and usable as payment rails by merchants, especially large platforms whose distribution can legitimize one token, protocol, or payment experience over another; adjacent mainstream adoption can also happen through regulated investing and custody infrastructure rather than checkout payments.

## Current Synthesis
The Coin and Crypto source frames merchant adoption as a bridge between cryptocurrency speculation and currency use. It argues that a retailer as large as [[Amazon]] could shape the cryptocurrency market not merely by accepting [[Bitcoin]], but by choosing a faster competitor, staying out of crypto payments, or launching an Amazon-controlled token usable across its own services. Fortune qualifies the adoption frame: [[Coinbase]] reached mainstream scale by making crypto easy to buy, store, and trade, not by making it common at checkout. The broader concept is therefore about where ordinary users first meet crypto: merchant payments are one path, while regulated exchanges, custody, and institutional investment products are another.

## Key Claims
- Merchant adoption is treated as necessary for a cryptocurrency to move from investment asset toward everyday currency.
- Large merchants can act as market validators because their payment choices expose many consumers to a selected currency or token.
- Transaction throughput and checkout waiting time are practical adoption constraints, not just protocol trivia.
- Platform-owned tokens can compete with open cryptocurrencies when the platform controls services, discounts, and user demand.
- Regulated exchanges can mainstream cryptocurrency without solving everyday merchant payment usage.
- The source's evidence is speculative and scenario-based, so the concept should remain qualified until supported by concrete merchant-payment adoption data.

## Evidence
- Currency bridge: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] says Bitcoin needs widespread merchant adoption to cross from investment to currency.
- Platform validation: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] argues that any cryptocurrency Amazon adopted would receive a major support surge.
- Checkout constraint: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] contrasts Bitcoin and Ethereum throughput with Amazon's peak Prime-sale transaction demand.
- Proprietary-token scenario: [[amazon-is-the-biggest-threat-to-bitcoin-right-now-by-coin-and-crypto]] imagines an Amazon-created cryptocurrency spanning Prime, Twitch, Audible, and purchase discounts.
- Exchange-adoption alternative: [[coinbase-wants-to-be-too-big-to-fail-fortune]] shows Coinbase reaching 25 million customer accounts through purchase, custody, trading, and institutional services rather than merchant checkout.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The Amazon source does not provide transaction-cost data, user-demand surveys, regulatory analysis, or evidence that Amazon actually planned a cryptocurrency rollout. It also predates later cryptocurrency market cycles and payment-infrastructure developments, so its scenarios should be treated as a 2017 strategic thought experiment. The Coinbase source is about investment access and financial infrastructure, so it qualifies rather than directly proves merchant-payment adoption.

## What Changed
- Added merchant adoption as a cryptocurrency platform-power concept centered on Amazon-scale payment choices.
- Added Coinbase as a counter-path where mainstream crypto exposure comes through regulated exchange and custody infrastructure.
