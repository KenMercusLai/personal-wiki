## Definition
[[ProjectBasedLearning]] is inquiry-oriented learning where students pursue a concrete question, gather evidence, build methods, and present conclusions through a complete project cycle.

## Current Synthesis
RORIRI proposes PBL as a low-friction place to make expanded computational thinking visible in schools. Instead of rebuilding every course, schools can use electives, small courses, interest groups, and extracurricular time for projects where statistics, programming, and argument writing naturally meet. The strongest version uses instructors or teacher networks who can guide research design, data collection, analysis, and presentation while allowing the question to begin from student curiosity.

## Key Claims
- PBL can train statistical, programming, and writing abilities together around a real inquiry.
- Student questions do not need to be questions the teacher already knows how to answer.
- Schools can use loose teacher networks when a single all-capable research mentor is unavailable.
- PBL and main-subject learning should form a loop rather than operate as isolated tracks.
- External experts and student peer networks can enrich projects when school capacity is limited.

## Evidence
- Integrated ability training: [[jiao-yu-de-xia-yi-bu-qi-er-luo-li-li-de-shu-ju-zhong-xin]] describes AI art, AI music, and LLM literary analysis as topics where experimental design, statistics, programming, and argumentation can combine.
- Unknown-answer inquiry: [[jiao-yu-de-xia-yi-bu-qi-er-luo-li-li-de-shu-ju-zhong-xin]] values teacher-student relationships that begin with shared investigation rather than known answers.
- Teacher network: [[jiao-yu-de-xia-yi-bu-qi-er-luo-li-li-de-shu-ju-zhong-xin]] suggests math, language, and information-technology teachers co-guiding a project from their respective strengths.
- Course loop: [[jiao-yu-de-xia-yi-bu-qi-er-luo-li-li-de-shu-ju-zhong-xin]] says PBL statistical or argument difficulties should send students back to main courses for needed knowledge.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is realistic about implementation difficulty: capable research-style instructors are scarce, and combined teacher guidance may be more feasible but lower quality than a single expert mentor. The essay also does not supply a tested school implementation.
