## Definition
[[WebsitePersonalization]] is the practice of changing a site's message, creative, proof, content, calls to action, or other components according to a visitor's known audience attributes or prior behavior.

## Current Synthesis
The Optimizely case treats personalization as a joint data, design, and measurement problem. Audience rules need usable signals; the page needs modular components that can vary without fragmenting the whole experience; and the experiment needs metrics that distinguish more immediate conversions from better-qualified and better-informed demand. In the case, audience definitions draw on named accounts, industries, geographies, customer status, and prior engagement, while the flexible hero, personalization bar, customer proof, and promotion cards provide distinct response surfaces. The design is therefore not just dynamic copy: it is an adaptable content system governed by audience hypotheses and evaluated against downstream business outcomes.

## Key Claims
- Personalization begins with explicit audience definitions rather than one assumed average visitor.
- Page components must be designed for controlled variation across messaging, imagery, proof, content, events, and calls to action.
- Firmographic, demographic, geographic, customer-status, and behavioral signals can support different personalization goals.
- Relevant proof and promotions depend on context: industry peers, regional brands, nearby events, and prior engagement imply different content.
- Success should include engagement and downstream lead quality, not only immediate conversion.
- Personalized experiences remain hypotheses that require testing against a baseline.

## Evidence

- Adaptable surfaces: [[designing-for-personalization-the-story-of-optimizelys-homepage-optimizely-blog]] describes a flexible hero, modular personalization bar, customer validation, and content or event promotions.
- Contextual proof: [[designing-for-personalization-the-story-of-optimizelys-homepage-optimizely-blog]] proposes showing customer logos and quotes from a visitor's industry or region.
- Measurement: [[designing-for-personalization-the-story-of-optimizelys-homepage-optimizely-blog]] proposes tracking engagement, lead conversion, accounts created, lead qualification, and sales velocity.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source describes a launch-stage test and supplies no results, so it does not establish that the selected audiences, signals, or experiences improved customer or business outcomes. Personalization can also introduce classification errors, inconsistent experiences, operational complexity, and privacy concerns that the post does not examine.
