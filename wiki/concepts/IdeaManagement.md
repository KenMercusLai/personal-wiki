## Definition
[[IdeaManagement]] is the capture, storage, review, and selective development of ideas so passing inspiration can become usable work without the idea system replacing execution.

## Current Synthesis
The creator-anxiety source treats idea drought as partly a system problem. Ideas need quick capture because they disappear, a centralized home because scattered notes become unusable, and high-quality input because not every captured thought can become publishable material. Its Notion database, iOS Shortcut, read-it-later tools, newsletters, podcasts, comment practice, conversations, and relaxed thinking form a practical idea supply chain.

Reorx adds a necessary output boundary for product ideas. Capture can preserve the core purpose and use, but repeatedly refining the same note may increase scope and expected effort while producing no artifact. His proposed response is to archive a stable Simple Project Idea, represent material evolution as a linked branch, restrict additions on the simple note to technical implementation guidance, and monitor execution status through frontmatter. Together the sources suggest that an idea system needs both a low-friction intake and an explicit rule for conversion, branching, or abandonment.

## Key Claims
- Ideas should be captured immediately because delayed writing often loses the original signal.
- A centralized idea database makes future review and development easier than scattered notes.
- Capture speed matters enough to justify shortcuts or automation when the default tool is too slow.
- Idea supply improves when creators expand high-quality inputs in their chosen domain.
- Published content, comments, conversations, and archived interactions can all become future idea material.
- Product-idea refinement needs a stopping rule because continued elaboration can substitute for implementation.
- Materially evolved product ideas can be linked branches rather than uncontrolled expansion of the original scope.

- Input expansion: [[xuan-ti-zhuan-ye-xing-wan-mei-zhu-yi-shu-ju-he-ling-gan-zhe-xie-chuang-zuo-jiao-lv-gai-ru-he-ying-dui]] uses creators, newsletters, podcasts, read-later tools, comments, conversations, and relaxed activity as idea sources.
- Reuse and archive: [[xuan-ti-zhuan-ye-xing-wan-mei-zhu-yi-shu-ju-he-ling-gan-zhe-xie-chuang-zuo-jiao-lv-gai-ru-he-ying-dui]] archives posts and interaction data from multiple platforms for later reuse.
- Conversion boundary: [[reorx-progressive-product-building-yu-yuan-ren-zhi-lei-bi]] reports that repeated product-note refinement increased complexity and expected effort while projects remained unbuilt.
- Stable origin and branches: [[reorx-progressive-product-building-yu-yuan-ren-zhi-lei-bi]] preserves a simple idea's purpose and use, moves material expansions to linked notes, and tracks delivery state through frontmatter.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Capturing more ideas can create a backlog that feels productive without producing finished work. The creator workflow depends on comfort with Notion, iOS Shortcuts, APIs, and multiple content platforms; simpler tools may fit other people better. Reorx's alternative is an intended personal practice with no measured completion, quality, user-value, or maintenance outcomes. Immediate building may be premature when problem discovery, safety, privacy, regulation, collaboration, or architectural risk requires more inquiry, while aggressive branching may fragment context if the note system lacks clear naming and lifecycle rules.

## What Changed
- Added an execution boundary: idea capture and cultivation should not let product documentation replace implementation.
- Added linked branching and explicit status metadata as ways to preserve idea lineage without silently expanding scope.
