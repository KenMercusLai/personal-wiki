## Definition
[[DeepPreparation]] is the deliberate concentration of research, expert consultation, questioning, review, and synthesis on a small number of consequential subjects or conversations so that understanding and trust can compound.

## Current Synthesis
Mai Yang's account of [[DwarkeshPatel]] contrasts deep preparation with “platform transactions”: accumulating many saved items or frequent outputs while forming little durable understanding. The proposed alternative is a network-quality relationship with knowledge and people. A learner repeatedly returns to important books, papers, authors, and questions; an interviewer uses weeks of research and expert input to create questions that respect a guest's time and can reveal original thinking.

AI changes the throughput of this practice without removing its selection and judgment requirements. The source uses [[Claude]] as a conversational layer over books and papers, asking for explanations, conceptual comparisons, objections, flashcards, and cross-domain connections. Spaced review then aims to preserve selected material. The resulting loop is depth first, acceleration second: tools reduce the cost of decomposition and rehearsal, while the human still chooses what merits attention, verifies outputs, and integrates it into a working worldview.

## Key Claims
- Preparation depth can improve question specificity and make a conversation more valuable to both guest and audience.
- Repeated high-quality interactions may compound into trust, referrals, and access more reliably than undifferentiated outreach volume.
- Low output frequency can be an intentional consequence of protecting research time and information density.
- LLM dialogue can accelerate explanation, comparison, counterargument, and reconstruction across a bounded reading set.
- Flashcards and spaced review can turn selected ideas into recurring retrieval prompts rather than one-time consumption.
- Cross-domain questions help integrate new information into existing models instead of leaving it isolated.

## Evidence
- Interview preparation and question quality: [[mai-yang-dwarkesh-ru-he-yong-ai-zuo-shen-du-zhun-bei]] attributes weeks of paper reading, expert consultation, and novel questions to Patel's preparation method.
- Trust and referral mechanism: [[mai-yang-dwarkesh-ru-he-yong-ai-zuo-shen-du-zhun-bei]] describes early guest referrals and later confidence from prominent guests as consequences of valuable conversations.
- Conversational reading: [[mai-yang-dwarkesh-ru-he-yong-ai-zuo-shen-du-zhun-bei]] describes uploading books and papers to Claude and interrogating chapters through explanation, comparison, and opposition prompts.
- Retention and integration: [[mai-yang-dwarkesh-ru-he-yong-ai-zuo-shen-du-zhun-bei]] recommends small AI-generated flashcard sets, recurring review, and questions that connect a new idea to other domains or prior beliefs.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The evidence is a secondary practitioner synthesis rather than a comparison of interview or learning methods. Guest willingness can reflect audience, reputation, timing, networks, editorial quality, or topic fit in addition to preparation. More research can also become avoidance, delay, or diminishing-return accumulation; some fields reward rapid iteration and frequent feedback. LLM explanations and cards require verification, and spaced repetition can preserve decontextualized fragments without producing judgment or transfer. The source's “10x” framing is unmeasured.

## What Changed
- Established deep preparation as a coupled learning, interview-quality, and trust-building strategy.
- Separated AI-enabled processing speed from the slower human work of selection, verification, attention, and integration.
