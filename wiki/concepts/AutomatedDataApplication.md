## Definition
[[AutomatedDataApplication]] is a data system in which data generation, processing, model output, and business action form a closed operational loop.

## Current Synthesis
The source contrasts insight applications with fully automated applications. Insight applications create reports that humans inspect before deciding what to do; automated applications use data and models to make or trigger decisions directly, making data part of the operating mechanism of the business. In this framework, automation is the difference between analytics that inform people and big-data systems that continuously reshape behavior.

## Key Claims
- Dashboards and reports are weaker transformation mechanisms when humans must manually interpret and act on every insight.
- Closed-loop automation lets data generation, processing, and action reinforce one another.
- Automated application is one of the required conditions for big data to transform an industry.
- The strongest business principle is to let data override unsupported assumptions while still iterating core business logic quickly.

## Evidence
- Insight versus automation: [[da-shu-ju-shi-fou-neng-gou-gai-zao-ni-de-hang-ye]] distinguishes report-based insight from fully automated applications.
- Closed loop: [[da-shu-ju-shi-fou-neng-gou-gai-zao-ni-de-hang-ye]] describes automation as a loop among data production, processing, and transaction/action.
- Transformation role: [[da-shu-ju-shi-fou-neng-gou-gai-zao-ni-de-hang-ye]] lists automated application as the third required condition for industry transformation.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source emphasizes the value of automation but does not develop a governance model for errors, bias, accountability, or human override. In regulated or high-stakes domains, the gap between useful automation and acceptable automation may be large.
