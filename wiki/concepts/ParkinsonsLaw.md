## Definition
[[ParkinsonsLaw]] is the heuristic that work tends to expand to occupy the time made available for its completion.

## Current Synthesis
The available source uses Parkinson's Law to interpret an entrepreneurial schedule experiment. [[PiaSilva]] reports that a narrow late-afternoon work window during travel, followed by a 1-6 weekday schedule, produced at least as much useful work as longer days because it forced planning, delegation, task filtering, and distraction control. The mechanism is therefore not mere clock compression: a shorter window changes which work remains, who performs it, and how clearly it is specified.

## Key Claims
- Open-ended work time can absorb procrastination, unclear priorities, and work that could be delegated.
- A deliberately smaller time window can force sharper choices about objectives and owner-only work.
- Time compression depends on operating changes such as advance planning, documented handoff, and distraction control.
- The law is a useful diagnostic heuristic, not proof that every task or role can be completed in less time.

## Evidence
- Compressed schedule: [[pia-silva-9-5-is-out-try-the-1-6-instead]] reports maintaining a business through a few late-afternoon hours during travel and then adopting a five-hour weekday schedule.
- Claimed mechanism: [[pia-silva-9-5-is-out-try-the-1-6-instead]] attributes the result to outsourcing, training, explicit objectives, weekly planning, and reduced procrastination.
- Retrospective comparison: [[pia-silva-9-5-is-out-try-the-1-6-instead]] characterizes prior eight-hour-plus days as roughly five hours of useful work stretched by three hours of delay and undelegated activity.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source invokes the law but does not test it. Its evidence is one entrepreneur's retrospective without defined output measures, a comparison group, revenue or customer outcomes, or controls for travel novelty, time-zone alignment, staffing, and demand. A shorter deadline can also reduce quality, learning, care, documentation, safety, or responsiveness when work is irreducible, uncertain, collaborative, or coverage-dependent.

## What Changed
- Created a source-scoped account that links time compression to planning, delegation, task selection, and autonomy.
