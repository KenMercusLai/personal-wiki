## Definition
[[BurnoutPrevention]] is the use of personal recovery practices and workplace systems to reduce exhaustion, always-on pressure, and the health costs of sustained overwork.

## Current Synthesis
The interview frames burnout prevention as both personal and institutional. Huffington's own turn toward sleep came after long hours, insufficient sleep, ill health, and fainting from exhaustion. At [[HuffingtonPost]], she describes structural responses to a 24-hour newsroom: nap rooms, explicit norms against checking work email after hours or on vacation, and a vacation email tool that deletes messages while an employee is away so the temptation to respond is removed rather than merely discouraged.

## Key Claims
- Burnout can follow from long work hours and insufficient sleep, even for highly successful leaders.
- Personal recovery practices matter, but they are not enough by themselves.
- Always-on work cultures require explicit communication boundaries.
- Workplace tools can remove temptation, not merely ask workers to resist it.
- Rest-supporting practices can be framed as performance support rather than anti-work softness.

## Evidence
- Personal exhaustion: [[arianna-huffington-on-sleep-mornings-and-work-the-early-hour]] says Huffington's ill health and fainting from exhaustion led her to study sleep, rest, technology boundaries, and breaks.
- Newsroom pressure: [[arianna-huffington-on-sleep-mornings-and-work-the-early-hour]] says the nonstop news cycle tempts editors, reporters, and engineers to match 24-hour demand.
- Nap rooms: [[arianna-huffington-on-sleep-mornings-and-work-the-early-hour]] says HuffPost installed two nap rooms that later became full most of the time.
- Email boundaries: [[arianna-huffington-on-sleep-mornings-and-work-the-early-hour]] says no one is expected to check work email after hours, on weekends, or while on vacation.
- Vacation email deletion: [[arianna-huffington-on-sleep-mornings-and-work-the-early-hour]] describes a tool that deletes email sent during time off and asks senders to resend later or contact a designated person if urgent.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source reports leadership intent and selected practices, but does not independently measure burnout reduction, employee uptake, or unintended consequences of deleting vacation email. The practices are described inside a 24-hour news organization and may need adaptation in workplaces with different urgency, labor power, or compliance requirements.
