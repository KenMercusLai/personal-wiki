## Definition
[[PlayerGuidance]] is the use of cues, mechanics, timing, layout, and prior exposure to steer player attention and action without necessarily giving explicit instructions.

## Current Synthesis
The source analyzes a form of weak guidance in [[WomenCommunication]] where players discover an untaught overlap-shot mechanic. The original Japanese sequence repeats a familiar sensitive-word fragment, introduces a headshot scoring incentive, distracts the player with bullet-dodging, then places overlapping sensitive terms in a visually central position. The Chinese localization reproduces the structure with different words so Chinese players are guided toward the same discovery, though the source notes that Chinese character length makes the cue somewhat weaker.

## Key Claims
- Player guidance can be embedded in text repetition and mechanical incentives rather than explicit tutorials.
- Prior exposure to a word primes players to notice it later under pressure.
- Scoring systems can shape where players click or aim.
- Localization must recreate the attentional pathway, not just translate the line.
- Language structure can make a localized guidance cue stronger or weaker than the original.

## Evidence
- Prior exposure: [[wei-jie-ru-he-yong-bao-li-ji-suan-fan-yi-xie-yin-geng-nv-xing-jiao-liu-fan-yi-bi-ji]] describes earlier appearances of the key Japanese term and its Chinese replacement.
- Mechanical incentive: [[wei-jie-ru-he-yong-bao-li-ji-suan-fan-yi-xie-yin-geng-nv-xing-jiao-liu-fan-yi-bi-ji]] explains that the headshot scope rewards shooting the first character of a sensitive word.
- Attention pressure: [[wei-jie-ru-he-yong-bao-li-ji-suan-fan-yi-xie-yin-geng-nv-xing-jiao-liu-fan-yi-bi-ji]] says incoming bullet patterns leave players less spare attention for analysis.
- Visual placement: [[wei-jie-ru-he-yong-bao-li-ji-suan-fan-yi-xie-yin-geng-nv-xing-jiao-liu-fan-yi-bi-ji]] notes that the overlapping sensitive-word segment is placed centrally and visibly.
- Localization limit: [[wei-jie-ru-he-yong-bao-li-ji-suan-fan-yi-xie-yin-geng-nv-xing-jiao-liu-fan-yi-bi-ji]] says the Chinese overlap is shorter and therefore easier to misclick.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The analysis is based on observed design logic and streamer examples rather than instrumentation from all players. It plausibly explains the effect but does not measure success rates across languages or skill levels.
