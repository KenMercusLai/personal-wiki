## Definition
[[AppLandingPages]] are standalone web pages for mobile apps, designed around one conversion goal such as validating an idea, collecting leads, driving downloads, or measuring marketing-message fit.

## Current Synthesis
The Appster source treats app landing pages as the bridge between product idea, market evidence, and acquisition. Before launch, a page can test demand through email signups and feedback; after or near launch, it can drive app-store traffic, capture lead information, and compare campaigns or offers. The page itself should be built around one visitor path: clear problem and benefit, differentiated value proposition, one dominant CTA, trustworthy proof, fast and polished visuals, and a sequence that answers likely objections before asking for action. The inspected screenshots make the advice concrete: WhatsApp's page focuses on simple, secure messaging; Slack shows team-communication value with product UI; Evernote makes account signup the dominant action; Paleo Leap shows how many competing CTAs can obscure next steps; Breather and Huemor demonstrate that minimalism and expressive branding can both work when coherent; Zendesk shows how a vague headline can hide the product explanation below it.

## Key Claims
- App landing pages are useful before the app exists because they can test interest and objections.
- A landing page extends acquisition beyond app-store surfaces by giving founders a shareable destination.
- Conversion forms create lead and demographic evidence that can inform later messaging.
- Value propositions need to state the user problem, benefit, and differentiation compactly.
- A single primary CTA reduces visitor confusion; extra requests should be visually subordinate.
- Visual polish matters only when it reinforces clarity, speed, and brand fit.
- Landing-page structure should follow the visitor's question sequence and keep testing open.

## Evidence
- Validation: [[an-8-min-guide-to-app-landing-pages-the-startup-medium]] says app founders can request email addresses or send the page to potential users for feedback before building.
- Acquisition and leads: [[an-8-min-guide-to-app-landing-pages-the-startup-medium]] says the page can drive downloads through shared links and collect demographic information from conversion forms.
- Value proposition: [[an-8-min-guide-to-app-landing-pages-the-startup-medium]] uses the Slack screenshot as an example of explaining a workplace communication problem with headline, copy, testimonial, and messaging-feature imagery.
- CTA clarity: [[an-8-min-guide-to-app-landing-pages-the-startup-medium]] contrasts the inspected Evernote sign-up button with Paleo Leap's many competing calls to action.
- Visual design: [[an-8-min-guide-to-app-landing-pages-the-startup-medium]] contrasts Breather's neutral whitespace with Huemor's colorful animation-heavy branding, while warning against clutter and slow load time.
- Social proof and hierarchy: [[an-8-min-guide-to-app-landing-pages-the-startup-medium]] recommends relevant testimonials, press mentions, tweet permalinks, and a question-led sequence ending in CTA and proof.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is practitioner advice rather than a controlled conversion study. Its examples depend on a 2017 page-design context, and the screenshots are illustrative rather than full funnel data. The durable claim is not that one layout always wins, but that landing pages should make one offer legible, remove competing actions, provide relevant trust evidence, and test changes with the intended audience.
