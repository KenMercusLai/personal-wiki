## Definition
[[FocusedReading]] is goal-directed reading that filters information around one or more selected creative or research topics, using classification and tags to make relevant material easier to notice and retrieve.

## Current Synthesis
The source positions focused reading as the bridge between broad discovery and deliberate output. Random reading collects potentially useful material, but once the author has one to three likely writing topics, focused reading filters the stream, ignores unrelated material, and increases sensitivity to high-value sources for the current project.

## Key Claims
- Focused reading starts after a learner chooses near-term creative or research topics.
- It improves attention by helping the reader ignore information unrelated to the chosen themes.
- It uses categories and tags to make intake searchable and reusable.
- It turns scattered information into structured material for output.
- It complements random reading by narrowing a broad discovery stream into a purposeful research stream.

## Evidence
- Topic trigger: [[feynman-technique-in-practice-indigo-information-acquisition-knowledge-output-methodology]] says focused reading begins after identifying one to three recent creative themes.
- Attention filter: [[feynman-technique-in-practice-indigo-information-acquisition-knowledge-output-methodology]] describes automatically ignoring unrelated information and increasing perceptual sensitivity to relevant material.
- Tags and categories: [[feynman-technique-in-practice-indigo-information-acquisition-knowledge-output-methodology]] recommends first-level topic categories and flexible tag-based archiving.
- Structured output: [[feynman-technique-in-practice-indigo-information-acquisition-knowledge-output-methodology]] connects focused reading to organizing information into long articles and courses.
- Relationship to random reading: [[feynman-technique-in-practice-indigo-information-acquisition-knowledge-output-methodology]] contrasts random reading's broad collection with focused reading's target-driven selection.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The approach depends on already having selected themes. It may miss valuable serendipity if the learner narrows too early, so the source keeps random reading as a complementary intake mode.
