## Definition
[[TimeDependentValue]] is value that is created, accumulated, or credibly demonstrated through sustained elapsed time and repeated conduct, so it cannot be reproduced solely by increasing the speed or volume of production.

## Current Synthesis
Mature trees provide the clearest case: a decades-old canopy embodies growth that money and effort cannot instantly reproduce. The same mechanism can apply to social and institutional goods. Trust depends on continued conduct, maturity on lived experience, and community on relationships that survive beyond initial enthusiasm. In software and companies, rapid generation can create an artifact quickly, but it cannot by itself establish that maintainers will support it, leadership will work through difficult periods, or customers will be treated responsibly when the project ends.

This does not make speed or automation inherently harmful. Fast iteration can produce learning, and incidental burden can be removed. The boundary is whether time is merely waiting or whether the interval performs work: enabling reflection, testing repeatability, exposing commitment, or allowing relationships and capability to accumulate. Reviews, permissions, compliance routines, and cooling-off periods should therefore be assessed by the function they serve rather than treated as waste solely because they slow a pipeline.

The same distinction qualifies claims about saving time. A tool may reduce the duration of a task without creating discretionary capacity if competition, expectations, or the user immediately refill the saved interval. Time leverage is real only when the reclaimed time remains available for a deliberately chosen use.

## Key Claims
- Some physical, social, and institutional goods accumulate through elapsed time and cannot be mass-produced on demand.
- Trust and maturity are demonstrated through repeated conduct, not a single correct action or a fast initial result.
- Durable software, companies, relationships, and communities require maintenance beyond the creator's first burst of enthusiasm.
- Process friction is valuable when it creates reflection, accountability, safety, or evidence that good performance can be sustained.
- Faster artifact production does not by itself accelerate stewardship, succession, customer relationships, or community formation.
- Nominal time savings do not become personal capacity when competitive pressure immediately converts them into additional output.

## Evidence
- Physical accumulation: [[some-things-just-take-time]] uses old trees, gardens, properties, watches, and crafted goods to illustrate value embedded through growth, age, or extended production.
- Social accumulation: [[some-things-just-take-time]] argues that maturity, trust, relationships, and community require lived experience and repeated behavior.
- Deliberate friction: [[some-things-just-take-time]] cites compliance processes, reviews, permission systems, and cooling-off periods as possible mechanisms for reflection and reliable conduct rather than mere delays.
- Software stewardship: [[some-things-just-take-time]] contrasts briefly active projects and silently disappearing startups with maintenance, succession planning, community formation, and responsible shutdown.
- Time-capture mechanism: [[some-things-just-take-time]] argues that AI and automation can shorten tasks while competition fills the freed hours with more expected output.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a reflective practitioner essay, not a comparative study. It does not measure whether AI-produced software has a shorter service life, whether slower organizations create more trust or quality, or which compliance and review steps prevent meaningful harm. Elapsed time can also entrench obsolete systems, weak institutions, exclusion, complacency, or repeated failure; longevity is evidence of persistence, not proof of worth. Some learning, testing, and relationship-building can occur faster through better tools or processes. The practical test is therefore whether the interval creates or reveals something material, not whether delay is inherently virtuous.

## What Changed
- Created the concept by separating value produced through sustained time from work that is merely slow.
- Added repeated conduct, deliberate friction, stewardship, and protected reclaimed time as distinct mechanisms.
