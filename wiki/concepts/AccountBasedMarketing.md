## Definition
[[AccountBasedMarketing]] is a B2B strategy that concentrates marketing and sales attention on selected accounts or account groups and tailors experiences to their attributes and likely needs.

## Current Synthesis
In the Optimizely homepage case, account-based marketing changes both targeting and the definition of success. Instead of seeking the largest possible pool of anonymous leads, the company prioritizes named accounts and strong-fit industries, then combines account, industry, geography, customer-status, and engagement data to select relevant messages, proof, content, events, and contacts. The source's durable contribution is the tradeoff it makes explicit: lower top-line conversion can be acceptable if the resulting leads are more qualified, better educated, and faster to progress through sales.

## Key Claims
- ABM prioritizes fewer high-fit leads over undifferentiated lead volume.
- Named-account and industry membership can guide tailored website experiences.
- Account context can be combined with geography, customer status, and behavioral engagement rather than used alone.
- Personalized proof, use cases, content, events, and company contacts can support account relevance.
- ABM performance should be judged through qualification and sales progression as well as lead creation.

## Evidence
- Strategic shift: [[designing-for-personalization-the-story-of-optimizelys-homepage-optimizely-blog]] explicitly links homepage personalization to Optimizely's move toward ABM.
- Audience focus: [[designing-for-personalization-the-story-of-optimizelys-homepage-optimizely-blog]] identifies current and prospective named accounts plus target industries as priority groups.
- Experience design: [[designing-for-personalization-the-story-of-optimizelys-homepage-optimizely-blog]] proposes tailored messaging, imagery, validation, use cases, content, events, and key contacts.
- Outcome tradeoff: [[designing-for-personalization-the-story-of-optimizelys-homepage-optimizely-blog]] accepts that homepage conversion might fall while engagement, qualification, or sales velocity improves.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a single vendor-authored implementation plan without reported results. It does not compare ABM with broad demand generation, quantify account coverage, explain false matches or anonymous visitors, measure sales and marketing coordination costs, or establish incremental revenue. The article also predates later privacy and consent expectations around combining firmographic and behavioral data.

## What Changed
- Created a source-scoped ABM concept centered on tailored website experience and downstream lead quality.
