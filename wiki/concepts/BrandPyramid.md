## Definition
The [[BrandPyramid]] is a bottom-up brand framework that connects product attributes to functional customer benefits, emotional benefits, and a long-term aspiration or “something bigger.”

## Current Synthesis
[[GibsonBiddle]] uses the pyramid to separate the product mechanisms that can change through innovation from the customer meaning intended to remain more stable. Attributes must enable practical benefits; exceptional delivery of those benefits should produce named feelings; and the top layer describes the larger future the company hopes to help create. A memorable internal headline can compress the full structure without replacing it.

Netflix illustrates the intended continuity. Its attributes moved from DVD selection, queues, fast mail delivery, and no late fees to streaming access and original content, while “Movie Enjoyment Made Easy,” delight, and escape from reality provided a more durable direction. Apple is offered as a second example in which devices and services support ease of use, productivity and creativity, imaginative freedom, and “revolutionary innovation.”

## Key Claims
- Product attributes are the foundation and must causally enable the promised customer benefits.
- Emotional benefits should name how customers feel when the product exceeds expectations, not merely repeat functional outcomes.
- The top aspiration should orient decades of work rather than describe a near-term feature or campaign.
- Lower-level attributes can evolve while upper-level meaning remains comparatively stable.
- An internal headline can make the pyramid memorable but takes iteration and customer evidence to refine.
- Focus groups, surveys, concept exploration, and A/B tests can challenge candidate expressions of the model.

## Evidence
- Layer structure: [[gibson-biddle-branding-for-builders]] defines attributes, product benefits, emotional benefits, and “something bigger” in bottom-to-top order.
- Netflix continuity: [[gibson-biddle-branding-for-builders]] traces attributes from DVDs and queues to streaming and original content while retaining ease, delight, and escape.
- Apple illustration: [[gibson-biddle-branding-for-builders]] maps hardware and services to ease of use, productivity, creativity, freedom, and revolutionary innovation.
- Iteration: [[gibson-biddle-branding-for-builders]] recommends taking a provisional first pass and testing ideas through research and experiments.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The pyramid is a normative workshop model, not evidence that brands naturally form four levels or that higher layers remain stable. The Apple and Netflix examples are retrospective interpretations of successful companies, and their outcomes cannot be attributed to the framework alone. Homepage conversion tests can evaluate specific expressions under tested conditions but do not by themselves validate emotional meaning, long-term aspiration, pricing power, or durable brand equity.

## What Changed
- Created the concept around the distinction between evolving product attributes and more durable customer meaning.

## Related Concepts
