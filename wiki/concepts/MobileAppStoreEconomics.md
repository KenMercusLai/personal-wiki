## Definition
[[MobileAppStoreEconomics]] is the distinction between app marketplace scale, monetization, geography, category mix, and platform-owner revenue in mobile ecosystems.

## Current Synthesis
The VentureBeat/App Annie source shows that app-store advantage can split across different metrics. In 2015, [[GooglePlay]] had twice as many downloads as Apple's [[AppStore]], but Apple's store generated 75% more revenue. The source therefore turns [[Android]] and [[IOS]] into different economic profiles rather than a single winner-take-all ranking: Android/Google Play held broader install and download reach, especially through emerging markets, while iOS/App Store concentrated higher user spending in China, the U.S., and Japan. Games were the shared revenue engine, but the dependence was stronger on Google Play.

## Key Claims
- Download volume and store revenue can point to different platform winners.
- In 2015, Google Play's download advantage widened while Apple's App Store revenue advantage also widened.
- Emerging-market growth can expand app-store downloads without immediately erasing monetization gaps.
- iOS App Store revenue strength reflected higher-spending user bases and country mix in the source.
- Games dominated mobile app-store revenue on both platforms, especially on Google Play.

## Evidence
- Download/revenue split: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] reports Google Play at 100% more downloads while Apple's App Store had 75% more revenue.
- Download momentum: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] says Google Play's download lead grew from 60% in 2014 to 100% in 2015.
- Emerging-market downloads: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] names Brazil, India, Indonesia, Turkey, and Mexico as Google Play download-growth drivers.
- iOS revenue geography: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] names China, the U.S., and Japan as primary drivers of Apple's App Store revenue growth.
- Game revenue share: [[app-annie-2015-google-play-saw-100-percent-more-downloads-than-ios-app-store-venturebeat]] reports games at 90% of Google Play revenue and 75% of iOS App Store revenue.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source reports relative metrics from a 2015 App Annie retrospective and does not include the original methodology, absolute downloads, absolute revenue, regional revenue breakdowns, or later shifts in subscription, ads, alternative payments, regulation, or app-store policy. It also treats store revenue as developer and platform economics without separately modeling Apple's or Google's commission take.

- Created the concept to capture the 2015 split between Google Play's download scale and Apple's App Store revenue concentration.
