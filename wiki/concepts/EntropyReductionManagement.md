## Definition
[[EntropyReductionManagement]] is a management model that treats organizations as systems that naturally drift toward disorder unless leaders create goals, rules, information systems, incentives, and managerial capacity that restore coherence.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents entropy reduction as [[WangTao]]'s language for what [[DJI]] lacked during its freewheeling growth period and what it needed after corruption and factional power centers emerged. In this model, a founder cannot rely only on product passion or high talent density; the company needs explicit mechanisms that make goals clear, process visible, results measurable, and contribution distinguishable.

## Key Claims
- Fast-growing organizations naturally accumulate disorder when authority, incentives, and information flows are not deliberately managed.
- Product-led talent density can worsen drift if everyone wants glamorous invention and nobody wants rule-making, oversight, or management work.
- A founder's entropy-reduction capacity is a bottleneck until the organization can produce capable managers and systems.
- Clear goals, transparent processes, measurable outcomes, and contribution-linked rewards are presented as core entropy-reduction mechanisms.
- Tools and management layers can reduce disorder but can also create new disorder if they are not grounded in capable leadership.
- The same metaphor extends to personal discipline: learning, humility, and reflection reduce disorder while pride, distraction, and indulgence increase it.

## Evidence
- Organizational disorder: [[dui-hua-da-jiang-wang-tao-qiu-zhen-li-de-zi-you-huo-cheng-gu-shi]] describes DJI before reform as full of informal "feudal" units, corruption risk, and insufficient company-level accountability.
- Product-culture limit: [[dui-hua-da-jiang-wang-tao-qiu-zhen-li-de-zi-you-huo-cheng-gu-shi]] says DJI had many people wanting to be bright leaves doing products and innovation, but too few willing to be branches and trunks doing management and rules.
- Founder bottleneck: [[dui-hua-da-jiang-wang-tao-qiu-zhen-li-de-zi-you-huo-cheng-gu-shi]] says Wang framed the CEO as the source of entropy reduction, with limited personal bandwidth until more strong managers and systems exist.
- Mechanisms: [[dui-hua-da-jiang-wang-tao-qiu-zhen-li-de-zi-you-huo-cheng-gu-shi]] summarizes ideal management as clear goals, transparent process, measurable results, and definable contribution.
- Personal extension: [[dui-hua-da-jiang-wang-tao-qiu-zhen-li-de-zi-you-huo-cheng-gu-shi]] extends entropy reduction to learning, overcoming inertia, humility, and inner calm.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept comes from one founder's retrospective model, not a general management theory validated across many organizations. It may clarify DJI's internal reform story, but it can also over-center the CEO and understate independent checks, worker voice, or external governance.

## What Changed
- Created a concept for Wang Tao's entropy-reduction model of management and personal discipline.
