## Definition
[[IndependentCreator]] describes a person who creates and sells products outside a conventional employer structure, often combining product, engineering, marketing, operations, and customer support personally.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats independent creation as both a work arrangement and an identity. For [[Hawstein]], leaving employment opened a path through content products, global SaaS, company setup, customer support, and marketing. The role is not romanticized as pure building: the creator must find viable markets, accept distribution work, handle financial infrastructure, and decide how much autonomy to trade for scale.

## Key Claims
- Independent creation can begin with the best available entry point rather than the creator's ideal business model.
- Community immersion can help a creator notice possible markets and business patterns.
- Independence requires both building and selling.
- Autonomy can be a rational operating priority, even when hiring could reduce workload.
- Global software distribution makes small creators less bounded by local markets.
- Independent creation can be understood as a form of [[CareerPlanning]] built through staged experiments.

## Evidence
- Entry point: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] presents [[AlgoCasts]] as the best starting point available in 2018 even though the author wanted SaaS later.
- Community immersion: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] describes deep reading and listening in overseas Indie Hacker spaces before identifying a SaaS opportunity.
- Build and sell: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] uses the "build" and "sell" distinction to explain why many technical people struggle as independent creators.
- Autonomy: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] explains rejecting hiring because added coordination would reduce freedom.
- Global reach: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] emphasizes that code lets one person's product reach internet-connected users worldwide.
- Career path: [[yi-ge-du-li-chuang-zao-zhe-de-wu-nian]] narrates five years of evolving work rather than a single fixed role.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is one creator's retrospective. It does not show survival rates, capital requirements, or how the path changes for people without strong engineering skill, savings, global payment access, or tolerance for uncertainty.
