## Definition
[[PublicRelationsStrategy]] is the deliberate communication practice of shaping how important publics understand and respond to a company, especially through goal-led stories, media relationships, targeted outreach, and credible public proof.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats PR as disciplined founder work rather than a luxury reserved for companies that can hire agencies. For bootstrapped startups, the first constraint is focus: founders should decide whether they want attention for a launch, hiring, fundraising, market entry, or another goal before choosing the story and audience. The practical system then compresses the company into a one-sentence pitch, targets the publications and journalists whose audiences match the startup, and builds a contact list that supports repeated but respectful outreach.

The strongest lesson is that PR is not a one-off launch blast. The article expands the set of legitimate PR occasions to include funding news, new features, industry changes, original customer data, guest articles, and source-request platforms such as HARO, ProfNet, and SourceBottle. Good PR therefore overlaps with [[StartupVisionSelling]], [[ContentLedAcquisition]], and [[PersonalBranding]]: the startup makes its future, proof, and expertise easier for outsiders to understand.

Crisis response is the pressured version of that same communication work. When public attention focuses on a visible product weakness or workaround, the company has to address the critique directly, restate the intended customer value, explain why the criticized component belongs inside a larger experience, and offer concrete accountability. [[JeffDunn]] does this for [[Juicero]] by connecting the Press, Produce Packs, app, sourcing, delivery, food-safety controls, and supply-chain data to the company's healthy-habit promise.

## Key Claims
- Startup PR should begin with a specific audience and outcome rather than a vague desire for exposure.
- The one-sentence pitch should state the problem, target group, and distinct value in plain language.
- Media outreach depends on relevance, personalization, brevity, timing, and respectful follow-up.
- Strategic targeting beats prestige targeting when a famous outlet does not reach the startup's actual customers.
- PR opportunities extend beyond product launches into data, feature, fundraising, market-change, guest-post, and reverse-pitching moments.
- Bootstrapped startups can build PR capacity through founder discipline, research, and lightweight contact systems before hiring outside firms.
- Crisis response should address the public critique directly, restate the intended customer value, and offer concrete accountability when trust has been damaged.

## Evidence
- Goal and story fit: [[8-pr-strategies-for-bootstrapped-startups-the-startup-medium]] says startups should define whether PR is meant to support launch attention, hiring, investment visibility, or geographic expansion before deciding what story to pitch.
- Pitch clarity: [[8-pr-strategies-for-bootstrapped-startups-the-startup-medium]] recommends a buzzword-free one-sentence pitch that names the problem, target market, and unique value.
- Media outreach norms: [[8-pr-strategies-for-bootstrapped-startups-the-startup-medium]] advises personalized short emails, one journalist at a time, time-zone awareness, subject-line testing, and polite feedback-seeking follow-up.
- Targeting and systems: [[8-pr-strategies-for-bootstrapped-startups-the-startup-medium]] recommends researching niche-relevant publications and keeping journalist/editor contacts in a spreadsheet or CRM.
- Continuous PR surface: [[8-pr-strategies-for-bootstrapped-startups-the-startup-medium]] lists investment announcements, feature releases, industry shifts, customer data, guest blogging, and reverse-pitching services as PR opportunities beyond launch.
- Crisis reframing: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] has [[JeffDunn]] respond to hand-squeezing criticism by explaining [[Juicero]]'s connected system value and distinguishing hand-squeezed output from the intended daily experience.
- Accountability gesture: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] extends a 30-day refund guarantee to any customer who feels Juicero is not making healthy habits easier.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The sources are operator or company-authored advice rather than controlled evidence, and several recommended tools or platforms may have changed since 2017. The bootstrapped PR article assumes founders have enough story quality, customer insight, or data to deserve attention; careful targeting cannot rescue an uninteresting or unsupported pitch. The Juicero note shows another limit: a crisis response can clarify the intended value proposition, but it cannot by itself prove that customers accept the cost, complexity, or system-level benefits.
