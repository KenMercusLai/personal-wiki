## Definition
[[BullseyeFramework]] is a startup channel-selection method that moves from broad possibility generation, through cheap comparative tests, to concentrated investment in the channel or channels producing traction at the current stage.

## Current Synthesis
The framework counters premature channel commitment. Its outer ring asks what could work across a broad taxonomy; its middle ring tests the most plausible strategies with limited cost; its center identifies the few channels worth focused execution. Because channel effectiveness changes as a company and market develop, reaching the center does not end the process: the team repeats it when the current channel saturates or the next growth stage requires a different mechanism.

The DuckDuckGo case illustrates the intended logic rather than proving the framework. Hacker News and StumbleUpon contributed early discovery, a billboard sharpened the privacy message, external privacy events increased motivation, and browser integration reduced distribution friction. Those shifts suggest stage dependence, but the source does not provide test records or isolate each channel's causal effect.

## Key Claims
- Teams should consider a broad channel set before committing to familiar options.
- Early tests should be cheap enough to compare possibilities without exhausting resources.
- Focus follows evidence: the few channels moving the relevant growth measure receive concentrated effort.
- Channel fit is temporary, so selection must be repeated across growth stages.
- Product quality, market timing, community support, and external events can interact with channel performance.

## Evidence
- Three-ring process: [[duckduckgo-the-former-solopreneur-that-is-beating-google-at-its-game-fourweekmba]] explains possibility, probability testing, and the central bullseye as the framework's ordered stages.
- Channel breadth: [[duckduckgo-the-former-solopreneur-that-is-beating-google-at-its-game-fourweekmba]] lists 19 channels spanning publicity, advertising, content, sales, partnerships, platforms, events, and community building.
- Stage change: [[duckduckgo-the-former-solopreneur-that-is-beating-google-at-its-game-fourweekmba]] moves from technical-community launch to offline publicity, privacy-driven word of mouth, and browser distribution.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The evidence is a retrospective interpretation of one successful company plus a founder-authored framework, not a controlled comparison with startups that selected channels differently. The article does not specify test duration, minimum sample size, success thresholds, attribution rules, channel interaction, or stopping criteria. A vivid external event can make a channel appear causal when product improvement, accumulated brand, defaults, and broader demand changed simultaneously.
