## Definition
[[ProgrammerFreelancing]] is a self-directed work path where a programmer earns outside conventional employment through a mix of products, teaching, services, audience-building, or other software-adjacent income streams.

## Current Synthesis
The source frames programmer freelancing as a financially bounded career experiment. It can offer escape from commuting, low-meaning work, interview cycles, and workplace anxiety, but it is not simply freedom from work. The viable pattern in the source is to secure stable revenue first, use product work as a learning and upside experiment, build a personal distribution channel, and preserve an exit option if the path fails.

## Key Claims
- Financial runway is the gating condition because early income is uncertain.
- Freelancing can begin as a reversible experiment rather than a permanent identity.
- Stable income streams should precede speculative product bets.
- Product work can be valuable even before it becomes financially meaningful.
- Personal audience-building can reduce future distribution costs.
- Solo freelancing requires explicit routines and mental-health safeguards.

## Evidence
- Financial runway: [[bu-shang-ban-de-1000-tian-cheng-xu-yuan-zi-you-zhi-ye-b-ji-hua]] warns against starting under heavy mortgage, car-loan, or family-support pressure.
- Reversible experiment: [[bu-shang-ban-de-1000-tian-cheng-xu-yuan-zi-you-zhi-ye-b-ji-hua]] says the author began like a gap year and could return to work if needed.
- Stable income first: [[bu-shang-ban-de-1000-tian-cheng-xu-yuan-zi-you-zhi-ye-b-ji-hua]] identifies [[Imooc]] courses as the main income source and advises earning before dream-product experimentation.
- Product learning: [[bu-shang-ban-de-1000-tian-cheng-xu-yuan-zi-you-zhi-ye-b-ji-hua]] says [[EasyCV]] improved technical, DevOps, copywriting, promotion, and business-validation skill.
- Distribution: [[bu-shang-ban-de-1000-tian-cheng-xu-yuan-zi-you-zhi-ye-b-ji-hua]] compares platform audiences and says self-owned traffic has concrete value.
- Routines: [[bu-shang-ban-de-1000-tian-cheng-xu-yuan-zi-you-zhi-ye-b-ji-hua]] recommends regular work rituals, milestones, exercise, social contact, and a two-year stop-loss window.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is one programmer's self-report and depends on conditions such as low financial pressure, teaching ability, product-building skill, and audience development. It does not show that freelancing is generally safer than employment, only that employment is not automatically sustainable either.
