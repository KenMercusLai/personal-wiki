## Definition
Mobile email engagement is the pattern of email opens and interactions on phones and mobile operating systems, especially as it affects campaign timing, responsive design, and the expectation that email can be checked quickly throughout the day.

## Current Synthesis
SendGrid's 2016 holiday analysis treats mobile email as the ordinary condition of consumer inbox behavior rather than a special channel. Mobile represented just under half of SendGrid's observed opens, external Litmus tracking put Apple and Android opens around 54%, and SendGrid's early-November analysis found mobile users opened slightly faster than desktop users. The article's practical conclusion is that email campaigns need responsive templates and cross-device consistency.

## Key Claims
- Mobile opens had reached parity-level importance for email marketers by the 2016 holiday period.
- Mobile users may respond faster because the inbox is continually available on phones.
- Responsive templates are necessary when campaigns are opened across phones, desktops, and other devices.
- External benchmarks such as Litmus can corroborate platform-specific open data.

## Evidence
Mobile opens had reached parity-level importance:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] reports SendGrid mobile opens just under 50% and cites [[Litmus]] for Apple and Android opens around 54%.

Mobile users may respond faster:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] says median delay was a little shorter for mobile opens than desktop opens.

Responsive templates are necessary:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] argues marketers must design responsive templates for a consistent multi-device experience.

External benchmarks can corroborate platform-specific data:
- [[black-friday-and-cyber-monday-by-the-numbers-sendgrid]] uses Litmus alongside SendGrid's own observations.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The article does not provide a full statistical model for mobile open timing, and the "near-real-time" interpretation is plausible but inferential. Device-open shares can vary by audience, campaign type, and measurement method.

## What Changed
- Added mobile email engagement as a cross-device marketing concept with SendGrid and Litmus evidence.
