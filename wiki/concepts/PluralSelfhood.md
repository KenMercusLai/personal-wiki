## Definition
[[PluralSelfhood]] is the view that a person contains multiple situated and potentially conflicting ways of being, none of which must serve as a single timeless essence.

## Current Synthesis
The source rejects both a high-versus-low split and the demand for one perfectly consistent identity. Solitude and relationship, reason and desire, freedom and attachment, spirit and bodily pleasure can coexist without one side invalidating the other. Which possibility becomes salient can change with age, relationship, and circumstance; action may therefore disclose a self that prior introspection could not predict.

This does not make identity empty or every impulse equally authoritative. Past descriptions remain informative, and conduct still has consequences, but a description should guide observation rather than operate as a sentence that forbids change.

## Key Claims
- A person's internal life can contain more than two genuine and conflicting tendencies.
- Psychological completeness need not require eliminating contradiction.
- Identity descriptions become restrictive when they are treated as permanent permissions and prohibitions.
- New situations and actions can reveal real capacities that introspection alone did not anticipate.
- Change across time does not by itself prove betrayal of an authentic core.

## Evidence
### Multiplicity and contradiction
- [[ru-chang]] interprets the “magic theater” in 《荒原狼》 as replacing Harry Haller's human-versus-wolf split with many possible selves.
- [[ru-chang]] uses the contrast between Narcissus and Goldmund to hold order and impulse, reflection and contact, solitude and relationship together rather than force a final choice between them.

### Identity as description rather than verdict
- [[ru-chang]] argues that enjoying solitude or intellectual life can be true without proving that relationship, bodily pleasure, play, or future change is false.
- [[ru-chang]] presents dancing as an action through which Harry discovers that “I do not belong here” was incomplete rather than an eternal fact.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source offers philosophical and literary interpretation, not a tested model of personality or identity development. Some identities involve durable obligations, histories, competencies, or group memberships that cannot be revised merely by adopting a new experience. Plurality also does not excuse harmful conduct, dissolve responsibility, or show that every desire should be enacted. The useful claim is openness to situated possibility under consequence, not the absence of continuity or standards.

## What Changed
- Created a model of identity as multiple, situated, and revisable.
- Distinguished an identity description from a permanent behavioral verdict.
- Preserved responsibility and continuity as limits on unrestricted self-reinvention.

## Related Concepts
