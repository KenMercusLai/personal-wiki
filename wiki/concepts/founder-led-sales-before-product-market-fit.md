Sources: [4 Lessons From a Failed Startup - From and For First Time Founders]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/maderight-failed-startup-lessons.md" >}}); [54 Screwups as a Startup CEO]({{< relref "/wiki/sources/anand-sanwal-54-screwups-startup-ceo.md" >}})

Founder-led sales before product-market fit means the founders keep direct responsibility for selling until the company knows what problem it solves, who urgently needs it, and what sales motion repeatedly works. Maderight's post-mortem calls hiring a VP of sales too early a mistake, and says the company compounded the error by taking too long to reverse it.

The source distinguishes pre-fit sales from post-fit sales. Before product-market fit, sales is not primarily a revenue machine; it is a way to talk to customers, identify the problem, and test whether the proposed solution works. Revenue can appear during that process, but it is a byproduct of a successful test rather than proof that the go-to-market system is ready to be delegated.

Founders are better suited to this stage because the product, pitch, and target customer may change repeatedly. Direct selling also teaches the founder what kind of salesperson should eventually be hired and what performance benchmark is realistic once the company has a more stable sales process.

Anand Sanwal adds the founder's behavioral side of that learning loop. At CB Insights, he spent too much early time on product work, hoped partnerships would solve distribution, chased low-quality leads, gave demos without enough follow-up, hesitated to ask for the sale, and talked at prospects instead of asking diagnostic questions. The improvement came when sales became a customer conversation rather than a scripted pitch.

## Failure Signals

- A sales hire is expected to discover the market while the product and customer profile are still changing.
- Revenue targets replace learning goals before the team knows why customers buy or refuse.
- Founders lose direct exposure to objections, buying triggers, and workflow details.
- The company cannot define a useful benchmark for judging a future sales hire.
- Partnerships are expected to compensate for a sales motion the founders have not learned themselves.
