## Definition
[[ComputerScienceZines]] are self-published, illustration-heavy educational works that explain computer-science topics with a low-barrier, approachable, creator-controlled format.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents CS zines as both an educational medium and a creative permission structure. [[AmyWibowo]] wanted math and science books with more drawings, then chose zines because self-publishing let her control topic and tone while avoiding publisher assumptions about what young readers or serious technical audiences would accept. The zine format lowered perfection pressure, which made the project less intimidating and compatible with cute drawings, photocopier aesthetics, and serious technical subjects.

## Key Claims
- Illustrated explanations can make computer science feel more accessible and less alienating.
- Self-publishing protects topic choice, voice, and visual style from gatekeeping.
- Low expected polish can reduce perfectionism enough to help creators ship.
- Educational artifacts can combine technical accuracy, art, and inclusive culture.
- Zines can turn a personal need for better explanations into independent educational work.

## Evidence
- Motivation: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says Wibowo had wished math books included more drawings and explanatory analogies.
- Medium choice: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says zines are self-published magazines and gave her full control over writing and drawing.
- Gatekeeping avoidance: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says she did not want a publisher rejecting encryption for young readers or cute drawings for serious topics.
- Perfectionism reduction: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says zine expectations made the project less scary.
- Launch validation: [[a-career-retrospective-10-years-working-in-tech-sailor-mercury-medium]] says the Kickstarter funded quickly and was framed in press as accessible and inclusive CS.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source does not compare zines with textbooks, documentation, videos, or interactive courses. It describes one creator's reasons for choosing zines and should be treated as a source-scoped medium argument.
