## Definition
[[HereAndNowMedia]] is a proposed media paradigm in which temporal immediacy, situated presence, participation, location, or ongoing mutability become part of a work’s value rather than incidental delivery characteristics.

## Current Synthesis
Danco distinguishes three stages. The web made content and distribution abundant, elevating pull through destination and search and push through feeds and publication. Front-facing cameras and ephemeral messages then made temporary, varied self-expression easier. Basche supplies a complementary Snapchat-centered account: when capture and consumption occur on the same carried device, vertical media and brief Stories can let a person “jump into” a friend’s unfolding experience rather than inspect a permanent-profile artifact.

Both sources stop short of equating ephemerality with complete presence. Basche describes Snapchat as everywhere and nowhere—a latent capacity for moments to be shared and forgotten—while Danco argues that its “there” is less definite than Twitch’s participatory setting or location-bound augmented reality. The resulting synthesis distinguishes immediate interpersonal access from durable shared place. Synchronous influence, physical location, and mutable works are other ways for time or place to become part of content.

The Instagram Stories retrospective adds a further boundary: immediate capture can make the present more shareable while also converting it into material for an audience. A disappearing post may reduce archive pressure, yet the visible viewer list and expectation of frequent updates can encourage staging, monitoring, and continuous documentation. Here-and-now media therefore does not guarantee unselfconscious presence; it can preserve edited memory while pulling attention from the lived event toward its broadcast version.

## Key Claims
- Media value can migrate when technology makes an old constraint abundant and a different resource scarce.
- Same-device capture and consumption can make momentary experience a native communication unit.
- Ephemerality lowers persistence pressure but does not by itself create a shared place or remove performance pressure.
- Presence becomes stronger when audiences can affect an unfolding experience rather than only receive it.
- Physical location, synchronous interaction, and post-release mutability are distinct ways to embed time or place in content.
- Capturing varied ephemeral expression does not guarantee control of the next profit pool.

## Evidence
- Scarcity sequence: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] moves from content and distribution to pull and push, then to momentary expression and presence.
- Experience jumping: [[ghost-in-the-machine-snapchat-isnt-mobile-first-its-something-else-entirely]] connects camera default, vertical media, same-device exchange, and expiring Stories to access to a friend’s present experience.
- Ephemeral boundary: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] and [[ghost-in-the-machine-snapchat-isnt-mobile-first-its-something-else-entirely]] praise Snapchat’s immediate expression while describing its “place” as weakly defined or everywhere and nowhere.
- Participatory place, location, and mutability: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] uses Twitch, Pokémon Go, and edits to *The Life of Pablo* as distinct cases.
- Performance boundary: [[instagram-stories-at-two-what-price-have-we-paid-for-recording-everything]] reports that disappearing Stories and absent public likes coexist with viewer monitoring, staged scenes, continuous documentation, and positive edited memory.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework comes primarily from two overlapping 2016 strategy essays and does not define or measure presence, scarcity, intimacy, value, adoption, or profit capture. The 2018 Stories essay supplies anecdotes and secondary research rather than a representative test of attention, performance, or wellbeing. Ephemeral products can create meaningful shared contexts, while live products can feel passive or placeless; immediate posting can also make people more self-conscious rather than more present. Search, feeds, durable archives, asynchronous exchange, and fixed works remain valuable rather than being superseded. Snapchat’s interface and sharing claims lack comparative user research; Danco did not use Twitch; and the examples may reflect social graphs, intellectual property, distribution, regulation, or execution more than one common paradigm.

## What Changed
- Added continuous documentation and audience monitoring as costs that can accompany ephemeral immediacy.
- Clarified that reduced archive pressure does not imply unselfconscious presence or freedom from performance.
- Added edited positive memory as a possible benefit of recorded moments.
