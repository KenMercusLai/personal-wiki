## Definition
[[HereAndNowMedia]] is [[AlexDanco]]'s proposed media paradigm in which temporal immediacy, situated presence, participation, location, or ongoing mutability become part of a work's value rather than incidental delivery characteristics.

## Current Synthesis
The source distinguishes three stages. The web made content and distribution abundant, elevating pull through destination and search and push through feeds and publication. Front-facing cameras and ephemeral messages then made temporary, varied self-expression easier. Danco argues that access to momentary expression is not the endpoint: the next bottleneck is a credible sense of being somewhere together now. Snapchat approaches that condition through camera-first ephemerality; Twitch adds a shared participatory setting; location-based [[AugmentedReality]] anchors the experience in physical place; and mutable streaming releases add a second time dimension to works formerly treated as fixed.

## Key Claims
- Media value can migrate when technology makes an old constraint abundant and a different resource scarce.
- Ephemerality lowers the persistence cost of temporary expression but does not by itself create a shared place.
- Presence becomes stronger when audiences can affect an unfolding experience rather than only receive it.
- Physical location, synchronous interaction, and post-release mutability are distinct ways to make time or place part of content.
- Capturing the old opportunity—such as varied ephemeral expression—does not guarantee control of the next profit pool.

## Evidence
- Scarcity sequence: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] moves from content and distribution to pull and push, then to momentary expression and presence.
- Ephemeral boundary: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] praises Snapchat's expressive variance while arguing that its “there” remains weakly defined.
- Participatory place: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] treats Twitch's gameplay, viewing, and audience interaction as a native shared experience.
- Location and mutability: [[from-pull-and-push-to-here-and-now-the-grand-bargain-of-facebook-and-the-feed-is-unraveling-what-comes-next]] uses Pokémon Go and edits to *The Life of Pablo* as different forms of situated or time-varying media.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The framework comes from one 2016 strategy essay and does not define or measure presence, scarcity, value, adoption, or profit capture. Ephemeral products can create meaningful shared contexts, while live products can feel passive or placeless. Search, feeds, durable archives, asynchronous exchange, and fixed works remain valuable rather than being superseded. The examples are heterogeneous, selected after early success, and may reflect social graphs, intellectual property, creator supply, distribution, regulation, or execution more than one common paradigm.

- Created the concept as a qualified synthesis of Danco's proposed shift beyond pull, push, and ephemerality.
