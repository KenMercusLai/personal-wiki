## Definition
[[EngineerInvestment]] is an engineer-oriented approach to investing that combines technical-domain understanding, business analysis, risk discipline, and personal leverage rather than treating markets as a pure price-trading game.

## Current Synthesis
The source argues that engineers can bring domain judgment to investing, especially around AI, semiconductors, software, cloud infrastructure, and digital assets, but that technical enthusiasm is not enough. The stronger version of the frame asks engineers to understand companies, cash flows, valuation, financial statements, ETF composition, and downside recovery math while refusing leverage-heavy or trend-following behavior. It also broadens "investment" beyond securities: code, writing, and sharing can turn expertise into assets that compound trust, distribution, and income.

## Key Claims
- Engineering knowledge can help interpret technology investment themes, but it does not remove market risk.
- Good investing requires company-level judgment, not only recognition of exciting technologies.
- Portfolio examples should be paired with downside scenarios because gains and recovery math are asymmetric.
- Engineers have non-financial investment paths through code, writing, products, and public sharing.
- The best "investment" in this source is improved judgment, not a specific ticker or trade.

## Evidence
- Domain judgment: [[gong-cheng-shi-ru-he-geng-hao-tou-zi]] maps AI hardware, cloud, software, applications, semiconductors, and blockchain exposure into example stocks and ETFs.
- Company frame: [[gong-cheng-shi-ru-he-geng-hao-tou-zi]] quotes the idea that buying stocks means buying companies and future discounted cash flows.
- Risk math: [[gong-cheng-shi-ru-he-geng-hao-tou-zi]] contrasts a +52% example portfolio with a high-point purchase scenario that would require about +81.8% recovery.
- Non-financial leverage: [[gong-cheng-shi-ru-he-geng-hao-tou-zi]] argues that code, writing, and sharing can package expertise into scalable products or trust assets.
- Judgment priority: [[gong-cheng-shi-ru-he-geng-hao-tou-zi]] ends by prioritizing clear thinking and judgment over effort alone.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a personal presentation, not a tested investing strategy or financial recommendation. The market examples are date-specific to 2024-2025 prices and may not generalize across future regimes, tax situations, jurisdictions, or individual risk tolerance.
