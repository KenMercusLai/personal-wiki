## Definition
[[PlatformStickiness]] is the tendency for users to stay with a platform because the apps, workflows, data, habits, or services they depend on are better supported there than elsewhere.

## Current Synthesis
The source gives platform stickiness a productivity-app mechanism. If a user comes to rely on serious productivity tools available on only one mobile platform, that user becomes less likely to consider rival platforms at upgrade time. Thompson argues that this makes subscription support strategically relevant to platform owners: helping productivity developers build viable recurring businesses can strengthen the platform's own retention.

## Key Claims
- Indispensable productivity apps can bind users to the platform where those apps work best or exclusively.
- Subscription support can make those apps more economically viable, especially for small developers.
- Platform-owner policy should be judged by whether it attracts and retains users, not by whether it satisfies developers in general.
- As smartphone growth shifts from first-time buyers to replacement buyers, retaining existing users becomes more important.

## Evidence
- Indispensable apps: [[adobes-subscription-model-why-platform-owners-should-care-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] says users who depend on platform-specific productivity apps may not consider another platform when upgrading.
- Subscription-policy link: [[adobes-subscription-model-why-platform-owners-should-care-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] argues platform owners should add subscription support for productivity apps.
- Developer-policy qualification: [[adobes-subscription-model-why-platform-owners-should-care-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] says developer-friendly policies matter only when they attract more users.
- Replacement-market logic: [[adobes-subscription-model-why-platform-owners-should-care-stratechery-by-ben-thompson]] says previous smartphone owners are an increasing growth opportunity, making retention a priority.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source argues from strategic logic rather than platform switching data. It also focuses on productivity apps; entertainment, messaging, payments, device integration, or cloud services may create different forms of stickiness.

## What Changed
- Created the concept to capture the platform-retention logic behind productivity-app subscription support.
