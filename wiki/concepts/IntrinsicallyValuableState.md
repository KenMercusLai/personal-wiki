## Definition
[[IntrinsicallyValuableState]] is [[PiotrWozniak]]'s term for a state whose value is not derived from another object, outcome, pleasure, religious premise, or arbitrary preference; in the source, it functions as a meta-goal behind all other [[GoalSetting]].

## Current Synthesis
The source presents intrinsically valuable state as an answer to the problem that most human goals are instrumental. Learning mathematics, seeking status, pursuing comfort, or even defending a moral ideal can be described as valuable because it points toward some further valued condition. Wozniak argues that science does not currently prove whether a genuinely intrinsic value exists, but that the possibility is not zero because scientific knowledge and models of reality can change.

The practical conclusion is epistemic rather than doctrinal: humanity's defensible shared purpose is to keep expanding knowledge and modeling reality while trying to discover or refute objective intrinsic value. This extends the wiki's earlier Wozniak material from educational motivation into existential orientation: [[KnowledgeValuationNetwork]] explains how knowledge becomes valuable for learners, while this concept asks what ultimate value, if any, could ground all such valuations.

## Key Claims
- Ordinary goals are usually instrumentally valuable because their worth depends on another desired outcome.
- Religion, pleasure, love, life, consciousness, or democracy may terminate a value chain for some people, but the source treats those endings as unproven or arbitrary unless objective intrinsic value is established.
- Science does not currently certify the existence or content of intrinsic value.
- Because the probability of intrinsic value is uncertain rather than proven impossible, the rational response is continued knowledge growth and reality modeling.
- A shared search for intrinsic value can reduce self-centered aims and existential anxiety.
- For an individual, adopting this meta-goal can simplify decisions and support productivity by removing some uncertainty about what matters.

## Evidence
- Instrumental-value model: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] describes value as a chain where A matters because it increases the probability of B.
- Unproven value terminuses: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] says many people stop the chain at religion, pleasure, love, life, consciousness, or democracy, but Wozniak treats those stops as insufficiently grounded.
- Scientific uncertainty: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] argues that current science does not define or prove intrinsic value, while future science might change the state of knowledge.
- Knowledge-growth response: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] says the wise path is to increase knowledge and model reality while investigating whether intrinsic value exists.
- Shared-purpose claim: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] presents the concept as a way to move beyond immortality seeking, self-interest, and conflict.
- Individual effect: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] says a settled meta-goal removed worry and uncertainty from Wozniak's own life and made productivity easier.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept is source-scoped and philosophical. The source does not prove that intrinsic value exists, does not give an operational test for recognizing it, and explicitly rests on uncertainty. Its peace and anxiety-reduction claims are autobiographical and theoretical rather than independently validated.
