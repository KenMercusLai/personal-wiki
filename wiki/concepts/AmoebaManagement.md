## Definition
[[AmoebaManagement]] is a management approach, associated in the source with Kyocera, that treats many small internal teams as autonomous business-like units.

## Current Synthesis
The Bloomberg source uses Amoeba Management mainly as the predecessor to Disco's Personal Will. Kyocera's version operates thousands of internal teams as individual companies, while [[Disco]] extends that logic down to individual employees through Will, auctions, internal billing, penalties, and bonus settlement. The concept therefore matters here as a bridge between team-level profit responsibility and a more radical individual-level internal market.

## Key Claims
- Amoeba Management treats internal teams as business-like units rather than only functional departments.
- Disco's Personal Will is presented as an extension of Amoeba Management from teams to individuals.
- The transfer from team autonomy to individual markets creates stronger price visibility but also more personal pressure.

## Evidence
- Predecessor model: [[charging-employees-for-conference-rooms-helps-disco-boost-profit-bloomberg]] says Kyocera's Amoeba Management operates thousands of internal teams as individual companies.
- Disco adaptation: [[charging-employees-for-conference-rooms-helps-disco-boost-profit-bloomberg]] says Naito extended that autonomy to individual employees and called it Personal Will.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source only briefly describes Amoeba Management and does not evaluate Kyocera's implementation in detail. This page should be expanded only after ingesting a source focused on Kyocera or the management method itself.
