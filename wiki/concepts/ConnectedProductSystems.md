## Definition
[[ConnectedProductSystems]] are products whose claimed value depends on coordinated hardware, software, data, supply chains, services, and user workflows rather than on a single visible device or output.

## Current Synthesis
The Juicero source presents a connected product system under reputational pressure. [[Juicero]] argues that its Press should not be evaluated only as a mechanical squeezer because the company designed a wider system: Produce Packs, app reminders, organic farm sourcing, doorstep delivery, recall response, calibrated pressing, and supply-chain management for live raw produce. The case shows both the appeal and risk of system-level value. If customers recognize the system benefits, connected design can make a daily habit safer and more convenient; if public attention isolates one component, the entire product can appear overbuilt or poorly matched to perceived value.

## Key Claims
- Connected products often make their value claim at the system level rather than at the single-device level.
- Hardware can act as a control point for quality, safety, data, and workflow consistency.
- Perishable or regulated supply chains can make connectivity more central to the product promise.
- App reminders and delivery services can turn the physical product into a habit-forming workflow.
- System-level value is fragile when a simple workaround makes the visible hardware seem unnecessary.

## Evidence
- System composition: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] describes Juicero's value as the Press, Produce Packs, app, produce sourcing, farm relationships, and doorstep delivery working together.
- Hardware control point: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] says the connected Press can remotely disable packs during recalls and press packs consistently by flavor.
- Supply-chain data: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] says connected data helps manage a tight supply chain for raw produce with a limited lifespan.
- Habit workflow: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] says app reminders help busy customers press packs before expiration and reduce waste.
- Workaround risk: [[a-note-from-juiceros-new-ceo-juicero-medium]] responds to headlines about hacking and hand-squeezing Produce Packs by arguing that hand-squeezing misses the intended system value.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a company defense and does not prove that the system benefits outweighed cost, complexity, customer friction, or alternative solutions. It also shows that connected-system arguments can sound abstract when the public evaluates the most visible physical task directly.

## Related Concepts
- [[PublicRelationsStrategy]] - companies may need to explain system-level value when criticism focuses on a visible component.
- [[ProductMarketFit]] - customers must value the whole system enough to justify its complexity and price.
- [[CustomerLedProductDevelopment]] - testing and iteration should reveal whether users perceive the full system value.
- [[ProductCommoditization]] - connected services and data can be defended as value beyond a copyable physical product.
