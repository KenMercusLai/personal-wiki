## Definition
[[CrossDomainWisdom]] is explanatory understanding that transfers across fields because it captures WHY-level patterns rather than only domain-specific facts.

## Current Synthesis
The source presents cross-domain wisdom as the most valuable outcome of learning. In the article's DIKW framing, knowledge usually belongs to a specific domain, while some wisdom can travel across domains because it explains underlying patterns. The indirect approach strategy is offered as a principle that can appear in military history, trade conflict, investing, enterprise competition, and careers; entropy is offered as a concept spanning thermodynamics, information theory, cryptography, management, politics, history, and cosmology.

This concept extends [[CreativeAbstraction]] by emphasizing transfer. The learner is not only looking for simpler organizing principles inside one subject; they are looking for principles that can illuminate several systems without flattening their differences.

## Key Claims
- Wisdom is more valuable when it transfers beyond one domain.
- WHY-level questions help distinguish transferable understanding from local information.
- Strategic principles can move across military, business, investing, and career contexts.
- Concepts such as entropy can connect scientific, technical, organizational, and political explanations.
- Cross-domain use requires abstraction, but the source still grounds examples in concrete domains.

## Evidence
- DIKW hierarchy: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] distinguishes domain-bound knowledge from cross-domain wisdom.
- Indirect strategy: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] presents the indirect approach as a military-history-derived strategy applicable to trade wars, investing, enterprise competition, and careers.
- Entropy examples: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] lists entropy in thermodynamics, information theory, cryptography, management, politics, history, and cosmology.
- WHY-level test: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] says wisdom corresponds to WHY questions and real understanding.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source celebrates cross-domain transfer but does not provide a guardrail for false analogy. A principle that travels well still needs domain-specific constraints; otherwise abstraction can become superficial pattern matching.
