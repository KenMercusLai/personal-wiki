## Definition
[[SaaSLandAndExpand]] is an enterprise-software growth motion in which a vendor wins an initial account footprint and then grows revenue by extending adoption, usage, products, seats, or organizational coverage within that customer.

## Current Synthesis
The AppDynamics case presents expansion as both a go-to-market mechanism and a signal of subscription quality. The source reports that all customers to date had purchased an average of five times their initial land purchase and that the top 25 averaged fourteen times. Read cautiously, these figures suggest that initial contracts served as footholds for substantially larger relationships and helped support the company's public-market narrative.

The source does not define the time window, cohort construction, revenue denominator, churn treatment, product mix, or distribution beneath those averages. Expansion multiples therefore describe the investor's claimed outcome but cannot establish net revenue retention, customer profitability, causal sales practice, or future durability.

## Key Claims
- A small initial enterprise purchase can reduce adoption friction and create a foothold for later account growth.
- Expansion multiples can reveal depth of account growth that raw customer counts miss.
- Large-account expansion can materially exceed the portfolio average.
- Expansion should be evaluated alongside retention, contraction, churn, acquisition cost, and service cost rather than treated as sufficient evidence of business quality.

## Evidence
- Portfolio expansion: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] reports average purchases across all customers reaching five times the initial land purchase.
- Large-account depth: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] reports an average fourteen-times multiple for the top 25 customers.
- Strategic role: [[from-campus-drive-to-cisco-our-journey-with-appdynamics]] places these metrics alongside subscription growth, lifetime-value focus, Gartner positioning, and IPO demand.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is one company-reported snapshot relayed by an investor during a transaction celebration. Averages may be skewed by a small number of large customers, and selecting the top 25 mechanically favors high expansion. Without cohort dates, starting contract values, churned accounts, contraction, margins, acquisition and support costs, or independently audited definitions, the figures are not a complete retention or unit-economics measure.

## What Changed
- Established land-and-expand as a distinct enterprise SaaS account-growth concept.
- Added AppDynamics' attributed five-times and fourteen-times purchase multiples as a bounded example.
