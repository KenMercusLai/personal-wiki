## Definition
[[KnowledgeValuationNetwork]] is the mental framework that assigns value to knowledge and makes some learning feel meaningful, desirable, or worth pursuing.

## Current Synthesis
The wiki now treats knowledge valuation at two levels. At the educational level, learners need a way to value knowledge before they can reliably enjoy learning. Goals can act as organizing centers in that valuation network, helping otherwise scattered facts connect to dreams, role models, missions, or big questions. This is why learners benefit from early exposure to inspiring people, causes, and general ideas, especially in fields where required details can feel arbitrary or constantly changing.

At the existential level, [[PiotrWozniak]] argues that ordinary value judgments form chains of instrumental value: one thing matters because it helps produce another valued thing. Since current science does not establish a final intrinsic value, Wozniak treats knowledge growth and reality modeling as the rational response to uncertainty. In that sense, valuation is not only a motivational tool for learning but also the open problem that learning is trying to resolve.

## Key Claims
- Knowledge is difficult to love when the learner has no way to value it.
- Goals can serve as centers around which knowledge receives value.
- Role models, missions, and emotionally salient cases can help build a valuation network.
- A strong valuation network can make study more enjoyable and efficient.
- Detail-heavy domains need valuation around general principles rather than isolated facts.
- Ordinary value judgments often derive from later outcomes rather than intrinsic worth.
- Continued knowledge growth is framed as the only defensible response when ultimate value remains scientifically uncertain.

## Evidence
- Valuation need: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] says that without goals it is hard to value knowledge, and without valuation it is hard to develop learning pleasure.
- Goal center: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] describes goals as valuation centers that can support rich mental connections.
- Emotional seeds: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] suggests inspiring practitioners, missions, and dramatic cases as ways to awaken passion.
- Enjoyment and efficiency: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] says interest seeds can make required learning more pleasant and effective.
- General principles: [[she-li-mu-biao-ke-yi-gai-bian-ni-de-sheng-huo]] argues that law students should seek big-picture theories and golden rules instead of memorizing all details.
- Instrumental chains: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] models ordinary value as derived from the probability of reaching some further valued outcome.
- Ultimate uncertainty: [[nei-zai-jia-zhi-zhuang-tai]] says science cannot currently determine intrinsic value, so inquiry into reality becomes the rational shared purpose.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The concept remains theoretical. The educational source does not provide independent empirical tests for the network metaphor, and the intrinsically valuable state source does not prove that objective intrinsic value exists. The wiki therefore treats knowledge valuation as Wozniak's motivational and philosophical framework, not as a settled scientific model.

## What Changed
- Broadened the concept from educational motivation to include Wozniak's larger question of whether any objective intrinsic value grounds all value chains.
- Added the qualification that the framework remains theoretical and does not establish intrinsic value.
