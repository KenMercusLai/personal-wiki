## Definition
[[CapTableDilution]] is the reduction in an existing shareholder's percentage ownership when a startup issues additional shares or converts investor instruments into equity.

## Current Synthesis
The Crunchbase case shows dilution as both arithmetic and governance. When seed instruments convert and Series A investors buy newly issued shares, the founders and employee pool own a smaller percentage of the company. That percentage decline is not automatically an economic loss if the company's total value has grown, but it can still matter for control because voting power often follows share ownership or share class.

## Key Claims
- Dilution happens when new shares are issued or future-equity instruments convert into shares.
- Founder and employee-pool ownership can fall even when the nominal value of their holdings rises.
- Discounts and valuation caps increase dilution by allowing early investors to receive more shares for the same cash.
- Post-money valuation math can be distorted when conversion terms create shares at prices below the new round's share price.
- Dilution affects control as well as economics because voting power can depend on share count and share class.

## Evidence
- New-share creation: [[cap-tables-share-structures-valuations-oh-my-a-case-study-of-early-stage-funding-crunchbase-news]] says valuation caps and discounts let investors buy more shares than they would at the regular Series A price.
- Founder percentage decline: [[cap-tables-share-structures-valuations-oh-my-a-case-study-of-early-stage-funding-crunchbase-news]] notes that Jack, Jill, and the employee pool own less of the company after Series A.
- Economic qualification: [[cap-tables-share-structures-valuations-oh-my-a-case-study-of-early-stage-funding-crunchbase-news]] says a shrinking slice can still be valuable if the company value grows.
- Post-money effect: [[cap-tables-share-structures-valuations-oh-my-a-case-study-of-early-stage-funding-crunchbase-news]] says the actual post-money valuation is about $28.875 million, not the simple $22 million implied by adding Series A cash to pre-money valuation.
- Control effect: [[cap-tables-share-structures-valuations-oh-my-a-case-study-of-early-stage-funding-crunchbase-news]] warns that dilution can leave founders and early investors outnumbered in key votes.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source describes a clean upward round. It explicitly notes that down rounds change the economics by both diluting shareholders and lowering stake value, but it does not analyze that scenario. The concept also needs future sources to cover liquidation preferences, option-pool expansions, pay-to-play terms, and weighted-average or full-ratchet anti-dilution provisions.
