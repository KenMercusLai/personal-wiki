## Definition
[[SociallyScriptedSuccess]] is the substitution of externally ranked milestones and status coordinates for one's own judgment about what kind of life is worth living.

## Current Synthesis
The source uses Aya in 《东京女子图鉴》 as the contrast to Harry Haller's inward overanalysis. Neighborhoods, work, partners, salary, housing, brands, and age-linked expectations become legible coordinates that always specify a next move. Such scripts can supply direction and ambition, but they make present life hard to experience directly because each achievement becomes ordinary beside the next ranked destination.

The central failure is not goal pursuit. It is depositing all permission for contentment at a future threshold and assuming that social specificity proves personal relevance. The corrective is not passivity but periodic re-ownership of the coordinate system: is the destination actually wanted, and can the journey contain life before arrival?

## Key Claims
- Social scripts reduce uncertainty by translating a good life into visible, comparable milestones.
- Externally supplied coordinates can motivate sustained movement while weakening direct perception of present value.
- Achievement adapts into ordinary life, so a rank-based system continually generates another destination.
- Deferring all happiness until arrival can turn a life into prolonged transit.
- The relevant distinction is not goals versus no goals, but owned direction versus automatic obedience to a social ranking.

## Evidence
### External coordinates
- [[ru-chang]] interprets Aya's movement through Tokyo neighborhoods, jobs, partners, and lifestyles as a conversion of geography and status into a life score.
- [[ru-chang]] contrasts Aya's outward question—what do others consider better?—with Harry's inward demand for a sufficiently meaningful or elevated life.

### Perpetual deferral
- [[ru-chang]] argues that a better home, income, body, or relationship often becomes ordinary after arrival, allowing a new horizon to replace it.
- [[ru-chang]] distinguishes worthwhile anticipation from postponing all of life until the next threshold.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source analyzes a fictional character and does not show how common or causally harmful this pattern is. Income, housing, health, safety, education, and partnership can materially improve a life; external standards can coordinate expectations, reveal options, or protect people from accepting preventable deprivation. Social influence is also unavoidable and not inherently inauthentic. The risk arises when comparative rankings become the exclusive measure and remain unexamined despite their costs.

## What Changed
- Created a concept for externally ranked life coordinates and milestone-based deferral.
- Distinguished goal pursuit from surrendering ownership of the goal system.
- Preserved the material value of many socially visible achievements.
