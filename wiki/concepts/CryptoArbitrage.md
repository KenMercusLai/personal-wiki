## Definition
[[CryptoArbitrage]] is the practice of earning cryptocurrency-market yield from price, rate, lending, or structural imbalances while trying to minimize directional exposure to token prices.

## Current Synthesis
The Taresky source frames crypto arbitrage as a risk-averse response to a speculative, leveraged, often zero-sum market. The profits are not presented as free money; they come from borrowers and leveraged traders who accept high costs to speculate. In this view, exchange lending and funding-rate arbitrage are beginner-accessible examples, while professional teams reduce risk through automation, multiple strategies, better margin systems, and faster information.

## Key Claims
- Crypto arbitrage yield is funded by other market participants' borrowing and leveraged speculation.
- "Risk-free" is a narrow position-exposure claim, not a promise that exchanges, stablecoins, APIs, or behavior cannot fail.
- More arbitrage participation can reduce trader friction by improving liquidity and narrowing excessive costs.
- Beginner-accessible arbitrage includes exchange lending and funding-rate collection, but deeper edge requires substantial operational work.
- Behavioral discipline is part of the strategy because chasing bull-market riches can turn the arbitrageur into the losing speculator.

## Evidence
- Profit source: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] says crypto-market profit comes from leveraged gamblers and borrowers whose costs become arbitrage yield.
- Risk boundary: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] excludes exchange failure and stablecoin trust failure from its "risk-free" label and later lists liquidation, spread, and execution risks.
- Market function: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] argues that arbitrage competition can lower trader friction and improve liquidity.
- Strategy ladder: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] covers exchange lending and funding-rate arbitrage as entry-level strategies while saying on-chain arbitrage is more specialized.
- Discipline warning: [[blog-taresky-wu-feng-xian-nian-hua-360-xiao-bai-crypto-tao-li]] warns readers that if they cannot resist gambling, they may become the counterparty being arbitraged.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is a practitioner explainer from a bull-market moment rather than a backtested performance study. It does not quantify counterparty risk, exchange legal risk, stablecoin redemption risk, tax treatment, liquidity gaps, or strategy capacity. The high yields shown in screenshots are explicitly time-sensitive and can disappear as positions close or arbitrage capital arrives.
