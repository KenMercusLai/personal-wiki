## Definition
[[LearningMediaChoice]] is the practice of choosing source formats and media based on whether they can support the breadth, depth, review, and structure a learning goal requires.

## Current Synthesis
The source treats medium form as a learning variable, not a neutral container. Borrowing the "medium is the message" idea, it argues that harder domains make media choice more consequential: encyclopedic sites may give breadth without depth, Q&A may solve local problems without a system, SNS fragments attention, video can teach well but remains linear and hard to review, while books best support systematic learning.

The article also separates reading material into popular, introductory, and specialized works. Popular works lower the barrier for outsiders, introductory works provide broad domain entry, and specialized works or papers pursue depth inside a branch. The learner should select high-quality material, compare different schools or authors, and use style differences as comprehension aids.

## Key Claims
- Medium form shapes learning quality, especially in complex domains.
- Fragmented social feeds are poorly suited to systematic study.
- Q&A and encyclopedic sites can help locally but do not by themselves provide broad and deep learning.
- Video can teach difficult content but is less convenient for nonlinear review than text.
- Books are presented as the strongest default medium for systematic learning.
- Popular, introductory, and specialized works serve different learning stages and should not be confused.

## Evidence
- Medium importance: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] argues that the more complex the domain, the more important media form becomes.
- Media comparison: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] contrasts encyclopedias, Q&A, SNS, video, and books by breadth, depth, fragmentation, and reviewability.
- Reading types: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] distinguishes popular, introductory, and specialized works by audience, prerequisite knowledge, breadth, and depth.
- Book selection: [[bian-cheng-sui-xiang-ru-he-xi-tong-xing-xue-xi]] recommends quality filtering, diversity across schools, and attention to writing style.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The source is deliberately opinionated against SNS and strongly pro-book. It does not deny that video, Q&A, or encyclopedias can help; the qualification is that local answers and fragmented exposure are not the same as a systematic learning path.
