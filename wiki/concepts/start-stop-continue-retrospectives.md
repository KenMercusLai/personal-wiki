Start Stop Continue retrospectives are a lightweight structure for turning team reflection into process adjustment. The source recommends using the format after product work so the team can discuss what went well and what could improve without getting lost in incidental detail.

In the described format, team members write separate notes for what they want the team to start doing, stop doing, and continue doing. Grouping the notes on a board helps reveal shared patterns while preserving how each person experienced the same cycle.

The source also recommends a neutral facilitator when possible. That role helps the retrospective stay focused on learning rather than on defending the choices made during the quarter. Running the exercise every few weeks gives the team a regular chance to refine its process.
