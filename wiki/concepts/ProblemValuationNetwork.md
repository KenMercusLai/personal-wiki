## Definition
[[ProblemValuationNetwork]] is [[PiotrWozniak]]'s proposed system for estimating whether a problem is worth attempting by balancing expected reward against difficulty, effort, failure probability, fear, injury, and other costs.

## Current Synthesis
The network is presented as an adaptive exploration controller rather than a one-time calculation. It can drive approach toward a valuable objective, reassess after partial exposure, permit retreat and consolidation, refine skills, and eventually choose either a better attempt or abandonment. Its target is not maximum difficulty but maximum expected value.

The retained chart in [[liang-xing-ya-li-de-ji-zhi]] illustrates a Goldilocks mechanism: task reward rises with difficulty while the probability of solving the task falls, so their combination peaks in a moderate range. Autonomy matters because overriding the network's retreat and reassessment signals can turn calibrated fear into [[ChronicStress]] and impair later threat appraisal.

## Key Claims
- Problems are valued through expected reward net of effort, risk, fear, and failure probability.
- Optimal challenge is usually intermediate rather than trivial or maximal.
- Valuation is iterative and can support approach, retreat, learning, retry, or abandonment.
- Fear supplies information about danger but becomes harmful when disproportionate or uncontrollable.
- Self-directed exploration is proposed to calibrate threat appraisal and build resilience.

## Evidence
- Tradeoff model: [[liang-xing-ya-li-de-ji-zhi]] says harder tasks offer more reward but lower success probability and higher execution costs.
- Chart evidence: [[liang-xing-ya-li-de-ji-zhi]] shows expected reward peaking near moderate difficulty rather than at either endpoint.
- Iterative exploration: [[liang-xing-ya-li-de-ji-zhi]] describes repeated river-entry attempts that update risk estimates and skills.
- Developmental claim: [[liang-xing-ya-li-de-ji-zhi]] argues that excessive externally imposed stress can disrupt development of balanced threat appraisal.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
This is a source-specific conceptual model, not a validated neural network specification or measurement instrument. The chart is illustrative: its curve shapes, units, and optimum are not empirical estimates, and real-world reward, risk, probability, and developmental effects may not combine in the plotted form.
