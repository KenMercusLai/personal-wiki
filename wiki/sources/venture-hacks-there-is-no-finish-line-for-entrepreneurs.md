## Summary
[[VentureHacks]] defines [[EntrepreneurialQualityAndScale]] as the continuing obligation to improve both how far a product advances each customer and how many people it reaches. The essay rejects quality without distribution and reach without customer advancement as incomplete forms of entrepreneurship, arguing that innovation is required when the two aims appear to conflict. This is a concise normative definition supported by restaurant examples rather than comparative evidence about entrepreneurs or business outcomes.

## Key Claims
- An entrepreneur has no finish line: any feasible improvement in a product's quality or accessibility remains work to do.
- Quality is how far a product advances the customer, while scale is how many people use it.
- [[EntrepreneurialQualityAndScale]] requires quality and scale together rather than treating them as substitutes.
- Quality without scale fails to reach enough people to count as entrepreneurship under the essay's definition.
- Scale without quality is ordinary business and leaves a company exposed to competitors that serve customers better.
- Apparent conflict between quality and scale is a prompt for innovation rather than a reason to abandon either objective.

## Key Quotes
> "There is no finish line." - on continuous improvement in quality and accessibility.

> "The job is to do both—not one or the other." - on combining customer advancement with reach.

## Connections
- [[VentureHacks]] - publication and attributed author of the essay's definition.
- [[EntrepreneurialQualityAndScale]] - the essay's central framework for combining customer advancement and reach.
- [[DifferentiationStrategy]] - better customer outcomes are presented as protection against competitors when scale alone is insufficient.
- [[DoingThingsThatDoNotScale]] - creates a useful stage qualification: high-touch early work may be entrepreneurial when it is a route to learning and later reach rather than the intended endpoint.
- [[StartupScaling]] - distinguishes product reach from the organizational systems needed to sustain a larger company.

## Contradictions
- The essay's categorical definition excludes high-quality local firms and large businesses that do not advance customer outcomes, creating tension with broader definitions of entrepreneurship based on ownership, risk-taking, innovation, or new-venture formation.
- Its rejection of quality without scale is only a partial tension with [[DoingThingsThatDoNotScale]], which treats unscalable early work as a temporary learning method for a business that may later scale.
