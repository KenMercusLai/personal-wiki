## Summary
[[Wulc]] summarizes the method-oriented second half of 《认知红利》 as a toolkit for emotional self-regulation, knowledge use, focused work, problem analysis, decision-making, planning, and innovation. The note's strongest through-line is that thinking improves when a person externalizes it, defines the gap between present and desired states, organizes evidence and causal relationships, and chooses between direct plans and adaptive evolution. Its named frameworks are practical prompts rather than independently validated psychological or management theories.

## Key Claims
- Negative emotion is framed as a signal of conflict between beliefs and circumstances or insufficient problem-solving capacity; the proposed short-term sequence is awareness, interpretation, and conversion into an alternative response.
- [[KnowledgeIntegration]] requires distinguishing information from tested knowledge and isolated experience from repeatable regularity, then capturing, classifying, structuring, linking, retrieving, and recombining what remains.
- Focus improves efficiency by reducing repeated context reconstruction; the source recommends removing internal and environmental interference while protecting short breaks, meditation periods, and sleep.
- [[StructuredProblemSolving]] begins by defining the gap between a desired state and the current state, then calibrates the goal, changes the method that produced the present outcome, and examines relevant variables.
- Causal, structured, and [[SystemsThinking]] are presented as complementary lenses: deduction, induction, and analogy form links; pyramid and MECE structures organize parts; feedback loops expose dynamic behavior.
- Deliberate choice uses metacognition, written comparison criteria, quantification, bias awareness, and reframing toward the underlying objective rather than accepting the initial option set.
- Planning method should match difficulty, from GTD-style task control through goal decomposition to resource-intensive coordinated plans, while evolution trades some efficiency for adaptability through variation and selection.
- Innovation must combine novelty, added value, and feasibility; it can emerge through recombining diverse connected elements or systematically modifying product components.

## Key Quotes
> "问题是期望值与现状的落差" - on defining a problem as a gap rather than as a vague complaint.

> "增加背景知识量、提高链接强度、增强知识结构性、提高梳理能力" - on four proposed levers for improving thought quality.

> "计划高效但抗风险差；演化抗风险强但耗时长" - on the central planning-versus-adaptation tradeoff.

## Contradictions
- This is a selective secondary reading note, not direct empirical support for the book. Claims that negative words stop the brain, that negative emotion has only two roots, or that fixed rest durations and seven hours of sleep suit everyone are overgeneralized and should be treated as prompts rather than established findings.
- Replacing negative language can make an action path easier to see, but it can also suppress accurate descriptions of constraints, loss, risk, or injustice. Emotional regulation does not require treating every negative emotion as a defect to eliminate.
- The problem, decision, planning, systems, and innovation frameworks simplify domains where goals may be contested, variables unobservable, causal evidence weak, options incommensurable, or outcomes path-dependent. Quantification can expose assumptions without making subjective judgments objective.
- The source's distinction between knowledge and information risks making verification look binary; confidence, source quality, domain transfer, and revision remain continuous and context-dependent.
