## Summary
[[JeffDunn]] responds to public criticism of [[Juicero]] after headlines about hacking and hand-squeezing its Produce Packs. He argues that the company's value is not merely extracting juice, but the connected system of Press, Produce Packs, app reminders, farm sourcing, logistics, food-safety controls, and convenience. The note functions as [[PublicRelationsStrategy]] under pressure: it reframes the critique around mission, system-level value, transparency, and a 30-day refund offer.

## Key Claims
- Juicero's mission was to make fresh, raw fruits and vegetables easier and more enjoyable to consume, framed against nutrition and obesity challenges.
- The Press was presented as critical to [[ConnectedProductSystems]] because it enabled recall-based remote disabling, calibrated pressing, and supply-chain data for perishable produce.
- Hand-squeezing Produce Packs was said to miss the value of the full system and produce a messier, less repeatable experience.
- Juicero's defended value proposition combined hardware, chopped organic produce packs, app reminders, direct farm sourcing, doorstep delivery, and habit formation.
- Dunn responded to criticism by promising listening, transparency, and a 30-day Happiness Guarantee refund for customers who felt Juicero was not making healthy habits easier.

## Key Quotes
> "The sum of the system - the Press, Produce Packs and App - working together is what enables a great experience." - on Juicero's system-level value claim.

> "That means that if you send us your Press, we'll refund the money you paid for it." - on the 30-day refund response.
