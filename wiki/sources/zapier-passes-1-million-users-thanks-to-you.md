## Summary
[[Zapier]] marks one million registered users and presents its growth as a product of making cross-application automation accessible to non-programmers. The company says customer requests drove its expansion from 34 to more than 650 app integrations and from one-to-one connections to multi-step workflows, while four million configured Zaps and more than one million daily tasks illustrate the breadth of adoption. The retrospective also connects [[NoCodeWorkflowAutomation]], [[CustomerLedProductDevelopment]], and [[RemoteWork]] to a capital-light company that began as a nights-and-weekends project and grew to more than 40 people across eight time zones.

## Key Claims
- Zapier began in late 2011 as a side project by three recent graduates in Columbia, Missouri, without venture funding; its first version integrated 34 apps.
- Customer demand for more integrations motivated a developer platform through which services could connect themselves, helping the catalog exceed 650 web apps by May 2016.
- Requests for workflows beyond one-to-one integration led to Multi-Step Zaps, while later dynamic-search and Formatter features continued the customer-guided expansion.
- The source reports more than one million registered users, four million configured Zaps, and more than one million automated tasks triggered each day.
- Surveyed users estimated that a Zap saved more than three minutes each time it ran, but the article supplies no sampling, response, or calculation method.
- [[NoCodeWorkflowAutomation]] let users connect specialized applications, manipulate data, build bots, maintain lightweight databases, and automate operational work without writing code.
- Zapier's team of more than 40 people worked fully remotely across eight time zones, which the company presents as part of a sustainable, scalable operating model.

- Registered users, configured Zaps, daily tasks, and estimated minutes saved measure different things; the article does not report active-user retention, task distribution, survey design, or independently audited results.
