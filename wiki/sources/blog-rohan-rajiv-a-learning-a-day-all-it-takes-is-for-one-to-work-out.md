## Summary
[[RohanRajiv]] reflects on a difficult graduate-school application season after an earlier failed attempt and a profile he felt needed explaining. A friend's repeated line, "All it takes is for one to work out," became a grounding maxim for high-stakes searches where the outcome depends on one right fit rather than universal approval. The source contains no effective image references.

## Key Claims
- [[OneFitSearchMindset]] can reduce the emotional burden of competitive selection processes by shifting attention from broad rejection to the one acceptance that matters.
- High-stakes searches such as school admissions, job searches, home buying, and finding a partner can feel personally brutal even when the real requirement is not unanimous validation.
- Past failure and imperfect credentials do not settle the outcome of a renewed attempt when the search only needs one compatible opening.
- A simple repeated maxim can become emotionally useful before it is fully believed.

## Key Quotes
> "All it takes is for one to work out." - the friend's recurring maxim.

> "And that one is all you need." - on the sufficiency of one right outcome.
