## Summary
[[CYY]] reflects on a career formed by opening technical black boxes, from early computing and robotics through OI, ICPC, CPU/OS teaching, open-source work, XiangShan, and Intel. The essay argues that modern AI coding and agent systems such as [[GitHubCopilot]], [[Claude]], [[OpenAI]] models, and [[OpenClaw]] now make it possible to offload not only boilerplate but debugging, performance analysis, RTL waveform inspection, and feature implementation. That speed creates a learning problem for hobbyist engineers: if AI completes the work while humans only steer outcomes, they may lose the experience that once generated judgment, connections, and research ideas. The proposed response is to live with the shift while deliberately preserving human-centered problem discovery, subjective taste, slow reflection, and fast action on personally felt opportunities.

## Key Claims
- [[BlackBoxLearning]] can be the core value of engineering work for hobbyists because debugging, tracing, waveform inspection, and architecture exploration teach more than task completion alone.
- [[AICodingPractice]] has shifted from simple offload and API hinting toward agentic delegation where AI can implement features, run tests, inspect CPU performance counters, and analyze RTL waveforms with little human rest time.
- [[JuniorEngineerLearning]] and hobbyist learning may suffer when goal-oriented AI use removes the intermediate struggle that builds domain intuition and creative recombination.
- AI commoditizes once-rare technical knowledge such as CPU microarchitecture, NoC, and PCI-E expertise, weakening knowledge scarcity as a career bargaining advantage.
- The best adaptation is not rejection of AI but deliberate use of freed time to explore unfamiliar directions, attend to subjective human needs, and act quickly on product, science, art, or open-source improvement ideas.

## Key Quotes
> "每一次的经历不仅仅是工作本身，更是打开了我们所想要探索的黑盒子的过程" - on why manual technical work matters to hobbyist learning.

> "只是我在这个事情中什么都没有学到" - on the cost of offloading hard performance analysis to AI.

> "更加关注自己主观感受中不足的细节" - on the author's proposed self-rescue strategy.
