## Summary
[[PiotrWozniak]] argues that an [[IntrinsicallyValuableState]] would be a non-instrumental state of value, functioning as a "meta-goal" behind all other goals. Because science cannot currently prove whether intrinsic value exists, the article says the most rational human purpose is to grow knowledge and model reality in order to discover or refute it. The essay extends Wozniak's learning-motivation ideas into a broader philosophy of purpose, anxiety reduction, and shared human orientation.

## Key Claims
- [[IntrinsicallyValuableState]] names a state whose value is not derived from external goals, rewards, religion, pleasure, or arbitrary preference.
- Most practical value judgments are instrumental: an action matters because it increases the probability of some later valued outcome.
- Science does not currently establish the existence or content of intrinsic value, but the possibility remains nonzero because knowledge and scientific models can change.
- The article's proposed rational response is to grow knowledge and model reality while seeking to discover or refute objective intrinsic value.
- Treating the search for intrinsic value as a shared purpose can reduce self-centered anxiety and redirect attention away from immortality, status, or narrow self-interest.
- For the individual, a clear meta-goal can simplify life, support productivity, and remove some existential uncertainty.

## Key Quotes
> "内在价值状态是目标的元目标。" - on the concept's role above ordinary goals.

> "今天，人类存在的最终目的，是为了搞懂人类存在的目的。" - on inquiry into purpose as the current purpose.

> "如今，人类存在的唯一受科学认证的目的，是通过科学探索来探究现实。" - on scientific exploration as the defensible shared aim.
