## Summary
[[MaiYang]] uses a [[NavalRavikant]]-framed exhortation to argue that people should respond to AI by acting, building, and learning through concrete work rather than consuming success-method content. The article contrasts passive "success pornography" with overwhelming desire, using [[ElonMusk]], [[MarkZuckerberg]], [[SteveJobs]], and [[JeffBezos]] as examples of founders who learned and committed through action. The embedded poster repeats the article's text and notes that the summary was produced by ChatGPT, but it does not add separate evidence beyond the prose.

## Key Claims
- [[ActionBiasInAI]] treats doing as the decisive response to AI: real learning comes from building, shipping, and confronting actual problems.
- Passive consumption of success formulas can feel productive while substituting short-term stimulation for creation.
- Serious creative or entrepreneurial drive is tested by whether a problem or desired future creates sustained, sleep-disrupting urgency.
- Founder examples are used to distinguish internally owned desire from hesitation or [[MimeticDesire]].
- AI-era ambition should be grounded in self-honesty about what one truly wants to make or pursue.

## Key Quotes
> "对待 AI 你得行动起来！" - the source's central imperative.

> "你需要的不是旁观者的指导，而是行动者的决心。" - on action rather than spectatorship.
