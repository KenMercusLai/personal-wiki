## Summary
[[JoelGascoigne]] reports [[Buffer]]'s August 2015 operating update as the company neared $7.5M ARR, grew toward 50 employees, and reorganized product and engineering into smaller autonomous teams. The post uses open startup metrics to connect revenue, usage, cash, hiring, support load, product launches, and organization design rather than treating growth as a single headline number. The remote hero image is a decorative rock-in-water photograph and adds no material evidence beyond the text.

## Key Claims
- Buffer reported 2,673,418 total registered users, 211,520 monthly active users, 50,489 average daily active users, $624,196 MRR, $7.49M ARR, $2,789,388 cash in bank, and 42 team members in 33 cities for August 2015.
- Team growth was accelerating: Buffer had 14 open roles, received about 1,500 applications in August, added five team members during August, and expected eight more people to start in September.
- Product and engineering had been split into smaller 4-6 person teams so they could work more autonomously and move faster.
- Buffer wanted each product person focused on a single area, because cross-team single-person roles were still slowing the new structure.
- Buffer for Video launched to a strong community reception, the optimal scheduling tool had validated product-market fit, and a new version of Pablo was close to launch.
- Customer-support organization design was also becoming more segmented, with Happiness Heroes grouped around paying customers, free-plan customers, mobile contacts, and emerging Enterprise customer success.
