## Summary
90千帕 offers three categorical relationship rules: leave harmful people rather than trying to reform them, work and form friendships with people who have already begun acting rather than trying to awaken the unwilling, and never volunteer help. Read narrowly, the post supports [[InterpersonalBoundarySetting]] and action-aware [[DeliberateNetworkBuilding]] by protecting attention from coercive or persistently one-sided relationships. Its absolute language supplies no context, behavioral test, or evidence, however, and its prohibition on proactive help conflicts with sources that treat freely offered support, mentorship, and long-horizon reciprocity as important forms of relationship building.

## Key Claims
- When another person's conduct is harmful, withdrawal may preserve more agency than attempting to change that person's character.
- Friendship and joint work should favor people who already show willingness through action rather than people who require another person to manufacture their motivation.
- Unsolicited help should not be offered, according to the post's strongest and least qualified rule.
- The three rules function as attention and relationship filters, but the post does not define “bad,” “started,” “pretending to sleep,” “help,” or the circumstances in which exceptions apply.

## Key Quotes
> “要学会远离坏人，而不是改造坏人” — on withdrawal rather than reform.

> “要去与启动了的人做朋友做事业” — on selecting friends and collaborators who are already acting.

> “绝不主动提供帮助” — the post's absolute rule against volunteering help.

## Connections
- [[InterpersonalBoundarySetting]] - withdrawal and withholding unwanted involvement are presented as ways to govern one's own participation.
- [[DeliberateNetworkBuilding]] - demonstrated initiative becomes a proposed selection signal for friends and collaborators.
- [[TransformationalRelationships]] - supplies a reciprocal relationship model that can justify limits without ruling out voluntary contribution.
- [[ProfessionalRelationshipCompounding]] - directly qualifies the post's ban on proactive help through a contrasting case for nontransactional, long-horizon assistance.

## Contradictions
- The rule against proactively helping conflicts with [[ProfessionalRelationshipCompounding]], which treats useful help without an immediate return as one way repeated relationships can develop trust and reciprocity.
- The post's advice to avoid “bad people” is compatible with disengagement from repeated harmful conduct, but [[InterpersonalBoundarySetting]] rejects the need to infer a fixed identity or motive before limiting access.
- These are four short assertions from one social-media post. No examples, outcome evidence, power analysis, cultural context, or exceptions are supplied, so the advice should not be generalized to emergencies, caregiving, teaching, mentorship, mutual aid, professional duty, or low-cost offers that preserve the recipient's choice.
