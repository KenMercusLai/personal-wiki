## Summary
Annie Ridout interviews [[AriannaHuffington]] about sleep, morning routines, work culture, parenting, and [[HuffingtonPost]] after the sale to AOL. Huffington frames sleep as a serious performance input rather than an indulgence, connects her view to personal exhaustion and [[TheSleepRevolution]], and describes workplace practices such as nap rooms, after-hours email expectations, and vacation email deletion.

## Key Claims
- [[SleepAsPerformanceEnhancer]] is the interview's central claim: Huffington says adequate sleep supports decision making, emotional intelligence, cognitive function, creativity, and daily joy.
- [[MorningRecoveryRoutine]] combines no devices, gratitude or intention-setting, exercise, meditation, showering, dressing, coffee, and natural waking when sleep is sufficient.
- [[BurnoutPrevention]] at [[HuffingtonPost]] included nap rooms, explicit permission not to answer email after hours or on vacation, and a tool that deletes vacation email to remove temptation.
- New parents need practical support from partners, family, siblings, or friends so they can nap or get uninterrupted sleep.
- Huffington treats outfit repetition and comfortable footwear as energy conservation, especially for women facing social pressure to vary clothing.
- [[HuffingtonPost]] is described as a mission-led media company aiming to inform, inspire, entertain, and empower across 15 country editions.

## Key Quotes
> "sleep is the ultimate performance enhancer" - on reframing sleep as a performance input.

> "95 percent of the time I get eight hours of sleep a night" - on natural waking after sufficient sleep.

> "all emails sent to you during your time off will be automatically deleted" - on the vacation email tool.
