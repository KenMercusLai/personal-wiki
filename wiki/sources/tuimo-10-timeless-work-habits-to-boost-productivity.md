## Summary
Bernard Marr's article, translated by 创之 for 褪墨, presents ten durable work habits for improving productivity without building a large formal system. It emphasizes defining daily priorities, protecting attention, simplifying inputs and outputs, batching similar work, delegating or deleting low-value tasks, and handling aversive tasks early.

## Key Claims
- [[PersonalProductivity]] improves when people identify their most important daily tasks before starting work.
- [[AttentionManagement]] depends on single-tasking, simplified information inputs, planned batching, and offline work when network distractions are costly.
- [[WorkHabits]] can be lightweight routines rather than elaborate systems, especially when tied to mornings, communication norms, and task triage.
- [[TimeManagementQuadrants]] frame non-urgent but important work as the category that most deserves protected time.

## Key Quotes
> "每天花几分钟定义好今天最重要的 3 - 5 件事情，能帮助提高你的整体效率" - on defining daily MITs.

> "每次专注于一件事情，更有助于高效地完成工作" - on avoiding multitasking.

> "提高效率并不一定需要制定多么完整、多么高大上的系统框架" - on favoring simple habits over elaborate systems.
