## Summary
This incomplete Mindset article reproduces the opening of a historical [[ElonMusk]] email about [[Tesla]] Model 3 production, precision, and profit. The available fragment reports three weeks above 2,000 Model 3 vehicles, planned factory upgrades, a move toward continuous production, and a 6,000-per-week subsystem burst-capacity target designed to leave operating margin across a coupled supply chain. The local Markdown ends at the start of the profit section and does not contain the productivity recommendations advertised by its headline.

## Key Claims
- The email reports Model 3 weekly production rising from 2,020 to 2,070 and then 2,250 vehicles across three consecutive weeks, alongside 2,000 Model S/X vehicles in the latest week.
- Tesla planned three-to-five-day factory shutdowns for upgrades intended to support 3,000–4,000 Model 3 vehicles per week the following month.
- A later upgrade was intended to enable 6,000 Model 3 vehicles per week by the end of June, with internal teams and suppliers asked to demonstrate the corresponding parts capacity through 850 sets in 24 hours.
- [[ProductionCapacityBuffer]] explains the stated choice of a 6,000-per-week burst target rather than 5,000: a complex production and logistics chain needs margin because system output is constrained by its weakest or least reliable component.
- The planned production ramp included 24/7 Fremont operation, an additional general-assembly, body, and paint shift, and roughly 400 new people per week across Fremont and Giga for several weeks.
- Musk set an aspirational goal for Model 3 build precision to become ten times better than any other car, while excluding suppliers unable or unwilling to meet the required tolerances.

## Key Quotes
> "Actual production will move as fast as the least lucky and least well-executed part" — Musk's explanation of why coupled production systems require capacity margin.

> "We are going to find a way or make a way to get there." — the memo's stance toward departments or suppliers missing the target.
