## Summary
[[Chongxi]] explains a personal blog design that combines NASA technical-document restraint, Swiss International Style, and the atmosphere of Arknights' Lone Trail event. The resulting [[ContentFirstWebDesign]] uses paper-like color, spacious typography, low-interruption article pages, and subordinate decoration to protect [[DeepReading]], while independent hosting and repository-held writing support [[DigitalContentDurability]] and personal control.

## Key Claims
- The blog's visual lineage runs from Swiss International Style through NASA's 1970s–early-1990s identity and document systems to Lone Trail's restrained science-fiction interface.
- Functional decisions such as grids, numbering, monospaced type, whitespace, and print-oriented color can produce beauty through clarity rather than decoration.
- An article page should keep the text dominant: large type, generous leading, and the absence of pop-ups, floating sharing controls, or progress indicators reduce competition for reader attention.
- Decoration can add identity without disrupting reading when it remains peripheral and optional, as with technical-report labels at page edges.
- Personally controlled domains, repositories, and exportable data reduce the risk that platform shutdowns, account bans, or algorithm changes erase a writer's record.
- Informal fragments are worth preserving outside algorithmic feeds even when they do not justify a formal article.
- A coherent personal site can remain unfinished and evolve through use while still expressing a recognizable authorial identity.

## Key Quotes
> “设计的目的是让内容被看见，而不是让设计本身被看见” — on content taking priority over visible design performance.

> “把记录放在不安全的地方是对自己的不尊重” — on treating storage and platform choice as part of respect for one's writing.

## Connections
- [[Chongxi]] — author and designer whose habits, values, and desired reading atmosphere shape the site.
- [[ContentFirstWebDesign]] — design approach that removes attention competition while retaining restrained identity cues.
- [[DeepReading]] — the blog deliberately creates a quiet, spacious environment for slow, continuous reading.
- [[DigitalContentDurability]] — repository-held writing, exportability, and an independent domain provide stronger custody than platform-only publication.
- [[AttentionEconomy]] — the informal-post stream is positioned as a record outside traffic, forwarding, and algorithmic-consumption incentives.

## Contradictions
- The article is a first-person design rationale, not a usability study; it reports no reading-completion, comprehension, accessibility, performance, or preference measurements.
- “Less is more” is presented as a lineage from Swiss International Style to NASA documentation and Lone Trail, but the source does not establish this as a documented historical chain of influence.
- Independent hosting improves control but does not by itself prevent domain expiry, link rot, repository loss, security failures, censorship, or inadequate backups.
- Removing visible controls and ancillary elements can protect focus, but progress, sharing, navigation, or accessibility features may help some readers when implemented without interruption.
