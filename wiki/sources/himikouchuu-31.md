## Summary
[[MiaoYi|缪伊]] reflects on withdrawing from Chinese social platforms and argues that recommendation systems can turn private experience, strong emotion, self-disclosure, and conflict into attention-producing commodities. Using [[Xiaohongshu]] as the main case, the essay connects the social value of sharing lived experience to [[PerformativeSelfPresentation]], [[AttentionEconomy]], [[RageBait]], and creator self-exploitation. It closes with a practical response to trolling: do not reward provocation with attention, resist compulsive self-justification, and use platform, official, or legal channels when intervention is genuinely needed.

## Key Claims
- Sharing personal experience can create recognition, narrative integration, connection, and a sense of control, so the value of social posting cannot be reduced to vanity or pathology.
- Recommendation and engagement incentives can turn lived events into raw material: creators begin selecting, polishing, or staging experience for its publishable version, until life becomes a means and the post becomes the end.
- Platforms can mistake anger, pain, voyeurism, envy, and conflict for demand because each produces measurable attention; this joins [[AttentionEconomy]] to an [[EngagementIncentiveConflict]].
- Self-disclosure becomes self-exploitation when visibility or commercial reward pressures people to expose intimate pain, relationships, or conflict for public judgment.
- [[RageBait]] deliberately provokes anger because hostile replies, quote-posts, screenshots, and arguments all extend reach, whether the provoking account is a marketer, content farm, or individual troll.
- Online hatred can intensify when criticism of a group is experienced as criticism of the self, while thin behavioral slices are treated as sufficient evidence for judging a whole person.
- Refusing to reply or retransmit provocation can interrupt its attention supply; serious threats or harms may instead require platform rules, official reporting, or legal action.

## Key Quotes
> “生活成了手段，笔记才是目的。” — on experience becoming subordinate to publishable content.

> “火焰不会因为争吵而熄灭，它只会越发熊熊燃烧。” — on why arguing with trolls can compound conflict.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction was found. The essay strengthens the wiki's incentive-based accounts of attention and performance while qualifying them with the genuine benefits of narrative integration, recognition, and connection.
- The piece is a first-person cultural essay, not a platform audit, behavioral study, clinical addiction assessment, or causal analysis of recommendation systems. Its comparisons among Xiaohongshu, Zhihu, Weibo, and Douban are impressionistic and time-bound.
- The essay accurately identifies Oxford University Press's 2025 Word of the Year as “rage bait” within the source, but the wiki treats the broader mechanism as the author's synthesis rather than independent evidence about prevalence or effect size.
- Disengagement is not a complete response to credible threats, coordinated harassment, impersonation, or illegal conduct; the essay itself preserves escalation through platform, official, and legal channels when needed.
