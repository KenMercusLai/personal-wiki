## Summary
plantegg argues that workplace learning improves when engineers treat real problems as practice cases, reconstruct an expert's reasoning, and repeatedly connect knowledge with logic until it becomes usable ability. The article adds a concrete [[WorkplaceLearning]] method: study solved incidents through command history, search terms, packet captures, experiments, and follow-up discussion, while seeking a field's big picture and key anchors. It strengthens [[ActiveLearning]] and [[JuniorEngineerLearning]] by warning that passive reading gives a false feeling of learning unless it is paired with hands-on verification and review.

## Key Claims
- [[WorkplaceLearning]] depends on converting solved work problems into reusable knowledge and reasoning patterns.
- Ability is described as knowledge plus logic: knowing facts is insufficient unless a learner can connect them to a concrete problem.
- Learners can accelerate by reconstructing how stronger colleagues solved a problem, including what they searched, tried, and inferred.
- New domains require both a macro-level map and a few key anchors; isolated facts fade when they are not connected to a structure.
- Hands-on practice creates "body feel" for abstract technical concepts, as in using Wireshark to observe TCP handshakes.
- Passive reading can create a comfort-zone illusion when learners avoid the setup, source-code reading, packet capture, or experiment work needed for real understanding.
- General diagnostic methods, such as tracing network packets, can transfer better than narrow experience with one known fix.

## Key Quotes
> "知识+逻辑 基本等于你的能力" - on ability as the connection between knowledge and reasoning.

> "所以新进入一个领域的时候要去找他的大图和抓手。" - on seeking the big picture and anchors in a new field.

> "只看不动手当然是最舒适的，但是这个最舒适给了你在学习的假象，没有结果" - on passive learning as a false comfort.
