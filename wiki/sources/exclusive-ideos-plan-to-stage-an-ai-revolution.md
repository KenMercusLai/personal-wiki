## Summary
This 2017 article presents [[IDEO]]'s acquisition of [[Datascope]] as an attempt to combine human-centered design with data science under the label [[AugmentedIntelligence]]. [[TimBrown]], [[MikeStringer]], and [[DeanMalmgren]] argue that data and algorithms are design media and that adaptive systems should learn through a continuing loop among people, data, and algorithms. The article frames that integration as a way to anticipate harmful outcomes such as [[AlgorithmicBias]], while acknowledging that human-centered design has not eliminated bias or solved every systemic problem.

## Key Claims
- IDEO acquired Datascope's 15-person data-science team after four years of collaboration, bringing the team into IDEO's Chicago office.
- The companies define augmented intelligence as technology that extends people's capabilities rather than operating as a separate replacement for them.
- Data, algorithms, and machine learning are design media because choices about data collection, model construction, objectives, and interaction shape human outcomes.
- Data science can inform design, while human-centered design can help data scientists frame people's needs and explore harmful outcomes before deployment.
- Adaptive systems should use a continuous human-in-the-loop cycle in which observations of interaction inform later algorithm design.
- Human-centered practice may improve AI in general without guaranteeing that every system will be fair, transparent, or successful.

## Key Quotes
> "we’re extending and enhancing people’s capabilities through technology" — Stringer defining augmented intelligence in contrast with replacement.

> "the constant loop of people, data, and algorithms" — Malmgren describing iterative human feedback in adaptive systems.
