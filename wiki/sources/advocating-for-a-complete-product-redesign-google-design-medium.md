## Summary
This Google Design article explains how the Crashlytics team used research, journey mapping, internal co-design sessions, and shared stakeholder context to justify a complete product redesign during the move from Fabric into Firebase. The central claim is that a visual refresh became an opportunity to repair accumulated design debt and unclear information hierarchy, but only after the team aligned on users' critical journeys and recurring pain points. Embedded images were inspected: comparison screenshots show the redesign moving crash-free statistics, event trends, issue filters, and issue tables into clearer Firebase surfaces; journey diagrams show multi-step monitoring and investigate-and-fix flows; paper-cutout photos document the internal co-design method; small screenshots and the GIF reinforce dashboard and issue-detail information hierarchy changes.

## Key Claims
- A required visual update can justify deeper [[ProductRedesign]] work when the existing product has accumulated design debt and feature discoverability problems.
- The redesign case began with user understanding: teammates, developer relations, user researchers, and users helped identify four critical [[Crashlytics]] journeys.
- Journey mapping exposed a recurring [[InvestigateAndFixFlow]] that connected monitoring, stability checks, crash prioritization, and customer debugging.
- Internal paper-cutout studies let experienced teammates redesign the dashboard independently, revealing repeated pain themes without group bias.
- The strongest redesign problem was unclear [[InformationHierarchy]]: users scrolled or clicked through less important UI to reach key crash data, and some users asked for features that already existed but were hidden.
- Team buy-in came from context-setting and participation, not just from finished design concepts.
- The inspected product images show the redesign making overview statistics, event trends, filters, issues, device/version breakdowns, session details, logs, keys, data, and stack traces more legible within Firebase's [[MaterialDesign]] surface.

## Key Quotes
> "rethink the entire user experience" - on using the Firebase migration as more than a visual port.

> "One does not simply start redesigning a product." - on why the case required research and alignment first.

> "a product redesign is a team effort" - on the need to align around why redesign is necessary.
