## Summary
[[Kakachen]] closes an eighteen-year employed engineering career and announces [[AstroVela]], a startup building the [[Vane]] product family for multimodal AI, Physical AI, and their infrastructure. The essay's broader thesis is that AI makes routine implementation faster while increasing the relative value of problem selection, systems principles, architecture, optimization, trade-offs, customer understanding, and verification. It frames infrastructure opportunity as a question of choosing the right technological wave and deployment scale, but offers a founder thesis and roadmap rather than product benchmarks, customer evidence, or proof that human judgment will remain outside AI capability.

## Key Claims
- Each major computing wave creates new infrastructure demand; the author places training, inference, reinforcement learning, agents, and Physical AI in the current wave.
- Infrastructure value depends on workload scale and opportunity selection as well as local optimization: a modest gain across a large fleet may matter more than a large gain on a tiny deployment.
- As AI handles more glue code and baseline implementation, infrastructure engineers should focus more on principles, architecture, optimization, trade-offs, customer needs, and critical low-level details.
- An infrastructure company's proposed moat is not code alone but identifying valuable problems, accumulating practice-derived knowledge, and understanding real customer demand.
- New companies may be better able than incumbents to make concentrated bets when existing products, assets, and organizational history constrain a pivot.
- [[AstroVela]] is betting on multimodal AI and Physical AI infrastructure through Vane Core, [[VaneData]], Vane RL, and Vane Agent, with Vane Data announced for open source in July 2026.
- The career decision is explicitly value-laden: the author prefers the risk of restarting to the anticipated regret of not trying.

## Key Quotes
> “对于 Infra 来说，选择往往比优化更重要。” — on selecting a consequential workload before optimizing it.

> “一家 Infra 公司真正的壁垒，不是代码本身，而是找到最有价值的问题。” — on the proposed source of durable advantage.

## Contradictions
- The essay says AI cannot replace human cognition, taste, and judgment, but also begins from uncertainty about AI's eventual capability. This is best treated as the author's operating thesis, not a demonstrated capability boundary.
- The scale example comparing a 100% gain on three machines with a 10% gain on one thousand isolates deployment scale and does not account for engineering cost, utilization, latency criticality, strategic constraints, or customer value.
- The claims about incumbents' historical burdens and startups' freedom to bet are plausible organizational hypotheses, not comparative evidence that a new company will execute better.
- The product section is an announcement. It provides no architecture detail below the four component names, release artifact, customer deployment, benchmark, reliability record, security analysis, or validation of the multimodal and Physical AI market thesis.
