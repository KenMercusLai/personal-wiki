## Summary
The saved Vanity Fair page contains only a standfirst for [[NickBilton]]'s investigation, followed by a subscription interruption and cookie text rather than the article itself. That standfirst attributes [[Theranos]]'s collapse to [[ElizabethHolmes]] overriding medical expertise about blood-testing technology it describes as discredited and building the company around [[OrganizationalSecrecy]] while hoping the technology could still be made to work. These are editorial assertions from the teaser, not a complete evidentiary account of the company's conduct or failure.

## Key Claims
- Vanity Fair describes Theranos as a once-lauded biotechnology startup whose blood-testing technology had become discredited.
- The standfirst says Holmes defied medical experts, including her own chief scientist, about whether the technology worked as claimed.
- It presents organizational secrecy as a mechanism that allowed the company to continue while Holmes hoped she could still make the technology succeed.
