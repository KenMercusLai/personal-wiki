## Summary
[[TheStillWandering]] argues that many corporate knowledge workers privately experience their jobs as performative coordination rather than tangible contribution, even while continuing to enact a professional identity in public. The essay interprets pandemic remote work as exposing some low-substance roles and describes workers who use salary, stability, skills, and spare capacity to build side projects or possible exits. This is a vivid cultural critique grounded in anonymous anecdotes, not evidence that corporate work generally lacks value or that using employer time and equipment for outside ventures is safe or permissible.

## Key Claims
- Corporate jargon, recurring meetings, decks, and coordination layers can obscure whether a role creates a concrete outcome.
- Workers may collectively sustain low-value processes because income, mortgages, status, and mutual dependence make open disbelief costly.
- The pandemic is presented as a visibility shock that revealed which activities could disappear or be completed in substantially less than a full workday.
- Some workers separate employment from identity and treat a corporate role as infrastructure for income, skill development, stability, and future options.
- Side projects can function as parallel sources of agency and meaning while an employee maintains a corporate persona.
- The author compares the corporate job's decline to loss of faith: institutions and rituals remain while belief in their meaning weakens.
- A job need not be a calling to be useful, but the essay does not establish how widespread its diagnosis is or distinguish wasteful coordination from necessary but intangible work.

## Key Quotes
> "The corporate role has become a funding mechanism for actual work." - on employment as a platform for side projects.

> "Once you see it as infrastructure rather than identity, as a resource rather than a calling, everything shifts." - on separating work from identity.

## Contradictions
- The essay's claim that many corporate roles are hollow is supported only by selected anonymous conversations and observation. It cannot distinguish unnecessary work from coordination, risk reduction, maintenance, compliance, relationship work, or delayed outcomes whose value is real but difficult to see.
- Its celebratory description of building businesses from a corporate desk conflicts with employment agreements, intellectual-property rules, confidentiality, security policy, and duties owed during paid working time in some contexts. The safer transferable claim is that stable employment can fund outside experimentation within explicit boundaries.
