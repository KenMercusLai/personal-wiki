## Summary
[[Wulc]] summarizes a Zhihu Live on how internet businesses turn free traffic into monetizable assets, especially through [[DataMonetization]] in advertising. The article and inspected diagrams present a progression from demographic contract ads to behavior-based auctions, first-party and third-party [[ProgrammaticAdvertising]], and mobile scene data, then apply the same logic to ecommerce recommendations, retargeting, and look-alike acquisition.

## Key Claims
- Free internet products can acquire traffic cheaply because marginal distribution cost is low, but their business goal is to create assets that can be monetized through later channels.
- Data usually monetizes by attaching to traffic: audience segmentation, preferences, and user state raise the value of the same impression inventory.
- Advertising data products developed from demographic contract allocation, to behavior-based bidding, to advertiser-owned and third-party data in programmatic trading, then toward mobile scene data.
- Contract advertising matches demand nodes such as demographic or geographic targets to supply nodes, while behavior data and long-tail inventory push the system toward auction allocation.
- Programmatic advertising lets ADX and DSP systems expose user/context signals so advertisers can decide whether to bid, using first-party data such as site visitors and sometimes third-party/media data.
- Ecommerce data can be used for onsite recommendation, offsite retargeting, and new-customer look-alike recommendation based on product views, searches, cart actions, sharing, and similar behavior traces.
- Mobile scene data infers user state from sensors and location patterns, such as identifying likely work location or commuter transitions, but the article treats this as less mature than earlier ad products.

## Key Quotes
> "数据依附于流量进行变现，数据提高了流量变现的价值" - on the article's central traffic-plus-data model.

> "场景是用户的状态，而非媒体的特征" - from the inspected scene-data diagram.
