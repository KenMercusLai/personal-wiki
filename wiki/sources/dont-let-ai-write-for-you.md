## Summary
[[AlexWoods]] argues that writing is not merely text production but a process for finding the right question, structuring uncertainty, increasing understanding, and demonstrating that the author has contended with the ideas. The essay therefore warns that delegating finished documents to an LLM can forfeit both [[LearningByWriting]] and reader trust, while still endorsing [[AIAssistedWriting]] for research, checking, transcription, information capture, and divergent idea generation.

## Key Claims
- Documents answer questions: product requirements ask what to build, technical specifications ask how to build it, and difficult writing can reveal that the original question itself needs revision.
- [[LearningByWriting]] develops understanding and capability because the writer must enter ambiguity, impose structure, and exercise judgment near the boundary of current ability.
- LLM-generated finished prose can obscure whether the named author personally grappled with the argument, weakening credibility to lead the work proposed by the document.
- [[AIAssistedWriting]] is safer when AI supports research, checking, transcription, capture, or brainstorming while the human performs the central reasoning and composition.
- Idea generation tolerates a low hit rate because one useful suggestion can justify discarding nine weak ones without transferring final judgment to the model.
- Faster software delivery from LLMs raises rather than removes the need for human thoughtfulness.

## Key Quotes
> "The goal of writing is not to have written." - on treating writing as a means to understanding rather than an output quota.

> "Letting an LLM write for you is like paying somebody to work out for you." - on losing the exercise that builds capability.

## Connections
- [[AlexWoods]] - author of the essay and advocate for human-owned writing.
- [[LearningByWriting]] - writing is presented as deliberate practice that produces understanding and stronger judgment.
- [[AIAssistedWriting]] - the source accepts bounded assistance but rejects outsourcing the central act of reasoning and composition.
- [[AIDependencySkillAtrophy]] - the workout analogy frames delegated writing as skipped cognitive exercise.
- [[StrategicWriting]] - PRDs and technical specifications are examples of documents that expose questions, decisions, and reasoning.
- [[KnowledgeOutput]] - the essay distinguishes genuine knowledge formation from merely producing a finished artifact.

## Contradictions
- The source qualifies pragmatic [[AIAssistedWriting]] accounts that find imperfect machine drafts useful scaffolding. It draws a stricter boundary around model-written documents because even acceptable prose may fail to show that the human author developed the underlying understanding or earned the trust implied by authorship.
- The essay is a concise practitioner argument. It gives no comparative study of reader trust, learning, writing quality, disclosure, editing intensity, or differences among genres and users, so its strongest causal claims remain reasoned cautions rather than measured effects.
