## Summary
[[Hanyang]] presents an AI-use method for humanities researchers, writers, editors, and content workers, drawing from [[FUNES]], [[ShiShuFeng]], and production work around writing and cultural material. The article argues that effective [[AIWorkflowDesign]] is not magic prompting but a traceable, controllable, verifiable process where AI acts as a workbench, intern, or production station rather than an oracle. Its core standard is that the human should still be willing to sign the final work, because real quality comes from materials, taste, workflow design, and accountable supervision.

## Key Claims
- [[AIWorkflowDesign]] should make AI work traceable, supervisable, verifiable, and controllable enough that the human user can still sign the output.
- AI requests should be converted from vague wishes into explicit tasks with audience, purpose, background, structure, tone, constraints, and judgment standards.
- Users should treat models like diligent interns with limited implicit context and uneven strengths, comparing multiple systems and assigning them different roles instead of treating one model as an oracle.
- Reliable AI-assisted production depends on decomposing work into repeatable pipelines, then giving AI narrow steps such as fact extraction, story-bank refinement, grouping, layout, and drafting.
- [[LLMContextManagement]] matters for humanities work because clean Markdown/text, staged compression, retrieval, and avoiding format noise let model capacity go toward understanding rather than parsing.
- [[AIAssistedWriting]] should optimize the upstream workflow and average output quality instead of trying to make one model draft perfect on the first pass; generating multiple candidates helps fight mediocre defaults.
- In cultural and humanistic work, final quality still depends on real-world materials and trained taste: AI changes the verbs around research and production, but the subject remains the human worker and the object remains the material.

## Key Quotes
> "可追溯、可监督、可验证" - on the process standard for AI-assisted work.

> "你不是在'抽卡'，你是在生产。" - on controllable workflows rather than chance prompting.

> "压缩比扩张容易得多" - on using AI to reduce rich material rather than generate from thin prompts.

> "材料 x 品味" - on the author's final quality formula for AI-era work.
