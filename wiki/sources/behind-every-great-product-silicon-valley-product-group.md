## Summary
[[MartyCagan]] revisits [[SiliconValleyProductGroup]]'s early product-management essay by showing six cases where individual product managers or product leaders helped iconic products succeed. The article argues that [[ProductManagement]] is distinct from project management, design, sales, and committee decision-making because the product manager must synthesize customer value, business viability, technology, operations, legal, marketing, and stakeholder constraints into a working product outcome. Its cases include [[MicrosoftWordForMac]], [[Netflix]], [[GoogleAdWords]], [[BBCMobile]], [[ITunes]], and [[CreativeCloud]].

## Key Claims
- Strong [[ProductManagement]] is not backlog administration, CEO escalation, project management, or design by committee.
- Product managers must understand the business broadly enough to produce business outcomes, not merely define features.
- Breakthrough product solutions often come from intense collaboration with design and engineering rather than from users, sales, or stakeholder voting.
- Strong product managers need intelligence, creativity, persistence, and leadership because they often have to push organizations beyond comfortable assumptions.
- The [[MicrosoftWordForMac]] case shows platform-specific customer experience can matter more than internal code-base convergence.
- The [[Netflix]] case shows product work can create the technology mechanisms needed to make a business model viable.
- The [[CreativeCloud]] case shows [[ProductLeadership]] is required when a large successful company must disrupt a major existing revenue model.

## Key Quotes
