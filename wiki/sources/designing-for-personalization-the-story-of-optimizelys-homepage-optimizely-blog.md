## Summary
[[Optimizely]] describes redesigning its homepage around [[WebsitePersonalization]] and [[AccountBasedMarketing]] rather than maximizing one generic lead-conversion path. The company segmented visitors using firmographic, demographic, geographic, customer, and behavioral data, then designed flexible page modules for tailored messages, calls to action, proof, content, and events. It treated the redesign as an experiment whose success would require [[DeepFunnelMetrics]] such as engagement, lead qualification, and sales velocity alongside conversion rate; however, the post reports plans rather than results. Eight referenced remote images were unavailable during ingest, so their visual details could not be independently incorporated.

## Key Claims
- A homepage optimized for immediate account creation can generate many low-quality leads and leave even qualified prospects without enough product context.
- [[WebsitePersonalization]] requires a page architecture whose hero, messaging, calls to action, customer proof, content, and promotions can vary by audience.
- The proposed audiences combine named-account membership, industry, geography, customer status, and prior engagement across company properties.
- [[AccountBasedMarketing]] favors fewer, better-qualified leads from target accounts and industries over maximizing undifferentiated lead volume.
- A redesign may rationally lower homepage conversion if it improves engagement, lead qualification, downstream opportunity creation, or sales velocity.
- The new design remained an A/B test against the old homepage, with future element-level tests planned after establishing a new baseline.

## Key Quotes
> "This is, importantly, a test." - framing the redesign as a measured hypothesis rather than a proven improvement.

> "The time had come to move from a single ‘average best’ experience" - explaining the departure from one universal homepage.

## Connections

- [[AccountBasedMarketing]] - business strategy motivating target-account and industry experiences.
- [[DeepFunnelMetrics]] - engagement, qualification, and sales velocity broaden success beyond form conversion.
- [[ConversionRateOptimization]] - the prior homepage was heavily optimized for lead conversion and became the control in the redesign test.
- [[AATesting]] - relevant calibration discipline for interpreting the planned A/B tests, though the article does not report results.

## Contradictions
- No direct contradiction found. The source qualifies conversion-first optimization by arguing that raw lead volume can conceal weak qualification and poor downstream outcomes.
- The post was published before the experiment reached statistical significance, so it provides no evidence that personalization improved engagement, lead quality, sales velocity, or revenue.
