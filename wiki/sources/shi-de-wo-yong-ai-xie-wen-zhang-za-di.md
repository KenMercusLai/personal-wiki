## Summary
[[FengRuohang]] defends deep [[AIAssistedWriting]] as an accountable creator workflow rather than a hidden shortcut. The article argues that topic choice, argument structure, fact checking, revision taste, title selection, image direction, and final responsibility remain human even when [[Claude]], [[Gemini]], [[ChatGPT]], image models, and Codex accelerate drafting, verification, packaging, and layout. The embedded screenshots show Claude extracting article candidates from chat history and drafting image prompts, while the final generated orchestra image illustrates AI-assisted visual production.

## Key Claims
- [[AIAssistedWriting]] should be judged by the quality, accuracy, and insight of the finished content rather than by whether AI helped generate prose.
- The author's workflow keeps human ownership at the topic, structure, argument, revision, title, and publication-responsibility layers while using AI for draft expansion, verification support, prompt generation, and formatting.
- Multi-model checking with [[Gemini]] and [[ChatGPT]] can reduce factual risk, but the author still treats critical facts as requiring original-source verification and personal accountability.
- Dismissing a piece as "AI written" can become a low-effort critique that avoids engaging the actual claims, evidence, and reasoning.
- AI is presented as a multiplier: it amplifies the user's judgment, taste, and confusion rather than replacing the need for them.
- In an era of cheap fluent answers, scarce value shifts toward question selection, angle, insight, and noise filtering.

## Key Quotes
> "选题是我的。" - on human ownership of the starting question.

> "思路和骨架是我的。" - on argument structure as the article's central authorship layer.

> "AI 是倍乘器，不是加法器。" - on AI amplifying the user's existing quality or confusion.

> "真正值钱的就是问题" - on question selection becoming scarce when answers are cheap.
