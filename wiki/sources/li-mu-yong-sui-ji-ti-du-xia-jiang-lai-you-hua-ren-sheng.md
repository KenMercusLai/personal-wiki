## Summary
[[LiMu]] uses [[StochasticGradientDescent]] as a metaphor for life strategy: define a meaningful objective, take repeated small steps, vary the pace, explore beyond comfort zones, and restart when progress destabilizes. The essay treats ambition, persistence, rest, randomness, and humility about starting conditions as parts of one iterative practice rather than separate self-improvement slogans.

## Key Claims
- [[GoalSetting]] needs an objective large enough that its endpoint is not already obvious; a too-easy goal resembles a simple convex optimization problem.
- [[PersonalProductivity]] is framed as small-step persistence: choose a roughly plausible direction, move, observe, and keep moving.
- [[SelfDiscipline]] involves accepting discomfort from changing oneself while checking that suffering actually points toward the goal.
- Periodic rest is compatible with progress because step size should vary with available passion and recovery.
- Exploration and wrong decisions can help people escape early comfort zones, echoing the stochastic part of [[StochasticGradientDescent]].
- Starting position matters, but iterative progress and restarts can still converge if direction and step rhythm are adjusted.

## Key Quotes
> "你需要有目标。" - on the need for an objective.

> "每一次你找一个大概还行的方向（梯度），然后迈一步（下降）。" - on the basic life-as-SGD loop.

> "小步快跑。" - on the essay's preferred operating rhythm.
