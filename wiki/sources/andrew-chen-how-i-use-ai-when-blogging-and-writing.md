## Summary
[[AndrewChen]] describes how [[ChatGPT]] changed his blogging workflow from mostly solo weekend drafting into an iterative two-window process with WordPress and AI side by side. The article argues that AI is weak at producing finished prose in the author's voice, but useful for reducing blank-page friction, brainstorming outlines and questions, shaping voice-captured ideas, and doing cleanup or tone passes. The inspected screenshots show a generic Gen Z/video draft and a mixed-quality VR-app brainstorming list, reinforcing Chen's claim that AI output is valuable when it gives the writer material to criticize, select, and revise rather than publish directly.

## Key Claims
- [[AIAssistedWriting]] works best as an iterative support workflow, not as a one-shot replacement for the writer's examples, storytelling, statistics, judgment, or voice.
- Blank-page drafting can be useful even when most of the generated prose is later rewritten, because reacting to flawed text is easier than starting from nothing.
- [[ChatGPT]] is strongest for brainstorming outlines, topic lists, interview questions, critique prompts, and book-scale structures where a partial hit rate can still inspire useful work.
- Voice capture plus AI cleanup can turn spoken, stream-of-consciousness thinking into rough text that a writer can edit and develop.
- AI cleanup is useful both at the beginning of the writing process, when organizing rough ideas, and at the end, when adjusting tone or professionalism.
- Chen's desired next capabilities include fresher data, AI-generated figures and lead images, social-media posting integration, personal style customization, and the ability to use long private documents as argument context.

## Key Quotes
> "it's still better than starting with a blank page" - on using imperfect drafts as material to revise.

> "20% inspires you in your actual work" - on why brainstorming outputs do not need a perfect hit rate.

> "WordPress open in one window, and then ChatGPT in the other" - on the new blogging workflow.
