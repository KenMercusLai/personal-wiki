## Summary
[[Wulc]] summarizes the portfolio-construction section of [[ZhangXiaoyu]]'s personal investing course. The article argues that [[AssetAllocation]] is central to controlling volatility and maximum drawdown, compares the Permanent Portfolio, Ray Dalio-style All Weather allocation, and David Swensen-style allocation, then extends ordinary-investor implementation through rebalancing, [[FactorInvesting]], [[IndexFundStrategy]], and skepticism toward vague investing language. The inspected screenshot adds a practical comparison of off-exchange and exchange-traded fund channels.

## Key Claims
- [[AssetAllocation]] should be treated as the primary lever for controlling portfolio volatility and maximum drawdown while pursuing long-term returns.
- Major asset classes for ordinary investors include equities, fixed income, commodities, real estate trusts, and cash-like products; bonds and commodities have distinct risk drivers rather than being simple substitutes for stocks.
- The Permanent Portfolio, All Weather portfolio, and Swensen-style portfolio offer progressively higher expected return, expected volatility, and implementation complexity.
- Portfolio rebalancing operationalizes the allocation rule by trimming appreciated assets and adding to lagging assets; annual rebalancing is presented as sufficient for most ordinary investors.
- [[FactorInvesting]] sits between passive indexing and active selection by seeking diversified exposure to rewarded factors such as size, value, low volatility, dividend, quality, and momentum.
- [[IndexFundStrategy]] should favor broad-based, low-cost, passive products unless the investor has above-average insight into an industry, strategy index, or valuation rule.
- Investors should distrust attractive labels such as blue-chip, white-horse, value investing, or market sentiment when the words are too vague to support a concrete decision rule.

## Key Quotes
> "资产配置，市场择时和产品选择" - course framing for the three activities investors perform.

> "投资组合的目的不是为了寻找涨停股，而是为了获取持续稳健的收益" - rationale for diversified portfolio construction.

> "投资这门学科里没有金科玉律，也没有绝对真理" - closing warning against treating investing phrases as final rules.
