## Summary
Juli Clover explains that [[LiveListen]], introduced for general AirPods users in iOS 12 after earlier support for MFi hearing aids, turns an iPhone into a remote microphone whose audio is relayed to nearby Bluetooth headphones. The feature can support people with hearing difficulties, but the same design lets a user leave an iPhone near a conversation and listen from another room, making it a compact example of [[AssistiveTechnologyDualUse]]. The supplied Markdown contains no image references.

## Key Claims
- [[LiveListen]] uses an iPhone as a remote microphone and relays captured sound to [[AirPods]] or other compatible Bluetooth headphones.
- The capability existed for MFi-compatible hearing aids before iOS 12 extended it to general AirPods users.
- A listener can leave the iPhone in one room and hear nearby conversation from another room while remaining within Bluetooth range.
- Live Listen is enabled by adding the Hearing control to Control Center and then activating the feature through its ear icon.
- The same capability can provide hearing assistance or enable covert listening, depending on placement, awareness, consent, and use.

## Key Quotes
> "any iPhone can be turned into a remote listening device." - the article's privacy warning.
