## Summary
Taylor Monahan explains how [[MyEtherWallet]] began as a two-person Ethereum wallet side project and became operationally central as Ethereum, ICOs, phishing threats, ENS activity, and support demand exploded in 2017. The article announces [[MyCrypto]] as a new company, brand, domain, and codebase intended to give Monahan's team a more durable legal, financial, technical, and organizational foundation. It frames [[CryptoWalletSecurity]] and [[SupportLoadScaling]] as inseparable: client-side wallet users need control and education, but rapid growth turns every support gap, bug, and phishing risk into a high-stakes operational problem.

## Key Claims
- [[MyEtherWallet]] started as a simple, client-side interface for sending Ether when Ethereum users otherwise relied on command-line tooling.
- The DAO hard fork, ENS, ICOs, phishing sites, and rising Ether values made wallet support and security far more urgent than the original side-project structure could handle.
- User-facing crypto infrastructure must balance safety, control, ease of use, privacy, decentralization, and trustlessness while helping users understand irreversible risk.
- [[MyCrypto]] was created so Monahan's assembled team could continue development, support, education, and security work under a new brand and more scalable company structure.
- [[Kvhnuke]] retained control of the MEW GitHub repository, domain, AWS instances, and social accounts, while Monahan's team moved to the MyCrypto domain, support channels, and audited React/TypeScript codebase.
- The referenced image is a MyCrypto logo and adds brand-identification context, but no independent factual evidence beyond the article text.

## Key Quotes
