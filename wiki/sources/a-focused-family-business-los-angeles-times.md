## Summary
The Los Angeles Times profiles [[SmugMug]] in 2007 as a profitable, self-funded online photo-sharing company run by the [[MacAskillFamily]]. The article frames the company as both a family business and a customer-centered software service: [[DonMacAskill]], [[ChrisMacAskill]], Toni MacAskill, and other relatives built a paid subscription business that resisted outside investment while competing with free photo services from larger companies. The embedded photograph shows a SmugMug workplace scene with dogs and employees, reinforcing the article's family-at-work atmosphere but adding no separate factual claim beyond the text.

## Key Claims
- [[SmugMug]] grew without outside investors by charging for privacy, storage, support, and customer trust rather than relying on free ad-supported photo sharing.
- The [[MacAskillFamily]] turned family participation into a distinctive operating model, with multiple relatives taking roles in leadership, finance, support, testing, analytics, and customer service.
- [[FamilyBusiness]] can be both a competitive advantage and a source of friction because family bonds create unusual commitment while making conflict harder to separate from work.
- The company used customer intimacy as a product and marketing asset through forums, personal service emails, loyalty grandfathering, customer trips, and visible family participation.
- [[SmugMug]] reported more than 100,000 paying subscribers, 28 employees, $12 million in annual revenue, yearly revenue doubling, and profitability in 2007.
- The company's culture mixed deliberate informality with employee benefits, including flexible schedules, bonuses, 401(k) matching, stock options, health club memberships, and full medical coverage.

## Key Quotes
> "We work in earnest to create a family atmosphere." - [[DonMacAskill]] contrasting SmugMug with Google's dorm atmosphere.

> "Families have the advantage of a common bond for life, and the disadvantage of a common bond for life." - David Russell on the double edge of family businesses.
