## Summary
This 褪墨 article presents [[RichardLeider]]'s [[GPVCareerFormula]]: gifts plus passion plus values can guide career choice. It emphasizes that a career is broader than a single job, so [[CareerPlanning]] should look for paths that use strengths, interests, and values while avoiding unwanted day-to-day work where possible.

## Key Claims
- [[GPVCareerFormula]] frames career reflection through gifts, passion, and values.
- [[CareerPlanning]] should consider what someone recognizes as worthwhile, does well, and loves doing.
- A career is a path rather than a fixed job title, so people can redesign the role, industry position, or route toward a desired direction.
- Hobbies do not always translate directly into desirable jobs; the practical task is to find adjacent forms that preserve what matters while avoiding bad-fit conditions.
- [[GoalSetting]] matters because some career directions require interim steps before the preferred work becomes reachable.

## Key Quotes
> "G + P + V = C" - on the source's career-reflection formula.

> "职业生涯并不等于工作。" - on distinguishing career from a single job.

> "职业生涯指一条前进的路" - on treating career as a path.
