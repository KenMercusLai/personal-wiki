## Summary
This personal note distills the argument that new ideas recombine older ideas, with innovation quality depending on how ideas are combined across fields. It uses [[MickPearce]]'s termite-inspired [[EastgateCentre]] example, [[BenjaminJones]]'s burden-of-knowledge argument, and company-team examples to connect interdisciplinary creativity with collaborative organizational practice.

## Key Claims
- New ideas are usually combinations of old ideas, but the value of the innovation depends on the quality of the combination.
- [[InnovationAtIntersection]] is especially visible when techniques from one field are adapted to another, as in termite-inspired architecture.
- [[BenjaminJones]]'s specialization argument implies that modern innovation increasingly requires complementary teams rather than lone generalists.
- Internal company innovation depends on cross-team sharing of pain points, objections, proof assets, and financial constraints.
- [[OrganizationalDataSharing]] turns fragmented team observations into stronger hypotheses.

## Key Quotes
> "所有新想法都是旧想法的组合，但组合方式决定了创新的质量。" - opening summary of the innovation frame.

> "企业内部的创新 = 跨团队共享数据和观察。" - business application of the same recombination pattern.
