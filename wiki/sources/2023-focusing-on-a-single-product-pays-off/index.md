## Summary

This source is a 2023 year-in-review about ROZENMD, a France-based business that runs OnlineOrNot and sells the narrator's products. ROZENMD grew about twice as fast as expected, which the article attributes to compounding business learning around OnlineOrNot and continuing sales from a React useEffect book.

The central argument rejects treating independent products like an investment portfolio of unrelated small bets. The article argues that reaching the first meaningful revenue tier is harder than growing beyond it, so repeating that discovery work across unrelated products can be worse than improving a product that already has traction.

OnlineOrNot provides the concrete evidence: people who rejected the pre-launch pitch later returned as customers, early free-tier users converted to paid plans, and some bugs only became important after the product had existed long enough to reach affected users.

The article also covers the narrator's full-time Cloudflare work, including becoming one of the founding engineers of D1, and hardware side projects such as replacing a MacBook Air M1 with an upgraded Lenovo T480.
