## Summary
[[Wulc]] summarizes a Zhihu Live argument about when big data can transform an industry. The source argues that [[DeepLearning]] became effective through stronger representation capacity, better optimization hardware, and larger datasets, while [[BigDataIndustryTransformation]] requires [[BehavioralData]], full-population processing, and [[AutomatedDataApplication]] rather than only human-inspected reports.

## Key Claims
- [[DeepLearning]] reduces dependence on manual feature engineering by combining representation learning and model training.
- Industries are most transformable by big data when they have behavior data, need full-population processing, and can close the loop through automated applications.
- Transaction data and behavior data support different kinds of processing, so not every digitized industry has equally useful data for automation.
- Full-population processing is central to big data, even though practical systems such as CTR prediction may still sample negative examples for class-balance reasons.
- Insight dashboards become more powerful when they are turned into closed-loop automated systems where data generation, processing, and action reinforce one another.
- Business data work should let evidence override prior assumptions: decisions should be driven by data rather than only experience.

## Key Quotes
> "数据高于经验，让数据来决策，不能只是先入为主做假设" - the source's core business principle.

> "不能被开源软件的开发进度控制" - on why core business iteration should not be constrained by open-source infrastructure cadence.
