## Summary
This Sumo article presents [[BestBuy]]'s “Our Experts Recommend” widget as a [[ProductBundling]] tactic for increasing [[AverageOrderValue]] without raising the advertised price of the core product. It proposes three bundle patterns—complementary goods, multiple variants of the same repeatable product, and a one-time-purchase product paired with a store gift card—then promotes a [[Sumo]] spending-threshold discount tool as a simpler site-wide alternative.

## Key Claims
- Revenue can rise at constant unit volume when [[AverageOrderValue]] rises, although the article's arithmetic is illustrative rather than evidence of realized profit.
- A product-page bundle can expose related merchandise, let shoppers select or deselect components, and add several items to the cart in one action.
- [[ProductBundling]] should follow purchase context: complement the core item, offer multiple variants when buyers may want more than one, or pair a one-time product with a store gift card.
- The [[BestBuy]] examples show potential basket increases of $190.98 for a four-item Beats offer and $44.97 for four Fingerlings, but the source reports offer totals rather than acceptance rates or observed AOV lift.
- [[Sumo]]'s promoted alternative uses progressive cart-spend thresholds: after an item is added, a site-wide notification offers a discount for spending more and can present a larger next threshold after the first discount is earned.
- A threshold incentive acts after a strong buying signal and avoids configuring a distinct bundle for every product, but discount cost, margin, substitution, returns, and customer behavior still determine whether it improves profit.

## Key Quotes
> "You have an opportunity to increase your AOV without increasing your price." - on raising basket value while preserving the core product's advertised price.

> "People can easily deselect what they don't want" - on preserving shopper control within a recommended bundle.

> "Customers buy what they want, and then get incentivized to buy more." - on Sumo's site-wide threshold-discount alternative.
